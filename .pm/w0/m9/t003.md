## Objective

Start `features/stripe/operations/checkout-recovery-email.ts` with the read-only checks that decide whether an expired session earns an email (ADR027 D2, D5).

## Context

- The expired session carries `metadata.userId`, `metadata.clientId`, the price in its line items (fetch them with `expand` if the event lacks them), `customer` when one was set, `created`, and `after_expiration.recovery.url`.
- Plans and `retired` come from `SUBSCRIPTION_CONFIG`. Paid history comes from `paid_customers` and Stripe subscriptions, as `get-user-tier.ts` and `subscriptionTier` read them. "Never paid" includes ended subscriptions.
- `checkout.sessions.list` filters by `created`, `customer`, `customer_details` and `status`, never by metadata. Match `metadata.userId` in code.

## Steps

1. Return a typed reason for each ineligibility: no user or email, unknown or retired plan, paid before, later session `complete` or `open`, suppressed address, recovery disabled (`config.sendGrid.checkoutRecoveryGroupId` unset, moved here from t002).
2. Find later sessions with `created >= expired.created` (plus `customer` when present), paginate, keep those for the same user other than the expired one, and stop if any is `complete` or `open`.
3. Check the suppression list last (it costs a SendGrid call).
