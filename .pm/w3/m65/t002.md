## Objective

Create a synthetic ledger and a report pack that contains known mistakes.

## Context

- Fixtures must be synthetic; `*ledger.beancount` fixtures are checked by `bean-check` and `bea check` in CI.

## Steps

1. Generate a pack from a synthetic ledger with report-pack.
2. Seed at least: a total that does not foot, a missing account mapping, a truncated commodity precision, and one below-threshold difference.
3. Record the expected findings next to the fixture.

## Files

- `skills/beancount-report-pack/` fixtures

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The ledger passes `bea check`.
- [ ] Each seeded error is listed with its expected address.

## Out of scope

- XLSX fixtures.
