# Preserve negative signs in Reports category amounts

**Severity:** major. **Estimate:** 35–50 minutes. Owner: mobile. A2/A3: public example reports must explain how their components add up, including income reductions and expense refunds.

This is not a sign convention disagreement: ordinary income is displayed positive, so its offset must remain negative. The empty bar is consistent with a negative row, but the amount conceals that sign.
