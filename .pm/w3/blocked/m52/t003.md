## Objective

Finish the quota accounting and visibility scope of source note 446 after its external contract is available. Preserve the monthly account entitlement, expose supported shared-provider availability with accurate scope, and show the same supported facts in cloud status and refusal guidance.

## Files

- Backend AI-CFO usage service and REST/GraphQL/MCP adapters
- Backend public contracts and generated clients
- CLI cloud status, Ask-AI guidance, documentation, and regression tests

## Acceptance criteria

- REST, GraphQL, MCP, and CLI agree on the operator-approved quota contract.
- A near-limit fixture proves accepted and refused requests agree with the reported window/capacity and reservation policy.
- No user is told their account quota is exhausted solely because a shared provider limit was hit.
- Required package, parity, generated-contract, and adoption checks pass.

## Blocked

The upstream shared-capacity and reservation contract is unavailable in this public repository; see the milestone blocker and ADR 011 D4.

**Unblock:** provider/deployment operator supplies supported window/usage/reset/reservation behavior or sufficient configured capacity, product owner approves the account-versus-service presentation, and a reproducible near-limit setup is available. Owners: provider/deployment operator and product owner.
