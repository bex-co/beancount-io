# Mobile Catalan catalog mixes formal and informal "you"

The native app's Catalan catalog, `mobile/src/translations/ca.ts`, mixes the formal "vosaltres" form (about 9 `vostre`/`vostra` lines and 12 `-eu` imperatives such as `torneu` or `afegiu`) with the informal "tu" form (about 19 `tu`/`teu`/`pots` lines). w21/012 did not cover Catalan, and its `form-of-address.test.ts` does not scan `ca`.

**Done when:** mobile `ca.ts` uses the one Catalan form of address that w5/m14/t004 records, and `form-of-address.test.ts` scans `ca`.

**Source:** follow-up from the w3 `dashboard-qa` routine, 2026-11-27 (it filed the web-side milestone as w5/m14 in `474bf980c`). Package: `mobile/`.
