# Rework the shipped creation flow to the 2026-10-27 design

m54's dashboard and native flows (t001, t002, shipped) predate the revised [PRFAQ001](../../docs/prfaqs/PRFAQ001-ledger-creation.md) §3 and [ADR021](../../docs/adrs/ADR021-backend-v2-ledger-creation-experience.md) dashboard flow. The design now differs from what shipped in four ways:

- **Two steps, no review page.** Purpose, then details with a summary beside **Create ledger** (visibility · currency · account count, expandable to the preview). Public visibility gets an inline warning.
- **No history-start question.** The "Books begin" field goes; a known balance's **from** date becomes `historyStart`. Before building, settle one open point: the builder has a single `historyStart`, so the form should ask one shared "balances as of" date rather than one per account.
- **Personal modules.** Under personal, an **Also track in this ledger** list of investments, a rental property, and a side business ([`ADR021-wireframe-purpose.svg`](../../docs/images/ADR021-wireframe-purpose.svg)). This consumes [m59](./done/m59/README.md) (done 2026-11-01).
- **Currency suggestion per [ADR026](../../docs/adrs/ADR026-backend-v2-locale-and-currency-defaults.md).** Saved `defaultCurrency`, a labelled time-zone candidate, and source labels. The `defaultCurrency` account field (ADR026 phase 3) is not on the board yet.

Wireframes: `docs/images/ADR026-wireframe-web.svg` and `ADR026-wireframe-mobile.svg`.

m54's W4 dashboard repairs ([W4/314](../w4/done/314.md), [315](../w4/done/315.md), [317](../w4/done/317.md), [320](../w4/done/320.md), [321](../w4/done/321.md)) fixed the current review page, the "Books begin" default, and draft retention. The rework replaces those surfaces, so carry their behaviors forward as regression checks: no create before the final action, local-date defaults, and a failed create keeping its draft and `requestId`.

Larger than an hour across dashboard and mobile: promote to a milestone after m59 lands.
