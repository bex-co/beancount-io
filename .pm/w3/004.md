# Cash Flow hides the filters that determine its reporting period

**Promoted to [m20](./m20/README.md).** Fresh narrow Journal testing also found
the shared header hides every global filter and Clear all below the desktop
breakpoint, without a replacement. m20 is the sole implementation queue for
both header visibility failures. The original desktop-only estimate and
evidence below remain as historical source; the expanded milestone has seven
tasks and 180 total minutes. No fix has been implemented by this QA pass.

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 35–50m; an inbox item because the fix and
focused regression fit within one hour. **Pillar:** A2 — a newcomer must be able
to see and change the period of the statement they are reading.

Reproduced 2026-09-07 on production, signed-in public-example reader, Chrome 152,
English/light, 1440×1000; source checkout `60dbd7fd`, deployed SHA unverified.

1. Open `/ledger/open_ledger/example/balance-sheet`.
2. Set the header's Time field to `2017-09` and press Enter.
3. Navigate through Reports → Cash Flow.
4. Inspect the header and use Export → Markdown report.
5. Reload Cash Flow and inspect the controls again.

Expected: Cash Flow exposes the same Time, Account and payee/tag filters as
Income Statement so the user can inspect, change and clear its reporting scope.
Actual: none of those controls is present. Monthly and At Cost remain available,
but they control grouping and valuation. The navigation in step 3 still carries
the selected period: the downloaded statement explicitly says
“September 1, 2017 – September 30, 2017,” while the Cash Flow URL has no time
parameter. The controls remain absent after a fresh load. Period persistence
across reload is not asserted here.

Root cause:
`dashboard/src/common/components/ledger-layout/layout-header.tsx:49–57` omits
`/cash-flow` from `shouldShowFilters`, preventing `LedgerSearchControls` from
rendering at lines 71–74. Meanwhile
`dashboard/src/features/reports/cash-flow/loader.ts:13–15` and
`cash-flow/index.tsx:50` consume the shared account/filter/time state.
Balance Sheet and Income Statement are working route controls.

Fix: include Cash Flow among the routes exposing the standard filter controls;
verify selecting, changing and clearing a period on that page updates the
report and Markdown scope. Preserve interval/conversion controls and the
existing behavior of routes that do not consume global filters. Add a focused
header/route regression. No accounting model, sign convention, API, or export
renderer change is needed.

Dedupe: scanned all open milestone READMEs and open/completed notes for Cash Flow
filters and header route gating; no open overlap. Completed
`w4/done/m2/done/t002.md` requires filters to behave like Income Statement.
This is a missed acceptance criterion in that completed work; no evidence
establishes that the controls once worked. Targeted header history and fetched
`origin/main` show no newer repair.

Acceptance: Time, Account and payee/tag controls are visible on desktop Cash
Flow; changing/clearing the period changes its displayed/exported scope; the
tested sibling report routes keep their controls. Narrow-screen filter
interaction, other locales/themes, and a patched implementation remain
unverified.

Local evidence: `dashboard/tmp/qa-20260907-w3/cash-flow-hidden-period.png`,
`cash-flow-september.md`, and `cash-flow-evidence.json`. The reproduction and
exported period above are sufficient without access to ignored artifacts.
