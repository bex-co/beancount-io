## Objective

Make the table able to distinguish a verb that *cannot* reach a surface from one that *has not yet*. Today 134 MCP absences and 119 REST absences all read as settled, which is exactly how three of them went stale unnoticed (ADR 0008 D1).

## Context

- `structural:` — will not change: a GraphQL response cannot stream an archive; a `session-only` op has no scope that unlocks it, deliberately (ADR 0006 D3).
- `deferred:` — not built yet; the string names what unblocks it.
- The 34 `session-only` verbs are the largest structural block and reach neither MCP nor REST. They are ADR 0008 D4's named boundary, not backlog.
- Expected split after this task: **39 out-of-scope on both MCP and REST** — 34 `session-only`, 3 Plaid Link browser-ceremony verbs, 2 screen-shaped projections (ADR 0008 D4) — plus 6 structural `gqlExempt` (streaming, byte-serving, foreign wire formats). Everything else is `deferred:`, leaving ceilings of 95 (MCP) and 80 (REST).
- **`plaidBinding` is the one to look at closely.** It covers 12 rows but its argument — a browser widget an agent cannot drive — reaches exactly 3 of them (ADR 0008 D4a). The other 9, plus 6 more Plaid verbs, are ordinary operations on an already-linked bank and are `deferred:`, not structural. Getting this wrong buries fifteen customer-facing verbs, including the bank-import workflow.
- Verify rather than assume — these counts are what t003's ceilings are set from.

## Steps

1. Prefix every exemption category constant, or every row, with one of the two markers.
2. Walk the categories and decide each: `notAgentShaped` and the `runBqlQuery` hatch are `deferred:` — they are arguments about today's tool list, not permanent limits.
3. Print the resulting per-surface counts; they are t003's input.
