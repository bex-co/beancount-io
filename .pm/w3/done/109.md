# Mobile Home and journal round nonzero crypto rewards to zero

Severity: minor. Estimate: 45m. Package: mobile. A2 — a public example must show the recorded commodity movement without erasing small balances.

Environment: HEAD 27873fc2, native development build 1.20260906.47 loading current JS, iPhone 17e / iOS 26.5, English/System/light, default large text size. Signed-in QA session at https://beancount.io; public synthetic open_ledger/crypto-example, read-only access verified with GetLedger.

Repro (September 10):
1. Open beancount:///ledger/open_ledger/crypto-example. Wait for Home to settle.
2. Home Recent Transactions shows Lido as +0.00 STETH and Ethereum 2.0 as +0.00 ETH.
3. Tap the card's See all. Transactions shows the same values on December 31, 2024; November 30's Ethereum 2.0 reward also shows +0.00 ETH.
4. Terminate/relaunch the app. The selected crypto ledger and the zeroed Home rewards persist.

Expected/actual: December 31 Ethereum 2.0 hash a2880e3ebb69187903bf71fda168e234 contains Assets:Staking:Ethereum:ETH +0.004 ETH and Income:Crypto:Staking -13.40 USD; its headline must preserve the nonzero 0.004 ETH quantity. Lido hash ee68f43ea4daa5b3d744fe857558e0c4 records +0.005 STETH and -16.75 USD; its displayed +0.00 STETH also loses the recorded quantity. This is numeric rounding, not clipped text. No conversion to USD is requested by these rows.

Root cause: mobile/src/screens/transactions-screen/utils/transaction-display-utils.ts:14-19 applies minimumFractionDigits:2 and maximumFractionDigits:2 to every currency. selectTransactionAmount:53-89 chooses the single Assets/Liabilities commodity bucket in these rewards and passes it to that formatter. EntryRow at entry-row/index.tsx:103,146-157 uses the resulting text verbatim and prefixes + from the unrounded sign, producing a positive-looking zero.

Fix: preserve meaningful commodity precision in the selected headline amount rather than applying a universal cents cap. For these fixtures render +0.004 ETH and +0.005 STETH, retain $9,785.30 for the Coinbase cash-leg control, and preserve signs and zero correctly. Keep currency buckets separate and the existing money-leg preference. If aggregating multiple commodity postings, preserve their decimal precision through summation as well as formatting; do not merely patch the label or replace the unit with a USD number. Reuse/consolidate the existing recorded-decimal formatting approach where appropriate without coupling the list to a detail screen. Add selector regression coverage for these small units, larger units, USD cents, negative and zero values, and same-currency multi-posting aggregation.

Blast radius: shared EntryRow is used by Home Recent Transactions, Transactions, merchant detail and Reports account-transactions cards. selectHeroAmount in transaction-detail-screen/selectors/select-transaction-detail.ts:35 also calls selectTransactionAmount; its hero therefore shares the source path, but live detail is currently blocked by w3/106's missing import. Recurring merchant-row.tsx:155 directly calls formatAmount for typical amounts; this caller is source-traced, not separately reproduced. AccountEntryRow uses a separate selector and is not claimed affected by this cause.

Working controls: Coinbase cash headline +$9,785.30 equals 9800.00 minus 14.70 USD and Compound +2,300.00 CUSDC agrees with its 2300.00 units. The API supplies the full small quantities; no server truncation or unauthorized mutation occurred. No new error was captured during the bounded log read.
