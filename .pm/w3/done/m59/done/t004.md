## Objective

Prove the hosted builder and the Python engine produce equivalent accounting results for every module fixture before modules are listed.

## Context

- ADR021: a preset or module is not listed until both engines pass its fixtures; modules need one fixture each plus all three together.
- w3/m53/t005 established the harness: `backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/features/ledger/setup/__tests__/build-ledger.test.ts` runs fixtures through `previewLedgerSetup`, and `cli/tests/test_setup_conformance.py` runs the same fixtures through `bea init --preset` plus `bea check`.

## Steps

1. Run the m53 conformance harness over the new fixtures on both engines.
2. Verify Appendix A.9's acceptance scenario: dividend, per-property rent and repair, and business-bank payment each total under their own group.

## Files

- `backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/features/ledger/setup/__tests__/build-ledger.test.ts`
- `cli/tests/test_setup_conformance.py`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every module fixture yields equivalent files and report values on both engines.
- [ ] The A.9 acceptance scenario passes on both engines.

## Out of scope

- New presets or modules beyond the three.

## Evidence (2026-11-01)

- Hosted side: `build-ledger.test.ts` ("module conformance fixtures") runs the four module fixtures through `previewLedgerSetup`: opened accounts equal the fixture's, the FIFO brokerage is booked, every account A.9's scenario posts to is opened, and the files equal the fixture's pinned `hostedFiles`, so any builder drift fails here.
- CLI side: `cli/tests/test_setup_conformance.py` builds the same fixtures with `bea init --preset personal@1 --module …`, compares opened accounts and booking, and runs `bea check`. A.9's scenario (dividend 40, rent 1,200 and repair 50 for Property1, client payment 500 into the business bank) is appended both to the hosted builder's pinned files and to `bea init`'s, checked, and queried: per-account totals are equal across the two and match the fixture, with nothing in everyday spending.
- Engines: hosted builder at this commit; `bea 0.3.1` with its bundled Beancount engine. All four fixtures pass; no module needed unlisting.
