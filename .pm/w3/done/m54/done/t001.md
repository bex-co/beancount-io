## Objective

Turn the dashboard's one-shot create form into the staged flow ADR021 describes: preset, title and currency, first accounts and opening state, review, create — with usable defaults at every stage.

## Context

- One `LedgerForm` (`dashboard/src/features/ledger-list/components/ledger-form.tsx`) serves the welcome page and the sidebar dialog today; ADR021 records its "Save" button and stale Starter copy as defects this flow corrects.
- Consume m53's preview operation for the review stage and its `requestId` for resumable, duplicate-free creation.

## Steps

1. Split the form into stages with defaults; resolving currency once via ADR021 precedence (explicit selection, device/region hint, USD) and preserving corrections across re-renders, switches, errors, back navigation, and OTP retries.
2. Derive the suggested address from the title with the existing slug function, falling back to the `my-book` suggestion when the slug is empty, shown for correction.
3. Review stage renders m53's preview (files, accounts, validation) before the create call.

## Files

- `dashboard/src/features/ledger-list/components/ledger-form.tsx`
- `dashboard/src/features/ledger-list/pages/welcome-page/`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Accepting every default creates a valid private ledger without answering a question.
- [ ] The review stage matches the created ledger; abandoning and returning resumes without a duplicate.

## Out of scope
