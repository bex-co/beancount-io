## Objective

Replace `userId: string` with `identity: Identity` on `IPlaidItemService`, and hand that identity to `authorizeLedger` instead of manufacturing a trusted one. A narrow token then stays narrow inside the service, matching what every ledger service already does.

## Context

- `src/features/plaid/service/plaid-item-service.ts` — fifteen interface methods; `assertLedgerAccess` / `assertLedgerWriteAccess` are the two places `trustedIdentity(userId)` is built.
- `src/features/plaid/api/resolvers/plaid-resolver.query.ts` and `.mutation.ts` — the call sites. They **already hold the real identity** (`ctx.getCurrentIdentity()`) and already call `assertLedgerAuthorization` before delegating; they simply pass `identity.userId` onward. Passing `identity` is a smaller change than it looks.
- `userId` is still needed inside — derive it from `identity.userId` rather than taking both, so the two can never disagree.
- Compare `src/features/ledger/service/ledger-data-service.ts`, which takes `identity` and passes it straight to `authorizeLedger`. That is the shape to converge on.

## Steps

1. Change the interface and implementation to take `identity: Identity`.
2. Point `assertLedgerAccess` / `assertLedgerWriteAccess` at that identity.
3. Update the resolver call sites to pass `identity`.

## Files
