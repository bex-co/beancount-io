## Objective

Pin the behavior with tests that would have caught the original defect — which means asserting on the **resulting date**, not on whether parsing succeeded.

## Context

- The defining case: under a day-first language, `12/09/2026` must produce **9 December**, not 12 September. A test that only asserts "parses successfully" passes on the buggy code.
- The second defining case: an input valid under a *different* locale's ordering must be **rejected**, not silently reinterpreted.
- Re-run completed `m24`'s cases under each tested locale: partial input, `02/30/2025`, `02/28/2025`, paste of `2025-06-15`, clearing to empty, `not-a-date`.
- Re-run completed `m22`'s guarantee: switching language does not reinterpret an existing selection or typed draft.
- Existing suites live beside `date-picker-utils.ts` and the forms that consume it.

## Steps

1. Table-drive the parser tests over at least English, a day-first locale and one more ordering.
2. Assert resulting dates, rejections, and the ISO fallback in every locale.
3. Re-run the m24 and m22 case sets per locale.
4. Add a component-level test that the rendered hint matches the pattern in force.
5. Confirm each test fails against the constant-pattern implementation.
