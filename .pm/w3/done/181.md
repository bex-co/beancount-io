Working/API control: independently aggregate the 23 cleared public fixture transactions into ten payees; counts and last dates agree with the ordinary-size UI (Contoso7/2026-05-22, Northwind4/2026-03-04, Transfer3/2026-05-31, Fabrikam2/2026-06-30, IRS2/2026-06-15). Root cause: mobile/src/screens/merchants-screen/merchant-row.tsx:24–36 lays out icon, flex:1 middle column and date in one horizontal row; date:99–104 has flexShrink:0 and no width budget. Name/subtitle at183–196 are restricted to one line, and the general trailing date at214–217 is also single-line. Scaled date width consumes the middle column. The recurring trailing container at75–79 has maxWidth42% and flexShrink:0; its amount/meta at201–211 are single-line, so enlarged amounts and Overdue labels ellipsize while the remaining middle column also truncates its name/cadence. Both branches need adaptive row layout.

Fix: adapt this row to constrained width/font scale so the merchant name and count keep usable width and the date can reflow below/beside them within the available space. Allow the row to grow vertically; do not solve it by disabling font scaling, hiding dates/counts, or shrinking the name to an unreadable size. Preserve ordinary-size layout, whole-row navigation and accessible payee label, sort/search grouping and recurring badge/cadence/amount presentation. Verify short IRS and longer Contoso/Northwind names at maximum and default sizes, with and without search. For recurring rows, reflow the name, full formatted amount, cadence and next/Overdue label into readable lines; do not ellipsize the financial amount. Add meaningful constrained-layout regression coverage for general and recurring rows, including short names and long names/amounts.
