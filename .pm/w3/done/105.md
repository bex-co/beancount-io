# Mobile Settings clips invitation copy in Spanish and at larger text sizes

Severity: minor. Estimate: 30m. Package: mobile. A3 — localized invitation copy should remain readable for community users.

Environment: native development build 1.20260906.47, iPhone 17e / iOS 26.5, logical 390×844, light appearance, System theme; authenticated QA session on https://beancount.io, public open_ledger/example. Initially reproduced at 7f4b6190 on September 9, still visible after native relaunch at 27873fc2 on September 10.

Repro:
1. Open the drawer → Settings → Language.
2. Scroll the native custom picker from English to Español and Confirm.
3. Read the invitation summary at the top of Settings; relaunch and return to Settings.

Expected: the full local translation ends “su futuro financiero.” Actual: the visible third line ends “su futuro fina”; the remaining text is absent on both runs. English shows its entire summary, including “financial future.” Switching to Spanish itself works and persists; this is a text-layout failure.

Root cause: mobile/src/screens/referral-screen/components/invite-section.tsx:22-36 constrains the row to height 80 with borders, reserves 80 points for the gift, and renders 20-point line-height text in the remaining width. The Spanish text needs more space than this constrained row supplies. At line 66 the component passes the complete translation directly to Text; mobile/src/translations/es.ts:212-213 contains the full “financiero.” ending. The exact native text-layout truncation mechanism was not instrumented. No API supplies this copy.

Fix: let the invitation row grow with its localized text and font size, retaining an 80-point minimum/icon footprint and usable spacing. Use the available layout width rather than a fixed screen-width assumption where necessary. Keep the whole row tappable and its translated accessibility label. Verify all Spanish text is visible on this device and English remains correctly aligned.

Blast radius/control: searched InviteSection callers; Settings About (mobile/src/screens/setting/about.tsx:11, route app/(app)/settings.tsx) is the sole caller. Its tap navigates to app/(app)/referral.tsx, whose separate content layout is not proven affected. English on the same device is a live working control. No invitation was sent. Dark mode, larger text sizes, other locales, VoiceOver and Android remain unverified for this defect.

Evidence: mobile/tmp/qa-2026-09-09/language-spanish-settings.jpg and language-spanish-relaunch.jpg show the repeated settled failure; mobile/tmp/qa-2026-09-10/english-wheel.jpg also shows the clipped summary behind the modal after a fresh launch, and english-restored.jpg shows the complete English control. Screenshots are 946×2048. locale-logs.json is a bounded error capture, not proof of absence of all errors. English/System and light appearance were restored.

Dedupe: searched all .pm markdown including done for inviteSummary, InviteSection, invitation, referral, Spanish, clipping and truncation.
