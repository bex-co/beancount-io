## Objective

Fix the accrual policy contract before any skill text: cumulative vs current-period targets, service interval, day-count convention, and how invoices, adjustments, and reversals net over the same scope.

## Context

- ADR020 shows the upstream shorthand (subtract prior accruals and invoices) is wrong under reversals; its worked example ($1,200 over twelve months) must distinguish $200 still booked from $200 reversed.
- An approved policy may use an estimate, but its method and source are explicit; this does not weaken `beancount-ask`'s no-estimation rule.

## Steps

1. Write the contract and calculation reference with the worked example in both states.
2. Define stable schedule/period identity and the duplicate-prevention rule for later posting.
3. Encode the calculation as executable recipes with fixture cases, not prose arithmetic.
