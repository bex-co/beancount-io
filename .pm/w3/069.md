# The date calendar cannot reach a valid next-year date

Severity: **minor**. Owner: **dashboard**. Estimate: **30–45 minutes**.
A2: users can schedule a future budget through the calendar and reopen it on
the date already entered, without working around an unrelated year cutoff.

Production `https://beancount.io`, 2026-09-08, Chrome 152, English, light.
Fresh QA contexts at 1440×1000 and 390×844, public `open_ledger/minimax`.
Local HEAD `a315c273`, fetched main `853a4942`; implicated source unchanged,
deployed SHA unverified. Date figures below use the current year 2026; repeat
with a fixed test clock or use the next calendar year when rerunning later.

1. Open `/ledger/open_ledger/minimax/budget` → Add Budget → Select date.
2. Choose December in the Month dropdown. The grid is **December 2026**,
   Next Month is disabled, and the Year dropdown ends at **2026**. Its
   101 options cover **1926–2026**; no 2027 option or January 15 day is available.
3. Close the popup and fill the Date text field with **01/15/2027**. Reopen
   Select date: the input retains that date, but the grid/year remain
   **December 2026 / 2026**, with zero selected cells.
4. As a control, choose October 15 through the calendar in a fresh form;
   Date correctly becomes **10/15/2026**, so future dates within this year work.
5. Choose Expenses:CostOfRevenue, Amount 100 MUSD and fill **01/15/2027**.
   Add Budget is enabled and prepares budget.date **2027-01-15**. The request
   was intercepted; no future budget was actually created.

All cases repeat at desktop and narrow widths. Today also restores
**09/08/2026**. The failure is calendar navigation/display, not a general
rejection of future date input or a plan/permission limit.

Root: `dashboard/src/common/components/ui/date-picker.tsx:158–165` opts into
`captionLayout="dropdown"` but supplies neither startMonth nor endMonth.
The common Calendar wrapper at `ui/calendar.tsx:12–41` forwards this layout
and supplies no bounds. The pinned react-day-picker 9.13.0 implementation:

- `helpers/getNavMonth.js:25–43` chooses the start of today minus 100 years
  and the end of the current year when a year dropdown has no explicit bounds.
- `helpers/getInitialMonth.js:16–20` clamps the requested January 2027 month
  to that December 2026 end bound.
- `helpers/getNextMonth.js:31–33` returns no next month at the boundary.
  `useCalendar.js:22–24,50–51,81–86` applies these navigation constraints.

This is documented default library behavior. The
[v9 dropdown contract](https://daypicker.dev/v9/docs/caption-and-nav-layouts)
calls for explicit start/end bounds and describes its default past-century
range. The installed version's archive integrity was verified and its actual
helpers inspected; no library upgrade or fork is needed.

Fix DatePicker's accounting-date configuration: give the dropdown explicit
rolling bounds, for example the start/end of the years 100 years before/after
the displayed month, falling back to the selected value or today. Recompute
when the displayed month changes so browsing to a boundary does not introduce
another fixed future cutoff. Keep the current selected/external date within
the range when opening/synchronizing the picker. Preserve month/year dropdowns,
past navigation and the existing valid-date contract; do not hard-code 2027,
disable future text input to match the popup, or require past-only dates.

A local control using the exact pinned helpers and date-fns4.1.0 operations
reproduces the default 1926–2026 bounds and December clamp. Explicit rolling
bounds around January 2027 yield 1927–2127, preserve January 2027 as the initial
month and enable December→January navigation. This is a helper-level control,
not a patched production UI.

component; other JSX Calendar hits are Lucide icons. Its seven form consumers
are Budget; Journal Transaction, Balance, Note and OpenAccountForm; Accounts
OpenAccountDialog; and ReceiptReviewForm. Only Budget is live-proven here.
No receipt processing or account/entry creation was performed.
