# Chart contact leaves the ledger drawer unresponsive

Severity: major. Estimate: 45–60m, with uncertainty around the native gesture cause. Owning package: mobile. A2: a chart interaction must not strand users away from ledger discovery and settings.

Environment: September 11, 2026, HEAD f1965988 / mobile 7538fb0d; io.beancount.ios 1.20260906.47, iPhone 17e / iOS 26.5, English, System/light, default large text. Signed-in QA reads public open_ledger/crypto-example on https://beancount.io. No ledger writes, outbound messages, or product edits.

Minimal native repro:

1. Relaunch and wait for the populated Home screen (6M net worth $114,581.88).
2. Tap the plot around logical x93,y360 on the 390×844 screen, then release.
3. Tap ledger-drawer-button. The drawer opens visibly.
4. Tap drawer-settings-row. The drawer remains open; Settings does not appear.

Initial PID98663 sequence included a chart tap, a horizontal scrub, 3M then 6M. Settings failed by test ID twice and by actual coordinate x110,y780; tapping the exposed content at x365,y440 did not close the drawer. Fresh PID6783 established a working control: Home -> drawer -> Settings succeeds before chart interaction. Back -> chart tap/scrub -> 3M/6M -> drawer -> Settings reproduces; Discover also fails. Fresh PID10213 narrowed the trigger to one chart tap, followed by drawer -> Settings, with no scrub or period change. This minimal repeat uses only Expo MCP native taps and screenshots, eliminating dependence on the custom scrolling helper. A subsequent leftward native swipe did not recover the drawer. Relaunch is a verified recovery; other recovery paths remain unverified.

Expected/actual: releasing the chart must release its touch ownership. The next drawer item should navigate and close the drawer, and tapping exposed content should dismiss it. Instead, the visible drawer ignores those actions after a chart touch. The chart itself still returns to its resting summary; the earlier 3M summary $5,535.30 / 5.08% agrees with the public balance-series API, so this is not a report-data failure. No crash or CPU spin observed; bounded JS capture was empty.

Source trace / likely cause, not yet proven by native state tracing: src/common/d3/interactive-line-chart.tsx:396 obtains useHorizontalSwipeOwnerGesture; :572–596 uses a simultaneous Pan and resets only the scrub index onFinalize; :629 mounts the owner detector around the chart. src/common/horizontal-swipe-owner.ts:34–41 creates Gesture.Manual().blocksExternalGesture(edgeSwipeRef) without explicit touch-up/cancel termination, relying on the comment's assumption that it ends with the touch. Installed react-native-gesture-handler/apple/Handlers/RNManualHandler.m:53–57 only forwards touch end and handles the gesture; it does not explicitly fail/end the manual recognizer there. This makes the marker's lifecycle the first hypothesis to validate, not a proven explanation for every ignored tap. The drawer's own handlers at src/components/ledger-drawer/ledger-drawer.tsx:433–435 call onClose and router.push normally; :663–668 provides the backdrop onPress. These work before chart contact.

Fix/acceptance: make chart ownership end on the final pointer release and every cancellation path while retaining protection against drawer opening during a chart scrub. Validate the suspected manual-marker lifecycle first, including its external-gesture dependency; do not merely reload the app, add a timeout, or disable chart interactions. After a tap and after a drag, verify drawer Settings and Discover navigate and backdrop dismissal works. Cover cancellation and multiple pointers in a meaningful gesture-lifecycle test, and verify the actual iPhone 17e sequence because mocked callback tests cannot establish native hit testing. Preserve ordinary drawer edge drag and chart period controls. If native investigation shows a broader fix exceeds an hour, promote the finding rather than claiming the hypothesis is confirmed.

Blast radius: useHorizontalSwipeOwnerGesture callers are InteractiveLineChartD3, ScrollableAxisChart, and SegmentedPages; the Home balance chart also sits in segmented pages. Home line-chart and Reports bar-chart contact are natively reproduced. Budgets, account/merchant charts, RTL, Android, physical hardware and concurrent/multitouch gestures remain unverified. No endpoint changes proposed. Empty, offline, denied and expired-session states were not exercised for this gesture failure.
