## Objective

Lift the `typer<0.29` cap and regenerate every lock with `uv`.

## Context

- The engine pins typer 0.27.2 in `engine-requirements.lock` and `engine-optional-*.lock`; `bea_engine/__main__.py` falls back to `click`, which 0.29 no longer installs.
- Lockfiles are generated, never hand-edited; declaring `click` as a direct dependency needs the owner's approval (root `AGENTS.md`).

## Steps

1. Remove the cap.
2. Fix the engine's `click` fallback (or get approval to declare `click`).
3. Regenerate `cli/uv.lock` and the engine locks with the documented commands.

## Files

- `cli/pyproject.toml`
- `cli/uv.lock`
- `cli/engine-*.lock`
- `cli/src/bea_engine/__main__.py`
