## Objective

Lift the scan predicate that found this damage into a guard covering all
fifteen locales, so the class cannot return in any language.

## Context

- The predicate exists but is scoped to two Russian catalogs, by key prefix,
  in `dashboard/src/features/journal/components/__tests__/russian-journal-labels.test.tsx`.
- It has already been proven to catch what a manual scan missed: it found
  `journal.exporting` = `Экспортing...`, which a Python scan had skipped because
  Python's `\w` is Unicode-aware and matched the preceding Cyrillic letter while
  JavaScript's is ASCII-only.
- The same damage has now been found in Catalan (`w4/m14`), Russian (`w4/149`)
  and Ukrainian (this milestone), so a per-locale guard is the third repeat.
- Some legitimate deviations exist and must be recorded rather than asserted
  away — `zh/journal.pending` is already carried as a known deviation in the
  Catalan guard.

## Steps

1. Move the predicate and its allowlist into one shared location the guards can share.
2. Assert over every locale that no message mixes its own script with a stray English word outside the allowlist.
3. Record any remaining offender in other locales as a named deviation with a filed note, rather than widening the allowlist to hide it.
4. Prove the guard is load-bearing by reverting one repaired catalog and watching it fail.

## Files

- `dashboard/src/features/journal/components/__tests__/russian-journal-labels.test.tsx`
- a new shared module for the predicate and allowlist
- possibly `dashboard/src/test/`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The guard covers all fifteen locales, not just two catalogs.
- [ ] Reverting any repaired catalog fails it.
- [ ] Every excluded offender is named with a filed follow-up, not silently allowlisted.
- [ ] The dashboard gates pass.

## Out of scope

- Repairing the offenders the widened guard finds in locales other than Ukrainian — those get filed. The remaining Russian ones are already `w4/153`.
