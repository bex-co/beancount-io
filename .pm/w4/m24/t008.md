## Objective

Walk the screens this milestone changed with `?lang=uk` and confirm the
repaired strings render as Ukrainian at both widths, rather than trusting the
catalogs alone.

## Context

- This milestone changes user-facing strings on most screens in the product, so
  the adoption surface is the screens themselves, not a package README.
- `w4/149` found two defects this way that a catalog diff alone had missed: a
  heading owned by a different catalog than the one under repair, and entries
  that were partly translated rather than untranslated.
- The public `open_ledger/example` ledger is anonymous and read-only, so
  write-path surfaces (the new-entry form, Danger Zone, SSH keys) cannot be
  reached there; say so rather than implying they were checked.

## Steps

1. Open the journal, account report, statistics, documents, events, gallery and settings screens with `?lang=uk` at 1440x900 and 390x900.
2. Collect the rendered headings, placeholders and button labels, and confirm none carries a stray English word outside the allowlist.
3. Confirm `lang="uk"` on the document and that rows still load.
4. Note any screen that could not be reached anonymously, and any defect found that belongs to a different catalog — file it rather than widening this milestone.
