# Keep collapsed sidebar group flyouts open after touch activation

Severity: minor navigation failure with an expanded-sidebar workaround. Estimate: 55m, dashboard. A2: tablet and touch-screen visitors should reach report and advanced pages from the compact sidebar.

Environment: production https://beancount.io, signed out, isolated headless Chromium149.0.7827.55 with actual Playwright touch input (`hasTouch:true`, `isMobile:true`), English/light. Reproduced at1024×768 after a390px phone session and in a separate fresh1024×768 context. Fresh browser reports innerWidth1024, maxTouchPoints1, hover:hover false. This is browser touch emulation, not a physical tablet or native app. Local/fetched main26d54a9db7d33c31a549bd12a0454a1c01f3f038; deployed main-BIr0jJni.js, exact deployed SHA unverified.

Reproduction: open `/ledger/open_ledger/example/journal` at1024px, tap Toggle Sidebar to collapse to the icon rail, and tap Reports. After settlement its aria-expanded is false and no flyout dialog exists. Three separately settled Reports taps on the first context all leave it closed. A fresh context reproduces Reports and Advanced. The expected outcome is a persistent flyout with tappable destinations. Do not use a global Income Statement link count as proof: Trial Balance has an unrelated Related Pages link farther down the document.

Controls: at390px the normal mobile drawer allows Reports → Trial Balance and closes after navigation. At1024px expanding the sidebar allows Reports → Income Statement by touch. In the collapsed rail, keyboard Enter opens Reports; then tapping Income Statement in that dialog navigates correctly. Screenshot touch-rail-after-tap.png was visually inspected and shows the compact rail without a flyout.

Cause: `dashboard/src/common/components/ledger-layout/ledger-group-flyout.tsx:58–64,77–90` treats every pointerenter/leave as hover, including touch, while PopoverTrigger independently toggles on click. Captured touch events enter/down/up/leave see expanded=false; by the click capture it is true, and the final state is false. Touch entry opens the popover before its click toggles it closed; touch leave also schedules the120ms hover-close timer. The trigger and popup both share these unqualified handlers. `ledger-sidebar.tsx:350` chooses this component for collapsed non-mobile layouts (the viewport breakpoint, independent of actual touch capability). History shows the implementation introduced in4652596f and unchanged on current main. No API failure or authentication issue is involved.

Bounded repair: restrict hover opening/closing to appropriate hover pointer events and let touch/keyboard activation control the existing popover. Handle both trigger and popup hover handlers and pending close timers; preserve mouse movement across the gap, keyboard open/Escape, outside dismissal, destination navigation, active-group styling and expanded/mobile behavior. Keep the existing component and primitives; no dependency or sidebar redesign.

Acceptance: fresh1024px touch context can tap Reports and Advanced once, wait beyond120ms, and choose their destinations; repeat after collapse/reload. Verify keyboard and mouse hover controls, gap crossing, outside dismissal and both compact and expanded layouts. Exercise real pointer/click ordering in regression coverage, not an isolated click handler. Run dashboard native gates when implementing. Physical devices, pen input, current WebKit/Firefox and the patched behavior remain unverified.
