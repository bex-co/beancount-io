## Outcome

Added `query-result-geometry.test.tsx`: six cases that render the **real**
react-window `List` — the sibling suite replaces it, so it can only prove text
reached the DOM, and the reported defect was text that was in the DOM and in
the CSV but clipped off screen. A ResizeObserver stub reports each row's height
from the content that row actually renders: one line per stacked inventory
unit, one per source line of a printed directive. JSDOM loads no stylesheet, so
the stub also honours the cell's whitespace rule by hand — `truncate` and
`whitespace-nowrap` collapse a value onto one line, which is exactly the
flattening that hid the postings. Row offsets, overlap and the list's own
extent then become assertable.

The cases:

- an eight-unit inventory row gets eight lines of height, and react-window's
  own sizer reports the same extent;
- with a tall row above three short ones, each row starts exactly where the
  previous one ended — no overlap, nothing squeezed;
- a four-line printed directive gets four lines while the one-line row beside
  it stays compact;
- every posting amount and every unit survives the sizing pass;
- the cell neither flattens nor clips its own content;
- a 400-row result holds the viewport at the 600px cap, keeps fewer than 200
  rows in the DOM, and reports `aria-rowcount` 401 for the whole result.

Both halves of the repair were proved load-bearing by reverting them:

- pinning rows back to `rowHeight={ROW_HEIGHT}` with the matching fixed
  container fails 3 of the 6 (the eight-unit height, the overlap offsets and
  the four-line directive);
- putting `truncate` back on the cell fails 4 of the 6;
- restoring both passes 6/6, and the whole BQL area 113/113.

Limitation recorded honestly: JSDOM has no layout engine, so the wrapping of a
single long line — the 390px PRINT case — cannot be measured here. That half is
covered by the browser checks in t002 and t003 and re-confirmed in t006, not by
this suite.

Gates from `dashboard/`: `yarn format:check`, `yarn lint`, `yarn test` (377
files, 4253 passed, 1 pre-existing skip) and `yarn build` all pass. `gitleaks`
reports no leaks in `dashboard/src`.
