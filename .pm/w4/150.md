# Keep the Russian public profile inside a 320px viewport

Severity: minor responsive-layout defect. Estimate: 30m, dashboard. A2: readers
on the narrowest phones should not have to scroll a profile sideways to reach
its actions.

Environment: local build of current `main` at `e1092bff`, served through the QA
SSR server, anonymous public `open_ledger` profile, isolated headless
Chromium 149.0.7827.55, English/light, 320×800. Found while verifying `w4/111`.

Repro: open `/ledger/open_ledger?lang=ru` at 320×800 and wait for the profile
to settle. `document.documentElement.scrollWidth` is 363 against a 320px
viewport, so the whole page scrolls horizontally. Enumerating the elements that
extend past the viewport attributes it to one control: the button labelled
`Копировать ссылку` ("Copy link"), whose right edge is at x363. English
(`Kopieren`-free "Copy link"), German and Japanese all report
`scrollWidth` 320 at the same size, so this is the Russian label's width, not a
general layout fault.

Not the tab strip: the social tabs deliberately overflow their own scroll
container — `["Книги", "Избранное (0)", "Подписки (1)", "Подписчики (162)"]`
reaches x542 — and keyboard End/Home still scroll the focused tab into view.
That container is working as designed and is not part of this finding.

Pre-existing: measured identically with the twelve `w4/111` catalogs reverted,
so the social-tab translations did not cause it. The button's own Russian label
predates that work.

Fix in the profile header's action row: let the copy-link control shrink, wrap
or shorten at the narrowest widths rather than setting the page's minimum
width. Preserve the control's accessible name, its copy behaviour and its
feedback, the adjacent Follow action, and the tab strip's own horizontal
scrolling. Do not truncate the label to an unreadable fragment, remove the
control, or apply a global button change.

Blast radius: the public profile header; other locales with long action labels
may benefit from the same repair but were not measured as failing. Only Russian
at 320px was reproduced.

Dedupe: `.pm` searched including `done/` and `blocked/` for profile overflow,
copy link and narrow-width buttons. `w4/111` owns the social-tab catalog
defaults and is closed; `w4/084` and `w4/087` concern a ledger header row and
the commodities grid, different components. No matching open item.

Acceptance: a Russian profile at 320px keeps `document.scrollWidth` at 320 with
the copy-link control fully reachable and its label readable; English, German
and Japanese stay unchanged; the tab strip still scrolls horizontally and
End/Home still work. Run the dashboard gates.

Evidence: measured live at `e1092bff` during the `w4/111` verification, with
the per-element overflow attribution above, and re-measured with that change
reverted. No product change was made for this note.
