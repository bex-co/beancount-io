# Extends w3/217: on a billion-scale ledger the Accounts balance itself is truncated, not just the name

**This is not a new finding — it revises the severity and acceptance of the open `w3/217.md`** ("Keep Accounts row identities readable beside large balances"). Same file, same lines, same cause. Read them together; fix once.

**Severity: raise `w3/217` from minor to major.** Its evidence came from `open_ledger/airbnb`, where balances are ~11 characters and survived intact, so it reads as a name-legibility problem. On a ledger with longer amounts the *balance* is the casualty, and a truncated balance is wrong financial data rather than an inconvenience.

Environment: branch `main`, HEAD `038faeeb`, native development build `io.beancount.ios` on iPhone 17e / iOS 26.5, English / System / light, authenticated production session at `https://beancount.io`, public `open_ledger/alphabet`. Report-only QA; no ledger mutation. Content size restored to `large` afterwards and verified.

## What is different from w3/217's evidence

`w3/217` step 2 records "Their full balances remain visible: $28,754,000,000 and -$20,955,000,000." On `open_ledger/alphabet`, whose balances run to 19 characters, that does not hold.

**At `accessibility-medium` (AX1 — the first size past `common/theme/dynamic-type.ts`'s 1.3 threshold):**

| Row | Shown | Actual |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Assets | `$921,983,000.6` | `$921,983,000,000.00` |
| Income | `-$1,743,597,000.` | `-$1,743,597,000,000.00` |
| Expenses | `$1,202,771,000.` | `$1,202,771,000,000.00` |

Every amount is cut mid-number at the screen edge with **no ellipsis**, so `$921,983,000.6` reads as ~$922 million — three orders of magnitude under the real ~$922 billion. Account names are readable at this size but their glyph bottoms are clipped by the row height ("Assets", "NonCurrent", "Liabilities" all lose their lower edge).

**At `accessibility-extra-extra-extra-large` (max):** the amounts are still truncated the same way, and the name column collapses past `w3/217`'s `To…` to a few pixels of ink — `Assets`, `Liabilities`, `Equity`, `Income`, `Expenses` are effectively invisible, as is the `Account | Balance` header row.

**Working control:** restore `content_size large` and every row shows its full name and its full `$921,983,000,000.00`-scale balance. Verified in the same session.

## Why the same root cause produces a worse symptom here

`account-table.tsx:244-258` lays the row out horizontally: `<Text style={nameStyle} numberOfLines={1}>` with `name: { flex: 1, marginEnd: 12 }` (lines 108-113), then an `<AmountText>` with **no `numberOfLines` and no shrink constraint**. `AmountText` scales up to `amountMaxFontSizeMultiplier = 1.4` (`common/theme/typography.ts:94`).

As the unshrinkable sibling, the amount claims its full intrinsic width first. With an 11-character amount that still leaves room for an ellipsized name — `w3/217`'s symptom. With a 19-character amount at 1.4×, the intrinsic width exceeds the row: the `flex: 1` name is squeezed to nothing **and** the amount overflows and is clipped by the container. `AmountText`'s own doc comment states the intent this violates — "a Dynamic Type cap so amounts — which can't ellipsize without lying — never scale out of their row." The 1.4 cap is not sufficient at this string length.

`31f1e4ce` ("make nine surfaces survive Dynamic Type and the keyboard") fixed this exact shape elsewhere — `w3/183` moved the Reports amount to its own line above the threshold, `w3/181` did the same for merchant rows — but `AccountTable` was not among the nine.

## What to change in w3/217

- Raise its severity to **major** and say why: the failure is a mis-stated balance, not only an unreadable name.
- Its fix contract already says "accommodate full account identity **and full signed balance** at supported widths/text scales" and "do not … truncate balances" — that is still the right instruction and needs no rewrite. The restacking helper and threshold from `31f1e4ce` (`common/theme/dynamic-type.ts`, 1.3) are the precedent to follow, as `w3/183` and `w3/181` did.
- Extend its acceptance: verify at **AX1 as well as maximum**, on a ledger whose balances are ~19 characters (`open_ledger/alphabet`), that no amount is clipped and no glyph is vertically cut by the row height. `w3/217`'s current acceptance names only default and maximum on `airbnb`, which would pass while this still fails.

## Blast radius
