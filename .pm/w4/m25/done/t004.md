## Objective

Walk the repaired screens in two Latin-script locales at both widths, rather
than trusting the catalogs.

## Context

- Spanish and Portuguese carry the most repairs, so they are the pair worth
  walking; German is worth a spot check because its noun capitalization made it
  the hardest case for the predicate.
- `w4/m24/t008` found two things this way that a catalog diff had missed, so
  this is not ceremony.
- Anonymous `open_ledger/example` reaches the journal, account report,
  statistics, documents, events, errors, trial balance, overview and ledger
  editor. It does not reach the dashboard, gallery, user settings or the
  new-directive dialog — say so rather than implying otherwise.

## Steps

1. Open the reachable screens with `?lang=es` and `?lang=pt` at 1440x900 and 390x900.
2. Confirm the account report cards and the statistics headings read as the target language.
3. Spot-check `?lang=de` for the dashboard strings t002 repaired.
4. Record what could not be reached.

## Files

- No source changes expected; this task reports.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The repaired screens show no English label in either locale at either width.
- [ ] Screens that could not be reached are named in the close record.
- [ ] Any out-of-scope defect found is filed rather than folded in.

## Out of scope

- Fixing defects in catalogs this milestone does not own.

## Result (2026-09-20)

Walked with `?lang=es`, `?lang=pt` and `?lang=de` at 1440x900 and 390x900.

| screen | locale | evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account report | es | cards `Saldo de la cuenta` / `Diario de la cuenta` (were `Cuenta Balance` / `Cuenta Journal`); headers `Período, Importe, Fecha, F, Beneficiario/Narración, Unidades, Cambio, Saldo, Asientos`; 10 rows |
| Account report | pt | cards `Saldo da conta` / `Diário da conta`; headers `Período, Valor, Data, F, Beneficiário/Descrição, Unidades, Mudança, Saldo, Lançamentos`; 10 rows |
| Statistics | es | `Últimas entradas de la cuenta` (was `Cuenta Last Entries`); 75 rows |
| Journal | es | subtype filters `Transacciones confirmadas`, `Transacciones pendientes`, `Otras transacciones` — the first two were `Limpiared transactions` and are the glued shape t001's predicate cannot see; 36 rows at 390px |
| Journal | de | `Abgeschlossene Transaktionen`, `Ausstehende Transaktionen`, `Sonstige Transaktionen`; headers `Datum, F, Zahlungsempfänger/Beschreibung, Buchungen`; 36 rows at 390px |

`lang` is correct on the document throughout and rows load on every screen.

One thing worth recording so it is not mistaken for a defect later: scanning
the Spanish statistics page for English words returns `Balance General`,
`Balance de Comprobación` and `Balance`. Those are Spanish — the balance sheet
and the trial balance — not leftovers. A naive English-word scan over rendered
Spanish will always produce them.

**Not reachable anonymously**, so repaired at the catalog level only: the
ledger dashboard, where the `Hauptbuch löschen` / `Eliminar libro` / `Excluir
livro` labels live; the gallery; user settings; and the sign-in and sign-up
screens carrying `auth.usernameRequired` and `auth.backToSignIn`. All need a
signed-in session or a form submission the public example ledger does not
offer.

No out-of-scope defect was found during the walk.
