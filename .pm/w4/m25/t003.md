## Objective

Work the remainder of t001's hit list — the Latin-script damage that does not
share a key with a repair already shipped elsewhere.

## Context

- t002 clears the keys with known-good precedents. What is left is whatever
  t001's predicate finds on its own, and its list belongs in that task.
- Candidates seen during triage, to be confirmed against the tuned predicate
  rather than taken on trust: `page.commodities.failedToLoadCommodities` (pt,
  `Falha ao Carregar Commodities`), `page.dashboard.feedError`, and the
  `auth.backToSignIn` shape in nl (`Terug to Sign in`).
- Some hits will be false positives that t001's tuning did not remove. Record
  them as such rather than translating a string that was already correct — a
  loanword rewritten into a calque is a regression, not a fix.

## Steps

1. Take t001's recorded hit list, minus everything t002 repaired.
2. For each, compare against `en[key]` and the same key in a locale where it reads correctly.
3. Translate the genuine ones; for a false positive, extend the predicate's allowlist and say which term and why.
4. Run the gates from `dashboard/`.
