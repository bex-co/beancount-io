# Repair the Ukrainian catalogs' first-word find/replace damage

Severity: major localization defect, widest of the three found so far.
Estimate: 3-4h, dashboard — milestone-sized, promote rather than work in place.
A2/A3: Ukrainian is currently the worst-rendering locale in the product.

Found while closing `w4/149` (the Russian Journal catalogs). The same mechanical
damage the Catalan audit (`w4/m14`) repaired is present in Ukrainian, at roughly
four times the Russian scale.

Two shapes, both from a translation pass that replaced a leading token and left
the rest of the string alone:

- **First word replaced.** `auth.emailRequired` = `Електронна пошта is required`,
  `page.accountReport.accountJournal` = `Рахунок Journal`,
  `userSettings.accountDeleted` = `Рахунок deleted successfully`.
- **First *letter* replaced**, which is worse because the result is not a word in
  either language: `page.documents.documents` = `Вocuments`,
  `page.statistics.loadingEntryStatistics` = `Пoading entry statistics...`,
  `journal.balancesAfterEntry` = `Балансs after entry`,
  `ledgerEditor.toggleComment` = `Комуggle Comment`,
  `page.dashboard.deleting` = `Вeleting...`.

Scan: 116 Ukrainian messages across 22 catalogs mix Cyrillic with stray English.
Worst concentrations are `user-settings` (23), `auth` (19), `journal` (17),
`ledger-list/pages/dashboard-page` (13) and `reports/account` (7). Subtract a
handful of false positives — `beancount`, `fava-option` and `cash-flow-role` are
directive and metadata keywords that belong in the string — so the real count is
ca 110.

Reproduce: `dashboard/src/features/journal/components/__tests__/russian-journal-labels.test.tsx`
carries the scan as a guard for the two Russian catalogs it covers. Point the
same predicate at `locales/uk.ts` to get the list; it needs the allowlist of
brand names, directive keywords, currency codes and literal account names that
guard already defines.

Suggested shape: promote to a milestone, one task per catalog cluster, reusing
the Catalan audit's rules — flag keys stay literal one- or two-character
Beancount characters, account-name examples and currency codes stay as
notation, prose gets translated. Extend the `russian-journal-labels` guard to
Ukrainian, or generalise it to every locale with an allowlist, so the class
cannot return.

Not in scope here: the eight remaining Russian catalogs, filed as `w4/153`.
