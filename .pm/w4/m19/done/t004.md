## Outcome

Reviewed the m19 diff across reuse, simplification, efficiency and altitude.
The diff is 37 lines in one mechanism, so this ran as one focused review rather
than a four-way fan-out — recorded here rather than implied.

The reuse finding was the valuable one, and it removed the code t001 added:
**router-core already exposes the exact string I hand-rolled.**
`ParsedLocation.href` is composed at `router.js:231` as
`pathname + searchStr + hash` from the **raw** hash, and the exposed `hash`
field is `hash.slice(1)` on the very next line. So `href` is built before the
strip that caused the defect. All three producers already hold a full
`ParsedLocation`.

So `toRelativeLocation` and its ten-line explanation of router-core's parser
are gone, and `getSafeReturnPath(location)` is now
`getSafeRedirectPath(location.href)`. Two checks before taking it rather than
after: `dashboard/src/router.tsx` configures no `rewrite`, so `href` and
`publicHref` agree (and for an internal redirect `href` is the field we want
anyway); and `href` is still percent-encoded where the parsed fields are
decoded — which is the safer input, since `getSafeRedirectPath` exists to
reject destinations that are unsafe at any decoding layer, and feeding it a
pre-decoded path would do one of those decodes outside the validator.

Net effect on the milestone's own diff: **54 lines removed against 31 added.**
The three producers now ask one question, there is no second URL serializer in
the repo, and `getSafeRedirectPath` — the security policy — is untouched.

Also applied: `RequireAuthLocation` narrowed to the one field it needs, and the
two test fixtures that hand-built `{pathname, searchStr, hash}` now carry
`href`, since the router never produces the shape they were asserting.

Verified equivalent, not assumed: the live checks return byte-identical results
to the hand-rolled version — 12/12 Star/Follow continuations and 6/6 guard
checks at 1440 and 390.

One finding was verified but left out of scope, and is filed as a follow-up
note rather than silently fixed: three further places build a login `next` from
only `pathname` (or `pathname + search`) with no validator —
`common/components/ledger-layout/ledger-route-error.tsx`,
`common/root-route/error-page.tsx` and
`common/apollo/links/auth-error-link.ts`. The first is the error component of
the very route t002 fixed, so that route has two producers that disagree about
how much of the destination to keep. FINDINGS scoped this milestone to three
producers, so these are not folded in here.

files, 4253 passed, 1 pre-existing skip) and `yarn build` all pass.
