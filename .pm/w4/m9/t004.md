## Objective

Make the two skills that depend on valuations use the new verbs: `beancount-close` fetches period-end prices as a confirm-gated step before it builds the balance sheet, and `beancount-ask` states how fresh the prices behind a market-value answer are and names `bea price fetch` as the next step instead of estimating.

- Skills must fall back cleanly when `bea` or the extra is missing: report stale prices, never fabricate.
- `python3 skills/scripts/ci-check.py` validates frontmatter, evals, and fixture paths.
