# Mobile valuation toggle: cost, market value, or units per ledger

After [w4/m11](./blocked/m11/README.md). The dashboard lets each ledger's reports switch between cost, market value, and units, and remembers the choice per ledger (`dashboard/src/features/reports/components/use-report-conversion.ts`, shipped with `w4/done/003`). Mobile still hard-codes `BALANCE_CONVERSION = "at_cost"` in `mobile/src/common/balance-util.ts`, so a mobile user who wants market value has no way to see it.

Offer the same three choices per ledger on Accounts, Home, and Reports. Market value must name the newest price date behind it and flag stale prices: the Beancount example ledger's prices stop in 2017, so a market-value total there would quote nine-year-old marks. Holdings with no price stay unconverted and named, never zero, as m11 establishes.

Likely more than an hour (selection state, three screens, stale-price disclosure, 13 locales); size it before `/pm promote`. Source: `/pm-brainstorm for w4` 2026-09-15 item 5; option 3 of [w4/027](./027.md); issue 176 ask 2 (one display basis across the app). A2.
