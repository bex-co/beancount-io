## Objective

Make the complete guest journey readable and recoverable in every shipped mobile language and in both themes.

## Context

- Mobile uses `useTranslations()` with 13 locales and locale-aware plural forms.
- Loading content uses layout-matched `LoadingTile` skeletons; shared headers own their geometry.
- Public examples can disappear, become private, or fail to load after selection.

## Steps

1. Complete the example action, chooser, guest context, authentication continuation, and recovery copy across all 13 locales.
2. Use theme tokens, labeled controls, and layout-matched first-load skeletons. Keep existing content visible during pull-to-refresh.
3. Verify the native Welcome-to-report flow in light/dark, ordinary/enlarged text, and representative longer translations; record time and taps to the first useful report.
4. Check unavailable, denied, and connection-failure states with controlled fixtures. If shared header geometry changed, run its contract tests and inspect all five tabs, absent/disabled actions, enlarged text, and both themes.

## Files

- `mobile/src/translations/`
- Guest and Welcome screens, plus the affected Home, Accounts, and Reports states.
- `mobile/src/components/` only for shared integrations required by the guest flow.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Every added string exists in all 13 locales and locale-integrity checks pass.
- [x] Light/dark and enlarged-text native checks show readable actions, labels, and errors without clipped controls.
- [x] Missing, denied, and unreachable examples offer recovery and never silently select another server.
- [x] A recorded signed-out launch-to-report walkthrough reports observed time and taps without adding analytics.

## Out of scope

Store publication, a redesign of shared navigation, event tracking, and production ledger writes.

## Completion evidence — 2026-09-27

New copy is present in all 13 locales and translation checks pass. Native checks covered light and dark, enlarged English controls, and German Reports at accessibility-medium. Welcome actions grow without overlapping the artwork, guest actions and tabs grow with text, and recovery content scrolls. Selector skeletons reserve the translated caption layout. Controlled Apollo fixtures cover FORBIDDEN, UNAUTHENTICATED, NOT_FOUND and connection failure; a native connection-failure screen and recovery were also inspected. The three-tap native walkthrough is recorded in the milestone README.
