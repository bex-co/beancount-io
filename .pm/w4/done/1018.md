# Keep negative chart axis ticks readable in Persian

**Severity:** minor RTL financial display. **Estimate:** 30–45 minutes. **Owner:** dashboard (shared `common/components/react-echarts` container). **A2:** a Persian reader of a chart with negative values must see `-300.0`, not `300.0-`. This is a sibling of [1005](./1005.md), seen while verifying it. The period tables are fixed there; this concerns the canvas chart itself.

## Observation

Local current-source dashboard (`c26b2f12d`) with the fixture API and the genuine recorded `open_ledger/example` `Assets:US:BofA:Checking` 2016-08 Account read. Its Changes Over Time has a genuine **-1948.75 USD** interval. At `lang=fa` the document is `dir=rtl`. In fresh 1440×1000 and 390×844 contexts, the Changes Over Time bar chart's y-axis reads **0.0, 300.0-, 0.6k-, 0.9k-, 1.2k-, 1.5k-, 1.8k-, 2.1k-**: every negative tick shows its minus after the number. The period table beneath it reads `-1948.75 USD` after 1005. Screenshots: `dashboard/tmp/1005/fa-{1440,390}-view1.png` (ignored, visually inspected). English renders `-300.0` as before. Other charts with negative values (Net Worth with liabilities, Income Statement, Cash Flow) were not checked here.

## Likely cause and bounded repair

ECharts draws axis labels on a canvas, and canvas text takes its `direction` from the canvas element's computed style, which inherits `rtl` from the page. A weak leading minus then moves to the end. The shared chart container (`react-echarts/client.tsx`) renders a bare `<div>` without a direction of its own. Give the chart drawing surface a left-to-right direction (for example `dir="ltr"` on that container), or set it through ECharts' own text options, so number labels keep their order. The surrounding card, legend semantics and page direction stay RTL. Verify axis labels, tooltips and legends across the chart kinds at both widths in fa and en. Keep localized digits and labels; no formatter rewrite.

Acceptance: a negative y-axis tick in a Persian chart reads sign → number → unit/suffix at both widths, English and positive-only charts are unchanged, and tooltip/legend text stays readable. Include a rendered regression on the container direction and a native check.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-22** — The shared `ReactEChartsClient` container is now `dir="ltr"`, so every chart's canvas (axis ticks, legend) and the ECharts tooltip DOM inside it lay out left to right. The surrounding card, its title and the page keep the page's direction; localized digits and labels are unchanged, and the server placeholder is untouched because the client renders the chart only after mount. The native check ran on a local fixture app with recorded reads, in Persian at 1440×1000 and 390×844 and in English at 1440. On Account Changes Over Time, Income Statement and Overview, the canvas computed `direction: ltr` inside the `dir=rtl` document. The Persian axes read **-300.0, -0.6k … -2.1k** (Account) and **-1.0k … -6.0k** (Income Statement), and tooltips read "USD: −۱٬۹۴۸٫۷۵" with the sign first; both screenshots were visually inspected. English rendering is unchanged, with no mutations and no page errors. Regression: `client-lifecycle.test.tsx` asserts the drawing surface is LTR inside an RTL ancestor; it fails with the fix reverted. Shipped in `e97f85e02` (`dashboard`).
