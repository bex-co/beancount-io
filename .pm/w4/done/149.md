# Finish the Russian Journal-surface catalog entries

Severity: minor localization defect. Estimate: 40m, dashboard. A2/A3: Russian
readers of the Journal and Account Journal should not meet English labels
beside translated ones.

Environment: local build of current `main` at `702b5182`, served through the QA
SSR server, anonymous public `open_ledger/example`, isolated headless
Chromium 149.0.7827.55, English/light, 1440×900 and 390×900. Found while
verifying `w4/100`, which repaired the one column header that note owned.

Repro: open `/ledger/open_ledger/example/account/Assets%3AUS%3ABofA%3AChecking?time=2016-02&lang=ru`
and read the table headers. They come back as
`[Дата, F, Получатель/Описание, Единицы, Изменение, Balance, Проводки]` — every
heading is Russian except `Balance`. The same URL with `lang=uk` returns
`Баланс`, so this is catalog content rather than a fallback or a shared
technical term. Open the new-entry form on the Journal in Russian: the payee
field's placeholder reads `Payee`, while Ukrainian shows `Отримувач`.

Root: `dashboard/src/features/journal/locales/ru.ts` keeps the English source
for `journal.balance` ("Balance") and `journal.payeePlaceholder` ("Payee"),
while its neighbours — `journal.narration` ("Описание"), `journal.change`
("Изменение") — are translated, and the corresponding `uk.ts` entries are
translated. Both values date to `af5339de`. `w4/100` corrected
`journal.payeeNarration` in the same file and deliberately left these, since it
was scoped to the one combined column header.

Scope: the Journal feature's Russian catalog only. Sweep
`features/journal/locales/ru.ts` for entries whose message is still byte-identical
to `en.ts` and translate the ones that are prose rather than canonical notation,
keeping the flag labels (`*`, `!`, `x`), the `F` column, account-name examples
and any Beancount directive keywords as they are. Preserve every key, the
`{placeholder}` interpolations, table semantics and layout.

Context, not scope: counting entries byte-identical to English across all
fifteen catalogs gives ca 268, nl 229, ru 220, pt 217, sk 215, uk 213, fa 211,
bg 210, fr 160, de 140, es 128, zh 72, ja 42, ko 42, out of 1275 English keys.
Many of those are legitimately identical — brand names, notation, single
symbols — so the totals are a rough signal, not a defect count, and a
catalog-wide audit is a separate, larger piece of work. `w4/m14` already owns
the Catalan audit. This note is the bounded Russian Journal follow-up.

Acceptance: the Russian Journal and Account Journal show no English heading,
placeholder or button label that has a Russian equivalent elsewhere in the same
catalog, at 1440px and 390px, with rows still loading and `lang="ru"` on the
document. Ukrainian and English are unchanged. Run the dashboard gates,
including the translation suites. Native-speaker review of the wording is
welcome; no particular phrasing is mandated.

Evidence: reproduced live at `702b5182` during the `w4/100` verification run,
which recorded the full header arrays for both JournalTable consumers in ru, uk
and en at both widths. No product change was made for this note.

---

**Resolved 2026-09-20** — translated the 15 English-identical prose entries in
`features/journal/locales/ru.ts` and repaired 11 half-translated ones there
(`Запись Context`, `Очиститьed transactions`, `Экспортing...`, `Прочее
transactions`, …), plus the four in `features/reports/account/locales/ru.ts`
that title the Account Journal card (`Счёт Journal` → `Журнал счёта`). Kept the
seven literal flag keys, the `Assets:Bank:Checking` example, `USD`, and the
`Beancount`/`pad` keywords as notation. Added
`features/journal/components/__tests__/russian-journal-labels.test.tsx`, which
fails 18 of 26 assertions when the catalogs are reverted. Shipped in `5bf51bb3`
(`dashboard`).

Two deviations from the note as written, both recorded rather than silent:

- The note scoped the sweep to `features/journal/locales/ru.ts`, but its
  acceptance covers the Account Journal surface, whose card headings live in
  the Account Report catalog. Those four entries were repaired too.
- The note's sweep method — entries byte-identical to `en.ts` — does not find
  half-translated entries, which are the larger defect on these surfaces. The
  audit was widened to any Russian message carrying a stray English word.

Verified live at 1440×900 and 390×900 on the public example ledger with rows
loaded and `lang="ru"`. The new-entry form needs write access, which the
anonymous example ledger does not grant, so the payee placeholder is covered by
the guard rather than by the live pass. Ukrainian and English are unchanged.

Out of scope, filed separately: the same first-word find/replace damage in the
Ukrainian catalogs (`w4/152`) and in eight other Russian catalogs (`w4/153`).
