# Do not summarize a costed investment purchase or loss sale as its expenses

Severity: **minor** — misleading Overview activity amount; source postings and account-filtered amounts are correct. Estimate: **45–60 minutes**, dashboard only. A2 — readers exploring the public investment example should not read a commission or realized-loss subtotal as the transaction amount.

## Reproduce

Production `https://beancount.io`, 2026-10-08 America/Los_Angeles, English/System, QA-owned isolated headless Chromium. Fresh 1440×1000 and 390×1000 repeats. Source rechecked after pulling main `c7038a0c`; production module `main-GcCTaQbi.js`, deployed commit unknown. Same-origin `/api-gateway/` controls; authenticated public-ledger reader, no writes.

1. Open `/ledger/open_ledger/stock-example?time=2025-09` and wait for Recent activity.
2. The single **Buy 100 ACME @ $84.60** row shows **-4.95 USD** as its prominent amount at both widths. At narrow width even the Commissions account subtitle is clipped, while the purchase narration and amount remain visible.
3. Add `&account=Assets%3ABrokerage%3ACash`. The same purchase correctly shows **-8,464.95 USD**.
4. Open `/ledger/open_ledger/stock-example?time=2026-05`. **Sell 100 NWRB post-split shares — long-term loss** shows **-139.95 USD**, although its cash postings produce a positive receipt.

Expected: without an account filter, costed commodity trades with incidental expense postings use the existing **Multiple postings** fallback instead of an unlabeled expense-only scalar. An explicit account filter continues to show that account's exact movements. Do not silently choose cash as the unfiltered transaction amount or replace one unexplained subtotal with another.

## Cause and bounded repair

`dashboard/src/features/reports/overview/lib/transaction-summary.ts:118–134` guards only entries that contain both income and expenses. The purchase and loss-making disposal bypass it. Lines 137–145 then negate and total the expense postings alone, returning `kind: "expense"`. RecentActivityCard renders that amount without a commission/loss basis label. Its sole application helper call is in `overview/components/recent-activity-card.tsx`; the overview and `/overview` redirect alias share it.

Extend the mixed-summary decision to the reproduced cost-bearing commodity acquisition/disposal plus expense case before the simple expense branch. Keep account-filter selection first and lossless, and keep ordinary expense, income and simple transfer behavior. Use the real cost-bearing posting shapes in regressions, not the old gain-sale test's cost-free approximation. Assert the rendered narrow card says Multiple postings rather than -4.95/-139.95; cover the purchase, loss disposal, cash-filter control, ordinary fee-only expense, and existing payroll/gain-sale controls. Run dashboard gates when implementing. No producer, REST/GraphQL/MCP contract, financial totals, dependency or cross-package changes are required.
