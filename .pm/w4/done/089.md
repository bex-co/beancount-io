**Resolved 2026-09-19** — Reproduced at 320px against a local build of the
public circle ledger: the `Assets:NonCurrent:RestrictedCash` name was left
25.22px of the 254px row and wrapped to 20 lines, making the name container
408px tall and the row 424px, with the status and balance stranded halfway down
it. German repeated it at 23.86px with `Offen` and `12.806.000,00 USD`.

Two causes, both in
`dashboard/src/features/reports/cash-flow/cash-account-status-panel.tsx`:

1. All three fields sat in one fixed horizontal flex row, so the badge and the
   `whitespace-nowrap` amount claimed their width first and the account got the
   remainder. Below `sm` the account now takes a line of its own and the status
   and balance share the next. From `sm` the header and every row are subgrids
   of one outer grid, so the column headers sit over the data they name — the
   old header was an independent flex row that sized itself from its own
   labels and never lined up with the columns.
2. An account name is a colon-separated path with no break opportunity a line
   breaker will use, which is why it wrapped one character at a time. The panel
   now offers a break after each separator, so the name wraps into its own
   segments, with `break-words` as the fallback for a single segment too long
   for the line. The name itself is unchanged — nothing is truncated or
   abbreviated.

`StatementAmounts` gained an optional `className` so the panel can place it in
the grid; its formatter, the classification, the closed-account toggle and the
invalid-role notice are all untouched.

After the fix, at 320px the account has the full 254px and wraps to two lines
at its separators, and the row is 92px with the status and unabridged balance
on the line below; 390px is one line and 68px; desktop is unchanged at 40px.
Verified in a real browser at 320 (en and de), 390 and 1440: 29/29 checks pass.
Three regression cases were added for the preserved name with its break
opportunities, a three-currency balance rendered in full, and the closed-account
status; the first fails without the change. The ship also stops four `Link`
test mocks forwarding `activeOptions` to the DOM, which React warned about
after `c92d8e71`. Gates from `dashboard/`: `yarn format:check`, `yarn lint`,
`yarn test` (388 files, 4389 passed, 1 pre-existing skip) and `yarn build` all
pass. Shipped in `643c6474` (`dashboard`).
