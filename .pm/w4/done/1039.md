# Keep exact Budget amounts and variance on mobile

**Minor · 45–60m · mobile · A2/A3.** Mobile counterpart of [m55](./done/m55/README.md), found by the m55 `/simplify` altitude review (2026-10-23). Source-only: not yet reproduced on a simulator.

The dashboard now carries Budget targets, actuals and variance as the ledger's decimals and prorates them exactly (`d6d36668e`). Mobile's Budget still converts the same source strings to doubles in `mobile/src/screens/budget-screen/selectors/budget-selectors.ts`: `Number.parseFloat` at lines 201, 221, 468 and 670. A daily target of 9007199254740993 USD against an actual of 9007199254740992 USD should therefore read as on target there too, and an exactly met monthly target relies on floating-point proration.

Before implementing, reproduce on the iPhone simulator with the m55 fixtures (`budget-exact-unit-{large,above,ordinary}` in the m55 FINDINGS), selecting the period that contains 2025-12-31.

Acceptance:
- Target …993 / actual …992 reads Below target −1.00 USD, and the reverse reads Above target +1.00 USD. The ordinary 100/99 control still reads −1.00.
- Exactly met 28-, 29-, 30- and 31-day monthly targets compare as met without a float tolerance. ISO minor units and commodity digits keep their current display.
- Regressions fail on the old selectors. Mobile gates pass. Mobile is a separate package, so it uses its own decimal code, not a cross-package import from the dashboard.

**Blocked 2026-10-23** — The fix is implemented and verified in source; only the simulator check the note asks for remains, and it needs a production write.

**What shipped (`mobile`, in the commit that moved this note):**
- A mobile-owned exact-decimal module (`src/common/decimal-number.ts`, with no import from the dashboard).
- `budget-selectors.ts` parses directive amounts as decimals and prorates through `budgetForIntervalExact`, which sums run-length day fractions exactly and rounds once at the existing judged scale. The numeric `budgetForInterval` and `calculateBudgetForInterval` now return those values as numbers.
- `groupDirection` reads the sign exactly. `formatBudgetEntryAmount` formats the exact magnitude.
- `varianceStatus` judges an exact variance at the digit the card shows (w21/596's half-unit rule, no float tolerance).
- `resolveCurrencyBalanceExact` reads actuals as the ledger's decimals.
- The actuals hook, `selectBudgetCardStats`, the Budget card, the Home panel rows and their spoken values carry and show `budgetExact`/`actualExact`/`varianceExact`; chart coordinates stay numbers.
- `formatMoneyWithCurrency` and `formatSignedMoneyWithCurrency` accept an exact decimal string.

**Verified:** `budget-exact-amounts.test.ts` covers:
- daily …993 against …992: Below target, `$9,007,199,254,740,993.00`, `$9,007,199,254,740,992.00`, `-$1.00`;
- the reverse: Above target, `+$1.00`;
- the 100/99 control at `-$1.00`;
- the Home row and its spoken value;
- exactly met 28-, 29-, 30- and 31-day monthly targets of 90071992547409.93 USD taking the daily walk: budget `90071992547409.93`, status on, `+$0.00`;
- exact formatting matching the number formatter for USD, JPY, KWD, BTC, MUSD and zero.

The same scenarios through the old selectors (a detached HEAD worktree) read the daily …993/…992 as "on" with `+$0.00` and a budget of `$9,007,199,254,740,992.00`, and the met monthly targets as below/above by −$0.02, +$0.06, +$0.05 and +$0.03. Two source guardrails were updated to the exact calls. Mobile gates pass: format, lint (Knip included), typecheck, and 4687 unit tests.
