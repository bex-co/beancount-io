**Severity:** minor financial correctness. **Estimate:** 25–40 minutes. **Owner:** dashboard. **A2:** a multi-currency history must not invent a monthly gain by subtracting amounts in different units.

## Cause and bounded repair

`dashboard/src/features/reports/overview/components/net-worth-card.tsx:284–299` chooses latestComparable and requests previousComparable in that currency. `lib/overview-utils.ts:169–176` returns the preferred currency **or the sole available amount**, even when that sole amount has a different currency. Thus the latest selection is100000JPY, the previous selection silently falls back to1000USD, and the card subtracts their numbers. At:418–438 it labels the result with the latest currency and applies a green gain indicator.

Require matching currency identities for the monthly subtraction. When no prior comparable currency exists, use the existing unavailable/no-change-comparison presentation rather than an invented mixed-unit gain. Keep the latest available currency fallback and all displayed balances, preserve the valid mixed-previous exact-JPY match, and retain view/bookmark and valuation disclosures. The shared helper also selects sortable magnitudes for Account balances and Money movement; changing its sole-currency fallback globally is not necessary for this pairwise comparison. Do not introduce implicit FX conversion or alter the ledger's valuation rules. No API, dependency or backend behavior change is needed.

Acceptance: a meaningful rendered NetWorthCard regression with the two different sole currencies must show the correct main/table amounts and no **+99,000 JPY** comparison. Same-JPY, same-USD and available-JPY-in-a-mixed-previous-period controls remain correct. Include no-prior-data and normal view/rerender coverage, repeat desktop/narrow, then run dashboard gates when implementing. Other clients, locales, engines and repaired behavior remain unverified.
