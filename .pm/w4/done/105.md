# Preserve full account identities in distribution pie tooltips

Severity: minor ambiguous financial display. Estimate: 30m, dashboard. A2: readers should distinguish exchange/wallet accounts that share the same final name.

Signed-out production Chromium149.0.7827.55, English/light: open `/ledger/open_ledger/crypto-example?lang=en` and hover the two USD slices in Assets Distribution. At1440×1000 the tooltips say only **USD: 38,313.69 (19.71%)** and **USD: 5,675.89 (2.92%)**. A fresh independent390×844 context repeats both through real pointer movement over the canvas. Neither tooltip identifies Coinbase versus Binance. The chart also has three separate BTC slices and two ETH slices with the same short names; this is account composition, not aggregation by currency.

The real GetLedgerOverview hierarchy identifies the USD balances as Assets:Crypto:Coinbase:USD38,313.69 and Assets:Crypto:Binance:USD5,675.89. The mounted ECharts option retains only name=USD plus value for each slice. These are correct distinct amounts; no lost row or calculation error is claimed here. Full account identity already exists in the distribution input.

Root: `dashboard/src/features/reports/overview/components/distribution-chart/distribution-pie.tsx` maps each item to `{name: d.label, value: d.value}` and its tooltip at28–37 prints only p.name. Both behaviors originate at af5339de. `buildDistributionData` already supplies full name and short label separately. The sibling DistributionBar preserves d.name in series data while keeping d.label on the axis; that is a source-level passing contrast, not a separate live bar reproduction. Local main26d54a9d, fetched origin358ba4dc differs only in board records; production main-BIr0jJni.js, exact deployed SHA unverified.

Retain full account identity in pie data and expose it through the tooltip, while keeping compact slice labels where needed. Keep the localized Other aggregate meaningful and preserve number formatting. Do not expand every narrow slice label into an overflowing full path merely to repair the tooltip.

Acceptance: the two USD tooltips identify their Coinbase/Binance accounts and keep the same values; BTC/ETH duplicates are likewise distinguishable. A single-account pie and Other grouping remain correct. A meaningful rendered option/formatter test should fail when full account identity is discarded; verify actual narrow/desktop pointer tooltips and native dashboard gates. Keyboard chart access, physical touch and other engines remain unverified.

This issue is independently reproducible with entirely USD-denominated slice amounts and does not depend on m21's mixed-unit case.

Evidence: `dashboard/tmp/qa-20260917/crypto-pie-identities.json` records actual chart data, real account identities and both viewport tooltip texts. `crypto-duplicate-pie-1440.png` was inspected and shows repeated USD/BTC/ETH labels. **Resolved 2026-09-19** — Reproduced against a local build of the public
crypto-example by sweeping the pointer around the Assets Distribution ring: the
two USD slices produced "USD: 38,313.69 (19.71%)" and "USD: 5,675.89 (2.92%)"
with nothing to tell Coinbase from Binance, alongside three identically named
BTC slices and two ETH slices, at 1440px and 390px.

`distribution-pie.tsx` mapped each item to `{ name: d.label, value: d.value }`
and printed only `p.name`, discarding the full account that
`buildDistributionData` already supplies — the same identity the sibling
`DistributionBar` keeps in its series data. The pie's data now carries the
account beside the compact label and the tooltip prefers it. Slice labels still
draw the short name through `{b}`, so narrow slices are not expanded into
overflowing full paths. Sums, order, percentages, the 1% grouping threshold,
the localized Other bucket, number formatting and navigation are unchanged, and
nothing here touches `m21`'s unit-aware work.

After the fix all sixteen slices name their account at both widths with
identical values and percentages: `Assets:Crypto:Coinbase:USD: 38,313.69
(19.71%)`, `Assets:Crypto:Binance:USD: 5,675.89 (2.92%)`, the three BTC
accounts (`Mining`, `Coinbase`, `Wallet:Ledger`) and the two ETH accounts
(`Staking:Ethereum`, `Wallet:MetaMask`) each distinct, and `Other: 2,001.16
(1.03%)` still meaningful.

Four rendered-option cases in
`components/__tests__/distribution-pie-identity.test.tsx` cover two slices
sharing a leaf name, the compact slice labels and their `{b}: {d}%` formatter,
the Other bucket, and a single-slice pie; the two identity cases fail when the
account is discarded while the label and Other controls keep passing. Gates
from `dashboard/`: `yarn format:check`, `yarn lint`, `yarn test` (396 files,
4440 passed, 1 pre-existing skip) and `yarn build` all pass. Keyboard chart
access, physical touch and other engines remain unverified. Shipped in
`4d9d679a` (`dashboard`).
