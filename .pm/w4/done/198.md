# Reject non-conserving Cash Flow valuation before inventing opening cash

Severity: **major**, incorrect cash balances and movements in the dashboard and exported statement. Estimate: **45–60 minutes for a bounded reconciliation guard**; promote a fuller valuation/reconciliation redesign separately if needed. A2 — readers must be able to trust reported cash movement independently of security valuation.

## Reproduce

Production `https://beancount.io`, 2026-10-08 America/Los_Angeles. QA-owned isolated headless Chromium, English, System theme, authenticated reader of the public stock example; no ledger/account mutations. Source rechecked after pulling main `9479d594`; deployed module previously observed `main-GcCTaQbi.js`, deployed commit unknown.

1. Fresh-load `/ledger/open_ledger/stock-example/cash-flow?time=2026-05-12` at 1440×1000 and wait for settled statement tables.
2. Select **At Cost**: opening CCE **27,390.30 USD**, net change **905.05 USD**, closing CCE **28,295.35 USD**.
3. Select **At Market Value** and wait for its own read: opening CCE changes to **27,450.30 USD**, net change to **845.05 USD**, while closing remains **28,295.35 USD**. Investing changes from **1,045** to **985 USD**; Operating remains **-139.95 USD**.
4. Fresh-load and repeat at 390×1000: identical settled figures. Export → Markdown report under At Market Value repeats opening **27,450.30**, change **845.05**, closing **28,295.35** in its statement summary for May 12–May 12.

Expected: valuing NWRB differently cannot change this day's recorded USD cash movement or opening USD cash balance. Independently verify CCE movement before presenting a reconciled statement. If the selected valuation cannot support a cash statement, show an actionable unsupported-basis/reconciliation state and prevent misleading exports; do not silently solve opening cash from the activity subtotal.

## Independent control and cause

Browser GraphQL `getLedgerAccountJournal`, account `Assets:Brokerage:Cash`, time `2026-05-12`, conversion `at_value`, Transaction directives, returns HTTP 200 with two rows: the May 12 sale changes USD by **+905.05**, closing brokerage cash **18,295.35**; its generated May 11 opening row is **17,390.30 USD**. The transaction contains cash **+910.00 USD** and **-4.95 USD**, disposes of **100 NWRB** at cost **10.45 USD**, and books **135 USD** capital loss. Bank Checking remains **10,000 USD** in the statement's CCE set. Actual opening CCE is therefore **27,390.30 USD**. The 60 USD discrepancy comes from security conversion, not a cash posting, date boundary, currency conversion of USD cash, or classification choice.

`dashboard/src/features/reports/cash-flow/lib/model.ts:95–99` assumes all converted account changes still sum to zero. Lines 300–319 skip CCE accounts and invert independently converted non-cash account changes into activity amounts. Lines 368–372 sum those activities as `netChange`, then manufacture `opening = closing - netChange`. Market-converted security changes violate that conservation assumption; the model never checks actual CCE movement. `cash-flow/index.tsx:110–124` supplies the merged per-account interval maps and closing CCE snapshots. `features/reports/export/model.ts:415–426` copies the resulting opening/change into exports.

Bounded repair: verify the per-unit conservation/CCE movement precondition before producing this model, propagate a truthful failure state through the report, and disable export of unsupported figures. Preserve At Cost's working control, exact decimals, account-role precedence, filtering, pending-read withholding and valid cash-only market reports. Do not globally weaken the tests or remove the discrepancy by adding an unexplained balancing amount. If choosing to support non-conserving valuation instead, derive cash movement/boundary balances independently and explicitly account for valuation differences; size that larger work through pm.

Acceptance: the fresh desktop/narrow reproduction never presents or exports 845.05 USD as actual cash change or 27,450.30 USD as opening cash. It either reports the independently verified cash figures with an honest reconciliation or explains why this basis is unsupported. A regression must use converted maps that fail conservation alongside actual CCE changes; a fixture balanced by construction misses the bug. Retain At Cost and ordinary cash-transfer controls; run normal dashboard gates when implementing.
