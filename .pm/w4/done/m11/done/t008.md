## Objective

Add tests that fail if the display rule regresses: a non-empty commodity reading as zero, a total silently dropping a commodity, a converted total losing its basis label, or an operating-currency total changing.

## Context

- Tests must import the real selector and components. `w4/done/026` found test files that never import the module they name; do not add another.
- Fixtures can be synthetic and follow the shapes in [w4/016](../blocked/016.md).

## Steps

1. Cover the selector with: a commodity held at cost, a multi-lot holding at different costs, a commodity with no cost currency, an empty account, and a USD-only control.
2. Cover parent and root disclosure, and the Net Worth exclusion, including the no-exclusion case.
3. Cover the basis label in every locale and the operating-currency totals staying equal to the pre-milestone computation.

## Files

- `mobile/src/common/__tests__/`, `mobile/src/screens/account-detail-screen/selectors/__tests__/`, `mobile/src/screens/home-screen/selectors/__tests__/`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each listed case has a test that fails when the corresponding behavior is broken on purpose.
- [ ] `yarn test:unit` passes.

## Out of scope

- Snapshot-everything tests.

## Result

The definition-of-done cases are covered as follows:

| case | where it is tested |
| --- | --- |
| commodity held at cost | `balance-display.test.ts` "reads a commodity held at cost in its units" |
| no cost currency | "reads a commodity with no cost in its units, never as a zero", plus the VACHR journal test |
| empty account | "leaves a genuinely empty account an empty money figure" |
| USD-only control | "leaves a plain cash account exactly as it was" |
| multi-lot holding at different costs | new test, `select-account-journal.test.ts` "costs a sale across lots bought at different prices, lot by lot" |

The multi-lot case was the only one missing. The new test sells 3 shares from lots at 80 and 90 USD and expects −250, not the averaged −255. Making the cost keep only the last lot fails it.

Mobile `format:check`, `lint`, `typecheck` and `test:unit` (1940) pass.
