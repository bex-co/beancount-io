**Resolved 2026-09-19** — A new `budgetVariance` in `budget-utils.ts` derives the displayed variance, status and favorability from one comparison that treats the proration's own round-off as zero; `budget-chart-card.tsx` calls it instead of subtracting directly, so the badge, the signed prefix, the colour and the progress-bar favorability all follow the same normalized value. The tolerance is `max(|actual|, |budget|, 1) × Number.EPSILON × 512` — it scales with the amounts being compared and allows one unit in the last place for each day summed (512 bounds the longest period the chart plots), which absorbs the accumulation while staying many orders of magnitude below a fractional unit of any commodity. Nothing is rounded to cents, the daily-equivalent proration and dated budget changes are untouched, and the chart's target series still uses the same helper unchanged. Both cases were reproduced first at HEAD with an explicitly simulated UTC browser clock against the real API, at 1440×1000 and 390×844: at 2026-02-28 the Expenses:Rent card showed budget 1,650 USD, actual 1,650 USD and **Above target +0 USD**, and at 2026-01-31 the same exactly met target showed **Below target −0 USD**. After the change both read **On target 0 USD**, unsigned and neutral, at both viewports. Controls in the same runs are unchanged: January Dining still reads Below target −39.5 USD against a 200 USD target and 160.5 USD actual, Groceries −280.54, Utilities −5.7 (a small but genuine difference, not flattened), and Travel and Insurance:Car still report their whole targets against a zero actual. Expenses:Transport, another exactly met target at 180 USD, also moved from a signed zero to On target. `__tests__/budget-variance.test.ts` adds twelve cases that run the real `calculateBudgetForInterval` and feed its result to the comparison, rather than asserting on hand-written floats: 28-, 29- and 31-day exact matches, an explicit check that neither a signed zero nor a direction is produced, the Dining control, a one-cent overspend, a fractional-commodity difference of 1e-5 ETH in both directions, a missing actual, a zero target, a mid-period target change, an income-direction target, and a 9.5 million target where a cent must still count. Seven of the twelve fail when the tolerance is removed; the five that pass are the "real differences survive" controls. Not verified: other engines, authenticated writes, and the deliberate empty-period "Last year" behavior, which was left alone. Dashboard gates all pass (`yarn format:check`, `yarn lint`, `yarn test` — 413 files, 4639 passed, 1 pre-existing skip — and `yarn build`). Shipped in `c1413c30` (`dashboard`).
