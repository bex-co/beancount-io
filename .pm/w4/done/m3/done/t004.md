## Objective

Make the overview Sankey consume the same role resolution as the cash-flow report: accounts declared `"cash"` are excluded from flow nodes, and declared `operating`/`investing`/`financing` roles are honored for non-`Income` accounts. Sankey vocabulary is unchanged — `Income` stays the source side, `Equity` stays excluded.

- The overview loader needs the same account-meta fetch established in t003.
- Spec: PRFAQ appendix, "Consumers" section.

## Steps

3. Verify an annotated ledger renders the Sankey consistently with the cash-flow page.
