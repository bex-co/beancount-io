## Objective

Give web Home's balance modules (Net Worth, Account balances, and the assets and liabilities distribution charts) an `at_value` read. The flow modules (Money movement, Income vs Expenses, Cash flow) keep the existing `at_cost` overview read. The same load also fetches the held units and the commodity price dates that t005 discloses.

## Context

- `dashboard/src/features/reports/overview/index.tsx` feeds every module from one `GetLedgerOverview` read at `overviewQueryDefaults.conversion` (`at_cost`, in `constants.ts`). `loader.ts` prefetches that read for server rendering.
- `getLedgerBalanceSheet` takes the same `ledgerId`, `conversion`, `account`, `filter`, `time` and `interval` arguments. One document can alias it for `at_value` and `units`, beside `getLedgerCommodities`.
- `dashboard/codegen.ts` reads the schema from `VITE_API_URL`, or from `CODEGEN_SCHEMA_FILE`; `src/graphql/definitions.ts` is generated.
- The Cash flow Sankey also takes the assets and liabilities hierarchies; keep it on the `at_cost` read with its flows.

## Steps

1. Add the valuation document to `overview/graphql/`, regenerate `definitions.ts`, and prefetch the document in `loader.ts`.
2. Point the balance modules at it, and show a loading state instead of a cost figure before it lands.
3. Leave the flow modules, the report pages and their conversion selector untouched.

## Files

- `dashboard/src/features/reports/overview/` (`index.tsx`, `loader.ts`, `constants.ts`, `graphql/`)
- `dashboard/src/graphql/definitions.ts` (generated)

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Net Worth, Account balances and the distribution charts show `at_value` figures, and the flow modules show exactly the figures they did before.
- [ ] A server render already has the market figures, and nothing on screen switches from cost to market after load.

## Out of scope

- The disclosure itself (t005), and the report pages' `At Cost` default.
