## Objective

Make every mobile balance read request `at_value` while flows and journals keep `at_cost`. Add one pure selector that, given a total's market, cost and units maps and the ledger's price dates, returns everything [the valuation rule](../README.md#valuation-rule) asks a total to disclose:

- its market value, cost basis and unrealized difference;
- the held priced commodities with their price dates;
- the prices-as-of date and whether it is stale;
- the holdings valued at cost for lack of a price;
- the holdings not in the total.

## Context

- `mobile/src/common/balance-util.ts` holds `BALANCE_CONVERSION = "at_cost"`, used by `use-balance-sheet.ts` (Home), `use-trial-balance.ts` (Accounts), `use-account-report.ts` (account detail), `use-income-statement.ts` (Reports) and `account-detail-screen.tsx` (journal).
- `mobile/src/common/balance-display.ts` decides units versus money per m11. Keep it free of `@/` imports so the jest-lite runner can require it; the new selector follows the same rule.
- `getLedgerCommodities(ledgerId)` returns `{ base, quote, prices: [{ date, value }] }`, with prices sorted by date. A price counts in either direction, as in `backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/price-map.ts`.
- The balance-sheet series' last point is the figure's own date (an interval end), valued with prices on or before it.

## Steps

1. Add a balance valuation constant (`at_value`) beside `BALANCE_CONVERSION`. Route Home, Accounts and the account report to it; Reports and journals stay on `at_cost`.
2. Add a commodities `.graphql` document and hook, and a slim `at_cost` plus `units` read of the Home series. Run `yarn codegen`.
3. Write the pure selector and its unit tests.
