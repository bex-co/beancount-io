## Objective

Make the Accounts tree's roots match Home's market figures, and label every commodity row and account detail header with its own basis, following rule 7 of [the valuation rule](../README.md#valuation-rule).

## Context

- `mobile/src/components/account-list/select-trial-balance.ts` and `components/account-table/account-table.tsx` render the m11 row notes through `balanceNotes`.
- `mobile/src/screens/account-detail-screen/` reads the account report (header and chart) and the journal. Journal rows keep each posting's own cost, which is historical and correct.

## Steps

1. Feed the `at_value` tree and the commodity price pairs into the row display. A units row's secondary figure reads `at market` when its commodity has a price and `at cost` when it fell back.
2. Apply the same rule to the account detail header and chart.
3. Keep journal rows and running balances at `at_cost` or `units`.
