## Objective

Make web Home's Net Worth card say what its figure is: its basis, the price date (stale form when due), the holdings at cost with no price, and the cost basis with its unrealized difference, following rules 4–6 of [the valuation rule](../README.md#valuation-rule). Mobile already names its basis; web names none today.

## Context

- `dashboard/src/features/reports/overview/components/net-worth-card.tsx` shows `FormattedAmounts` for the latest point, a month-over-month change, and a chart or table.
- Overview strings live in `overview/dashboard-translations.ts`, in 15 languages, each with a description entry.

## Steps

1. Derive the disclosure from the t004 reads with a pure, unit-tested function, following the same rule as mobile's selector.
2. Render it in the card without pushing the chart around, readable at 390px and in dark mode, and announced to screen readers with the figure.
3. Add the strings in all 15 languages.

## Files

- `dashboard/src/features/reports/overview/components/net-worth-card.tsx`, `overview/lib/`, `overview/dashboard-translations.ts`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On `open_ledger/example`, the card reads `At market value`, shows the price date flagged as possibly out of date, and shows `Cost … · Unrealized …`.
- [ ] A cash-only ledger's card shows no basis line.

## Out of scope

- The report pages, which keep their own conversion selector.
