## Objective

Make the Home card's Net Worth, Assets and Liabilities pages say what each figure is, following rules 4–6 of [the valuation rule](../README.md#valuation-rule), and remove the "At cost" caption that m11 added.

## Context

- `mobile/src/screens/home-screen/home-screen.tsx` builds `chartCaptions`, and `components/account-charts-card.tsx` renders the caption, value and change for each page.
- The skeleton rule in `mobile/AGENTS.md` still applies: the loaded view must not shift when data lands, and captions must fit Dynamic Type.
- Strings go to `mobile/src/translations/en.ts` and all 12 other locales.

## Steps

1. Caption each page from the t001 selector: basis and price date (stale form when due), holdings at cost with no price, and holdings not in total. A page that holds no commodity gets no basis label.
2. Show the cost basis and unrealized difference with Net Worth whenever they differ.
3. Add the strings in all 13 locales, and delete `valuedAtCost` if nothing uses it any more.
