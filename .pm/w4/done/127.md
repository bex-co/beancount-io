**Resolved 2026-09-19** — All three reproduced consumers now share one bound in `common/lib/chart/tooltip.ts`: `confine: true` plus a width cap and wrapping, spread into the tooltip option of `features/reports/overview/components/cash-flow-sankey.tsx`, `features/reports/balance-sheet/line-chart.tsx` and `features/bql/lib/chart-utils.ts`. It is applied per consumer rather than to every chart's defaults, as the note directs. All three were measured in a real rendered browser at 390×844 against the public read-only API, before and after, by reading each popup's own target transform rather than a mid-animation rect.
