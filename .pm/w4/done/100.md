**Resolved 2026-09-19** — Confirmed in source and reproduced against a local
build: `features/journal/locales/ru.ts` held the unchanged English
`Payee/Narration` and `uk.ts` held the half-translated `Отримувач/Narration`,
while the thirteen other catalogs all render this column as
`<payee>/<description>`.

matching its own catalog's `journal.narration` (`Описание`, `Опис`) and payee
wording. Catalog content only — no new key, locale, dependency or
translation-framework change — and the `F` column, canonical flags,
ledger-written payee and narration text, API inputs, table semantics and layout
are untouched.

Verified in a real browser across both shared `JournalTable` consumers, the
Journal and the Account Journal, in `ru`, `uk` and `en` at 1440px and 390px:

loading normally (72 and 18). Gates from `dashboard/`: `yarn format:check`,

`yarn build` all pass. Native-speaker review of the wording is still welcome.
