## Objective

After a successful create, select the new ledger, refresh the ledger list, and land on Home; surface backend tier-limit and name-conflict failures as inline errors without leaving a half-created ledger.

## Context

- Ledger selection is `ledgerVar` in `src/common/vars/ledger.ts`; the drawer reads `useListLedgersQuery`.
- Backend creation runs under a per-user lock with tier enforcement in `createLedgerOperation`; its error messages are the contract to map from.
- w5/m1 established post-create landing on the dashboard; mirror the intent, not the code.

## Steps

1. On success: write the new id to `ledgerVar`, refetch `listLedgers`, `router.replace` to Home.
2. Map conflict and tier errors to field-level messages; other errors to a retryable inline error.
3. Verify on the development server (`deploy/docker-mac`) that a duplicate name and an over-tier account each fail cleanly; delete any ledger created during verification.

## Files

- mobile/src/screens/create-ledger-screen/
- mobile/src/common/vars/ledger.ts

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A created Starter and Sample ledger each become the selected ledger and Home shows their data; both appear in the drawer after an app restart.
- [ ] Duplicate-name and tier failures show inline and leave no ledger behind.
- [ ] No production ledger is created during verification.

## Out of scope

- Editing or deleting ledgers from mobile.
