# Match Open Account Unicode names to the ledger grammar

**Severity:** minor residual of [279](./done/279.md)'s shared account-name grammar. **Estimate:** 55–60 minutes, dashboard. **A2:** account creation should accept supported names and explain unsupported ones before a reader submits them.

## Cause and bounded repair

`dashboard/src/common/lib/beancount/account-token.ts:10` permits only letters, combining marks, **Nd** and hyphens after the initial character. It excludes the two joiners (Cf) and numeric continuation categories No/Nl. Its initial class and COMPONENT_START also use **Nd**, admitting non-ASCII initial digits that this engine cannot parse. `accountTokenProblem` supplies validation through `open-account-schema.ts:34` and `use-open-account-schema.ts`. Both form buttons depend on that shared schema's isValid.

Admit the independently supported U+200C/U+200D joiners and Unicode numeric continuations in the shared continuation rule, preserving exact input strings. Restrict an initial digit to **0–9**, keeping both the complete-token and error-classification rules aligned, and provide localized guidance that explains this restriction. Keep supported uppercase/titlecase/caseless initials; accepting a Roman numeral **after** Qa must not accept the separately refused Roman-numeral **initial**. Preserve rejection of underscores, whitespace, empty segments and lowercase-led components, configured root names, duplicate/open/closed-account checks and dates. Do not strip joiners, transliterate initial digits or normalize account identities silently. Keep the existing lightweight browser validator; no engine dependency, backend/API behavior or cross-package import is needed.

The shared hook has two production consumers: Accounts OpenAccountDialog and Journal OpenAccountForm. Both were exercised live. The server's `backend-cluster/ledger/src/features/ledger/utils/entry-tokens.ts` already explicitly permits these two joiners; the current structured-entry and parser controls also admit the suffix examples. Existing server validation and protocol adapters remain unchanged.

Acceptance: real shared-schema and form coverage accepts the four exact continuation names, keeps supported numeric suffixes/ASCII-digit initials/combining/titlecase controls valid, and refuses the three non-ASCII initial-digit examples with useful guidance. Preserve the other refused initials/underscore plus root/empty/whitespace controls. Include raw-string identity, disabled-state/error recovery when switching between supported and refused names, both form consumers and cancellation focus. Repeat native desktop/narrow checks and run dashboard gates when implementing.

**Resolved 2026-10-23** — The shared account grammar (`common/lib/beancount/account-token.ts`) now matches the ledger at both positions:
- After a component's first character it admits any Unicode number (`\p{N}`, so `Qa²` and `QaⅣ`) and the zero-width joiners U+200C and U+200D (Persian `فارسی‌حساب`, the Devanagari conjunct `क्‍ष`).
- An initial digit must be ASCII 0–9, in both the complete-token and component-start rules. `١٢٣`, `۱۴۰۲` and `１２３` are refused as `component-start`, while `Qa١` and `401k` stay valid.

Initial Roman numerals and circled letters, underscores, whitespace, empty and lowercase-led components, configured roots and the open/closed checks are unchanged. Names are kept exactly as typed. The joiners sit in an alternation rather than the character class, for `no-misleading-character-class`. The component-start guidance now says "a capital letter or a digit 0–9" in all fifteen catalogs.

Tests:
- `account-token.test.ts` covers the four continuation names, the suffix and `401k` controls, the refused initials, the three non-ASCII initial digits as `component-start`, and raw-string identity through the schema.
- `open-account-already-open.test.tsx` covers both form consumers, Accounts' Open Account and New Entry's Account tab. Joined and numbered names are accepted as typed with submit enabled. Non-ASCII initial digits are refused with the new guidance, `aria-invalid` and submit disabled, then recover when corrected to `Qa…`.
- The 22 new cases fail on the old grammar.

Native check on the built repaired client (local fixture SSR, synthetic owner; unsaved drafts, mutations blocked), at 1440×1000 and 390×844 in both forms:
- the ZWNJ, ZWJ, `²`, `Ⅳ` and `١` suffixes, ASCII and `401k` keep their exact value, are not `aria-invalid`, and enable submit;
- `١٢٣`, `۱۴۰۲` and `１２３` initials and `ⅣFund` are refused with "Each part must start with a capital letter or a digit 0–9 — …";
- `Bad_Name` keeps its letters/digits/hyphens message;
- Escape returns focus to the opener.

Zero mutations or page errors. At 390, the Accounts toolbar control's visible name is "New" (its "Open Account" text is hidden at that width). That is existing behaviour, noted here and not changed. Dashboard gates pass. Not verified: active IME composition, actual account creation, other engines and locales, and the deployed client. Shipped in the commit that moved this note (`dashboard`).
