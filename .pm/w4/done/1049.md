# Keep a nonzero range for very small BQL chart values

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 45–60 minutes, dashboard. **A2:** a query chart should retain meaningful differences between supported nonzero Decimal results.

## Genuine reproduction and controls

Production `https://beancount.io`, October24 UTC2026; anonymous public `open_ledger/example`, English/UTC/light, isolated headless Chromium156.0.8078.47, fresh1440×1000 and390×844 contexts. All queries are genuine read-only SELECTs; no ledger or response data is substituted.

On `/ledger/open_ledger/example/query`, execute:

```sql
SELECT 'qa-20261024-tiny' AS label,
       0.000000000000000000000001 AS amount LIMIT 1
```

HTTP200 QueryShell returns dtype **Decimal** and the exact nonzero string. The result table retains it. The mounted chart's series also retains the nonzero number **1e-24**, but its computed Y-axis extent is **[0,0]**, its only tick is0, and the actual bar rectangle has **height0/area0**. Repeating with **-0.000000000000000000000001** reproduces the same collapsed range and zero-height bar. Both fresh widths repeat.

A genuine dated query independently demonstrates lost variation:

```sql
SELECT date, number * 0.000000000000000000000001 AS amount
FROM year = 2016 AND month = 4
WHERE account = 'Expenses:Food:Coffee' LIMIT 3
```

It returns two rows: April12 **0.00000000000000000000000594** and April19 **0.00000000000000000000000523**. The line series retains the distinct numbers **5.94e-24 / 5.23e-24**, but its axis again computes **[0,0]** and both rendered point Y coordinates are **216.5**. The screenshot shows a flat line beside a lone0 tick. The dates and exact table values are correct.

Working controls in each context: **1e-18** yields extent[0,1.2e-18]/247.5px bar height; **1e-12** yields[0,1e-12]/297px; **4.00995** yields[0,5]/238.19px. Actual pointer hover over the failed charts' plot area still shows the exact tiny source strings. No ECharts dispatch or formatter invocation was used to manufacture tooltips. There are twelve genuine successful QueryShell responses across the retained cases, with no GraphQL errors.

## Cause and bounded repair

`dashboard/src/features/bql/lib/chart-utils.ts:184–187` supplies only type/value-axis name and leaves the numeric domain to ECharts. The builder retains exact tooltip strings while plotting finite numeric coordinates; the observed nonzero series proves these values did not become zero during decoding.

In the actual served/installed **ECharts6.0.0**, `lib/scale/Interval.js:311–314` rounds both automatically calculated extent endpoints. `lib/scale/helper.js:94–97` requests interval precision, but `lib/util/number.js:56,151–160` caps rounding at20 fractional places. Those very small endpoints both become0. The resulting degenerate domain collapses the bar/line coordinates even though their input values remain distinct and nonzero.

Handle this range at the BQL chart boundary with an appropriate display scale/domain and honest axis labels, or clear non-chart feedback when the range cannot be plotted. Retain original decimals in table, CSV and tooltips. Preserve meaningful proportions for mixed magnitudes and legitimate zero data; do not substitute zero, force arbitrary bar heights or round the query results. Keep date/category identity, repeated-label alignment, signs, units and unsupported/multi-unit result omission. No backend, API, dependency or global formatter change is needed, and vendor files should remain generated dependencies.

QueryResultCard (`components/query-result-card.tsx:331`) is the builder's sole production consumer. Its string/Decimal bars and date/Decimal line were independently exercised here. Other report builders and tiny Amount/Inventory wire variants remain compatibility scope, without a claim that every chart is affected.

Acceptance: evaluate the real chart configuration with ECharts and assert a nondegenerate domain and meaningful geometry for the recorded positive/negative and differing dated values, plus exact native tooltip/table text. Cover ordinary/small/zero/mixed-magnitude and repeated-category controls, then repeat desktop/narrow and run dashboard gates. A test of only the retained series or tooltip formatter would pass this defect.

**Resolved 2026-10-23** — The BQL chart builder (`bql/lib/chart-utils.ts`) now plots a series whose largest magnitude is below 1e-15 scaled by the power of ten of that magnitude (`10^-floor(log10(max))`). Its y-axis ticks are labelled in the same notation, for example `2e-24` or `-0.4e-24`. Below roughly 1e-20, ECharts' 20-decimal extent rounding had collapsed such an axis to [0, 0].

Tooltips still say each row's exact source decimal, by index, and the table and CSV are untouched. Series down to 1e-15 (1e-12, 1e-18-scale controls), ordinary amounts, zero data and mixed magnitudes keep their plain axis and proportions. Signs, units, date and category identity and multi-unit omission are unchanged.

`chart-utils-tiny-range.test.ts` runs the built option through real ECharts (SVG SSR). It covers:
- positive and negative 1e-24 bars: a nondegenerate extent, more than 50 px of bar height, a `1e-24`/`-1e-24` tick label in the SVG, and the exact tooltip;
- the dated 5.94e-24 / 5.23e-24 line: points more than 5 px apart, with exact tooltips;
- controls: 1e-12, 1e-15, 4.00995 and zero plot as before with no axis formatter, a mixed 1e-24/1 series stays unscaled, and repeated labels keep their own exact tooltip.

The three defect cases fail on the old builder; the six controls pass on both.

Native check on the built repaired client (local fixture SSR, public example ledger, genuine recorded `QueryShell` reads), at 1440×1000 and 390×844, reading back the chart canvas:
- the positive and negative 1e-24 queries draw full-height bars (about 296 px of series pixels, against the 4.00995 control's 237 px) with ticks `-0.2e-24`…`-1e-24`;
- the dated line slopes between distinct points, with ticks `6e-24`, `5e-24`…;
- hovering each plot shows its exact source decimal, for example `-0.000000000000000000000001` and `0.00000000000000000000000594`.

Screenshots were inspected. Zero mutations or page errors. Dashboard gates pass. Not verified: charts outside BQL, tiny Amount/Inventory wire variants natively, other engines and locales, screen-reader speech, and the deployed client. Shipped in the commit that moved this note (`dashboard`).
