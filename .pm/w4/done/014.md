# Keep Money flow postings identifiable at enlarged text — and stop "FROM" breaking mid-word

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 35–50 minutes. Owner: mobile. A2: the transaction detail is where a reader goes to see *which accounts* moved; at enlarged sizes it stops answering that.

Environment: branch `main`, HEAD `038faeeb`, native development build `io.beancount.ios` on iPhone 17e / iOS 26.5, English / System / light, authenticated production session at `https://beancount.io`, public `open_ledger/alphabet`. Report-only QA; no ledger mutation. Content size restored to `large` afterwards and verified.

## Repro

1. `xcrun simctl ui <udid> content_size accessibility-medium` (AX1). Terminate and relaunch.
2. Home → Recent Transactions → first row (`Q2 2026 Expenses`, `$26,560,000,000.00`). Dismiss the development warning banner.
3. The **Money flow** card renders:

   ```
   TO
   E…ax            +$26,560,000,000.00  >

   FRO
   M
   A…ts            -$26,560,000,000.00  >
   ```

   - `Expenses:…:Income-Tax` is ellipsized to **`E…ax`** — four characters, two of which are the ellipsis's neighbours.
   - `Assets:…:Cash-And-Equivalents` becomes **`A…ts`**.
   - The direction label **`FROM` is broken across two lines as `FRO` / `M`**.
4. Relaunch at AX1 and repeat — identical.

**Working control:** at `content_size large` the same card reads `TO  Expense…ome-Tax  +$26,560,000,000.00` and `FROM  Assets:…valents  -$26,560,000,000.00`, each on one line with the label intact (evidence `w4l1-04-recent-tap.jpg` from an earlier pass this session).

**Expected:** the posting's account stays identifiable, and a four-letter uppercase label never breaks mid-word.
**Actual:** both accounts are reduced to four characters and `FROM` is hyphen-lessly split, so neither posting can be read.

## Root cause

`mobile/src/screens/transaction-detail-screen/components/posting-row.tsx`:

- `row: { flexDirection: "row", gap: 12 }` holds `accountColumn`, the `AmountText`, and a 16pt chevron.
- `accountColumn: { flex: 1, minWidth: 0 }` is the only flexible child.
- `amount: { fontSize: fontSizes.md, flexShrink: 0 }` — unshrinkable, so at AX1 it takes its full intrinsic width first and `accountColumn` absorbs the entire shortfall.

Inside that collapsed column:
- `account` (line 77) has `numberOfLines={1} ellipsizeMode="middle"`, so it degrades to `E…ax` rather than overflowing — correct behaviour, wrong amount of space.
- `direction` (line 75) has **no `numberOfLines`**, so when the column is narrower than the rendered word the text engine breaks inside it: `FRO` / `M`.

Same "unshrinkable trailing block" family as `w4/013` (`EntryRow`) and `w4/012`/`w3/217` (`AccountTable`), but a different component and file.

## Fix

Apply the pattern `31f1e4ce` established: above `common/theme/dynamic-type.ts`'s 1.3 threshold, stack the row — direction label and account on their own lines, amount beneath — and keep today's single-row geometry unchanged below it. Give `direction` `numberOfLines={1}` regardless, so a short label can never break mid-word at any size.

Do not let the amount shrink or ellipsize (`AmountText`'s contract). Do not drop `ellipsizeMode="middle"` on the account — the middle ellipsis is what keeps the root and the leaf visible, and it is the right behaviour once the column has room. Keep the whole row tappable, its chevron, and its existing `accessibilityLabel`, which already carries the full account name and is unaffected.

**Acceptance:** on a fresh iPhone 17e launch at AX1 and at maximum size, both Money flow rows show enough of `Expenses:…:Income-Tax` and `Assets:…:Cash-And-Equivalents` to identify them, `FROM` renders on one line, and the amounts stay complete; at `large` the card is pixel-identical to today. Add constrained-layout regression coverage for a long account with a large amount, a short account, and a posting with no direction label (`sign === 0`, which renders no label at all).

## Blast radius

`PostingRow` is used only by `transaction-detail-screen.tsx:531`, which every entry link reaches — Home recent, Transactions, account detail, merchant detail, Reports account transactions, and the edit-return path. Reproduced at AX1 from the Home recent-transactions entry point. Not exercised: maximum size, RTL (where `LEADING_TEXT_ALIGN` and `directionalIcon` also matter), dark mode, VoiceOver, Android.

## Adjacent states

Two things were re-observed on this screen at AX1 and belong to other notes, not this one:

- The hero amount still renders its minus sign orphaned on its own line, and at AX1 additionally wraps mid-thousands-group (`$26,560,000,0` / `00.00`). That is **`w4/005`**, now corroborated at a second text size — its proposed `numberOfLines={1}` + `adjustsFontSizeToFit` fix has to hold here too.
- The title wraps to `Q2 2026` / `Expenses` and stays complete here, but `w3/209` (open) owns the two-line `numberOfLines={2}` limit that truncates longer payees at maximum size.

## Dedupe

Searched all `.pm/**/*.md` including `done/` for `PostingRow`, posting-row, money flow, direction label, and enlarged-text terms. **`w3/209.md` explicitly scopes this out**: "Posting-account labels have their own ellipsis/navigation behavior and are outside this finding." `w4/013.md` is `EntryRow` (list rows), `w4/012.md`/`w3/217.md` is `AccountTable` — same defect family, different components, each needing its own restack. `w3/done/181.md` and `w3/done/183.md` are the completed precedents for the technique. ---

**Resolved 2026-09-14** — fixed in `mobile` by `fix(mobile): keep Money flow postings identifiable at enlarged text`. Triage confirmed the root cause at HEAD before the fix: `PostingRow` kept its amount at `flexShrink: 0` beside a `flex: 1` account column, so at AX1 the account collapsed to `E…ax`. The direction label had no `numberOfLines`, so `FROM` broke as `FRO` / `M`. The note was not stale.

The direction label now renders on one line at every size. Following `31f1e4ce`, the row reads `useWindowDimensions().fontScale`, and at or above `prefersStackedLayout`'s 1.3 threshold it becomes a column. The direction label and account take the full row width, keeping the account's middle ellipsis, and the amount and chevron move to a line below. Below the threshold the card is unchanged. The amount is never truncated, the row stays tappable with its full accessibility label, and a posting without a direction label still renders none.

Regression test: `mobile/src/__tests__/posting-row-dynamic-type.test.ts`. It is a source guardrail, because the unit runner cannot lay out text. It checks the threshold wiring, the one-line direction label, the middle ellipsis, the untruncated amount, and the accessibility label, and three of its tests fail with the row reverted. The device acceptance at AX1 and at maximum size, RTL, and dark mode were not repeated in this run.
