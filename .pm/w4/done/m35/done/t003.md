## Verification 2026-10-23

The bounded focus handoff shipped in `fd3c84e72`: `use-suggestions-fallback.tsx` keeps Try Again focusable while it runs (`aria-busy`/`aria-disabled`, extra activations ignored), so a failed attempt keeps focus on it. When a successful retry removes it, focus returns to the field its notice described (found through that field's `aria-describedby`), unless the reader moved focus elsewhere. Native journeys ran on a local fixture app (synthetic owner; the recorded suggestion reads served normally, one read aborted in the page at a time; mutations aborted) at 1440×1000 and 390×844. Each case aborted GetLedgerPayees or GetLedgerNarrations (Transaction), GetLedgerAccounts (Note) or GetLedgerCurrencies (Balance), typed a draft, used native Tab to the field's own Try Again and pressed Enter while still failing, then restored the read and pressed Enter again. All eight cases kept focus on **Try Again** after the failed retry and returned focus to the **same field** with the exact draft after the success. They reopened genuine suggestions (Payee 36, Narration 33, Account 60, Currency 9 options), with zero mutations and no page errors. Account's background and form reads were both aborted, which is why it has four reads. Regression: `suggestions-retry-focus.test.tsx` drives all four wrappers inside a real Radix Dialog with `userEvent` Tab/Enter and asserts `activeElement` after the failed and successful retries; every case fails with the fix reverted. `suggestions-failure.test.tsx` now waits on `aria-busy` rather than a disabled state. No New Entry help text describes this behavior, so no content changed. Limits: verified on the local build, not yet on a deployed one; physical devices, speech and other engines are unverified.
