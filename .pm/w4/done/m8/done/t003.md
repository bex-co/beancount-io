## Objective

Produce Play-accepted phone screenshots and the 1024×500 feature graphic from the deterministic captures that already feed the App Store sets.

## Context

- `yarn screenshots:build` renders 1284×2778 assets per locale; Play requires each side between 320 and 3840 px and a max:min ratio of at most 2:1, so the App Store size is rejected as-is.
- The feature graphic is required for some Play placements and there is none in the repo.
- `metadata/screenshots.json` is the story manifest; add a Play device entry rather than a parallel manifest.

## Steps

1. Add a Play phone target to the screenshot build that composes the same three stories on a 1080×1920 canvas.
2. Generate the feature graphic from the brand assets and the first story caption per locale.
3. Extend `yarn screenshots:validate` with the Play dimensions.

## Files

- mobile/metadata/screenshots.json
- mobile/scripts/build-screenshots.sh
- mobile/metadata/screenshots/

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Every locale has three Play phone screenshots and one feature graphic that pass the validator.
- [x] Generated files stay gitignored like the App Store sets.

## Out of scope

- Tablet screenshots for Play.

## Validation evidence

yarn screenshots:build produced 64 Play images (16 locales × three 1080×1920 phone screenshots plus one 1024×500 feature graphic), alongside the existing 84 Apple images. yarn screenshots:validate passed for all 148 files.
