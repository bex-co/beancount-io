**Resolved 2026-09-19** — Both reproduced tooltips now format at the source's own precision. A new `formatQuantity` in `common/lib/format/format-number.ts`, reached through `useFormatQuantity`, takes the fraction-digit count that `fractionDigitsOf` reads off the ledger's decimal string and passes it as `maximumFractionDigits` (or to `toFixed` when `render_commas` is off). Reading the budget from the string rather than the parsed double matters: asking a double for all its digits prints binary artifacts — `0.1 + 0.2` becomes `0.30000000000000004` — whereas the source string says exactly how much precision is real. Each chart remembers the digits per date/commodity before the values are parsed for plotting, so the numeric series, axes and conversion behavior are untouched. `useFormatNumber` is unchanged and its other consumers were not modified, as the note directs. Both defects were reproduced first at HEAD against the public read-only API: at 1440×1000 the Units Account Balance tooltip for May 2026 read `ETH: 4.01` while the journal on the same page showed `4.00995`, and at 390×844 Changes Over Time read `ETH: +2.01`. After the change the same hovers read `ETH: 4.00995` and `ETH: +2.00995`. The surrounding months are unchanged and confirm nothing was over-corrected: whole balances still read `2`, `4` and `6` with no invented decimals, `4.5` stays `4.5`, negatives keep their sign (`-1.5`), and a zero change still reads `+0`. Tests: `common/lib/format/__tests__/format-quantity.test.ts` covers the helpers directly — the two reproduced quantities, small and negative values, zero, ordinary USD, locale separators, the `render_commas=false` branch, and the binary-artifact guard. `features/reports/__tests__/chart-tooltip-precision.test.tsx` runs each chart's own tooltip formatter through the real `formatQuantity` with the params shape ECharts passes, covering both reproduced quantities plus whole-number, USD, small-holding, negative and zero controls; the four precision cases fail when the three-digit cap is restored, while the six controls pass either way by design. Two existing suites that stubbed `use-format-number` for these two components were updated to stub the hook they now use; the stacked sibling still uses `useFormatNumber` and was left alone, since the note records its precision as source-only and not independently reproduced. Not verified: current Safari or Firefox, other locales in the browser, the stacked chart, and `render_commas=false` in production. Dashboard gates all pass (`yarn format:check`, `yarn lint`, `yarn test` — 409 files, 4607 passed, 1 pre-existing skip — and `yarn build`). Shipped in `fe202103` (`dashboard`).
