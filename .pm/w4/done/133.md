**Resolved 2026-09-19** — The five repeated pressed/unpressed style branches in `journal-filters.tsx` now come from one `filterButtonState` helper, which lives in its own module (`journal-filter-button-state.ts`) because a component file cannot export it without breaking the fast-refresh lint rule. It adds a forced-colors branch: a pressed button takes `Highlight` with `HighlightText` and a matching border, while an unpressed one's otherwise-transparent border becomes `Canvas` so it stops showing. The two branches are mutually exclusive rather than layered, since same-specificity utilities would resolve in an order this file does not control. Two things the original note did not call out came up in verification and are handled: the pressed colours also hold through `hover`, because the shared Button lightens its background there and would have put HighlightText on a near-Canvas fill — a regression the fix would otherwise have introduced — and focus gets its own solid `CanvasText` outline, since the shared Button's focus ring is a box-shadow that forced colors drops. The defect was reproduced first at HEAD under forced-colors emulation at 1440×1000: pressed Transaction and Metadata measured a white background against unpressed neighbours' transparent one over the same white page, with black text, a 1px black border and no shadow on every button. After the change, at 1440×1000 and 390×844, the pressed filters measure a Highlight fill with white text while `*`, `x`, Open and Postings keep Canvas borders and black text; selecting Pending shows all three of Transaction, `!` and Metadata pressed and distinct with the pointer and focus elsewhere, alongside the truthful `0–0 / 0`. Behaviour is unchanged: deselecting Pending returns the real `1–36 / 36` February control, and with forced colors off the pressed button keeps its ordinary secondary fill, border and shadow while unpressed stays transparent with muted text. `__tests__/journal-filter-forced-colors.test.ts` adds seven cases that compile the classes the helper actually produces with the project's own Tailwind and assert on the emitted CSS rather than on class names: the Highlight set for pressed, its survival under hover, the absence of Highlight for unpressed, the Canvas border, an explicit check that the two branches share no forced-colors class, the focus outline in both states, and that the ordinary shadow stays outside the media query. Four of the seven fail when the forced-colors rules are removed. As the note requires, the rendered styling itself was established by the browser measurements above. Not verified: real Windows palettes, other engines, screen-reader speech, the document and custom subtype buttons with populated records, and the Account report's shared `JournalFilters` consumer, which inherits the same helper by construction but was not exercised. Dashboard gates all pass (`yarn format:check`, `yarn lint`, `yarn test` — 416 files, 4659 passed, 1 pre-existing skip — and `yarn build`). Shipped in `68ea256f` (`dashboard`).
