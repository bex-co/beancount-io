## Outcome

Reviewed the milestone's own diff. It is small and spread across six auth
forms plus fifteen locale files, so this ran as a direct review rather than an
agent fan-out.

Applied:

- **One `ScriptRequiredNotice` component.** The `<noscript>` block was pasted
  verbatim into both credential forms — the same markup, class, role and key.
  It is now one component, so the wording and styling have a single home. The
  same move as `ChartUnitScope` in m21.
- **Comment prose cut roughly in half.** The four sibling forms each carried an
  identical four-line explanation of why they got `method="post"`; that is two
  lines now. The five-line block above the login and register forms is two,
  because `useHydrated`'s own doc comment already explains why a handler-less
  form is dangerous — the reasoning belongs there, not repeated at every call
  site.
- **Comment style normalised.** The login and register forms used a bare
  `/* … */` in expression position before the JSX; they now use the same
  `// …` form as their neighbours.

Not applied: extracting a shared `<CredentialForm>` wrapper around the two
forms. They differ in layout classes, `noValidate`, their fields and their
schemas; the genuinely shared parts are one attribute, one disabled clause and
the notice — and the notice is exactly what was extracted. A wrapper over two
call sites with that little in common would be the "unnecessary abstraction"
this kind of review exists to prevent.

The SSR safety and authentication contracts are unchanged, and verified rather
than assumed: 9/9 credential checks still pass — no pre-hydration submission,
no `password` key, submit enabled after hydration, hydrated validation intact —
and the noscript matrix is identical (notice shown and button disabled without
scripts, notice absent and button enabled with them, German string for
`?lang=de`).

Gates from `dashboard/`: `yarn format:check`, `yarn lint`, `yarn test` (384
files, 4331 passed, 1 pre-existing skip) and `yarn build` all pass.
