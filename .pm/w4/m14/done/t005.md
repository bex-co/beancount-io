## Implementation and verification

Completed 2026-09-19. Added `dashboard/src/features/journal/components/__tests__/catalan-control-labels.test.tsx`, 33 cases that render the real Catalan catalog rather than keys: the suite re-mocks `useTranslations` against `@/i18n/locales/ca`, so every assertion is about a shipped value.

It pins the repair three ways. Each of the eleven controls named in FINDINGS is asserted to carry its own concept, and each is separately asserted **not** to carry the exact string it held while misassigned — stated as key/wrong-value pairs, because several of those strings are legitimate Catalan on other keys ("Emplenar" is Pad, "Carregant el context de l'entrada..." is the entry-context loader), so the regression is the pairing rather than the string. Rendered cases then drive the real `JournalFilters`: the directive controls show Obrir, Nota, Metadades and Apunts; the subtype controls keep their literal "!", "x" and "V" text with "Transaccions pendents", "Altres transaccions" and "Documents vinculats" as accessible names, which is the flag name/value separation the milestone asks for; clicking Obrir still calls `onDirectiveTypesChange` with `DirectiveType.OPEN`, and the Metadades and Apunts toggles still fire their own handlers. English is asserted unchanged, and Equity and Net Worth are asserted distinct so the collision fixed in t004 cannot return.

A final case generalizes the guard: flag columns must stay one or two characters with no whitespace in every one of the fifteen languages. That catches this defect class in any locale without being noisy for CJK, since a flag is a character everywhere. One pre-existing deviation is excluded by name rather than silently asserted away: zh renders `journal.pending` as the word "待处理" instead of "!", which predates this milestone and is a separate call about Chinese flag columns.

The test was proved real rather than assumed: restoring both catalogs to their pre-repair state (`bb4dd35d~1`) fails 27 of the 33 cases; restoring the repair passes all 33.

Gates from `dashboard/`: `yarn format:check`, `yarn lint`, `yarn build` and `yarn test` all pass — 375 files and 4,223 tests, up from 374 and 4,190, with the one pre-existing skip unchanged. gitleaks reports no leaks in the changed tree.

Treated honestly: the authenticated entry forms remain untested here, as the milestone scope requires. The narrow-width dialog and English controls were exercised live in t003 rather than in this suite, and this suite does not claim a browser run.
