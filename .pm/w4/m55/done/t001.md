## Verification

- `budget-utils.ts`:
  - `parseBudgetAmount` returns the ledger's `exact` decimal. Directive validity now uses `parseDecimalNumber`.
  - `readSparseBalanceAmount` returns the interval's decimal string; missing is `"0"`, malformed is `null`.
  - `calculateBudgetForInterval(end, interval, history, digits)` groups the days into exact terms, one per directive and period length. The new shared `sumDecimalFractions` (`common/lib/beancount/decimal-number.ts`, BigInt) sums them and rounds once, half away from zero, to `digits`. `budgetVariance` subtracts exactly.
- Daily …993/…992 now differ by −1 and +1. Ordinary 28-, 29-, 30- and 31-day exact-match targets prorate to exactly `1650.00`.
- **Precision policy:** a share that does not terminate rounds once to the card's precision. That is the currency's minor units or the commodity's digits (at least 3), or the recorded digits when those are finer. For example, 53550/31 → `1727.42`. No float tolerance or binary residue remains, and the old `MAX_PRORATION_DAYS` allowance is gone.
- Cadence changes, same-day precedence, partial periods, sparse balances and income direction keep their behavior: `budget-utils.test.ts` and the rewritten `budget-variance.test.ts` cover each. No new dependency.
