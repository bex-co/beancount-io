## Objective

Integrate a clear selected/separate-unit or unavailable mixed-unit presentation and actual-unit tooltips.

See [FINDINGS.md](./FINDINGS.md) for evidence, source and limits.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Synopsys revenue is7054 MUSD, never7054 USD; Adyen NetRevenue remains26,328,539,000 EUR; ordinary single-USD data remains usable.
- [x] Mixed example tax data preserves52047.35 USD separately from18000 IRAUSD, including totals and Savings.

## Outcome

The Sankey tooltip appended a literal ` USD` to every edge. It now prints the
unit t001's transformer actually chose, and the chart says plainly when the
ledger holds units it cannot show.

- `cash-flow-sankey.tsx` formats each edge as `value + sankeyData.unit`.
- Below the chart, when the underlying accounts hold more than one unit, a
  short note names the unit shown and the ones left out. A Sankey adds its
  links into node totals, the centre and Savings, so it can only be truthful
  in one unit; saying which is the honest alternative to silently dropping the
  rest.
- New key `page.overview.cashFlowUnitScope`, written in all fifteen locales.
  The suite rejects `[TODO]` placeholders in non-English catalogs, so each is
  a real translation; it also caught the key while it was still unreferenced,
  which is how I knew the wiring was incomplete.

Verified live at 1440 on four public ledgers:

| Ledger | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Synopsys `Income:Revenue` | 7,054 USD | **7,054 MUSD** |
| Adyen `Income:NetRevenue` | 26,328,539,000 USD | **26,328,539,000 EUR** |
| Example `Expenses:Taxes` | 42,116.2 USD | **52,047.35 USD** |
| crypto-example | USD | USD (unchanged control) |

The scope note appears exactly where it should: on the mixed example ledger it
reads *"Amounts are shown in USD. Balances in IRAUSD, VACHR are not
included."*, and on the single-unit Synopsys and crypto ledgers it does not
render at all, because nothing is being left out.

That note is also the answer to this task's second criterion: the example's
52,047.35 USD is shown while its 18,000 IRAUSD is preserved in the data,
excluded from every USD total and Savings figure, and disclosed to the reader
rather than folded in or hidden.
