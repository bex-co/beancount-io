# Keep native stepping exact in the Receipt and Budget amount fields

**Minor amount integrity · 30–45m · dashboard · A2.** Follow-up from [1017](./done/1017.md)'s audit. Source-only observation, not reproduced in a browser.

1017 gave the New Entry Transaction and Balance amounts `ExactAmountInput`. ArrowUp/ArrowDown step in exact decimal arithmetic there, the spin buttons are hidden, and the wheel cannot step a focused amount, because the browser's own stepping runs through a double (9007199254740993.01 → ArrowUp → 9.00719925474099e+15). The two other `type="number" step="any"` amount callers keep the native stepping:

- `features/receipt/components/receipt-review-form.tsx` (receipt amount, `min="0"`, validated with `isPositiveDecimalNumber`), and
- `features/ledger-data/budget/add-budget-dialog.tsx` (budget amount).

`ExactAmountInput` lives in the journal feature. Features may not import each other's components, so sharing it means moving it to `common/components/` and adopting it in both forms. Keep each form's own validation (Receipt's positive-only rule, 982's step) and do not widen what either accepts.

Acceptance:
- Native ArrowUp/ArrowDown on a large decimal in both fields keeps the exact value plus or minus one.
- The wheel and spin buttons can't round a focused amount.
- Each form's prepared request carries the exact amount.
- Rendered regressions fail on the old fields; native 1440/390 checks and dashboard gates pass.
