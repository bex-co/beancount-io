## Objective

State the basis wherever a converted total appears, and make Net Worth name the holdings it cannot include, so `$106,723.05` cannot be read as today's market value or as a complete total.

## Context

- [Display rule](../README.md#display-rule), rules 2 and 5. Decision background: [w4/027](../../027.md).
- Net Worth: `mobile/src/screens/home-screen/components/account-charts-card.tsx` (the `netWorth` series), fed by `selectors/select-balance-sheet-series.ts` and `hooks/use-balance-sheet.ts`.
- Translations: `mobile/src/translations/*.ts` (13 locales). The locale-integrity suite enforces that every key is translated; a deliberate English term needs an entry with a reason in `src/translations/__tests__/known-gaps.ts`.

## Steps

1. Add the "at cost" label to the Net Worth card and to the Accounts totals.
2. When the balance sheet holds unconvertible commodities, name them beside Net Worth.
3. Translate the new strings in all 13 locales.
