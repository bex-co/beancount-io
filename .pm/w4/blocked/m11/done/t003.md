## Objective

Make the account detail screen for a commodity account say what the ledger records: the header balance, each journal row, the running balance, and the header's change and percentage all follow the [display rule](../README.md#display-rule), so none of them reads as an unlabelled dollar amount.

## Context

- Repro: [w4/016](../../../016.md) Repro A (RGAGX header `$49,049.67`, rows `+$150.02` for `1.843 RGAGX {81.40 USD}`).
- `mobile/src/screens/account-detail-screen/account-detail-screen.tsx`; `selectors/select-account-journal.ts` takes the operating-currency figure through `resolveCurrencyBalance(item.change, currency)`.
- Show units at their recorded scale, as the Transactions list does. Do not route them through the two-decimal money formatter: that rounding is tracked separately in `w3/189`.

## Steps

1. Feed the header and journal rows from the t001 selector, requesting units for commodity accounts.
2. Decide once how the header change and percentage read for a commodity account (in units, or labelled as cost) and apply it consistently.
3. Keep plain operating-currency accounts on their current rendering path.
