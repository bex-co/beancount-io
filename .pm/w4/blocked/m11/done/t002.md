## Objective

Render the Accounts tree through the t001 selector: commodity accounts lead with units and a labelled cost amount, no non-empty account reads `$0.00`, and every parent or root names the commodities its operating-currency total leaves out.

## Context

- [Display rule](../README.md#display-rule), rules 3–5. Repro: [w4/016](../../../016.md) Repro A and the per-root table.
- Row component: `mobile/src/components/account-table/account-table.tsx`, used by `src/screens/accounts-screen/accounts-screen.tsx`.
- The same row has open layout work: `w3/217` (row identity beside large balances) and `w3/218` (enlarged text). Keep the new figures compatible with both; do not solve them here.
- The accessibility-label guardrail from `w4/done/m7` scans these rows. Labels must carry the units and the basis, not just the visible number.

## Steps

1. Replace the row's single operating-currency amount with the selector's primary and secondary figures.
2. Add the unconverted-holdings line to parents and roots using the merchant header's one-line-per-currency shape.
3. Update the row's accessibility label to read the same information.
