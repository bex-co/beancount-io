## Reproducer

Use this valid synthetic ledger, with operating currency **USD** and a cost in **EUR**:

```beancount
option "title" "qa-20261023 foreign valuation quote"
option "operating_currency" "USD"
2026-01-01 commodity QACUTOFF
2026-01-01 open Assets:QaCutoff QACUTOFF
2026-01-01 open Equity:QaCutoffOpening EUR
2026-07-01 * "qa-20261023 acquire"
  Assets:QaCutoff 1 QACUTOFF {10.00 EUR}
  Equity:QaCutoffOpening -10.00 EUR
2026-07-15 price QACUTOFF 20.00 EUR
2026-08-20 price QACUTOFF 99.00 EUR
2026-12-31 price QACUTOFF 1000.00 EUR
```

Open Overview with Time **2026-07**, open Valuation details, close with Escape, change Time to **2026-08**, open/close the dialog, then return to July.

| Fixture | Actual, correct July / August values | Expected holding disclosure | Actual disclosure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EUR cost, EUR quotes above | 20 / 99 EUR | priced, July15 / August20 | **No price / At cost**; headline says “1 at cost (no price)” |
| Same EUR cost, change only the three quotes to USD | 10 / 10 EUR, cost fallback | no applicable price / at cost | **Price Jul15 / Aug20**, without the at-cost warning |

The backend's `at_value` policy uses a lot's cost-currency quote and falls back to its cost when no such quote exists. It does not convert these positions to the operating currency or hop through an unrelated USD quote. Returning to July repeats the same disclosure error. The December future quote is present in both models and correctly does not enter the amounts.
