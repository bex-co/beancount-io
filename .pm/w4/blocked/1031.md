# Use actual lot cost currencies in Net Worth valuation disclosures

**Minor · dashboard · A2/A3.** Residual of [m26](../done/m26/README.md). **Estimate: 45–60m for the dashboard consumer after the data dependency below is available; producer/contract work is separate.** A position bought in EUR is called “No price / At cost” when the backend uses an EUR quote, and called priced when its only USD quote is ignored by the backend. The actual amounts are correct.

**Blocked 2026-10-23** — The current dashboard receives aggregate `at_value`, `at_cost` and `units` maps plus price pairs, without each lot's cost currency or actual valuation basis. Those maps cannot generally attribute a cost currency to each holding, especially when cash and other lots share a currency. Related data limitations are already documented in [W21/554](../../w21/blocked/554.md) and [W21/556](../../w21/blocked/556.md). Do not copy the candidate-currency heuristic from the mobile-only [W21/124](../../w21/done/124.md):556 demonstrates why that remains ambiguous.

**Unblock:** an approved, deployed read supplies the actual cost currency/valuation basis per held commodity or lot, or an approved scoped BQL position read supplies enough position data to derive it. It must distinguish mixed-cost lots and cash sharing a cost currency; a cost-less aggregate alone does not establish every lot's quote currency. The repository owner and ledger/API owners can clear this dependency. Keep any new read contract in parity across REST, GraphQL and MCP. This note owns the downstream dashboard adaptation, not a duplicate implementation of that shared dependency.

Open Overview with Time **2026-07**, open Valuation details, close with Escape, change Time to **2026-08**, open/close the dialog, then return to July.

| Fixture | Actual, correct July / August values | Expected holding disclosure | Actual disclosure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EUR cost, EUR quotes above | 20 / 99 EUR | priced, July15 / August20 | **No price / At cost**; headline says “1 at cost (no price)” |
| Same EUR cost, change only the three quotes to USD | 10 / 10 EUR, cost fallback | no applicable price / at cost | **Price Jul15 / Aug20**, without the at-cost warning |

The backend's `at_value` policy uses a lot's cost-currency quote and falls back to its cost when no such quote exists. It does not convert these positions to the operating currency or hop through an unrelated USD quote. Returning to July repeats the same disclosure error. The December future quote is present in both models and correctly does not enter the amounts.

## Cause and downstream target

`dashboard/src/features/reports/overview/index.tsx:166–177` passes `primaryCurrency` to `describeLatestNetWorth`. In `overview/lib/net-worth-valuation.ts:250–260`, `priceDatesUpTo` searches only the held commodity/operating-currency pair. Its basis ternary at277–284 then equates no operating-currency price with cost fallback, and an operating-currency price with market valuation. This disagrees with `backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/lot-inventory.ts:134–141`, which explicitly reads `lot.costCurrency` for `at_value`. Price dates, managed-source matching and freshness use the same wrong pairing.

The actual source-control context loaded `ledger._ledgerOwner._ledgerName.index-Dt5Wt5tH.js`.
