## Verification

`/simplify` ran four independent reviews of the m56 diff: reuse, simplification, efficiency and altitude.

**Applied:**
- `cashAccountMembership(accountMeta, roots)` reads its accounts from the meta map, which holds every `open` directive, so the page and loader call it the same way in one line.
- `collectCashAccounts` lost its redundant `isCash` and its unreachable `?? "heuristic"`.
- The local regex escaper was replaced by the shared `escapeFilterRegex` (`common/components/ledger-search-controls/utils.ts`).
- The page names `membershipPending` once.
- The tests use lighter directive fixtures and a meta-map helper.
- `AGENTS.md` now states the per-transaction limit: mixed cash/noncash transactions still report their noncash legs.
- The schema-drift refresh and its `FileError` type fix moved to their own preceding commit.

**Skipped, with reasons:**
- `skipToken` instead of `skip`. It breaks every existing test that mocks `@apollo/client/react` as `{ useQuery }`; the `""` placeholder is never sent.
- A wrapper for the page/loader filter expression. Both are the same single line.
- Unifying the model's row-level cash check with membership inheritance. A child of a declared-cash parent can still appear as a row; this predates m56 and touches statement classification beyond it.
- Overlapping the balance-sheet read with membership, and sharing the directives cache with the accounts page. Small and optional.

Tests, the revert proof and the native journey were rerun on the simplified code. All pass.
