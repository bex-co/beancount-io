# Expose per-commodity cost currency and cost-less balances on balance reads

**Producer for 1031, w21/554 and w21/556 · ledger + backend-v2 (+ IDL) · A1/A2.** Owner decision 2026-10-31 (via `/unblock w4`): supply the missing valuation facts as new fields on the existing balance reads, not as a client-side BQL query.

The dashboard and mobile valuation selectors receive only aggregate `at_value`, `at_cost` and `units` maps per currency. From those they cannot tell (a) which cost currency each held commodity's lots use, so the quote the backend actually applied is unknown ([1031](./blocked/1031.md), [w21/556](../w21/blocked/556.md)), or (b) how much of a commodity is held without a cost when the same commodity is also another lot's cost currency ([w21/554](../w21/blocked/554.md)). `backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/lot-inventory.ts` already reads `lot.costCurrency` when it computes `at_value`, so the facts exist at the producer.

**Scope:** add to the balance reads the clients already call, for each held commodity: its units held without a cost, and its units held at cost grouped by cost currency (enough to name the valuation basis and quote currency per holding, including mixed-cost lots and cash that shares a cost currency). Keep existing fields and amounts unchanged. Ship in REST, GraphQL and MCP together per the repository parity rule and its CI gate; update the OpenAPI snapshot and generated clients. Deploy ledger and backend-v2 before any consumer relies on the field.

**Acceptance:** for the fixtures in 1031 (EUR cost with EUR quotes; same cost with USD-only quotes), w21/554 (1 BTC {USD} + 40 ETH {BTC}; margin VEUR {EUR} with −400 EUR) and w21/556 (500 EUR cash + 10 VTI {EUR}), the new fields let a client name each holding's cost currency and cost-less remainder without heuristics; parity and ledger tests cover them; the deployed revision serves them. Then unblock 1031, w21/554 and w21/556 for their consumer changes.

Sizing: likely more than an hour across ledger, backend-v2 and IDL — promote to a milestone with `/pm promote w4/1119` when picked up.
