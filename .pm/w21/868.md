# A signed-in reader of a ledger with no budgets is told to "Set a spending target for any account" on Home and on Budget, which they can't do

**Severity:** minor (a dead end). **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3:
- `home-screen/components/budget-card.tsx:206-219` chooses `budgetPanelEmptyGuest` vs `budgetPanelEmpty` by guest status only, not write access, and the row pushes `/budget`.
- `budget-screen/budget-screen.tsx:181,245-257` correctly hides + and "Add your first budget" when `!canWrite`, yet keeps "No budgets found — Set a spending target for any account to start tracking against it." with nothing to tap.
- Files' `ledgerEmptyHint` (`ledger-file-browser-screen/index.tsx:812-820`) has the same pattern.

Partly run natively (iPhone 17e): Drawer ▸ Browse ledgers ▸ Explore ▸ `open_ledger/3m`, opened read-only and not starred. Home hides + as expected. The Budget card is below the fold and couldn't be reached by tap. The app was switched back to the QA ledger afterwards. The copy path is from the agent's trace with `useGuest() = null` and `canWrite = false`. w21/293 confirmed the public examples have no budgets.

## Fix

Pass `canWrite` to `BudgetCard`, and use reader copy ("This ledger has no budgets") without the call to action. Switch the Budget screen's description, and Files' hint, to read-only copy when `!canWrite`. Add strings in all 13 locales.

**Acceptance:** a reader never sees the set-a-target prompt. A source case is added.
