# Switching tabs never refreshes a tab that's already open: after an outside change, Home and Transactions keep the old data while a first-visited Accounts tab shows the new figures

**Severity:** major. Within one session the tabs disagree: Home's net worth leaves out an entry that Accounts' balances include, and the only fixes are backgrounding the app or relaunching it. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `mobile/src/components/lazy-tab-screen/index.tsx:15-28` mounts each tab on its first visit and keeps it mounted. The default fetch policy (`src/common/apollo/default-options.ts:14-18`) checks the network once per mount, then serves the cache. The only refresh while the app is open is the foreground hook (`app/(app)/_layout.tsx:21` → `use-foreground-refresh.ts`). No tab screen refetches on focus.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator, English UI, Metro `--clear` at `045715ae1`, on the disposable QA ledger, which was restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`:
1. Launch. Home shows net worth **$17,108.51**, and Transactions shows Aug 28 at the top.
2. Add `2026-10-22 * "Qa20261020 Fresh Probe"` (Groceries 9.99 USD from Assets:Cash) through the API.
3. Tap Home: still **$17,108.51**, and no Fresh Probe row. Tap Transactions: it still starts at **August 28, 2026**.
4. Tap Accounts (first visit this launch): **Cash $39.76** (that is 49.75 − 9.99) and Assets **$17,241.77**, which include the new entry.

w21/024 shipped only the background-and-return refresh; tab focus was listed there as optional. The agent's trace also shows the file editor applying a newer file on focus but refreshing only the error list.

## Fix

On tab focus, refetch the tab's on-screen queries (or bump the ledger revision), throttled to once every N seconds per tab. When the editor adopts a newer file on focus, refresh the ledger's data, not just its errors.

**Acceptance:** after step 2, returning to Home and Transactions shows the new entry without a relaunch. A unit or source case pins the focus refetch.

## Dedupe

Gap in w21/024 (done). Not w21/094 or w21/527 (detail screens). Searched `.pm/**` for refetch, focus, stale, lazy-tab-screen and useFocusEffect.
