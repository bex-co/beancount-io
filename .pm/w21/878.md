# If ledger settings fail to load, Update Budget saves an income target as positive, and the card switches to spending rules

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `add-budget-screen.tsx:256` treats a failed settings read (`metaError`) as settled, enabling Done. Line 288 then passes `incomeRoot: undefined`, and `build-budget-entry.ts:97` writes the number as typed. `groupDirection` follows the latest entry's sign, so the card switches to expense rules.

Not run natively (a failing settings read can't be forced here). Under `tsx` (the agent's proof): Update without `incomeRoot` writes `{"number":"5000","currency":"USD"}` after a history of −4000.00, and the direction goes from −1 to 1.

## Fix

When the income root is unknown, keep the sign of the entry being updated (`params.currentAmount`), or fall back to the default "Income" root.

**Acceptance:** this case writes −5000. A unit case is added.

Not w21/303, 302, 394 or 630. Searched `.pm/**` for incomeRoot together with budget and metaError.
