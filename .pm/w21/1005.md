# No screen has a readable-width cap, and the Picker and valuation bottom sheets stretch the full width on iPad

**Severity:** low to medium (iPad only). **Estimate:** 45 minutes. Owner: mobile.

**Root cause (A3).** A search of `mobile/src` and `mobile/app` finds no screen-level `maxWidth` or centered container; the only caps are `text-input-modal` (400) and `ledger-guard` (300). The `Picker` sheet (`src/components/picker/index.tsx:71-80`) and `valuation-sheet` (`src/components/valuation-sheet/index.tsx:29-40`) both use `justifyContent: "flex-end"` with no width limit on the sheet.

Not run: code level only (no iPad simulator in this session). On a 1024 pt iPad, forms such as Add Budget (the label is capped at `58%`), Open Account and Edit Transaction run edge to edge, list rows put a description about 700 pt from its amount, and the Picker's Cancel and Done sit at opposite screen edges.

**To confirm:** on an iPad, open a date or currency Picker and Edit Transaction.

## Fix

Add a shared content column (`maxWidth` about 640, `alignSelf: "center"`) in the screen scaffold, and give the two sheets `maxWidth: 560` with `alignSelf: "center"`.

**Acceptance:** a render at a 1024 pt window width where the sheet and form columns are at most 640 pt wide.

Searched for "maxWidth", "readable", "Picker" and "iPad": nothing found.
