# Home's headline still counts later entries of the current month, and the Accounts tab gives no hint that later entries exist (follow-up to w21/674)

w21/674 made the Accounts tab and account detail read balances through today (`throughToday`, `0001 - <today>`). Two gaps remain:

- Home's net worth, assets and liabilities come from the monthly series, whose current-month point is the month-end balance. An entry dated later this month still counts on Home but not on the Accounts tab. Example: today 2026-10-22, rent dated 2026-10-28; Home and Accounts differ by the rent. Fix: read Home's headline from the same through-today trial balance, or give the series an as-of-today point.
- The 2026-10-30 owner decision asked for an "Upcoming" note when later entries exist. Needs a cheap probe (a journal read for entries dated after today, limit 1) and a line on the Accounts tab and account detail; one new string in all 13 languages.

**Acceptance:** a future-dated entry changes neither Home's headline nor the Accounts tab's net worth, and a ledger that has one says so.

---

**Blocked 2026-10-30** — triaged at `main` before building, and the first gap is a choice about what a balance series means, which reaches beyond the app. Home's net worth, assets and liabilities come from the balance sheet's monthly series; its current-month point is the balance at the end of the month, so an entry dated later this month counts. The two ways to make Home stop at today differ in who they change:

- (a) Cap the last interval of the series at today in the ledger service (a series point for the month in progress would mean "through today"). One change, correct for Home, but it changes the same series for the dashboard's charts, the CLI and agents, with a ledger deploy first.
- (b) Home replaces its last point with the through-today trial balance (`throughToday`, w21/674). No contract change, but Home's three curves at market, cost and units would each need a trial-balance twin, and its headline could differ from the chart's last point by price-date rounding.

The second gap (a hint that later entries exist) needs a cheap probe read plus a line on the Accounts tab and account detail with one new string in all 13 languages, placed where it cannot be checked on a simulator here.

**Unblock:** the owner picks (a) or (b) for the series, and says whether the "later entries exist" hint is wanted at all. Recommendation: (a), because the dashboard has the same same-month gap, and the hint left out until someone misses it. Who can clear it: the repository owner.
