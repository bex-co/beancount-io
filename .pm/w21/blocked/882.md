# Charts write money in the app language on the axis ("1,5 Tsd. €") and in English beside it ("€1,500.50"), in nine comma-decimal languages

**Severity:** minor. A German reader parses "€1,500.50" as one and a half euros, under ticks that say 1.5 thousand. **Estimate:** 30 minutes, plus a product call. Owner: mobile. A3: `common/number-utils.ts:405-416` (`formatShortMoneyWithCurrency`) follows the app language (decimal comma, currency after), as w21/177 chose. The full amounts beside it go through `groupThousands` and `annotateCurrency`, which are always English (w3/189).

Not run natively (it needs a language switch). Under `tsx` with the real formatters (the agent's proof), in de with EUR:
- The axis reads `0 € · 500 € · 1 Tsd. € · 1,5 Tsd. €`.
- `spendingTotalsCaption` reads `€1,500.50 · €987.65`, and the Budget card's Budget figure reads `€1,500.00`.
- fr, es, ru, uk, bg, sk and ca behave the same way, and pt and nl show `€1,5 mil` / `€1,5K` beside `€1,500.50`.

Screens: Home ▸ Spending (ticks and the w21/418 caption), the Budget card, and the Reports chart.

## Fix

Use one convention per card: either the app language for both ticks and amounts (as the dashboard did in w4/216), or the ledger's convention for both, translating only the suffix words.

**Acceptance:** in de, a card's ticks and amounts use the same separators and symbol position. Unit cases are added.

w21/177 and 360 (axis only), w21/418 (caption only), w4/216 (dashboard). Searched `.pm/**` for Tsd, currencyAfter, spendingTotalsCaption and "decimal comma".

Reconciling a card means either moving all full amounts to the app language (a change to every money string in the app, its VoiceOver text and many tests, and to what the user sees against their ledger files) or moving the axis back to the English convention (undoing w21/177). Neither is a bug fix; the note itself says "plus a product call".

**Unblock:** the owner chooses one convention for money in the app: the app language everywhere, or English everywhere with only compact suffix words translated. Then this is workable in `mobile` `common/number-utils.ts` and its callers. Clearable by the owner only.
