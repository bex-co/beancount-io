# Expense and income accounts are valued at market on Accounts and account detail, while Reports, Spending, Budget and the journal count them at cost

**Severity:** minor. It needs a flow posting that carries a cost, such as donated shares (`Expenses:Charity 5 VOO {400 USD}`). Then the expense figure moves with the share price after the money is spent, and account detail reports spending in a month that had none. **Estimate:** 45 minutes. Owner: mobile.

**Root cause (A3).** The w4/m26 rule is "flows and history stay at cost", but the balance reads value every account at market (`at_value`), Income and Expenses included:
- `mobile/src/screens/accounts-screen/hooks/use-trial-balance.ts:19` and `use-account-report.ts:20` default to `VALUATION_CONVERSION`.
- `account-detail-screen.tsx:160` calls `useAccountReport(ledgerId, account)` with no conversion. `:190-195` takes today's figure from the market trial balance, and `select-account-balance-series.ts:208-241` charts market month-end balances.

Everything else reads at cost: Reports (`use-income-statement.ts:15`), Home Spending (`spending-card.tsx:133`), Budget (`use-budget-actuals.ts:51`), and the account-detail journal itself (`account-detail-screen.tsx:228`).

**Native (iPhone 17e, 2026-10-23).** On the disposable ledger, appended:
- `Expenses:Qa20261023Gifts`: 30.00 USD on 09-12, and donated `5 QAVOO {400.00 USD}` on 09-15;
- QAVOO prices of 420 on 09-30 and 500 on 10-20.

What the screens showed:
- **Accounts:** the Expenses root carries "At market value".
- **Expenses detail:** the header reads "$6,621.49 · At market value", while the journal's latest running balance reads $6,121.49. The $500 gap is the donated shares' rise from 400 to 500.
- **1M:** "+$400.00 (+6.43%)", although no expense was posted after 09-15.

The ledger was restored byte for byte afterwards.

**`tsx` proof (the agent's, with the real engine and selectors), for `Expenses:Gifts`:**
- At cost, `intervalTotals` gives September `{USD: 2030}` and October `{}`; that is what Reports and Spending show.
- At market, `accountHierarchy` gives `{USD: 2530}`.
- The chart series is `[09-30: 2130, 10-23: 2530]`.

## Fix

Read Income and Expenses accounts (category from `accountCategory`) at `BALANCE_CONVERSION` everywhere:
- In account detail, pass the conversion to `useAccountReport`, and take `current` from a trial balance read at cost.
- On Accounts, source the Income and Expenses roots from a trial balance read at cost.

Assets and Liabilities stay at market.

**Acceptance:**
- With the fixture, the Accounts row, the account-detail header, the journal's last balance and 3M all read $2,030.00, and 1M reads +$0.00.
- An Assets account holding the same lot still reads at market.
