# In Persian (right-to-left), the y-axis tick labels that carry a "هزار" (thousand) suffix overflow the axis column: the last glyph is cut off at the plot's left edge and the labels no longer share one right edge

**Severity:** minor (fa, cosmetic; the amounts are still readable). **Estimate:** 30–45 minutes, cause unverified. Owner: mobile. A3: `mobile/src/common/d3/scrollable-axis-chart.tsx:80-90` draws each tick with `SvgText` at `x={axisWidth - 4}` and `textAnchor="end"`, and `income-expense-bar-chart.tsx:127` sizes the column with `axisLabelWidth` (`axis-label-width.ts`: label length × 0.7 × font size + 8, which is generous for these strings). Under a right-to-left layout the labels that contain the Persian suffix word still end past the column edge.

## Repro

`main` at `3730a55fb`, dev build `io.beancount.ios` on iPhone 17e (iOS 27.0), light mode, the QA account's `example` ledger (income-statement series 2015-01 to 2026-10, so the chart scales to $15K).

1. Settings ▸ Language ▸ **فارسی** ▸ Confirm; accept the layout restart ("اکنون دوباره اجرا کن"). The app reopens right-to-left.
2. Reports tab (گزارش‌ها), range **همه** (ALL).
3. Look at the y-axis: the ticks for 5, 10 and 15 thousand read "$۵ هزار", "$۱۰ هزار", "$۱۵ هزار" with a half-drawn extra "ر" at the right end, flush against the plot's left edge (the cut-off sits under the scroller). The zero tick "$۰" ends further left, so the right edges do not line up. The 6M range shows the same on its top tick ("$۱ هزار").

Repeated: the same result on 6M and on ALL, after a fresh restart. Controls: the English and Bulgarian charts (left-to-right, "$15K" and "15 хил. $") have all ticks ending on one right edge with nothing clipped; the Persian zero tick (no suffix word) is not clipped.

A zoomed crop of the ALL view is kept locally under `.tmp/qa-20261104/`.

## Expected / actual

Expected: every tick label ends inside the axis column, on one right edge, with no glyph hidden under the plot. Actual: labels with the suffix word extend about one glyph past the column and are clipped by the scroller.

## Fix

Cause unverified: the candidates are (a) `textAnchor="end"` resolving against the text's own direction for a run containing an RTL word (so the anchor moves to the other side of the string), and (b) the bidi order of "$۱۵ هزار" in a left-to-right `Svg` placing the suffix word's last glyph beyond the measured box. Check by logging the measured width of the rendered `SvgText` for one tick under `I18nManager.isRTL`. Target: keep the tick's end on `x = axisWidth − 4` regardless of the string's direction, for example by wrapping the label in a left-to-right isolate (as `isolateLeadingNumber` already does for the minus sign, w21/883) or by anchoring with an explicit `direction`.

**Acceptance:** in Persian every tick on the Reports and Budget charts ends at the same x inside the axis column with no cut-off glyph; English and the other 11 languages are unchanged. Add a unit case for the tick string handed to `SvgText` in `fa`.

## Blast radius

The Home Spending chart was not looked at in Persian this run. Any other language whose compact suffix is a word (Russian "тыс.", Bulgarian "хил.") is left-to-right and showed no clipping in Bulgarian.

## Dedupe

Related but different: w21/883 (done, the minus sign of a negative Persian tick drew on the wrong side; fixed in `number-utils`, noted there as "not run natively"), w21/177 (done, per-language abbreviations), w21/360 and w2/048 (done, axis column too narrow for long ticks in left-to-right languages).
