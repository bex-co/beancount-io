# A holding whose commodity is also another lot's cost currency drops out of the holdings sheet, so BTC's stale price is never flagged (crypto pairs, margin, foreign-currency debt)

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `mobile/src/common/valuation.ts:285-295` computes `converted = units − market[commodity]`. But the market map's entry for a commodity also holds the value of *other* lots whose cost currency is that commodity, which w21/278 assumed it doesn't.

Native (iPhone 17e at `e47e7641c`, disposable USD ledger, restored byte-for-byte): `Assets:QaCrypto` holds 1 BTC {30000.00 USD} and 40 ETH {0.05 BTC}, with price BTC 60000.00 USD on 2026-08-01 and ETH 0.05 BTC on 2026-10-21. Home ▸ Net Worth's caption reads "At market value · 1 not in total", with no stale-price warning. The Holdings and prices sheet lists "40 ETH · Price Oct 21, 2026" and "2 BTC · Not in total", while the real 1 BTC holding (priced only on Aug 1) isn't listed as priced at all.

Under `tsx`, a margin account with 10 VEUR {50.00 EUR} plus −400 EUR, and EUR priced in USD, shows a phantom priced holding "−550 EUR · Price Jun 1 · Not updated" and a false stale warning.

## Fix

Compute each commodity's priced remainder from its own lots (units minus that commodity's cost-less holdings), not from the market map's per-currency sum, which mixes in other lots' values.

**Acceptance:** the BTC/ETH fixture lists "1 BTC · Price Aug 1 · Not updated" with the warning in the caption, and the margin fixture has no phantom EUR row. Unit cases are added.

**Blocked 2026-10-22** — Still wanted, but it cannot be done from the data `selectValuation` is given. It reads three balance maps (`at_value`, `at_cost`, `units`) per currency. A commodity X that is also the cost currency of other lots (ETH bought at 0.05 BTC) shows up in the market map as its own cost-less units plus those other lots' value, and in the cost map as its cost-less units plus their cost; the two cannot be separated, and X's units (1 BTC) carry no sign of which are held with a cost. The note's fix ("units minus that commodity's cost-less holdings") needs the cost-less part as its own figure, which no current read supplies.

**Unblock:** an approved source for it: a per-commodity cost-less balance from the ledger (a new field on the balance reads, across REST, GraphQL and MCP, with a ledger and backend-v2 deploy before mobile can read it), or a BQL read of positions without a cost that the app may run for every valued total (a performance and ledger-version question).
