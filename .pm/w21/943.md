# A charge and its same-day reversal (net 0) counts as a payment: Next moves, "~" appears, and a missed bill stops showing Overdue

**Severity:** minor to moderate. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile.

**Root cause (A3).**
- `payee-series.ts:183-184,206` sums a payee's entries per day and currency, so a +1.00 card check and its −1.00 reversal become one point of `0`. A $0.00 free-trial entry gives the same point.
- `withoutCounterSign` (`mobile/src/screens/merchants-screen/selectors/detect-recurrence.ts:476-498`) keeps zero amounts on purpose ("zero amounts stay").
- `:523-540` then makes every point an event.

So the zero day:
- becomes `lastPaid` and the base for Next (the fallback at `:697`);
- widens the amount spread (`:423-443`), so the row gets "~".

Not run natively in this pass. `tsx` proof, with Spotify at 11.99 USD on the 5th, Jan–Oct 2026, plus a +1.00/−1.00 pair on 2026-10-15:

| Today | With the pair | Without it (control) |
|---|---|---|
| 10-23 | Next 2026-11-15, "~", last paid 10-15 | Next 2026-11-05, exact amount |
| 11-14 and 11-20 (Nov charge missed) | Overdue: false | Overdue: true |

A single $0 entry anywhere, including the first month, also gives "~".

**Native steps** (disposable ledger):
1. Add "QA SPOTIFY" at 11.99 on the 5th, Jan–Oct, plus +1.00 and −1.00 on Oct 15.
2. Open Merchants ▸ Recurring.

It reads "Monthly · ~$11.99 · Next Nov 15, 2026" instead of "$11.99 · Next Nov 5, 2026".

## Fix

After summing, drop zero-amount points from the event set, in both `detectRecurrence` and `hasEnoughHistory`. Optionally, keep $0 days out of the amount statistics.

**Acceptance:** cases in `src/__tests__/merchant-refunds.test.ts`. With the net-zero day, or with a $0 first month, the verdict matches the control: Next 11-05, an exact amount, and Overdue on 11-14.
