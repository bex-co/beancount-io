# A biweekly series with one payment a day late reads "Twice a month" (w21/530 implemented only exactly-14-day gaps)

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `merchants-screen/selectors/detect-recurrence.ts:561`, `gaps.every((gap) => gap === 14)`. w21/530's fix called for "exactly 14 days (or 14 ± tolerance)", but only exact gaps were implemented.

Not run natively (the agent's `tsx` proof). A sweep of every start day in 2024–25, with one payment shifted by ±1 day, misfires 231/4386 times with 4 payments, 278/5848 with 5 and 252/7310 with 6; 7 or more payments are fine. Example: a 6-payment series starting 2024-01-01 gets "semimonthly" with Next 03-29, where every two weeks from Mar 12 gives Mar 26. Fixture: "Qa20261020 Daycare" at 1850 USD on 2026-01-30, 02-13, 02-27, 03-13 and 03-28. It would read "Twice a month · $1,850.00 · Last paid Mar 28, 2026", and should read "Every 2 weeks".

## Fix

Treat a series as biweekly when every gap is within 14±1 (or ±3) days, with at most one gap off.

**Acceptance:** this fixture is biweekly. A true 1st/15th pattern stays semimonthly. Unit cases are added.
