# Searching two visible words finds nothing unless they sit together, in order, in one field

**Severity:** moderate. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `mobile/src/screens/transactions-screen/utils/plain-search-filter.ts:45` (`quoted`, `:61`) turns the whole input into one phrase pattern (`"sushi\s+dinner"`). The ledger engine then tests that pattern against payee and narration separately (`backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/directive-filter.ts`, `stringPredicate`, about `:651`). Fava treats bare `sushi dinner` as both words (AND).

**Native (iPhone 17e, 2026-10-23).** On the disposable ledger, the Transactions list shows the row "SUSHI PLACE / Dinner". Typing `sushi dinner` in Transactions search shows "No transactions match your search or filters." Typing `sushi` alone lists the row.

**`tsx` proof (the agent's, real helpers and the real `filterDirectives`).** Each search below returns nothing:
- "starbucks coffee" and "coffee starbucks" (Starbucks / "Coffee with Sam")
- "foods whole" and "whole foods groceries" (Whole Foods Market)

Sending the words separately (`"starbucks" "coffee"`) matches. "whole foods" works only because the two words are adjacent in the payee.

## Fix

Add a word-splitting helper used only by the Transactions search (`transactions-screen.tsx:146`). It should:
- split the input on whitespace;
- encode each word with the existing `quoted` logic, applying the `#tag`/`^link` rule per word;
- join the words with a space, which the engine reads as AND.

Keep `toPlainSearchFilter` as a phrase match: `two-posting-query.ts` and `filter-exact-payee.ts` depend on it.

**Acceptance:**
- "sushi dinner" and "dinner sushi" match the payee/narration pair.
- "whole foods" still matches "Whole  Foods".
- The 256-character cap holds per word.
