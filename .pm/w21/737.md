# `close_tree`'s generated child closes aren't enforced: postings after the parent's close are accepted with no error

**Severity:** major (money moves into a closed account, and Notifications says the ledger is healthy). **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: ledger. A3: the snapshot contains the generated `close …Broker:VTI`, but validation never checks postings against it (an explicit child `close` does give E1003).

Production, through `getLedgerErrors` on the disposable ledger (restored byte-for-byte): the QA ledger plus `plugin "beancount.plugins.close_tree"`, opens of `Assets:Qa20261020Broker`, `…Broker:VTI` and `…Cash`, `2024-04-01 close Assets:Qa20261020Broker`, and a 2024-05-01 posting to `Broker:VTI`. `getLedgerErrors` returns **nothing new**. bean-check reports "Invalid reference to inactive account 'Assets:Qa20261020:Broker:VTI'". In the agent's run, the trial balance still shows `Broker:VTI {"USD":"1"}`. Related root cause seen by the agent: with `auto_accounts` plus `close_tree`, an account referenced only by a `close` isn't auto-opened, giving a false E1001 that bean-check doesn't report.

## Fix

Validate against the plugin-generated closes (run the inactive-account check after the plugin pass), and auto-open accounts referenced only by `close`.

**Acceptance:** this fixture reports the inactive-account error at the posting's line, and the auto_accounts variant raises nothing. Unit cases are added.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` for close_tree and auto_accounts together with close.
