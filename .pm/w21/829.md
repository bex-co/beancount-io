# Catalan and Portuguese give "Equity" and "Net Worth" the same label ("Patrimoni net" / "Patrimônio líquido"), so two different figures share one name

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 5 minutes. Owner: mobile. Keys `equity` and `netWorth`: ca "Patrimoni net" (`ca.ts:282`, `:486`), pt "Patrimônio líquido" (`pt.ts:280`, `:484`). Every other locale keeps them apart (de Eigenkapital / Nettovermögen, es Patrimonio / Patrimonio neto).

Not run natively (it needs a language switch). `netWorth` is a Home chart tab (assets − liabilities, `account-charts-card.tsx:289`), and `equity` is the Accounts section header for Equity accounts (`account-table.tsx:546`), usually a negative figure. In ca or pt, a tester sees the same name over two different numbers.

## Fix

ca `netWorth` → "Valor net", the choice the dashboard fix made. pt `netWorth` → "Patrimônio" or "Valor líquido".

**Acceptance:** the two keys differ in ca and pt. A catalog test can flag identical translations of these two keys.
