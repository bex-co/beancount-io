# Slovak: the Transactions filter's "clear account" button is labelled "Vymazať účet", the same words as Settings' "Delete account"

**Severity:** minor (VoiceOver users hear a destructive action). **Estimate:** 5 minutes. Owner: mobile. Key `clearAccount` in `sk.ts:342` is "Vymazať účet", identical to `deleteAccount` (`sk.ts:155`). In English they are "Clear account" and "Delete Account".

Not run natively (it needs a language switch and VoiceOver). The label is the ⊗ button's accessibility label next to the chosen account in Transactions ▸ Filters (`transaction-filters-screen.tsx:411`).

## Fix

"Zrušiť výber účtu" (or "Vymazať filter účtu").

**Acceptance:** sk `clearAccount` differs from `deleteAccount`.
