# In a 3-decimal ledger (KWD), the Accounts tree loses a parent's own sub-cent balance: "Qa20261020 د.ك15.004" over rows of 10.000 and 5.000, with Wallet folded into its Savings child

**Severity:** minor (for ledgers whose operating currency is KWD, BHD, OMR, JOD or TND). The rows don't add up to their parent, and tapping the folded row opens the child, not the account holding the money. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `mobile/src/components/account-list/select-account-list.ts:212-213`. `amountTolerance` uses a flat half-cent (0.005) for any currency the app has a symbol for, though `currencyMinorUnits("KWD")` is 3. A parent's own 0.004 is under the tolerance, so it gets no "(own)" row, and `compressChain` folds the parent into its only child.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator, English UI, Metro `--clear` at `6ce8607ed`, on the disposable QA ledger. Its `operating_currency` was switched to KWD for the probe, then restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`. Fixture: `Assets:Qa20261020:Wallet:Savings` 10.000 KWD, `Assets:Qa20261020:Wallet` 0.004 KWD, `Assets:Qa20261020:Bank` 5.000 KWD. Accounts ▸ Assets ▸ Qa20261020 reads **د.ك15.004**, and its rows read **"Wallet:Savings د.ك10.000"** and **"Bank د.ك5.000"**. The 0.004 appears nowhere, and Wallet's own row is gone.

## Fix

Set the tolerance to `0.5 * 10 ** -max(currencyMinorUnits(currency), recordedScale)`.

**Acceptance:** with this fixture, Wallet shows as its own row (د.ك10.004, with Savings under it, or an "(own) 0.004" row), and the rows sum to the parent. A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

The same fix as w21/451 (done), which covered only currencies with no symbol. Not w21/541, 698 or 805. Searched `.pm/**` for amountTolerance and compressChain together with KWD.
