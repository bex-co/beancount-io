# Open Account's Back skips the "Discard your changes?" prompt after changing only the account type, currency or open date

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `mobile/src/screens/open-account-screen/open-account-screen.tsx:286`. The guard fires only when the name differs from its starting value (`subPath !== seed.subPath`). The account type, currency and date are never counted as changes.

Reproduced natively with w21/809's build; nothing was written. In Accounts ▸ +, change the account type from Assets to **Liabilities** (Choose account type ▸ Done), then tap Back. Accounts appears at once, with no prompt. Editing the name does prompt ("Unsaved Changes — Discard your changes?").

## Fix

Also compare `rootPrefix`, `currency` (once its default is set) and `date` against their starting values.

**Acceptance:** changing only the type, currency or date and tapping Back prompts. An untouched form doesn't. A source case is added.

## Dedupe

Sibling of w21/800 (Profile). Searched `.pm/**` for useUnsavedChangesGuard together with Open Account, and discard.
