# A return to the app within 30 seconds of the last refresh is dropped, not deferred, so an outside change stays hidden until the next return or a relaunch

**Severity:** moderate. Pulling down Notification Center or Control Center, or dismissing a system prompt, also counts as a return and starts the window. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `src/common/apollo/foreground-refresh.ts:22-29`. A return inside the 30-second window does nothing, and nothing runs later to catch up. Combined with w21/837, the screen stays stale.

Not run natively: the app can't be backgrounded and resumed here without a relaunch. The agent drove the real refresher with a fake clock: with returns at 10 s and 35 s, it refreshed only at 10 s, and the 35 s return (25 s after the first) was skipped.

## Fix

When a return falls inside the window, schedule one refresh for when the window ends instead of dropping it. Optionally, ignore active↔inactive flickers that never reach background.

**Acceptance:** the second return in this scenario triggers a refresh once the window ends. A unit case with a fake clock is added.

## Dedupe

The 30-second limit came from w21/024's fix (done), and no item covers the dropped return. Searched `.pm/**` for foreground-refresh, AppState and throttle.
