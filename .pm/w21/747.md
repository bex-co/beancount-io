# Cash flow draws the synthetic "Savings" (surplus), "Other" and "Refunds" nodes beside real categories with the same name

**Severity:** minor (two identical labels; `Expenses:Savings`, `Expenses:Other` and `Income:Refunds` are common). **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: w0/034's disambiguation compares only income names with expense names (`reports-screen/selectors/sankey-data.ts:436-444`), and the synthetic nodes (`:467-550`) use the translated labels "Savings", "Other" and "Refunds" with no check against the categories already drawn.

Native (iPhone 17e, Metro `--clear` at `3263cb449`; disposable ledger restored byte-for-byte; today 2026-10-23): the QA ledger plus October entries: `Income:Qa20261020:Salary` −3,000.00, `Expenses:Qa20261020:Savings` 500.00 and `Expenses:Qa20261020:Rent` 300.00 (all against `Assets:Qa20261020:Checking`). Reports ▸ **1M** ▸ Cash flow: the right column reads "**Savings**" (red, the expense category), "Rent", then a second "**Savings**" (blue, the 2,200 surplus). Under `tsx` (the agent's proof), the nodes are `Qa20261020[source], Savings[operating], Rent[operating], Savings[savings]`. The same happens for an `Expenses:…:Other` category next to the folded "Other", and for `Income:…:Refunds` next to the synthetic "Refunds".

## Fix

When a synthetic label collides with a drawn category, suffix the synthetic one ("Savings (surplus)", "Other (smaller)", "Refunds (netted)") or the category ("Savings (expenses)"), as w0/034 does for income vs expense.

**Acceptance:** with this fixture, the two nodes are distinguishable, and the Other and Refunds variants likewise. Unit cases are added.
