# New Transaction refuses an amount that starts with a decimal comma (",5"), though ".5" works (gap in w21/075)

**Severity:** minor. On comma-decimal devices (de, fr, es, ru…), the decimal pad's separator key is ",", so typing it first is natural. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `postings-utils.ts:121` turns only a leading `.` into `0.`. A leading `,` fails `^-?\d+,\d+$` and parses to null.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator, English UI, Metro `--clear` at `fca74a1ca`; the draft was discarded. New Transaction ▸ row 1 amount ▸ paste `,5`. The row shows a red ",5", Remainder shows "—", and **"Use digits and one decimal point, like 1234.56"** appears. Pasting `.5` works. Under `tsx` (the agent's proof), `",5"`, `"-,5"` and `"，5"` all parse to null, while `".5"`, `"0,5"` and `"٫۵"` give 0.5.

## Fix

Normalise a leading comma (ASCII or fullwidth) the same way as a leading dot, for example `/^(-?)[.,，](?=\d)/` → `$10.`.

**Acceptance:** ",5" and "-,5" parse as 0.5 and −0.5. A unit case is added.

Gap in w21/075 (done; leading "."). Not w21/031, w21/463 or w21/514 (other comma cases). Searched `.pm/**` for ",5" and "leading comma".
