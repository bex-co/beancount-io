# The sellgains plugin never flags a sale whose price disagrees with its proceeds: Notifications says "healthy" where bean-check reports an error

**Severity:** moderate. **Estimate:** 45 minutes. Owner: ledger. A3: the engine's native `sellgains` returns no errors, even when run directly with `runPlugin("sellgains")`. The service lists the name (`plugins/collect-plugins.ts:143`) but has no compensating check like `one-commodity.ts`.

Proven on `main` (`053f4eb84`) against Beancount 3.2.3 `bean-check` (the agent's run). Selling 1 HOOL {10.00 USD} @ **50.00** USD, with Cash 12.00 and Gains −2.00, makes `bean-check` report at line 9 "Invalid price vs. proceeds/gains: (50.00 USD) vs. (12.00 USD)"; the service reports `[]`. The fees-counted variant is missed too. In the app, Notifications would say "Ledger is healthy".

## Fix

Add a service-side `validate_sell_gains` port (Beancount's tolerance rules included), or fix it upstream in rustledger.

**Acceptance:** this fixture reports the error at line 9, and a consistent sale stays clean. Unit cases are added.
