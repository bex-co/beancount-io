# Changing the payee to one with no history, or clearing it, keeps the previous payee's filled-in accounts

**Severity:** minor. The harm is the same as w21/411: the entry saves against the previous payee's card and category. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile.

**Needs an owner's call.** w21/411's resolution says a payee with no history should "behave as before". This may be a deliberate leftover.

**Root cause (A3).**
- `mobile/src/screens/multi-postings-transaction/payee-seed.ts:62` returns `null` when the new payee has no history.
- The screen then leaves the postings alone (`multi-postings-transaction-screen.tsx:579-592`), even when they still hold exactly the last fill (`lastPayeeSeedRef`).
- An empty payee never requests a fill at all (`:815`).

Not run natively in this pass. `tsx` proof:
1. "TRADER JOES" fills `[Liabilities:CreditCard:Visa, Expenses:Food:Groceries]`.
2. Picking "Corner Deli" next, with `lastSeed` set to that fill, returns `null`.
3. The form keeps Visa / Groceries.

**Native steps:**
1. On the QA ledger, Payee → TRADER JOES; the accounts fill as Visa / Groceries.
2. Payee → clear → paste "Corner Deli".

The postings still show Visa / Groceries.

## Fix

When the accounts still equal `lastSeed` and the new payee is empty or has no usable history, put back the opening accounts (`initialDraft.postings`) instead of returning `null`.

**Acceptance:**
- After TRADER JOES → Corner Deli, or after clearing the payee, the postings are back on the defaults.
- An account the user edited after the fill is kept.

## Dedupe

Only w21/411 is related, and it excluded this case. Searched for "no history", "new payee", "cleared payee", "seedPostingsFromPayee" and "lastPayeeSeedRef".

**Unblocked 2026-10-30** — Owner decision: a payee with no history, or an emptied payee, resets an untouched fill (accounts still equal to the last fill) to the opening accounts; an account the user edited is kept. This reverses w21/411's "behave as before" for that case. Change in `payee-seed.ts` and the screen's seed call, with unit cases for untouched, edited and cleared. Mobile-only.
