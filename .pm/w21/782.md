# Account detail rounds an 18-decimal holding through a float: "1.234567890123460000 QAETH", while Accounts reads "1.234567890123456789 QAETH" (w21/594 left this screen out)

**Severity:** minor. The same balance shows two different figures one tap apart. **Estimate:** 25 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the headline (`account-detail-screen.tsx:272`) and journal rows (`components/account-entry-row.tsx:148,157`) format a parsed float with `formatUnits`. `formatOtherChanges` (`selectors/select-account-journal.ts:345-348`) ignores the `Holding.exact` string that `amountIn` sets. Only `formatHolding`, used by the Accounts row, reads `exact`.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator, English UI, Metro `--clear` at `2dffe6ff5`, on the disposable QA ledger, which was restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`. Fixture: `2026-01-01 commodity QAETH`, `open Assets:Qa20261020Eth`, and `2026-10-05 * "Qa20261020 Exchange"` with `Assets:Qa20261020Eth 1.234567890123456789 QAETH` against `Equity:Qa20261020Opening`. The Accounts row reads **1.234567890123456789 QAETH**. Tapping it, the headline, its change line, the Oct 5 day header, the row amount and its "Balance:" all read **1.234567890123460000 QAETH**. Under `tsx` (the agent's proof), an "other currency" leg of 0.123456789123456789 ETH in a USD-read account prints `+0.123456789123457000 ETH`.

## Fix

Carry `exact` on journal rows and through `otherChanges`, and format them with `formatExactUnits`. When the chart is at rest, the headline prints the latest point's exact string.

**Acceptance:** with this fixture, every figure on the account detail screen reads 1.234567890123456789 QAETH. Unit cases are added.
