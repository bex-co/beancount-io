# The chart's change percentage has no thousands separators: "−$15,000.00 (−10471.20%)"

**Severity:** trivial (cosmetic). **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `common/d3/change-percent.ts:32` formats with `rounded.toFixed(2)`, with no grouping and no cap, next to a grouped money figure.

Not run natively. Under `tsx` (the agent's proof), the QA ledger's Liabilities (about −$143.25 at September's close) plus a −$15,000.00 October card purchase gives "−$15,000.00 (**−10471.20%**)". A card going from −$0.50 to −$1,500.50 reads "(−300000.00%)".

## Fix

Format the percentage with the locale's grouping, and cap or drop it beyond a threshold (e.g. "> 999%"), as most finance apps do.

**Acceptance:** large percentages are grouped or capped in every locale. A unit case is added.

Searched `.pm/**` for formatChangePercent and toFixed together with percent.
