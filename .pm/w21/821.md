# VoiceOver's Budget chart summary reads unrounded float sums: "−$0.0000000000000036", "$0.3", "$222.6"

**Severity:** low (VoiceOver only). **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `budget-screen/selectors/budget-labels.ts:285-290`. `money()` sums actuals and budgets as floats and passes them to `formatShortMoneyWithCurrency` with no `roundMoney`; w21/597 fixed this for the Reports chart only. Separately, `shortNumber` keeps a single decimal below 1K, so a 222.58 budget is spoken as "$222.6", the string w21/807's own resolution quotes.

Not run natively (VoiceOver). Under `tsx` (the agent's proof), actuals [10.10, 20.20, −30.30] give `"-$0.0000000000000036"`, actuals [0.1, 0.2, 0] give `"$0.3"`, and budgets summing to 222.58 give `"$222.6"`. Fixture: `2026-08-01 custom "budget" Expenses:Qa20261020Food "monthly" 100.00 USD`, with spending of 10.10 on Aug 10, 20.20 on Sep 10 and −30.30 on Oct 5.

## Fix

Round each sum with `roundMoney(sum, currency)`, as the Reports chart does, and speak full money values rather than the short axis format.

**Acceptance:** this fixture is spoken as "$0.00" actual, and 222.58 as "$222.58". A unit case is added.
