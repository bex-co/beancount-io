# Home's chart tabs: the selected Net Worth / Assets / Liabilities pill is nearly invisible (1.08:1 in light, 2.07:1 in dark)

**Severity:** minor (accessibility; the selected state of the first control on Home). **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `components/segmented-pages/index.tsx:95-97` fills the active tab with `black20`, while the card behind it is `controlFill` (`components/dashboard-card/index.tsx:27`). Commit `5ee213286` (2026-08-17) changed the card fill and brought the two together. With the repo's `contrastRatio` on `src/common/theme/palette.ts`: light `#eeece2` on `#f6f4ec` = **1.08:1**, dark `#585a4c` on `#282a21` = **2.07:1**. WCAG 1.4.11 asks 3:1 for a selected-state indicator. The only other cue is the label colour (`text01` vs `black80`, about 1.9:1 apart).

Native (iPhone 17e, light mode): Home's "Net Worth" tab sits on a pill that is barely distinguishable from the card. Tapping "Assets" moves it, but the change is hard to see. Contrast was measured from the palette, not from pixels.

## Fix

Use a fill that reaches 3:1 against `controlFill` in both themes (for example `surface01`/white with a hairline border in light, or `black40` in dark), or add an underline.

**Acceptance:** the active chart tab has at least 3:1 against its card in light and dark. A contrast test covers the pair.
