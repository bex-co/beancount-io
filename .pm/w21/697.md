# In a JPY ledger, a pasted "1,500" becomes 1.500 JPY: a three-digit comma group is read as decimals even for a zero-decimal currency

**Severity:** minor (money entered 1000× too small for JPY, KRW and VND users pasting from receipts or bank apps). **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `parseExactAmount` (`multi-postings-transaction/postings-utils.ts:98`) treats a single comma group as a decimal comma, whatever the currency. "1,500" becomes `1.500`, and a German-style "1.500" stays `1.500`. Add Budget uses the same parser (`budget-screen/build-budget-entry.ts:86`). w21/031 kept "1,250 = 1.250" as the general rule, but a currency with no minor unit can't have three decimals, so for it the group can only be thousands.

Native (iPhone 17e, fresh Metro `--clear` at `4d9abc812`; disposable ledger with its `operating_currency` line swapped for the probe, then restored byte-for-byte; today 2026-10-23): `option "operating_currency" "JPY"`. Home ▸ + ▸ Enter new transaction, with "1,500" pasted into Assets:Bank:Checking's amount. The row shows "1,500" with a "+" badge, while the auto leg Expenses:Food:Groceries reads **"1.500"** and Remainder reads "✓ ¥0". Done is enabled. Under `tsx` (the agent's proof), the saved postings are `-1.500 JPY` / `1.500 JPY`, and Home shows them as "¥2". Add Budget turns "30,000" JPY into `30.000`. Discarded without saving.

## Fix

Pass the currency's scale to the parser. For a 0-decimal currency, read `,ddd` and `.ddd` groups as thousands; refuse the input when that is ambiguous. More generally, refuse a fraction longer than the currency's scale instead of writing it.

**Acceptance:** in a JPY ledger, "1,500" and "1.500" save as 1500 JPY, or are refused as ambiguous, never as 1.5. USD behaviour from w21/031 is unchanged. Add Budget is covered. Unit cases are added.
