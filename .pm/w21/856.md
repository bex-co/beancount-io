# Ask AI is never told today's date or time zone, and a receipt with no date is saved under the server's UTC date (tomorrow in a US evening, yesterday in a NZ morning)

**Severity:** moderate. "How much did I spend this month?" depends on the model's guess at the date, and a receipt can be filed on the wrong day. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: backend-v2, plus clients. A3:
- `backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/features/ai-agent/service/agent-handler/beancount-agent.ts:16` (and the read-only prompt below it) is fixed text with no date or time zone, and `self-hosted-agent-handler.ts` adds none.
- The mobile client sends only `{ledgerId, sessionId}` and `Accept-Language` (`mobile/src/screens/agent-screen/use-agent-chat.ts:83`).
- `insert-receipt-transaction-tool.ts:66` falls back to `new Date().toISOString().slice(0, 10)`, which is the UTC date.

Not run natively: Ask AI is disabled in the mobile build (`config.features.agentChat` false), and no AI quota was spent. The agent's proof ran the same expression with node and a fixed clock:
- `TZ=America/Los_Angeles`, 2026-10-23 17:30 local, writes **2026-10-24**.
- `TZ=Pacific/Auckland`, 2026-10-24 08:30 local, writes **2026-10-23**.

## Fix

Have clients send their local date and time zone with each agent request. The agent route should put them in the instructions and use the local date as the receipt-date fallback, across every surface that reaches the agent (parity).

**Acceptance:** the agent's instructions carry the client's date, and an undated receipt is filed under the client's local date. Unit cases cover both time zones.
