# `onecommodity` ignores Beancount's opt-outs (`onecommodity: FALSE`, multi-currency opens, the account regex) and reports the same account twice

**Severity:** minor (false errors users can't clear the documented way). **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: ledger. A3: rustledger's native `onecommodity` doesn't implement `skip_accounts` from `beancount/plugins/onecommodity.py`. Its errors pass through `engine.ts:714-720` unreconciled.

Proven with the real engine under ts-node (in-process snapshot, `LedgerDataService.getErrors`/`getTrialBalance`/`getAccountJournal`) against Beancount 3.2.3 `bean-check`/`bean-query` (the agent's run). `plugin "beancount.plugins.onecommodity" "Expenses:Qa20261020:Travel"`; an `Assets:Qa20261020:Cash` opened with metadata `onecommodity: FALSE`; an `Assets:Qa20261020:Multi` opened `USD,EUR`; and Travel and Broker (VTI and VOO) postings. bean-check reports one error, on Travel (with no regex, Broker too). The service reports five, with `source: null`: Cash twice, plus Multi and Broker.

## Fix

Honour the three opt-outs and report each account once, with its location.

**Acceptance:** this fixture matches bean-check. A unit case per opt-out is added.

Searched `.pm/**` for onecommodity.
