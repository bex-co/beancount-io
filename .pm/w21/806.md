# Budgets on one account in two currencies cancel each other's status: both cards go grey and unjudged, and each lists the other's spending as "Not in total" (side effect of w21/709)

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `standingStatus` (`budget-screen/selectors/select-budget-card-stats.ts:61`) withholds a status whenever another currency appears in "Not in total". It doesn't check whether this account has its own budget in that currency.

Not run natively: the Travel cards sit below the fold on the Budget screen, which this automation can't scroll. Under `tsx` (the agent's proof), Travel with budgets of 300 USD and 250 EUR (both from 2026-10-01), and $100 plus €50 spent in October, returns `status: null, unjudged: true` for both cards. Without the sibling currency, both are "below". The USD card also says "Not in total: 50.00 EUR", which the EUR card already counts.

## Fix

Leave out of "Not in total", and out of the unjudged decision, any currency the same account budgets separately.

**Acceptance:** both cards read "Below target", and neither lists the other's currency. A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

Side effect of w21/709 (done). Searched `.pm/**` for 709, standingStatus, and "Not in total" together with budget.
