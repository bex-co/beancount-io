# Account detail's day header totals only the first page's rows when a day runs past 20 entries: "October 22, 2026 · −$80.00" for a −$100.00 day

**Severity:** minor to moderate. The wrong total is on first paint, above the fold. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `account-detail-screen/selectors/select-account-journal.ts:413-432` (`groupAccountJournalRowsToSections`) sums only the loaded rows, and the journal pages 20 rows at a time (`use-account-journal.ts`).

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator, English UI, Metro `--clear` at `51bc7dccd`, on the disposable QA ledger, which was restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`. Fixture: `Assets:Qa20261020Cash` funded with 500.00 USD on 2026-10-01, then 25 entries "QA Cafe 1…25" of −4.00 USD on 2026-10-22. Accounts ▸ Qa20261020Cash: the header reads $400.00, and the first section reads "**October 22, 2026 · −$80.00**". The day's 25 entries total −$100.00.

## Fix

Treat the last loaded day as partial while more pages remain: hide its total, or keep fetching until the day is complete.

**Acceptance:** this day's header reads −$100.00, or shows no total until it's complete. A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

Not w21/163 or 784 (rounding), nor w21/613 or 419 (paging loops). Searched `.pm/**` for day total together with page and partial.
