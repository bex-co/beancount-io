# Merchant detail's "scheduled" and "at the recorded rates" lines are symbol-checked apart from the totals, so two currencies both read "$" (or "¥")

**Severity:** minor (brings back w21/535's ambiguity through lines added by w21/796 and w21/725). **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `merchant-detail-screen.tsx:406-418` calls `formatMerchantTotals` separately for `totalLines`, `scheduledLines` and `pricedLines`. Each call checks shared symbols only within its own list (`format-merchant-total.ts:58-66`).

Not run natively (it needs a multi-currency, partly scheduled fixture). Under `tsx` in a EUR ledger (the agent's proof):
- Paid USD 100 with CAD 50 scheduled gives "$100.00" and "$50.00 scheduled".
- JPY with CNY scheduled gives "¥1,500" and "¥50.00".
- Paid CAD 150, priced as USD 110, gives "$150.00" and "$110.00 at the recorded rates".

## Fix

Pass the union of currencies across all three lists to each call, for example as an `others` parameter on `formatMerchantTotals`.

**Acceptance:** in each case the two lines' currencies are distinguishable. A unit case is added. (Fixing w21/896 alone removes only the "$" cases.)
