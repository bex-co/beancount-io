# A bill paid late shows Overdue right after it was paid

**Severity:** moderate to high. The false state is user-visible on Merchants and Home. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. This is a regression of w21/944 (`17385fd26`).

**Root cause (A3).** In `monthDayAnchor` (`mobile/src/screens/merchants-screen/selectors/detect-recurrence.ts`, about `:285-306`), an event more than 3 days off the due day is now skipped with `continue`, and `lastDue` stays at the last on-anchor event. If the newest payment is the late one, `nextExpected` is computed from the previous month's due date.

Not run natively in this pass. `tsx` proof (the agent's, with the real selectors): 9 monthly payments on the 1st (Jan to Sep), then the 10th paid on 2026-10-08, with today 2026-10-23.
- **Now:** `lastPaid 2026-10-08`, `nextExpected 2026-10-01`, `isOverdue: true`.
- **Before the fix:** `nextExpected 2026-11-08`, `isOverdue: false`.

A payment on the 6th gives the same wrong result. A series of 10 with a payment on 09-19 gives `next 2026-10-01` instead of `2026-10-20`. I confirmed the skip in the code (`if (++outliers > allowedOutliers) return null; continue;` before `lastDue` is advanced).

**Native steps:** on the disposable ledger, add a monthly "QA Spotify" on the 1st for nine months and then a payment on the 8th of the tenth month. Merchants ▸ Recurring shows the merchant as overdue with the next date in the past.

## Fix

An off-anchor event later than the last on-anchor event should still advance `lastDue`: map it to the nearest due date, and treat it as an outlier only when it falls in a month already counted or before the previous anchor.

**Acceptance:** the series above returns `nextExpected '2026-11-01'` or later and `isOverdue false`. The extra-charge and month-paid-on-the-10th cases from w21/944 and w21/459 stay green.
