# The balance chart's dashed "start" line and VoiceOver's "From" figure use the window's first point, while the change row measures from the opening balance

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3 (code-level at `main` `5743f62d0`, proven under `tsx`): `common/d3/interactive-line-chart.tsx:570` (`baselineY = pointYs[0]`) and `common/d3/chart-accessibility.ts:46` (`startValue = numbers[0]`), whereas the change row and `chartPointText` (line 63) measure from `balanceSeriesBaseline`, the close before the window.

Scenario: 10,000 USD of opening balance into Checking on 2026-04-15 and a 5,000 bonus on 05-10. Home ▸ Net Worth ▸ 6M reads "$15,000.00 · +$5,000.00 (+50.00%)" in green, while the curve lies flat on the dashed start line. VoiceOver says "From MAY 2026, $15,000.00, to OCT 2026, $15,000.00…" and then "+$5,000.00 (+50.00%) since start". w21/316's own example has the same gap ("From Jan 2026, $1,000" … "+$400.00 since start"). w4/036 noted the dashed line as a known leftover, but no item tracks it.

Not run natively (VoiceOver).

## Fix

Use the same baseline (`balanceSeriesBaseline`) for the dashed line and the "From" sentence.

**Acceptance:** with that fixture, the dashed line sits at $10,000 (or the change is measured from May everywhere), and VoiceOver's From and "since start" agree. Unit cases are added.

**Unblocked 2026-10-30** — Owner decision ("follow Fava", revised after a product review the same day): the change is measured from the opening balance, the balance before the window, as Fava does. The change row and VoiceOver's "From" use that baseline (`balanceSeriesBaseline`). The y-domain stays auto-fit to the plotted points: widening it to include the opening flattens the curve and opens ALL at zero on every balance chart, a cost the mismatch does not justify. The dashed start line is drawn at the opening balance only when that value lies inside the plotted domain; otherwise it is hidden (or the opening balance is named beside the chart), so the line never disagrees with the change row. Unit cases cover the fixture (10,000 opening, 5,000 bonus, flat 15,000 curve). Mobile-only.
