# A pad used for one currency still hides a later failing balance in another currency (w21/675 gap)

**Severity:** major (a failed reconciliation reads as passing). **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: ledger. A3: `pad-recheck.ts:13-40` and `:77` key pads by account only (`padKey` ignores currency). A pad that padded USD therefore counts as "used", and the recheck never runs for the account's EUR balances. rustledger's own pass misses the EUR failure in the same way w21/675 described.

Proven with the real engine under ts-node, with `bean-check` 3.2 as the reference:

```
2026-01-02 * "dep"
  Assets:A  50.00 EUR
  Equity:Open  -50.00 EUR
2026-02-01 pad Assets:A Equity:Open
2026-02-02 balance Assets:A  100.00 USD
2026-02-02 balance Assets:A  50.00 EUR
2026-02-03 * "spend"
  Assets:A  -10.00 EUR
  Expenses:F  10.00 EUR
2026-02-04 balance Assets:A  50.00 EUR
```

bean-check reports "Balance failed for 'Assets:A': expected 50.00 EUR != accumulated 40.00 EUR" (the 02-04 line). The engine reports no errors, and `getLedgerAccountJournal` gives the 02-04 row no Off-by. Not on production yet (the ledger service hasn't been redeployed).

## Fix

Key "used" by account and currency, and recheck the currencies a pad didn't fill.

**Acceptance:** this fixture reports the EUR failure, and w21/675's single-currency case is unchanged. A unit case is added.

Gap in w21/675 (done). Searched `.pm/**` for padKey and pad together with currency.
