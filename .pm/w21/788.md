# VoiceOver reads a Reports category as "N% of total" when N is its share of gross spending, not of the total the card shows

**Severity:** low (accessibility). When a category is net-refunded in the period, the spoken percentage contradicts the headline above the rows. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: since w21/370, the share comes from `roundedShares`/`shareBase` (`reports-screen/selectors/select-breakdown-rows.ts:56-87`), which divides by the sum of the positive rows only. The row's accessibility value still uses `breakdownRowShare: "{{amount}}, {{percent}}% of total"` (`translations/en.ts:856`, the same wording in the other locales). The card's headline is the net total (`components/category-breakdown.tsx:221-223, 310-311, 375-377`).

Not run natively: VoiceOver can't be driven here, and the Expenses card is below the fold. Under `tsx`, the agent ran `selectRangedAccountTree` → `topNWithOther` → `roundedShares` → `breakdownRowAccessibilityValue` on Groceries 300, Dining 100 (both under Food), Shop 200 and Tv −500. The headline total is **100**, and the Food row is spoken as **"$400, 67% of total"**. 67% is Food's share of the 600 of positive spending, not of the 100 shown.

## Fix

Say what the share is of: "{{percent}}% of spending" for expenses and "of earnings" for income, in all 13 locales. Alternatively, drop "of total" when refunds net into the headline.

**Acceptance:** the spoken wording names the actual base. A unit case covers a net-refunded period.

## Dedupe

w21/370, w21/373 and w21/039 (done) changed the share's base but not its spoken wording. Searched `.pm/**` for breakdownRowShare, "of total", shareBase and roundedShares.
