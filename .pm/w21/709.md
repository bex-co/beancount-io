# Budget hides spending in other currencies: €250 on a USD Travel budget reads "Actual $0.00 · Below target" with no "not in total" note

**Severity:** minor (a budget looks safe while the account is spending in another currency). **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the budget actuals are read in `units` (`budget-screen/hooks/use-budget-actuals.ts:24`), and `resolveCurrencyBalance` (`:139`) keeps only the budget's currency. The card (`budget-group-card.tsx`) and Home's budget panel have no "not in total" line, unlike Reports (w21/038), Home and Accounts. A purchase posted in EUR to Expenses:Travel, or one priced with `@@` in USD but booked in EUR, simply vanishes from the budget.

Native (iPhone 17e, Metro `--clear` at `dc37649ce`; disposable ledger restored byte-for-byte; today 2026-10-23): the QA ledger plus `Assets:Qa20261020Wise` opened with 500.00 EUR, `2026-10-01 custom "budget" Expenses:Qa20261020Travel "monthly" 300.00 USD`, and `2026-10-10 * "QA Hotel Paris"` with Expenses:Qa20261020Travel **250.00 EUR**. Home ▸ the Travel budget row ▸ Budget card reads "Budget $222.58 · Actual **$0.00** · Variance −$222.58", with a green "**Below target**" and an empty bar. Nothing on the card mentions the €250. Under `tsx` (the agent's proof), `selectBudgetCardStats` returns `{actual: 0, status: "below", favorable: true, progressPercent: 0}`, while `selectRangedAccountTree` for the same data returns `notInTotal [{Travel, 450 EUR}]` (with a second `@@` purchase).

## Fix

Show other-currency spending on the card and Home's panel ("Not in total: €250.00"), as Reports does. Optionally, convert at value when prices exist and say so. Don't call the budget "Below target" while unconverted spending exists.

**Acceptance:** with this fixture, the card discloses €250.00 and doesn't show a plain green "Below target". A USD-only budget is unchanged. A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

Budget counterpart of w21/038 (done; Reports). Searched `.pm/**` for resolveCurrencyBalance, budget together with "other currency", and "not in total" together with budget.
