# Home's headline still counts later entries of the current month, and the Accounts tab gives no hint that later entries exist (follow-up to w21/674)

w21/674 made the Accounts tab and account detail read balances through today (`throughToday`, `0001 - <today>`). Two gaps remain:

- Home's net worth, assets and liabilities come from the monthly series, whose current-month point is the month-end balance. An entry dated later this month still counts on Home but not on the Accounts tab. Example: today 2026-10-22, rent dated 2026-10-28; Home and Accounts differ by the rent. Fix: read Home's headline from the same through-today trial balance, or give the series an as-of-today point.
- The 2026-10-30 owner decision asked for an "Upcoming" note when later entries exist. Needs a cheap probe (a journal read for entries dated after today, limit 1) and a line on the Accounts tab and account detail; one new string in all 13 languages.

**Acceptance:** a future-dated entry changes neither Home's headline nor the Accounts tab's net worth, and a ledger that has one says so.
