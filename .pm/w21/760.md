# Brand logos miss payees typed with a curly apostrophe ("Trader Joe’s", which iOS Smart Punctuation inserts)

**Severity:** minor (any payee typed on iPhone with an apostrophe loses its logo app-wide). **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `common/brand-matcher/index.ts:277` (`matchBrand`) lowercases the text but doesn't fold quotes, and the brand keys (`:45`, `:54`) use a straight `'`. `merchantKey` has folded quotes since w21/519, but the matcher doesn't. Transactions, Home and merchant detail use the same matcher.

Native (iPhone 17e, Metro `--clear` at `62882d0ec`; disposable ledger restored byte-for-byte; today 2026-10-23): "Qa20261020 Trader Joe’s" (U+2019) on 2026-10-05 and "Qa20261020 McDonald's" (straight) on 10-06. Merchants ▸ search "Qa20261020": **McDonald's shows its logo**, while **Trader Joe’s shows the generic glyph**. The base ledger's "TRADER JOES" shows the Trader Joe's logo on the same screen. Under `tsx` (the agent's proof), the curly spellings of Lowe’s, Wendy’s, Domino’s, Peet’s Coffee and Applebee’s also return null. A merchant whose merged display spelling is curly loses its logo entirely.

## Fix

Fold curly quotes (and other apostrophe variants) before matching, the same as `merchantKey`.

**Acceptance:** "Trader Joe’s" and "Trader Joe's" both show the logo. A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

Not w21/172, 270, 333, 407 or w4/031 (matching rules), or w21/519 (the payee field). Searched `.pm/**` for matchBrand together with apostrophe, and U+2019.
