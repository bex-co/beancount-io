# Entries flagged U are left out of Home's net worth, assets and liabilities, while the Accounts tab counts them

**Severity:** minor (wrong numbers, rare input). **Estimate:** 20 minutes, plus a scope call. Owner: ledger. A3: `backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/interval-series.ts:386` (`accountBalanceSeries`) drops transactions flagged `U`, but `account-tree.ts` (`accountHierarchy`, behind the Accounts trial balance) doesn't. The filter mirrors Fava, where it stops the `unrealized` plugin's entries from being counted twice under market valuation.

Not run natively. With the real rustledger parser and the ledger's own functions under `tsx` (the agent's proof), a `2026-10-10 U "Qa20261020 dinner"` posting 250.00 USD from `Liabilities:Qa20261020Card` gives a Home liabilities series of `{}` ($0.00) and an unchanged net worth, while the Accounts Liabilities root reads `{"USD":"-250"}`.

## Fix

Make Home's series and Accounts' tree agree: stop filtering `U` in the series (as w5/083 did for the CLI), or filter it in both. Decide the scope with the `unrealized` plugin in mind.

**Acceptance:** with this fixture, Home's Liabilities and Accounts agree. A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

Same root cause as w5/083 (CLI only), not a regression. Searched `.pm/**` for FLAG_UNREALIZED and "U-flag".
