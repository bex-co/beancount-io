# A budget whose cadence changed back-fills the first entry over periods it never covered: Monthly $300 set on Oct 21, then Yearly, reads "Budget $2,922.95 · Below target" for $400 spent

**Severity:** minor (an overspent budget reads as comfortably under). **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `budget-screen/selectors/budget-selectors.ts:542-555` treats the first directive as in effect from the start of the *chart* period that contains it. The chart uses the cadence of today's entry (yearly), so a Monthly budget set on Oct 21 is back-filled to Jan 1. w21/301 meant "a budget set on the 21st is a budget for that month" and only tested a single cadence.

Native (iPhone 17e, Metro `--clear` at `b1f7c144a`; disposable ledger restored byte-for-byte; today 2026-10-23): `2026-10-21 custom "budget" Expenses:Qa20261020Gym "monthly" 300.00 USD`, then `2026-10-22 … "yearly" 3600.00 USD`, plus a 400.00 USD expense on 2026-10-05. Home's Gym budget row ▸ Budget card reads "**Yearly · Budget $2,922.95 · Actual $400.00 · Variance −$2,522.95 · Below target**" in green. The expected figure is about $222.95 (Oct 21–22 at the monthly rate plus the yearly rate from Oct 22), Above target. Under `tsx` (the agent's proof), the Monthly→Quarterly and Weekly→Monthly switches over-budget the same way.

## Fix

Prorate each directive from its own date at its own cadence (as w21/301 intends), regardless of the chart's cadence.

**Acceptance:** this fixture reads about $222.95 and Above target, and single-cadence behaviour is unchanged. Unit cases per switch are added.
