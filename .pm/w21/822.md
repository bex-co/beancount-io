# Home Spending's two bars sit off-centre: a 28 pt gap on the left, 48 pt on the right

**Severity:** trivial (visual). **Estimate:** 5 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `mobile/src/common/d3/bar-chart-layout.ts:44-45`. The step is `plot/(n+0.2)`, but `barWidth = plot/n*0.6`, not the d3 bandwidth (`step*0.8`) its own doc comment claims, so the pair is shifted left.

Not run natively: the Spending card is below the fold. Under `tsx` (the agent's proof), on a 393 pt screen the bars sit at [78.3–171.6] and [219.6–312.9], with a 28.3 pt left gap and a 48.1 pt right gap.

## Fix

`barWidth = step * (1 - BAND_PADDING)`.

**Acceptance:** the left and right gaps are equal for n = 2. A unit case is added.
