# Errors from `nounused` and `check_commodity` are downgraded to warnings and hidden, and `pedantic` skips `nounused`: "Ledger is healthy" for a ledger bean-check rejects

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: ledger (and backend-v2). A3: the engine marks these plugin errors `severity: "warning"` (passed through by `backend-cluster/ledger/src/features/ledger/service/ledger-data-mappers.ts:55`), and backend-v2 drops warnings (`backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/features/ledger/service/ledger-data-service.ts:250-252`, since w21/188). `pedantic` doesn't include `nounused` at all.

Production, through `getLedgerErrors` on the disposable ledger (restored byte-for-byte): the QA ledger plus `plugin "beancount.plugins.nounused"` and an unused `2024-01-01 open Assets:Qa20261020Unused USD` gives **no errors**. bean-check exits 1 with "Unused account …". In the agent's run, `pedantic` gives `[]`, and `check_commodity` with no `commodity` directives gives warnings only, while bean-check reports "Missing Commodity directive for 'USD'".

## Fix

When the user explicitly enables these plugins, report their findings as errors (as Beancount does), and make `pedantic` include `nounused`. Keep w21/188's warning filter for the engine's own advisory warnings.

**Acceptance:** each fixture reports what bean-check reports, and a ledger without these plugins is unchanged. Unit cases are added.
