# The receipt parse contract carries no currency, so a foreign receipt is always booked in the ledger's currency (split from w21/870)

**Severity:** moderate for multi-currency users. **Estimate:** 45 minutes. Owner: backend-v2 (with mobile and dashboard). A3: `ReceiptParseResult` (the `parseReceipt` mutation's result) has an amount but no currency, so Scan Receipt, on mobile and the dashboard alike, books a receipt in a foreign currency in the ledger's own: a 12.50 EUR receipt on a USD ledger saves 12.50 USD. w21/870 made the "Receipt read" reveal show the exact amount the form holds; it did not touch this.

## Fix

Add an optional `currency` to the parse result across REST, GraphQL and MCP, have the model read it from the receipt, and carry it into the form (as the form's currency for the amount, so a currency the ledger does not use is a conscious choice). Needs owner sign-off on the contract, as w21/206 did for refunds.

**Acceptance:** a EUR receipt parsed on a USD ledger is offered, and saved, in EUR. Unit cases cover the parse result and the form.
