# On a ledger with no plugins, a padding entry opens to "This transaction was changed or removed"; the same entry opens fine once any plugin is declared

**Severity:** moderate. Every padding row on a plugin-free ledger is a dead end. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile or ledger (root cause not yet traced). A3: not yet traced. Likely the padding entry's ID, or its `generated_from`/context lookup, differs between the journal read and the detail's context read when no plugin pass runs; compare w21/508's plugin-copy path, which is exercised whenever a plugin is declared.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator, English UI, Metro `--clear` at `1d3fd1a10`, on the disposable QA ledger, which has no `plugin` lines and was restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`. Fixture: `2026-10-20 open Assets:Qa20261020Cash USD`, `… open Equity:Qa20261020Opening USD`, `2026-10-21 pad Assets:Qa20261020Cash Equity:Qa20261020Opening` and `2026-10-22 balance Assets:Qa20261020Cash 500.00 USD`. Transactions lists "(Padding inserted for B… · Pad · +$500.00)". Tapping it, three times, shows **"This transaction was changed or removed. Go back to see the current list."** FARMERS MARKET on the same list opens normally. Adding only `plugin "fava.plugins.amortize_over"` and relaunching makes the same padding row open, showing its +$500.00 detail and the padding notice.

## Fix

Trace the detail's lookup (selectOwnEntry, use-transaction-context and the ledger's context for a padding entry) when no plugins run, and make a padding entry resolve the same way it does with plugins.

**Acceptance:** with this fixture and no plugins, the padding row opens its detail. A unit or engine case is added.

## Dedupe

Not w21/841 (wording, done) or w21/508 and 660 (plugin copies). Searched `.pm/**` for padding together with "changed or removed".
