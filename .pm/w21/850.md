# The valuation caption's VoiceOver hint promises "each holding's latest price date" when the sheet lists holdings with no price at all

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `balance-chart-card/index.tsx:150` (account detail) and `account-charts-card.tsx:302` (Home) always use the hint "Shows each holding's latest price date". But `valuationDetails` (`common/valuation.ts:369-377`) opens the sheet whenever there are `atCostNoPrice` or `notInTotal` holdings, whose rows read "No price · At cost" or "Not in total" (`valuation-sheet/index.tsx:150-178`).

Not run natively (VoiceOver). For example, a cash account holding USD plus 50 EUR with no price is spoken as "Balance · …not in total, button, Shows each holding's latest price date", while the sheet shows "50 EUR — Not in total" with no date.

## Fix

Use a neutral hint ("Shows the holdings behind this total"), or build the hint from what the sheet will contain.

**Acceptance:** the hint never promises price dates for a sheet without them. A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` for valuationDetailsHint and "latest price date". Only w4/m27 and w4/1027 matched, and both are about the dates themselves.
