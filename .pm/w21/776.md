# With a fava plugin declared, w21/755's dedupe also drops w21/753's located "Unopened account … is being closed", leaving an unlocated, differently worded error

**Severity:** trivial. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: ledger. A3: `engine.ts:760` (`if (!validatedTransformed)`) skips all of `inactiveAfterGeneratedCloses`, including its E1001 for a child opened after its parent's close, which the revalidation doesn't duplicate.

Proven with the real engine under ts-node (as `yarn verify:rustledger` runs it) against Beancount 3.2.3 `bean-check` (the agent's run). Not on production: the ledger service hasn't been redeployed with these fixes. w21/753's fixture plus `plugin "fava.plugins.tag_discovered_documents"`. Without the fava plugin, the engine gives one "E1001 main.beancount:4 Unopened account Assets:Qa20261020Broker:Roth is being closed", matching bean-check. With it, the engine gives "E1001 **null:null** Account Assets:Qa20261020Broker:Roth was never opened". Posting behaviour is correct.

## Fix

Skip only the duplicated E1003 checks under `validatedTransformed`, and keep the E1001 "being closed" error with its location.

**Acceptance:** both plugin sets report the located E1001. A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

Conflict between w21/755 and w21/753 (done); wording overlaps w23/067. Searched `.pm/**` for validatedTransformed and "being closed".
