# Scan Receipt's shutter swallows capture failures: a tap before the camera is ready does nothing, with an unhandled rejection

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `camera-view.tsx:134-153` wraps the capture in try/finally with no catch. On a real device, `takePictureAsync` throws `CameraNotReadyException` when tapped before the preview is ready (`expo-camera/ios/Current/CameraPhotoCapture.swift:66`).

Not run natively: the simulator returns a generated photo when it has no camera. This is a code-level trace (the agent's).

## Fix

Catch the error and give a haptic or toast, and keep the shutter disabled until `onCameraReady` fires.

**Acceptance:** a premature tap gives feedback and never leaves an unhandled rejection. A source case is added.

Only w3/176 (accessibility). Searched `.pm/**` for takePictureAsync, shutter and "not ready".
