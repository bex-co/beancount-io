# A balance assertion with an explicit `~` tolerance hides the tolerance, so a passing assertion reads as a silent 0.03 mismatch

**Severity:** trivial. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the server sends `tolerance` on balance entries, but `select-account-journal.ts:298-303` drops it, and `account-entry-row.tsx:185-194` renders only the amount.

Not run natively (the row sits below the fold on the QA ledger's Cash account). Under `tsx` (the agent's proof), `2026-10-15 balance Assets:Qa20261020Cash 49.75 ~ 0.05 USD` on a balance of 49.72 gives `{"detail":{"kind":"balance","amount":"49.75 USD","offBy":null},"balance":49.72}`. The row reads "Balance 49.75 USD" above "Balance: $49.72", with no sign that it passed within tolerance.

## Fix

Show the tolerance ("49.75 ~ 0.05 USD"), or mark the row "within tolerance".

**Acceptance:** a passing toleranced assertion shows its tolerance. A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

Related to w21/533 and w21/681 (explicit tolerance on the server side). Searched `.pm/**` for tolerance together with account detail.
