# A merchant whose entries are all scheduled shows its first date as if it already happened ("Oct 25, 2026 – Scheduled Oct 31, 2026")

**Severity:** trivial. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `merchant-detail-screen/selectors/merchant-date-range.ts:18-22` marks only the last date as scheduled, and `merchants-screen/merchant-row-text.ts:47` shows the furthest pre-entered date instead of the next one.

Not run natively. Under `tsx` (the agent's proof), "Qa20261020 Prepaid" 10.00 USD on 2026-10-25 and 2026-10-31 (both after today, 2026-10-23) gives the detail line "Oct 25, 2026 – Scheduled Oct 31, 2026", and the list row reads "Scheduled Oct 31, 2026".

## Fix

When the first date is also in the future, mark the range as scheduled from its start ("Scheduled Oct 25 – Oct 31, 2026"), and have the row show the next scheduled date.

**Acceptance:** this fixture reads as entirely scheduled, and the row says "Scheduled Oct 25, 2026". A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

Not w21/335 or w21/616 (the last date only, and the single day). Searched `.pm/**` for merchant-date-range and Scheduled together with first.
