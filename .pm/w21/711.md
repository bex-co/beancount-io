# A fixed $500 monthly investment shows in Merchants as "Monthly · ~1.5625 VTI": the recurring series reads shares, not the cash paid

**Severity:** minor (Merchants' recurring amount is meaningless for brokerage auto-investments). **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: for a payee whose entries are only transfers, the series BQL keeps the positive leg (`merchants-screen/selectors/payee-series.ts:56`, `OR number > 0`), and line 158 reads its units. A buy held at cost is therefore summarized in shares (1.6, 1.5625 VTI…) instead of the $500 that left the bank. It is also marked approximate, because the share counts vary.

Not run natively: the Recurring row sits in a section below the fold, and the automation can't scroll. Proven under `tsx` (the agent's proof) on six "Vanguard" "monthly investment" entries from 2026-05-05 to 2026-10-05, each `Assets:Vanguard:VTI 1.6 VTI {312.50 USD}` or `1.5625 VTI {320.00 USD}` against `Assets:Checking -500.00 USD`. The app's own series BQL returns only the VTI legs, and `detectRecurrence` gives `{cadence: monthly, typicalAmountByCurrency: {VTI: 1.5625}, isApproximate: true}`, which renders as "~1.5625 VTI".

## Fix

For transfer-only payees, use the cash leg (the negative leg in the operating currency), or the cost of the positive leg when it is held at cost.

**Acceptance:** this fixture reads "Monthly · $500.00" with no "~". The cash transfer payees from w21/173 and w21/334 are unchanged. A unit case is added.
