# A commit subject for `*"Coffee"` with no space after the flag is read as a non-transaction (w21/961 regression)

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: ledger. This is a regression of w21/961 (`19a48b77b`).

**Root cause (A3).** In `backend-cluster/ledger/src/features/ledger/utils/commit-message.ts`, `wordsOf` and `isTransaction = keyword === "txn" || keyword.length === 1`: `*"Coffee"` tokenizes as one 9-character word, so the entry is treated as a non-transaction directive.

Not run natively in this pass (it needs the ledger deploy that carries w21/903). `tsx` proof (the agent's): `entryCommitMessage("Update", '2026-10-22 *"Coffee" "x"')` gives `Update 2026-10-22 *"Coffee" x`. Before w21/961 it gave `"Coffee"`.

## Fix

Test for a flag character followed by anything (for example `/^[*!A-Z#?%&]/`), instead of a word of length one. A subject that starts with a quote should also count as a transaction.

**Acceptance:** cases for `*"X"` and `!"X"` give `"X"`.

## Dedupe

Searched for "commit-message", "entryLabel" and "flag". The commit-message callers only produce subjects (`ledger-journal-service.ts:980,1118`, `api/entries.ts:435`); nothing parses the old subject format.
