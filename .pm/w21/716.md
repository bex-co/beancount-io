# Home's Latest Updates shows only two lines of a multi-commit push, and hides the "+N more commit(s)" line (which is English in every language anyway)

**Severity:** trivial. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile and backend-v2. A3: `feed-card.tsx:124` caps the row subtitle at two lines. The server's summary for a push lists the commits newest-first and then "+N more commit(s)" (`backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/features/gitea/feed/service/activity-content-parser.ts:279`, used by `activity-transformer.ts:218`), so for anything over two commits the count line is cut off. It is also hardcoded English.

Not run natively: it needs a multi-commit `git push`, and the card is below the fold. Proven under `tsx` with the real `parseActivityContent`, `summarizeParsedContent`, `stripHtml` and `feedRowDetail`: a five-commit push produces "Import statement 5 / 4 / 3 / +2 more commit(s)", of which two lines are visible.

## Fix

Put the count first, or on the title line ("Pushed 5 commits"), and localize it (return a count, not a sentence, from the server).

**Acceptance:** a five-commit push row says how many commits it holds, in the app's language. Unit cases are added.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` for "more commit", summarizeParsedContent and feed-card together with numberOfLines.
