# Transaction detail repeats the narration under the title when the payee is only spaces or equals the narration

**Severity:** trivial (transaction detail). **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `transaction-detail-screen.tsx:724` renders the subtitle whenever `entry.payee && entry.narration` (the raw text), while the title comes from the trimmed `selectTransactionTitle` (`select-transaction-detail.ts:291`). A payee of " " gives the title "qa Coffee" with "qa Coffee" again as the subtitle. Importers often write identical payee and narration, and that also shows twice. The list row's `entryRowSecondaryText` already hides both cases. w21/568's recheck didn't open the detail.

Native (iPhone 17e at `d731eb32a`; disposable ledger restored byte-for-byte): `2026-10-15 * " " "qa Coffee"` and `2026-10-16 * "QA AMAZON MKTPLACE" "QA AMAZON MKTPLACE"`, each 4.50 / 9.00 USD from a cash account. Home lists them as "qa Coffee" and "QA AMAZON MKTPLACE" with no second line. Opening either one shows the name as the title and again in grey under the amount.

## Fix

Show the subtitle only when the trimmed narration is non-empty and differs from the displayed title. Reuse `entryRowSecondaryText`'s rule.

**Acceptance:** both fixtures show the name once. A normal payee and narration pair still shows both. Unit case added.
