# Changing the date after picking a budget account doesn't recheck when the account opens

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile.

**Root cause (A3).**
- `mobile/src/screens/add-budget-screen/add-budget-screen.tsx:262` rechecks only the close date when the date changes. Nothing calls `isOpenOn`.
- The backend's `assertBudgetAccountsExist` (`backend-v2/.../ledger-entry-service.ts:568`) checks only that the account exists.

Not run natively in this pass. `tsx` proof: `Expenses:Gym` opens 2026-11-01 and is listed when the date is 2026-11-15. Moving the date to 2026-10-01 leaves Done enabled, while `isOpenOn` is false. A budget dated before its account exists is written, and the card then judges October against an account that didn't exist yet.

## Fix

Refuse the save when `!isOpenOn(account, date)`, and name the open date.

**Acceptance:** with `Expenses:Gym` opening 2026-11-01, a 2026-10-01 date disables Done and shows the open date.
