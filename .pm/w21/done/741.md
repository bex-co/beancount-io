# Budget directives appear in account detail as a blank "Custom · Custom" row with $0.00, in English, and can't be opened

**Severity:** minor (every budget written by the Budget tab adds a meaningless row to its account's and all its parents' journals). **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the ledger includes Custom directives whose values name the account (`journal-running-balance.ts:437-442`, as Fava does). Mobile's `entryTitle` falls back to `directive_type` (`account-detail-screen/selectors/select-account-journal.ts:320`), `entryDetail` (`:276-307`) has no Custom case, and rows open only for transactions (`account-detail-screen.tsx:429-432`).

Native (iPhone 17e, Metro `--clear` at `b2ec6ca23`; disposable ledger restored byte-for-byte; today 2026-10-23): the QA ledger plus `2026-10-01 custom "budget" Expenses:Qa20261020Food "monthly" 300.00 USD` (and a 42.00 grocery). Accounts ▸ **Expenses** shows an October 1, 2026 section headed "$0.00", with a row "**Custom** [Custom]", a generic icon, "$0.00" and "Balance: $4,133.49". Under `tsx` (the agent's proof), the row is `{"title":"Custom","chipKey":"custom","change":0}` for both the account and its parent. In German it reads "Custom" beside the chip "Eigene".

## Fix

Render `custom "budget"` as a budget row ("Budget · Food · $300.00 monthly", translated, opening the Budget card), and other Custom types with their type name and values. Or leave Custom directives out of account journals entirely.

**Acceptance:** a budget directive reads as a budget, in the app's language, or doesn't appear. No "$0.00" day header for it. Unit cases are added.
