# w21/737 regression: a `close_tree` child opened after its parent was closed gets a false "inactive account" error, and saves to it are refused

**Severity:** major once deployed (a valid save is refused). **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: ledger. A3: `backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/generated-closes.ts:36-48` applies the plugin's generated close date to every child, without checking that the child was opened after it.

Proven with the real engine under ts-node (as `yarn verify:rustledger` runs it) against Beancount 3.2.3 `bean-check`, also at the commit before the fix (the agent's run). Not on production: the ledger service hasn't been redeployed with the fix. `plugin "beancount.plugins.close_tree"`; `2024-01-01 open Assets:Qa20261020Broker` and `…Cash`; `2024-04-01 close Assets:Qa20261020Broker`; `2025-01-01 open Assets:Qa20261020Broker:Roth`. Then `addEntries` (New Transaction) with `2025-02-01 * "Qa20261020 Contribution"` (Broker:Roth 100.00 / Cash −100.00). bean-check accepts it. The engine reports "Posting to inactive account Assets:Qa20261020Broker:Roth on 2025-02-01 (closed on 2024-04-01)", and `refuseNewLedgerErrors` refuses the save. Before w21/737: 0 errors.

## Fix

Only treat a child as closed by `close_tree` if it was open at the parent's close date, as Beancount does.

**Acceptance:** this add is accepted, and w21/737's fixture still errors. A unit case is added.

Regression from w21/737 (done). Searched `.pm/**` for generated-closes and close_tree together with reopened.

**Resolved 2026-10-23** — `inactiveAfterGeneratedCloses` (`backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/generated-closes.ts`) now only treats a child as closed by `close_tree` when the child was opened on or before the generated close date.

One correction to the note's premise: bean-check 3.2.3 does not accept this fixture. `close_tree` closes every child, the late one included, and Beancount then reports `<beancount.plugins.close_tree>:0: Unopened account Assets:Qa…:Broker:Roth is being closed`. It raises no error on the posting, because its date-ordered check leaves the child open. The engine now matches that: one E1001 "Unopened account … is being closed" for such a close, placed on the parent's written close line so the row can be opened, and none on the postings.

The ledger has that error as soon as the child is opened, so the note's `addEntries` adds no new error and is no longer refused. w21/737's fixture still errors. Unit cases are in `generated-closes.test.ts`. Live engine cases in `scripts/verify-rustledger.ts` check the late child, that the posting adds no error, and w21/737's case; at the previous HEAD they give the note's E1003. Needs a ledger-service deploy. Shipped in the commit that moves this note.
