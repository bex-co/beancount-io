# A pasted "−12.50" (typographic minus) is saved as negative but shows a green "+", and the sign toggle then makes it unreadable (w21/463 brings back w21/232)

**Severity:** minor (New and Edit Transaction; amounts pasted from Numbers, a bank PDF or a localized keyboard). **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: w21/463 taught `parseExactAmount` (`postings-utils.ts:47-50`) to map U+2212 to `-` and strip direction marks. The posting row still reads the sign from the raw text: `multi-postings-transaction-screen.tsx:298` uses `typed.startsWith("-")`, and `handleToggleSign` (`:332-334`) prefixes another `-`. That is the w21/232 bug again, with a different character.

Native (iPhone 17e at `d731eb32a`; nothing saved): Home ▸ + ▸ Enter new transaction. Copy `−12.50` (U+2212), focus the first posting's amount and Paste. The row shows "−12.50" in green beside a "+" badge, while the auto leg reads +12.50 and Done is enabled; Done would save `-12.50 USD`. Tapping the "+" badge turns the text into "-−12.50" in red: "Use digits and one decimal point, like 1234.56", the auto leg drops to 0.00, and Done is disabled. Under `tsx`, an LRM-prefixed "-12.50" and Persian LRM+U+2212 behave the same way.

## Fix

Derive the sign from the normalized text (the same normalization `parseExactAmount` uses), and have the toggle rewrite the normalized absolute value.

**Acceptance:** pasting "−12.50" shows a red "−" badge, and one tap gives 12.50 positive. " -12.50" (w21/232) still works. Unit cases cover U+2212 and the direction-mark variants.

## Dedupe

Regression of w21/232 (done) through w21/463 (done). Searched `.pm/**` for U+2212, handleToggleSign and startsWith("-").

---

**Resolved 2026-10-23** — the posting row reads its sign through `splitAmountSign`, which normalizes U+2212 and direction marks as `parseExactAmount` does, so a pasted "−12.50" shows the red minus badge and the toggle flips it cleanly. Cases in `postings-utils.test.ts`, shipped in the commit that closes this note (`mobile`).
