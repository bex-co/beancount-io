# Switching the app language leaves a long Reports/Budget chart scrolled short of the newest month: October, the only month with income, is cut off at the right edge

**Severity:** minor (stale scroll position after an in-app language change). **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `mobile/src/common/d3/scrollable-axis-chart.tsx` scrolls a long plot to its newest period only from `onContentSizeChange` (`:108-109`, `scrollToEnd`). `onLayout` (`:98-99`) only stores the new viewport width. A language change re-derives the y-axis width (`axisLabelWidth` in `income-expense-bar-chart.tsx:127`, because the compact tick text differs: "$15K" in English, "15 хил. $" in Bulgarian), so the viewport narrows while the plot width stays the same. No content-size change fires, the scroll offset stays where it was, and the newest months now sit beyond the right edge.

## Repro

`main` at `3730a55fb`, dev build `io.beancount.ios` on iPhone 17e (iOS 27.0), light mode, the QA account's `example` ledger (income-statement series 2015-01 to 2026-10, only October 2026 with income, USD 932.02).

1. Reports tab, tap **ALL**. The chart opens on the newest months: APR … OCT, OCT flush with the right edge, the green October income bar fully visible.
2. Drawer ▸ account row ▸ Settings ▸ Language ▸ **Български** ▸ Confirm.
3. Back to the Reports tab (still on ALL; the tab stays mounted).
4. The chart now shows АПР … СЕП and a clipped "О…" at the right edge; the October income bar is cut off after its first few pixels.

Repeated: the same result on a second EN → BG switch. Controls: after a cold relaunch in Bulgarian, or after tapping another range chip and back to ВСИЧКИ, the chart opens on МАЙ … ОКТ with October fully visible (the plot width changed, so `onContentSizeChange` fires). Switching BG → EN does not clip (the viewport widens and the offset clamps).

## Expected / actual

Expected: the chart keeps showing the newest period at the right edge after the viewport width changes, unless the reader had scrolled away from the end. Actual: the offset is frozen, so a narrower viewport hides the newest months until the reader scrolls or changes range.

## Fix

In `ScrollableAxisChart`, when `onLayout` reports a different viewport width, and the plot was at its end before the change (previous `offsetX >= plotWidth − previousViewportWidth − 1`), call `scrollToEnd({ animated: false })`. A reader who had scrolled left keeps their position. `onContentSizeChange` stays as is.

**Acceptance:**
- The repro above ends with OCT flush with the right edge in Bulgarian.
- A chart scrolled left before the language change keeps its position.
- Add a unit case for the "was at end" predicate (a small pure helper next to `hasClippedStart` in `scroll-cue.ts`).

## Blast radius

Callers of `ScrollableAxisChart` (searched `mobile/src`): `income-expense-bar-chart.tsx` (Reports) and `budget-bar-chart-d3.tsx` (Budget). Both share the stale offset after a language change. Any other cause of a narrower viewport with an unchanged plot (a Dynamic Type change that widens the axis labels) goes through the same path. Charts that fit without scrolling are unaffected.

## Adjacent states

Not exercised: right-to-left languages, a text-size change while Reports is mounted, Android, the Budget chart natively (read from the source only).

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` (including `done/` and `blocked/`) for scrollToEnd, ScrollableAxisChart, scrollable-axis, viewport and language-switch chart terms. Related but different: w21/1015 (done, a cue that long ranges scroll), w21/177 (done, axis amounts abbreviate per language; it made the axis width depend on the language, which is what exposes this). The 2026-11-04 suspicion that Reports "ALL" showed only April–October was checked and is not a bug: the series has 142 monthly points from 2015-01 and the chart opens on the newest seven.

---

**Resolved 2026-11-05** — `ScrollableAxisChart` now returns to the newest end when the viewport width changes while the chart was at its end (`wasAtEnd` in `scroll-cue.ts`); a chart the reader had scrolled left keeps its place. Unit cases in `scroll-cue.test.ts`. The EN → BG switch itself was not rerun on a simulator. Shipped in the commit that moves this note (`mobile`).
