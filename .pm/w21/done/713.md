# After a stock split, account detail's chart reads "+90 QANVDA (+900.00%)" in green, and the split row reads "+90 QANVDA", though no money moved (w21/571 gap)

**Severity:** minor (a split looks like a 900% gain on the account's main chart). **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: account detail charts the units series (`account-detail-screen.tsx:241-242`, `selectAccountUnitsSeries`). The change line and trend colour (`common/d3/interactive-line-chart.tsx:454-465`, `:673`) treat the unit jump as a favourable change. The journal row (`components/account-entry-row.tsx:147,161`) shows the net units in green. w21/571 made splits neutral only in Transactions' row and hero.

Native (iPhone 17e, Metro `--clear` at `dc37649ce`; disposable ledger restored byte-for-byte; today 2026-10-23), with w21/712's fixture (a 10-for-1 split of QANVDA on 2026-10-01; price 105.00 USD on 10-20):

## Fix

For a commodity account, chart the at-cost (or at-market) value rather than the units, or at least don't colour or percent-format a units change. Render a units-only change with no cost movement neutrally, as w21/571 does.

**Acceptance:** after this split, the account's change line shows no +900% gain (its value change is $0 at cost, or the market move), and the split row isn't green. Buys still show their units and cost. Unit cases are added.
