# Budget dates ignore when the account opens or closes: an Update can be dated after the account closes (a budget that never applies), and an account opening later can't be picked

**Severity:** low to moderate (the w21/624 harm, reached another way). **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3:
- `add-budget-screen.tsx:266` opens the account picker without the form's date (`entryDate`), so `account-picker-screen.tsx:324` lists the accounts open today.
- `add-budget-screen.tsx:441`'s date picker has no maximum, and nothing checks the date against the account's close date.
- w21/624 hid Update only after an account has already closed.

Not run natively. Under `tsx` (the agent's proof), with `Expenses:QaLease` opened 2026-01-01 and closing 2026-12-31, and `Expenses:QaGym` opening 2026-11-01: today's list is `[QaFood, QaLease]`, the 2027-01-01 list is `[QaFood, QaGym]`, and Update is not hidden for QaLease. So `2027-01-01 custom "budget" Expenses:QaLease "monthly" 200 USD` can be written, and QaGym can't be picked for a budget starting 2026-11-01.

## Fix

Pass the form's date as `entryDate` to the picker, set `maximumDate` to the account's close date in both Add and Update, and re-check the picked account when the date changes.

**Acceptance:** a date past the close date is refused with the close date named, and an account opening on the chosen date is listed. Unit cases are added.
