# A flat range reads grey "+$0.00 (0.00%)" in the chart header, but the line and fill stay green (or red for a near-flat drop)

**Severity:** trivial (the chart contradicts its own header). **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: w21/237 and w21/416 made the change row neutral when the move rounds to flat (`describeChange`), but the line colour still comes from the raw `value >= opening` test (`common/d3/interactive-line-chart.tsx:683-692`). `ScrubHeader`'s comment says the line and the figure "can never disagree".

Native (iPhone 17e, Metro `--clear` at `3263cb449`; QA ledger unchanged; today 2026-10-23): Accounts ▸ Income ▸ **1M** (Income has no October postings) reads "−$8,000.00 · **+$0.00 (0.00%)**" in grey above a **green** flat line with a green fill. The same holds on Home's Liabilities tab at 1M. Under `tsx` (the agent's proof), w21/237's [2,500,000 → 2,499,988] gives a grey "−$12.00 (0.00%)" over a **red** line.

## Fix

Derive the line and fill colour from the same flat/favourable decision as the header (`describeChange` / `trendFavorable`), and use a neutral colour when flat.

**Acceptance:** a flat or rounds-to-flat range draws a neutral line and fill, and real moves keep their colours. A unit case is added.
