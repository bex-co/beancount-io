# Cash flow clips the left-column income label at the card's edge (")a20261020" for "Qa20261020")

**Severity:** trivial. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the left node's label is right-aligned to the node and drawn past the plot's left margin, so a name as wide as the margin is cut at the card edge.

Native (iPhone 17e, Metro `--clear` at `3263cb449`; disposable ledger restored byte-for-byte; today 2026-10-23): with w21/747's fixture, Reports ▸ 1M ▸ Cash flow shows the income node's label as "**)a20261020**". The leading "Q" is cut off at the card's left edge, while the right column's labels fit.

## Fix

Reserve the left label width (measure it, or truncate with "…" at the margin), as the right column does.

**Acceptance:** a 10-character income label shows in full or with a trailing ellipsis, and never clipped at the edge. A render case is added.

## Dedupe

Not w21/540 or w21/748 (suffix truncation). Searched `.pm/**` for sankey together with clip and "left edge".

---

**Resolved 2026-10-23** — Cash flow labels are now cut to the label column's width, not only to ten characters. The new `fitSankeyLabel` in `reports-screen/selectors/sankey-data.ts` is applied to every label line in `cash-flow-sankey.tsx`. It measures with `sankeyLabelWidth`, which takes per-character advances from CoreText's widths at the chart's 12 pt and never reads a label narrower than it is drawn.

At 12 pt "Qa20261020" is 73.5 pt against the column's 68 pt (72 less the 4 pt gap). It now reads "Qa20261…" instead of ")a20261020". The right column's "(Einnahme…" (69.3 pt, clipped at the right edge in German) reads "(Einnah…". Labels that fit, such as "Savings" and "Groceries", are unchanged.

Not a render case, since the sankey has no render harness: a unit case checks the estimate against eleven CoreText-measured labels (Latin, Cyrillic, CJK) and the cuts, and a source case checks the chart applies it. Both fail at the previous HEAD. Not run natively. Shipped in the commit that moves this note.
