# A BTC budget reads a green "On target" while its own figures show it 17% over: "Budget 0.0022258 · Actual 0.0026 · Variance +0.0003742 BTC" (w21/290 regression via w21/595)

**Severity:** minor to moderate. Visible on the Budget card and on Home. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the status tolerance is half the budget's last written digit (`budgetScale`, `budget-screen/selectors/budget-selectors.ts:427`; `varianceStatus`, `:658`). For "0.003 BTC" that is ±0.0005. w21/595 then made `moneyDigits` (`number-utils.ts:316`) print every digit a crypto amount has, so the prorated budget's extra digits and a variance far larger than "on target" are shown beside the green badge.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator, English UI, Metro `--clear` at `4c314cfc7`, on the disposable QA ledger, which was restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`; today was 2026-10-23. Fixture: `2026-01-01 custom "budget" Expenses:Qa20261020Fees "monthly" 0.003 BTC`, with 0.0026 BTC spent on 2026-10-05. The Budget card (YTD) reads **"Monthly · BTC · On target"** in green, with **"Budget 0.0022258 BTC · Actual 0.0026 BTC · Variance +0.0003742 BTC"** and a full green meter. Under `tsx`, Home speaks "0.0026 BTC of 0.0022258 BTC, 117% used, On target".

## Fix

Judge the status at the precision actually shown. Alternatively, show budget, actual and variance at `budgetScale` (here 0.0022 / 0.0026 / +0.0004) so the badge and the numbers agree.

**Acceptance:** with this fixture, the card doesn't read "On target" beside a 17% overrun. A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

Regression of w21/290 (done), caused by w21/595 (done). Searched `.pm/**` for 290, 595, "guard digit", and "On target" together with BTC.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-23** — A budget's status is now judged at the precision of the figures beside it. The new `statusScale` (`budget-screen/selectors/budget-selectors.ts`) keeps `budgetScale` for money. For a commodity it is at least eight places, which is how far `moneyDigits` prints its budget, actual and variance (`COMMODITY_MONEY_DIGITS`, now exported). `selectBudgetCardStats` and `selectPointFavorability` judge with it, so the Budget card, its chart bars and Home's panel (which reads the card's stats) agree.

With this fixture (budget 0.0022258, actual 0.0026), the card now reads "Above target", unfavourable, and Home speaks "Above target". A commodity budget whose figures match, float noise included, is still On target. USD keeps the half-cent tolerance.

Unit cases are in `selectors/__tests__/fractional-commodity.test.ts`; two fail at the previous HEAD, and w21/290's cases still pass. Not checked natively. Shipped in the commit that moves this note.

Native recheck (iPhone 17e, fresh Metro `--clear` at `c008b4558`, second launch; disposable ledger with the note's 0.003 BTC monthly budget and 0.0026 BTC spent, restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`; today 2026-10-23): the Budget card (YTD) now reads "Monthly · BTC · **Above target**" in red, with a red meter and October bar, beside "Budget 0.0022258 BTC · Actual 0.0026 BTC · Variance +0.0003742 BTC". The badge and the figures agree. Fixed. Home's spoken value was not exercised.
