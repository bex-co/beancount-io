# `onecommodity` ignores Beancount's opt-outs (`onecommodity: FALSE`, multi-currency opens, the account regex) and reports the same account twice

**Severity:** minor (false errors users can't clear the documented way). **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: ledger. A3: rustledger's native `onecommodity` doesn't implement `skip_accounts` from `beancount/plugins/onecommodity.py`. Its errors pass through `engine.ts:714-720` unreconciled.

Proven with the real engine under ts-node (in-process snapshot, `LedgerDataService.getErrors`/`getTrialBalance`/`getAccountJournal`) against Beancount 3.2.3 `bean-check`/`bean-query` (the agent's run). `plugin "beancount.plugins.onecommodity" "Expenses:Qa20261020:Travel"`; an `Assets:Qa20261020:Cash` opened with metadata `onecommodity: FALSE`; an `Assets:Qa20261020:Multi` opened `USD,EUR`; and Travel and Broker (VTI and VOO) postings. bean-check reports one error, on Travel (with no regex, Broker too). The service reports five, with `source: null`: Cash twice, plus Multi and Broker.

## Fix

Honour the three opt-outs and report each account once, with its location.

**Acceptance:** this fixture matches bean-check. A unit case per opt-out is added.

Searched `.pm/**` for onecommodity.

**Resolved 2026-10-23** — new `backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/one-commodity.ts` (`reconcileOneCommodityErrors`). When `beancount.plugins.onecommodity` is declared, it replaces rustledger's per-transaction "Account 'X' uses multiple currencies" errors with Beancount's, computed over the booked stream:
- an account opened `onecommodity: FALSE`, opened with several currencies, or not matched from its start by the config pattern (`re.match`) is skipped;
- units come from postings and balances, and cost currencies are checked too;
- each account is reported once, on its last entry in Beancount's order (date, balances before the day's transactions, line).

Pads are expanded after user plugins in Beancount 3 (`PLUGINS_POST`), so padding never counts. `collect-plugins.ts` gains `collectLedgerPluginDeclarations`, which keeps each plugin's config. Checked against bean-check 3.2.3 on the note's fixture (with and without the pattern), a cost-currency mix, a same-day balance, and a pad. Unit cases (one per opt-out) are in `one-commodity.test.ts`. The live engine case in `scripts/verify-rustledger.ts` fails at the previous HEAD with four unlocated errors. Needs a ledger-service deploy to reach production. Shipped in the commit that moves this note.
