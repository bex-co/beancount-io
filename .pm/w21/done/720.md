# w21/705's fix now hides a later failing balance when a second pad on the same account and currency is unused

**Severity:** major (a failed reconciliation reads as passing, w21/675's bug again). **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: ledger. A3: w21/705 keys the recheck's `lost` set by account and currency (`backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/pad-recheck.ts:179-192`). When one pad on the account was used for USD, every USD failure the recheck finds is dropped, including one hidden by a *different*, unused pad.

Proven with the real engine under ts-node at `HEAD` and at the parent of the fix commit (as `yarn verify:rustledger` runs it), against Beancount 3.2.3 `bean-check`. Not on production: the ledger service hasn't been redeployed since before w21/675.

```
2024-01-01 open Assets:Qa20261020Wallet
2024-01-01 open Equity:Qa20261020Opening
2024-01-01 open Expenses:Qa20261020Food
2024-01-01 * "EUR in"         ; Wallet +50 EUR
2024-01-02 pad Assets:Qa20261020Wallet Equity:Qa20261020Opening
2024-01-03 balance Assets:Qa20261020Wallet 100 USD
2024-01-04 balance Assets:Qa20261020Wallet 50 EUR
2024-03-01 pad Assets:Qa20261020Wallet Equity:Qa20261020Opening
2024-03-02 balance Assets:Qa20261020Wallet 100 USD
2024-04-01 * "Groceries"      ; Wallet -30 USD, Food +30 USD
2024-04-02 balance Assets:Qa20261020Wallet 100 USD
```

Expected (bean-check): E2003 on the second pad, plus "Balance failed … expected 100 USD != accumulated 70 USD" on the 04-02 line. At HEAD: only E2003. At the commit before w21/705 the failure is reported, and without the EUR lines HEAD reports it too.

## Fix

Key "lost" failures by the pad that would have filled them (the pad's location), not by account and currency.

**Acceptance:** this fixture reports both errors. w21/705's and w21/675's fixtures keep their results. A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

Regression from w21/705 (done) of w21/675 (done). Searched `.pm/**` for pad-recheck and lost.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-23** — the re-check no longer blanks a pad that filled some currencies: it puts the padding that pad did add in its place (written after the end of its file, so no line number moves; on the plugin path the same padding transactions replace it in the transformed stream), and blanks only the pads that filled nothing. The currencies a pad filled keep their padding, so their later balances read the true figures, and its other currencies are checked on their own (w21/705), without the account-and-currency filter that dropped this failure. Live engine: this fixture reports "Unused Pad entry" on the second pad and "expected 100 USD, got 70 USD" on the 04-02 line, as bean-check 3.2.3 does ("accumulated 70 USD"), with and without amortize_over; w21/675's, w21/676's, w21/705's and w21/706's fixtures keep their results. Tests: a `verify:rustledger` check (fails at the previous HEAD with only E2003) and `pad-recheck.test.ts` (the w21/705 case rewritten for the stand-in padding, and this note's case). Shipped in the commit that closes this note (`backend-cluster/ledger`). Needs a ledger deploy to reach production.
