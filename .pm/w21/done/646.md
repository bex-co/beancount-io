# The same "1M" and "3M" pills mean a rolling window on Transactions but calendar months on Reports, so Reports' 1M Food $60 becomes $100 when filtered on Transactions

**Severity:** low (a confusing mismatch). **Estimate:** 20 minutes, needs a product call. Owner: mobile. A3 (code-level at `main` `97494115f`, proven under `tsx` with the real selectors): the Transactions filter's `resolveDateRange` (`transactions-screen/filters/select-filter-query.ts:86-89`) uses `monthsBack(today, n)` through today. Reports (`select-ranged-account-tree.ts:155-167`, `select-account-transactions.ts:75-83`, through `rangeStartMonth` in `common/series-util.ts:141`) uses whole calendar months. Both pills are labelled with `range1M`. On 2026-10-22, with Food 40.00 on 09-25 and 60.00 on 10-05, Reports 1M reads Food $60, while Transactions 1M plus Food lists both ($100). 3M is Jul 22–Oct 22 on Transactions and Aug–Oct on Reports. Budget's 1Y (`budget-labels.ts:171-183`) is also rolling, while Reports' 1Y is twelve whole months.

## Fix

Pick one meaning per label: make Transactions' 1M and 3M calendar windows (matching Reports, Home and Spending), or relabel them "Last 30 days" and "Last 90 days".

**Acceptance:** a pill label means the same window on every screen. Unit cases cover the resolver.

See w21/067, w21/078, w21/166 and w21/523. Searched `.pm/**` for "rolling", monthsBack and "calendar month".
