# In Persian, chart y-axis ticks use Latin digits and "." while the x-axis labels beneath them use Persian digits

**Severity:** trivial to minor (Persian only, visible on screen). **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile.

**Root cause (A3).** The `fa` compact style has `decimal: "."` (`mobile/src/common/number-utils.ts:63-66`), and `shortNumber` never localizes digits, although its comment says compact values follow the language. w21/884 gave `withYearMarks` and `selectedPeriodLabel` Persian digits. The dashboard's equivalent, w3/632, localized its tick digits.

Not run natively; this needs Persian and a right-to-left restart. `tsx` proof:
- The x-axis gives `['دسا ۲۵','ژان ۲۶','فور']`.
- The ticks on the same chart give `"$500" "⁦$1⁩ هزار" "⁦$1.5⁩ هزار"`.
- For reference, `Intl.NumberFormat("fa", {notation:"compact"}).format(1500)` gives `"۱٫۵ هزار"`.

**Screens:** the Reports income/expense chart, the Budget chart and Home Spending.

## Fix

For `fa`, put the compact digits through `localizeDigits` and use `"٫"` as the decimal separator.

**Acceptance:**
- In Persian, tick digits match the x-axis.
- The w21/883 minus-isolation cases still pass; their Latin expectations in `number-utils.test.ts` need updating.
- Full amounts stay Latin, under the w3/189 rule.
