# A merged payee suggestion fills in accounts only from the spelling it shows, so case, space or accent variants don't fill in

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the account fill-in lookup (`common/suggestion-utils.ts:346`) matches the payee exactly after trimming and straightening quotes only. The suggestion list merges spelling variants (`match-suggestions.ts`, w21/509 and w21/257), and Merchants' `merchantKey` also ignores case. The server query (`toPlainSearchFilter` → `"Qa\s+Whole\s+Foods"`) returns every spelling, and the app then drops all but the exact one. When the newest variant labels the merged row, picking it fills in nothing.

Not run natively: it needs payees that differ only in spacing, accents or case, and a newer variant ranked first. Proven under `tsx` (the agent's proof):

- **Spacing.** Two "Qa Whole Foods" entries (Card → Groceries) and a newer "Qa Whole  Foods" (double space; a Card → Groceries and Household split). `matchSuggestions` shows one row, "Qa Whole  Foods", and filling in from it gives null/null. The single-space spelling alone gives Card/Groceries.
- **Accents.** An NFC "Qa Café" against NFD history gives null.
- **Case.** "Qa Bean Shop" against "QA BEAN SHOP" history gives null, although `merchantKey` treats them as equal.

## Fix

Look up the fill-in by the same normalized key the suggestion list merges on (NFC, collapsed spaces, case-folded), across all spellings returned.

**Acceptance:** each variant above fills in its merchant's usual accounts. Exact-spelling behaviour is unchanged. Unit cases are added.
