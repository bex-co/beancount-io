# An amount written with a leading dot (`.5 USD`, `-.5 USD`) is read as 5, ten times too large, with only a generic parse error

**Severity:** moderate. Real Beancount accepts `.5`, and the ledger engine reads the entry with a wrong amount while it reports an error, so Home, Accounts and Reports can total the wrong figure. **Estimate:** 45 minutes. Owner: ledger (all surfaces get it). A3: the `@rustledger/wasm` parser rejects `.5` at the dot (code `P0012`, "parse error: unexpected input"), and then yields the posting with `number: "5"`. For `-.5` it also drops the sign.

**Native (iPhone 17e, 2026-10-23, production ledger service).** On the disposable ledger I appended:
```
2026-10-21 * "Qa20261020 DotAmount" "leading dot"
  Assets:Cash  -3.00 USD
  Expenses:Transport  .5 USD
  Assets:Cash
```
- `getLedgerErrors` lists one "parse error: unexpected input".
- Transactions shows the entry as "-$5.00": the amount the engine kept, ten times the 0.50 written.
- The bell shows 1 error.

The ledger was restored byte for byte afterwards.

**Engine proof** (`@rustledger/wasm` 0.24, loaded under Node):
- `Expenses:E  .5 USD` → `units.number: "5"` plus `P0012 unexpected input` at the dot.
- `Assets:A  -.5 USD` / `Expenses:E  .5 USD` → both postings carry `"5"` (the minus is lost), with `P0012 … a +/- must be followed by a number` pointing at the dot.
- `-0.5 USD` parses correctly as `-0.5`.

## Fix

Accept `.5`, `-.5` and `+.5` in the number grammar, as Beancount's lexer does (`\.[0-9]+`). Failing that, drop a posting whose amount failed to parse, as w21/900 does for a header, so the wrong number never reaches balances and totals.

**Acceptance:**
- `.5 USD` and `-.5 USD` parse as 0.5 and -0.5 with no error.
- If they were refused instead, the entry contributes no amount anywhere and the error says to write `0.5`.

## Dedupe

w21/899 and w21/900 cover repeated parse errors and a half-parsed header, not a half-parsed amount. Searched `.pm/**` for ".5", "leading dot", "P0012" and "unexpected input".

---

**Resolved 2026-10-24** — the files the engine parses (`parseableFiles`, which already comments out the lines Beancount skips) now give a number written without its leading zero one (`withLeadingZeros`: `.5` → `0.5`, `-.5` → `-0.5`, `+.25`, inside expressions), leaving strings, `;` comments, tags, links and numbers after a letter or digit alone. Checked on the live engine: the note's entry parses with no error and posts -3.00, 0.5 and the inferred 2.5; a signed `-.5` keeps its sign. Cases in `parseable-files.test.ts`; every path that builds a ledger from files goes through this, so REST, GraphQL and MCP agree (`backend-cluster/ledger`). Editing and slice reads still see the text as written. Not run natively.
