# Open Account says a still-open account "was closed on December 31, 2026 and can't be reopened" when its close date is in the future

**Severity:** minor. The notice is false, and the Accounts tab lists the same account as open. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `mobile/src/screens/open-account-screen/account-name.ts:335`. `existingAccountNotice` returns `openAccountClosed` for any close date and never compares it with today.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator, English UI, Metro `--clear` at `6ce8607ed`, on the disposable QA ledger, which was restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`; today was 2026-10-23. Fixture: `2026-01-02 open Assets:Qa20261020:Savings USD` and `2026-12-31 close Assets:Qa20261020:Savings`. Accounts lists "Qa20261020:Savings $0.00" under Assets. In Accounts ▸ + ▸ paste `Qa20261020:Savings`, the screen reads **"Assets:Qa20261020:Savings was closed on December 31, 2026 and can't be reopened."** The form was discarded, so nothing was written.

## Fix

Use the closed wording only when `closedAt <= today`. Otherwise say that the account is already open and closes on that date, with a new string in all 13 locales.

**Acceptance:** with this fixture, the notice says the account is already open and closes on Dec 31, 2026. An account closed in the past keeps the current wording. A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

Not w21/122, 723 or 734 (done). Searched `.pm/**` for existingAccountNotice, openAccountClosed and "future close".

---

**Resolved 2026-10-23** — `existingAccountNotice` (`open-account-screen/account-name.ts`) now uses the closed wording only from the close date on (`closedAt <= today`). A close after today gets a new `openAccountClosesLater`, in all 13 locales, built on each locale's own "already open" line. With this fixture (closing 2026-12-31, today 2026-10-23), the notice reads "Assets:Qa20261020:Savings is already open in this ledger and closes on December 31, 2026." An account closed in the past keeps "was closed on … and can't be reopened". An account that opens later still says that first.

Unit cases are in `__tests__/future-open-date.test.ts`, including a check that every locale names the account and the date; the two date cases fail at the previous HEAD. Not checked natively. Shipped in the commit that moves this note.
