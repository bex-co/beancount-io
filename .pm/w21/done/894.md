# In an account holding only foreign currencies, every journal row reads "Balance: $0.00" (gap in w21/100)

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `select-account-journal.ts:544` reads `amountIn(item.balance, "USD")` (0 here), and `account-entry-row.tsx:185` formats it. w21/100's fixture held some USD.

Reproduced natively with w21/892's build and restore. Fixture: `Assets:Qa20261020Wise` with +1,000.00 EUR on 2026-10-05 and +800.00 GBP on 2026-10-12. Accounts ▸ Qa20261020Wise: the rows read "**+800.00 GBP · Not in total · Balance: $0.00**" and "**+1,000.00 EUR · Not in total · Balance: $0.00**"; the header reads $0.00 with "2 not in total".

## Fix

When a row's balance has no display-currency amount, list its holdings (`formatOtherChanges`): "Balance: 1,000.00 EUR, 800.00 GBP".

**Acceptance:** these rows show their running holdings. A unit case is added.
