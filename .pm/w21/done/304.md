# Budgets on Liabilities or Assets accounts get their favourability backwards (owner decision)

**Severity:** minor. It needs an owner decision because w1/023 kept every root. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the budget picker offers every root, and direction comes only from the amount's sign. Credit-normal accounts move negative when you spend, and on asset accounts going over a target is good.

Code-level at `main` `10857997b`. Run under Node:
- `Liabilities:CreditCard` budget 500 with 900 of charges (raw −900): below, favourable, progress 0. Any amount of spending looks fine.
- `Assets:Savings` 500 with 900 saved: above, unfavourable, 180%. Beating a savings goal shows red.

## Root cause

`mobile/src/screens/account-picker-screen/account-picker-screen.tsx:305-311` (`PICKER_ORDER.budget`), `budget-selectors.ts:444-446` and `use-budget-actuals.ts:79-82`.

## Fix

Derive direction and the actuals' sign from the account's root (`name_*` options): for liabilities, compare the absolute charges against the cap; for assets, treat it as a savings goal. Alternatively, limit the picker to Income and Expenses, with clear copy.

**Acceptance:** with the chosen rule, a 900 charge against a 500 card budget reads Above target, and 900 saved against a 500 savings goal reads favourable. Unit cases are added.

## Dedupe

w1/023 (dashboard) kept every root, reasoning that "budget math is sound for any account"; this contradicts that for credit-normal accounts. Searched `.pm/**` for "Liabilities budget", "every account type" and budgetDirection.
