# Reports ALL drops an entry pre-entered for later this month but keeps next month's (w21/523 × w21/166)

**Severity:** minor (Reports). **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: w21/523 replaces the current month with a month-to-date read (`reports-screen/selectors/month-to-date-statement.ts:9-16,36`, called at `reports-screen.tsx:125`). ALL, though, keeps every later month (no upper bound at `common/series-util.ts:256` or `select-ranged-account-tree.ts:161-167`; this is w21/166's scope). An entry dated later this month therefore disappears, while one dated next month is charted. ALL counts neither "everything" nor "through today".

## Fix

Pick one rule for ALL. Either keep the full current month whenever later months are included, or cap ALL at today too (consistent with 523, and with w21/166's other ranges).

**Acceptance:** ALL counts the $400 whenever it counts the $1,500, or counts neither. 1M is unchanged. A unit case is added.
