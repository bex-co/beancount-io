# Merchants shows a steady $10.99 bill as "~$10.99" because charges in another currency vary

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `merchants-screen/selectors/detect-recurrence.ts:628-630` sets `isApproximate` when any currency's amounts vary, but the row shows only the main currency's amount.

Reproduced natively with w21/797's build and ledger state. Fixture: "Qa20261020 Streamer" at 10.99 USD on the 5th of each month Jan–Oct 2026, except April (980 JPY) and July (1500 JPY). The Recurring row reads **"Qa20261020 Streamer · Monthly · ~$10.99 · Next Nov 5, 2026"**, though every USD charge is exactly 10.99.

## Fix

Keep the "approximate" flag per currency, and show the main currency's own flag.

**Acceptance:** this row reads "$10.99" without "~". A row whose USD amounts vary keeps "~". A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

Not w21/171, 268, 336, 461, 615 or 711 (approximate amounts within one currency, and the currency choice). Searched `.pm/**` for isApproximate and "~" together with currency.

**Resolved 2026-10-23** — `detectRecurrence` (`merchants-screen/selectors/detect-recurrence.ts`) now notes the approximate flag per currency, and `isApproximate` is the flag of the currency the row shows. With this fixture (10.99 USD every month but April and July, which were 980 and 1500 JPY), the row reads "$10.99" without "~". A bill whose USD amounts vary keeps "~". Unit cases are in `src/__tests__/detect-recurrence.test.ts`; the steady case fails at the previous HEAD. Not checked natively. Shipped in the commit that moves this note.
