# Tapping Home's partly hidden Liabilities tab selects it but never scrolls it into view: the selected pill stays cut off at "Liabilitie" under the fade

**Severity:** minor. It shows in English at the default text size. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `mobile/src/components/segmented-pages/index.tsx:195` (`handleTabPress`) sets the active index and pages the pager but never scrolls the tab strip; the range pills already do this (`selectedPillRevealOffset`). Per w2/059's measurements, the strip content is about 300 pt in a 276 pt viewport, so the last tab stays clipped.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator, English UI, default text size, Metro `--clear` at `05f7ed943`, on the disposable QA ledger (unchanged). Home ▸ tap **Liabilities** in the net-worth card's tab strip. The headline changes to −$143.25, but the selected pill's right edge and label stay cut off at "**Liabilitie**" under the 28 pt fade, beside "See all".

## Fix

Keep a ref to the strip's ScrollView, and on tab press `scrollTo` so the tapped tab's measured x and width are fully visible.

**Acceptance:** after tapping Liabilities, the whole pill and its border are visible and the fade is gone. A source case is added.

## Dedupe

w4/064 (the fade), w2/059 (strip width) and w3/171 (scrolling accepted) don't cover the selected tab staying hidden. Searched `.pm/**` for segmented, "into view" and "tab strip".

---

**Resolved 2026-10-24** — tapping a tab that is cut off at the strip's edge scrolls the strip so the whole pill shows (`tabRevealScrollX`, using each tab's measured x and width; a tab already whole stays put). Done for left-to-right strips; an RTL strip's scroll coordinates are mirrored and left to the system. Cases in `tab-strip-overflow.test.ts` (`mobile`). Not run natively.
