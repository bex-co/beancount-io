# Since w21/804, a whole-unit commodity budget ("1 BTC") reads "Above target" when spending is under the prorated budget: "Budget 0.74 · Actual 0.7419 BTC"

**Severity:** minor. A red status and an over-100% meter appear while under budget. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: w21/804 judges commodity status at 8 places (`budget-selectors.ts:446`, `statusScale`), but `budgetForInterval` (`:642-645`) still rounds the prorated budget with `budgetScale`, which is 2 for "1 BTC". 1 × 23/31 = 0.741935 becomes 0.74.

Reproduced natively with w21/853's build and restore. Fixture: `2026-01-01 custom "budget" Expenses:Qa20261020:Fees "monthly" 1 BTC`, with 0.7419 BTC spent on 2026-10-05. The Budget card reads **"Monthly · BTC · Above target"** in red, with **"Budget 0.74 BTC · Actual 0.7419 BTC · Variance +0.0019 BTC"** and a red meter. Before w21/804, ±0.005 gave "On target".

## Fix

In `budgetForInterval`, round with `statusScale(ordered, currency)` so non-fiat budgets keep 8 or more decimals.

**Acceptance:** this card reads Below target against a 0.741935 budget. w21/804's 0.003 BTC case stays Above target. Unit cases are added.
