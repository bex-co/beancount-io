# A merchant paid by card in a foreign currency shows its total in that currency ("€150.00") over rows in dollars (−$110.00, −$55.00)

**Severity:** minor (the header and its own rows disagree in currency and amount). **Estimate:** 20 minutes, plus a product call. Owner: mobile. A3: `merchant-detail-screen/selectors/merchant-stats.ts:67` sums the Expenses/Income legs' `number`, so the total is in EUR. Each row shows the card leg through `selectTransactionAmount`, in USD. w2/041 states the header is the Expenses/Income net, not the sum of the rows' cash amounts, but nothing on screen explains why €150 sits above $165 of rows.

Drawer ▸ Merchants ▸ Qa20261020 Paris Cafe reads "2 transactions · **€150.00**", above rows "−**$55.00**" (dinner) and "−**$110.00**" (lunch).

## Fix

Pick one basis for both header and rows. Either total at cost in the operating currency ($165.00, with "€150.00" as a secondary line), or show the rows in the expense currency. Keep w2/041's sign convention.

**Acceptance:** with this fixture, the header and rows are in the same currency, and the header equals the rows' sum (or explicitly shows both). A unit case is added.
