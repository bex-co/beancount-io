# Transaction detail drops the Forecast mark and the Upcoming label that the same entry's row shows

**Severity:** low. A planned entry opened from its row reads as a booked transaction. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `transaction-detail-screen.tsx:688` derives only `isPending` (`flag === "!"`), and the hero (`:803-818`) has a badge only for pending. The row badges from w21/746 and the Upcoming label (`entry-row/index.tsx:213-219`) have no counterpart on the detail screen.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator at `main` `73480f666`, on the disposable QA ledger, which was restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`. Fixture: `2026-10-30 # "Qa20261020 Supplier" "Printer paper"` with `Expenses:Qa20261020Office 42.00 USD` / `Assets:Qa20261020Checking`; today was 2026-10-23. On Transactions, the row reads **"Qa20261020 Supplier · Forecast · Upcoming · Printer paper"**. Tapping it opens a detail screen with only "October 30, 2026": no Forecast badge and no Upcoming mark.

## Fix

Show the same flag badges in the detail hero as on the row: Forecast for `#` and Pad for `P`, beside Pending for `!`. Mark an entry dated after today as Upcoming. Reuse the row's flag-label selector.

**Acceptance:** with this fixture, the detail hero shows Forecast and Upcoming. A booked past entry shows neither. A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

Follows w21/746 (done; row badges for `#`) and w21/261/471 (done; Upcoming on Home). Neither covers the detail screen. Searched `.pm/**` for detail together with forecast or upcoming.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-23** — The detail hero now shows the same marks as the entry's row. A new `selectHeroBadges` in `transaction-detail-screen/selectors/select-hero-badges.ts` reuses the row's `entryFlagBadge`:
- **Flag:** Pending for `!` (the existing warning pill), Forecast for `#`, Pad for `P`, and any other flag as written, each in a neutral pill.
- **Upcoming:** added for an entry dated after today, judged by `useToday`.

The fixture (`#`, 2026-10-30, today 2026-10-23) reads "October 30, 2026 · Forecast · Upcoming"; a booked past entry shows neither. The meta row now wraps, so the date and two pills fit at large text sizes.

Unit cases are in `selectors/__tests__/select-hero-badges.test.ts`, including a source check that the hero draws through the selector; it fails at the previous HEAD. The screen's hooks can't run in the unit runner, and this was not checked natively. Shipped in the commit that moves this note.
