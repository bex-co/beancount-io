# w21/749 regression: Cash flow now cuts the right column's suffix line too, so w21/747's "Savings (expenses)" reads "Savings (expense…"

**Severity:** trivial (the disambiguating suffix loses its last letters). **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: w21/749 applies `fitSankeyLabel(text, LABEL_COL - LABEL_GAP, fontSizes.xs)` to every label line (`reports-screen/components/cash-flow-sankey.tsx`), including the second, suffix line that w21/540 and w21/747 rely on. "(expenses)" at the label font is slightly wider than the column, so it's cut to "(expense…". English is the shortest of the 13 languages' suffixes, so longer translations are cut more.

Native (iPhone 17e, Metro `--clear` at `baa6d0581`; disposable ledger restored byte-for-byte): w21/747's fixture (`Expenses:Qa20261020:Savings` 500, Rent 300, Salary 3,000 in October). Reports ▸ 1M ▸ Cash flow's right column reads "Savings" over "**(expense…**", then Rent, then Savings (the surplus). Before w21/749, it read "Savings (expenses)" in full (w21/747's recheck).

## Fix

Size the right column's label width to its actual space (the right margin is wider than the left), or let the suffix line wrap instead of truncating. Keep the left-column fix from w21/749.

**Acceptance:** "Savings (expenses)" and the "(income)" / "(expenses)" suffixes show in full in English, and are not cut mid-word in the longer languages. A render case is added.
