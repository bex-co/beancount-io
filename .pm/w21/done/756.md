# A `pad` into a child account closed by `close_tree` still raises no error (w21/737 variant)

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: ledger. A3: `generated-closes.ts:45` checks transactions only. After a close, Beancount allows only balance, document and note entries, so a pad is an error.

Proven with the real engine under ts-node (as `yarn verify:rustledger` runs it) against Beancount 3.2.3 `bean-check`, also at the commit before the fix (the agent's run). Not on production: the ledger service hasn't been redeployed with the fix. w21/737's opens and close, plus `Equity:Qa20261020Opening`, `2024-05-01 pad Assets:Qa20261020Broker:VTI Equity:Qa20261020Opening` and `2024-05-02 balance … 5 USD`. bean-check reports "Invalid reference to inactive account" at the pad's line (twice); the engine reports 0 errors.

## Fix

Apply the generated-close check to pad directives too (all directive types except balance, document and note).

**Acceptance:** this fixture matches bean-check. A unit case is added.

Variant of w21/737 (done). Searched `.pm/**` for pad together with close_tree.

**Resolved 2026-10-23** — new `inactivePads` in `backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/generated-closes.ts`, run by the engine on every parse. A pad naming an account after its close gets bean-check's "Invalid reference to inactive account 'X'" (E1003) on the pad's line. It applies whether close_tree generated that close or the source wrote it; checked against bean-check 3.2.3, which gives line 6 for both, while the engine gave nothing for either. w21/753's rule holds: a close dated before the account's open doesn't count. Balances, documents and notes stay allowed after a close, as in Beancount. Deliberate deviation: one row per pad. bean-check repeats the error for the padding transaction the pad makes, on the same line, and w21/755 removed exactly that kind of duplicate row. Unit cases are in `generated-closes.test.ts`, live engine cases for both closes in `scripts/verify-rustledger.ts`; at the previous HEAD the live cases get no error. Needs a ledger-service deploy. Shipped in the commit that moves this note.
