# w21/737 regression: E1003 is reported twice when `close_tree` is combined with a fava plugin (amortize, forecast, link/tag documents)

**Severity:** trivial. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: ledger. A3: `engine.ts:759` adds `inactiveAfterGeneratedCloses` on top of the revalidation at `engine.ts:707`. That revalidation already sees the generated closes whenever a TS-ported fava plugin is declared.

Proven with the real engine under ts-node (as `yarn verify:rustledger` runs it) against Beancount 3.2.3 `bean-check`, also at the commit before the fix (the agent's run). Not on production: the ledger service hasn't been redeployed with the fix. w21/737's fixture plus `plugin "fava.plugins.tag_discovered_documents"` gives two identical "Invalid reference to inactive account" rows at main.beancount:10 (bean-check reports one). Before w21/737 there was one.

## Fix

Dedupe the generated-close check against errors the revalidation already produced (same code, account and location), or skip it when the revalidation ran.

**Acceptance:** one error with any plugin combination. A unit case is added.
