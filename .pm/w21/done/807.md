# VoiceOver's budget chart summary counts spending from before the budget existed: "Actual $2.4K of $222.6 budgeted across 10 periods, 0 over target"

**Severity:** minor (accessibility). **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `budgetChartSummary` (`budget-screen/.../budget-labels.ts:268-302`) sums Actual over every period. Periods before the first budget entry are drawn greyed and unjudged, but their spending still counts.

Not run natively (VoiceOver). Under `tsx` (the agent's proof), a 300/month budget dated 2026-10-21, with $250 spent in each of Jan–Sep and $100 in October, reads at YTD "Actual $2.4K of $222.6 budgeted across 10 periods, 0 over target".

## Fix

Sum only the periods that have a budget (`favorables[i] !== null`), or say from when the budget applies.

**Acceptance:** the summary's Actual covers only budgeted periods. A unit case is added.

Not w21/033, w21/457 or w21/098. Searched `.pm/**` for budgetChartSummary.

**Resolved 2026-10-23** — The budget chart's spoken summary now covers only the periods that had a budget. The new `selectBudgetedPeriods` (`budget-screen/selectors/select-budget-card-stats.ts`) marks the periods that ended before the first directive, the ones drawn greyed and unjudged. `budgetChartSummary` (`budget-labels.ts`) takes those marks and leaves those periods out of its span, Actual, budget and count, and `BudgetGroupCard` passes them. The note's YTD case (a budget dated Oct 21, $250 spent in each of Jan–Sep) reads "Budget versus actual for OCT. Actual $100 of $222.6 budgeted across 1 period, 0 over target". A budget that had none yet keeps every period. Periods that are budgeted but unjudged for "Not in total" are still counted.

Unit and source cases are in `selectors/__tests__/budget-labels.test.ts`; they fail at the previous HEAD. VoiceOver can't be driven here. Shipped in the commit that moves this note.
