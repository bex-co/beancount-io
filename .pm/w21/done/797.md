# Merchants' Recurring row keeps the old currency after a payee switches: "Monthly · ¥120,000" for rent now paid as $2,000 (gap in w21/615)

**Severity:** low to moderate. Someone who moved countries keeps seeing the old rent until the new currency outnumbers it in a 37-month window. w21/615's own text named this case. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `merchants-screen/selectors/detect-recurrence.ts:601-609` picks the row's currency as the one with the most charges in the window.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator, English UI, Metro `--clear` at `54323231c`, on the disposable QA ledger, which was restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`. Fixture: "Qa20261020 Rent Moved" with 120000 JPY on the 1st of Jan–Jun 2026, then 2000.00 USD on the 1st of Jul–Oct. Drawer ▸ Merchants ▸ Recurring reads **"Qa20261020 Rent Moved · Monthly · ¥120,000 · Next Nov 1, 2026"**.

## Fix

Take the currency of the latest charges (for example the last three), or of the last charge.

**Acceptance:** this fixture's row shows $2,000.00. A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

Gap in w21/615 (done; it covered a single foreign charge). Searched `.pm/**` for detect-recurrence together with currency, and 615.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-23** — `detectRecurrence` (`merchants-screen/selectors/detect-recurrence.ts`) now takes a recurring bill's currency from its latest three charges when they agree. With this fixture (JPY January–June, USD July–October), the last three charges are in USD, so the row reads $2,000.00 instead of ¥120,000. When the latest three are mixed, w21/615's rule still applies: the most charges decide, and a tie goes to the latest. A $10.99 bill with one ¥980 charge abroad among its recent charges stays in dollars.

Unit cases are in `src/__tests__/detect-recurrence.test.ts`: the switch case fails at the previous HEAD, and the abroad case pins w21/615. Not checked natively. Shipped in the commit that moves this note.

Native recheck (same build and restore; the note's Rent Moved fixture of JPY Jan–Jun, then USD Jul–Oct): Drawer ▸ Merchants ▸ Recurring now reads "Qa20261020 Rent Moved · Monthly · **$2,000.00** · Next Nov 1, 2026", instead of ¥120,000. Fixed.
