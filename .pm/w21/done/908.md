# Reports' Recent transactions lists entries dated after today, which the charts above it leave out

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile.

**Root cause (A3).** w21/523 made the chart, Cash flow and the breakdowns read the current month only up to today (`reports-screen.tsx:142-144`). The list below them was not changed:
- It still asks for rows through the end of the month (`account-transactions-card.tsx:105`, `accountTransactionsWindow`).
- It caps rows only by month (`mobile/src/screens/reports-screen/selectors/select-account-transactions.ts:99`, `month <= anchorMonth`), never by today.

Home's card drops future rows with `recentTransactionsUpTo` (w21/471, w21/664); Reports doesn't.

Not run natively in this pass. Proven with `tsx`: with today 2026-10-23, Groceries 120 on 10-05 and Rent 400 on 10-30, 1M lists `['2026-10-30 Rent (pre-entered)', '2026-10-05 Groceries']`, while the Expenses total is `120`. The rent row shows as "Upcoming" (w21/261) but is in no figure above it. Several pre-entered entries, such as forecast copies, can take most of the 10 slots.

**Native steps:** add `Expenses:QaSpend` 120.00 on 10-05 and 400.00 on 10-30. Open Reports at 1M and compare the expense bar (about $120) with the top Recent transactions row.

## Fix

For every range except All, keep only rows dated on or before today, as Home does. Filter in `selectAccountTransactions`, or apply `recentTransactionsUpTo` in the card.

**Acceptance:** with that fixture, 1M, 3M and 6M list only Groceries, and All still lists Rent. A unit case is added in `select-account-transactions.test.ts`.
