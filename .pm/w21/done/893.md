# An account first funded this month shows "Not enough data to chart yet" on every range chip, under "+$1,000.00" (side effect of w21/491)

**Severity:** minor. Every new account hits this for its first month. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `account-detail-screen/selectors/select-account-balance-series.ts:176` (`boundedMonthEnds`) drops every month before the first posting, including the $0 month right before it, so the series has a single point. The chart needs two, while the change row already measures from 0.

Reproduced natively with w21/892's build and restore. Fixture: `Assets:Qa20261020Fresh` with one 1,000.00 USD deposit on 2026-10-05. Accounts ▸ Qa20261020Fresh reads "**$1,000.00 · +$1,000.00**" over "**Not enough data to chart yet**" (6M; per the agent's `tsx` run, every chip gives one point).

## Fix

Add a zero point at the end of the month before the first posting (for the units series too), so the line starts at 0.

**Acceptance:** this account draws a line from $0 to $1,000. A unit case is added.
