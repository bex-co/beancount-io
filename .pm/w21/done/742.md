# Account detail's "1 not in total" can't be opened, so the excluded amount appears nowhere on that screen

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: account detail's header gets the valuation status from `common/valuation.ts:374-391`, which gives counts only (the holdings "live in the detail sheet"), via `select-account-balance-series.ts:62-73` and `account-detail-screen.tsx:272-275`. The `ValuationSheet` is mounted only on Home (`home-screen.tsx:277`), and w4/m27's display rule 2 says the status line opens it. The Accounts root's "N not in total" doesn't open anything either.

Native (iPhone 17e, Metro `--clear` at `b2ec6ca23`; disposable ledger restored byte-for-byte; today 2026-10-23): `Assets:Qa20261020Wise` with 1000.00 USD (08-05) and 500.00 EUR (08-06). On Accounts, the row says "$1,000.00 · **Not in total: 500.00 EUR**". Opening it, the header reads only "**Balance · 1 not in total**" over $1,000.00, and tapping that line does nothing. The EUR amount appears only on the 08-06 row further down.

## Fix

Mount the valuation sheet on account detail and make the status line open it (or name the amounts inline, as the Accounts row does). Do the same for the Accounts root's count.

**Acceptance:** tapping "1 not in total" on account detail shows 500.00 EUR. A unit or render case is added.
