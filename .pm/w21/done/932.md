# Reports' two chart summaries read amounts in the short axis format, so VoiceOver says "$222.6" for $222.58

**Severity:** low (VoiceOver). **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile.

w21/821 moved Budget's summary to full amounts, and Home Spending already uses them. The two Reports summaries didn't change.

**Root cause (A3).**
- `mobile/src/common/d3/income-expense-bar-chart.tsx:188-210` formats income, expenses and net with `formatShortMoneyWithCurrency`.
- `sankey-data.ts:665` (`money`) does the same.
- Below 1K, `shortNumber` keeps one decimal (`number-utils.ts:141-146`).

**Native steps:** open Reports and read the `accessibilityLabel` of the Income & expenses chart and of the Cash flow card.

## Fix

In both summaries, read out `formatSignedMoneyWithCurrency(roundMoney(sum, currency), currency)`, as `budgetChartSummary` does.
