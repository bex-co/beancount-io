# In Persian, a negative chart tick of 1,000 or more draws its minus on the far side: "-$2 هزار" renders as "رازه $2-"

**Severity:** minor (fa). A deficit tick reads as a positive amount. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `formatShortMoneyWithCurrency` returns `-$2 هزار`. The Persian suffix makes the label right-to-left, and nothing isolates the signed number. The ticks are drawn at `scrollable-axis-chart.tsx:84`, `income-expense-bar-chart.tsx:121` and `bar-chart-d3.tsx:59`.

Not run natively (it needs Persian, an RTL restart). CoreText visual order (the agent's harness, which reproduced w21/095 exactly):
- `-$2 هزار` draws as `رازه $2-`, `-€1.5 هزار` as `رازه €1.5-`, and `-2 هزار MUSD` as `MUSD رازه 2-`.
- The control `-$500` draws as `-$500`, and with the number isolated (`⁦-$2⁩ هزار`) the tick draws correctly.

Screens: Reports ▸ the income/expense chart with a deficit month, and Home Spending with a net-refund month.

## Fix

Wrap the signed number-and-symbol part in `isolateLtr` before adding the suffix word.

**Acceptance:** fa negative compact ticks read with the minus next to the number. A unit case is added.

The mobile twin of w4/1018 (dashboard). Not w21/095, 249 or 698. Searched `.pm/**` for tick together with Persian, and هزار.
