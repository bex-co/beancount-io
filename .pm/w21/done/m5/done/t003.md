## Objective

A BQL query over the fixture must give the same cash and position as the balance reads.

## Context

- BQL runs inside the engine over the files (`queryLedgerFilesResultInProcess`), where the failed entry is booked as written
- Its location is known from `locateBookErrors` (file and line)

## Steps

1. Blank the failed transaction's lines in the sources handed to the query (keep line numbers), then run the query.
2. Test: `SELECT` of the broker position and cash equals the balance reads on the fixture.

## Files

- `backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/engine.ts`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A BQL balance of the fixture's cash account is 4000.00 and the broker holds 10 VTI.
- [ ] A ledger with no booking errors is queried exactly as before.

## Out of scope

- Unrelated booking behavior of the engine.

## Shipped

`queryLedgerFilesResultInProcess` probes its (cached) parse for `BOOK` errors and, when there are any, runs the query over the source with those transactions blanked line for line (`withoutUnbookedTransactions`); a ledger with no booking error is queried exactly as before, on the same single parse. Checked on the real engine: the fixture's cash is 4000.00 and the broker holds 10 VTI, matching the ledger without the sale. `engine-query-unbooked.test.ts` fails without the change.
