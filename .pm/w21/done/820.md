# Scrubbing a flat line chart turns its grey line green or red while the change row stays grey "+$0.00 (0.00%)" (deferred half of w21/750)

**Severity:** minor. On a dormant ledger, Home's 1M net-worth chart is flat and grey at rest, and a finger on it paints it green. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `mobile/src/common/d3/interactive-line-chart.tsx:691-697`. The line goes neutral only when `scrubFavorable === null && restingFlat`. While scrubbing, `scrubTrendFavorable` uses a raw `value >= opening` test, while `ScrubHeader` (`:488`) greys the row whenever the change rounds to nothing. The chart's own comment says the line and the figure "can never disagree". w21/750's resolution says the scrubbed line "still follows the scrubbed point's direction".

Native baseline (iPhone 17e, Metro `--clear` at the current build; disposable ledger at `f1a0b83c`, which has no activity in Sep or Oct): Home ▸ Net Worth ▸ **1M** shows a flat grey line and "+$0.00 (0.00%)". A tap on the plot doesn't start a scrub, and this automation can't drag, so the scrubbed colour wasn't seen natively. Under `tsx` (the agent's proof), the series [17108.51, 17108.51] with baseline 17108.51 gives `rowFlat: true` with a green line at either scrub index. The w21/237 case (2,500,000 → 2,499,988) is flat at rest and turns red when its last point is scrubbed.

## Fix

While scrubbing, apply the same flat check to the scrubbed value (`changeReadsFlat(baseline, numbers[index], format)`), and draw neutral when it reads flat.

**Acceptance:** scrubbing a flat series keeps the line grey, and a real change still colours it. A unit case is added.
