# The range pills (1M, 3M, 6M, YTD, 1Y, ALL) are about 28 pt tall with no touch slop, so a tap a little above or below one misses

**Severity:** low to moderate (a primary control on Home, Reports, Budget and the account pages). This is the pill sibling of the fixed w21/485. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile.

**Root cause (A3).** `mobile/src/components/time-range-pills/index.tsx`: `PILL_HEIGHT = PILL_PADDING_V * 2 + 16` (`:87`) is 28 pt, and the pill's `TouchableOpacity` (about `:295`) has no `hitSlop` or `minHeight`. Neighbouring pills sit 6 pt apart. The pills are used by Home's charts card, Reports, Budget, the balance chart card and the account picker.

**Native (iPhone 17e, 2026-10-24).** Home → Net Worth chart, 6M selected, with the 3M pill centred at y ≈ 419 pt:
- Tapping the 3M pill's centre selects 3M (the control).
- Tapping 19 pt above its centre or 19 pt below it does nothing: 6M stays selected.

A 44 pt target would have registered both taps, so the effective touch height is under 38 pt. I restored 6M afterwards.

## Fix

Add `hitSlop={{ top: 8, bottom: 8 }}` to the pill, or give it `minHeight: iconActionSize`. If the visual size stays, only widen the target.

**Acceptance:** a source test pins the pill's touch height at 44 pt or more, as w21/485's test does.

## Dedupe

Searched for "TimeRangePills", "PILL_HEIGHT", "range pill" and "touch target". w21/560, w21/886 and w21/891 cover labels and scrolling only.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-24** — each range pill gets a vertical `hitSlop` of `(44 − PILL_HEIGHT) / 2` on top and bottom, so the touch target is 44 pt while the pill is still drawn 28 pt tall (no layout change; the slop is vertical only, so neighbouring pills 6 pt apart don't overlap). A source test pins the slop and its use on the pill and fails without it. Native recheck: not measured; hit slop can't be read from the driver, so it rests on the source pin (`mobile`).
