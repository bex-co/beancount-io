# A merchant marked Recurring still reads "Not enough history" when its last payments fall just inside the 37-month window (w21/617 incomplete)

**Severity:** minor (merchant detail). **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: w21/617 reports `inactive` only when `endedBeforeSeriesWindow` holds (`merchants-screen/selectors/resolve-recurring.ts:27`), meaning the last payment predates the series cutoff, which today is 2023-09-22. A payee whose last one or two payments fall just inside the window has 1–2 points in the series. That is too few to analyse, it isn't "ended", and the verdict is `unknown`.

Native (iPhone 17e at `d731eb32a`; disposable ledger restored byte-for-byte; today 2026-10-22): 58 monthly "QA Gym" payments of 45.00 USD on the 1st, from 2019-01-01 to 2023-10-01. Merchants ▸ QA Gym reads "58 transactions · $2,610.00 · Jan 1, 2019 – Oct 1, 2023". Switching **Recurring** on adds the badge "Not enough history". Under `tsx`: one date in the window, `enough: false`, `ended: false`, verdict `unknown`.

## Fix

Base "ended" on recency rather than on whether a payment falls inside the window: for example, the last payment is older than a few of the payee's own cycles (or more than N months ago). Alternatively, when the in-window series is too short but the full count isn't, say "No recent payments".

**Acceptance:** this fixture reads "No recent payments" (`inactive`), the 2019–2023 fixture from w21/617 still does, and a new payee with two payments this year still reads "Not enough history". Unit cases are added.
