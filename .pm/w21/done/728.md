# Clearing a negative amount leaves a hidden "-", so an empty-looking field shows "Use digits and one decimal point" and blocks Done

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the amount field's change handler stores `isNegative ? `-${input}` : input` (`multi-postings-transaction/multi-postings-transaction-screen.tsx:329-331`). Deleting every digit of a negative amount therefore stores `"-"`. The field renders only its "0.00" placeholder and the sign badge disappears (`hasNonZeroAmount` is false), but `hasUnparsedAmount` is true and `validatePostings` returns `invalidAmount`. The draft also counts as changed, so Back asks to discard a field that looks untouched, and typing again silently produces a negative amount.

Native (iPhone 17e, Metro `--clear` at `47afd172e`; nothing saved, forms discarded; today 2026-10-23): Home ▸ + ▸ Enter new transaction. On Assets:Bank:Checking, type 3 and 0, tap the green "+" badge so it turns to a red "−" ("30" in red, auto leg +30.00), select the digits and press delete. The field shows the grey "**0.00**" placeholder with no badge. Under the postings, Remainder reads "—" and the red error reads "**Use digits and one decimal point, like 1234.56**". A cleared positive amount shows no error. Under `tsx` (the agent's proof), the stored input is `"-"` and the amount is null.

## Fix

Treat an input that is only a sign as empty (store "" and keep the sign as a separate flag), or strip the sign when the digits are cleared.

**Acceptance:** clearing a negative amount looks and behaves exactly like clearing a positive one (no format error; Done says the amount can't be zero), and retyping digits keeps the chosen sign visibly. A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

Not w21/232 or w21/666 (pasted signs), or w21/159 or w21/321 (seeded "0.00"). Searched `.pm/**` for `isNegative` together with clear, and "hidden minus".
