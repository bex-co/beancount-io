# Home's Latest Updates rows carry only a date, so same-message commits on one day look like duplicates

**Severity:** trivial. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `home-screen/components/feed-card.tsx:105-108` formats each row's date with `formatFeedDate` (`common/date-format.ts`, month, day and year only). Since w21/599 added the commit message line, repeated commits read identically. w21/487 gave Notifications' rows a time, but its resolution left Home's card unchanged.

Not run natively (the card is below the fold). Proven with production data: the read-only `getFeed` for the QA account, rendered through `sortFeedItems`, `feedRowDetail` and `formatFeedDate` under `tsx` (TZ America/Los_Angeles), gives three identical rows "Committed to mcp-agent-eval | qa-20261020: mobile append | Ledger · Oct 23, 2026" and two identical "… restore snapshot" rows.

## Fix

Show a time (or a relative time) on rows from today and yesterday, as Notifications does since w21/487.

**Acceptance:** two same-day commits with the same message are distinguishable on Home. A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

Left over from w21/487 (done); made visible by w21/599 (done). Searched `.pm/**` for formatFeedDate and "Latest Updates" together with time.
