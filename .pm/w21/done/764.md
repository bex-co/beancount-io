# On LY, a weekly budget's last bar is labelled "1/4" (a 2026 date), and VoiceOver reads the span as "1/5–1/4"

**Severity:** trivial. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: weekly bars are labelled by the week's Sunday (`budget-labels.ts:54-56`), and Dec 29–31, 2025 belongs to the week ending Jan 4, 2026. `axis-year-marks.ts` adds no year mark, because every series date is in 2025. The spoken summary uses `labels[0]–labels[last]` (`budget-labels.ts:262-267`).

Not run natively (it needs a full-year weekly budget plus scrolling the chart to its end). Under `tsx` (the agent's proof), `2025-01-01 custom "budget" Expenses:Qa20261020Food "weekly" 70.00 USD` plus a 50.00 USD expense on 2025-12-30, on LY: the axis ends `… 12/21, 12/28, 1/4`, and the summary's span is "1/5–1/4" across 53 periods. The last bar's budget is $30 (3 days).

## Fix

Clip the label of a partial last week to the window's end ("12/31"), or add the year, and build the spoken span from the window dates.

**Acceptance:** LY's last label and spoken span stay within 2025. A unit case is added.

**Resolved 2026-10-23** — `periodAxisLabel` (`budget-screen/selectors/budget-labels.ts`) takes the last day of a window that ended before today and ends the last period there. The Budget card passes it from the new `timeWindowEnd`, which reads "year-1" or a "start - end" range. On LY a weekly budget's last bar (the week of Dec 29) now reads "12/31", not "1/4", and the spoken span, built from the same labels, reads "1/5–12/31". A window running through today passes no end, so an in-progress week keeps its own end, as w21/219 set. Unit cases are in `period-axis-label.test.ts`, and the card's source assertion is updated; both fail at the previous HEAD. Not run natively. Shipped in the commit that moves this note.

Recheck (2026-10-23): not exercised natively. It needs a full-year 2025 weekly budget, and the chart scrolled to its end to read the last LY label. The automation here cannot scroll, and the spoken summary needs VoiceOver. Covered by the `budget-labels` unit cases named in the resolution.
