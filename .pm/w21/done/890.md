# Every January, Reports' YTD shows an empty report under "Showing through December …, the latest month with entries"

**Severity:** minor (every January until the first January entry; also any ledger whose entries stop in an earlier year). **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `reports-screen/selectors/select-income-expense-chart.ts:56` (`staleAnchorMonth`) returns the anchor for every range except ALL, but YTD always starts in January of the current year (w4/037; `series-util.ts` `rangeStartMonth`), so it never shows an anchor month from an earlier year.

Not run natively (it needs a January clock, or a ledger whose entries end in an earlier year). Under `tsx` (the agent's proof), with the clock at 2027-01-05 and entries through 2026-12-20, YTD gives `months: []` and banner `2026-12`. With entries ending 2025-08 and today 2026-10-23, it gives `months: []` and banner `2025-08`. The screen shows the banner over "No cash flow in this range" and empty breakdowns.

## Fix

Return no banner for YTD when the anchor month is before January of the current year, or show a YTD-specific line ("No entries yet in 2027").

**Acceptance:** neither case shows a "Showing through …" banner over an empty YTD. A unit case is added.
