# A posting in CLF, UYW or UYI shows different decimals from the headline amount

**Severity:** low. Chile's UF (CLF) is used for rent and mortgages. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile.

**Root cause (A3).** Two checks disagree on what counts as a currency:
- The postings decide it with `getCurrencySymbol(currency) !== ""` (`mobile/src/screens/transaction-detail-screen/selectors/select-transaction-detail.ts:143`).
- The headline uses `isFiatCurrency` (`common/currency-util.ts:111`), which also counts the currencies listed in the minor-units table.

`currency-icons` has no symbol for CLF, UYW or UYI, so only the headline treats them as currencies.

Not run natively in this pass. `tsx` proof: for 12.5 CLF, the headline shows `-12.5000 CLF`, while Money flow shows `+12.5 CLF` and `-12.5 CLF`.

**Native steps:** open a `12.5 CLF` transaction; the headline and Money flow disagree.

## Fix

Use `isFiatCurrency(currency)` at `:143`.

**Acceptance:** for 12.5 CLF, the headline and both postings show `12.5000`.

## Dedupe

Searched for "CLF" and "UYW" (no hits) and "minor units" (w21/445, w21/506, w21/595 are different).
