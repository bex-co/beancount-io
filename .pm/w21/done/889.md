# A price dated after the last entry moves Reports' anchor onto an empty month: "Showing through September 2026, the latest month with entries" over "No cash flow in this range"

**Severity:** minor to moderate. Ledgers that fetch prices (bean-price) commonly have prices dated after their last transaction. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: ledger or mobile. A3: `backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/interval-series.ts:210` (`firstAndLastDate`) lets price directives extend the series end. Mobile's `selectStatementAnchorMonth` then treats that empty month as the latest with entries. Under `tsx` (the agent's proof), a price dated in the current month (2026-10-05) instead empties October and switches off w21/166's stale-ledger fallback, with no banner.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator, English UI, Metro `--clear` at `69037a0e2`, on the disposable QA ledger (entries end in August 2026), restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`. Fixture: `2026-09-15 price QAX 10.00 USD` alone. Reports ▸ **1M** reads "**Showing through September 2026, the latest month with entries.**", with "No cash flow in this range" in both the chart and Cash flow. Without the price, it shows August's figures.

## Fix

Anchor on the latest month with transaction activity, capped at the current month: on mobile, from the latest point with non-empty balances; or in the ledger, end flow-series spans at the last transaction and keep prices for balance series only.

**Acceptance:** with this fixture, 1M shows August under "Showing through August 2026". Unit cases cover the price-in-September and price-in-current-month cases.

## Dedupe

Not w21/166 (future-dated transactions) or w21/491 (account balance series). Searched `.pm/**` for firstAndLastDate, and price together with anchor.
