# The dashboard's net worth headline reads the series' last point, so a future-dated entry makes it show next month and a falling arrow

**Severity:** moderate, and only for ledgers with entries dated after today. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. **Owner: dashboard.** The mobile side (w21/641, w21/665) already caps at today.

**Root cause (A3).**
- Mobile: `latestBalance(points, today)` (`mobile/src/common/series-util.ts:169`) takes the latest point in or before the current month.
- Dashboard: `net-worth-card.tsx:283-285` takes `data.slice(-12)` and uses `.at(-1)` as the headline and `.at(-2)` as the "from previous month" baseline, with no cap at today; `describeLatestNetWorth` (`net-worth-valuation.ts`, about `:210`) uses `market.at(-1)` the same way. The ledger service returns months past today when entries are future-dated.

Not reproduced in the browser (code path only). Example: a series of Sep 1000, Oct 1200, Nov 700 (the November point comes from a transaction dated Nov 2), with today at 2026-10-24.
- Mobile headline: `{"USD":"1200.00"}`.
- Dashboard headline: 700 for 2026-11-30, with a change of `-500.00` and "from previous month", comparing November to October, under a red falling arrow.

w0/029 handles the same series only for the Money movement panel.

## Fix

In the net worth card and `describeLatestNetWorth`, drop points whose month is after the current month before choosing the latest and previous points.

**Acceptance:** with the fixture above and today 2026-10-24, the headline is 1200 and the change is +200; once November arrives, the headline is 700.
