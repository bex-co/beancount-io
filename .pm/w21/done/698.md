# Arabic-script currency symbols (KWD, BHD, OMR…) flip money right-to-left: "1,234.500ك.د-" puts the minus at the far right, so a liability reads as positive

**Severity:** minor (wrong-looking signs for Gulf-currency ledgers, even in an English app). **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `annotateCurrency` (`common/number-utils.ts:285-293`) glues the symbol in front of the number, and `currency-icons` gives KWD as "د.ك", BHD as ".د.ب" (with a stray leading period) and OMR as "ر.ع.". iOS lays out each Text in its natural direction, so the first Arabic letter turns the whole string right-to-left, and the minus that leads the string is drawn at the far right. The Home hero splits the symbol into its own run (`components/amount-text/rolling-glyphs.ts:32`), which gives yet another order. w21/070's resolution noted "RTL symbols such as KWD's 'د.ك' still prefix", but this was never filed.

Native (iPhone 17e, fresh Metro `--clear` at `4d9abc812`; disposable ledger with its `operating_currency` line swapped for the probe, then restored byte-for-byte; today 2026-10-23): `option "operating_currency" "KWD"` plus `Liabilities:Qa20261020Card -1234.500 KWD` against `Equity:Qa20261020Open`. The Accounts tab shows Liabilities and Qa20261020Card as **"1,234.500ك.د-"**, with the minus at the far right, and Equity in red as "1,234.500ك.د". Home ▸ Net Worth shows the hero as "ك.د-1,234.500", with the change line reversed: "(%0.00) 0.000ك.د+". Under `tsx`/CoreText (the agent's proof): `formatSignedMoneyWithCurrency(-1234.5,"KWD")` = "-د.ك1,234.500", BHD = "-.د.ب1,234.500", and the visual order is `1,234.500|ك|.|د|-`.

## Fix

Isolate the number from an RTL symbol: either put the code or symbol after the number for RTL-script symbols, or wrap them in LTR isolates (U+2066…U+2069) / set `writingDirection: "ltr"` on amount text. Fix BHD's symbol string.

**Acceptance:** with an English UI, a −1,234.500 KWD balance shows its minus beside the digits on Accounts, Home's hero and its change line, and BHD has no stray period. Persian and Arabic UI behaviour (w21/095/249/536) is unchanged. Render or unit cases are added.

## Dedupe

Noted but not filed in w21/070's resolution (done). Distinct from w21/095, w21/249 and w21/536 (the Persian UI). Searched `.pm/**` for KWD, "د.ك", annotateCurrency and RTL together with currency.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-23** — `getCurrencySymbol` wraps a symbol in Arabic or Hebrew script in a right-to-left isolate (U+2067…U+2069, `isolateRtlSymbol` in `common/bidi.ts`), so the symbol keeps its own order without turning the figure around it right-to-left: −1,234.500 KWD now lays out as `-ك.د1,234.500` (minus at the start, as "-$1,234.50"), the hero's separate sign-and-symbol run as `-ك.د`, the change line as `+ك.د0.000 (0.00%)`, and a currency-after short form as `-1,5 Tsd. ك.د`. Checked against Apple's CoreText on macOS (the before string reproduced `1,234.500ك.د-` exactly). BHD's symbol is `د.ب` without the stray leading period; OMR, SDG and MAD drop the closing period that landed against the minus. Persian sentences keep their w21/095 LTR isolates, with the RTL isolate nested inside (USD unchanged). Tests in `number-utils.test.ts` (fail without the change) and `bidi.test.ts`; shipped in the commit that closes this note (`mobile`). Not re-run natively.
