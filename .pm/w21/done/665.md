# Home's net-worth caption and cost footnote still value next month's holdings: "Unrealized +$200.00" for a lot bought in November (w21/641 incomplete)

**Severity:** minor (Home's headline). **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: w21/641 caps Home's curves at today through `carryBalanceToToday`, but the valuation beside them still reads the series' last point. `home-screen/selectors/select-balance-sheet-series.ts:36-37` takes `points[points.length - 1]` from the market series, plus `latestBalance` of the cost and units series. When there are entries next month, that point is November. The caption ("At market value"), the footnote (cost and unrealized) and the "N not in total" note therefore describe next month's holdings, while the chart ends today.

## Fix

**Acceptance:** with this fixture, Home shows no market caption or cost footnote today, and the caption appears once the buy's date arrives. Unit cases are added.
