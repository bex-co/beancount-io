# Update Budget is refused for a closed parent account whose children are still open, though its card offers Update

**Severity:** moderate. Budgets on a parent category are supported (w21/396), but once the parent has been closed and split into children, its budget can't be updated or added again. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile.

This is a regression of w21/486, caused by w21/877.

**Root cause (A3).**
- The form's date check, `mobile/src/screens/add-budget-screen/add-budget-screen.tsx:262`, uses `closedBeforeDate` (`common/account-close-dates.ts:248-255`). That reads only the account's own close date.
- The card uses `budgetEndsOn` and `budgetHasEnded` (`budget-group-card.tsx:218,294`). Those were made aware of child accounts in w21/486.
- The account picker lists the closed parent too: `picker-sections.ts:276` adds parents back with `withParentAccounts(open)`.

Not run natively in this pass. `tsx` proof with w21/486's fixture: `Expenses:Food` opened 2024-01-01 and closed 2026-06-30; `:Groceries` and `:Restaurants` opened 2026-06-01 and are still open; today is 2026-10-23.
- `budgetHasEnded` is `false`, so the card shows Update.
- `closedBeforeDate("Expenses:Food", "2026-10-23")` returns `"2026-06-30"`, so Done is disabled and the form says "Expenses:Food was closed on June 30, 2026".
- The picker lists `Expenses:Food`, and Add then hits the same refusal.

**Native steps:**
1. Use that fixture plus `custom "budget" Expenses:Food "monthly" 500 USD`.
2. Go to Budget and tap Update on the Food card.

Done stays grey under the close message.

## Fix

- Refuse a date only when the `budgetEndsOn`-based `closedBefore` is true, and show the subtree's end date.
- In the budget picker, keep a closed parent only while one of its children is open on that date.

**Acceptance:**
- With this fixture, Update and Add on `Expenses:Food` can save today.
- A closed account with no open children is still refused, as in w21/877.
- Unit cases are added in `account-close-dates.test.ts`.

## Dedupe

Only done w21/877 mentions `closedBeforeDate`. Searched for "closedBeforeDate", "budgetEndsOn" and "closed parent".

---

**Resolved 2026-10-24** — `closedBeforeDate` asks `budgetEndsOn`, as the card does, so a parent closed when it was split into children that are still open is not refused by Update or Add; a closed account with no open child, or whose children all closed, is still refused, with the subtree's end date. The budget picker needed no change: its parents come only from `withParentAccounts(open)`, so a closed parent is listed only while a child is open. Cases in `account-close-dates.test.ts` (the first fails without the change; `mobile`). Not run natively.
