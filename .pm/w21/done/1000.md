# The Accounts tab's expand chevron is a small target nested inside a row that opens Account detail, so a near miss navigates away

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile.

**Root cause (A3).** `mobile/src/components/account-table/account-table.tsx`, about `:303-312` (`hitSlop` at `:310`): the chevron `TouchableOpacity` is 18 pt wide (`INDENT_STEP = 18`, `:59`) by 14 pt tall, plus `hitSlop` of 12, about 42×38 pt. It sits inside a row where every other tap drills into Account detail.

Not run natively in this pass (code level only).

**Native steps:** Accounts tab → tap just left or right of a parent's chevron (for example "Assets"). It navigates to Account detail instead of toggling the group.

## Fix

Make the chevron a 44×44 box, or give it a `hitSlop` that reaches at least 44 pt.

**Acceptance:** a source test pins the chevron target at 44×44 or more.
