# Picking a buy-then-return payee fills its accounts in the refund's direction: "Qa20261020 Shoe Store" puts Clothing first and Visa second

**Severity:** minor to moderate. Typing the amount the usual way records a refund. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `mobile/src/common/suggestion-utils.ts:368` (`deriveTwoPostingSuggestions`). When account pairs tie, the first seen wins, and the journal is newest first (`ledger-journal-service.ts:722`). One purchase plus a later refund therefore fills the refund pair.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator, English UI, Metro `--clear` at `f206fb1ef`, on the disposable QA ledger, which was restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`; the draft was discarded. Fixture: `2026-09-01 * "Qa20261020 Shoe Store" "Boots"` (Visa −80.00, Clothing +80.00), then `2026-09-10 … "Boots returned"` (Clothing −80.00, Visa +80.00). New Transaction ▸ Payee ▸ tap the **Qa20261020 Shoe Store** suggestion ▸ Save. The first posting row reads **Expenses:…020:Clothing**, and the second (auto) reads **Liabilitie…020:Visa**.

## Fix

Count A→B and B→A together, and fill the majority direction. On a tie, put the expense account on the receiving side, or prefer the older pair.

**Acceptance:** this fixture fills Visa first and Clothing second. A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

Not w21/412, 411, 729 or 730 (seeding, ranking and closed accounts). Searched `.pm/**` for refund together with payee seed, deriveTwoPostingSuggestions and ties.
