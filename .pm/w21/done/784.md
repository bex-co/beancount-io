# A sub-cent account change shows a red "−$0.00" row under a "$0.00" day header

**Severity:** trivial. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `account-detail-screen/utils/format-account-journal-balance.ts:24` takes the sign from the raw value (line 15 does the same for the balance), and `journalChangeTone` colours by the raw value. w21/055 and w21/163 rounded only the shared formatter and the day total.

Not run natively (the agent's `tsx` proof). In a USD-plus-BTC account read at cost, the change `{USD: "-0.00300000"}` with moneyScale 2 gives the row `-$0.00` with tone `down` (red), while the day header shows `$0.00`. Fixture: `Assets:Qa20261020Crypto` holding 100.00 USD and 0.001 QABTC {60000.00 USD}, then `2026-10-05 * "Dust sweep"` moving `0.00000005 QABTC {60000.00 USD}` to `Assets:Qa20261020Cold`.

## Fix

Round to the shown scale before choosing the sign and tone, as `signedAmount` and `roundMoney` already do.

**Acceptance:** this change reads "$0.00" in neutral tone. A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

Same class as w21/055 and w21/163 (done), on a path they didn't cover. Searched `.pm/**` for "-$0.00", formatAccountJournalChange, 163 and 055.

**Resolved 2026-10-23** — In `account-detail-screen/utils/format-account-journal-balance.ts`, the change and balance formatters now take their sign from the value rounded at the shown scale. `journalChangeTone` takes an optional `shownScale` and reads a change that rounds to nothing there as flat; `AccountEntryRow` passes the row's units or money scale. The note's change of −0.003 USD at scale 2 now reads "$0.00" in the neutral tone, under its "$0.00" day header. A change the scale shows keeps its sign and colour: −0.005 reads "-$0.01", and −0.003 at scale 3 reads "-$0.003".

Unit cases are in `src/__tests__/format-account-journal-balance.test.ts` and `selectors/__tests__/split-reads-neutral.test.ts`, with a source check that the row passes its scale; they fail at the previous HEAD. Not checked natively. Shipped in the commit that moves this note.
