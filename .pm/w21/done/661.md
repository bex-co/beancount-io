# Update Budget opens on a budget pre-entered for next year, so today's budget can't be corrected (w21/623 regression)

**Severity:** minor (budget editing). **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: w21/623 made Update Budget start at, and refuse dates before, `latestBudgetDate(group.budgetHistory)` (`budget-screen/budget-screen.tsx:144`, `selectors/budget-selectors.ts:706`). That is the latest date in the whole history, future entries included. When the next budget is already written down for a later date, the form starts on that future date, and the entry in effect today can't be updated.

Native (iPhone 17e at `d731eb32a`; disposable ledger restored byte-for-byte; today 2026-10-22):

```
2026-01-01 open Expenses:Qa20261020Food
2026-01-01 custom "budget" Expenses:Qa20261020Food "monthly" 300.00 USD
2027-01-01 custom "budget" Expenses:Qa20261020Food "monthly" 400.00 USD
```

Home ▸ the Food budget row ▸ Budget shows the card with October's prorated $212.90 of the $300 target. **Update Budget** opens with Date "2027-01-01" and the Amount placeholder "300.00". Under `tsx`, the picker's initial and minimum date are both 2027-01-01. A correction to this year's 300 (for example 350 from today) can't be dated today.

## Fix

Use the date of the entry in effect today (the latest one dated on or before today) as both the default and the floor. When no entry is in effect yet, fall back to the earliest future one. Alternatively, keep the floor at the in-effect entry and default to today.

**Acceptance:** with this fixture, Update Budget opens on 2026-10-22 and can go back to 2026-01-01. A history that holds only past entries behaves as w21/623 intended. Unit cases are added.

## Dedupe

Regression from w21/623 (done). Searched `.pm/**` for latestBudgetDate, "Update Budget" together with a future date, and w21/623.

**Resolved 2026-10-23** — Update Budget now floors and defaults on the entry in effect (`budgetInEffectDate`), not the newest entry in the history, so a budget pre-entered for next year no longer blocks correcting today's; unit cases in `update-date.test.ts`, shipped in the commit that closes this note (`mobile`).
