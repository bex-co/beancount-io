# New Transaction books a plain amount on the first row as money coming *into* the bank: typing 4.50 gives Checking +4.50 and Groceries −4.50

**Severity:** minor (wrong-signed entries that look valid). **Estimate:** 30 minutes, needs a product call. Owner: mobile. A3: `mobile/src/screens/multi-postings-transaction/postings-utils.ts:216` (`createInitialPostings`) puts the source account (Assets) on row 1 and the expense on the auto row, but the sign comes only from the typed text (`:345` `updatePostingAmount`; `multi-postings-transaction-screen.tsx:294, 324`). The iOS decimal pad has no minus key, and the sign button only appears once an amount exists. So the obvious entry, an amount on the first row, is a refund into the bank. The receipt prefill (negative source) and the payee seed (the account with negative history goes on row 1) both assume row 1 is negative. `postings-utils.test.ts:998` calls a typed `20.00` on row 1 a "refund".

Native (iPhone 17e at `dc8e781e1`, nothing saved): Home ▸ + ▸ Enter new transaction. Tap the Assets:Bank:Checking amount and type 4.50 on the decimal pad. The row shows a green **+** and "4.50", Expenses:Food:Groceries shows **−** 4.50 (auto), and the remainder is a green "✓ $0.00". Done would write Checking +4.50 and Groceries −4.50.

## Fix

Product call: either start the source row negative (show "−" and treat a typed magnitude as an outflow, as receipts already do), or label the rows From/To and let the auto row carry the sign. In either case, a purchase should take no extra tap.

**Acceptance:** typing 4.50 on the first row of a default New Transaction books Checking −4.50 and Groceries +4.50, or the form clearly marks the row as income before Done. The unit test's "refund" case is updated.
