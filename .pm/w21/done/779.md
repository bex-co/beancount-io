# A sale's cost/price line still squeezes Money flow's account name to "Asset…guard"

**Severity:** low. The posting being sold can't be identified. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: w21/160 capped the amount column at 60% so that the basis line would wrap. Since w21/603 added the lot date, a sale's basis line ("cost $200.00 · 2026-10-01 · price $250.00") fills the whole 60% (`posting-row.tsx:104-110`, `:184`). That leaves the account about 40% of the row, middle-truncated to 11 characters.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator at `main` `73480f666`, on the disposable QA ledger, which was restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`. Fixture: a buy of `10 VTI {200.00 USD}` into `Assets:Qa20261020Brokerage:Retirement:Vanguard` on 2026-10-01, then a sale of `-10 VTI {200.00 USD} @ 250.00 USD` on 2026-10-23. In the sale's Money flow, the first row reads **"FROM Asset…guard · −10 VTI · cost $200.00 · 2026-10-01 · price $250.00"**. The cash and income rows show their full names.

## Fix

Put the basis line on its own full-width line under the account and amount, rather than in the amount column. Alternatively, give the account a minimum share (for example, 50%) and let the basis wrap more.

**Acceptance:** with this fixture, the account reads at least `Assets:Qa20261020Brokerage…`, or wraps in full, at the default text size. The basis line stays fully readable.

## Dedupe

Regression of w21/160's acceptance ("Let the basis line wrap or truncate, not squeeze the account"), made worse by w21/603's lot date. Both are done. Searched `.pm/**` for squeez, together with ellipsis and Money flow.
