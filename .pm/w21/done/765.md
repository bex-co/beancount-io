# A budget whose only entry is in the future highlights that entry as the one in effect, while the card says "Starts Nov 1"

**Severity:** trivial. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: when every entry is in the future, the earliest one is picked (`budget-selectors.ts:268-273`), and `selectHistoryRows` marks it `isCurrent` (`:711-715`). The history row then gets the in-effect highlight (`budget-history-rows.tsx:183`), contradicting the code's own comment ("a future-dated entry … without being marked live").

Not run natively (the history toggle sits below the chart). Under `tsx` (the agent's proof), `2026-11-01 custom "budget" Expenses:Qa20261020Gym "monthly" 300.00 USD` alone gives a card with a "Starts Nov 1, 2026" badge and `selectHistoryRows` → `[{date: "2026-11-01", isCurrent: true}]`.

## Fix

Mark no history row current when no entry is in effect yet.

**Acceptance:** with this fixture, no row is highlighted. A unit case is added.
