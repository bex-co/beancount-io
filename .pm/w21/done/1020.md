# In Persian, the "not in total" and "at cost (no price)" counts still print Latin digits beside Persian-digit text: "۶ قیمت از ۸ سپتامبر ۲۰۱۷ به‌روز نشده · 1 خارج از جمع" (a residual of w21/885)

**Severity:** trivial to minor (fa). **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `mobile/src/common/valuation.ts:402-405` and `:430-432` pass a bare `count` to `t("notInTotalCount")` and `t("atCostNoPriceCount")`; i18n-js prints it in Latin digits. The sibling sentence a few lines above (`pricesNotUpdatedSince`, `:419-427`) was fixed by w21/885 with `localizeDigits(…, locale)` so its count takes the language's digits; these two were not.

## Repro

`main` at `3730a55fb` plus the `example` ledger, dev build `io.beancount.ios` on iPhone 17e (iOS 27.0), light mode.

1. Settings ▸ Language ▸ **فارسی** ▸ Confirm ▸ accept the layout restart.
2. Accounts tab (حساب‌ها).
3. The Assets root's caption reads `به ارزش بازار · ۶ قیمت از ۸ سپتامبر ۲۰۱۷ به‌روز نشده · 1 خارج از جمع`: the "۶", the date and the amounts use Persian digits, the "1" before "خارج از جمع" is Latin. The Income and Expenses roots read `2 خارج از جمع`, Latin "2" under amounts drawn as `$۳۵۸٬۸۷۶٫۶۰`. Repeated after a fresh restart.

Controls: the `۶` in the stale-price sentence of the same caption is in Persian digits (the w21/885 fix works). English and the other left-to-right languages are unaffected (their digits are Latin).

## Expected / actual

Expected: one digit system per caption, following the rule `localizeDigits` states (a language renders its own digits): "۱ خارج از جمع", "۲ خارج از جمع". Actual: Latin "1" and "2" in the middle of Persian text.

## Fix

Run the two parts through `localizeDigits(…, locale)` the way `pricesNotUpdatedSince` is. `localizeDigits` is a no-op when the locale's digits are Latin, so every other language is unchanged. Check every other `t(…, { count })` that builds a valuation caption (the account detail header and the holdings sheet's section titles read the same strings).

**Acceptance:** in fa the Accounts roots read "۱ خارج از جمع" / "۲ خارج از جمع" and an at-cost count reads in Persian digits; en and de unchanged. Add a unit case beside the existing "a count, in the language's digits" case.

## Blast radius

`notInTotalCount` and `atCostNoPriceCount` are read by `common/valuation.ts` only (`rg` over `mobile/src`, ignoring `translations/` and the screenshot script), but their output reaches Home's Net Worth, Assets and Liabilities captions, the Accounts roots and rows, and account detail. Only Persian uses non-Latin digits among the 13 languages.

## Wider sweep (read from source, not run natively)

`useTranslations().t` (`common/hooks/use-translations.ts`) is a bare `i18n.t(key, params)`, so no call site gets localized digits unless it opts in, and only `pricesNotUpdatedSince` does (w21/885). Other fa strings that interpolate a bare `{{count}}` / `{{number}}` and are filled by callers outside `localizeDigits` (callers found with `rg`; each still to be confirmed natively): `filtersActive` ("فعال: {{count}}", `transactions-header.tsx:89`; checked natively 2026-11-05: it is only the filter button's VoiceOver value, the screen shows just a dot, so it is spoken, not seen), the merchants search placeholder and the "{{count}} تراکنش" row count (`translations/fa.ts:80-85`), `postingNumber` ("سطر {{number}}"), the budget and income/expense chart summaries' period counts (`:829-859`), and the error and "N items" counts (`:694-707`). The fix is best made once, in the place the strings are filled (for example a `count` formatter in `useTranslations`) rather than per call site; decide that when implementing, and keep the unit case per string family.

## Adjacent states

Not exercised: the Home caption and the account detail header in Persian (same function), a plural form with a count above 1 in other languages, Android.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` (including `done/`) for notInTotalCount, "خارج از جمع", localizeDigits and Persian digits. Extends w21/885 (done: fixed only `pricesNotUpdatedSince`) and w21/884 (done: chart and budget labels); neither touched these two strings. First native look at them in Persian.
