# In Chinese, Cash flow still shows the same truncated name for a same-named income and expense group: w21/540's label split misses full-width parentheses

**Severity:** minor (zh users can't tell the income node from the expense node). **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `sankeyLabelLines` (`reports-screen/selectors/sankey-data.ts:204`, used at `components/cash-flow-sankey.tsx:373-376`) splits off the suffix only when it matches `/^(.*\S)\s+(\([^()]*\))$/`, which needs a space and ASCII parentheses. zh's `sankeyIncomeNodeLabel` and `sankeyExpensesNodeLabel` are `"{{name}}（收入）"` / `"{{name}}（支出）"` (`translations/zh.ts:724-725`): full-width parentheses and no space. So the label isn't split, it's cut to 10 characters, and the suffix is lost. w21/540 was checked in English only. In ca, de and nl the suffix line itself is cut ("(Einnahme…"), but the two nodes remain distinguishable.

Not run natively: it needs switching the app to Chinese (the wheel picker plus a restart). Proven under `tsx` (the agent's proof) with October `Income:Qa20261020Interest` −1,500.00 and `Expenses:Qa20261020Interest` 1,200.00. In English, `sankeyLabelLines` gives `["Qa2026102…","(income)"]` and `["Qa2026102…","(expenses)"]`. In zh it gives `["Qa2026102…"]` for both, and the d3 layout labels both nodes.

## Fix

Accept full-width parentheses (`（…）`) and an optional space in the suffix pattern, or have the label function return name and suffix separately.

**Acceptance:** in zh the two nodes read "Qa2026102…" over "（收入）" and "（支出）". Other locales are unchanged. A unit case per locale is added.
