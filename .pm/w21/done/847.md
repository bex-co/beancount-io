# The noduplicates plugin only catches duplicate transactions; a duplicated balance, note or event passes, where bean-check flags it

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: ledger. A3: the engine checks transactions only, and `noduplicates.ts` places only those errors (the `DUPLICATE` regex). Beancount's `hash_entries` flags every directive type except Price.

Proven on `main` (`053f4eb84`) against Beancount 3.2.3 `bean-check` (the agent's run). With a duplicated `balance`, `note` and `event`, `bean-check` reports lines 4, 6 and 8 ("Duplicate entry: Balance/Note/Event"); the service reports `[]`. In the app, Notifications would say "Ledger is healthy".

## Fix

Hash every non-Price directive with metadata excluded, and raise the duplicate error at the later copy, with Beancount's message.

**Acceptance:** this fixture reports three errors at those lines. A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

w21/740, 739 and w3/302 cover where these errors point and their wording, not whether they're raised. Searched `.pm/**` for noduplicates and "Duplicate entry".

---

**Resolved 2026-10-24** — with `noduplicates` (or `pedantic`) declared, a repeated balance, note or event is flagged on the later copy by Beancount's sort order (date, type rank, line, across files), prices excluded, metadata and spacing ignored (`duplicateEntryErrors`). This fixture reports three errors at lines 4, 6 and 8, as bean-check does. The message names the type and the repeated entry rather than printing Python reprs. Tests: `noduplicates-entries.test.ts`, shipped in the commit that closes this note (`backend-cluster/ledger`). Needs a ledger deploy to reach production.
