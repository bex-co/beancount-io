# Add Budget can't budget the whole Expenses (or Income) root: searching "Expenses" lists only child accounts, with no root row and no Create

**Severity:** low to moderate. "Total monthly spending" is a common budget shape. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `picker-sections.ts:237` adds parent accounts from depth 2, so a root is never in the list, and `showCreateBelowResults` is false for it.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator, English UI, Metro `--clear` at `8b5ba34e1`, on the disposable QA ledger, which was restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`; nothing was saved. Home ▸ the Groceries budget row ▸ Budget ▸ + ▸ Add Budget ▸ Account ▸ paste **Expenses**. The list shows Expenses:Food, Expenses:Transport, Expenses:Food:Groceries, Expenses:Housing and the other children, with **no "Expenses" row**. Under `tsx` (the agent's proof), `pickerAccounts("budget")` starts at depth 2, and the create row is false.

## Fix

For `type === "budget"`, offer the income and expense roots. The card already rolls children up by prefix. Before shipping, confirm natively that a `custom "budget" Expenses "monthly" 2000 USD` card reads correctly.

**Acceptance:** Add Budget's picker offers "Expenses" and "Income". A unit case is added.
