# A recurring merchant says "Next Oct 21, 2026" on Oct 23: a date already past stays "Next" for a quarter of the cycle

**Severity:** minor (a missed payment reads as upcoming). **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `merchants-screen/selectors/detect-recurrence.ts:648-656` sets `isOverdue` only after `nextExpected + round(gap × 0.25)`, and `merchant-row-text.ts:89` keeps rendering "Next {nextExpected}" until then, even once that date has passed.

Native (iPhone 17e, Metro `--clear` at `62882d0ec`; disposable ledger restored byte-for-byte; today 2026-10-23): "Qa20261020 Cleaner" 45.00 USD every two weeks on 08-26, 09-09, 09-23 and 10-07; the 10-21 payment is missing. Drawer ▸ Merchants shows under Recurring "Qa20261020 Cleaner · Every 2 weeks · $45.00 · **Next Oct 21, 2026**" on Oct 23. Under `tsx` (the agent's proof), it becomes "Overdue" only on Oct 26. Monthly: "Next Oct 15" on Oct 23 for 8 days. Yearly: past for up to 91 days.

## Fix

Once `nextExpected` is in the past, say so ("Due Oct 21", "2 days late"), keeping "Overdue" for the end of the grace window, or start "Overdue" the day after.

**Acceptance:** no row reads "Next <past date>". Unit cases per cadence are added.

## Dedupe

Not w21/007, 167, 233, 267, 409 or 529 (Next/Overdue computation and lapse). Searched `.pm/**` for isOverdue together with grace, and "Next" together with past.
