# One off-schedule charge anywhere in the 37-month window drops the day-of-month anchor, so Next runs up to 3 days late

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile.

**Root cause (A3).** `monthDayAnchor` (`mobile/src/screens/merchants-screen/selectors/detect-recurrence.ts:230`) gives up on the whole series in two cases:
- any single event is more than `ANCHOR_TOLERANCE_DAYS` (3, `:216`) from the due day (`:276`);
- two events fall in one month (`:281`).

Next then falls back to `lastDay + round(medianGap)` (`:697`). w21/271 and w21/459 relaxed the anchor for one shifted or skipped month, but a single extra charge or late payment still ends it, for as long as that event stays in the window (up to 3 years).

Not run natively in this pass. `tsx` proof, with 15.99 on the 5th from Jan 2024 to Feb 2026, checked on 2026-02-20:

| Series | Next |
|---|---|
| Control | 2026-03-05 |
| Plus one extra charge on 2025-02-18 | 2026-03-08 |
| June 2024 paid on the 10th | 2026-03-08 |

The same series ending on Sep 5 gives a Next of 10-06 instead of 10-05.

**Native steps** (disposable ledger):
1. Add "QA GYM" at 40.00 on the 5th of each month, plus one extra 40.00 on another day the year before.
2. Open Merchants ▸ Recurring.

Next isn't on the 5th.

## Fix

Let the anchor tolerate a few off-anchor events, as `semiMonthlyDays` already allows one miss: for example, skip up to one event, or about 10% of events, that is off the due day or is a second payment in the same month. Still require that most events sit on the anchor.

**Acceptance:**
- Both outlier series above predict 2026-03-05, and the September series predicts 10-05.
- Every-four-weeks series still don't anchor; the w21/408 tests stay green.

## Dedupe

Done w21/007, w21/271, w21/408, w21/458, w21/459 and w21/529 all touch `monthDayAnchor`, but none covers an extra charge or a payment more than 3 days late. Searched for "monthDayAnchor", "one-off", "extra charge", "outlier" and "late fee".

---

**Resolved 2026-10-24** — `monthDayAnchor` lets a series of six or more events carry an outlier or two (a tenth of them, at least one): an event more than three days off the due day, or a second payment in one period, is left out of the period count and of the last due date instead of ending the anchor, which must still be held by at least half the events. The extra charge, the month paid on the 10th and the series ending on the 5th all predict the 5th; shorter series and every-four-weeks payments are as strict as before (w21/408 tests green). Cases in `merchant-anchor-outliers.test.ts` (three fail without the change; `mobile`). Not run natively.
