# Indian (lakh) and apostrophe-grouped amounts are refused: "1,00,000" and "1'234.50" read as invalid

**Severity:** minor. The form refuses the amount rather than saving a wrong one, but pasting an amount from an Indian, Swiss or Liechtenstein bank message always fails. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile.

**Root cause (A3).** In `mobile/src/screens/multi-postings-transaction/postings-utils.ts`:
- Comma grouping is accepted only as `\d{1,3}(,\d{3})+` (`:123-138`). Indian two-digit groups such as `1,23,456.78` fall through to `normalized.includes(",") → return null` at `:136`.
- The grouping step at `:101` strips spaces only, not `'` or `’`.

**Native (iPhone 17e, 2026-10-23).**
1. Home → + → New Transaction.
2. Paste `1,00,000` into the first amount.

The amount turns red, the Remainder shows "—", and "Use digits and one decimal point, like 1234.56" appears. Done stays disabled. The draft was discarded without saving.

**`tsx` proof (the agent's).** `parseExactAmount` returns `null` for `"1,23,456.78"`, `"12,34,567"`, `"1,00,000"`, `"1'234.50"`, `"1’234.50"` and `"12'345"`. The controls work: `"1,234.56"` → 1234.56 and `"1 234,56"` → 1234.56.

## Fix

- Before the other rules, accept `^-?\d{1,2}(,\d{2})*,\d{3}(\.\d+)?$` as lakh grouping.
- At `:101`, also strip `'`, `’` and U+02BC when they sit between a digit and a group of three.

Both forms are unambiguous, so w21/031's rule that a lone `1,250` is a decimal comma still holds.

**Acceptance:**
- `1,23,456.78` → `123456.78` and `1,00,000` → `100000`.
- `1'234.50` and `1’234.50` → `1234.50`.
- `1,250` stays `1.250`, and `1,2,3` stays `null`.

**Resolved 2026-10-24** — `parseExactAmount` reads Indian grouping (`1,23,456.78`, `1,00,000`: a group of three after at least one pair) and apostrophe grouping (`1'234.50`, curly or modifier apostrophe, between a digit and a group of three), so pasted bank amounts parse. `1,250` stays `1.250`, `1,2,3` and `1'23` stay refused.
