# New Transaction pasting "-20" into a negative row makes it "--20": the amount turns unreadable and Done is refused

**Severity:** minor. A natural paste blocks saving. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `handleAbsValueChange` (`multi-postings-transaction-screen.tsx:335-337`) puts the row's minus in front of whatever was pasted, through `signedAmountInput` (`postings-utils.ts:71`). Text that already has its own sign gets a second one.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator, English UI, Metro `--clear` at `fca74a1ca`; the draft was discarded, so nothing was written. Steps: Home ▸ + ▸ Enter new transaction ▸ type 12.50 on row 1 with the decimal pad. The auto row shows a "−" badge and 12.50. Tap the auto row's amount (its text is selected) ▸ Paste `-20`. The row shows a red **"-20"** with no sign badge, Remainder shows "—", **"Use digits and one decimal point, like 1234.56"** appears, and Done is disabled. Under `tsx` (the agent's proof), the row's value becomes `"--20"`; `" -20"` gives `"- -20"` and `"−20"` gives `"-−20"`, and all three parse to null. The same paste into a positive row correctly gives −20.

## Fix

When the pasted text carries its own sign (read with `splitAmountSign`), take that sign instead of the row's, and update the badge.

**Acceptance:** pasting "-20" into the negative auto row gives a valid −20 on that row. Pasting "20" keeps the row's sign. A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

Not w21/232 (leading space), w21/666 (U+2212) or w21/728 (hidden minus), which cover positive rows or emptied fields (all done). Searched `.pm/**` for "second minus", "double minus" and `"--`.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-23** — A row's digits field now goes through the new `typedAmountInput` (`multi-postings-transaction/postings-utils.ts`). It reads the typed or pasted text with `splitAmountSign`, which strips direction marks and maps U+2212 to `-`, and text with its own sign takes that sign. Pasting `-20`, ` -20` or `−20` into the negative auto row stores `-20`, which parses to −20, and the badge follows from the stored value. A leading `+` makes the row positive. Unsigned digits keep the row's sign, so pasting `20` into the negative row still gives −20.

Unit cases are in `__tests__/postings-utils.test.ts`, with the screen's wiring check moved to the new helper; three fail at the previous HEAD. Not checked natively. Shipped in the commit that moves this note.
