# A KWD (3-decimal) budget is prorated to cents, so spending exactly on target reads a red "Above target": "Budget د.ك74.190 · Actual د.ك74.193"

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `budgetForInterval` calls `budgetScale(ordered)` without the currency (`budget-screen/selectors/budget-selectors.ts:616`), so the prorated budget is rounded to 2 decimals. 100 KWD × 23/31 = 74.1935 becomes 74.19. This is the 3-decimal sibling of w21/596's JPY fix.

Reproduced natively with w21/804's build and ledger state. Fixture: `2026-01-01 custom "budget" Expenses:Qa20261020Rent "monthly" 100 KWD`, with 74.193 KWD spent on 2026-10-05. The card reads **"Monthly · KWD · Above target"** in red, with **"Budget د.ك74.190 · Actual د.ك74.193 · Variance +د.ك0.003"** and a red meter.

## Fix

Pass the group's currency into `budgetScale` inside `budgetForInterval`, so that KWD/BHD/OMR keep three decimals.

**Acceptance:** the budget reads د.ك74.194 (or 74.1935), and 74.193 spent is on or below target. A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

Sibling of w21/596 (done; JPY). Searched `.pm/**` for KWD, BHD and "three-decimal" together with budget.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-23** — `budgetForInterval` (`budget-screen/selectors/budget-selectors.ts`) now passes the budget's currency, read from its history entries' amounts ("100 KWD"), to `budgetScale`. A prorated budget is therefore rounded no coarser than the currency's minor unit, plus the usual guard digits. 100 KWD prorated to 2026-10-23 is 74.1935… (shown د.ك74.194) instead of 74.19, and 74.193 spent is below target, not "Above target". USD and JPY prorate as before (74.19 for 100 USD). Every caller, the Budget card and Home's panel included, prorates through this function, so none needed changing.

Unit cases are in `selectors/__tests__/fractional-commodity.test.ts`; two fail at the previous HEAD. Not checked natively. Shipped in the commit that moves this note.
