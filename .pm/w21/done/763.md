# An overspent $0 budget shows no progress bar on its Budget card, but a full red bar on Home

**Severity:** trivial. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `select-budget-card-stats.ts:178-181` returns `progressPercent: null` when the budget is ≤ 0, and `budget-group-card.tsx:360` then hides the meter. Home's panel (`select-budget-panel.ts:140-144`) uses 100 for the same case, per the w21/076 decision.

Native (iPhone 17e, Metro `--clear` at `b1f7c144a`; disposable ledger restored byte-for-byte; today 2026-10-23): `2026-01-01 custom "budget" Expenses:Qa20261020Gifts "monthly" 0.00 USD` plus a 40.00 USD expense on 10-05. The Budget card reads "Budget $0.00 · Actual $40.00 · Variance +$40.00 · Above target", with **no meter bar**; the Gym card below it has one.

## Fix

Use the same rule as Home: a full red meter when actual > 0 on a zero budget.

**Acceptance:** the card shows a full red meter, matching Home. A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

Card counterpart of w21/076 (done). Searched `.pm/**` for progressPercent together with zero.

**Resolved 2026-10-23** — `selectBudgetCardStats` (`budget-screen/selectors/select-budget-card-stats.ts`), which both the Budget card and Home's panel read, now applies w21/076's rule itself. A budget of zero or less has its meter full once anything is spent against it, and empty when nothing is, instead of no meter. The note's Gifts card now draws a full red meter beside "Above target", as Home does. Home's panel drops its own copy of the rule and reads the shared value. The old unit case that pinned the hidden meter is rewritten for the new rule (full when spent, judged unfavourable; empty when untouched). It fails at the previous HEAD. Not run natively. Shipped in the commit that moves this note.
