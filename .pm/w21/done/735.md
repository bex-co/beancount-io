# `unique_prices` flags identical same-day prices as errors; Beancount only flags prices that disagree

**Severity:** minor (false red errors in Notifications; a same-day price write may be refused as a "new error"). **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: ledger. A3: rustledger's native `unique_prices` reports every same-day duplicate. Its errors pass through `backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/engine.ts:614` and `:714-720` with no reconciliation against Beancount's rule (`beancount/plugins/unique_prices.py` flags only *disagreeing* prices).

Production, through `getLedgerErrors` on the disposable ledger (restored byte-for-byte): the QA ledger plus `plugin "beancount.plugins.unique_prices"` and two identical `2024-01-08 price QAVTI 100 USD` lines gives "**Duplicate price for QAVTI/USD on 2024-01-08**", with no file or line. Proven with the real engine under ts-node (in-process snapshot, `LedgerDataService.getErrors`/`getTrialBalance`/`getAccountJournal`) against Beancount 3.2.3 `bean-check`/`bean-query` (the agent's run). `implicit_prices` duplicates (VOO from a buy plus an explicit equal price) are flagged too, while bean-check reports only the disagreeing EUR pair ("Disagreeing price entries").

## Fix

Filter `unique_prices` errors to price pairs whose values differ, and give the error the second price's location.

**Acceptance:** identical same-day prices raise nothing, and disagreeing ones raise one located error, as bean-check does. A unit case is added.

**Resolved 2026-10-23** — new `backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/unique-prices.ts` (`reconcileUniquePriceErrors`, run by the engine after the AVERAGE check) judges each "Duplicate price for X/Y on D" against that day's prices in the booked stream, `implicit_prices`' own included, comparing numbers as Decimals (`100` = `100.00`). Agreeing prices raise nothing. Disagreeing ones raise Beancount's single "Disagreeing price entries", on bean-check's line: the day sorted by line number (not file), the last entry of the first number, and an implicit price on its transaction's line. Checked against bean-check 3.2.3 on explicit, implicit (cost, total cost, a reduction), and cross-file fixtures. Unit cases in `unique-prices.test.ts`; live engine case in `scripts/verify-rustledger.ts` (fails at the previous HEAD with three unlocated "Duplicate price" errors). Needs a ledger-service deploy to reach production. Shipped in the commit that moves this note.
