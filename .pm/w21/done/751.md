# The chart's y-range scales to the move itself, so −$12 on $2.5M or +$0.01 on $15,000 draws a full-height crash or spike

**Severity:** trivial (may be deliberate auto-scaling; it reads as a bug where the header says the change is flat). **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `common/d3/interactive-line-chart.tsx:167-172` (`computeDomain`) pads by 10% of max−min, with no floor relative to the size of the balance. A move that w21/237's header calls "(0.00%)" fills the plot.

Not run natively (it needs a $2.5M fixture). Under `tsx` (the agent's proof, running `computeDomain`'s exact source), w21/237's setup gives a domain of [2,499,986.8, 2,500,001.2], and the line falls from y=27.5 to y=142.5 on a 170 pt plot, drawn red. +$0.01 on $15,000 gives the mirror case: a full-height green spike.

## Fix

Floor the domain's span at a small fraction of the magnitude (for example 0.5% of |max|), or flatten when the header classifies the move as flat. Pairs with w21/750.

**Acceptance:** a rounds-to-flat move draws a visually flat line, and real moves still use the full height. A unit case is added.

Related to w21/237 and w21/750. Searched `.pm/**` for computeDomain and "y-range".

---

**Resolved 2026-10-23** — the line chart's y-domain (`computeDomain`, moved to the new pure `common/d3/line-chart-domain.ts`) now floors its span at 0.05% of the series' magnitude, centred on the series. A move too small to read draws flat: w21/237's −$12 on $2.5M and +$0.01 on $15,000 now take under 1% of the plot's height, where before they took all of it. Any move of 0.05% or more keeps exactly the old domain, padded a tenth either side, and still fills the plot. An unmoved series keeps its old domain too. Together with w21/750, a flat range now reads flat in both colour and shape. Unit cases are in `common/d3/__tests__/line-chart-domain.test.ts`; the flat case fails under the old formula. Not run natively. Shipped in the commit that moves this note.
