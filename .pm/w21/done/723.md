# An account opened with a future date vanishes from Accounts, and Open Account then says it is "already open" (w21/614 vs Open Account's free date)

**Severity:** minor (a just-created account seems lost, and can't be recreated or found). **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: Open Account's date picker has no maximum (`open-account-screen/open-account-screen.tsx:619-628`), so a user can open an account next month. w21/614 made the Accounts tab list only accounts opened by today (`listedAccountsAsOf`, `common/account-close-dates.ts:158-169`, applied at `accounts-screen.tsx:113-114`). After Done, the reveal (`:373`, `account-table.tsx:509-526`) expands the parent, finds no row and clears itself silently. Open Account's duplicate check (`:308-311`) reads `meta.accounts`, which does include the future account.

Native (iPhone 17e, Metro `--clear` at `4723c757b`; disposable ledger restored byte-for-byte; today 2026-10-23). The end state of "Open Account ▸ Assets ▸ Qa20261020Future ▸ Open date Nov 2 ▸ Done" was written directly as `2026-11-02 open Assets:Qa20261020Future`, because the automation can't drive the date picker. Then:

1. The Accounts tab lists Assets ▸ Bank, Cash. There's no Qa20261020Future and no note.
2. Accounts ▸ + ▸ paste `Qa20261020Future` reads, in red, "**Assets:Qa20261020Future is already open in this ledger.**", with Done disabled.

Under `tsx` (the agent's proof), `listedAccountsAsOf` on `[Bank (2026-01-01), Future (2026-11-02)]` with today 2026-10-23 returns only Bank.

## Fix

Either list future-opened accounts tagged "Opens Nov 2" (dimmed, with no balance), or cap Open Account's date at today from this entry point. At minimum, make the "already open" message say when it opens.

**Acceptance:** after opening an account dated next month, the user can find it (or was prevented from creating it), and the duplicate message is never the only trace. Unit cases are added.

Collision of w21/614 (done) with Open Account. w21/008, w21/229 and w21/374 cover the picker paths only. Searched `.pm/**` for listedAccountsAsOf, "already open" and future together with "open date".
