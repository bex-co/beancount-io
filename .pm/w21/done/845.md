# Entry Context's "balance before" for a balance assertion on a parent account leaves out its subaccounts ("0" on an assertion that passes)

**Severity:** minor to moderate. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: ledger. A3: `backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/source-slice.ts:1073-1083` (`entryBalances`) adds only postings whose account exactly equals the assertion's account. Beancount checks a balance assertion against the account plus all its subaccounts.

Proven on `main` (`053f4eb84`) against Beancount 3.2.3 (the agent's run). With `Assets:Bank:Checking` +100 and then `2024-01-10 balance Assets:Bank 100 USD`, `bean-check` passes and `bean-query` sums 100 USD, but `getContext` returns `balances_before: {"Assets:Bank": []}`. In the app, Entry Context on that assertion would show the balance before as 0.

## Fix

For a Balance target, sum the account and its `:` children, as Beancount's check does.

**Acceptance:** this fixture's balance before reads 100 USD. A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

Not w21/092 (formatting) or w1/117 (CLI). Searched `.pm/**` for balances_before, entryBalances, and parent together with assertion.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-24** — for a Balance entry, `entryBalances` sums the postings of the asserted account and every subaccount (`account:` prefix, so a sibling like `Assets:Banking` is not taken), as Beancount's check does; transactions are unchanged. Tests in `source-slice.test.ts` (the first fails without the change), shipped in the commit that closes this note (`backend-cluster/ledger`). Needs a ledger deploy to reach production.
