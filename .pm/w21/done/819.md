# Since w21/570, a priced purchase is read as an exchange with a fee: a $130 card charge shows as an unsigned "$130.00" transfer, and a $100 miles purchase as "−$95.00"

**Severity:** minor to major (a regression of w21/570; spending reads as a transfer or loses value). **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `transactions-screen/utils/transaction-display-utils.ts:338-353` (`exchangeWithFee`) fires for any entry without a cost that has an `@` price somewhere and only balance-sheet, Expenses and Income legs. That covers ordinary purchases:
- **Fee lines in two currencies:** `feeAmount` returns null, and the entry falls into the neutral exchange branch.
- **Priced miles:** the Expenses and Income lines are netted as if they were a fee.

Before w21/570 both read the operating-currency spend ("−$130.00", "−$100.00"). w21/570's own resolution says a miles purchase "still reads as the money spent".

Reproduced natively with w21/818's build and ledger state:
- `2026-10-22 * "Qa20261020 Trip"` with `Liabilities:Qa20261020Card -130.00 USD`, `Expenses:Qa20261020Hotel 100.00 EUR @ 1.10 USD`, `Expenses:Qa20261020Tips 20.00 USD`, `Assets:Qa20261020CashEUR -10.00 EUR` and `Expenses:Qa20261020Food 10.00 EUR`. The row reads **"$130.00"**, unsigned and black like a transfer.
- `2026-10-20 * "Qa20261020 Miles"` with `Liabilities:Qa20261020Card -100.00 USD`, `Expenses:Qa20261020Travel 100.00 USD`, `Assets:Qa20261020Miles 500 MILES @ 0.01 USD` and `Income:Qa20261020Miles -5.00 USD`. The row reads **"−$95.00"**.

## Fix

Call an entry an exchange only when the `@` sits on a balance-sheet posting and that leg's weight exceeds the net of the Expenses and Income lines. That keeps w21/570's case (a $1 fee on a $109 leg) and returns both of these to the operating-currency spend.

**Acceptance:** Trip reads "−$130.00" and Miles reads "−$100.00", and w21/570's fixtures are unchanged. Both are added to the w21/570 block in `transaction-display-utils.test.ts`.
