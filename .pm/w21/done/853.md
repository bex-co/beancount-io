# Since w21/799, a twice-monthly bill on the 1st/16th or 15th/30th reads "Every 2 weeks", with the wrong next date ("Next Oct 31" instead of Nov 1)

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `merchants-screen/selectors/detect-recurrence.ts:563`. w21/799's tolerance (`Math.abs(gap - 14) <= 1`) also accepts the 15-day gaps that true semimonthly series have. Compared with the pre-fix file (the agent's `tsx` run), 06-15/06-30/07-15/07-30, 09-01/09-16/10-01/10-16, 01-31/02-15/02-28/03-15, and the 1st/15th over a leap-year Feb–Mar all flip from semimonthly to biweekly.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator, English UI, Metro `--clear` at `045715ae1`, on the disposable QA ledger, which was restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`; today 2026-10-23. Fixture: "Qa20261020 Payroll", 1,200.00 USD on 2026-09-01, 09-16, 10-01 and 10-16. Drawer ▸ Merchants ▸ Recurring reads **"Qa20261020 Payroll · Every 2 weeks · $1,200.00 · Next Oct 31, 2026"**. It should read "Twice a month", next Nov 1.

## Fix

Apply the ±1 tolerance only when some gap is exactly 14 and no two consecutive gaps are 15, or try `semiMonthlyDays` first when two day-of-month anchors each repeat.

**Acceptance:** these four series stay semimonthly, and w21/799's Daycare fixture stays biweekly. Unit cases are added.

Regression of w21/799 (done, `29de9a6d0`). Searched `.pm/**` for semimonthly and "Every 2 weeks".

**Resolved 2026-10-24** — a near-biweekly series (every gap within a day of 14) that has a 15 in it is read as twice a month when it fits two days of the month exactly: every payment on one of them (a short month's end counts for a 31st), each repeated (`exactlyTwoDays`). The 1st/16th, 15th/30th, end-of-January/15th and leap-February 1st/15th series stay "Twice a month"; w21/799's Daycare fixture and its every-start-day sweep stay biweekly. Cases in `detect-recurrence.test.ts` (fail without the change), shipped in the commit that closes this note (`mobile`). A "13th and 30th" bill with a short February is not told from a biweekly one with a payment a day late; it stays biweekly.
