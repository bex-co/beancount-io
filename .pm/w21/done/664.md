# Home's Recent Transactions says "Nothing up to today" when more than 20 entries are booked for later this month (w21/513 × w21/573)

**Severity:** minor (Home). **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `home-screen/selectors/recent-window.ts:23` asks the server for the card's 5 rows plus `RECENT_FUTURE_BUFFER` (20) through month-end, and `recentTransactionsUpTo` drops the future ones on the device. With more than 20 entries dated after today this month, the page holds only future rows. `recent-transactions-card.tsx:95-109` then shows w21/573's "upcoming only" message, although many past transactions exist. With 21–24 future entries the card shows fewer than 5 rows.

Native (iPhone 17e at `d731eb32a`; disposable ledger restored byte-for-byte; today 2026-10-22): the QA ledger's normal history (latest past entry Aug 28) plus 27 transactions of 1.00 USD, three a day from 2026-10-23 to 2026-10-31. Home ▸ Recent Transactions reads "Nothing up to today. Upcoming entries are listed under Transactions." with no rows. Without the fixture, the card lists Farmers Market, City Bus and the rest. Under `tsx`: shown `[]`, `upcomingOnly: true`.

## Fix

Bound the query at today (the window w21/513 keyed per month could end at today while keeping a stable cache key per day, or the server could take an end date). Alternatively, page until 5 past rows are found or the list is exhausted.

**Acceptance:** with this fixture, the card shows the 5 newest past transactions. w21/471's, w21/513's and w21/573's acceptance still holds. Unit cases are added.

## Dedupe

Interaction of w21/513 (done; month-end window) and w21/573 (done; upcoming-only message), both w21/471 follow-ups. Searched `.pm/**` for RECENT_FUTURE_BUFFER and "Nothing up to today".

**Resolved 2026-10-23** — when the month-end page comes back full with fewer than five entries up to today (`pageEndedBeforeToday`), the card reads a second page ending today, so entries booked for later this month no longer empty it; the upcoming-only message waits for that read. Cases in `recent-window.test.ts`, shipped in the commit that closes this note (`mobile`).
