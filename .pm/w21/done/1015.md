# Reports' income/expense chart gives no cue that long ranges scroll: YTD and 1Y open on April–October and look identical, so the earlier months seem not to exist

**Severity:** minor (discoverability; the range label says one thing, the chart shows seven months). **Estimate:** ~45 minutes. Owner: mobile. A2: `mobile/src/common/d3/scrollable-axis-chart.tsx` wraps the plot in a horizontal `ScrollView` with `showsHorizontalScrollIndicator={false}` and an `onContentSizeChange` that scrolls to the end ("open long spans on the most recent periods"), and `income-expense-bar-chart.tsx` sets `MIN_GROUP_WIDTH = 44`. Once about eight or more months are charted, the older ones start off-screen to the left, with no edge fade, scroll indicator or caption saying so.

iPhone 17e, iOS 27.0, dev build `io.beancount.ios`, `main` at `434d05dc6`, a synthetic example ledger, light mode, default text size.

1. Open the Reports tab.
2. Tap **YTD**, then **1Y**.
3. Both show columns APR to OCT only, the first flush against the y-axis. A full year (1Y) and the year to date (YTD) look identical, and 3M shows AUG to OCT. (Screenshots are kept locally and are not committed.)

The same shape shows on **ALL**: on this ledger the history runs from 2015 to 2026, so the y-axis scales to $15K while the visible Apr–Oct bars sit near zero, which reads as broken when the large months are simply off-screen to the left.

## Fix

When the plot is wider than the visible area, tell the reader. Either an edge fade or an always-visible scroll cue on the clipped side, and/or a caption naming the visible span (for example "Jan–Oct 2026 · swipe for earlier"). Alternatively narrow the columns so the whole range fits. One change in `ScrollableAxisChart` covers both consumers.

**Acceptance:** on a ledger with more months than fit, YTD, 1Y and ALL each show a visible cue (or the whole range) on the clipped side, and a range that fits shows no cue; the chart still opens on the most recent months; a unit or rendered-component case covers "overflows" and "fits".

## Blast radius

`ScrollableAxisChart` is shared by the income/expense chart and the Budget bar chart (extracted in `mobile/.pm/w1/done/028`); check the Budget chart at long ranges. Adjacent states: loading and no-data placeholders are unaffected (they return before the scroller).

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` and `mobile/.pm/**` for `ScrollableAxisChart`, `showsHorizontalScrollIndicator` and `scrollToEnd`. Only `w4/064` (a tab-strip nudge in Russian) and `w21/220` (the Budget chart overflowing its card) are adjacent, and both are different problems.

**Unverified:** that swiping left reveals the earlier months on device (the QA tooling has no swipe gesture, so this is read from the source), Android, right-to-left languages.

**Resolved 2026-10-31** — `ScrollableAxisChart` now pins a chevron cue to the plot's start edge when the plot is wider than its viewport and scrolled past the start; a range that fits shows none, and the chart still opens on the latest months. Both consumers (income/expense and Budget) get it from the shared shell. The `hasClippedStart` helper has unit tests. Not checked on a device (the QA tooling has no swipe gesture), Android or right-to-left, shipped in the commit titled "feat(mobile): cue that long chart ranges scroll" (`mobile`).
