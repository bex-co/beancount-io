# w21/749 regression: Cash flow cuts Cyrillic and Persian labels that fit ("Продукты" → "Прод…"), including the app's own Savings label in ru, uk, bg and fa

**Severity:** minor (non-Latin category names become unreadable; in ru, w21/747's disambiguation collapses to two "Сбер…" labels). **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `reports-screen/selectors/sankey-data.ts:217` (`advanceEm`) charges every non-ASCII character 1.05 em, the width of the widest glyphs (Ж, CJK). Typical Cyrillic and Persian letters are about half that. `fitSankeyLabel` (`:233`, applied at `components/cash-flow-sankey.tsx:382`) therefore cuts labels that fit.

Native (iPhone 17e, Metro `--clear` at `2f2eac40f`; disposable ledger restored byte-for-byte; English UI): October `Income:Qa20261020:Зарплата` 3,000, `Expenses:Qa20261020:Продукты` 500 and Rent 300. Reports ▸ 1M ▸ Cash flow's right column reads "**Прод…**", then Rent and Savings, though "Продукты" measures about 58.5 pt against the 68 pt column (agent's CoreText check). Under `tsx`, the Savings node reads "Сбер…" in ru and "پس‌ا…" in fa (37 pt real, so no cut is needed). Before w21/749, all of these showed in full.

## Fix

Measure with per-script advance widths (or the real font metrics, via `onTextLayout` or a measured table) instead of the worst-case em for all non-ASCII text.

**Acceptance:** "Продукты" and "Зарплата" show in full, and ru/fa Savings is cut only when it really overflows. CJK stays safe. Unit cases are added.

**Resolved 2026-10-23** — Most of this was already fixed at HEAD by w21/768 (`b42803b89`), which replaced the 1.05 em bound for non-ASCII text with CoreText-measured advances. "Продукты" (58.5 pt drawn) and "Зарплата" (55.0 pt) now show in full in the 74 pt column. ru's Savings "Сбережения" draws at 74.1 pt, so its cut to "Сбережен…" is a real overflow, as are uk (82.5 pt) and bg (75.8 pt).

One gap remained: a character not in the table was charged the widest advance (1.15 em). That included Persian's zero-width non-joiner, so fa's Savings label "پس‌انداز" (37 pt drawn) was read as 62 pt, and a Persian name that fits could still be cut. `advanceEm` in `reports-screen/selectors/sankey-data.ts` now charges nothing for format controls (`\p{Cf}`) and nonspacing marks (`\p{Mn}`), which CoreText draws at zero width. CJK is unchanged.

Unit cases in `sankey-data.test.ts` pin the drawn widths of the Cyrillic and Persian labels (read never narrower, and within 1.4× of drawn), keep "Продукты", "Зарплата" and fa Savings whole, and pin the ru, uk and bg cuts. At the previous HEAD, fa Savings reads 1.68× its drawn width. Not checked natively. Shipped in the commit that moves this note.
