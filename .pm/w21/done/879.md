# The Accounts tab cuts a long folded account name at the end, hiding the account the row opens: "Qa20261020Brokerage:Fidelity:…" for …:Individual:RothIRA

**Severity:** minor. You can't tell which account a row is, and tapping it opens a page titled with a name the row never showed. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the row label at `components/account-list/account-table.tsx:384` uses `numberOfLines={1}` and is cut at the end (the default). Folded chains build long labels (`select-account-list.ts:259,306`). w3/217's stacked layout applies only at font scale 1.3 and above.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator, at the default text size, English UI, Metro `--clear` at `05f7ed943`, on the disposable QA ledger, which was restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`. Fixture: `Assets:Qa20261020Brokerage:Fidelity:Individual:RothIRA` funded with 12,500.00 USD on 2026-10-01. On Accounts, under Assets, the row reads **"Qa20261020Brokerage:Fidelity:…  $12,500.00"**; "RothIRA", the account the row opens, isn't shown.

## Fix

Use `ellipsizeMode="head"` (or `"middle"`) on labels below the root, so the leaf stays visible, as the account picker did in w4/050.

**Acceptance:** this row shows "…:RothIRA" (or "Qa2026…RothIRA") at the default size. A source case is added.

## Dedupe

The Accounts-tab sibling of w4/050 (picker), left over from w3/217. Not w1/034 (balances). Searched `.pm/**` for account-table together with ellipsize and numberOfLines.

**Resolved 2026-10-24** — the Accounts tab row label is cut in the middle (`ellipsizeMode="middle"`), so a folded chain keeps its root and its leaf ("Qa2026…RothIRA"). Source case in `label-ellipsis.test.ts` (`mobile`). Not run natively.

Native recheck (iPhone 17e, fresh Metro `--clear` at `69037a0e2`, second launch, default text size; the disposable ledger had the note's RothIRA fixture, then was restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`): on Accounts, under Assets, the row now reads "**Qa20261020Br…dividual:RothIRA  $12,500.00**", truncated in the middle with the leaf visible. Fixed.
