# A reinvested $60 dividend shows as "+$0.48" (the cash residue), or as "+0.5 VTI" without one, instead of the income earned

**Severity:** minor (every reinvested dividend, RSU vest or staking reward is headlined wrongly in rows and detail). **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the headline amount selector (`transactions-screen/utils/transaction-display-utils.ts:245-386`) takes the balance-sheet side. With one balance-sheet posting it uses that posting's units ("+0.5 VTI"). With a cash residue, `:345` picks the cost-currency bucket, which is only the 0.48 left over. The same selector feeds Transactions rows, Home's and Reports' Recent Transactions, and the detail hero (`select-transaction-detail.ts` `selectHeroAmount`). The dashboard's `transaction-summary.ts` classifies the same entry as income of $60. Shares given away (`-10 VTI {100 USD}` against `Expenses:Charity`) read "−10 VTI" for the same reason.

Under `tsx` (the agent's proof), `selectEntryRowAmount` and `selectHeroAmount` both return `{"amountStr":"+$0.48","isPositive":true}`, and without the 0.48 cash leg they return "+0.5 VTI". Buys on the same run read "−$1,000.00" as expected.

## Fix

When an entry has an Income (or Expenses) leg in the operating currency and its balance-sheet legs are held at cost, headline the income or expense side (+$60.00), as the dashboard does.

**Acceptance:** this dividend reads "+$60.00" in rows and detail, and the no-residue variant does too. Ordinary buys, sells and transfers are unchanged. Unit cases are added.

**Resolved 2026-10-23** — the headline selector reads a holding received or given at cost for income or an expense by that side: when an entry has Income/Expenses legs in one currency, at least one balance-sheet leg held at cost, and every balance-sheet leg moving the same way, the amount is the income or expense total (`incomeFundedAmount` in `transaction-display-utils.ts`, ahead of the trade picks; corporate actions are still read first).
