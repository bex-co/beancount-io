# Scan Receipt's "Receipt read" screen shows a rounded total ("¥13", "$5.00") while the form saves the exact parsed value (12.50, 4.995)

**Severity:** moderate for multi-currency users. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile (plus backend for the currency gap). A3: `receipt-utils.ts:179` formats `Math.abs(amount)` with `formatMoneyWithCurrency`, which rounds to the ledger currency's minor unit, while the form gets the unrounded `receiptAmountText(amount)` (`receipt-capture-screen.tsx:196`). Separately, the parse contract (`ReceiptParseResult`) has no currency, so a foreign receipt is always booked in the ledger currency.

Not run natively (it needs a parse call, which spends AI quota). Under `tsx` (the agent's proof): on a JPY ledger, a parsed 12.5 reveals "¥13" and the form gets −12.50 / 12.50. On USD, 4.995 reveals "$5.00" and the form gets −4.995; 0.004 reveals "$0.00" and the form gets −0.004.

## Fix

Build the reveal from the same exact text the form receives. Separately, add currency to the parse contract (a backend change; owner sign-off, as w21/206).

**Acceptance:** the reveal and the form agree on the amount. A unit case is added.
