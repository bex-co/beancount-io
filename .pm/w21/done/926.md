# Green and red figures on Home and Budget cards fall below 4.5:1 contrast in light mode (4.17:1 and 4.27:1)

**Severity:** minor to moderate. These are the most prominent figures on Home. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile.

**Root cause (A3).** In light mode, `theme.success` `#0a8748` and `theme.error` `#cc4534` (`mobile/src/common/theme/palette.ts:193-194`) reach 4.5:1 only on white (4.59 and 4.70). `DashboardCard` paints `controlFill` `#f6f4ec` (`components/dashboard-card/index.tsx:27`). Affected text, all at 14–16 pt:
- Home ▸ Recent Transactions income amounts (`entry-row/index.tsx:126-128`).
- The Net Worth / Assets change row, colored by `lineColor` (`common/d3/interactive-line-chart.tsx:702-708, 491`).
- The Budget card's Variance figure (`budget-group-card.tsx:271-275, 364`) and its `errorText` (`:174-178`).

Not run natively in this pass. `tsx` proof with the real styles: in light mode the income amount is **4.17:1** and red is **4.27:1** against the card. Dark mode passes (6.23 and 4.58). No test checks `success` or `error` against `controlFill`.

**Native steps:** in light mode, on a ledger with a recent income entry, look at Home's Recent Transactions and the Net Worth change row.

## Fix

Add light-mode `successText` and `errorText` tokens that reach 4.5:1 on `controlFill` (for example about `#087a41` and `#b83d2e`), following w21/329's `warningText`. Use them wherever these colors are text, and keep the current tokens for bars and lines.

**Acceptance:**
- `control-contrast.test.ts` checks text on `controlFill` and on white at 4.5:1 or better in both themes.
- The three sites above use the new tokens.
