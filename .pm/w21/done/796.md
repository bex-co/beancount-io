# Merchant detail's total and count include pre-entered entries: "4 transactions · $6,000.00" when $4,500 has been paid

**Severity:** minor. The header reads as money paid. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `merchant-detail-screen/selectors/merchant-stats.ts:42` (count) and `:70` (totals) have no `date <= today` condition. The list's count from `PAYEE_ROLLUP_BQL` doesn't either. The date range already says "Scheduled Nov 1, 2026" (w21/335, w21/761), but the total doesn't separate scheduled money.

Reproduced natively with w21/795's fixture and build: Drawer ▸ Merchants shows **"Qa20261020 Landlord · 4 transactions · Scheduled Nov 1, 2026"**. The detail header reads **"4 transactions"** and **"$6,000.00"**, though only three payments ($4,500.00) are on or before today.

## Fix

Total and count only entries dated on or before today, and show the scheduled amount on its own line (for example "+ $1,500.00 scheduled"). Alternatively, label the total as including scheduled entries.

**Acceptance:** with this fixture, the header reads 3 transactions and $4,500.00, with the scheduled $1,500.00 shown separately or labelled. A unit case is added.
