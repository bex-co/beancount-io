# Files: main.bean's lock button (1.34:1) and the editor's new path line (1.78:1) are drawn near-white in light mode

**Severity:** minor (accessibility, light mode). **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: two controls in Files use placeholder greys on the white page:

- **The lock button.** `ledger-file-browser-screen/index.tsx:290-304` colours it `theme.black40`, and it is its own tappable button (it opens an alert). `#e0dfd3` on `#ffffff` = **1.34:1**; WCAG asks 3:1 for a control. Dark passes at 4.07.
- **The path line under the editor title.** `ledger-file-editor-screen/index.tsx:204-210` styles it `pathLine` in `black60` (rendered at `:1180-1188`, 12 pt monospace). `#c2c3b6` on `#ffffff` = **1.78:1**. This is the w21/590 addition, and it breaks w21/481's rule that text uses at least `black80`. `text-style-contrast.test.ts` doesn't cover this style. Dark passes at 6.29.

Native (iPhone 17e, light mode): Files shows `main.bean` with a near-white lock at the row's end (visible only on close inspection); tapping it opens the lock alert. The path line wasn't captured, because it needs a nested file. Contrast was measured from the palette.

## Fix

Use `black80` (5.22:1) for both, and add `pathLine` to the contrast test.

**Acceptance:** both reach at least 3:1 (the lock) and 4.5:1 (the text) in light mode. Dark is unchanged.

## Dedupe

w4/022, w4/051 and w3/626 cover the lock's behaviour, not its colour. w21/128 fixed `black40` placeholders. w21/481 set the text rule. Searched `.pm/**` for pathLine, the lock together with black40, and w21/590.
