## Objective

Check on the hosted endpoint that a burst of `dry_run` previews does not exhaust the write budget, and that previews get the wider bucket from m26.

## Context

- m26 gave previews their own bucket at three times the write budget.
- Probes spend the QA account's budget; run this probe last and keep it bounded.

## Steps

1. Read the documented budgets in `docs/authentication-rate-limits.md`.
2. Send previews up to just above the write budget; confirm a write still succeeds.
3. Stop at the first `RATE_LIMITED` and record `retryAfter`.
