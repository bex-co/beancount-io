## Objective

Finish "every write result carries `commit`": the writes that commit through the ledger service's `commitLedgerFiles` and its single-file endpoints still answer no sha.

## Context

- `t002` threaded the sha through `change-files` (`changeExistingFiles`); the file tools, `appendLedgerText`, rename and revert have it.
- Still without it: `addLedgerEntries` and its REST and GraphQL twins (the ledger service's entries endpoint), the source-slice edits and deletes (`updateEntrySource`, `deleteEntrySource`, `deleteEntrySources` and twins; `commitTransforms` in the ledger package's journal service), `insertReceiptTransaction` and its twins, and the GraphQL file mutations (`createLedgerFile`, `updateLedgerFile`, `deleteLedgerFile`, which use the single-file endpoints: `repoCreateFile`, `repoUpdateFile`, `repoDeleteFile`, whose Gitea responses carry `commit.sha`).
- `commitLedgerFiles` (`ledger/src/features/ledger/operations/commit-ledger-files.ts`) retries on a lost race and settles a commit that landed and lost its answer; the sha is the one of the attempt that succeeded, and none for a settled one.
- The IDL (`backend-cluster/idl/beancount-ledger.openapi.json`) is hand-maintained; regenerate the fava client with `yarn generate-fava-client`.

## Steps

1. Make `commitLedgerFiles` return `{commit?}`; add it to the entries and slice endpoints' responses and the IDL.
2. Carry it through the backend services into each result and its REST, GraphQL and MCP schemas; a preview has none.
3. Do the same for the single-file endpoints and the GraphQL file mutations.
4. Regenerate OpenAPI, re-sync the CLI pin, update `docs/mcp.md` (the "Undo a change" section lists what is not covered) and the id-handoff matrix.
