# On a new ledger, `appendLedgerText` puts the first transactions above the template's `* Transactions` heading, unlike `addLedgerEntries`

**Severity:** minor (layout only; balances are unaffected). **Estimate:** 30 minutes. **Owner:** `backend-cluster/backend-v2` `DirectiveAppendWorkflow` (date-ordered insertion for `appendLedgerText`) versus the bulk entry writer behind `addLedgerEntries`. Both are shared by REST, GraphQL and MCP. **A2:** the starter ledger's org sections (`* Options`, `* Accounts`, `* Transactions`) are what a newcomer opens in Fava, an editor, or the dashboard's file view. After an agent's first writes, transactions sit inside `* Accounts` and `* Transactions` is an empty heading at the bottom.

## Repro (production, 2026-10-22, fresh `qa-` ledgers from the default template, since deleted)

1. `appendLedgerText` with a 2021-06-01 transaction, then `addLedgerEntries` with a 2021-07-01 one. The file then reads (directive and heading lines only):

   ```
   1970-01-01 open Equity:Initial
   2021-06-01 * "via-append"
   * Transactions
   2021-07-01 * "via-add"
   ```

2. Seven appends in mixed date order, with a tie, all land in correct date order, and all of them above `* Transactions`.

## Cause

Date-ordered insertion places new text right after the last directive dated on or before it. On a template ledger that is the last `1970-01-01 open`, which sits before the `* Transactions` heading. The bulk writer evidently appends after the heading when no later directive exists. The two writers disagree on the same input.

## Fix

Use one placement rule for both writers. When the insertion point is followed only by blank lines and org headings (`^\*+ `) before the next directive or the end of the file, insert after those headings. Equivalently, a transaction goes under the last heading that precedes the following directive. Tests: the first append and the first add on a template ledger both land under `* Transactions`; ordering among existing transactions is unchanged; a ledger without headings is unchanged.

**Resolved 2026-10-23** — on the starter ledger, `appendLedgerText`'s first transactions now land under `* Transactions`, where `addLedgerEntries` puts them.

`insertDirectives` (`backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/features/ledger/utils/directive-text.ts`, shared by REST, GraphQL and MCP) still inserts after the last directive dated on or before the new one. The new rule (`placeUnderHeading`): when that directive is of a different kind, and only blank lines and org headings stand between it and the next directive or the end of the file, the new one goes after those headings. On the template, a transaction after the last `1970-01-01 open` therefore starts `* Transactions`.

The note's suggested rule skipped headings for every directive. Taken literally, it would move a new `1970-01-01 open` out of `* Accounts`, and on a ledger with per-year headings it would put a 2021-12-31 entry under `* 2022`. The kind condition keeps both where they belong. One case it still places by section rather than by period: a mixed-kind entry dated exactly at a year boundary (a 2021 balance after the last 2021 transaction) goes under the next `* 2022` heading, still before every 2022 entry, so date order holds.

Not changed: `addLedgerEntries` appends at the end of the file (the ledger service's Python-parity rule). That is why its first write already landed under the template's trailing heading, so the two writers now agree on the starter ledger. A comment above the next directive stops the walk, since it belongs to that directive. `docs/mcp.md` records the placement.

**Tests:** `directive-text.test.ts`, on the real `simpleLedgerTemplate`: three appends in mixed order land under `* Transactions` in date order. A new `open` stays with the opens, and `* Transactions` stays last. A 2021-12-31 transaction stays above `* 2022`. A ledger without headings is unchanged. Reverting the fix fails the template case; the other three guard against over-moving and pass either way.
