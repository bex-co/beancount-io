# A budget for an account the ledger doesn't have is accepted as "No new bean-check errors" and silently never applies

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. **Owner:** `backend-cluster/backend-v2`, the structured `budget` entry in `ledger-entry-service.ts`, shared by MCP `addLedgerEntries`, REST entries and GraphQL. **A1:** "budget 500 USD a month for groceries" is a typical agent request. A misspelt account (`Expenses:Food:Grocery` for `…:Groceries`) produces a budget no report ever matches, and the tool reports success.

## Repro (production, 2026-10-22, disposable `qa-` ledger, since deleted)

`addLedgerEntries` with `{type: "budget", entry: {date: "2021-04-01", account: "Expenses:NopeQa", interval: "monthly", amount: {number: "100", currency: "USD"}}}`. The ledger has no `Expenses:NopeQa` and no account under it. The call returns "Added 1 entry to main.bean. No new bean-check errors.", and `checkLedger` stays clean.

Controls: the same call for an opened account succeeds, as it should. A `balance` against an unopened account is refused with `VALIDATION_FAILED`, as the tool description promises ("a balance against an unopened account"). A budget is a `custom "budget"` directive, whose account argument bean-check never validates, so the new-errors guard cannot catch it.

## Fix

Before writing a `budget`, require its account to be opened or to be an ancestor of an opened account. w21/396 deliberately allows budgets on parents like `Expenses:Food`. Otherwise refuse with `VALIDATION_FAILED` naming the account and the nearest existing ones. Honor `allowInvalid` as the other checks do. Tests on REST, GraphQL and MCP: unknown account refused; opened leaf and unopened parent of opened accounts accepted; `allowInvalid` writes it.

## Dedupe

w21/396 (done) made parent-account budgets work on mobile. w23/059 (blocked) is `auto_accounts` silently opening transaction accounts; budgets are `custom` and are not touched by that plugin. No item covers budget accounts on the API.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-23** — a structured `budget` whose account the ledger does not have is refused before anything is written, on REST, GraphQL and MCP alike.

`assertBudgetAccountsExist` (`backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/features/ledger/service/ledger-entry-service.ts`) runs in the bulk-entries writer, so it covers dry runs too. When a batch holds a budget and `allowInvalid` is not set, it reads the ledger's accounts (`reports.getLedgerAccounts`). Each budget account must be opened, be a parent of an opened account (w21/396's `Expenses:Food`), or be opened by an `open` in the same call. Otherwise the batch is refused with `VALIDATION_FAILED`: "entry 0: budget account Expenses:Food:Grocery is not opened in this ledger, nor is any account under it; nearest: Expenses:Food, Expenses:Food:Groceries, Expenses:Food:Restaurant". The nearest accounts are the opened ones under its deepest existing parent. The hint names `allowInvalid: true`, which records the budget anyway without reading the accounts. The `addLedgerEntries` tool description and the REST route description say so; to keep `tools/list` under its budget, "to the ledger" came out of the tool's first sentence. `docs/mcp.md` documents the rule. The OpenAPI snapshot was regenerated (description only).

Out of scope: a budget appended as Beancount text (`appendLedgerText`) is not checked. That path's guard is bean-check's own errors, and bean-check never reads a `custom` account.

**Tests:**
- `ledger-entry-service.test.ts`: an unknown account is refused with the message, nearest accounts and hint, and nothing is written. An opened account, a parent of opened accounts, and an account the same call opens are accepted. `allowInvalid` writes without reading the accounts.
- `structured-entry-parity.test.ts`, on REST, GraphQL and MCP: `VALIDATION_FAILED` naming the account and its nearest, no write. A leaf and a parent are accepted, and `allowInvalid` commits. The fixture gained the ledger's account list.

Reverting the check fails the 4 refusal cases.

**Gates:** backend-v2 typecheck, ESLint, Knip, 6905 unit tests, `tools/list` size budget, OpenAPI regenerated. `upstream-survival.test.ts` failed as usual (environment-only). `archive-parity` and `batch-processor` (timing) failed under full-suite load and pass alone (33/33). Needs a backend-v2 deploy.
