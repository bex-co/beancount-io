# In Persian the Notifications section headers and the commit detail's message, author line and section labels sit on the physical left over rows that mirror

**Severity:** trivial to minor (fa). **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the sixth note of the leading-edge class (`w22/038`–`041`, `w22/045`; `w0/049`, `w21/019`, `w21/037`); batch the fix with them.

Environment: `main` at `5bb9e90e3` (`origin/main` has nothing under `mobile/` ahead), dev build `io.beancount.ios` on iPhone 17e / iOS 27.0, light, large text, app language Persian after the layout restart, QA account on the hosted server, its synthetic `example` ledger. Read-only: nothing was applied.

## Repro

1. Settings → Language → **فارسی** → Restart now. The bell opens Notifications.
2. The section titles **خطاهای دفتر کل** (Ledger errors) and **تغییرات اخیر** (Recent changes) start at the **left** edge, about 16pt in. The healthy-ledger row under the first is at the right, and every commit row under the second is right-aligned with its chevron at the left.
3. Tap the first commit. The message title (`Update file main.bean`, ledger data) and the author line (`نویسنده: … · ۱ نوامبر ۲۰۲۶، ۲۱:۱۴`) are flush left, and so are the three section labels **آمار** (Stats), **فایل‌ها** (Files) and **تفاوت‌ها** (Diff). Below them the stats row and the file row mirror: the `+1` / `−1` counts at the right, the file name at the right with its counts at the left.

**Control, same flow:** the commit list the user just tapped from shows the same Latin message right-aligned, so the detail jumps to the other side. English is unaffected.

**Expected:** every heading, the message and the author line start at the leading edge, like the rows they head.
**Actual:** they sit at the physical left in Persian only.

## Evidence

Local, gitignored captures under `.tmp/qa-20261112/loop74/`: `05-fa-notifications.png` (the two section titles at the left over right-aligned rows), `06-fa-commit-detail.png` (the message, author line and three labels at the left over mirrored rows).

## Root cause

All are full-width `Text` with no `textAlign`, in files that already import `LEADING_TEXT_ALIGN` (`common/rtl.ts:168`):

- `mobile/src/screens/notifications-screen/notifications-screen.tsx:78-88` (`sectionHeader`, used at `:433` and `:496`); its neighbours `showMoreText`, `errorMessage` and `errorHint` already take it.
- `mobile/src/screens/commit-detail-screen/commit-detail-screen.tsx:75-83` (`sectionLabel`, used at `:319`, `:351`, `:404`), `:89-96` (`messageLine`, `:296`), `:97-103` (`messageBody`, `:300`) and `:104-109` (`metaLine`, `:304`); the same file uses `LEADING_TEXT_ALIGN` further down (`:139`, `:151`, `:184`).
- The guard `mobile/src/common/__tests__/leading-text-align.test.ts` lists none of them.

## Fix

Add `textAlign: LEADING_TEXT_ALIGN` to these six styles and add the blocks to `SURFACES` in the guard; revert the fix and watch the new cases fail. English does not change.

## Blast radius

Notifications (both section headers) and Commit detail, which is also reached from app links (`common/app-links/resolve-app-link.ts`). `messageBody`, shown only for a commit with a body, was not on screen and is read from the source.
