# In Persian the Edit Transaction screen's instruction line sits on the physical left under a header that mirrors

**Severity:** trivial (fa). **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the eighth note of the leading-edge class (`w22/038`–`041`, `w22/045`–`047`; `w0/049`, `w21/019`, `w21/037`); batch the fix with them.

Environment: `main` at `0599445ab` (`origin/main` has nothing under `mobile/` ahead), dev build `io.beancount.ios` on iPhone 17e / iOS 27.0, light, large text, app language Persian after the layout restart, QA account on the hosted server, its synthetic `example` ledger. Read-only: the screen was opened and left; nothing was typed or saved.

## Repro

1. Settings → Language → **فارسی** → Restart now.
2. Transactions tab → the first transaction → the pencil at the left of the header.
3. The header mirrors: **لغو** (Cancel) at the right, **ذخیره** (Save) at the left. The line under it, `متن اصلی beancount را در زیر ویرایش کنید. جمع کنترلی منسوخ یا beancount نامعتبر با خطا رد می‌شود.`, wraps to two lines, and its short second line (`beancount نامعتبر با خطا رد می‌شود.`) starts at the **left** edge, so the whole paragraph is left-aligned. A right-to-left paragraph at the leading edge would end its last line at the right.

The code editor below stays left-to-right on purpose.

**Expected:** the instruction starts at the leading edge, like the header's buttons around it.
**Actual:** it sits at the physical left in Persian only. English is unaffected.

## Evidence

Local, gitignored capture `.tmp/qa-20261112/loop78/06-fa-edit-transaction.png`.

## Root cause

- `mobile/src/screens/edit-transaction-screen/edit-transaction-screen.tsx:88-95` (`hint`, rendered at `:716-718`) is a full-width `Text` with no `textAlign`, and the file does not import `LEADING_TEXT_ALIGN` (`common/rtl.ts:168`) at all.
- `errorText` (`:112-118`, drawn through `InlineError` at `:720`) has none either: `components/inline-error/index.tsx` passes `style` straight to its `Text`. It was not on screen; read from the source.
- The guard `mobile/src/common/__tests__/leading-text-align.test.ts` lists none of it.

## Fix

Add `textAlign: LEADING_TEXT_ALIGN` to `hint` and `errorText`, import it, and add the blocks to `SURFACES` in the guard; revert the fix and watch the new cases fail. English does not change.

## Blast radius

Edit Transaction only. Its Entry Context card is `w22/041`.

## Not part of this

The editor's own text direction (source code, deliberately left to right) and the Latin word `beancount` inside the sentence.
