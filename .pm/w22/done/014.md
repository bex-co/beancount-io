# Home's cost-basis caption can leave the plus sign alone at the end of a line: English at AX2 ("Unrealized +" / "$10,823.44") and Portuguese at default size

**Minor · mobile · 20–30m · A3.** Native mobile QA, 2026-11-10, repository HEAD `6183533d9`, development build `io.beancount.ios` 1.20261002.50 on iPhone 17e / iOS 27.0, light, signed in as the QA account on an authenticated ledger with a priced holding. Read-only pass. Two reproductions: Português at the default text size, and **English at `accessibility-large` (AX2), the supported text-size ceiling** in `.pm/DO_NOT_DO.md`.

## Repro

**A. English, AX2 (reproduced twice, each from a cold launch at that size).**

1. `xcrun simctl terminate <udid> io.beancount.ios`, then `xcrun simctl ui <udid> content_size accessibility-large`, then launch the app and open **Home**, range 6M.
2. Under the net-worth headline the cost basis reads `Cost $107,567.07 · Unrealized +`, and the next line is `$10,823.44`.

**B. Português, default size (reproduced twice, the second from a cold relaunch).**

1. Settings → Language → **Português** → Confirm (no restart needed).
2. **Home**, range 6M. The caption reads `Custo $107.567,07 · Resultado não realizado +` and then, on the next line, `$10.823,44`.

**Control (same screen and data):**

- English at the default size fits on one line: `Cost $107,567.07 · Unrealized +$10,823.44`. Accounts at AX2 is not affected: each balance sits on its own line.
- A second, unrelated synthetic ledger of the same account shows the same split at AX2 (`Cost $31,300.00 · Unrealized +` / `$30,040.00`; one sighting, reached by an accidental ledger switch, so not repeated), so the split does not depend on this ledger's data.
- Dutch wraps before the sign, so the amount stays whole: `Kostprijs $107.567,07 · Ongerealiseerd resultaat` / `+$10.823,44`.
- Spanish puts the currency after the number (`Resultado no realizado` / `+10.823,44 $`), so the plus sits directly before a digit, which is not a break point. (German also writes the currency after the number; its caption was not opened.)

So whether the split happens depends on how wide the line is (translation length, text size), not on the data.

**Expected:** a signed amount stays one unit, as `w4/005` required of the transaction detail's hero amount.
**Actual:** the line breaks between the `+` and the `$`. A gain reads as `… realizado +` followed by an unrelated-looking `$10.823,44`.

Local evidence: `.tmp/qa-20261110/ax2b/home-cold-ax2.png` (English, AX2, full screen), `ax2b/caption-2.png` (second launch, the two caption lines), `ax2c/home-other-synthetic-ledger-ax2.png` (the second ledger), `pt/home.png` (full screen), `pt2/caption-crop.png` (Português cold relaunch, the caption's first lines; the amount is on the line below the crop).

## Root cause (inferred from the layout, not traced through TextKit)

- `mobile/src/common/number-utils.ts` `annotateCurrency`: where the currency symbol precedes the number it returns `` `${sign}${symbol}${written}` `` with nothing between the sign and the symbol.
- `mobile/src/common/valuation.ts:509-524` `costBasisLine` interpolates that string as `{{gain}}` into the translated sentence. Plain Text wraps wherever the Unicode line-breaking rules allow, and a `+` followed by `$` (both prefix-numeric characters) is an allowed break. A `+` followed by a digit is not, which is why the currency-after locales are unaffected.
- `isolateLtr` (`mobile/src/common/bidi.ts`) wraps the amount in directional isolates; it does not stop a line break inside the isolate.

`w4/done/005` fixed the same symptom only for hero amounts (`numberOfLines`, `adjustsFontSizeToFit` in `AmountText`); captions that embed a signed amount in a sentence were outside it.

## Fix

Target: in every locale a signed amount with a leading currency symbol never breaks between the sign and the symbol. Insert a word joiner (U+2060) between them in `annotateCurrency`, and nothing else, so currency-after locales and the number itself are unchanged. Then check the places that compare exact strings (the `-$50` style expectations in the number-utils and valuation tests) and that VoiceOver does not announce the joiner. Alternatively keep the sentence's amount in its own non-wrapping Text; the joiner is the smaller change. Mobile only; no API surface.

## Blast radius

`formatSignedMoneyWithCurrency` is imported by 15 non-test files (among them `home-screen`, `reports-screen` and `budget-screen` code, e.g. `category-breakdown.tsx`, `budget-group-card.tsx`, `budget-labels.ts`, `select-budget-panel.ts`, `sankey-data.ts`). Only the uses that put the result inside a wrapping sentence can show the split: this caption, and possibly the budget and chart summaries. None of the others was checked on screen. A negative amount (`-$…`) follows the same rule on paper (a hyphen-minus before a currency symbol is also a break point), but only the `+` case was observed.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` (including `done/`) for word joiner, U+2060, no-break, stranded/orphaned sign, `costBasisLine` and `annotateCurrency`. Only `w4/005` is close (hero amounts, resolved); no item covers a sign split inside a sentence.

## Unverified

The `-$` case, other languages and widths where the break lands (English was checked at the default size and AX2 only), the other captions listed above, and VoiceOver.

---

**Resolved 2026-11-10** — a signed amount's sign now stays with its currency symbol wherever a visible sentence can wrap: Home's cost-basis caption breaks before the `+`, not after it, so the gain reads as one unit. Shipped in `d3e66b003` (`mobile`). `number-utils.ts` gains `keepSignWithSymbol`, which puts U+2060 (word joiner) between a leading `-`, `+` or `−` and a symbol that follows it, and nothing else: an unsigned amount, one with a digit after the sign (the currency-after languages) and a code-suffixed one come back as they were. It is applied at the three visible sentence sites: `costBasisLine` (cost and gain), the at-market / at-cost note in `valuation.ts`, and `spendingTotalsCaption` under the spending bars. Decision on where to put it: not in `annotateCurrency`, the note's first choice. A trial there changed 32 exact-string tests and put the joiner into every spoken label and comparison; the sentence-site route leaves every formatter result, and so every spoken budget row and chart summary (which never wrap), exactly as it was. The cost is that a new sentence site must opt in; the helper's doc says when. Tests: the helper (all three sign characters, the formatter's real output, "only the joiner is added", the no-op cases, a sign that is not at the start), the cost-basis caption and the spending caption (nine cases fail with the fix reverted), and the source pin on the at-market note. Verified natively on iPhone 17e / iOS 27.0, English at AX2 from a cold launch, the note's reproduction: before, `Cost $107,567.07 · Unrealized +` then `$10,823.44`; after, `Cost $107,567.07 · Unrealized` then `+$10,823.44`. Not checked: the Portuguese default-size reproduction (the same mechanism, a longer sentence), a negative gain's `-$` break on screen, whether VoiceOver passes over the joiner in the spoken Home card (it is invisible and a default-ignorable character; no VoiceOver here), the budget and chart summaries (spoken, so they do not wrap), and Android.
