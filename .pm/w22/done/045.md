# In Persian the account detail's "Transactions" section title sits on the physical left over a screen whose rows, date headers and balance card mirror

**Severity:** trivial to minor (fa). **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the same class as `w22/038`, `w22/039`, `w22/040` and `w22/041` (and `w0/049`, `w21/019`, `w21/037`); batch the fix with them.

Environment: `main` at `47c01a502` (`origin/main` has nothing under `mobile/` ahead), dev build `io.beancount.ios` on iPhone 17e / iOS 27.0, light, large text, app language Persian after the layout restart, QA account on the hosted server, its synthetic `example` ledger. Read-only: nothing was applied.

## Repro

1. Settings → Language → **فارسی** → Restart now.
2. Accounts tab → open the **AccountsPayable** row (any account with entries).
3. The **تراکنش‌ها** (Transactions) title above the entry list starts at the **left** edge, about 16pt in.

**Control, same screen:** the account path caption above the card (`Liabilities:AccountsPayable`), the card's caption and balance, the date headers (`۲۱ مارس ۲۰۱۷`), and the row titles with their icons are all at the right; the amounts and the `مانده:` (Balance) lines are at the left. English is unaffected.

**Expected:** the title starts at the leading edge, like the rows it heads.
**Actual:** it sits at the physical left in Persian only, on the opposite side of everything it labels.

## Evidence

Local, gitignored capture `.tmp/qa-20261112/loop73/05-fa-account-detail.png` (the title at the left, the date header and rows at the right).

## Root cause

- `mobile/src/screens/account-detail-screen/account-detail-screen.tsx:101-108` (`sectionTitle`) is a full-width `Text` with no `textAlign`; it is used once, at `:600-603`.
- `accountPath`, defined just above it (`:93-100`), sets `textAlign: LEADING_TEXT_ALIGN` (`common/rtl.ts:168`), which is why that caption is already on the right.
- The guard `mobile/src/common/__tests__/leading-text-align.test.ts` lists nothing from this screen.

## Fix

Add `textAlign: LEADING_TEXT_ALIGN` to `sectionTitle` and add the block to `SURFACES` in the guard; revert the fix and watch the new case fail. English does not change.

## Blast radius

This one style. The other text on the screen was in view and already follows the leading edge.

## Not part of this

The Latin account path and the payee names are ledger data; sitting at the right of a mirrored row is correct for them.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` including `done/` for the account detail with RTL, "leading edge", "physical left" and `LEADING_TEXT_ALIGN`. `w22/038`–`041` are the same class on the filter sheet, Home, the text-input modal, and the transaction detail with Entry Context; none names the account detail. `w4/066`'s Persian passes looked at the Transactions tab, and `w21/320` only gave the section titles a header role.

## Unverified

Persian at AX2 and in the dark theme; the account detail's empty and error states; Android. The cause is read from the source, the screenshot confirms the symptom.
