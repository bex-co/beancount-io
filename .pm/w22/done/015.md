# Budget screen prorates the month in progress to today ("Budget $40.67") while Home shows the full target ($122.00): w21/994's fix does not reach the screen's real data

**Major (the opposite-verdict case below is derived from the figures, not observed) · mobile · 30–45m · A3.** Native mobile QA, 2026-11-10 (the simulator's clock: Nov 10), repository HEAD `6183533d9`, development build `io.beancount.ios` 1.20261002.50 on iPhone 17e / iOS 27.0, light, English, signed in as the QA account on an authenticated synthetic ledger (a copy of Beancount's example file). Read-only pass; nothing was changed, and the delete icons in the budget entries list were not touched.

## Repro (reproduced twice, the second from a cold relaunch)

1. **Home.** Scroll to the **Budget** card (a tap on the Spending card's testID scrolls it into view). The first row reads `Health:Life:GroupTermLife` · `$0.00 / $122.00`.
2. Tap the card's **See all**. The Budget screen opens on **YTD**. The same account's card reads `Monthly · USD · Below target`, `Budget $40.67 · Actual $0.00 · Variance −$40.67`.
3. Expand **2 entries** at the bottom of that card. Both rows read `Aug 27, 2026 · Monthly · $122.00` (two identical directives; the chart does not double-count them).
4. The chart's dashed Budget line is $122 for Aug, Sep and Oct, then drops to about $40 for Nov, the month in progress.
5. Switch the range chips to **1Y** and **ALL**: the headline is `Budget $40.67` in both. Back on YTD, same. The screen's second card (`Liabilities:AccountsPayable`; its entries were not expanded) also reads `Budget $40.67`, and Home's second row reads `$0.00 / $122.00`.

**Expected:** `w21/994` (done 2026-10-30, commit `1261ea4b9`) says the period in progress is judged against its full target and that "the Budget screen, its card and Home's budget panel therefore agree with the dashboard". So the Budget screen's November figure is the full month, `$122.00`, as Home shows and as August to October already are.
**Actual:** `$40.67` = 122 × 10/30, the target prorated to today, on every range.

Opposite verdicts (derived, not observed; this ledger has no spending this month): with $50 spent in November, Home reads `$50.00 / $122.00` (below target) while the Budget screen reads `Budget $40.67 · Actual $50.00 · Variance +$9.33`, above target. That is the contradiction `w21/076` and `w21/994` set out to remove.

Local evidence (`.tmp/qa-20261110/`): `home-low/after-spending-tap.png` (Home's Budget card), `home-low/budget-screen.png` and `home-low/budget-entries.png` (YTD), `budget/head-1Y.png` and `budget/head-ALL.png`, and from the cold relaunch `budget2/home-budget-row.png` and `budget2/head.png`.

## Root cause (the function's behavior is verified; the server's date for the last point is inferred)

- `mobile/src/screens/budget-screen/selectors/budget-selectors.ts:604`: `budgetForIntervalExact` still does `let end = intervalEnd;` and budgets only up to it. `w21/994` removed the clip to `asOf` just above it, but the function prorates to whatever `intervalEnd` it is given.
- `mobile/src/screens/budget-screen/hooks/use-budget-actuals.ts:129` passes each charted point's own `date` as that `intervalEnd`, and its comment at `:175` says "A windowed range ends today on the server". The in-progress month's point is therefore dated today (Nov 10), not the month's last day.
- Running the repo's own function with this ledger's history (two directives, Monthly 122 USD, 2026-08-27) and today = 2026-11-10: `intervalEnd` 2026-10-31 → `122.00`, **2026-11-10 → `40.67`**, 2026-11-30 → `122.00`. The screen shows exactly that. What was not observed is the API response itself; "dated today" is inferred from the exact match and the hook's comment.
- Home does not use the server's date: `mobile/src/screens/home-screen/hooks/use-budget-panel.ts` takes `{ start, end }` from `currentPeriodRange(group.interval, today)` and hands the selector that `end` (`:199`), the period's own last day, hence the full `$122.00`.
- The dashboard handles the same server behavior explicitly: `budgetHeadlinePeriod` (`dashboard/src/features/ledger-data/budget/budget-utils.ts:261`) headlines "the whole interval containing `today`, never a window edge that clips the period and so prorates its target", and its test builds the series as `[...monthEnds(...), TODAY]`. Mobile has no such step.
- Why the fix's tests pass: the mobile cases I read (`budget-selectors.test.ts`) feed period-end dates such as `"2026-10-31"`, never a today-dated last point.

## Fix

Target: on the Budget screen the period in progress is judged against its full target on every range (YTD, 1Y, ALL), so the headline, the chart's last bar, the variance and the favorable/unfavorable colouring match Home and the dashboard: for this ledger `Budget $122.00 · Actual $0.00 · Variance −$122.00`.

Map a point dated today to the end of the period that contains it before calling `budgetForIntervalExact` (in `use-budget-actuals.ts`), leaving the deliberate cases alone: a window that ended before today still ends its last period with it (`w21/764`), a window start still clips the first period (`w21/102`), a closed account's `endsOn` still applies, and a period not yet begun has no budget. Add a case with a today-dated last point beside the existing ones (`budget-selectors.test.ts`, `budget-headline` tests) so the server's shape is covered. Mobile only; no API surface.

## Blast radius

`budgetForIntervalExact` has two live callers in `mobile/src`: this hook (every Budget card's series, headline stats, chart bars and favorability, and the spoken chart summary built from them) and Home's panel (`select-budget-panel.ts:98`, not affected). Only monthly budgets were observed; a quarterly or yearly budget would be prorated the same way on paper, and a weekly one only to the day. Not checked on screen.

## Adjacent states

Loading and error states (`budgetActualsState`) are not part of this. A period before the budget's first directive, and one not yet begun, keep returning no budget; the fix must not change that.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` (including `done/`) for prorate, "in progress", "full target", `budgetForInterval`, `use-budget-actuals`, and the Budget/Home figures. This is the remaining half of **`w21/994`** (resolved; its definition of done is that the three surfaces agree), plus `w21/076`, `w21/219` and `w21/764` for the window rules. `git log` shows `1261ea4b9` is the last commit to touch `budget-selectors.ts` and the hook. Nothing later covers it, so it is a gap in a completed item, not a duplicate.

## Unverified

**Resolved 2026-11-10** — the Budget screen now judges the month in progress against its full target on every range, as Home and the dashboard do: on the note's ledger YTD, 1Y and ALL all read `Budget $122.00 · Actual $0.00 · Variance −$122.00`, the chart's dashed line stays at $122 through November, and the second card agrees. Shipped in `18af5adbe` (`mobile`). Cause confirmed against the ledger service as well as the arithmetic: a `time`-filtered series ends exactly at its window's last day (`intervalRanges` with `complete=false`), so a window that runs through today dates its last row today, and `budgetForIntervalExact` walks the budget only up to the date it is given; `w21/994` removed the clip to `asOf` but not that one. The fix is the date the hook hands over, not the function: the new `budgetedThrough(date, interval, today)` maps the row dated today to the last day of its own period, and `use-budget-actuals.ts` calls it for every charted row. Every other date is untouched, so the deliberate cases hold: a window that finished before today still ends its last period with it (`w21/764`), a window start still clips its first period (`w21/102`), a closed account still ends its budget (`w21/398`), and a period not yet begun still has none. Tests (`in-progress-period.test.ts`): the mapping for monthly, weekly, quarterly, yearly and daily budgets and for dates that must stay; the note's ledger (two Monthly 122 directives from 2026-08-27, today 2026-11-10) through the real `budgetForIntervalExact` with hand-checked figures (122.00 for the period in progress, 40.67 for the unmapped date, 59.03, 105.73 and 12.20 for the clipping cases); and the hook's use of the mapping. The full-target and wiring cases fail with the fix, or only the hook's call, reverted. The hook's older source guard (`budget-actuals-as-of.test.ts`) keeps its intent, that today is still passed as `asOf`, and now expects the new call. Verified natively on iPhone 17e / iOS 27.0 on the note's ledger, from a cold launch, Home → Budget → See all. Not checked: a quarterly, yearly or weekly budget on screen (covered by the mapping's unit cases), a ledger with real November spending (the verdict flip the note derived), the dashboard's figure on this ledger, and Android.
