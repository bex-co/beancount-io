# Settings rows that have only a title (Plan, Help Center, and Log out below the fold) set their text about 10pt above the centre of the row's icon and chevron, while the two-line rows are balanced

**Severity:** trivial (visual polish, both languages). **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3.

Environment: `main` at `4620f4794` (`origin/main` has nothing under `mobile/` ahead), dev build `io.beancount.ios` on iPhone 17e / iOS 27.0, light, large text, English and Persian, QA account on the hosted server, its synthetic `example` ledger. Read-only: nothing was applied, and the Log out row was not tapped.

## Repro

1. Open Settings (drawer → the account row at the bottom).
2. **Account** block: the profile row (name and email, two lines) is balanced. The **Plan** row under it has its single title line at the top of a 64pt row, while its card icon and its chevron sit at the vertical middle: the title's centre is about 21pt from the row's top edge, the icon's and the chevron's 32pt.
3. **App Settings**: Language / English and Theme / System are balanced. **Support**: the review row (title and `App Store`) is balanced, and **Help Center** is top-heavy the same way as Plan.
4. Persian shows the same (the title at the right, the icon at the right and the chevron at the left, both lower than the text).

**Expected:** a title-only row's text is centred on its icon and chevron.
**Actual:** it hugs the top, leaving about twice the space below it as above.

## Evidence

Local, gitignored captures under `.tmp/qa-20261112/loop76/`: `13-en-settings-CONTAINS-ACCOUNT-EMAIL.png` (English) and `05-fa-settings-CONTAINS-ACCOUNT-EMAIL.png` (Persian). Both contain the account's email and must not be quoted or attached.

## Root cause

`mobile/src/screens/setting/list-item.tsx`: `container` (`:43-47`) has `minHeight: 64` and `paddingVertical: 12`, and `topRow` (`:48-52`) sits at the top of it. `iconContainer` (`:53-61`, `top: 18` for a 28pt icon in a 64pt row) and `arrow` (`:91-95`, `top: 22`) are pinned to the middle of that 64pt. A row with a second line (`content` or `description`) fills the height; a title-only row does not.

## Fix

Centre the row's text block, for example `justifyContent: "center"` on `container`, so the `topRow` sits in the middle of the minimum height; keep the icon and the chevron as they are. Add a case that the container centres its content (the rows with a second line must not move).

## Blast radius

The title-only rows of `ListItemHorizontal`: Plan, Help Center and Log out (Delete account has a description).

## Not part of this

The pins at `top: 18` / `top: 22` also assume a 64pt row, so at larger text sizes, where the row grows, the icon and the chevron would stop being centred; that is a separate question.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` including `done/` for `ListItemHorizontal`, `setting/list-item`, "Plan row", "Help Center" and "title-only", with centre, vertical and align. `w22/021` (the rows re-measure after a live text-size change), `w21/286` (Help Center's language) and `w21/320` (section titles as headings) touch the same rows, none the vertical placement of a one-line title.

## Unverified

The dark theme (same layout); AX2; Android; the Log out row. The cause is read from the source, the screenshots confirm the symptom.

---

**Resolved 2026-11-12** — the Settings row (`ListItemHorizontal`) `container` now has `justifyContent: "center"`, so a title-only row (Plan, Help Center, Log out) centres its one line in the 64pt minimum height that its icon (`top: 18`) and chevron (`top: 22`) are already pinned to the middle of, in both languages; a row with a second line is about that height already and moves by at most a point. The new `screens/setting/__tests__/list-item-layout.test.ts` renders the real row and reads the styles it hands to the native views: the container centres, and the icon and chevron pins stay at the middle of the same minimum height; with the fix reverted the two centring cases fail. Not rechecked on a simulator in this change: the Settings screen in English and Persian, the dark theme, AX2 (where the row grows past 64pt and the pins stop being centred — the separate question the note leaves out) and Android. Shipped in the commit that moves this note (`mobile`).
