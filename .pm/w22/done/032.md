# The largest ordinary text size (extra-extra-extra-large) already switches rows to the stacked accessibility layout, against the documented rule and a unit test whose size ladder is wrong

**Minor · mobile · 30–45m · A2.** Native mobile QA, 2026-11-10, repository HEAD `59f13b939`, development build `io.beancount.ios` 1.20261002.50 on iPhone 17e / iOS 27.0, light, English, signed in as the QA account on its synthetic `example` ledger. Read-only pass. This is the first check of a size between the default and AX2: every earlier native pass used `large` and the AX2 ceiling.

## Repro (each size from a cold launch; XXXL twice)

1. `xcrun simctl terminate <udid> io.beancount.ios`, `xcrun simctl ui <udid> content_size extra-extra-large`, relaunch, dismiss the dev toast, open **Transactions** (a control).
2. Repeat with `content_size extra-extra-extra-large`.

**Expected** (the rule `mobile/src/common/theme/dynamic-type.ts:19-22` states): "the default and every ordinary larger setting keep the existing single-line geometry byte for byte, and only the accessibility sizes (AX1 and up) switch to the stacked variant." So at XXXL, the largest ordinary size, rows stay side by side.
**Actual:** at `extra-extra-large` the rows are side by side: the payee and its note on the left, the amount right-aligned on the same line, long names ellipsized (`STRIPE; TRANSFER; T…`). At `extra-extra-extra-large` the rows are **stacked**: the amount drops under the payee, starts at the **left edge under the icon** (not under the text column), and the long names wrap onto two or three lines. The same switch shows on Home's Recent Transactions rows and on the Accounts tab (the balance moves under the account name). The threshold therefore sits between the two sizes.

Evidence is local only (`.tmp/qa-20261110/w22g/`): `04-transactions-xxl.png` (side by side) against `03-transactions-xxxl.png`, `05-transactions-xxxl-repeat.png` (and `05-xxxl-crop.png`), plus `01-home-xxxl.png` and `02-accounts-xxxl.png`.

## Root cause

`STACKED_LAYOUT_FONT_SCALE = 1.3` (`dynamic-type.ts:24`) is documented as "the first step past xxxLarge ≈ 1.235". The numbers are one step off. React Native's own table (`node_modules/react-native/React/CoreModules/RCTAccessibilityManager.mm:260-271`, in effect because nothing in `src/` or the RN JS sets `setAccessibilityContentSizeMultipliers`) has `extra-extra-large` = **1.235**, `extra-extra-extra-large` = **1.353**, and `accessibility-medium` (AX1) = 1.786. So 1.3 lies below XXXL, and XXXL stacks, which is what the screenshots show.

The unit test that should guard the rule carries the same mistake and so cannot fail on it: `src/__tests__/dynamic-type.test.ts:10-19` lists `1.235, // xxLarge` and `1.235, // xxxLarge` (a duplicate) as the ordinary sizes, and puts `1.35` first in `IOS_ACCESSIBILITY_SCALES` (iOS has five accessibility sizes, AX1 to AX5, and that list has six values; its first, 1.35, is XXXL). Its "keeps the side-by-side row at every ordinary larger size" test (`:26`) therefore never runs against the real XXXL value.

## Fix

Decide which behaviour is wanted, then make the constant, the comment and the test say the same thing:

- **Keep the documented rule** (side by side through XXXL): raise the threshold above 1.353 and below 1.786, for example 1.5, correct the ladder in `dynamic-type.test.ts` to RN's table (0.823, 0.882, 0.941, 1, 1.118, 1.235, 1.353 ordinary; 1.786, 2.143, 2.643, 3.143, 3.571 accessibility) and assert that 1.353 does not stack and 1.786 does. Then check Transactions, Home's recent rows, Accounts, Merchants, the Budget and report rows natively at XXXL, because side by side at 1.353 has never been seen: the rows were designed and tested against 1.235.
- **Or accept stacking from XXXL**: change the comment to say so and the ladder to match; keep the XXXL stacked rows (the names are fully readable there, where XXL ellipsizes them) and look at the amount's left-edge placement under the icon (`transactions-screen/entry-row/index.tsx:120-125`, `amountStacked`: `marginStart: 0`, `alignSelf: "flex-start"`), which reads detached from the name at this size.

Either way the test must use real values. Mobile only; no API surface.

## Blast radius

Every caller of `prefersStackedLayout` (20 non-test files, among them merchant rows, report breakdown rows, Accounts, transaction and journal rows and the Budget card title; `grep -rln prefersStackedLayout mobile/src`), at the one size between XXL and AX1 that was never exercised. Nothing at XXXL looked broken in the four screens opened (Home, Transactions, Accounts, Reports; `.tmp/qa-20261110/w22g/06-reports-xxxl.png`), so this is a mismatch with the stated design, not a layout failure.

## Adjacent states

`extra-extra-large` and below (unchanged, the control), `accessibility-medium` and up (stacked by design), and live changes across the threshold (`w22/017`–`031` cover the live-change clipping separately).

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` (including `done/`) for `xxxLarge`, `extra-extra-extra-large`, `STACKED_LAYOUT_FONT_SCALE`, `prefersStackedLayout`, `1.353`, `1.235`. `w4/done/013`, `w4/done/014` and `w3/done/181` introduced and applied the stacked layout at enlarged sizes without examining where it starts. No item covers XXXL or the threshold's numbers.

## Unverified

Languages other than English (the first language × text-size findings are `w22/033`–`035`), the other stacked screens at XXXL (Merchants, the Budget screen, account detail), the exact `fontScale` the device reports at XXXL (inferred from the layout switch and RN's table, not read from JS), dark mode, Android.

---

**Resolved 2026-11-11** — the note's first option: the documented rule stands, so the threshold now follows it. `STACKED_LAYOUT_FONT_SCALE` is 1.5, between React Native's xxxLarge (1.353, the largest ordinary size) and accessibility-medium (1.786), so rows stay side by side through xxxLarge and stack from AX1; the comment gives RN's real table (xxLarge 1.235, xxxLarge 1.353, AX1 1.786, from `RCTAccessibilityManager.mm`, which nothing in the app overrides) and why 1.3 was wrong. Shipped in `08c85921d` (`mobile`). Tests: `dynamic-type.test.ts` now carries RN's real ladder (seven ordinary sizes, the five accessibility sizes AX1–AX5, instead of xxxLarge as a second 1.235 and six accessibility values), pins 1.353 side by side and 1.786 stacked, and asserts the threshold lies between them; the Welcome actions test, which used 1.3 as an accessibility size, now uses AX1. Verified natively on iPhone 17e / iOS 27.0, light, from cold launches: at `extra-extra-extra-large` Home's recent rows, Transactions, Accounts, Merchants and the top of Reports are side by side with long names ellipsized and nothing overlapping or clipped (side by side at 1.353 had never been seen), and at `accessibility-medium` Transactions still stacks, with the amount under the payee. Not checked at XXXL: the Budget screen and the report breakdown rows below the first screen (no scroll tool), account detail, languages other than English, dark mode, Android. The second option (accept stacking from XXXL) was not taken; the amount's left-edge placement under the icon in the stacked row, which the note calls detached from the name, is unchanged and now appears only at accessibility sizes.
