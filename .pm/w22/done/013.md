# In Ukrainian and Slovak, Reports' "Showing through …" note puts the month in the nominative after "до" / "do": "Показано до жовтень 2026 р.", "Zobrazené do október 2026"

**Trivial · mobile · 10–15m · A3.** Native mobile QA, 2026-11-10, repository HEAD `6183533d9`, development build `io.beancount.ios` 1.20261002.50 on iPhone 17e / iOS 27.0, light, signed in as the QA account on an authenticated ledger whose latest entries are in October 2026. Read-only pass.

## Repro (reproduced in both languages; each language was switched once)

1. Settings → Language → **Українська** (or **Slovenčina**).
2. **Reports**, range **6M** (the note appears for any range except ALL when the latest month with entries is earlier than the current one, `w21/727`).
3. Under the range pills the note reads, in Ukrainian, `Показано до жовтень 2026 р. — останнього місяця із записами.` and, in Slovak, `Zobrazené do október 2026, posledného mesiaca so záznamami.`

**Expected:** the month agrees with the preposition. Both languages take the genitive after "до"/"do": `до жовтня 2026 р.`, `do októbra 2026`. The same sentence needs no change in the other languages: Russian `по октябрь` (accusative, the same form as the nominative) and German `bis Oktober` were seen natively in this session; Catalan `fins a`, Spanish `hasta` and Bulgarian (no cases) were read from the translation files only.
**Actual:** nominative month names, which a native reader sees as a grammar error in the app's own sentence.

Local evidence: `.tmp/qa-20261110/uk/reports.png`, `sk/reports.png`.

## Root cause

- `mobile/src/translations/uk.ts:892` and `sk.ts:901` write `до {{month}}` / `do {{month}}` around an interpolated month.
- `mobile/src/screens/reports-screen/reports-screen.tsx:283` fills it with `formatLedgerMonth` (`mobile/src/common/date-format.ts`), which formats `{ month: "long", year: "numeric" }`. ICU's month-plus-year pattern is the standalone (nominative) form. Checked with Node's `Intl.DateTimeFormat`: `uk` gives `жовтень 2026 р.`, `sk` gives `október 2026`; the genitive appears only when a day is formatted (`1 жовтня 2026 р.`, `1. októbra 2026`). The month is not a translator's typo; the template cannot hold a nominative where its sentence needs a genitive.
- This is the only string in any locale that interpolates `{{month}}` (`grep` over `mobile/src/translations`); the other date sentences use numeric or abbreviated day dates (`від {{date}}`, `з {{date}}`), which do not decline.

## Fix

Target: in Ukrainian and Slovak the note's month is grammatical. Prefer rewording the two strings so the month stands in the nominative, which needs no code or ICU change: for example a colon form (`Показано останній місяць із записами: {{month}}.` / `Zobrazuje sa posledný mesiac so záznamami: {{month}}.`). Do not change the English sentence or the other locales. Add the two translations' form to the translation tests only if one already pins this key. Mobile only; no API surface.

## Blast radius

One key, two locales. Pl, cs and other Slavic languages are not shipped. The same pattern (a month name inside a preposition phrase) would recur if a future string adds one; `translations/__tests__/` has no case-agreement check, and none is proposed here.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` (including `done/`) for `reportsThroughLatestMonth`, "Showing through", genitive, nominative, declension and grammar. `w21/727`, `889` and `890` introduced and adjusted the note's logic (which month and when) and not its wording in these languages; nothing covers case agreement.

## Unverified

Android (same strings), Slovak and Ukrainian wording preferences beyond the grammar (a native speaker should pick the final phrasing).

---

**Resolved 2026-11-10** — Reports' "Showing through …" note no longer puts a nominative month after "до" / "do". Shipped in `a11e177c9` (`mobile`). The two strings now take the month after a colon, where no preposition governs its case: Ukrainian `Показано останній місяць із записами: жовтень 2026 р.` and Slovak `Zobrazuje sa posledný mesiac so záznamami: október 2026.` The English sentence and the other eleven locales are untouched, and no code or ICU change was needed. The wording follows the note's suggested colon form; as the note says, a native speaker should still pick the final phrasing. One thing the note's example got wrong and the simulator caught: Ukrainian's month-plus-year already ends in `р.` (рік), so a closing period in the string rendered `2026 р..`. The Ukrainian sentence therefore ends on the month and lets its `р.` close it, and the Slovak one, whose month has no period, keeps its own. Tests (in `translations/__tests__/meaning-fixes.test.ts`): both sentences rendered with the real `formatLedgerMonth`, no doubled period in any of the thirteen languages' rendering of the note, `{{month}}` still carried everywhere, and English unchanged; two fail with the old strings. Verified natively on iPhone 17e / iOS 27.0 in both languages after a cold relaunch. Not checked: Android (same strings), and a runtime whose ICU writes the Ukrainian month without `р.`, where the sentence would end without a period.
