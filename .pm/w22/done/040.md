# In Persian the shared text-input modal's title and message sit on the physical left, over a form whose rows mirror

**Severity:** trivial to minor (fa). **Estimate:** 10–15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the same class as `w22/038`, `w22/039`, `w0/049`, `w21/019` and `w21/037`. This is a shared component, so one fix covers every place it appears.

Environment: `main` at `80c7e4241` (`origin/main` was 5 commits ahead, none under `mobile/`), dev build `io.beancount.ios` on iPhone 17e / iOS 27.0, light, app language Persian after the layout restart, QA account on the hosted server, its synthetic `example` ledger. Read-only: the form was left untouched and the modal was cancelled.

## Repro

1. Settings → Language → **فارسی** → Restart now.
2. Accounts tab (حساب‌ها) → the **+** at the left end of the header → **افتتاح حساب** (Open Account).
3. Tap the **واحد پول** (currency) row, pick the last item of the picker (the "other currency" entry), تأیید.
4. The modal opens. Its title **ارز دیگر** and its message **کد ارز یا دارایی را وارد کنید، مثلاً EUR یا VTI.** start at the **left** of the card (about 36pt in; the card spans 16–374pt), and the title ends at 90pt, leaving the whole right side empty. The form behind it is mirrored: **واحد پول** and **تاریخ افتتاح** are at the right edge with their chevrons on the left. The modal's own buttons mirror correctly (لغو on the right, تمام on the left).

**Expected:** the title and the message start at the leading edge, like every other heading and help line in the app (`w21/019`'s rule).
**Actual:** both sit at the physical left in Persian only. English is unaffected.

## Evidence

Local, gitignored capture `.tmp/qa-20261112/loop63/07-fa-text-input-modal.png` (the modal over the mirrored form).

## Root cause

- `mobile/src/components/text-input-modal/index.tsx:61-66` (`title`) and `:67-71` (`message`) are full-width `Text` with no `textAlign`, and the file imports nothing from `common/rtl.ts`.
- `mobile/src/common/rtl.ts:168` `LEADING_TEXT_ALIGN` is the documented answer for a `Text` wider than its content. The guard `mobile/src/common/__tests__/leading-text-align.test.ts` does not list this component.

## Fix

Add `textAlign: LEADING_TEXT_ALIGN` to `title` and `message`, and add the two blocks to `SURFACES` in that test; revert the fix and watch the new cases fail. English does not change.

## Blast radius

The component is shared: Open Account and Add Budget ("other currency"), Files (create a file), and Settings (the account-deletion confirmation, `screens/setting/main-content.tsx:370-373`, whose title and message use the same two styles). The last was **not** opened natively, on purpose: it is a destructive flow, and the fix is the same two styles.

## Not part of this

The input inside the modal takes a Latin currency code or file name and sits at the left with its caret. `rtl-input-align.test.ts` does not list it among the leading-edge inputs, and a Latin value at the left is reasonable. Not filed.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` including `done/` for `text-input-modal`, `TextInputModal`, "other currency" and Persian, RTL, "leading edge" and "physical left". `w4/043` (the account-deletion failure strings) touches the same flow but not alignment; none of the others names this component. `w22/038` and `w22/039` are the same class on the filter sheet and Home.

## Unverified

The Files create-file and the Settings delete-account instances natively; Persian at AX2 and in the dark theme; Android. The cause is read from the source, the screenshot confirms the symptom.

---

**Resolved 2026-11-12** — the shared text-input modal's `title` and `message` now take `textAlign: LEADING_TEXT_ALIGN`, so in Persian they start at the right over the mirrored form instead of the physical left; every place the modal appears (Open Account and Add Budget's "other currency", Files' create-a-file, Settings' account-deletion confirmation) follows from the one component. The same component's two other full-width texts, the toggle label (`flex: 1` beside its switch) and the input's error line, had the same gap and were fixed with them. All four blocks are in the `SURFACES` list of `common/__tests__/leading-text-align.test.ts`; with the fix reverted the four new cases fail. Not rechecked on a simulator in this change: the Persian modal, the toggle and error variants (neither is reachable in the instances opened for this note), the destructive instance (deliberately not opened), the dark theme and Android. Shipped in the commit that moves this note (`mobile`).
