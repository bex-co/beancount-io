# A EUR amount valued at cost loses its decimals on Accounts and in the holdings sheet: "Not in total: 1,800 EUR" beside "970.00 EUR", while the account's own journal reads "+1,800.00 EUR"

**Minor · ledger service (`backend-cluster/ledger`), seen on mobile · 30–45m · A3.** Native mobile QA, 2026-11-10, repository HEAD `6183533d9`, development build `io.beancount.ios` 1.20261002.50 on iPhone 17e / iOS 27.0, light, English, signed in as the QA account on one of its synthetic ledgers (operating currency USD; it holds VTI bought at a EUR cost, with no VTI price). Read-only pass: the ledger was only selected and read, never edited.

## Repro (reproduced twice, the second from a cold relaunch)

1. Select that ledger from the drawer, then open **Accounts**.
2. Under **Assets**, the `BrokerUsd` row reads `$500.00` and `Not in total: 1,800 EUR`. Its siblings on the same screen read `Mixed` → `Not in total: 970.00 EUR`, `BrokerEur` → `2,300.00 EUR`, `QaMargin` → `100.00 EUR`.
3. Open `BrokerUsd`. The July 1 day header reads `+$500.00 · +1,800.00 EUR` and the buy row `+1,800.00 EUR` / `Not in total`. Open that transaction: `+10 VTI`, `cost 180.00 EUR · 2026-07-01`, and Money flow `FROM Equity:Opening  −1,800.00 EUR`.
4. On the account card tap the `›` after "1 not in total": **Holdings and prices** lists `10 VTI · No price · At cost` and `1,800 EUR · Not in total`.

**Expected:** one amount, one precision. The cost is written `180.00 EUR`, so ten units cost `1,800.00 EUR`: Python's `Decimal` gives `10 × 180.00 = 1800.00` (the reference the service aims to match), the journal and Money flow already read `1,800.00 EUR`, and EUR is a fiat currency read to cents elsewhere in the app (`+12.00 EUR`, `970.00 EUR`).
**Actual:** `1,800 EUR` on the Accounts row and in the holdings sheet, with no decimals, so the same amount reads two ways on neighbouring screens.

**It depends on what else the account holds.** `BrokerEur` buys the same `10 VTI {180.00 EUR}` and also holds a passed-through `+500.00 EUR` of cash; its row reads `Not in total: 2,300.00 EUR`. `Mixed` (3 VTI at 180.00 EUR, 2 VTI at 190.00 EUR, 4 VTI at 200.00 USD, and `+50.00 EUR` cash) reads `970.00 EUR`. So the identical purchase prints `1,800` in one account and keeps its two decimals in another only because a written-scale amount sits beside it.

Local evidence (`.tmp/qa-20261110/`): `ubc/03-accounts.png`, `ubc/04-brokerusd-detail.png`, `ubc/07-buy-vti-detail.png`, `ubc/08-valuation-sheet.png`, and the cold relaunch `ubc2/accounts-crop.png`.

## Root cause (the service behavior is verified by running its code; the rest is read from source)

- Accounts reads the trial balance with `conversion: "at_value"` (`mobile/src/common/balance-util.ts:33`): a lot is valued at its price, and **at its cost when the ledger has no price** for it.
- `backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/lot-inventory.ts:260-264`, in `reduceScaled`: for an amount that is converted rather than passed through, `scale = value.decimalPlaces() ?? 0`. A `BigNumber` product `10 × 180.00` is `1800` with **0 decimal places**; the written `.00` is gone. `account-tree.ts:147` then writes `value.toFixed(scale)`, which is `"1800"`. (The second conversion hop, `:285`, uses the same call.)
- Run in `backend-cluster/ledger` with `npx --no-install tsx` against `LotInventory`: for a lot `10 VTI {180.00 EUR}`, `reduceScaled(parseConversion("at_value"), no prices)` returns `{"EUR":"1800"}`, and so does `at_cost`. Cost `180` gives the same. (The scratch file was deleted afterwards.)
- The same code reproduces all three accounts' EUR figures from their lots (read from their transaction details), `at_value`, no prices: `BrokerUsd` (`10 VTI {180.00 EUR}`) → `{"EUR":"1800"}`; `BrokerEur` (the same lot plus `500.00 EUR`) → `{"EUR":"2300.00"}`; `Mixed` → `{"EUR":"970.00","USD":"800"}`. The sum keeps the widest scale (`addScaledInto`, `lot-inventory.ts:~296`), and the passed-through cash is the only part that carries one. That is why the neighbouring rows look right.
- `w21/125` fixed the cases where the written scale survives: a passed-through amount keeps `lot.scale` and a sum takes the widest (`1250.50`, `999.50 + 0.50 = 1000.00`). Its own comment says "a valued amount's own places otherwise", which is exactly where BigNumber forgets the written zeros. Its production recheck probed passthrough amounts only.
- Mobile only displays what it is given: `formatHolding` → `formatUnits(number, currency, scale)` (`mobile/src/common/balance-display.ts:173`), whose doc reads "a commodity amount at its recorded scale". Why the journal path shows `1,800.00` was not traced.

## Fix

Target: an amount valued at a cost or a price keeps Decimal's scale, the units' written scale plus the rate's written scale (`10 × 180.00` → `1800.00`; `10 × 200.00` → `2000.00`), so the API returns `"1800.00"` and every client (REST, GraphQL, MCP, mobile, dashboard) reads the same digits. The lot stores only the units' written scale today, so the cost's (and the price directive's) written scale has to be carried alongside it. Add cases to `__tests__/lot-inventory.test.ts` and `account-tree.test.ts` for an unpriced lot at cost and a priced one, under `at_value` and `at_cost`, next to the existing `1250.50` cases. Check that `w21/207`'s expectation (units × cost products with many decimals) still holds: the product of two scales is what Decimal keeps, so it should.

An alternative on the mobile side, which `w21/125` listed as its second option, is to read ISO fiat holdings to at least the currency's minor units in `formatHolding`. It would hide this case but not fix the API's digits, so take one or the other deliberately.

## Blast radius

`reduceScaled` feeds the account tree (`account-tree.ts:138`) and the interval series (`interval-series.ts:227`), so every converted amount in a trial balance, balance sheet or chart series can lose its trailing zeros the same way. Observed on mobile only: the Accounts row, and the holdings sheet (the root's "Not in total" note was not separately checked). The dashboard and MCP were not exercised. A lot valued at a price (rather than at cost) goes through the same `decimalPlaces()` call; it was not observed on screen.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` (including `done/`) for `decimalPlaces`, "converted amount" scale, "valued amount", trailing zeros and "Not in total" with decimals. `w21/125` (resolved) covers passthrough amounts and sums; `w21/207` (resolved) is the opposite symptom (a product with too many decimals); `w3/189` states the contract that one amount reads the same on every surface. Nothing covers a converted amount losing its written zeros.

## Unverified

Why `QaMargin` (`100.00 EUR`) keeps two decimals (its lots were not opened; it is probably passed-through cash like the others), a priced valuation, the dashboard and MCP outputs, and Android. The ten commits `origin/main` has added under `backend-cluster/ledger/` since this checkout (checked 2026-11-10 09:47) do not touch `lot-inventory.ts` or `account-tree.ts`; the only change near it is `interval-series.ts`'s empty-prefix rule (`w1/191`), so this is not deployment lag.

**Resolved 2026-11-10** — a lot valued at its cost or a price now keeps the decimal places Python's `Decimal` keeps, so `10 VTI {180.00 EUR}` with no VTI price comes out of the trial balance as `"1800.00"`, the same string as the account's own journal and the lone passed-through cash beside it, and the QA ledger's three accounts read `1800.00`, `2300.00` and `970.00 EUR` (`Mixed` also `800.00 USD` where it read `800`). Shipped in `cb1454bef` (`backend-cluster/ledger`). Cause as the note traced it, and the fix follows the journal path's own rule, which already carried scales (`journal-running-balance.ts`): `reduceScaled` took a converted amount's places from the `BigNumber` product, which has none to give. Each lot now carries the places its per-unit cost was written with (a total cost takes its quotient's, as Python keeps them), each price carries its written places through the new `PriceMap.getScaledRate` (an inverse keeps its own, as before), and `valueLot` returns units + cost, units + price, or units + both rates across a two-hop conversion; a sum keeps the widest. Units, passed-through amounts and amounts written without places are unchanged. The note's two options: this is the API-side one, so REST, GraphQL, MCP, mobile and the dashboard all read the same digits; the mobile-side minor-unit rounding was not needed. `w21/207`'s expectation (a product with many decimals) still holds. Two existing tests encoded the old output and now read the Decimal result: `10 × 100.00` is `1000.00` (`unbooked-balances.test.ts`) and `-2.000 × 84.60` plus `4.950` is `-164.25000` (`current-earnings.test.ts`). New tests: unpriced and priced lots, units' places, a two-hop conversion, a total cost, netting, widest-wins and raw units (`lot-inventory.test.ts`), `getScaledRate`, and the QA ledger's accounts through `accountHierarchy` (nine of the lot tests fail with the fix reverted). `typecheck`, `lint:deadcode`, `eslint`, the 3,330 unit tests and `verify:rustledger` (including its 24 report cases against stock beancount) pass. Two caveats, stated plainly. This is a behaviour change across the service: a converted amount may now carry trailing zeros it did not (`10 × 1.1` is `11.0`, as Python writes it), which is the contract the note and `w3/189` ask for, and the only two tests it moved are listed above. And it is not seen on a device yet: the fix reaches the app only after the ledger service is deployed, so the production recheck of Accounts and the holdings sheet is the owner's after that deploy. Not exercised: the dashboard and MCP outputs for this ledger, and Android.
