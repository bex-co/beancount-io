# At AX2 the Budget card's "Variance" label breaks mid-word in German ("Abweich" / "ung"), Russian ("Отклоне" / "ние") and Ukrainian ("Відхилен" / "ня") and pushes its amount below the other two stats

**Minor · mobile · 20–30m · A2.** Native mobile QA, 2026-11-10, repository HEAD `59f13b939`, development build `io.beancount.ios` 1.20261002.50 on iPhone 17e / iOS 27.0, light, German, signed in as the QA account on its synthetic `example` ledger (the app's own example data, which carries two Monthly 122 USD budgets). Read-only pass; nothing was tapped on the card and the app language was restored to English afterwards. Found in the same language × text-size pass as `w22/033`, on a different component.

## Repro (reproduced twice, each from a cold launch at AX2)

1. Settings → **Language** → **Deutsch** → Confirm.
2. `xcrun simctl terminate <udid> io.beancount.ios`, `xcrun simctl ui <udid> content_size accessibility-large` (AX2, the supported ceiling in `.pm/DO_NOT_DO.md`), relaunch, dismiss the dev toast.
3. Home → scroll the Budget card into view (`automation_tap` on testID `spending-card`) → the Budget card's **Alle ansehen**. The Budget screen opens on YTD.

**Expected:** the stat row reads `Budget  Ist  Abweichung` over `122,00 $  0,00 $  -122,00 $`, each label whole.
**Actual:** the third column's label wraps by character: `Abweich` on one line and `ung` on the next (in Russian, `Отклоне` / `ние`, reproduced once from a cold launch at AX2: `.tmp/qa-20261110/w22i/27-budget-ru-ax2.png`, `-crop`; in Ukrainian, `Відхилен` / `ня`, once from a cold launch at AX2: `.tmp/qa-20261110/w22j/05-home-budget-uk-ax2.png`), and its amount `-122,00 $` drops to a third line, so the three columns have different heights and the variance, the figure the card exists to show, sits lowest. `Budget` and `Ist` are whole. The card's title wraps onto two lines (by design, `w21/027`), and the chips (`Monatlich`, `USD`, `Unter dem Ziel`) are whole.

**Control:** the same screen at `large` in German, and English at AX2 (`Variance`, 8 letters, seen in earlier passes), are fine. Only `Abweichung` (10 letters) at AX2 exceeds a column.

Evidence is local only (`.tmp/qa-20261110/w22i/`): `17-budget-de-ax2.png`, `19-budget-de-ax2-repeat.png` (and `-crop`).

## Root cause

`mobile/src/screens/budget-screen/components/budget-group-card.tsx:343-377`: the stat row is three sibling `View`s with `flex: 1` (`styles.stat`, `:146-148`) inside a row (`styles.stats`, `:141-145`), so each column is one third of the card whatever its content, at about 120 pt wide at AX2. The label is `styles.statLabel` (`:149-154`, `fontSizes.xs`), a plain `<Text>` with no `numberOfLines` and no fit rule, so a word wider than the column breaks by character. The card does read `prefersStackedLayout(fontScale)` (`:209`), but only for its title row; the stat row has no stacked variant. German `budgetVariance` is `"Abweichung"` (`mobile/src/translations/de.ts:728`); the other 10-letter labels are `uk` `Відхилення`, `ru` `Отклонение`, `bg` `Отклонение`, `es` `Diferencia`, `ca` `Diferència`, so those are at the same risk: Russian and Ukrainian were run and break the same way; Bulgarian, Spanish and Catalan were not.

## Fix

Make the stat row survive a label wider than a third of the card; any one of these:

- At large sizes (the card's own `stacked` flag) lay the three stats out as a column of label/value pairs instead of three columns, as the card's title row already does. This also helps the `Ist` and amount widths with long amounts.
- or keep three columns but let the columns size to their content (`flex: 1` → `flexGrow`/`flexShrink` with a `minWidth`) and wrap the row (`flexWrap: "wrap"`).
- or `numberOfLines={1}` with `adjustsFontSizeToFit` on `statLabel`, accepting a slightly smaller caption.

Add a unit case beside `budget-screen/components/__tests__/` that renders the card's stat row at the AX2 scale with the German label (and one Cyrillic 10-letter one) and asserts no label shares a line with a broken word, or at minimum that the stacked variant is chosen at AX2; then verify natively in German at AX2. Mobile only; no API surface.

## Blast radius

Every Budget card (one per budget and currency). Home's Budget panel rows (`select-budget-panel`) and the Add Budget form were not part of this finding. Filed beside `w22/033` (a caption that breaks mid-word in German at AX2), `w22/035` (a menu row that breaks mid-word in Russian at AX2) and `w22/018` (live text-size changes on the same card).

## Adjacent states

A card with only two stats (no actual yet: the middle column is absent and the other two widen), a signed long amount in a wide currency, and the stacked title row (`w21/027`).

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` (including `done/`) for `budgetVariance`, `statLabel`, "stat row", "Variance", AX2, German and mid-word. `w21/done/027` (the card title at AX2), `w4/done/038` (YTD) and `w22/018` (live size changes) concern the same card, not this label in a long language. Nothing covers a long translated label in the stat row.

---

**Resolved 2026-11-11** — a Budget card's stat row (Budget / Actual / Variance) now stacks from the shared stacking threshold, the one the card already reads for its title row: from accessibility sizes the three stats take a line each, label above amount, so a label has the card's whole width; at ordinary sizes the three columns are unchanged. The fix is language independent, which covers Bulgarian, Spanish and Catalan (the other long labels the note listed) without a run of their own. Shipped in `77c4713fc` (`mobile`). Tests: the stacked styles and their use from the threshold are pinned in `budget-card-title-layout.test.ts` (the new case fails with the change reverted). Verified natively on iPhone 17e / iOS 27.0, light, from cold launches at AX2, with nothing tapped on the card: in German `Budget`, `Ist` and `Abweichung`, and in Russian `Бюджет`, `Факт` and `Отклонение`, each read whole with its amount beneath it; the language was restored to English afterwards. Not checked: Ukrainian (same fix, not re-run), a card with only two stats or a `Starts on` note, Home's Budget panel rows, dark mode, Android.
