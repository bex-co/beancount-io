# After a live text-size change nearly every Settings row and section title is cut to its top half

**Minor · mobile · 30–40m · A2.** Native mobile QA, 2026-11-10, repository HEAD `59f13b939`, development build `io.beancount.ios` 1.20261002.50 on iPhone 17e / iOS 27.0, light, English, signed in as the QA account. Read-only pass: no row was tapped, and nothing near *Delete account* was touched. The local captures show the account's name and email and are not for quoting.

## Repro (reproduced twice, the second from a fresh relaunch)

1. Terminate the app, `xcrun simctl ui <udid> content_size large`, relaunch, dismiss the dev toast.
2. Open the drawer and open **Settings** from the account row at its bottom (`automation_tap` on testID `drawer-identity-row`; verify with a screenshot before anything else).
3. With Settings open, `xcrun simctl ui <udid> content_size accessibility-large` (AX2, the supported ceiling in `.pm/DO_NOT_DO.md`). Take screenshots only.

**Expected:** every title, subtitle and section title reads whole and the rows grow.
**Actual:** `Invite Friends`, `Account`, `App Settings` and `Support` show only their upper halves; every row title (`Plan`, `Language`, `Theme`, `Help Center`, the review row) and subtitle (`English`, `System`, `App Store`) is cut the same way, in the old row heights; the invite blurb is one line cut at the gift icon (`Share this professio`) instead of wrapping.

**Control (cold):** terminate, set AX2 first, launch, open the drawer, then **Settings** by `automation_tap` on testID `drawer-identity-row`: every title, subtitle and section title is whole, and the invite blurb wraps onto six lines beside the gift icon. (An earlier version of this note could not give a cold control; the drawer's account row has this testID, so it can be reached at AX2 without a scroll.)

**The other direction (observed on this screen, 2026-11-10):** cold-launch at AX2, open Settings, then `content_size large` with it open. The text re-renders at the default size but the rows keep their AX2 heights: the invite block stays about 700 px tall around a three-line blurb, `Invite Friends` and `Account` sit in tall bands, and each row has a blank gap under its title, with the gift icon floating in the middle. This is the "shrinking leaves blank space" half of the mechanism `mobile/AGENTS.md` describes, and the same keys fix both directions. Evidence: `.tmp/qa-20261110/w22d/10-settings-ax2-start.png`, `11-settings-shrunk.png`.

Evidence is local only (`.tmp/qa-20261110/w22b/`, `.tmp/qa-20261110/w22c/`): `43-settings-live-ax2.png`, `46-settings-live-ax2-repeat.png` (and `-crop`) against `w22c/41-settings-cold-ax2.png` (cold control) and `42-settings-large.png`.

## Root cause

`grep fontScale mobile/src/screens/setting/*.tsx` finds nothing. The rows' Texts are in `mobile/src/screens/setting/list-item.tsx` (`title` `:99`, `secondaryText` `:106`, `:122`, the section `title` `:175`, `text` `:236`); the invite blurb sits in the same screen's header block (the exact site is untraced).

## Fix

Key the row and section Texts on `fontScale` (distinct key per sibling), never the screen, so the language wheel, the theme choice and any open modal survive. Add the file to the `KEYED_TEXTS` table in `mobile/src/__tests__/live-font-scale-remeasure.test.ts`, then verify default → AX2 → default natively. Do it with taps by testID only (`drawer-identity-row`, `setting-profile`, `setting-plan`, `setting-theme`) and never near the delete row. Mobile only.

## Blast radius

Every Settings row. The pushed Plan screen is `w22/025` and the Referral screen `w22/029`. Filed beside `w22/017`–`020`, `022`–`029`.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` including `done/` for `screens/setting`, `list-item`, "Settings screen", `fontScale`, live text size. `w3/done/184` and `w4/blocked/002` do not concern text size. Nothing covers live size changes on Settings.
