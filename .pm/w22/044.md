# The scroll cue on long chart ranges is a hard-edged strip in the page's surface colour, so it reads as a box on the card in light and dark, and it washes out the first month's label

**Severity:** trivial to minor (visual). **Estimate:** 15–20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the first native look at the cue `w21/1015` shipped, whose resolution says "not checked on a device".

Environment: `main` at `0c2f63206` (`origin/main` has nothing under `mobile/` ahead), dev build `io.beancount.ios` on iPhone 17e / iOS 27.0, large text, English (the Persian Budget screen shows the same strip), QA account on the hosted server, its synthetic `example` ledger. Read-only: nothing was applied, and the earlier months were not revealed (the QA tooling has no swipe gesture).

## Repro

1. Home → the Budget card → tap a budget row (or **See all**). The Budget screen opens on YTD. Each card's chart has a strip at the left edge of the plot, about 18pt wide and 190pt tall, with a `<` chevron, because the earlier months are scrolled off.
2. Measured on the screenshots: in light mode the strip is `rgb(253,252,250)` on a card of `rgb(246,243,236)`, a lighter box with a visible hard edge. In dark mode the strip is `rgb(25,29,21)` on a card of `rgb(40,41,33)`, a clearly darker box. The faded budget dashed line runs under it, and a ghost fragment of the clipped month label's tail (the end of `MAY`) shows at its lower-left.
3. Reports tab → **1Y**: the same strip over the income/expense chart. The first visible month's label reads as a faded **A** followed by `PR`: the `A` sits under the strip, and the Net Profit line's first segment is dimmed there too.

**Expected:** the cue blends into the card (the card's own fill, ideally fading to transparent) and does not hide the first column's label.
**Actual:** a box in the page's base colour over an inset card, covering the first column's label.

## Evidence

Local, gitignored captures under `.tmp/qa-20261112/loop71/`: `01-after-budget-row.png` (light Budget), `02-budget-dark.png` (dark Budget), `04-reports-1y.png` (Reports 1Y).

## Root cause

- `mobile/src/common/d3/chart-chrome.tsx:43-51` (`clipCue`) sets `backgroundColor: theme.white` at `opacity: 0.85`. `theme.white` is the base page surface (`common/theme/palette.ts:174`, `#ffffff` in light; the charcoal base at `:228` in dark).
- Both consumers draw their chart inside a `DashboardCard bleed` (`budget-group-card.tsx:294`, `reports-screen.tsx:308`), whose fill is `theme.controlFill` (`components/dashboard-card/index.tsx:27`, `#f6f4ec` in light), a different layer from the page surface.
- The cue is placed at `left: axisWidth` (`common/d3/scrollable-axis-chart.tsx:161`), over the plot's first 18pt, where the first column's label starts.

## Fix

Give the cue the card's fill (`theme.controlFill`) and preferably an SVG gradient from that fill to transparent, so the first label stays readable. Keep the chevron and the "only when the plot overflows" rule (`hasClippedStart`). Add a rendered or unit case that the cue's colour equals the card's fill in both themes (and, if a gradient is used, that it ends transparent); revert the fix and watch it fail.

## Blast radius

`ScrollableAxisChart`'s two consumers: `income-expense-bar-chart.tsx` (Reports, YTD / 1Y / ALL when more months than fit) and `budget-bar-chart-d3.tsx` (every Budget card at long ranges).

## Not part of this
