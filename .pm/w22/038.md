# In Persian the Transactions filter sheet's section titles and its range-error line sit on the physical left while the chips, rows and buttons mirror

**Severity:** trivial to minor (fa). **Estimate:** 15–20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the same class as `w0/049` and `w21/019`, which fixed it for Settings, the Payee screen, Spending, Add Budget and the drawer, and which put a guard test on those surfaces; this sheet was not on the list.

Environment: `main` at `9c1c1dbc2` (`origin/main` was 16 commits ahead, none under `mobile/`), dev build `io.beancount.ios` on iPhone 17e / iOS 27.0, light, app language Persian after the layout restart, QA account on the hosted server, its synthetic `example` ledger. Read-only: nothing was applied.

## Repro

1. Settings → Language → **فارسی** → Restart now (the layout restart).
2. Transactions tab → the filter icon (now at the left end of the search field).
3. Look at the sheet: the chips (تأییدشده، در انتظار، سایر / همه، ۱م، ۳م، از آغاز سال، دلخواه) start from the right edge, the START/END rows (از / تا) and the Account row are right-aligned with their chevrons on the left, "لغو" is on the right and "بازنشانی" on the left. The three section titles, **وضعیت**, **بازهٔ زمانی** and **حساب**, are flush against the **left** edge (about 16pt in), the side the rest of the sheet has just left.
4. Tap **دلخواه** (Custom), tap the از row, move the month wheel to December, تأیید. The red line **تاریخ پایان باید برابر یا پس از تاریخ شروع باشد.** appears and spans only the left 16–247pt of the row, again on the left, with 127pt of empty space to its right.

**Control, same session:** Settings' section titles (دعوت دوستان، حساب، تنظیمات برنامه، پشتیبانی) and the Transactions tab's date headers (`۲ اکتبر ۲۰۲۶`) sit on the right, at the leading edge, as they should. English is unaffected: the titles are at the left, where they belong.

**Expected:** the titles and the error line follow the layout's leading edge, like every other caption in the app.
**Actual:** the titles and the error line are at the physical left in Persian only, so each heading ends up on the opposite side of the controls it labels.

## Evidence

Local, gitignored captures under `.tmp/qa-20261112/loop61/`: `08-fa-sheet.png` (the sheet with its titles at the left), `09-fa-custom.png` (Custom rows), `11-fa-reversed.png` (the error line at the left), `07-fa-tx.png` (the right-aligned date headers on the tab behind it).

## Root cause

- `mobile/src/screens/transaction-filters-screen/transaction-filters-screen.tsx:67-74` (`sectionTitle`) and `:117-122` (`rangeError`) set no `textAlign`. Both are full-width `Text`.
- `mobile/src/common/rtl.ts:168` exports `LEADING_TEXT_ALIGN`, and its comment says why: left at `natural`, even Persian UI copy lands on the physical left on current iOS builds, beside rows that mirror; "any `Text` wider than its content ... needs this".
- The guard `mobile/src/common/__tests__/leading-text-align.test.ts` keeps `LEADING_TEXT_ALIGN` on eight listed style blocks (Settings, Spending, Budget card, drawer, Add Budget's help and error lines, Invite Friends). None of them is on this screen, which is why it was missed.

## Fix

Add `textAlign: LEADING_TEXT_ALIGN` to `sectionTitle` and `rangeError`, and add the two blocks to `SURFACES` in `leading-text-align.test.ts`. Revert the fix and watch the new cases fail. English does not change (`"left"` is already the leading edge there, and React Native swaps it under RTL).

## Blast radius

The sheet's two styles. A grep for uppercase captions without `LEADING_TEXT_ALIGN` finds one more file, `screens/merchants-screen/merchants-screen.tsx`; it was not checked natively here, so it is a pointer, not a finding.

## Not part of this

The START and END values read `2026-11-01` in Latin digits in Persian. That is the app's form convention (New Transaction's date row also shows the stored ISO string through `getFormatDate`), and the wheel it opens is localised by iOS. Not filed.

Searched `.pm/**` including `done/` for `LEADING_TEXT_ALIGN`, "leading edge", "physical left", and the filter sheet together with Persian and RTL. `w0/049` (the Payee caption), `w21/019` (the rule itself: UI text left at natural alignment sits on the physical left, with Settings, Spending and others) and `w21/037` (Invite Friends, a residual of `w21/019`) are the same class on other screens; `w21/927` is account-path direction in this sheet's Account row; `w22/011` is the Files row icon.
