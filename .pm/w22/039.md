# In Persian Home's Latest Updates rows and the Recent Transactions empty line sit on the physical left while the card titles and the neighbouring cards mirror

**Severity:** trivial to minor (fa). **Estimate:** 15–20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the same class as `w22/038`, `w0/049`, `w21/019` and `w21/037`; Home's Spending subtitle and Budget empty line were fixed and guarded, these three styles were not.

Environment: `main` at `b94152708` (`origin/main` was 2 commits ahead, none under `mobile/`), dev build `io.beancount.ios` on iPhone 17e / iOS 27.0, light, app language Persian after the layout restart, QA account on the hosted server. Two of its synthetic ledgers: one with no transactions, one with a few commits. Read-only: nothing was applied.

## Repro

1. Settings → Language → **فارسی** → Restart now.
2. Open the drawer and pick a ledger with no transactions. Home shows, top to bottom, the Net Worth card, **تراکنش‌های اخیر** (Recent Transactions), **هزینه‌ها** (Spending), **بودجه** (Budget) and **آخرین تغییرات** (Latest Updates).
3. In Recent Transactions the empty line **هنوز تراکنشی نیست** starts at the **left** (about 33pt in, ending near 140pt), while the card's own title is at the right edge (357pt) and the Add Transaction button spans the card. In the next card, the Spending lines (این ماه در برابر ماه گذشته, هنوز هزینه‌ای ثبت نشده) and the Budget line (برای یک حساب هدف تعیین کنید) all start at the right.
4. Scroll to Latest Updates. The title **آخرین تغییرات** is on the right, but every row beneath it is flush left: the translated first line (**تغییر در** and the ledger name), the commit message line, and the meta line (**دفتر · ۱۱ نوامبر ۲۰۲۶، ۲۰:۰۰**). Each row starts at 33pt and leaves the right half of the card empty, under a title that sits on the opposite side.

**Expected:** these lines follow the layout's leading edge, like the Spending and Budget lines one card above and below.
**Actual:** they sit at the physical left in Persian only. English is unaffected.

## Evidence

Local, gitignored captures under `.tmp/qa-20261112/loop62/`: `08-fa-feed.png` (the empty line at the left, the Spending and Budget lines at the right), `09-fa-feed-bottom.png` (the Latest Updates rows).

## Root cause

- `mobile/src/screens/home-screen/components/recent-transactions-card.tsx:41-44` (`emptyText`, also used for the upcoming-only variant) sets no `textAlign`.
- `mobile/src/screens/home-screen/components/feed-card.tsx:50-55` (`title`) and `:59-62` (`metaText`, used for both the subtitle and the meta line) set no `textAlign`.
- `mobile/src/common/rtl.ts:168` `LEADING_TEXT_ALIGN` is the documented answer for a `Text` wider than its content, and its comment adds that ledger data left at `natural` also lands on the physical left (a Latin commit message inside a Persian row). The guard `mobile/src/common/__tests__/leading-text-align.test.ts` lists `spending-card` `subtitle` and `budget-card` `emptyText`, but none of these three.

## Fix

Add `textAlign: LEADING_TEXT_ALIGN` to `emptyText`, `title` and `metaText`, and add the three blocks to `SURFACES` in that test; revert the fix and watch the new cases fail. English does not change.

## Blast radius

Home only. The Net Worth card's captions, the card titles and the Recent Transactions rows were checked in the same session and are already at the leading edge. The pointer in `w22/038` to `screens/merchants-screen/merchants-screen.tsx` was also checked natively here: its uppercase style is the date header, which is right-aligned in Persian, so it is not a finding.
