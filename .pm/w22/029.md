# After a live text-size change the Referral screen's title, blurb, link field and Copy and Share buttons are cut off

**Minor · mobile · 10–15m · A2.** Native mobile QA, 2026-11-10, repository HEAD `59f13b939`, development build `io.beancount.ios` 1.20261002.50 on iPhone 17e / iOS 27.0, light, English, signed in as the QA account. Read-only: the screen was opened and left with Back; **Copy and Share were never tapped**, so nothing reached the clipboard or the share sheet. The local captures contain the account-specific invite link and are not for quoting (the link is truncated on screen, so the code is not visible in them). This is the screen `w22/025` named as unchecked, and the same mechanism as `w22/017`–`028`.

## Repro (reproduced twice, the second from a fresh relaunch)

1. Terminate the app, `xcrun simctl ui <udid> content_size large`, relaunch, dismiss the dev toast.
2. Open the drawer, then **Settings** (`automation_tap` on testID `drawer-identity-row`; verify with a screenshot), then the **Invite Friends** block at the top of Settings.
3. With **Referral** open, `xcrun simctl ui <udid> content_size accessibility-large` (AX2, the supported ceiling in `.pm/DO_NOT_DO.md`). Take screenshots only.

**Expected:** the title, the two-line blurb, the link and the `Copy` and `Share` labels read whole and the blurb wraps, as they do from a cold AX2 launch.
**Actual:** the title `Invite Friends` shows `Invite I…` cut to its top; the blurb is **one line cut at the screen edge** (`Share this professional fi`) instead of wrapping; inside the link field `https://beanco…` and `Co` are cut to their tops; the `Share` label is a sliver in its button. The gift icon is fine.

**Control (cold):** terminate, set AX2 first, launch and open the same screen: `Invite Friends` is whole, the blurb wraps onto five lines, the link reads `https://bean…` beside a whole `Copy`, and `Share` is whole.

Evidence is local only (`.tmp/qa-20261110/w22e/`): `14-referral-live-ax2.png`, `19-referral-live-ax2-repeat.png` (and `-crop`) against `16-referral-cold-ax2.png`.

## Root cause

`mobile/src/screens/referral-screen/referral-screen.tsx` does not read `fontScale`. Its Texts are plain: the title (`:107`, `styles.title`), the blurb (`:109`, `styles.summary`), the link (`:113`, `styles.shareLink`, one line) and its `copy` label (`:128`), and the `share` label (`:159`). The sections under `components/` (`invite-section.tsx`, the gift icons) are not involved.

## Fix

Key those Texts on `fontScale`, a distinct key per sibling (`mobile/src/__tests__/font-scale-key-collisions.test.ts`), and add the file to the `KEYED_TEXTS` table in `mobile/src/__tests__/live-font-scale-remeasure.test.ts`; then verify default → AX2 → default natively against a cold AX2 launch, **without** tapping Copy or Share (use the screenshot only). Mobile only; no API surface.

## Blast radius

The one Referral screen. It shares the app's `Button` with Transaction Filters, Welcome, Server Settings, the Budget empty state and the OAuth error page; `w3/done/182` and `212` fixed that component's cold-size fit and its accessibility role, not live changes. Filed beside `w22/017`–`028`.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` (including `done/`) for `referral`, `fontScale`, live text size, AX2. `w3/done/207` (done) keeps the Referral actions reachable at a **cold** extra-large size, a different defect. `w22/025` listed this screen as unchecked.

## Unverified

The shrink direction here (observed on Settings, `w22/021`), dark mode, Android, and what the share sheet and the clipboard do at large sizes (not exercised on purpose).
