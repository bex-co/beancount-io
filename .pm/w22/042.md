# In Persian the Reports category rows print their share as Latin "100%" under Persian-digit amounts, and the chart change line ends in a Latin % sign

**Severity:** minor (fa; consistency). **Estimate:** 15–20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the same family as `w21/884` ("a language renders its own digits"), `w21/882`, `w21/933` and `w21/1017`, which moved the chart ticks, amounts and period pills to Persian digits; these two percent texts were not on their lists.

Environment: `main` at `ab76e5ea3` (`origin/main` one commit ahead, nothing under `mobile/`), dev build `io.beancount.ios` on iPhone 17e / iOS 27.0, light, large text, app language Persian after the layout restart, QA account on the hosted server, its synthetic `example` ledger. Read-only: nothing was applied.

## Repro

1. Settings → Language → **فارسی** → Restart now.
2. Reports tab (the 6M range is the default), scroll to the income and spending breakdown cards. Each row has the amount at the left in Persian digits (`$۹۳۲٫۰۲`) and, directly under it, the row's share of the total as **`100%`**: Latin digits and the Latin percent sign, in the same row as Persian-digit amounts.
3. Home tab, the Net Worth card. The change line reads **`+$۷۴۲٫۰۲ (+۰٫۶۳%)`**: the digits and the decimal mark are Persian, the sign is the Latin `%`.

**Expected:** `۱۰۰٪` and `(+۰٫۶۳٪)`. The app already writes Persian percents that way where it spells them out: `fa.ts` uses `٪` in `breakdownRowShareSpending`, `breakdownRowShareEarnings`, the budget row values and `receiptUploadingProgress`, and `w21/991` settled "Persian uses `٪` throughout".
**Actual:** the two texts that are drawn on screen are built in code, so they skip both the language's digits and its sign. English is unaffected.

## Evidence

Local, gitignored captures under `.tmp/qa-20261112/loop67/`: `08-fa-reports-scrolled.png` (the amount `$۹۳۲٫۰۲` in Persian digits over `100%` in Latin, twice), `05-fa-home.png` (the change line with the Latin `%`).

## Root cause

- `mobile/src/screens/reports-screen/components/category-breakdown.tsx:364` writes `` `${printed}%` ``, where `printed` is a bare number from `roundedShares` (`:226`); nothing localizes it.
- `mobile/src/common/d3/change-percent.ts:37` (`formatChangePercent`) returns `` `${sign}${groupThousands(rounded, 2)}%` ``. `groupThousands` goes through `localizeAmount` (`number-utils.ts:243`, `number-locale.ts`), so the digits and decimal mark follow the language; the trailing `%` is a literal.
- `number-locale.ts` keeps a hand-kept per-language table (`NUMBER_STYLES`) with `localDigits: true` for `fa`, but no percent sign.

## Fix

Write the share through `groupThousands(printed, 0)` and take the percent sign from the active language (`٪` for `fa`, `%` elsewhere: a `percent` field in `NUMBER_STYLES`, or the existing translation). Use the same helper in `formatChangePercent`. Add cases that no `fa` share or change text contains a Latin digit or `%`, and that English, German and the other languages read as before (`w21/991` keeps its no-break space for de, fr and sk in the translated strings; check the visible text agrees with it).

## Blast radius

The Reports breakdown rows (`pctLabel`) and every change row built by `formatChangePercent`: the interactive line chart's header and its accessibility text (`interactive-line-chart.tsx:470`, `chart-accessibility.ts:74`). Home was seen; the account-detail and Reports charts use the same function and were not opened here.

## Not part of this

The period pills' Latin digits were fixed by `w21/1017`, and the account path `Home:Internet` in the breakdown row is ledger data in Latin, so both stay as they are. The `,` / `٫` marks that look alike in the amounts are the language's own marks (`number-locale.ts`), not a finding.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` including `done/` for `pctLabel`, `category-breakdown`, "percent" with Persian, digit, Latin and `٪`. `w21/884` (chart ticks and dates mixing digits), `w21/1017` (the pills; its text quotes the change line with the Latin `%` and does not file it), `w21/991` (the percent sign convention per language, translated strings only: it did not touch the two hard-coded ones) and `w21/882` / `w21/933` (amounts and compact ticks) are the same family; none names the share label or the change line's sign.
