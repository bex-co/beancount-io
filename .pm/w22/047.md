# In Persian the valuation sheet's title sits against the Done button at the left and every holding sits beside its price date, leaving the right half of the header and of each row empty

**Severity:** minor (fa); the most visible of the leading-edge class, because the title and the Done action touch. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the seventh note of the class (`w22/038`–`041`, `w22/045`, `w22/046`; `w0/049`, `w21/019`, `w21/037`); batch the fix with them.

Environment: `main` at `47cd55c42` (`origin/main` has nothing under `mobile/` ahead), dev build `io.beancount.ios` on iPhone 17e / iOS 27.0, light, large text, app language Persian after the layout restart, QA account on the hosted server, its synthetic `example` ledger. Read-only: nothing was applied.

## Repro

1. Settings → Language → **فارسی** → Restart now.
2. Home → tap the Net Worth caption (`به ارزش بازار · ۶ قیمت از ۸ سپتامبر ۲۰۱۷ به‌روز نشده · ۱۰ خارج از جمع`). The sheet **دارایی‌ها و قیمت‌ها** opens.
3. The title and the green **تمام** (Done) are both at the **left**: the title (about 42–180pt) directly touches **تمام** (about 17–42pt), and the right half of the header is empty. In English the title is at the left and Done at the right (see the control below).
4. Each holding row (`۱۹۳٫۴۴۲ VBMPX`, `۳۶ VEA`, …) occupies about 140–255pt, right next to its price-date block (`قیمت ۸ سپتامبر ۲۰۱۷` / `به‌روز نشده ⚠`, at the left edge), instead of starting at the right edge. The `-۸ VACHR` row and its `خارج از جمع` label do the same.

**Expected:** the title and the holdings start at the leading edge (right), with Done and the price block at the trailing edge, like the mirrored row structure around them.
**Actual:** the row structure mirrors (Done and the price block are at the left) but the two `flex: 1` texts are left-aligned inside their boxes, so they pile up against them.

## Evidence

Local, gitignored captures under `.tmp/qa-20261112/loop75/`: `05-fa-valuation-sheet.png` (the Persian sheet) and `11-en-valuation-sheet.png` (the English control: title and holdings at the left, Done and the price block at the right).

## Root cause

- `mobile/src/components/valuation-sheet/index.tsx:51-56` (`title`) and `:70-74` (`holding`) are `flex: 1` `Text` with no `textAlign`, so under RTL they align to the physical left inside the mirrored row.
- The file already imports `LEADING_TEXT_ALIGN` (`:27`) and uses it only for `dateStacked` (`:94`). `date` (`:96-100`) sets `textAlign: "right"`, which React Native swaps to the trailing (left) edge on purpose.
- The guard `mobile/src/common/__tests__/leading-text-align.test.ts` lists none of it.

## Fix

Add `textAlign: LEADING_TEXT_ALIGN` to `title` and `holding` and add the two blocks to `SURFACES` in the guard; revert the fix and watch the new cases fail. English does not change.

## Blast radius

Every place the valuation caption opens this sheet (`openValuation`: Home, the Accounts roots and rows, the account detail). Only Home was opened here.
