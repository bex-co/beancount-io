# In Persian the transaction detail's section titles and the Entry Context card's headings sit on the physical left, over rows that mirror

**Severity:** trivial to minor (fa). **Estimate:** 15–20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the same class as `w22/038`, `w22/039`, `w22/040`, `w0/049`, `w21/019` and `w21/037`. The Entry Context card is also rendered by Edit Transaction, so that part is shared.

Environment: `main` at `2abe1e562` (`origin/main` identical, nothing under `mobile/` ahead), dev build `io.beancount.ios` on iPhone 17e / iOS 27.0, light, app language Persian after the layout restart, QA account on the hosted server, its synthetic `example` ledger. Read-only: nothing was applied.

## Repro

1. Settings → Language → **فارسی** → Restart now.
2. Transactions tab → open the first transaction (a plain two-posting entry).
3. The section title **جریان پول** (Money flow) starts at the **left** edge, about 16pt in. The card under it is mirrored: the account names at the right with the به / از captions, the amounts and chevrons at the left.
4. The **زمینه ورودی** (Entry Context) card: its `</>` icon is at the right edge (about 347pt), but the header text starts at about 65pt, next to the chevron at the far left, so the title sits at the opposite end from the icon it labels.
5. Tap the card header to open it. Its two table headers, **موجودی قبل از ورودی** and **موجودی بعد از ورودی**, start at the left (about 42pt in), while the rows under them put the account names at the right and the amounts at the left.

**Control, same screen:** the account names in those balance rows and in Money flow are at the right, at the leading edge. English is unaffected.

**Expected:** every heading starts at the leading edge, like the rows it heads.
**Actual:** these headings are at the physical left in Persian only.

## Evidence

Local, gitignored captures under `.tmp/qa-20261112/loop64/`: `04-fa-detail.png` (the Money flow title at the left, the Entry Context header away from its icon), `05-fa-context-open.png` (the two balance headers at the left above right-aligned account names).

## Root cause

All four styles are full-width `Text` with no `textAlign`, in files that already use `LEADING_TEXT_ALIGN` for their neighbours:

- `mobile/src/screens/transaction-detail-screen/transaction-detail-screen.tsx:212-218` (`sectionTitle`, used at `:961` for Money flow and at `:990` for Details); the same file's `errorText`, `errorAction` and `copyNoticeText` (`:232-257`) do take it.
- `mobile/src/screens/transactions-screen/entry-context/index.tsx:100-104` (`contextHeaderText`) and `:118-123` (`sectionTitle`, the Source title at `:327`).
- `mobile/src/screens/transactions-screen/entry-context/balance-section.tsx:26-30` (`balanceSectionHeaderText`); `balanceAccount` in the same file (`:52-58`) takes it.
- The guard `mobile/src/common/__tests__/leading-text-align.test.ts` lists none of them.

## Fix

Add `textAlign: LEADING_TEXT_ALIGN` to those four styles and add the four blocks to `SURFACES` in the guard; revert the fix and watch the new cases fail. English does not change.

## Blast radius

Transaction detail, and the Entry Context card inside both transaction detail and Edit Transaction (`edit-transaction-screen.tsx` renders the same component). The Details title (`:990`, shown only when the entry has tags, links or metadata) and the Source title were not on screen for this entry, so they are read from the source, not observed.
