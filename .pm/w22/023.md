# After a live text-size change the account detail's path, Balance card figures, "Transactions" title and entry amounts are cut off

**Minor · mobile · 30–40m · A2.** Native mobile QA, 2026-11-10, repository HEAD `59f13b939`, development build `io.beancount.ios` 1.20261002.50 on iPhone 17e / iOS 27.0, light, English, signed in as the QA account on its synthetic `example` ledger. Read-only pass; nothing was written. This is the same mechanism as `w22/017`–`022`, on another screen.

## Repro (reproduced twice, the second from a fresh relaunch)

1. Terminate the app, `xcrun simctl ui <udid> content_size large`, relaunch, dismiss the dev toast.
2. **Accounts** tab → tap an account's name (not its chevron) → its detail screen opens.
3. With it open, `xcrun simctl ui <udid> content_size accessibility-large` (AX2, the supported ceiling in `.pm/DO_NOT_DO.md`).

**Expected:** the full account path, the Balance card with its label, total and period change, the `Transactions` title, and each entry's amount and running balance read whole, as they do from a cold AX2 launch.
**Actual:** the path shows `Assets:Bank:Mercury:Chec…` cut at its top (it does not wrap); inside the Balance card `Balance` is a sliver and the total `$6,161.08` and the change `+$6,161.08` lose their lower edge and their right part (`$6,161`); `Transactions` is a sliver; in the entry row the amount reads `+$12.` and `Balance: $6,16` is cut. The day header with its total and the entry's payee and note re-lay out correctly.

**Control (cold):** terminate, set AX2 first, launch and open the same account: the path wraps onto two lines, `Balance`, `$6,161.08` and `+$6,161.08` are whole, `Transactions` is whole and the entry row grows.

Evidence is local only (`.tmp/qa-20261110/w22c/`): `03-account-detail-live-ax2.png`, `06-account-detail-live-ax2-repeat.png` (and `-crop`) against `04-account-detail-cold-ax2.png`.

## Root cause

`mobile/src/screens/account-detail-screen/account-detail-screen.tsx` does not read `fontScale`; its list header (`listHeader`, `:556-600`) holds plain Texts: `accountPath` (`:559`) and `sectionTitle` (`:593`), around `BalanceChartCard` (`mobile/src/components/balance-chart-card/index.tsx`, which does not read it either; its own Text is `captionText` at `:152`, and the label, total and change are rendered by the card's body). In `components/account-entry-row.tsx` only the layout switch reads the scale (`:142-143`); the amount and balance lines (`AmountText`, `:279-306`) and the `title` (`:250`) are not keyed. Home's `account-charts-card.tsx` already scopes its measurements on `fontScale` (`:159-160`, a keyed caption at `:206-207`), which is the pattern to follow.

## Fix

Key the header Texts, the Balance card's label, total and change, and the entry row's amount and balance `AmountText`s on `fontScale`, a distinct key per sibling (`mobile/src/__tests__/font-scale-key-collisions.test.ts`). Key only those, never the list, the chart or the card, so scroll position, the selected period and the chart scrub survive. Add the files to the `KEYED_TEXTS` table in `mobile/src/__tests__/live-font-scale-remeasure.test.ts` (the entry row needs a rendered case if its `AmountText` is not a plain `Text`), then verify default → AX2 → default natively against a cold AX2 launch. Mobile only; no API surface.

## Blast radius

The one account detail screen, for every account. `BalanceChartCard` is shared: its other caller is the screen above (searched `src`), so keying it affects no other screen. Filed beside `w22/017`–`022`, `024`–`029`, `030`.
