# After a live text-size change the Budget cards' chips, Budget / Actual / Variance labels and amounts, and "N entries" row are cut off

**Minor · mobile · 30–40m · A2.** Native mobile QA, 2026-11-10, repository HEAD `59f13b939`, development build `io.beancount.ios` 1.20261002.50 on iPhone 17e / iOS 27.0, light, English, signed in as the QA account on its synthetic `example` ledger, which carries two Monthly 122 USD budgets. Read-only pass; the red delete icons in the entries list were not touched.

## Repro (reproduced twice, the second from a fresh relaunch)

1. Terminate the app, `xcrun simctl ui <udid> content_size large`, relaunch, dismiss the dev toast.
2. Home → scroll the Budget card into view (`automation_tap` on testID `spending-card`) → the Budget card's **See all**. The Budget screen opens on YTD.
3. With it open, `xcrun simctl ui <udid> content_size accessibility-large` (AX2, the supported ceiling in `.pm/DO_NOT_DO.md`).

**Expected:** every card shows its chips (`Monthly`, `USD`, `Below target`), the three stats with their labels, and the `N entries` row whole.
**Actual:** the chip row keeps its old height and shows `Mon`, `US`, `Below` cut at the top; `Budget`, `Actual` and `Variance` show only their upper halves and the amounts under them lose their lower edge; `2 entries` is a sliver. The account title and `Update Budget` re-measure (they are keyed or restructure); the chart legend does too, since `w22/008`.

**Control (cold):** terminate, set AX2 first, launch and open the same screen: the chips wrap onto two lines, all labels and amounts are whole, and `2 entries` has its full row.

Evidence is local only (`.tmp/qa-20261110/w22b/`): `14-budget-live-ax2.png`, `18-budget-live-ax2-repeat.png` (and `-crop`) against `16-budget-cold-ax2.png`.

## Root cause

Only the account title is keyed on the scale (`mobile/src/screens/budget-screen/components/budget-group-card.tsx:288`, `w21/027`). The rest are plain Texts: the chips `badgeText` (`:316`, `:321`) and `statusText` (`:326`, `:335`), the stat labels `statLabel` (`:345`, `:352`, `:365`) and their `AmountText` values (`:346-377`), and in `budget-history-rows.tsx` the toggle (`:174`), the entry date (`:190`) and its interval (`:193`).

## Fix

Key those Texts and `AmountText`s on `fontScale`, a distinct key per sibling (`font-scale-key-collisions.test.ts`), as `DateSectionHeader` does; key only Texts, never the list or the chart, so the expanded entries and scroll position survive. Add the file to the `KEYED_TEXTS` table in `mobile/src/__tests__/live-font-scale-remeasure.test.ts`, then verify default → AX2 → default natively against a cold AX2 launch. Mobile only.

## Blast radius

Every Budget card (one per budget and currency). Home's budget panel was keyed by `w21/015`. Filed beside `w22/017`, `019`–`030` as separate sites of the same mechanism.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` including `done/` for `budget-group-card`, `badgeText`, `statLabel`, "Variance", `fontScale`, live text size. `w21/027` (done) is the cold-AX2 title ellipsis, fixed by keying only the title; `w21/015` is Home; `w22/008` fixed the legend. Nothing covers the chips, stats or entries row.

## Unverified

A card with a "Starts on" or "Not in total" note, the Update Budget form (Add Budget is `w22/024`), the shrink direction here (observed on Settings, `w22/021`), dark mode, Android.
