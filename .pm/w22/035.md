# In Russian at AX2 the Home "+" menu breaks "Сканировать чек" mid-word and leaves a lone "ь" on its own line

**Minor · mobile · 15–20m · A2.** Native mobile QA, 2026-11-10, repository HEAD `59f13b939`, development build `io.beancount.ios` 1.20261002.50 on iPhone 17e / iOS 27.0, light, Russian, signed in as the QA account on its synthetic `example` ledger. Read-only: the menu was opened and dismissed without choosing a row (its second row is the camera; no photo was taken). The app language was restored to English afterwards. The third finding of the language × text-size pass, after `w22/033` and `w22/034`, on a third component.

## Repro (reproduced twice, the second from a cold launch at AX2)

1. Settings → **Language** → **Русский** → Confirm (left to right, so no restart prompt).
2. `xcrun simctl terminate <udid> io.beancount.ios`, `xcrun simctl ui <udid> content_size accessibility-large` (AX2, the supported ceiling in `.pm/DO_NOT_DO.md`), relaunch, dismiss the dev toast.
3. On **Home**, tap the `+` at the top right (screenshot it before tapping a row).

**Expected:** the two menu rows read `Записать новую транзакцию` and `Сканировать чек`, wrapping at word boundaries.
**Actual:** the first row wraps correctly (`Записать` / `новую` / `транзакцию`). The second reads `Сканироват` on one line and `ь чек` on the next: the word `Сканировать` is wider than the popover's text area, so iOS breaks it by character and the soft sign `ь` is left in front of `чек`.

**Control:** German at AX2 (`Neue Transaktion erfassen`, `Beleg scannen`) and English at AX2 (`Scan Receipt`) wrap at word boundaries; at `large` the Russian menu is a normal two-line popover. The Latin 11-letter words (`transaction`, `Transaktion`) are narrower than the Cyrillic 11-letter `Сканировать`.

Evidence is local only (`.tmp/qa-20261110/w22i/`): `28-menu-ru-ax2.png`, `29-menu-ru-ax2-repeat.png` (and `-crop`).

## Root cause

The menu is the app's own `MenuButton` (`mobile/src/components/menu-button/index.tsx`), not a system menu. Its popover is a fixed width, measured when it opens: `const menuWidth = Math.min(title ? 300 : 260, screen.width - 32)` (`:133`), so 260 pt on this device whatever the text size. Each row's label is a plain `<Text style={styles.itemLabel}>{item.label}</Text>` (`:228`) beside the row's icon, with no `numberOfLines` and no fit rule. At AX2 the label font is about 2.1 times larger, the text area (260 pt less the row's padding and the 22 pt icon) is narrower than the one word `Сканировать`, and it is broken by character. The Russian string is `scanReceipt: "Сканировать чек"` (`mobile/src/translations/ru.ts:555`); the rows come from `home-screen.tsx:233-258`.

## Fix

Let the popover grow with the text instead of staying 260 pt: scale `menuWidth` by the font scale (`useWindowDimensions().fontScale`, capped at `screen.width - 32`); at AX2 that is the full width minus the 16 pt margins on a phone. A narrower fix is a `numberOfLines`/`adjustsFontSizeToFit` rule on `itemLabel`, but a menu row should wrap rather than shrink. Add a unit case that, for the AX2 scale and the longest menu word in each locale, the computed width is not smaller than the word's width (or that the width follows the scale), and verify natively in Russian at AX2. The other menu in the app that uses the component with a `title` (the 300 pt variant) should be checked in the same pass. Mobile only; no API surface.

## Blast radius

Every `MenuButton`: Home's `+` (`home-screen.tsx:231`), the header actions of Accounts and Transactions that go through `ledger-drawer-header.tsx:205`, the transaction detail's `…` menu (`transaction-detail-screen.tsx:800`) and the drawer's ledger row menu (`ledger-drawer.tsx:986`), per `w21/done/558`'s list of the same menus. By word length only Russian is expected to break on these two rows (the longest word in every other language is at most 11 Latin or 10 Cyrillic letters, and `транзакцию` fitted), so Ukrainian and Bulgarian are lower risk (Ukrainian was run at AX2 and wraps cleanly: `Сканувати` has 9 letters against Russian's 11, `.tmp/qa-20261110/w22j/03-menu-uk-ax2.png`; Bulgarian was not run); the labels of the detail `…` menu and the drawer row menu were not enumerated.

## Adjacent states

A row with a long unbroken word in any language, the titled 300 pt variant, and a screen narrower than the phone tested (the popover is already capped at `screen.width - 32`).

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` (including `done/`) for `menu-button`, `MenuButton`, `quickAdd`, `scanReceipt`, mid-word, Russian and AX2. `w21/done/558` named the rows for assistive technology, `w4/done/020` (testIDs) and `w3/done/137` (Share menu closing) are other defects of the same component. Nothing covers its row text at a large size or in a long language.

## Unverified

Bulgarian at AX2 (Ukrainian was run and wraps cleanly: `Сканувати` has 9 letters against Russian's 11, `.tmp/qa-20261110/w22j/03-menu-uk-ax2.png`), the other menus built from `MenuButton`, dark mode, Android.
