# Inventory lots and cost of goods sold for product sellers

The small-business review (item 6, triaged 2026-10-21) asked for inventory bought into lots at cost, with a sale reducing the lot and booking cost of goods sold, reusing `beancount-trade`'s lot machinery. The payout half of that item is [w2/m58](../m58/README.md).

**Blocked 2026-10-21** — demand is unvalidated: the reviewer runs a services business, and [PRFAQ001](../../../docs/prfaqs/PRFAQ001-ledger-creation.md) defers inventory "according to validated demand". **Unblock:** a product seller (or user research) asks for it and names the volume and costing method they need. **Who can clear it:** the owner (demand decision).
