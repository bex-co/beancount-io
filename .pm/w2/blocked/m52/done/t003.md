## Objective

Extend trade's own-account transfer with an optional network-fee leg paid in the transferred commodity: units out = units in + fee units, fee disposed from the same lot at its cost (gain booking-produced at a stated value or expensed at cost, as the user states), basis and date preserved on the moved units.

## Context

- Eval 7 of m45 covers the feeless transfer; real on-chain moves almost always burn a fee in-kind, which today forces users to hand-split the entry.

## Steps

1. Derive the shape on the m45 crypto fixture pattern (partial lot + fee) with `bea check`, deciding the fee lot treatment from what the engine books.
2. Write the procedure with the fee's disposition shown in the proposal.

## Files

- `skills/beancount-trade/SKILL.md`

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Moved units keep original cost/date, the fee units leave the same lot with their disposition explicit, and total units reconcile exactly.

## Out of scope

- Exchange withdrawal fees charged in a third currency (state the cash-fee shape instead).

## Result

Own-wallet transfers can now carry an in-kind network fee: fee units leave the same lot in the same transaction (units out = units in + fee, reconciled exactly in the proposal), disposed either at a user-stated value with the gains leg inferred or — when no value is stated — expensed at lot cost with that choice said out loud. Both shapes verified against `bea check` on a two-transfer fixture (wallet 0.300000 BTC, exchange 0.199000, one 10.00 booking-produced fee gain on the priced variant). The fee is never folded into the moved units' cost or hidden by shrinking arrivals; third-currency fees stay ordinary cash legs. `ci-check.py` and the skills suite (122) pass; eval coverage lands with t004.
