## Objective

Record a supplier bill as a payable against its expense, and each payment that clears it, with the same identity and confirmation rules as invoices.

## Context

- Payable account and bill identity from w2/m55/t001.

## Steps

1. Write the bill and bill-payment procedures, including a partial payment.

## Files

- The new skill's `SKILL.md` and references

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Bill 500 then pay 500 leaves zero payable on that bill; a partial payment leaves the remainder open.

## Out of scope

- Approval workflows and purchase orders.

## Result

`beancount-invoices` now records supplier bills: `Liabilities:AccountsPayable` with `bill: "<supplier>:<number>"` identity (a supplier's number is unique only for that supplier), `due:` when stated, `^bill-<supplier>-<number>` links, the receiving and paying procedures (a part payment leaves the rest owed and says so; unmatched or excess payments are asked about), and the `bill-open` recipe. Verified by `test_bill_open_balances_follow_partial_payments`: a 450 bill paid 200 leaves -250.00 owed, and another supplier's bill with the same number stays separate (fails when the recipe reads the wrong key). Full skills suite: 157 tests pass.
