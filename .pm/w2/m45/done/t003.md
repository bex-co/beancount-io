## Objective

Give the agent a tested, read-only recipe that lists every lot closed in a year. Each row shows acquisition date, sale date, holding period (one year or less, or more), quantity, proceeds, cost, and gain. The total must tie to that year's gain postings. Every figure comes from a `bea --json query` statement shown with the answer, never from model arithmetic.

## Context

- `beancount-ask` already holds the read-only query style and its no-estimation rule (`skills/beancount-ask/SKILL.md`).
- Reductions carry the lot's cost and date in the posting, so BQL can read acquisition date and cost from the reducing postings. Verify which columns `bea --json query` exposes for this before writing the recipe.
- The holding-period split is the one-year boundary only. Jurisdiction rules stay out of scope.

## Steps

1. Write the query, or queries, and the presentation, as a `<!-- recipe -->` block so `test_beancount_skills.py` can execute it.
2. Show the independent tie-out: the sum of the per-lot gains equals the balance change of the gain account for the year.
3. Say what the report does not cover: open lots, unrealized gains, and positions without prices.

## Files

- The chosen skill's `references/` (for example `realized-gains.md`), and `skills/beancount-ask/SKILL.md` if ask hands off to it

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] On a synthetic ledger with partial sells across two lots and a transfer, the recipe's rows and total match a hand-checked expectation, and the tie-out holds.
- [x] The recipe is read-only and states its exclusions.

## Out of scope

- Tax forms, wash sales, and FX gains.

## Result

Shipped `skills/beancount-trade/references/realized-gains.md` with three read-only recipes: per-lot rows (acquired, sold, held days, quantity, proceeds, cost, gain — all engine expressions over the reducing postings, sales separated from transfers/splits by the recorded price), holding-bucket totals (one year or less vs more, per cost currency, never summed across currencies), and an independent booked-gains tie-out query whose disagreement is reported, never forced. Exclusions stated: open lots, unrealized gains, unpriced positions, wash sales, FX, tax rules. Columns were verified against `bea 0.3.1` first (`price`, `cost_number`, `date - cost_date`; `@@` totals normalize per-unit so one proceeds formula covers both). The recipe test builds partial sells across three lots plus a transfer and asserts hand-checked rows, both bucket subtotals, and the 390.00 booked tie-out, read-only. Full gates in the ship run.
