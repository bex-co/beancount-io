# In Persian, table column headings sit on the physical left while their values start on the right

**Minor · dashboard · 20–30 minutes.** A2: a Persian reader (`dir="rtl"`) should find a column's heading above its values. The shared `TableHead` primitive hard-codes `text-left`, so in Persian every heading without its own override sits at the physical left of its column, while the cells, which inherit `start`, begin at the right. This is the table counterpart of [w2/132](./done/132.md) (dialogs, fixed `4e4badcd8`) and of mobile's [w22/045](../w22/045.md).

Production https://beancount.io, 2026-11-12, `main-CuYafm8C.js` (includes w2/132), signed out, `?lang=fa`, isolated headless Chromium at 1440×1000, public `open_ledger/example`. Read only. Local main `bd4d4373e`.

## Reproduce

Measured as the space between the heading's text, or the first row's text, and its column's left and right edges:

| Page | Column | Heading `text-align`, left / right gap | First cell `text-align`, left / right gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Events | تاریخ (Date) | `left`, 12 / 184 px | `start`, 127 / 12 px |
| Events | نوع (Type) | `left`, 12 / 165 px | `start`, 113 / 12 px |
| Events | توضیحات (Description) | `left`, 12 / 650 px | `start`, 380 / 12 px |
| Journal | دریافت‌کننده/شرح (Payee/Narration) | `left`, 8 / 732 px | `start` (payee and narration drawn from the right) |
| Account `Assets:US:BofA:Checking` → period table | دوره (Period) | `left`, 8 / 498 px | `start`, 450 / 8 px |

In each row the heading is at one edge and the values at the other. Columns that set their own logical alignment (`text-end` amounts, the centred Date and Flag in Journal) are fine. `chart-period-table.tsx:74,118` sets `text-right` on both its amount heading and cells, so they agree with each other but don't mirror.

## Root cause

- `dashboard/src/common/components/ui/table.tsx:71` (`TableHead`): `… h-10 px-2 text-left align-middle …`. 24 feature files render `TableHead`, and only a handful override the alignment with a logical class.
- `features/reports/components/chart-period-table.tsx:74,118`: `text-right` on the amount column.

## Fix

- `text-left` → `text-start` in `TableHead` (and `[&:has([role=checkbox])]:pr-0` → `pe-0`), and `text-right` → `text-end` in `chart-period-table.tsx`. Then grep for remaining `text-left`/`text-right` on table headings and cells.
- Add a render test under `dir="rtl"`, like `dialog-rtl.test.tsx` from w2/132.

**Acceptance:** in `fa`, every heading on Journal, Events, the account page and the statements' period tables sits on the same side as its column's values. Nothing moves in English.
