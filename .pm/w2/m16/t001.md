## Objective

Map parsing, suggestion, asset, and AI verbs to stable canonical actions and explicit composite relationship/credential requirements in the one executable catalog.

## Context

- Receipt/AI operations often read one resource and write another.
- Endpoint-specific FGA relations would duplicate business workflows in the model.

## Steps

1. Inventory all GraphQL/REST/MCP aliases and define canonical actions plus trusted resource shapes.
2. Express AND/OR relationship requirements without endpoint relations; keep denial/concealment and audit class beside each action.
3. Preserve credential method, legacy scope, ledger pin, operational rate class, and explicit budget overrides without minting the future fine-grained grant vocabulary.
