## Cause and bounded repair

`mobile/src/screens/transactions-screen/entry-row/index.tsx:79–84` gives **secondary**13-point text `theme.black60`; the narration renders at183–192 through `entryRowSecondaryText`. Rows deliberately have no background at39–47. Transactions and merchant journals inherit `theme.white`; Home/Reports DashboardCards paint `theme.black10`. This is informative text in an actionable row, not a disabled control. The same detail's heroSubtitle uses black80 and passes its native light control.

Actual pure palette/contrastRatio measurements, without antialiased-pixel sampling:

| Foreground / surface | Light | Dark | Existing black80 alternative, light / dark |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| black60 / white | **1.7834:1** | **6.2916:1** | **5.2229 /7.3557** |
| black60 / black10 | **1.6195:1** | **5.2059:1** | **4.7429 /6.0864** |

Light black60 is `#c2c3b6`, white `#ffffff`, black10 `#f6f4ec`; dark counterparts are `#9a9c8d`, `#171a14`, `#282a21`. These13-point narrations fail the4.5:1 small-text target used by the app's contrast helpers and [W3C guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html).

Change this **one shared local text role** to an existing readable secondary color, such as black80, checking both actual surfaces and themes. Preserve hierarchy,13-point sizing, inherited card backgrounds, the helper's payee/narration equality rules, default one-line ellipsis, enlarged wrapping, signed/mixed amounts, row accessibility names and actual navigation. Do not globally retune the neutral ramp, remove narrations, disable text scaling, add a dependency/helper, or change any query/API.

Acceptance: repeat Example Home→Transactions→actual Cafe detail at default/max and fresh light/dark; the same narration is readable directly in each row and unchanged in detail.
