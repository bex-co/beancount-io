## Objective

Procedure for the recurring payment: principal, interest, and escrow legs taken from the loan statement's printed figures (pasted text or described), principal reducing the liability, escrow in/out (tax/insurance disbursements from the escrow account), with a tie-out to the statement's new balance before proposing.

## Context

- The principal figure is read from the statement, never computed from an amortization schedule by the agent; if the statement lacks the split, the skill asks rather than deriving it.
- Reconcile's statement-arithmetic gate is the pattern: opening balance - principal = printed new balance, or stop.

## Steps

1. Build a synthetic 3-month statement sequence; derive entries and the balance tie-out with `bea check` and balance queries.
2. Write the procedure, including escrow disbursement entries and the missing-split stop.

## Files

- Owning skill text/references

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Three sequential payments leave the liability at the statement's month-3 balance to the cent; a statement without a printed split produces a question, not an entry.

## Out of scope

- HELOCs and adjustable-rate recasts beyond statement-read figures.

## Result

Monthly mortgage payments added to `beancount-property`: the split is read from the loan statement's printed figures only (never amortized), gated twice before proposing — split sums to the total paid, and ledger mortgage balance + printed principal equals the statement's printed new balance — with escrow disbursements as their own escrow-down/expense-up entries tied to a printed escrow balance when given.
