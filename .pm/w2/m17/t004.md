## Objective

Centralize authorization for entry changes, receipt promotion, bulk/file edits, and pull-request review workflows.

## Context

- These workflows coordinate multiple side effects and may require content plus asset permissions.

## Steps

1. Put one complete composite action at the workflow boundary before multi-service execution; simple single-domain methods stay service PEPs.
2. Pass resolved `Identity` explicitly and thread trusted domain handles only within the operation, never an authorization/request wrapper or cached decision.
3. Remove per-surface policy while preserving validation, atomic owner predicates, transaction/cleanup ordering, and client-visible errors.

## Files

- ledger entry/receipt workflows
- pull-request features
- related resolver/tool adapters

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Missing any required capability denies before the first side effect.
- [ ] Receipt cleanup and commit transactional behavior is unchanged.
- [ ] Approval/rejection authority is explicit and surface-independent.
- [ ] A direct protected service/workflow call cannot bypass the PDP and uses canonical-action audit fallback.

## Out of scope

- Changing accounting or review semantics.
