## Objective

Write the decision record every business procedure in m55–m59 follows, so account names, identities, and boundaries are decided once instead of per skill.

## Context

- Catalog presets in `backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/features/ledger/setup/catalog.json`: `freelance@1` (`Income:Services`, `Equity:OwnerContributions`, `Equity:OwnerDrawings`; its invoices question adds `Assets:AccountsReceivable`) and `business@1` (`Equity:Capital`, `Equity:RetainedEarnings`; its invoices-bills question adds `Assets:AccountsReceivable` and `Liabilities:AccountsPayable`). Shipped preset versions change only through a new version.
- Neither preset opens payroll or collected-tax liability accounts ([w2/097](../097.md)).
- Shared suite conventions in `skills/AGENTS.md`; [ADR020](../../../docs/adrs/ADR020-skills-accounting-workflow-candidates.md)'s test for a new skill; [PRFAQ001](../../../docs/prfaqs/PRFAQ001-ledger-creation.md) defers payroll calculation and tax determination.
- Links already group related entries (`beancount-options` gives each trade a `^link`).
- Dimension options: metadata is single-valued and already queried with `entry_meta()` (`skills/beancount-import/references/bea-import.md`); tags are multi-valued, so an entry tagged with two clients counts twice in a per-client total.

## Steps

1. Inventory the names already used by the catalog, the init template, and every customer skill.
2. Decide and record, each with the rejected alternative and the reason: receivable and payable account names; invoice and bill identity (the `^link` scheme); the client/project key (metadata or tags, transaction or posting level, split expenses); collected-tax and payroll-liability account shapes; owner contributions and draws; which procedures are new skills and which extend existing ones, by ADR020's test.
3. Record the non-goals — sending invoices, calculating payroll or withholdings, tax determination or advice, inventory until [w2/099](../blocked/099.md) clears — and the `DO_NOT_DO.md` lines proposed for the owner.
4. List any catalog change the names require as input to a new preset version ([w2/097](../097.md)).

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Every convention states its choice, the rejected alternative, and the reason.

- [x] Non-goals and proposed `DO_NOT_DO.md` lines are recorded; `.pm/DO_NOT_DO.md` changes only if the owner asks.

## Out of scope

- Implementing any procedure.
- Editing the catalog.

## Result

[ADR025](../../../../docs/adrs/ADR025-skills-business-books.md) (Proposed, 2026-10-26) records the names (catalog-aligned receivables and payables; new `Liabilities:SalesTax`, `Liabilities:Payroll:<Item>`, `Assets:Clearing:<Processor>`), document identity (`invoice:`/`bill:` metadata read with `any_meta()`, transaction-level for one document and per posting when a payment spans several, plus a `^link` for navigation), the `client:`/`project:` keys, the figures-from-documents rule, proposed placement per milestone, and the non-goals with two proposed `DO_NOT_DO.md` lines left for the owner. Each convention was checked on a scratch ledger with the checkout's `bea` 0.3.1 (HEAD `ab72f5c36`): per-invoice open balances tied to the receivable balance, including one payout across two invoices; per-project totals with a split posting tied to the account totals; the native recipe wrote a transaction-level invoice identity. The ADR index and `skills/AGENTS.md` shared conventions link it.
