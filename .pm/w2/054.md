## Cause and bounded repair

`mobile/src/screens/transaction-detail-screen/transaction-detail-screen.tsx:644–657` detects the route/selection mismatch and unmounts TransactionDetailImpl in favor of RouteLedgerUnavailable. The old body had installed a `headerRight` renderer at467–509, including MenuButton and captured Share/Copy callbacks at383–394. `src/components/route-ledger-unavailable.tsx:54` updates only `{title}`; it does not clear that renderer or its callbacks.

Installed Expo Router57.0.4 `build/views/Screen.js:23–30` forwards options to `navigation.setOptions`, without an option-reset cleanup on this child replacement. Its installed React Navigation implementation at `build/react-navigation/core/useNavigationCache.js:147–151` shallow-merges new options into the existing route-keyed options. Updating title therefore leaves the prior headerRight in place. The surviving Share callback still uses Example's captured entry URL, matching the native share-sheet identity; `src/common/app-links/ledger-url-actions.ts:4–6` presents the local iOS sheet. This is an owned screen-options lifetime gap, not a framework defect requiring a dependency patch.
