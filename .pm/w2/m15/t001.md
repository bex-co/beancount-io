## Objective

Partition the social inventory into canonical protected actions and explicit public exclusions, then define each protected action's trusted resource, credential, relationship, audit, denial, and operational-risk behavior.

## Context

- Social data lives primarily in Gitea and must not be copied to an authz store.

## Steps

1. Inventory feed/profile/follow/star aliases and classify each exactly once as protected or deliberately public.
2. Put protected requirements in the one TypeScript catalog; keep transport aliases and operational classes in `op-class.ts` without using those classes as credential policy.
3. Specify stable-ID resource derivation and composite checks such as self preference plus readable ledger; update `.fga` only for real durable relationship changes.

## Files

- `backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/server/api/authorization/**`
- `backend-cluster/backend-v2/authz/**`
- `backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/server/api/op-class.ts`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every social verb has one protected action/resource shape or a written public-exclusion reason.
- [ ] Usernames and caller arguments never substitute for stable authenticated IDs.
- [ ] Public behavior is explicit rather than inferred from missing identity.
- [ ] Credential reachability, audit class, denial behavior, and rate budget are reviewable without a second policy file.

## Out of scope

- Changing social product semantics or modeling follow/star/request facts as credential or contextual tuples.
