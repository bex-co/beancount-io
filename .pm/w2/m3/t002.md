## Objective

Write per-source reference files for Mint, Monarch, and QuickBooks Online exports: column layouts, sign/date conventions, category semantics, plus the shared mapping strategy — categories → expense/income accounts, accounts → asset/liability accounts, opening balances via `Equity:Opening-Balances`, and inter-account transfer deduplication (each transfer appears once, not twice).

## Context

## Steps

2. Write `references/mapping.md`: hierarchy proposal rules, opening-balance construction, transfer pairing, currency handling.
3. Note briefly how other CSV exports (Copilot's limited export, Bench handoffs) map onto the generic path.
