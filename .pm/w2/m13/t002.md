## Objective

Evolve the one-action authorization module into a closed, typed PDP contract that can be extended domain by domain.

## Context

- `src/server/api/authorization/` currently recognizes only `user.delete`.
- Transport operation IDs must remain aliases rather than permissions.

## Steps

1. Define canonical action/resource/requirement and structured decision types.
2. Compose credential and relationship ceilings in one final decision.
3. Add request-local memoization and central decision audit hooks without a second policy catalog.

## Files

- `backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/server/api/authorization/**`
- `backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/server/api/audit.ts`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Unknown actions/resources and evaluator failures deny.
- [ ] Action requirements live in the authorization module, not transport adapters or `*-policy` files.
- [ ] Authorization results cannot survive beyond the request identity.

## Out of scope

- Ledger, billing, social, AI, or Plaid action migrations.
