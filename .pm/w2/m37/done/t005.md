## Result

`mobile/src/__tests__/live-font-scale-remeasure.test.ts` executes the real DateSectionHeader, AccountRow and TimeRangePills bodies under a small persistent-hook runtime. It asserts: keys change in both directions and stay put for data or selection changes; the text, the row's selected state and its pick callback survive; pill identity (including the null "All" key) holds while labels remount; and the selected fill re-places on the remeasured pill both ways but ignores unselected pills. The read-only notice is checked through the TypeScript AST: only that Text is scale-keyed, never the editor session (mounting the session needs Apollo and the DOM editor). Removing each owner's key fails its tests (header 1, row 2, pills 1, notice 2). Mobile gates pass: format, lint, typecheck, 2147 unit tests.

Native iPhone 17e, public Example: date headers, picker rows/sections/pills and the Files→README notice match cold-launch frames in both directions and themes (t001–t004). Dark Settings background/resume is covered for date headers. The picker search query, focus, results and head-ellipsis survive both directions in dark. A Transactions list scrolled into its second page keeps its offset and loaded rows through default→maximum→default and returns to the identical view.

Limits: Android, physical devices, other locales, VoiceOver speech, and the other TimeRangePills consumers (Home charts, balance card, Reports, Budget) have source and unit coverage only. Merchant and account journals share the header component but were not replayed natively.
