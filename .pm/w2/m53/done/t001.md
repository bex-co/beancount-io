## Objective

During init's existing interview, ask which of brokerage / retirement / crypto / property / loan accounts the household has, propose the matching `open`s (with commodity lists), and explain the booking-method choice per investment account in plain terms before writing it (STRICT names lots per sell, FIFO/LIFO/HIFO auto-select, average cost unsupported). Nothing opens that the user did not confirm.

## Context

- The starter template opens 14 everyday accounts and no investment ones; `beancount-trade` never invents accounts, so a first trade currently stalls on missing opens.
- `bea add open --booking` writes the method into the `open` line (m45/t004 verified the accepted list).

## Steps

1. Write the checklist, the method explainer, and the proposal shape into init's text/references, keeping the no-`bea` template path equivalent.
2. Rehearse init on a synthetic investor household and record the resulting ledger.

Investor onboarding added to `beancount-init`: Step 1 now asks which of brokerage/retirement, crypto, and property/mortgage the household has (HOUSEHOLD); new Step 3b proposes the matching opens as one confirmed set — per-account commodities, shared `Income:Dividends`/`Income:CapitalGains`, the beancount-property account model — and explains the booking-method choice in plain terms at open time (including "average cost is not supported" up front), writing only the chosen method.
