## Reproduction and controls

1. Drawer → Browse → Explore → search `example` → Open the real-estate example. Wait for Home to settle. Its recent **Sale — Dispose property — recognize gain** is green **+$422,294.00**.
2. Tap that actual Sale row. Detail repeats **+$422,294.00**, above Money flow rows which correctly show the following source postings:
   - Property: **−1 PROP123MAIN**, cost **400,000 USD**.
   - CapitalImprovements: **−6,000 USD**.
   - AccumDepreciation: **+19,394 USD**.
   - Checking: **+408,900 USD**.
   - DepreciationRecapture income: **−19,394 USD**.
   - LongTerm capital-gain income: **−2,900 USD**.
3. Tap the Checking posting. Its account journal correctly shows Sale **+$408,900.00**. Home's **+$422,294** is the partial sum `408900 - 6000 + 19394`, neither that account's proceeds nor the **22,294 USD** recognized income/book-value gain. It excludes the property's **−400,000 USD** cost while adding its other balance-sheet adjustments.
4. Open the real public purchase entry `beancount:///ledger/open_ledger/real-estate-example/entry/690b9a3858448980ab97c4e8f5c45464`. Detail says **Escrow — Down payment — 20% of $400,000**, but its headline is **−$400,000.00**. The three correctly rendered postings are Property **+1 PROP123MAIN** at **400,000 USD**, Checking **−80,000 USD**, and Mortgage **−320,000 USD**. The selected USD bucket adds cash paid and borrowing into an unlabeled amount; it cannot identify the down payment or express the complete multi-unit transaction.
5. Terminate/relaunch, then open each real entry link again. Sale (fresh PID51065) remains **+$422,294**; purchase (fresh PID67185) remains **−$400,000**. The sale hash is `2f4431f658f3553e73512ea3ebc1a2d4`.

**Expected:** these mixed transactions have an explicit neutral summary such as localized **Multiple postings**, with their exact posting amounts available below. A partial money-currency subtotal should not be presented as an unexplained signed cash inflow/outflow. An explicit account journal continues to report that account's exact change. This does not require choosing a new unfiltered cash metric, hiding liability postings, or converting every transaction into a gain calculation.

**Actual:** the unfiltered headline picks one currency, loses the account distinctions inside it, and renders its partial sum as a positive or negative transaction amount. The source postings and account-specific values remain correct.

The real `getLedgerJournal` request returns HTTP200, all **91** transactions, including both exact entries and cost annotations. `getLedgerAccountJournal` with Checking / `at_cost` returns HTTP200, **66** entries: sale `change.USD = 408900.00`, purchase `change.USD = -80000.00`. No malformed input or service failure is involved. Home net worth **178,145.77** and its six-month change **−35,479.75 (−16.61%)** independently match the balance-sheet control, so this finding does not concern financial totals.

## Cause and bounded fix

`mobile/src/screens/transactions-screen/utils/transaction-display-utils.ts:58–72` describes preferring the cost/price currency as the trade's cash side. `selectTransactionAmount` at74 filters all Assets/Liabilities at80–83, then sums units by currency at94–117 without retaining account identity. At125–139, the property's cost makes USD the preferred bucket. At140–158 the resulting USD subtotal is returned as a singular signed amount even when it combines cash, financing or noncash adjustments and excludes the commodity's costed leg.

There are two direct production consumers: `transactions-screen/entry-row/index.tsx:138–146` and `transaction-detail-screen/selectors/select-transaction-detail.ts:34–45`. The latter explicitly describes positive values as cash inflows. Both blindly format this result; `null` currently means no usable amount and produces a blank, so use an explicit mixed result rather than disguising ambiguity as an empty transaction.

Add a bounded mixed-summary decision for the reproduced cost-bearing commodity acquisition/disposal whose chosen money bucket spans distinct balance-sheet accounts. Render its translated, neutral summary in EntryRow and the detail hero. Preserve the single-money-account trade policy, including several fee/cash postings into that same account, and the complete Money flow rows. Preserve ordinary expense/income netting, same-currency transfers, commodity-only precision and explicit account-journal amounts. Avoid account-name guesses or a blanket change to every transaction with several postings. Clarify the selector's documented contract to distinguish a genuine money-leg amount from an ambiguous partial bucket.

Acceptance and regressions: use these real posting shapes for sale and financed purchase; assert both consumers show the neutral mixed summary, without a sign or inflow color. Keep the existing ordinary multiple-USD-account netting test, zero-net transfer tests and recorded-precision tests green. Keep single-money-account stock sale **+905.05 USD** (`910 - 4.95` in the same account) and crypto purchase **−7,575.13 USD** (`-7563.78 - 11.35` in the same account) unchanged; both were exercised natively against real public controls earlier in this sweep. Recheck Home Sale → detail → Checking, and the purchase entry link. No backend, REST/GraphQL/MCP contract, dependency, aggregate report or cross-package import change is needed.

Blast radius: Home Recent Transactions, Transactions, merchant detail and Reports account-transactions cards share EntryRow; transaction detail shares the selector directly. Home Sale and both details were reproduced; the other list hosts are source-traced, not individually claimed. AccountEntryRow uses a separate account-specific selector and passed the Sale control.
