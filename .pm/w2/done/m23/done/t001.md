## Verification

Recorded the public `open_ledger/example` ledger through a dependency-free record/replay fixture (`dashboard/scripts/perf-fixture-api.mjs`) and traced cold SSR and client navigations on the baseline production build (`aa0cbd3e`) with Chrome 152 under the m21 profile. Required data: `GetLedger` (route access) and `GetLedgerOverview`. A 2,000 ms delay on the optional `GetLedgerEntriesCountPerType`, `GetLedgerFile`, and `GetLedgerAccountMeta` moved baseline primary content from 1,273 ms to 3,308 ms on cold loads and from 1,225 ms to 3,370 ms on client navigation, because the layout and overview loaders awaited every operation. Evidence and method: `dashboard/docs/performance-route-loading.md`.
