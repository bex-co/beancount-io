## Objective

Write behavior evals that would catch the ways a manual entry goes wrong: wrong sign, invented account, duplicate, wrong file, and a forced close.

## Context

- Format: `evals/evals.json` entries with `prompt`, `expected_output`, `files`, `expectations`, like the other skills. Fixture ledgers named `*ledger.beancount` are checked by `ci-check.py`.

## Steps

1. 2. Add synthetic fixtures under `evals/files/`.

`skills/.claude/skills/beancount-add/evals/evals.json` holds 11 evals with checkable expectations, over two synthetic fixtures that pass `bea check` and the oracle `bean-check` in `ci-check.py`: `eval1_ledger.beancount` (checking, savings, an old savings account still holding 100.00 USD, an Amex with four PIZZERIA NAPOLI dinners including one on 2026-10-15) and `eval2_ledger.beancount` (loads `nounused`).
