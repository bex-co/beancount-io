## Objective

Make every PDP call independently observable and fail closed without an authorization decision cache.

## Context

- Old scope and ledger seams may emit separate audit events or blur relationship denial, concealment, source outage, and audit-sink failure.

## Steps

1. Emit the applicable result for every authorization call using the isolated transport operation ID, or canonical action for direct/background calls, with safe subject and ledger-pin metadata only.
2. Remove decision memos and cross-request authorization state; verify duplicate and concurrent calls each evaluate current source facts and cannot overwrite/suppress audit.
3. Keep catalog-defined denial/concealment/actionable messages distinct from source-unavailable logging + `error` audit + service-unavailable response; keep audit persistence fail-open.

## Files

- authorization/audit/identity/context files
- affected observability tests

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every final decision produces its applicable audit result, including repeated writes in one request.
- [ ] Logs contain no request arguments, tokens, secrets, or PII.
- [ ] Source/evaluator failure performs no domain work and is never emitted as a policy denial; audit-sink failure does not fail an allowed operation.
- [ ] No authorization decision memo exists at request or process scope.

## Out of scope

- A new audit service or external monitoring platform.
