## Objective

Remove obsolete final authorization decisions now that every protected public application-service/workflow boundary calls the PDP.

## Context

- Existing authority is spread across op-class scope evaluation, GraphQL auth/pin middleware, route guards, and service helpers.

## Steps

1. Reduce coarse read/write/admin gates to operational rate/audit classification and PDP routing; they must not decide credential reachability for migrated actions.
2. Remove superseded GraphQL decorators/middleware, route guards, raw-ID bypasses, request wrappers, authorization-only workflows, and service policy assertions.
3. Retain explicit `Identity` service inputs, authentication, public/webhook ingress, validation, atomic owner/path predicates, quotas, not-found protections, and transactional invariants with non-policy names.

## Files

- `backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/server/api/**`
- `backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/server/graphql/**`
- affected domain helpers and adapters

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A protected business request receives one final PDP decision.
- [ ] No transport can bypass the same action's relationship or credential ceiling.
- [ ] Authentication/public/webhook/infrastructure gates remain intact.
- [ ] Direct protected service calls authorize with canonical-action audit fallback, while operation IDs remain only in isolated AsyncLocalStorage.

## Out of scope

- Removing defense-in-depth validation or authentication.
