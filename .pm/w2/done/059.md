## Cause and repair boundary

`mobile/src/components/segmented-pages/index.tsx:29–42` puts the flexible tab viewport in one horizontal row; its trailing wrapper at65–69 has `flexShrink:0`. `tabsContent` adds16points at the start, and the existing continuation fade is28points wide. Home's trailing label at `screens/home-screen/components/account-charts-card.tsx:57–67,149–168` scales normally and has no width/layout budget. The long German label therefore leaves only38.67points for the strip. Tabs deliberately use one line at205–208; `PagerView` remains `scrollEnabled={false}` at240 so the plot owns scrubbing.

Make this local header allocation adapt when the enlarged action cannot coexist with a readable tab viewport, for example by placing the navigation action on its own row at that width. Keep the action visible and reachable, meaningful text enlargement, correct accounting translations, horizontal scrolling and its conditional fade, current page/range, the full170-point plot, caption interaction and the Accounts destination. Do not shorten the accounting terms, globally disable font scaling, remove the fade or enable pager swiping. No dependency, API, generated-code or cross-package change is needed.

Acceptance: fresh German maximum-text Alibaba has identifiable chart controls; actual native scroll/taps select Assets1,962,109MRMB and Liabilities−848,215MRMB, retain6M and the full plots, and Alle ansehen still opens Accounts in both themes.
