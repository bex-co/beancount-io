## Objective

## Context

- Current Plaid reads and writes are broadly `admin` or `write` despite different resource effects.

## Steps

1. Inventory Link, item, account mapping, status, reconcile, and transaction aliases with their current credential reachability, rate budgets, audit, and error contracts.

3. Specify customer and background provenance/bindings without request/workload tuples; update `.fga` only if durable bank/ledger relationship semantics actually change.
