## Spike outcome (2026-07-31) — **GO** (milestone-sized, ~5–6h)

- **Conventions**: follow the beancount-docs stock-vesting pattern — per-grant synthetic commodities (`AAPL.UNVEST260315` per vest tranche or per grant) held at zero cost in `Assets:Brokerage:<Broker>:Unvested`; on vest, convert N unvested units → real shares with cost basis = vest-day FMV, income leg to `Income:Salary:RSU` at FMV (matches W-2 treatment); sell-to-cover is a same-day lot sale with normal capital-gains mechanics (usually ≈0 gain). Account hierarchy mirrors `beancount-options` (`Assets:Brokerage:<Broker>:{Cash,Stock,Unvested}`, `Income:Salary:{RSU,ESPP-Discount}`, `Income:Trading:CapitalGains`).
- **Event coverage**: grant (record tranches, no income), vest (+ sell-to-cover), ESPP purchase (discount → `Income:Salary:ESPP-Discount`, basis = FMV, qualifying/disqualifying disposition noted), sale (explicit lot selection, ST/LT by acquisition date), forfeiture (departure), dividend-on-unvested edge case.
- **Reference plan**: `grants.md`, `vesting.md` (sell-to-cover math), `espp.md`, `sales-and-lots.md`, `tax-notes.md` (ordinary-vs-capital, disqualifying dispositions, no wash-sale computation — same carve-out as options).
- **Trust rails as usual**: confirm-gated writes, per-lot `bean-check` verification, evals with fixture vest schedules.
