## Objective

Book the tax a user's invoice or sale states as a liability leg, the remittance that clears it, and an owed-for-period answer tied to the liability balance.

## Context

- Amounts and rates come only from the user's documents, the same discipline as `beancount-property`'s printed mortgage split.
- PRFAQ001 defers tax determination; this records stated figures only.

## Steps

1. Add the tax leg to the invoice procedure.
2. Write the remittance procedure.
3. Write the owed-for-period recipe.

## Files

- The new skill's `SKILL.md` and references
- `scripts/skills/test_beancount_skills.py`

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Invoice 1,000 with 80 stated tax books 1,080 receivable, 1,000 income, and 80 collected-tax liability.
- [x] A remittance clears the liability, and the owed-for-period query equals the liability balance for that period.
- [x] With no stated tax amount or rate, no tax leg is written and the skill asks.

## Out of scope

- Rate lookup, jurisdiction rules, filing.

## Result

`beancount-invoices` books a stated tax amount (or one computed by the engine from a stated rate) as a collected-tax leg on the invoice, writes no tax leg when the document states none and asks when the user says tax applies without a figure, and records remittances from the user's filed amount against the collected-tax account (the ledger's own, else `Liabilities:SalesTax`). New recipes `tax-period` (collected within a period) and `tax-owed` (still owed at its end). Verified by `test_collected_tax_for_a_period_and_its_remittance`: 1,000 + 80 tax books 1,080 receivable; Q3 collected and owed read -80.00; after the remittance Q3 still shows -80.00 collected while only Q4's -16.00 stays owed, and Q4's collection excludes the remittance (fails without the collected-only filter). Full skills suite: 158 tests pass.
