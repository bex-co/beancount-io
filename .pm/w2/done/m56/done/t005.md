## Objective

When `beancount-import` or `beancount-reconcile` stages a deposit that matches an open invoice, offer it as that invoice's payment through t002's procedure instead of categorizing it as income.

## Context

- Import's Suggest stage (`skills/beancount-import/references/categorization.md`) and reconcile's proposed entries categorize from payee history today.

## Steps

1. Hand matching deposits to t002's payment application, shown in the review table with the invoice they would clear.
2. Keep the user's decision per row; never apply silently.

## Files

- `skills/beancount-import/references/categorization.md`
- `skills/beancount-reconcile/SKILL.md`

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A staged deposit equal to an open invoice's balance is offered as its payment and, after the yes, reduces that receivable.
- [x] A deposit matching two open invoices is asked about.

## Out of scope

- Processor payout exports ([w2/m58](../../m58/README.md)).

## Result

`beancount-import` (`references/categorization.md`, Payments of open invoices) and `beancount-reconcile` (categorizing a missing entry's other leg) now check staged inflows against beancount-invoices' `invoice-open` read: an inflow equal to one open invoice's balance is offered as that invoice's payment (receivable leg with its `invoice:` and link, shown beside the invoice, written only with the row's yes); a sum of several invoices or an equal match to two is asked about; a smaller deposit is a part payment only when the user names the invoice. Import writes such rows on the normalized batch path. The open-balance read is covered by `test_invoice_open_balances_follow_partial_and_multi_document_payments`; the agent behavior is measured by t007's import eval (an imported deposit applied to an invoice).
