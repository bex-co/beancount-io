# Mobile's committed GraphQL snapshot is stale against the deployed api-gateway

`mobile/src/generated-graphql/` (`schema.graphql`, `graphql.schema.json`, `types.ts`) still documents `ManagedPriceSource.freshness` as the ten-minute `observed-at` rule and has no `latestPriceDate`. Production has served the seven-day newest-price-date rule and `latestPriceDate` since `15173444f` (ADR 015 §8, amended 2026-11-15).

Mobile selects neither field today, so nothing is broken. Regenerating while that change shipped (`CODEGEN_SCHEMA_FILE=<local SDL> yarn codegen`) also showed about 1,400 lines of unrelated drift from other schema changes, so the refresh is its own change and was left out of `d967f57cd`.

**Done when:** `yarn codegen` in `mobile/` against `https://beancount.io/` leaves no diff, and mobile `yarn typecheck`, `yarn lint` and `yarn test:unit` pass on the regenerated files.

**Resolved 2026-11-17** — shipped in this commit (`mobile`). `yarn codegen`
against `https://beancount.io/` rewrote `schema.graphql`, `graphql.schema.json`
and `types.ts`; `graphql.tsx` was already current, since no mobile document
selects a changed field. The snapshot now carries the seven-day
`ManagedPriceSource.freshness` text and `latestPriceDate`, plus the other
production drift (account rename, entry moves, `deploymentInfo`, nullable
`sshUrl` and holdings cost fields, paged unsynced Plaid transactions). It also
drops the `subscription: Subscription` root and the `@specifiedBy` directives:
the earlier snapshot was built from an SDL file, where graphql-js treats the
billing `Subscription` type as the subscription root, and the deployed
introspection has no subscription root. A second `yarn codegen` leaves no
diff. Mobile `yarn format:check`, `yarn lint`, `yarn typecheck` and
`yarn test:unit` (5219 passed) pass on the regenerated files.
