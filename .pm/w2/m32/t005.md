## Objective

Stop offering Edit/Delete on price entries that come from a managed include. The ledger refuses those writes with `OPERATION_NOT_ALLOWED` (ADR 015 §9).

## Context

- `dashboard/src/features/journal/components/entry-context-panel.tsx` gates actions only on `canWrite`. `entry-context-dialog.tsx` already hides editing for generated entries.
- A managed entry's `meta.filename` is its virtual include key, a URL resolved against the including file (`resolveIncludeTarget` in the ledger's `file-map-loader.ts`).

## Steps

1. Detect managed entries from their source path and hide Edit/Delete, with a short note naming the managed source.

## Files

- `dashboard/src/features/journal/**`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A managed price entry shows no Edit/Delete. Ordinary entries are unchanged.
- [ ] A test fails when the gate is removed.

## Out of scope

- Journal filtering of managed entries.
