## Objective

Centralize access-list and public-key control while preserving collaborator and self-leave semantics.

## Context

- ACL reads are control-plane reads and must not inherit ordinary content permission accidentally.

## Steps

1. Put collaborator list/get/add/update/delete and leave actions behind protected service/workflow PEPs with explicit `Identity`.
2. Migrate public-key list/get/create/delete actions through the same catalog and preserve their operational budgets.
3. Keep target validation, atomic owner/rank predicates, rank rules, not-found concealment, and Gitea errors as domain/data behavior.

## Files

- ledger collaborator/public-key services and resolvers
- authorization integration files

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] ACL/key reads and writes require their explicit capability.
- [ ] A caller cannot modify its relationship through another user's target ID.
- [ ] Denial precedes every Gitea mutation.
- [ ] Missing/foreign targets do not become an existence oracle and source outage remains a distinct service error.

## Out of scope

- New collaborator roles or key types.
