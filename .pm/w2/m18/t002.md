## Objective

Authorize ledger lifecycle operations once at protected application-service/workflow boundaries before control-plane side effects.

## Context

- Update/delete currently rely heavily on coarse scopes and downstream Gitea/Fava enforcement.

## Steps

1. Pass explicit resolved `Identity` to the protected service/workflow; apply User-resource authorization plus quota for create.
2. Apply current-ledger administration checks for update/delete, keeping operation IDs in AsyncLocalStorage and policy out of resolvers.
3. Preserve atomic owner/name predicates, locks, Plaid cleanup, transaction ordering, not-found concealment, and client error behavior as domain/data defenses.

## Files

- ledger workflow/operations/resolvers
- authorization integration files

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Denied operations call no create/update/delete or cleanup side effect.
- [ ] Only current administrators may update/delete an existing ledger.
- [ ] Creation limits remain domain checks and successful ownership comes from the source system.
- [ ] Direct protected calls cannot bypass the PDP and audit under the canonical-action fallback.

## Out of scope

- Adding step-up confirmation, an authorization-only workflow, or product changes.
