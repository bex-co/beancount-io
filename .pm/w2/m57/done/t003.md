## Objective

Book each remittance that clears a payroll liability, and answer quarter totals per wage and liability account for the user's accountant, tied to booked balances.

## Context

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A remittance equal to the liability clears it to zero; a partial one leaves the remainder.
- [x] Quarter totals equal the account movements for the quarter from an executed query.

## Result

Remittances are part of `beancount-add`'s Recording payroll (each liability cleared at the amount the deposit confirmation states, the `payroll-liabilities` read first, anything larger or leaving an unexpected balance asked about). `beancount-ask`'s `bql-recipes.md` gains section 11: `payroll-quarter` (movement per payroll expense and liability account) and `payroll-withheld` (accrued before remittances). Verified by `test_payroll_remittance_and_quarter_totals_tie_to_account_movements`: two runs and one in-quarter remittance give 16,000.00 wages, 992.00 employer taxes, liabilities moving -1,100.00/-992.00 against -2,200.00/-1,984.00 withheld, and the same amounts still owed at quarter end (fails when withheld counts remittances).
