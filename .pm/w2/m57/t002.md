## Objective

Book a payroll run exactly as the provider's summary prints it: gross wages and employer taxes to expense, each withholding and employer tax to its liability, net pay out of the bank.

## Context

- Same discipline as `beancount-property`'s mortgage split: figures are read as printed, never computed.

## Steps

1. Write the inputs list and gates: printed gross minus printed withholdings equals printed net; employer taxes as printed.
2. Write the proposal shape and the confirmed write.

## Files

- The owning skill's text and references

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A summary that ties produces a balanced, check-passing entry; one that does not tie stops with both figures shown.
- [ ] A summary missing a figure produces a question, never a computed amount.

## Out of scope

- Calculating wages, withholdings, or employer taxes.
- Contractor payments (ordinary entries through `beancount-add`).
