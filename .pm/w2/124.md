# Billing's usage card counts AI tokens as "requests" in Japanese and Korean, and prints the Persian ledger count in Latin digits

**Minor (i18n) · dashboard · 15–20 minutes.** A2: the usage card is where a reader checks how much of their plan is left. In Japanese and Korean it reports **12,489 / 2,000,000 requests**, while the plan line just above it says **2,000,000 AI tokens / month**. Reading a token budget as two million requests overstates the allowance by orders of magnitude. In Persian, the ledger count beside it uses Latin digits while every other number on the card uses Persian ones.

Production https://beancount.io, 2026-11-10, isolated headless Chromium 156 at 1440×1000, signed in. Read only. Local main `4a6e70a42`.

## Reproduce

Open `/settings/billing` with each `?lang=` below and read **Usage**:

| Language | AI usage label and count | Plan line on the same page | Ledgers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| en (control) | AI Tokens · 12,489 / 2,000,000 tokens used this month | 2,000,000 AI tokens / month | 6 / 20 ledgers |
| ja | **AIリクエスト** · 今月 12,489 / 2,000,000 **リクエスト**使用済み | 2,000,000 AI **トークン** / 月 | 6 / 20 元帳 |
| ko | **AI 요청** · 이번 달 12,489 / 2,000,000 **요청** 사용됨 | 2,000,000 AI **토큰**/월 | 6 / 20 원장 |
| fa | توکن‌های هوش مصنوعی · ۱۲٬۴۸۹ / ۲٬۰۰۰٬۰۰۰ | ۲٬۰۰۰٬۰۰۰ توکن | **6 / 20** دفتر |

The other 12 locales say tokens.

## Root cause

- `dashboard/src/features/user-settings/locales/ja.ts:538–548` and the same three keys in `ko.ts` (`userSettings.aiCfoUsage`, `aiCfoUsageCount`, `aiCfoUsageUnlimited`) translate the unit as "requests". The English source and the other 12 translations say tokens, and the counts passed in are tokens (`aiCfoTokensUsed` / `aiCfoTokensMax`).
- `features/user-settings/pages/general/subscription-usage-overview.tsx:134–136` passes the ledger counts as `String(ledgersUsed)` / `String(ledgersMax)`. The AI counts beside them go through `useQuotaNumberFormat()` (`formatCount`), which gives Persian digits.

## Fix

- ja: `AIトークン` / `今月 {used} / {max} トークン使用済み` / `今月 {used} トークン使用済み（無制限）`.
- ko: `AI 토큰` / `이번 달 {used} / {max} 토큰 사용됨` / `이번 달 {used} 토큰 사용됨 (무제한)`.
- Format the ledger counts with `formatCount`, like the token counts.
- Add a test that renders the usage card under `fa` and expects `۶ / ۲۰`. If the locale test helpers allow it, also assert that no locale's `aiCfoUsage*` strings contain the ja/ko words for "request".

## Blast radius

Only the Billing usage card (`/settings/billing` and the General page's subscription summary, if it reuses the card). Mobile has no such strings. Its own billing screen was not checked here.
