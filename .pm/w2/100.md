# Give business books a canonical home inside the suite

From the `/simplify` review of m55–m58, 2026-11-01. Each is larger than a behavior-preserving cleanup, so none shipped there.

- ADR025 is the convention's canonical text, but `docs/adrs` is outside the installed suite: skills link to GitHub and restate its rules, and the client/project rule already reads differently in `beancount-add` and `beancount-import`. Add `beancount-init/references/business-books.md` (names, document identity, client/project keys, deposits that pay open invoices) and point add, import, invoices, reconcile, and `skills/AGENTS.md` at it.
- Import and reconcile read all of `beancount-invoices/SKILL.md` for `invoice-open`, and invoices reads all of `beancount-add/SKILL.md` for `open-accounts` and `entry-destination`, about 4,000 tokens per run. Moving those recipes to `bea-cli.md` also touches trade and property.
- In Codex's `workspace-write` sandbox the first `bea` write can stop on `~/.cache/bea/locks`, then recovers through the `XDG_CACHE_HOME` hint in the error. `bea-cli.md` could say so once.
- `cli/tests/test_release.py::test_customer_smoke_runs_against_the_installed_cli` takes about 67 s alone and exceeded its 120 s limit under `-n auto`.
