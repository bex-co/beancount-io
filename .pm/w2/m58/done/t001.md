## Objective

Teach `beancount-import` to read a Stripe- or Shopify-style payout or balance export into entries that keep gross sales, processor fees, collected tax, refunds, and the net payout separate.

## Context

- `skills/beancount-import/references/broker-activity.md` is the pattern: a confirmed column and action mapping stored in the config block, an entry shape per action, and an unmapped action stops for a question.
- Charges that settle open invoices apply through [w2/m56](../blocked/m56/README.md)/t002's payment procedure.

## Steps

1. Write the mapping and per-row shapes: charge, fee, refund, tax, payout.
2. Gate each payout on the export's own arithmetic (gross minus fees minus refunds, with tax as stated, equals net), shown in the review.
3. Stamp `import-id` per `references/dedup.md`, using the processor's native IDs.

## Files

- `skills/beancount-import/references/payouts.md` (new)
- `skills/beancount-import/SKILL.md`

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A payout whose rows tie writes gross, fees, refunds, and tax separately; one that does not tie stops with both figures.
- [x] An unmapped row type stops for a question; exact re-import adds zero entries.

## Out of scope

- Live processor APIs.

## Result

New `skills/beancount-import/references/payouts.md`, linked from the import skill's scope and Normalize stage: a clearing account per processor (`Assets:Clearing:<Processor>`), sales booked gross with the fee and any stated tax apart (or applied to an open invoice per the invoices rule), refunds reversing their legs, payouts moving the clearing account to the confirmed bank account; a stored `payout-csv` mapping with a confirmed `type_map` (unmapped types and adjustments are asked about); the `payout-tie` gate by engine arithmetic before staging; native `<processor>:<id>` import-ids; the `clearing-balance` read for money in transit. Verified by `test_processor_payout_ties_to_its_rows_and_empties_clearing`: three rows tie to a 157.28 payout and a missing refund shows a -16.90 gap; sales with fee and stated tax, a refund, the payout, and a later sale leave the clearing account at 0.00 after the payout and 48.25 in transit (fails when the gap ignores the rows). The gate reads `FROM #accounts`, which answers on a ledger with no postings yet. Full skills suite: 162 tests pass.
