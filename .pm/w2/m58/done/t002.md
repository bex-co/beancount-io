## Objective

Land payouts in a processor clearing account, so the later bank deposit matches as a transfer and neither side books the money twice.

## Context

- Transfer-side duplicate detection is Layer 2b in `skills/beancount-import/references/dedup.md`.

## Steps

1. Write the clearing-account flow.
2. Make the bank-side import offer the payout transfer as a suspected duplicate, decided by the user.

## Files

- `skills/beancount-import/references/payouts.md`
- `skills/beancount-import/references/dedup.md` if Layer 2b needs a note

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Importing the payout export and then the bank export leaves the clearing account at zero for settled payouts and books revenue once.

## Out of scope

- Reconciling the clearing account across months (reconcile already handles one account against one statement).

## Result

The clearing flow is in `references/payouts.md` (t001); `references/dedup.md` Layer 2b now names a payout from a payout export as a transfer from the clearing account, so the later bank deposit is skipped there and the sales behind it count once. Verified by `test_bank_deposit_meets_the_payout_transfer_in_the_duplicate_window`: after a sale and its payout, the import's `duplicate-window` read on checking around a later bank deposit returns the payout's counter posting with its `stripe:` id, the clearing account reads 0.00, and checking holds the payout once.
