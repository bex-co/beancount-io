## Objective

Answer "who owes me" and "whom do I owe": each open invoice and bill with issue date, due date when stated, days outstanding, and open amount, tied to the account balances.

## Context

- Local `bea` supports date subtraction (`skills/beancount-trade/references/realized-gains.md` computes `date - cost_date`). Hosted query surfaces do not yet ([w6/blocked/017](../../w6/blocked/017.md)), so aging ships on local `bea` first.

## Steps

1. Write the aging recipes keyed by invoice and bill identity.
2. Write the tie-out to the receivable and payable balances.
3. Add executable recipe tests.

## Files

- The new skill's references or `skills/beancount-ask/references/bql-recipes.md`
- `scripts/skills/test_beancount_skills.py`

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Open amounts sum to the receivable and payable balances from an executed query.
- [x] Fully paid invoices do not appear; days outstanding are counted from today's stated date.

## Out of scope

- Hosted aging through MCP and GraphQL until w6/017 clears.

## Result

`beancount-invoices` gains a read-only Aging section: `invoice-aging` (open documents with issue date, days outstanding, and open amount, grouped by identity alone), `invoice-terms` and `bill-terms` (client and due date from the issuing or receiving posting, days past due computed by the engine), and `document-balance` for the tie-out to the receivable or payable balance; documents without stated terms show days outstanding only. Verified by `test_aging_reads_terms_from_the_issuing_posting_and_ties_to_the_balance` (a part-paid invoice 56 days out and 26 past due, an invoice with no due date, a bill due in 19 days, open amounts tied to 800.00 receivable and -450.00 payable; fails when terms read payment postings). Full skills suite: 159 tests pass. Hosted aging stays out of scope until w6/017.
