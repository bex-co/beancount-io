## Objective

Record an invoice the user describes (receivable against income, linked by the invoice's identity) and apply each payment to it, including a payment processor's gross, fee, and net, proposed first and written after a yes.

## Context

- Inputs come from the user's invoice and payment documents: client, invoice number, issue date, due date when stated, lines, stated tax.
- Same rails as the other entry skills: propose then confirm, existing accounts only, the root `bea check`.

## Steps

1. Write the issue procedure: inputs, the proposal shape, the link.
2. Write payment application: read the invoice's open balance from its link; handle a partial payment; book a processor payment as gross to the receivable, fee to an expense, and net to cash; ask on an overpayment or a payment that matches no open invoice.

## Files

- The new skill's `SKILL.md` and references

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Invoice 1,000 then receive 400 leaves 600 receivable on that invoice (`freelance@1`'s acceptance case).
- [ ] A 1,000 processor payment with a 29.30 fee books 1,000 against the receivable, 29.30 to fees, and 970.70 to cash.
- [ ] A payment that matches no open invoice is asked about, never applied silently.

## Out of scope

- Sending, numbering, or rendering invoice documents.
- Payments found in imports and statements (t005).
