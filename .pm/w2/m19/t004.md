## Objective

Stop webhook/job paths from masquerading as capability-exempt browser sessions while preserving trusted background execution.

## Context

- Plaid webhooks and scheduled sync currently use `systemIdentity(userId)` after resolving an item.

## Steps

1. Define an explicit internal invocation path bound to trusted item/user/ledger data.
2. Route background domain authorization through the same action requirements without credential/request tuples.
3. Preserve webhook signature and scheduler authentication outside relationship authz.

## Files

- Plaid webhook service and scheduler job
- identity/authorization integration files

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] No request path can claim background/capability-exempt identity.
- [ ] Background work cannot escape the resolved item/user/ledger binding.
- [ ] Webhook/scheduler behavior remains operationally unchanged.

## Out of scope

- A general service-identity platform or FGA `service` type.
