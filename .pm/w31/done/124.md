# `beancount-close` SKILL.md step 3 says "on or after period end"; its own checklist and beancount-reconcile use the day-after rule

**Minor · skills (beancount-close) · 15m · A1.** `skills/beancount-close/SKILL.md` § 3 "Assert" tells the agent to "check that each active account has an assertion dated on or after period end".
