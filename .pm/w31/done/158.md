# `check_drained` still checks its zero balance when a balance is already written on the close date; stock skips it

**Minor · ledger (`check_drained` reconciliation) · 20–30m · A1.** Left over from `w31/156` (`57288c11c`), recorded in `done/156.md` § "Still different". Per that note, a written `balance` on the close date makes stock skip its own zero balance for that account, but the engine still checks it. A ledger stock accepts can therefore get an extra balance error here. The error now sits on the close line (`w31/155`/`w31/156`), and that only affects where it is reported.

- First confirm the exact rule in stock 3.2.3's `beancount/plugins/check_drained.py` (which date and which account and currency key the skip). Then reproduce: a ledger with that written balance is clean on stock and reports an error here.
- Fix: drop the engine's drained-balance error in exactly the cases stock skips, and keep it otherwise.
- Done when both the written-balance case and the plain case match stock. A unit test and a `verify:rustledger` check cover the written-balance case and fail with the fix reverted.

---

**Resolved 2026-11-27** — Stock 3.2.3's `check_drained` collects every balance on a balance-sheet account as `(date, currency)` and, at each `close`, adds no zero balance for a currency whose balance is dated the close date itself (not the day after, not another account, a parent included). Balances sort before a close on the same day, so file order does not matter. Before this fix the engine checked it anyway. A funded account with `2026-08-30 balance Assets:Cash 49.75 USD` and `2026-08-30 close Assets:Cash` was clean on stock and got `Balance failed for 'Assets:Cash': expected 0 USD != accumulated 49.75 USD (49.75 too much)` on the close line here.

- **Fix** (`check-drained.ts`, now `reconcileCheckDrainedErrors`). When `check_drained` runs, declared or through `pedantic`, the engine's zero-balance error for a closed account and currency is dropped once for each close date of that account that has a balance in that currency. Every other error keeps its w31/156 words and line. This now matches stock for: a balance that holds the money, one written after the close, one under `pedantic`, a close made by `close_tree`, a balance in one of two currencies (the other is still reported), a wrong written zero balance (only its own failure remains), and two closes (one error is left). A balance the day before or the day after the close, or one on another account, keeps the error, as in stock.
- **Still different.** These differences existed before this fix. A written balance's own failure is in the engine's words (`got …`). On a duplicate close, the remaining error sits on the first close's line, where stock uses the second's. A balance the day after a funded close lacks stock's `Duplicate balance assertion with different amounts` (w21/656). The engine's synthesized zero balance stays in the directive stream even when stock would not have added one.
- **Tests.** `plugin-locations.test.ts` covers the per-currency skip, the stream order, `pedantic`, another day or account, and one drop per close date. `scripts/drained-and-attr-errors.ts` (run by `yarn verify:rustledger`) checks four new rows against stock 3.2.3 on the live engine: the written balance, the same written after the close under `pedantic`, one of two currencies, and a balance the day before. The unit cases and the first verify row fail when run against the previous reconciler.
- **Gates.** `backend-cluster/ledger`: `yarn lint`, `yarn typecheck`, `yarn test` (239 suites, 4,420 tests) and `yarn verify:rustledger` pass. The fix reaches production with the next ledger deploy.
