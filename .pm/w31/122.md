# The `reconcile-account` and `close-month` prompts never say a statement's ending balance is asserted the day after its last day, so a reconciliation that ties fails its preview and is reported `partial`

**Minor · backend-v2 (`src/features/ai-agent/api/mcp-prompts.ts`) · 15–20m · A1.** A Beancount `balance` assertion checks the balance at the start of its date, so it leaves out that day's transactions. The playbook the prompt is built from says so twice. `skills/beancount-reconcile/SKILL.md:186` dates the assertion "the **day after** the statement's last day", and `:319` explains why. `skills/beancount-close/references/close-checklist.md:31` counts an assertion as pinning the close only when it is "dated ≥ period end + 1 (the day-after rule)".

The MCP prompts carry neither rule. A claude.ai or Codex session that reads only the served text gets this:

- `reconcile-account`, phase 4: "one `addLedgerEntries` call with `dry_run: true` carrying the missing transactions and one period-end `balance` assertion at the statement's ending balance" (`mcp-prompts.ts:264`). The prompt's description says "a period-end balance assertion" (`:405`).
- `close-month`, phase 3: "Every reconciled account should now have a `balance` assertion dated on or after period end" (`:190-191`). That counts an assertion dated on the last day, which leaves out that day's activity, as pinned.

An agent that dates the assertion on the statement's last day sees a failing preview on a ledger that ties. The playbook then tells it to "withhold the assertion and say why", so it reports `partial` and writes nothing that proves the tie-out.

## Repro (live)

1. `prompts/get` `reconcile-account` with `{"account": "Assets:Cash", "ledger": "<qa-owner>/mcp-agent-eval"}`, and `close-month` with `{"month": "2026-08", …}`. Neither text contains "day after", "next day" or "start of".
2. A statement for `Assets:Cash` whose last day is 2026-08-28 ends at 49.75 USD. The ledger agrees: its last cash entry is `2026-08-28 * "FARMERS MARKET"` at -23.50.
   - `addLedgerEntries` `dry_run: true` with `{type: "balance", entry: {date: "2026-08-28", account: "Assets:Cash", amount: {number: "49.75", currency: "USD"}}}` gives `valid: false`, "Balance failed for Assets:Cash: expected 49.75 USD, got 73.25 USD".
   - The same assertion dated `2026-08-29` gives `valid: true`, `newErrors: []`. That is the assertion the fixture already carries.

## Fix

In `mcp-prompts.ts`:

- `reconcile-account` phase 4: date the assertion the day after the statement's last day, and say why in one clause (a `balance` checks the start of its date, so it leaves out that day's transactions).
- `close-month` phase 3: an assertion pins the period only when it is dated on or after the day after period end.
- The `reconcile-account` description may keep "period-end".

Prompts are MCP-only: REST and GraphQL serve no prompt text, so no other surface changes. Add a case to the prompt tests (beside `mcp-prompt-empty-args.test.ts`) asserting both texts state the rule. Check that the `yarn mcp:agent-eval` prompt journeys (`docs/mcp-agent-eval.md`) still expect the dates they do now.
