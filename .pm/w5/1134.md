# Trial Balance reports a false Reconciliation Difference under At Market Value, Units, or Converted to a currency

**Severity:** major (false financial reconciliation result). **Owner:** dashboard. **Estimate:** 40–60 minutes. **A2:** the page promises "Verify that debits equal credits across all accounts", and a reader who only changes how amounts are valued is told a balanced ledger is out of balance. **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` on 2026-11-16 against `https://beancount.io` (local HEAD `3d80f8ba4`; the implicated dashboard and ledger sources are unchanged at HEAD), headless Chromium at 1440×1000, signed in as the QA account, public read-only `open_ledger/example`. Read-only.

## Repro

1. Open `/ledger/open_ledger/example/trial-balance?lang=en` (all time). Select **At Cost**: the last row reads `Reconciliation Difference 0.00 -`. Correct.
2. Select **At Market Value**: it reads `Reconciliation Difference 10,823.44 -`.
3. Select **Converted to USD**: `Reconciliation Difference 10,823.44 -`.
4. Select **Units**: `Reconciliation Difference -105,919.47 17 GLD 104 ITOT 597.748 RGAGX +3 more`.

Reproduced from fresh loads, and under `?lang=de` (`Abstimmungsdifferenz 10.823,44`). Scoped periods at cost (`time=2016`, `2016-03`, `2017-q1`) all reconcile to `-`, a working control.

**Control:** the Balance Sheet at At Market Value adds `Equity:Unrealized -10,823.44`, and its print summary reads `Reconciliation Difference USD 0.00000`. 10,823.44 is exactly that unrealized gain.

API control (GraphQL `getLedgerTrialBalance`, `ledgerId: "open_ledger/example"`): with `conversion: "at_value"` or `"USD"`, the root `balanceChildren` are Equity `{"USD":"-3490.52"}`, Liabilities `{"USD":"-2703.29"}`, Income `{"USD":"-357944.58","IRAUSD":"-54500","VACHR":"-355"}`, Expenses `{"USD":"254609.05","IRAUSD":"54500.00","VACHR":"368"}`, Assets `{"USD":"120352.77828","VACHR":"-13"}`. These sum to USD 10823.44, and Equity has no Unrealized line. With `"units"`, Assets holds bare commodity units (`GLD 17`, `ITOT 104`, …) against USD costs elsewhere, so the per-unit sum can never be zero.

## Root cause

- `backend-cluster/ledger/src/features/ledger/service/ledger-finance-service.ts` `getTrialBalance` (~:288) builds the five trees directly from the converted directives. That is deliberate and matches Fava's trial balance. `getBalanceSheet` (~:238–246) alone applies `balanceSheetCap`, which adds the Conversions and Unrealized balancing lines.
- `dashboard/src/features/reports/trial-balance/trial-balance-content.tsx:152–165` passes those uncapped roots to `computeTrialBalanceReconciliation` (`lib/trial-balance-summary.ts`) for every conversion, then labels the result as a reconciliation difference. Only the at-cost basis is a debits-equal-credits check. Market value and currency conversion differ by the unrealized gain, and units cannot be summed across commodities.

w4/267 (done) fixed the display-sign version of this row. This is a separate cause: the conversion.

## Fix

The fix is in `dashboard` only. Keep the producer unchanged on REST, GraphQL and MCP, since it matches Fava's uncapped trial balance.

- Always compute the Reconciliation Difference row from at-cost root balances for the same ledger, time, account and filter scope. When the displayed conversion is not `at_cost`, read that basis with a second `GetLedgerTrialBalance` (`conversion: "at_cost"`). Apollo serves it from cache once At Cost has been viewed. Keep the displayed rows in the selected conversion.
- When the displayed conversion is not at cost, label the row as checked at cost (for example "Reconciliation Difference (at cost)"), in all supported locales.
- While the at-cost read is loading, or if it fails, show no difference rather than a provisional one.
- Keep exact per-unit summation and genuine nonzero at-cost differences unchanged. Do not silently force the row to zero.

## Acceptance

- On `open_ledger/example`, all time: the row reads zero/dash under At Cost, At Market Value, Converted to USD and Units, and is marked as checked at cost under the last three.
- A ledger with a real at-cost imbalance still shows its nonzero difference under every conversion.
- `trial-balance-content.test.tsx` covers a market-value payload whose uncapped roots differ by an unrealized gain while the at-cost roots balance. The test fails without the fix. The dashboard checks pass.
