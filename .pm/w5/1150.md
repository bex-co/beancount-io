# Ledger switcher: Enter on "Create Ledger" or "Manage ledgers" opens whichever list item is highlighted instead

**Severity:** major (keyboard access: the control runs a different action than its name). **Owner:** dashboard. **Estimate:** 20–30 minutes. **A2:** a keyboard user who opens the ledger switcher to create a second ledger should get the Create Ledger dialog, not be sent to their profile or into another ledger. **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` on 2026-11-18 against `https://beancount.io` (entry chunk `main-LGv2TZLj.js`), headless Chrome at 1440×1000 and 390×844, English/light. QA owner account on its designated disposable synthetic ledger. Read-only: no ledger was created. Local HEAD `73a6d7599`.

## Repro

1. Open any ledger page, for example `/ledger/<owner>/<ledger>/journal`. Activate the sidebar combobox **"<ledger> — switch ledger"**. The **Select a ledger** popover opens with focus in **Search ledgers...**. The first option, **Go to <owner>'s account**, is highlighted.
2. Press Tab. Focus moves to the **Create Ledger** button at the bottom of the popover. Press Enter.
3. Result: no dialog opens. The page navigates to `/ledger/<owner>`, the highlighted option's destination.
4. Fresh load. Open the switcher again, press ArrowDown once (option **example** is highlighted), Tab to **Create Ledger**, press Enter. The page navigates to `/ledger/<owner>/example/journal`, a different ledger.
5. Repeat with Tab twice to **Manage your Beancount ledgers** and Enter: same result, `/ledger/<owner>` or the highlighted ledger.

The same happens at 390×844 inside the mobile sidebar sheet. Each case was repeated from a fresh load.

Controls: a pointer click on **Create Ledger** opens **Create New Ledger**, and a click on **Manage your Beancount ledgers** goes to `/ledger`. Space on the focused **Create Ledger** button also opens the dialog. Only Enter is wrong.

## Cause

`dashboard/src/common/components/ledger-layout/ledger-switcher.tsx:374-397` renders both footer buttons inside the cmdk `<Command>` root (`:272-398`). The comment there says they are kept outside the scrollable list, but they are still inside the root. cmdk 1.1.1's root `onKeyDown` handles every Enter that bubbles to it: `case "Enter": e.preventDefault(); … item.dispatchEvent(SELECT_EVENT)` (`node_modules/cmdk/dist/index.mjs`). So the button's native activation is cancelled and the highlighted `CommandItem`'s `onSelect` runs instead (`handleSelectLedger`, or the owner-profile item).

The repository already knows this mechanism. The switcher's own read-failure **Try Again** (`:285-300`, w4/275) and Go to Account's retry (`go-to-account.tsx`, w4/135) both stop the Enter bubble with `if (event.key === "Enter") event.stopPropagation();`. The two footer buttons never got that guard. A scan of `<Command>` roots for nested buttons finds only these two files, so no other picker is affected.

## Fix

Give both footer buttons (or their wrapping `div`) the same Enter guard: stop propagation of Enter so the browser performs the button's own activation exactly once. Do not `preventDefault`. An equivalent fix is to move the footer outside the `<Command>` element. Keep pointer and Space behavior unchanged, and keep Enter on a list option selecting that option.

## Definition of done

- In the repro, Tab to **Create Ledger** and Enter opens **Create New Ledger** and the URL does not change, whatever option is highlighted. Enter on **Manage your Beancount ledgers** goes to `/ledger`.
- Enter on a highlighted ledger option still switches to that ledger, and Enter in the search box still selects the highlighted option.
- A rendered test with the real `Command` wrapper: with an option highlighted, Enter on the focused Create Ledger button opens the dialog and does not call the ledger-select handler. Same for Manage. The test fails without the guard. Dashboard gates pass.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` for cmdk, ledger switcher, switch ledger + Enter, Manage ledgers and Create Ledger. w4/done/135 fixed the identical mechanism for Go to Account's Try Again and listed "authenticated ledger switchers" as unverified. w4/done/275 added the switcher's retry with the guard. w4/done/252 covers active-descendant naming, and w3/done/478 the switcher's accessible name. None covers the footer actions. Not deployment lag: HEAD has no guard on these buttons.
