# Overview's "Top spending" lists a refund as spending, with a share of the month: a −12.00 USD Groceries refund reads "35.8%" under Money out 33.50 USD, and Restaurants 45.50 USD is held at "100%"

**Severity:** minor (a misleading summary on the ledger's first page). Money movement ranks a month's categories by the size of their amount, ignoring its sign. It divides each by the month's net total and caps the result at 100%. When a category nets to a refund that month (a return or a reimbursement booked back to the expense), three things go wrong. The refund is listed under **Top spending** with a spending bar. The shares add up to more than 100%. The largest real expense is cut to "100%" because it is larger than the net total. Money in has the same rule, so a reversed income category would be listed as a top source. **Owner:** `dashboard` (`features/reports/overview`). **Estimate:** about 45 minutes, including tests. **A2:** a newcomer who returns a purchase sees the return ranked among the month's biggest expenses, with a share that cannot be true, on the page that is meant to explain the month at a glance.

## Repro

1. On a ledger with the fixture's accounts, add two entries in the current month (here November 2026):

   ```beancount
   2026-11-01 * "qa-20261128-Smith, John" "qa-20261128 dinner"
     Assets:Bank:Checking        -45.50 USD
     Expenses:Food:Restaurants    45.50 USD

   2026-11-02 * "qa-20261128-Acme" "qa-20261128 refund"
     Assets:Bank:Checking         12.00 USD
     Expenses:Food:Groceries     -12.00 USD
   ```

2. Open the ledger's Overview. The month stepper reads **November 2026 · through 2026-11-02**.
3. **Money out** reads **33.50 USD**, which is correct (45.50 − 12.00). Under **Top spending**:
   - `Expenses:Food:Restaurants` **45.50 USD**, bar full, **100%**. That is 136% of the 33.50 total.
   - `Expenses:Food:Groceries` **−12.00 USD**, an orange spending bar, **35.8%** (12.00 / 33.50).

The listed shares add up to 135.8%. Repeated from a fresh load, with a screenshot kept locally. The Cash Flow diagram below the cards already shows the refund as money coming in (w4/m54).

From the code, a refund larger than the month's net spending is listed first at 100%. For example, Money out 80.00 USD with a −120.00 USD `Expenses:Shopping` return puts the return at the top of Top spending. This case was not run.

**Expected:** Top spending lists only categories that are spending that month (a positive amount on the Money out side), largest first. Each share is of the month's gross spending in the card's unit, the sum of those categories, so the shares add up to at most 100% and none needs a cap. In the repro, Top spending lists `Expenses:Food:Restaurants 45.50 USD 100%` alone, and Money out still reads 33.50 USD. A month without refunds looks exactly as it does today. Money in follows the same rule for income.

## Cause

- `dashboard/src/features/reports/overview/lib/overview-utils.ts:359-409` (`getMovementSnapshot`) keeps every category with a nonzero amount, sets `sortValue: Math.abs(…)` (`:393`) and sorts by it (`:398`). A refund therefore ranks by its size.
- `dashboard/src/features/reports/overview/components/money-movement-section.tsx:124` sets `monthTotal = Math.abs(net total)`. `:131-133` takes the first three categories. `:171-174` sets `share = Math.min(sortValue / monthTotal, 1)`, and `ShareMeter` (`components/share-meter.tsx:22`) clamps it again. The denominator is the net, while the rows are gross amounts.
- Working control: the Financial position card's `sharesByAccount` (`components/account-balances-card.tsx:53-64`) divides by the sum of the rows' sizes, so its shares add up to 100%.

## Fix

Owned by `dashboard`, in `getMovementSnapshot` and `MovementCard`.

- Rank and list only categories whose comparable amount is positive in the card's direction: expenses as booked for Money out, income inverted for Money in.
- Compute each share against the sum of those positive categories in the comparable unit, and drop the `Math.min(…, 1)` cap.
- The card's headline total stays the net. A refund still lowers it and still shows in **View all** (the Income Statement).
- Categories without the comparable unit keep today's rule: no share.

## Definition of done

- In the repro, Top spending shows only `Expenses:Food:Restaurants 45.50 USD` at 100%, and Money out reads 33.50 USD.
- With Restaurants 45.50 and Groceries 20.00 (no refund), the shares read 69.5% and 30.5%, as today.
- A month whose only expense category nets to a refund shows "No money movement for this period." under Top spending, or an equivalent empty state, and never a refund row with a share.
- Unit tests on `getMovementSnapshot` and a render test of the card fail on the current code. They cover the refund-excluded list, shares that sum to 100% or less, and the same rule on the Money in side.

## Adjacent states

- Multi-currency months: the share stays limited to rows in the total's unit (the `inUnit` rule at `money-movement-section.tsx:128-133`).
- The 3-month average and the above/below-average line use the net totals and do not change.
- A month with no comparable unit keeps listing what it has, without shares.
