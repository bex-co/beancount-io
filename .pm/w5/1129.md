# Budget "All time" chart and period table stop at the ledger's last transaction

**Severity:** minor. **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` on 2026-11-15 against `https://beancount.io` (local HEAD `a27546bcd`; the code below is unchanged at HEAD, so this is not deployment lag), headless Chromium at 1440×1000, signed in as the QA account, on the designated disposable ledger (restored byte-for-byte afterwards).

## Repro

1. On a ledger whose last transaction is in August 2026 (today is 2026-11-15), open `/ledger/<owner>/<ledger>/budget`.
2. Add Budget: date 2026-08-01, account `Expenses:Food:Groceries`, Monthly, 1000 USD.
3. Leave the time span on **All time** and open **Period data** on the card.

**Actual:** the headline says Current budget 1,000.00 USD, Latest actual 0.00 USD, Variance -1,000.00 USD, which describes November. The chart and the Period data table end at `2026-08-31 | 1,000.00 USD | 180.85 USD`. September, October and November are missing, although the budget is active in all three and nothing was spent. So the table has no row for the period the headline describes, and the reader cannot see the months furthest under budget.

**Controls (same card, same session):** **This year** lists every month through `2026-12-31` (Sep–Dec at 1,000.00 budget / 0.00 actual). **Last 12 months** runs through `2026-11-15` (prorated 500.00).

## Root cause

`budget-chart-card.tsx` builds `periodRows` (chart and table) only from `getLedgerIntervalTotals` buckets. With no `time` (All time), the ledger service's `interval-series.ts` follows Fava and spans only the transaction date range: an unfiltered query for `Expenses:Food:Groceries` at monthly returns its last bucket at `2026-08-31`, and with `time: "year"` it returns buckets through `2026-12-31`. `budgetHeadlinePeriod` already treats a missing current bucket as 0, which is why the headline and the table disagree.

## Fix

Owned by `dashboard` (`src/features/ledger-data/budget/`). Under All time, extend the card's periods through the period that contains today, with zero actuals and the effective target from `calculateBudgetForInterval`. Do this either by padding the empty trailing buckets on the client or by requesting a `time` range that ends today. Leave the Fava-faithful interval series in `backend-cluster/ledger` unchanged; other reports depend on it.

## Acceptance

- In the repro, All time lists 2026-09-30, 2026-10-31 and 2026-11-30 at 1,000.00 budget / 0.00 actual, and the chart plots them. The last row is the period the headline describes.
- This year, Last year and Last 12 months render as they do today.
- A dashboard test covers a budget whose account's last activity is before the current period, and it fails against the current code.
