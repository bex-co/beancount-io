# Overview Net Worth change badge: the percentage share fails text contrast again after the redesign

**Severity:** minor (text contrast). **Owner:** dashboard. **Estimate:** 15–25 minutes. **A2:** readers with reduced contrast sensitivity should be able to read how much their net worth changed. This regresses `w4/done/109` and was introduced by the Overview redesign in `aabf2eced` (2026-11-16). **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` on 2026-11-16 against `https://beancount.io`, using headless Chromium at 1440×1000 in English/light. axe-core 4.14.1 ran with the WCAG 2 A/AA rules. Local HEAD `85c2548ba`. The check was read-only.

## Repro

1. Signed out, open `/ledger/open_ledger/example`. Signed in, the QA disposable ledger's Overview shows the same badge.
2. The Net Worth badge reads `+<amount> USD · +1.8%`. On the QA ledger it reads `… · +15.3%`.
3. axe `color-contrast` (serious) flags the `· +x%` span: foreground `#2a9271` on background `#e1f4ec`, 14px, normal weight, ratio **3.36:1**. Normal-sized text needs 4.5:1. The amount next to it, in the same badge, passes.

Dark mode passes: axe reports no contrast violations on the same page with `prefers-color-scheme: dark`.

## Cause

`dashboard/src/features/reports/overview/components/net-worth-card.tsx:572` wraps the share in `<span className="font-normal opacity-80">`. This sits inside the badge from `:551-557` (`bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-700`, and for a fall `bg-rose-500/10 text-rose-700`). `w4/109` chose `text-emerald-700` because it measured 5.36:1 on the plain card, and that fix assumed a white surface and full opacity. The redesign added both the tinted badge background and the 80% opacity, which brings the share below 4.5:1.

## Fix

Remove `opacity-80` from the share span. If it still needs to look secondary, use a tone that measures at least 4.5:1 on the tinted badge in light mode, for both the rising and the falling variant. Keep the dark-mode colors, signs, icons and arithmetic unchanged.

## Definition of done

- In light mode, axe reports no `color-contrast` violation on `/ledger/open_ledger/example` for the badge, and the share measures at least 4.5:1 on the rendered badge background.
- The falling (rose) variant is measured the same way. Reproduce it on a period where net worth fell, or measure the computed colors in a rendered test.
- The test or guard covering the w4/109 fix checks the share span as well as the amount, so another opacity or tint change cannot pass unnoticed.
