# Holdings prices a lot from the ledger's own purchases: 10 VTI bought at 180 EUR reads "Price 190.00, Market Value 1,900.00 EUR, +5.56%" when no VTI/EUR price exists, while Balance Sheet, the holdings API and `bea` value it at cost (gap in w1/229)

**Severity:** major. The Holdings page is where a reader checks what their investments are worth, and it invents a quote and a gain from their own later purchase. The same app's Balance Sheet (**At Market Value**), the account page, and the holdings read behind REST, MCP and `bea report holdings` all say the position has no price. **Owner:** `dashboard`. **Estimate:** 45–60 minutes including tests. **A2:** a newcomer who records two purchases of a fund and no `price` lines (the usual first month) sees "+4.6% unrealized" on Holdings that is only the difference between their own two buy prices, and a different total on the Balance Sheet. **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` (`dashboard-qa` routine) on 2026-11-27 against `https://beancount.io`, production entry chunk `main-BBmi61Hs.js`. QA-owned headless Chromium 158, isolated context, 1440×1000, English, signed in as the QA owner of a disposable `qa-` synthetic ledger. Read-only: no ledger writes. Local HEAD `dd228d85f`.

The only `price` directive for VTI is in USD. There is none in EUR, and none for VEUR at all.

1. Open `/ledger/<owner>/<ledger>/holdings`. The `Assets:BrokerEur` row `10 VTI` reads **Cost 180.00 · Price 190.00 · Book Value 1800.00 EUR · Market Value 1900.00 EUR · Unrealized Profit 5.56**. `Assets:Mixed 3 VTI {180 EUR}` reads Price 190.00, Market Value 570.00 EUR, 5.56. **Holdings by Currency** gives 27 VTI (EUR cost) a Price of 190.00 and a Market Value of 5,130.00 EUR against a 4,900.00 EUR book value.
2. On the same ledger, any lot with no quote in its cost currency shows its own cost as the price: `1 QAMIRROR {10.00 EUR}`, which has USD quotes only, reads Price 10.00. `10 VEUR {50.00 EUR}`, which has no quote at all, reads Price 50.00. Each reads 0% gain and nothing says the price is missing.
3. Open **Balance Sheet** and choose **At Market Value**. `Assets:BrokerEur` reads **2,300.00 EUR** (500 cash plus the 1,800 cost), where Holdings adds the same two rows to 2,400 EUR. The account page `/account/Assets:BrokerEur` at **At Market Value** also reads 2,300.00 EUR for every month.
4. API control (GraphQL `getLedgerHoldings(conversion: "units")`, which REST, MCP and `bea report holdings` share): the `Assets:BrokerEur` VTI group has `price: null` and `marketValue 1800 EUR`. The read reports `valuation: "partial"`, `missingPrices: [QAMIRROR→EUR, VEUR→EUR, VTI→EUR]` and `asOf: 2026-11-27`.
5. Where the 190 comes from, through the page's own BQL path (`queryShell`): `getprice('VTI','EUR', today())` = **190.00**, `getprice('VTI','EUR', 2026-07-02)` = **180.00**, `getprice('VTI','EUR', 2026-06-30)` = empty. `getprice('VTI','USD', 2026-10-20)` = **200.00**, from the `{200.00 USD}` lot, the day before the real 210 USD quote. `SELECT date, currency, amount FROM prices` lists no VTI/EUR row. The engine's BQL price database derives a price from every cost-booked posting, so a purchase newer than the last quote also replaces that quote on Holdings.

Public ledgers do not show it today, because every costed commodity in `open_ledger/example`, `stock-example` and `crypto-example` has a `price` directive newer than its last purchase. Comparing the page's `getprice` against `getLedgerHoldings` for each lot found no difference there.

## Cause

`dashboard/src/features/ledger-data/holdings/holdings-statement.ts:21,27,29,40,50,52,62` values every Holdings view with BQL `getprice(currency, cost_currency, today())` and `value(sum(position), today())`, which `holdings-table.tsx:144` sends through `QueryShellDocument`. That BQL price database includes the engine's implied prices from `{cost}` and `@ price` postings (upstream rustledger behaviour, recorded in w1/229 and w3/113).

w1/229 (done) resolved this for every other holdings surface through `w23/m41/t004` (`b8b316107`). `backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/features/ledger/utils/holdings.ts` asks the engine only for units and cost. It reads quotes from `HOLDINGS_PRICES_QUERY` (`:97`, `FROM prices`, which lists directives only), and `valueHoldingRows` (`:226`) takes the latest directive on or before today for the pair or its inverse. A lot with no directive is valued at cost and reported as a missing leg. The statements and the account page use the directive-only price map too. The dashboard Holdings page was not moved, and `holdings-statement.ts` has not changed since `291b90977` (2026-10-23).

## Fix

Holdings values a row the way `getLedgerHoldings` does. Its price comes from the ledger's `price` directives only: the latest on or before today, either direction of the pair. A lot without one shows no price, a market value equal to its book value and no gain, and the page says which pairs have no price, as the read's `missingPrices` does. Two ways to get there:

- (a) Keep the BQL rows for units, cost, book value and acquisition date. Drop `getprice` and `value` from them, read `SELECT date, currency, amount FROM prices ORDER BY date`, and compute price, market value and gain in the dashboard with the exact-decimal helpers.
- (b) Read `getLedgerHoldings` for the by-account and by-currency views, if its groups can carry the per-lot detail the Holdings view shows.

Both are dashboard-only. Do not import backend code: packages stay independent. Keep the explicit-quote cases unchanged. `Mixed 4 VTI {200 USD}` stays at 210 USD, `1 BTC {30000 USD}` at 60000 USD, `QACUTOFF` at today's 99 EUR and not its future 1000 EUR quote, and `40 ETH {BTC}` at 0.05 BTC.

## Definition of done

On a ledger with the repro entries, all four Holdings views show no price for the VTI-in-EUR, VEUR and QAMIRROR lots. They show `Assets:BrokerEur` VTI at a 1,800.00 EUR market value, and an unpriced-pairs notice naming VTI→EUR, VEUR→EUR and QAMIRROR→EUR. Every lot with a quote keeps its current figures. Holdings, Balance Sheet **At Market Value** and `getLedgerHoldings` agree on each row's market value. A rendered test feeds a later `{cost}` purchase and a quote-less lot through the page's data path and fails on the current statements. Dashboard gates pass.
