# Overview's Recent activity names a transfer by its accounts' last segments only: "Transfer · Checking · Checking"

**Severity:** minor (readability). **Owner:** dashboard. **Estimate:** 20–30 minutes including tests. **A2:** each Recent activity row says where the money moved by the last segment of its first two accounts. When those segments match, the row names no account at all. Two banks' checking accounts (`Assets:Bank1:Checking` → `Assets:Bank2:Checking`) or two brokers' `Cash` are common. The redesign already keeps full names in Financial position and Money movement, and qualifies duplicate leaves in the Cash Flow Sankey. **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` on 2026-11-18 against `https://beancount.io` (entry chunk `main-LGv2TZLj.js`), headless Chromium at 1440×1000, English, isolated profile, signed in as the QA owner on a disposable QA ledger. Local HEAD `c12e43044`. The probe entry was removed afterwards, and the file was restored byte for byte.

## Repro

1. On a ledger you can write to, add
   ```beancount
   2026-10-06 * "qa-transfer" "Move to bank two"
     Assets:Qa:Bank2:Checking    100.00 USD
     Assets:Qa:Bank1:Checking   -100.00 USD
   ```
   (with the two accounts opened), then open the Overview.
2. **Actual:** the row reads "qa-transfer · Move to bank two · **Transfer · Checking · Checking** · 100.00 USD". The row's button text, which is what a screen reader announces, says the same.
3. The same happens with leaves that match under one root. On the QA ledger's cap table, vesting entries (`Equity:CapTable:Options:Unvested:Carol` → `…:Vested:Carol`) read "Transfer · Carol · Carol", and an option exercise reads "Carol · Carol".
4. Control: rows whose leaves differ ("Slate · Restaurant", "Checking · Salary" on `open_ledger/example`) are fine.

**Expected:** when the shown accounts share a last segment, each gets enough of its path to tell them apart. For example "Bank2:Checking · Bank1:Checking", the shortest distinguishing suffix, or the parent segment muted the way `account-name.tsx` styles full names. A row whose leaves differ stays as it is.

## Cause

`dashboard/src/features/reports/overview/components/recent-activity-card.tsx:203-206` maps `summary.accounts.slice(0, 2)` through `name.split(":").pop()`. Nothing checks the two leaves for a collision. Working controls: `cash-flow-sankey.tsx:73-87` (`sankeyLabel`, "qualified duplicate leaves", from `aabf2eced`) and the full-name `AccountName` in Financial position. Last changed in `c97ffa8af` (`dir="auto"` truncation only), so production matches `main`.

## Fix

Owned by `dashboard`. Add a small pure helper that, given the displayed account names, returns for each the shortest suffix of `:`-segments that differs from the others (falling back to the full name). Use it for the row's account summary. Keep `dir="auto"` and the truncation rules (w2/138).

## Definition of done

- The transfer above reads "Transfer · Bank2:Checking · Bank1:Checking", or an equivalent that names both banks. The vesting row names Vested and Unvested.
- "Slate · Restaurant" rows are unchanged.
- A unit test of the helper covers equal leaves, equal leaves at different depths, three-way collisions and no collision. A rendered Recent activity test asserts the transfer row's text. Both fail on the current code. Dashboard gates pass.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**`, including `done/` and `blocked/`, for Recent activity with leaf, last segment, duplicate or same name, "Checking · Checking" and "Cash · Cash". w4/done/1034 (exact posting sums), w4/done/302 (unique cards) and w4/done/105 (pie tooltips with full identities) are other defects. w3/done/183 and w4/done/014 are mobile truncation fixes.
