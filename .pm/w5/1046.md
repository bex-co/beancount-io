# Skip incompatible currencies in partial realized-gain arithmetic

**Minor · cli · 25–35m · A1 · worker1.** With `--allow-errors`, Capital
Gains subtracts a **100USD** basis from **150EUR** proceeds and labels the
numeric difference **50USD**, adding it to realized-gain totals. The ledger
has the expected native parser warning about incompatible cost/price
currencies; strict refusal is correct. The opt-in partial path must keep
known sales readable without performing arithmetic between different units.
No product fix implemented.
