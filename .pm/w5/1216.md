# Commodities price charts space prices evenly by row and curve between them: a four-year gap takes one step, and this year's daily feed takes a quarter of the chart (gap in w5/1188)

**Severity:** minor. The chart is the Commodities page's picture of how a price moved over time, and it misstates that movement whenever prices are not evenly spaced, which a managed price feed guarantees. **Owner:** `dashboard`. **Estimate:** 30–45 minutes including tests. **A2:** a reader checking how a holding's price developed sees a false shape and false dates. **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` (`dashboard-qa` routine) on 2026-11-26 against `https://beancount.io`, production entry chunks `main-B-XRW1Dk.js` and `main-BBmi61Hs.js` (deployed during the run). QA-owned headless Chromium 158, isolated context, 1440×1000, English, signed in as the QA owner. Read-only: no ledger writes. Local HEAD `78eb9e1da`.

## Repro

1. A ledger with uneven price dates, for example:

   ```beancount
   2026-01-01 commodity QAPX
   2026-07-15 price QAPX  20.00 EUR
   2026-08-20 price QAPX  99.00 EUR
   2026-12-31 price QAPX 1000.00 EUR
   ```

   Open `/ledger/<owner>/<ledger>/commodities`. The QAPX/EUR card ("Price history with 3 data points") puts the three points at equal x steps under the labels 2026-07, 2026-08, 2026-12. The 36-day and the 133-day gap are the same width, and the line is a rising curve through values the price never had, though the 99.00 EUR price held until 2026-12-31. This was observed on a QA-owned disposable ledger holding exactly these three prices.
2. A ledger with its own weekly prices from 2015-01-02 to 2017-09-08 that also includes a managed GLD/USD feed (the feed adds 3 prices in 2021 from 2021-10-29, 12 a year for 2022–2024, 18 in 2025 and 66 so far in 2026; 264 points in total, read through `getLedgerCommodities`). The GLD/USD chart's x-axis reads 2015-01, 2015-09, 2016-05, 2017-01, 2022-01, 2025-01, 2026-05, 2026-11. The 1,512 days from 2017-09-08 to 2021-10-29 take one step, the width of one week in 2015, and draw as a near-vertical cliff. The 66 points of 2026 take a quarter of the chart's width and the three years 2022–2024 about a seventh.

The public `open_ledger/example` has weekly prices with no gap, so the distortion is not visible there.

Expected: the x-axis is time. Points sit at their dates, spacing is proportional to elapsed days, and the line holds each price until the next one (step after), because a Beancount price is in effect from its date until the next price for the pair. The Query page's date charts were corrected to exactly this in w5/1188 (`e481b3c70`).

## Cause

- `dashboard/src/features/ledger-data/commodities/index.tsx:72-79` builds `xAxis.type: "category"` with one category per price (`data: dates`), and `:101` sets `smooth: true` on the line. The tooltip reads the category name (`:58-59`).
- `build-commodity-chart-labels.ts` already produces `[date, value]` pairs (`chartData`), which a time axis can plot as they are.

## Fix

- Plot on an ECharts `time` x-axis with `[date, value]` points, parsing the ledger's `yyyy-MM-dd` as a UTC calendar date (see `dashboard/AGENTS.md` on ledger dates). Reuse what w5/1188 did in `features/bql/lib/chart-utils.ts` (`type: "time"`, `minInterval` of a day, UTC axis-pointer label) rather than writing a second variant.
- Draw the line and area stepped (`step: "end"`), not smoothed.
- Key the tooltip on the hovered point's own date and exact source decimal (w4/1003), not on the category name. Keep the w4/1067 tiny-value scaling, the w4/170 confined tooltip and the narrow-screen layout (w4/087).
- While in the same option, note that the y-axis `min`/`max` functions (`index.tsx:91-92`, `min * 0.8` / `max * 1.1`) print their raw bounds as extra labels: `532.13` just above `500.00`, `187.48` overlapping `180.00`, `1100.00` above `1000.00`. A fix should not leave a bound label colliding with the nearest nice tick (for example, hide the min/max labels or round the bounds to the tick interval).
- Tests in `features/ledger-data/commodities/__tests__/`, using the real ECharts runtime harness from `commodity-tiny-axis.test.tsx`: the three-price repro maps the 36-day and 133-day gaps to x distances in the ratio of the days (about 1 : 3.7), not equal steps; the series is stepped; the tooltip for each point shows its own date and exact price. Each fails on the current code.

## Definition of done

- With repro 1, the QAPX/EUR chart places 2026-08-20 at about 21% of the way from 2026-07-15 to 2026-12-31, the line steps at each date, and hovering each point shows its date and `20.00`, `99.00` or `1000.00 EUR`.
- With repro 2, the 2017-09-08 → 2021-10-29 gap takes about 35% of the plot width (1,512 of 4,345 days) and 2026's points about 7.5%.
- No y-axis bound label overlaps a tick label at 1440×1000 or 390×844. Dashboard gates pass.

## Blast radius

`CommodityChart` has one caller, the Commodities page. The other date charts use evenly spaced period buckets (the Account page line chart, statement and budget charts, Overview month charts), where a category axis is correct. The Query page was fixed by w5/1188. The mobile app's price charts were not checked (another package).

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**`, including `done/` and `blocked/`, for commodities with axis, chart, category, time axis, smooth, step and evenly. w5/1188 (done) fixed the same defect for Query results only and scoped other charts out. w4/done/1003, 1066, 1067 and 170 concern this chart's tiny-value ticks and tooltip; w4/done/087 its narrow layout; w4/done/332 and w4/done/1097 refresh behavior; w5/blocked/1136 managed-feed status on public ledgers. None touches the x-axis or the line shape, and `main` still has the category axis and `smooth: true`, so this is not deployment lag.

**Unverified:** right-to-left locales, dark theme, a pair with a single price.
