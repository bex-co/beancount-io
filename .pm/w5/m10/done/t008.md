## Result

`skills/scripts/test_verify_migration.py` now has 18 tests in the skills CI entrypoint, with no paid client calls.

- **Fixture integrity:** the export is identical to the eval's, the IDs are recomputed independently from the documented hash input, and the anchors are checked arithmetically.
- **Guide consistency:** every checkpoint is run by the guide, and the copied inputs and approved amounts match the fixture.
- **Real `bea import` runs:** overlaps from both transfer sides write nothing, and new activity is written exactly once.
- **Negative cases, each of which fails when its behavior breaks:** a missing row, a transfer duplicated or booked from both sides, a dropped `import-id-2`, a pad adjustment, a fabricated residual, a conflicting account asserted anyway, a costed posting, and a missing or stale `--before` snapshot.
- **Mutation check:** removing the transfer-identity check, the cost guard, or a guide checkpoint makes a test fail.

beancount-migrate eval 7 covers the identities later imports depend on. The real-client rehearsals stay separately evidenced in `skills/docs/migration-rehearsal.md`. No agent-facing instruction changed after them, and all four rehearsal workspaces re-verify with the refactored verifier.
