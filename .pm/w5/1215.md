# Account names in the Journal and on Overview break across two lines with the last part on the first: "Assets:US:  :Cash" over "ETrade" (regression of w5/1195)

**Severity:** minor. Nothing is computed wrongly, but the account name, the one thing a reader scans the Journal's Account column for, reads as `Assets:US:` … `:Cash` with `ETrade` dropped onto a second line under it. **Owner:** `dashboard`. **Estimate:** 20–30 minutes including tests. **A2:** a newcomer reading their brokerage account's journal cannot read which account each row moved. **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` (`dashboard-qa` routine) on 2026-11-26 against `https://beancount.io`, production entry chunk `main-B-XRW1Dk.js` and again on `main-BBmi61Hs.js` (deployed during the run). QA-owned headless Chromium 158, isolated contexts, 1440×1000 and 390×844, English. Reproduced signed out on the public ledger. Local HEAD `78eb9e1da`. Read-only: no ledger writes.

## Repro

Public ledger, no sign-in needed.

1. At 1440×1000 open `/ledger/open_ledger/example/journal?account=Assets:US:ETrade`.
2. The 2017-08-27 "Buy shares of VHT" row's Account cell reads `Assets:US:` then a gap then `:Cash`, `→`, `Expenses:` then a gap then `:Commissions`, `+1` on the first line, and `ETrade` and `Financial` alone on a second line. Every three- and four-posting row on the page does the same: 48 account names on the first page are two lines tall. The account's path element measures 40px high at a 20px line height.
3. At 390×844 open `/ledger/open_ledger/example`. In Account balances, `Assets:US:Vanguard:RGAGX` reads `Assets:US:` + `:RGAGX` with `Vanguard` on the line below. So do `Assets:US:Vanguard:VBMPX` and `Expenses:Taxes:Y2017:US:Federal`.

Screen readers are unaffected: the visually hidden full name is still read once.

Expected (the contract in `AccountName`'s own doc comment): one line, the path cut first with an ellipsis and the last segment whole, e.g. `Assets:US:ETr…:Cash`. Before `3fb341eaf` the path was plain text in the same `truncate` span, which cuts it that way.

## Cause

- `3fb341eaf` (w5/1195, right-to-left account paths) introduced `AccountPath` (`dashboard/src/common/components/account-name.tsx:6-33`). It writes `:<wbr />` after every colon so that paths may wrap after a colon (`account-name.tsx:20`).
- The same commit made `AccountName` render its muted path through `AccountPath` inside a `truncate` span (`account-name.tsx:72-75`). `truncate` is `white-space: nowrap; overflow: hidden; text-overflow: ellipsis`, but Chromium still breaks at a `<wbr>` under `nowrap`. Measured in the page: a 60px-wide `nowrap` span holding `Assets:<wbr>US:<wbr>ETrade` is 48px tall (3 lines); the same text without `<wbr>` is 16px tall and ellipsized. The leaf sits in its own `shrink-0` box beside the path's first line, so the parent's last segment ends up under it.
- jsdom has no layout, so `common/components/__tests__/account-name.test.tsx` could not see it.

## Fix

- `AccountName` renders its path with no break opportunity, so `truncate` cuts it with an ellipsis again. For example, give `AccountPath` an option (default on) for the `<wbr>` after each colon, and have `AccountName` turn it off. Keep the per-segment `dir="auto"` isolation from w5/1195 for paths with right-to-left letters.
- The callers that want a wrapped path keep it: the narrow Journal's balance-account line (`features/journal/components/journal-lines.tsx:406`), Journal postings (`journal-postings.tsx:73,76`), the narrow Accounts parent path (`features/ledger-data/accounts/index.tsx:440`), Budget's narrow path (`features/ledger-data/budget/budget-summary.tsx:337`) and Cash Flow's account list (`features/reports/cash-flow/cash-account-status-panel.tsx:106`).
- Test: `AccountName`'s rendered path contains no `wbr` element, and `AccountPath` by default still has one after each colon. The first assertion fails on the current code.

## Definition of done

- On `/ledger/open_ledger/example/journal?account=Assets:US:ETrade` at 1440×1000, every account name in the Account column is one line high, its path ellipsized and its last segment whole. On `/ledger/open_ledger/example` at 390×844, Account balances shows `Assets:US:Vangu…:RGAGX` on one line.
- The wrapping callers listed above still break after a colon at 390px, and the w5/1195 right-to-left tests still pass. Dashboard gates pass.

## Blast radius

`AccountName` callers, all affected wherever their cell is narrower than the name: the Journal's desktop account columns (`journal-lines.tsx:425,460,468`, `journal-days.tsx:438`), Overview's Account balances (`features/reports/overview/components/account-balances-card.tsx:214`) and Money movement (`money-movement-section.tsx:194`), and Budget (`features/ledger-data/budget/budget-chart-card.tsx:341`, `budget-summary.tsx:321`). Wrapping measured on production: the account-filtered Journal at 1440px (48 names) and Overview at 390px (3 names). On a QA-owned copy of the example ledger, the unfiltered Journal, Accounts, Budget and Cash Flow had no name long enough for its cell at 1440, 1024 or 390px.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**`, including `done/` and `blocked/`, for `wbr`, `AccountPath`, `AccountName`, `truncate` with account, and wrapped/two-line account names. w5/1195 (done) introduced the markup; w5/1208 (done) changed which accounts a Journal row names, not how a name is laid out; w5/1196 is horizontal page scroll. No open item covers it, and `main` still renders `<wbr>` inside the truncated path, so this is not deployment lag.

**Unverified:** Safari/WebKit and Firefox (the `<wbr>`-under-`nowrap` break was measured in Chromium only), dark theme, right-to-left locales after a fix.
