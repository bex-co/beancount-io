# Saving your name in Account Settings says "Ledger updated successfully" (German "Hauptbuch erfolgreich aktualisiert"), though no ledger changed

**Severity:** minor (wrong feedback after a write). **Owner:** `dashboard`. **Estimate:** 20–30 minutes including a rendered test and 15 locale entries. **A2:** a newcomer filling in their profile is told that a *ledger* changed. They may then go looking for the ledger they think they edited, or wonder whether their name was saved at all. The same component's Username save already has its own message ("Username updated successfully"). **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` (`dashboard-qa` routine) on 2026-11-28 against `https://beancount.io`, production entry chunk `main-BBmi61Hs.js`, local HEAD `4b8a920fe`. Isolated headless Chromium 158 at 1440×1000, English, signed in as the QA account.

## Repro

The account's profile was not changed. The run let the production client send `UpdateProfile`, answered that one request in the browser with a success response carrying the submitted name, and aborted any other mutation. Every read went to production.

1. Open `/settings/general`. Under **User Profile**, Name reads "Not set". Press Name's **Edit**.
2. Type `qa-20261128-name` in **First Name** and press **Save**.
3. The dialog closes and the toast reads **"Ledger updated successfully"**.
4. Reload. Name still reads "Not set" (nothing was written), and the toast text comes only from the client.

Control: the Username dialog on the same card uses `userSettings.usernameUpdatedSuccess` ("Username updated successfully").

## Cause

`dashboard/src/features/user-settings/pages/general/user-profile-section.tsx:130-131`: `updateProfile`'s `onCompleted` calls `toast.success(t("page.dashboard.ledgerUpdatedSuccess"))`. That key belongs to the dashboard sidebar's ledger edit dialog (`features/ledger-list/pages/dashboard-page/components/dashboard-sidebar.tsx:577`, defined in `features/ledger-list/pages/dashboard-page/locales/en.ts:83`). In every language it says a ledger was updated: German "Hauptbuch erfolgreich aktualisiert", Japanese "台帳が正常に更新されました". It also makes the settings page depend on another feature's private catalog key.

## Fix

- Add `userSettings.nameUpdatedSuccess` ("Name updated successfully", or "Profile updated") to `features/user-settings/locales/en.ts` and translate it in the 14 other catalogs. `yarn i18n:prepare` / `yarn i18n:check` must stay clean, with no English copies.
- Use it in `updateProfile`'s `onCompleted`. Leave the sidebar's ledger toast as it is.
- Test: a rendered `UserProfileSection` test that mocks a successful `UpdateProfile` and asserts the success toast is the name message, not the ledger one. It fails on current code.

## Definition of done

On `/settings/general`, saving a changed name shows a toast that names the name/profile and never mentions a ledger, in English and in at least one other language. Username and ledger-sidebar toasts are unchanged. Dashboard gates pass.

## Blast radius

`page.dashboard.ledgerUpdatedSuccess` has two callers: the sidebar ledger edit (correct) and this one (wrong). `UserProfileSection` is rendered only by `/settings/general`.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` (open, `done/`, `blocked/`) for "Ledger updated successfully", `ledgerUpdatedSuccess`, `UpdateProfile`, `user-profile-section` and "Change Name". w4/m47 (done) fixed the same dialog's pending-save lifecycle and w4/296 its IME Enter. Neither looked at the success toast. `git log -S ledgerUpdatedSuccess` shows the line unchanged since the dashboard was imported (`af5339de0`).
