# Choosing a conversion or interval on Balance Sheet, Income Statement, Cash Flow or Trial Balance drops keyboard focus to the page body, and an open "Period data" table closes (gap in w4/m16)

**Severity:** minor (accessibility, keyboard; WCAG 2.4.3). Nothing is computed wrongly, but each new conversion or interval the reader picks sends focus back to the top of the document. From there it takes 31 Tab presses on the desktop Balance Sheet to reach **Select conversion** again. Any **Period data** table the reader opened to read the figures also closes. **Owner:** `dashboard`. **Estimate:** 40–60 minutes including tests. **A2:** a newcomer comparing their books At Cost, At Market Value and by quarter is a keyboard or screen-reader user too. After every choice they lose their place and the table they opened. **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` (`dashboard-qa` routine) on 2026-11-27 against `https://beancount.io`, production entry chunk `main-BBmi61Hs.js`. QA-owned headless Chromium 158, fresh isolated contexts, 1440×1000 and 390×844, English. Reproduced signed out on the public ledger and signed in on a QA-owned `qa-` ledger. Read-only: no ledger writes. Local HEAD `9a7b7a3b9`.

## Repro

1. In a fresh signed-out tab, open `/ledger/open_ledger/example/balance-sheet`. Under the Net Worth chart, focus **Period data** and press Enter. The table opens.
2. Tab to **Select conversion** (31 presses from the top of the page). Press Enter, move to **Units** and press Enter. This tab has not read Units yet.
3. While `GetLedgerBalanceSheet(conversion: "units")` is in flight, the combobox is no longer in the document. After the read settles, the selector reads Units, `document.activeElement` is `<body>`, and the next Tab lands on **Skip to main content**. **Period data** is closed.
4. Reload and repeat with **Select time period**, going from Monthly to Quarterly. Focus again ends on `<body>` and Period data closes.

The same result on every run:

| Page | Change | Focus after the read | Period data |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Balance Sheet | At Cost → Units; Monthly → Quarterly/Weekly | body | closed |
| Income Statement | At Cost → At Market Value; Monthly → Quarterly/Weekly | body | closed |
| Cash Flow | → Converted to USD; Monthly → Quarterly/Weekly | body | closed |
| Trial Balance | Converted to USD → At Market Value | body | (no table) |
| Balance Sheet at 390×844 | Monthly → Yearly | body | — |
| Account page `/account/Assets:US:ETrade` | Monthly → Quarterly | stays on the select | closed |

**Controls:**
- Picking a conversion this tab has already read keeps focus on the select and the table open. Two examples: Balance Sheet Units → At Cost, and Trial Balance → At Cost. Only a read that has to wait shows the problem.
- The account page keeps focus on its selectors. Its pending state covers only the chart cards (see below), but its Period data still closes.
- On Overview, **Previous**/**Next** month keeps focus on the button, and an open Period data stays open.

## Cause

- `dashboard/src/features/reports/balance-sheet/index.tsx:106–107`, `income-statement/index.tsx:126–127`, `cash-flow/index.tsx:219–220` and `trial-balance/index.tsx:104–105` return `<ReportLoadingState />` whenever `selectSettledReportData` says the read is pending. The `IntervalSelect` and `ConversionSelect` the reader is using sit inside the content component that branch removes: `balance-sheet-content.tsx:250–254`, `income-statement-content.tsx:275–279`, `cash-flow-content.tsx:284–288`, `trial-balance-content.tsx:269`. So the focused trigger is unmounted. When Radix Select closes, it focuses a node that is no longer in the document, and focus falls to `<body>`. w4/m16 moved the selected chart tab above this branch for the same reason, but it left the controls inside.
- `dashboard/src/features/reports/components/chart-data-table.tsx:22` renders an uncontrolled `<details>`. Any unmount of the chart recreates it closed: the pending branch above, or the account page's `ReportLoadingIndicator` swap at `account/index.tsx:584` and `:625`. w4/1106 fixed the same loss for Budget's card with controlled state.
- Working control: `account/index.tsx:477–488` scopes the pending state to the chart cards "so the selectors stay usable".

## Fix

- On the four statement pages, keep the report toolbar mounted through a pending read: view tabs, interval, conversion, Hide charts and Export. Scope the pending state to the figures, as the account page does. Keep the w3/m35 guarantee that `selectSettledReportData` enforces: no old figures and no export appear under the newly chosen basis until its read settles.
- Hold each chart's Period data open/closed choice above the pending boundary, per report and chart, as w4/1106 does for Budget. A table the reader opened reopens with the new rows, and one they closed stays closed. Another ledger starts closed. Add no global store or persisted preference.

## Acceptance

- On Balance Sheet, Income Statement, Cash Flow and Trial Balance, choosing a conversion or interval this tab has not read, by keyboard, leaves focus on that select after the read settles, at 1440 and 390 widths. The next Tab reaches the control after it.
- While the read is pending, no figures or Export from the previous basis are shown. The existing pending-state tests still pass.
- A Period data table opened before the change is open after it, with the new rows, on the four statement pages and the account page. A closed one stays closed.
- Rendered tests drive the real content through an uncached pending → success transition and check focus and disclosure state, with a cached transition as control. They fail on the current code. Dashboard gates (`yarn format:check`, `yarn lint`, `yarn test`, `yarn build`) pass.

## Blast radius

`ChartDataTable` callers: `income-statement/date-balance-chart/single-date-balance-chart.tsx` and `stack-date-balance-chart.tsx`, `cash-flow/cash-flow-charts.tsx`, `balance-sheet/line-chart.tsx`, `account/index.tsx`, and three Overview components (`cash-flow-sankey.tsx`, `month-hero-chart.tsx`, `income-expenses-chart.tsx`). The Overview month change does not unmount them. Budget uses its own controlled disclosure since w4/1106.

- w4/m16 (done) kept the selected chart view across these same pending reads. Its findings name the pending branch but not focus or disclosures.
- w4/099 (done) scoped the account page's pending state to its charts. That is the working control here.
- w4/1106 (done) fixed Period data for Budget only.
- w4/1006 (done) is the narrow View selector hiding on resize.
