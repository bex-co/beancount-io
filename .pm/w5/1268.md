# Printing a page with a chart keeps the chart at its screen width: the account page prints at two-thirds size (6.5pt text) and Trial Balance cuts its treemap to two of seven blocks (gap in w5/1142)

**Severity:** minor. w5/1142 made non-statement pages flow across printed pages. A chart still prints at the width it had on screen. When that width is wider than the paper and the chart's card does not clip it, the browser shrinks the whole printout to fit the chart. When the card does clip it, the right-hand part of the chart is lost and nothing says so. **Owner:** `dashboard` (`common/components/ledger-layout/ledger-print.css`, `common/components/react-echarts`). **Estimate:** 30–45 minutes including a print-media test. **A2:** browser printing (Cmd/Ctrl+P → Save as PDF) is the only paper or PDF copy of the account page, Overview and Trial Balance. A reader who prints one gets small type or a chart with accounts missing.

**Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` (`dashboard-qa` routine) on 2026-11-28 against `https://beancount.io`, entry chunk `main-BBmi61Hs.js`. The run used isolated headless Chromium 158 at 1440×1000, English/light, browser zone America/Mexico_City, signed in as the QA owner. Everything below is read-only on the public `open_ledger/example`. Printing used Playwright's `page.pdf()` (Letter), which renders print media as the print dialog does, as in w5/1142. Local HEAD `8a576c696`. No dashboard file has changed since, so this is not deployment lag.

## Repro

1. Open `/ledger/open_ledger/example/trial-balance?time=2016` (charts shown, Assets tab). On screen, the **Assets Hierarchy** treemap is 1094px wide and shows RGAGX, VBMPX, ITOT, VEA, VHT, GLD and Checking.
2. Print it to PDF. Page 1's treemap shows RGAGX and part of VBMPX. ITOT, VEA, VHT, GLD and Checking, the right third of the chart, are cut off at the card's edge. Body text stays at 10.5pt.
3. Open `/ledger/open_ledger/example/account/Assets:US:ETrade` (the Account Balance chart is 1086px wide on screen) and print it. The PDF is scaled down to fit the chart. The most common body sizes are 7.0pt and 6.5pt, and the heading `Assets:US:ETrade` is smaller than 14pt. The Journal's printout (`/journal`, no chart) uses 10.5pt and 9.8pt with an 18pt heading. Sizes were read from the PDF text spans.
4. `/ledger/open_ledger/example` (Overview) prints the same way. The month chart's last bar pair ("Sep") is cut at the right edge, and so are the Cash Flow Sankey's right-hand labels ("Assets · ETrade 4,353.0…").

Each case was repeated from a fresh load. A probe attached a `ResizeObserver` to every `[_echarts_instance_]` and a window `resize` listener. Neither fired during `page.pdf()`, and the canvases kept `width: 1094px` / `1086px`.

**Control.** The same pages printed with one injected print rule give the expected output. The rule sizes each chart root, its first wrapper and its canvases to `width: 100%; height: auto; position: static`. With it, the account printout's body text is back to 10.5pt and 9.8pt with an 18pt heading, and the Trial Balance treemap shows all seven blocks, scaled to the card.

## Cause

- `dashboard/src/common/components/react-echarts/client.tsx:165-188` resizes a chart only on window `resize` and through a `ResizeObserver` on its box. Browsers fire neither for print layout (Chart.js documents the same limit for its charts). The canvas renderer (`react-echarts/runtime.ts:18`) fixes each canvas's CSS width in pixels.
- `dashboard/src/common/components/ledger-layout/ledger-print.css` (w5/1142, `51c32f01f`) lets the shell and tables flow across pages, but it has no rule for chart boxes.
- The Trial Balance chart card is `overflow-clip` (`features/reports/trial-balance/trial-balance-content.tsx:251`), so its overflow is cut. Elsewhere the wide canvas widens the printed document, and the browser shrinks every page to fit it.

## Fix

In `ledger-print.css`, under print, size every ECharts chart inside `[data-ledger-layout]` to its container. Make the chart root, its wrapper and its canvases `width: 100%; height: auto`, so the chart prints as a proportionally scaled picture of what was on screen. Keep any hover or tooltip layer out of the printout. A `matchMedia("print")` resize in `react-echarts/client.tsx` is an acceptable alternative if it produces the same output. Screen layout and the statement print portal (`features/reports/export/statement-print.css`) must not change.

## Definition of done

- The Trial Balance printout (`?time=2016`) shows all seven Assets Hierarchy blocks inside the card.
- The `Assets:US:ETrade` account printout uses the same body text size as the Journal printout (no shrink-to-fit), with the whole Account Balance chart visible.
- The Overview printout shows the month chart's last month and the Sankey's right-hand labels in full.
- A rendered print-media test fails on the current code. For example, it asserts that a chart canvas is no wider than its container under print media.
