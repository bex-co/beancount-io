# Screen readers hear English library defaults in every language: the ledger and account pickers' lists are "Suggestions", the toast region "Notifications alt+T"

**Severity:** minor (accessibility, localization). **Owner:** `dashboard`. **Estimate:** 20–30 minutes including tests. **A2:** a screen-reader user working in German, Japanese or Persian hears the two pickers they use to move around the ledger, and the region every confirmation arrives in, named in English; in English the lists are named "Suggestions", which says nothing about ledgers or accounts. **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` (`dashboard-qa` routine) on 2026-11-26 against `https://beancount.io`, production entry chunk `main-BBmi61Hs.js`. QA-owned headless Chromium 158, fresh isolated contexts, 1440×1000, language chosen with `?lang=`; names read from the accessibility tree. Reproduced signed out and signed in. Read-only. Local HEAD `d5e0d2bdd`.

## Repro

Public ledger, no sign-in needed.

1. Open `/ledger/open_ledger/example/journal?lang=de`.
2. The page's toast region (present on every page, even with no toast showing) is a `region` named `Notifications alt+T`. Same in `?lang=ja` and `?lang=fa`.
3. Open the ledger switcher in the sidebar (combobox `example — Hauptbuch wechseln`). The dialog is `Hauptbuch auswählen` and its search field `Hauptbücher durchsuchen...`, but the list of ledgers is `listbox "Suggestions"`. Japanese: dialog `台帳を選択`, field `台帳を検索...`, list `Suggestions`; Persian likewise.
4. Press Escape and open the sidebar's account search (the search button beside **Konten**). Field `Konten suchen...`, list `listbox "Suggestions"`.

An en/de comparison of every visible string and `aria-label`/`title`/`placeholder` on 27 pages and 11 dialogs and menus found no other untranslated interface text: these two are the only English names in German apart from ledger data.

## Cause

- cmdk's `Command.List` defaults its `aria-label` to `"Suggestions"` (`label = "Suggestions"` in `dashboard/node_modules/cmdk/dist/index.mjs`). The dashboard's wrapper `CommandList` (`src/common/components/ui/command.tsx:91-103`) passes no label, and neither caller does: `common/components/ledger-layout/ledger-switcher.tsx:298` and `common/components/ledger-layout/go-to-account.tsx:85`.
- sonner's `Toaster` names its region `${containerAriaLabel} ${hotkey}` with `containerAriaLabel` defaulting to `"Notifications"`. The dashboard's `Toaster` (`src/common/components/ui/sonner.tsx:27`, mounted once in `common/providers/root-provider.tsx:20`) does not set it.
- Neither string is in a locale catalog, so `src/test/no-untranslated-user-facing-strings.test.ts` and `locale-stray-english.test.ts` cannot see them.

## Fix

- Give each `CommandList` a translated `label` naming what it lists, for example `page.dashboard.ledgerList` ("Ledgers") in the switcher and `component.accountCombobox.accountList` ("Accounts") in account search. Making `label` a required prop of the wrapper keeps a future caller from falling back to cmdk's English default.
- Pass `containerAriaLabel={t("common.notifications")}` to `Toaster` (new key, real translations in all 15 catalogs). sonner still appends the hotkey.
- Tests: in German, the switcher's and account search's listbox names are the German words and the toast region's name starts with the German word; English names are no longer "Suggestions". The German assertions fail on the current code.

## Definition of done

On production in German, Japanese and Persian, the accessibility tree names the ledger switcher's list, the account search's list and the toast region in that language; in English the lists read "Ledgers" / "Accounts" (or the chosen words). Dashboard gates pass.

## Blast radius

`CommandList` has exactly these two callers (searched `src/` for `CommandList`); the shared `Combobox` (`ui/combobox.tsx`) names its own list and was not affected. `Toaster` is mounted once, for every page. The other primitives with hidden text (`dialog.tsx`, `sheet.tsx`, `pagination.tsx`, `sidebar.tsx`, `date-picker.tsx`) already read translated keys.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**`, including `done/` and `blocked/`, for `Suggestions`, `cmdk`, `containerAriaLabel`, `alt+T`, `Notifications` and Toaster. w4/135 and w4/252 fixed keyboard and `aria-activedescendant` behaviour in the same pickers, not their names; w5/1198 renamed the editors' English "Editor content". Nothing covers these names, and `main` still has both defaults, so this is not deployment lag.
