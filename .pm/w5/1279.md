# The same report has two names in 12 languages: Japanese Journal is "ジャーナル" in the sidebar and heading but "仕訳帳" in the tab and Related Pages, Russian Trial Balance "Оборотно-сальдовая ведомость" and "Пробный баланс" (gap in w5/1139 and w5/1135)

**Severity:** minor (localization). **Owner:** `dashboard`. **Estimate:** 45–60 minutes including the catalog edits and the test. **A3:** a reader who follows a Related Pages link, or comes back to a browser tab, lands on a page whose heading uses a different word from the link they clicked. In Japanese the Journal is "ジャーナル" in the sidebar and heading, but "仕訳帳" in the browser tab and in every Related Pages link to it. In Russian, Trial Balance is "Оборотно-сальдовая ведомость" in the sidebar and heading, but "Пробный баланс" in the tab and the links. These are two accounting terms, not two spellings of one term, so the reader cannot tell they name one page. w5/1139 fixed this for Holdings and Commodities, and w5/1135 for German Trial Balance. The guard added in `locale-glossary.test.ts` covers only those pages, so the same mismatch remains on eight other pages in twelve locales. **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` (`dashboard-qa` routine) on 2026-11-28 against `https://beancount.io`, production entry chunk `main-BBmi61Hs.js`, local HEAD `906f4dd9b` (same strings). Isolated headless Chromium 158 at 1440×1000, signed in as the QA account, public ledger `open_ledger/example`. Read-only.

## Repro

Each case reproduced on a fresh load (`?lang=` sets the language):

1. `/ledger/open_ledger/example/journal?lang=ja`: the sidebar link and H1 read `ジャーナル`, and the document title is `仕訳帳 - example`. On `/ledger/open_ledger/example?lang=ja`, Related Pages links to the same page as `仕訳帳`.
2. `/ledger/open_ledger/example/trial-balance?lang=ru`: the sidebar link and H1 read `Оборотно-сальдовая ведомость`, and the title is `Пробный Баланс - example`. Balance Sheet's Related Pages links to it as `Пробный баланс`.
3. `/journal?lang=nl`: the sidebar and H1 read `Journaal`, and Overview's Related Pages says `Dagboek`.
4. `/balance-sheet?lang=es`: the sidebar, H1 and title read `Balance General`, and Journal's Related Pages says `Balance`.
5. `/journal?lang=es`, `fr`, `fa` or `ko`: the sidebar's Overview link reads `Vista General` / `Vue d'ensemble` / `خلاصه`, and the Related Pages link to the same address reads `Resumen` / `Aperçu` / `نمای کلی`. In Korean the Journal heading is `저널`, while its tab and Related Pages say `분개장`.

## Fix

- In each locale above, choose one name per page and use it for the heading and sidebar key, `common.relatedLinks.<page>`, `seo.ledger<Page>.title` and the matching `seo.*.description` and `common.pageDescription.*` where they name the page. Default to the heading key, since it is what users see most. Prefer the standard accounting term where the two disagree: `Оборотно-сальдовая ведомость` for ru Trial Balance, and the full "profit and loss" form for ru and uk Income Statement.
- Set `common.ledgerNavigation` to each language's established ledger word (`книга`, `台帳`, `장부`, `دفتر`, …), so it no longer reads as the Journal in ru and uk.
- Files: `dashboard/src/i18n/locales/common/<lang>.ts`, `dashboard/src/i18n/locales/seo/<lang>.ts`, `dashboard/src/features/journal/locales/<lang>.ts`, `dashboard/src/features/bql/locales/<lang>.ts`, `dashboard/src/features/ledger-data/statistics/locales/<lang>.ts`.
- Test: widen the `describe.each` in `dashboard/src/test/locale-glossary.test.ts:66` from Holdings and Commodities to every page that has a sidebar entry, a related link and a tab title: Journal, the four statements, Statistics, Query, Documents, Events, Errors and Files. Check Overview's related link against `common.overview`. Add a case that `common.ledgerNavigation` differs from `journal.journal` in every locale. All of them fail on current catalogs.

## Definition of done

In every locale, each listed page's sidebar link, H1, browser tab title and Related Pages link use one term. Spot-check live: `/journal?lang=ja` and `/trial-balance?lang=ru` show one name in all four places. In Russian and Ukrainian, the sidebar landmark no longer has the Journal's name. The widened glossary test passes and fails with any one catalog reverted. Dashboard gates pass (`yarn format:check`, `yarn lint`, `yarn test`, `yarn build`).

## Blast radius

Mobile has its own catalogs and is not covered here. English is consistent: "Query" and "BQL Query - …" pass w5/1139's rule, because the tab title contains the heading.
