# Collapsed sidebar: the mouse passing over Reports, Import or Advanced takes focus out of the field being typed in, and closing the Reports flyout drops focus to the page body (regression of w4/1012)

**Severity:** minor (keyboard focus; typed text is lost). **Owner:** dashboard. **Estimate:** 30–45 minutes. **A2:** with the sidebar collapsed to its icon rail (the default from 768px to 1279px, and a remembered choice wider), a reader typing in a search box who moves the mouse across the rail loses what they type next. A keyboard user who opens the Reports flyout with the arrow key and presses Escape is dropped back at the top of the page. **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` on 2026-11-21 against `https://beancount.io`, production entry chunk `main-CIyNN76T.js` (built from `main` at or after `ebbf43fed`, so `aac0a0086` is deployed). QA-owned headless Chromium at 1440×1000, English/light, signed in as the QA owner, on the public `open_ledger/example` ledger. Read-only. Local HEAD `08f5f78be`.

## Repro

Collapse the sidebar first (**Collapse sidebar** at its foot, or Ctrl+B).

1. **Hover takes focus.** Open `/ledger/open_ledger/example/accounts`. Click **Search accounts...** and type `Che`. Move the mouse onto the rail's **Reports** icon and off it again toward the page, without clicking.
   - While the pointer is on the icon, the flyout opens and `document.activeElement` is the flyout's `<div>` (the dialog), no longer the input.
   - After the pointer leaves, the flyout closes and focus lands on `<body>`.
   - Typing `ck` now changes nothing: the field still reads `Che`.
   - Repeat over **Import** and **Advanced**: the same, except focus ends on the **Import** / **Advanced** button. A Space or Enter typed next opens that flyout instead of reaching the field.
2. **Escape after the arrow key.** Tab to the rail's **Reports** link and press ArrowRight (or ArrowDown). The flyout opens with focus in it; Tab moves through Income Statement and Balance Sheet. Press Escape. The flyout closes and focus is `<body>`; the next Tab starts again from **Skip to main content**.

Control: focus **Advanced** (or **Import**), press Enter, then Escape. Focus returns to **Advanced**, as w4/1012 recorded for all groups, including Reports, before `aac0a0086`. Each case was repeated from a fresh load.

## Cause

`dashboard/src/common/components/ledger-layout/ledger-group-flyout.tsx`:

- **Escape (Reports only).** Since `aac0a0086`, a group with a `landingPath` (Reports, `ledger-sidebar.tsx:314`) renders its icon as a `Link` inside `PopoverAnchor` (`:148-178`) instead of a `PopoverTrigger` (`:190-191`). Radix Popover returns focus on close to `context.triggerRef` (`@radix-ui/react-popover` non-modal `onCloseAutoFocus`), and only `PopoverTrigger` sets that ref. With an anchor, nothing is focused on close.
- **Hover (every group).** `handlePointerEnter` opens the flyout for a mouse (`:95`), and the shared `content` (`:112-145`) passes no `onOpenAutoFocus` / `onCloseAutoFocus`. Radix therefore moves focus into the popover when it opens, whatever opened it, and moves it to the trigger (or nowhere, for Reports) when it closes. A hover never asked for focus. The hover path dates from `4652596f5`; `aac0a0086` added Reports to it.

## Fix

Owned by `dashboard`, in `ledger-group-flyout.tsx`.

- Record how the flyout opened. When the mouse opened it, call `event.preventDefault()` in `onOpenAutoFocus` and `onCloseAutoFocus`, so focus stays wherever the reader left it. Click, tap and keyboard opening keep today's behaviour: focus moves into the flyout and comes back on close.
- For the landing-path variant, return focus to the Reports link on a keyboard close: keep a ref on the `Link` and focus it in `onCloseAutoFocus` (unless the close came from an outside click), as `PopoverTrigger` does for the other groups.
- Keep w4/097 (touch opens and keeps the flyout), w4/1012 (the dialog is named by its title) and the Reports click-through to Income Statement.

## Definition of done

- With the sidebar collapsed, focus in **Search accounts...**, moving the mouse over and off Reports, Import and Advanced leaves focus in the field, and the next characters typed land in it.
- Reports: ArrowRight, then Escape, leaves focus on the **Reports** link. Advanced and Import: Enter, then Escape, still leaves focus on their button.
- Clicking Reports still opens Income Statement; tapping Import still opens and keeps its flyout (w4/097).
- A rendered `ledger-group-flyout` test covers both: a mouse-opened flyout does not move focus, and Escape on the landing-path flyout returns focus to its link. Both fail on current `main`. Dashboard gates pass.

## Blast radius

Every ledger page renders `LedgerSidebar`; the rail shows by default at 768–1279px and by choice wider. All three groups with flyouts (Reports, Import, Advanced) take focus on hover; only Reports loses it on Escape. Any field can lose keystrokes this way: the Accounts search, the header filters, the Journal's New Entry fields and the Query editor sit beside the rail. The `/ledger` dashboard's own sidebar was not checked.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**`, including `done/` and `blocked/`, for flyout, icon rail, collapsed sidebar, `landingPath`, `aac0a0086`, hover with focus, and focus "steal". w4/done/1012 named the flyouts and recorded Escape returning focus to Reports and Advanced: this note is that control's regression for Reports. w4/done/097 is touch opening. w5/1192 is the Collapse/Expand toggle's own focus loss and `aria-expanded`, a different control and cause (the `SidebarMenuButton` remount); fixing it does not fix this. No open item covers hover taking focus. Not deployment lag: production carries every dashboard commit on `main`.
