# The Actions column header reads "Transactions" in 12 languages: Accounts, Budget history and the Ledger Settings collaborators table

**Severity:** minor (localization and accessibility; WCAG 1.3.1 Info and Relationships, 2.4.6 Headings and Labels). **Owner:** dashboard. **Estimate:** 15–20 minutes. **A2:** a German, French, Spanish or Chinese reader who moves through the Accounts table by column hears "Transaktionen" / "Transactions" / "交易记录" for the column that holds each account's action menu, and on Ledger Settings reads "Transactions" over the column of collaborator Remove controls. **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` on 2026-11-18 against `https://beancount.io` (entry chunk `main-LGv2TZLj.js`), headless Chromium 153 at 1440×1000, light theme, signed in as the QA owner on the synthetic ledger `mcp-agent-eval`. Read-only. Local HEAD `dc3a1d108`.

## Repro

1. Open `/ledger/<owner>/<ledger>/accounts?lang=de` as the ledger's owner. The accessibility tree of table **Konten** lists the column headers `Konto, Status, Eröffnungsdatum, Schließdatum, Buchungen, Saldo, Transaktionen`. The last header labels the column of row action menus (English: `Actions`, sr-only).
2. `?lang=fr` gives `… Écritures, Solde, Transactions`; `?lang=zh` gives `… 过账, 余额, 交易记录`. `?lang=ja` correctly gives `アクション`, and `?lang=en` gives `Actions`.

Control: Smart Import's own preview column header `importer.preview.actions` ("Actions") is translated correctly in every locale (de `Aktionen`, fr `Actions`, es `Acciones`, ca `Accions`, pt `Ações`, nl `Acties`, ru `Действия`, uk `Дії`, bg `Действия`, sk `Akcie`, fa `اقدامات`, zh `操作`).

## Cause

`common.actions` (English "Actions", description "Plural form of actions") is translated as the word for *Transactions* in 12 of the 14 non-English catalogs under `dashboard/src/i18n/locales/common/`: bg `Транзакции`, ca `Transaccions`, de `Transaktionen`, es `Transacciones`, fa `تراکنش‌ها`, fr `Transactions`, nl `Transacties`, pt `Transações`, ru `Транзакции`, sk `Transakcie`, uk `Транзакції`, zh `交易记录` (each at the `"common.actions"` entry, line 32–36). Only ja and ko are right. The key has only ever labeled an actions column (checked back to the import commit `af5339de0`). Its three live callers:

- `features/ledger-data/accounts/index.tsx:682` (sr-only header of the row-actions column)
- `features/ledger-data/budget/budget-history-table.tsx:99` (sr-only header, owners only)
- `features/ledger-data/settings/collaborators-section/index.tsx:268` (visible header of the collaborators table)

`features/collaboration/components/collaborators-table.tsx:131` uses it too, but that component is only re-exported from its folder's `index.ts` and nothing imports it.

A scan of every feature catalog for the de/fr/es/ru/zh word for "transaction" under keys whose English is not about transactions found no other case.

## Fix

Translate `common.actions` as "Actions" in each of the 12 catalogs, using the wording `importer.preview.actions` already uses in that language (listed above). No key, caller or English change.

## Definition of done

- Accounts at `?lang=de`, `fr` and `zh` exposes `Aktionen`, `Actions` and `操作` as the last column header; the other nine languages show their "Actions" word.
- A test fails on the current catalogs, for example one asserting that `common.actions` matches `importer.preview.actions` in every language. `yarn i18n:check` and the dashboard gates pass.

Nothing open covers it. Present at HEAD.
