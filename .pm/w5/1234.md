# Budget "Last year" on a ledger that stopped says "figures are as of" a day outside the year it shows, and calls last December "In progress" when the entries ended before that year

**Severity:** minor (a wrong caption, and a wrong verdict on one row). **Owner:** `dashboard`. **Estimate:** 30–45 minutes including tests. **A2:** a newcomer who imported a few years of history and then paused picks **Last year** to see how the year went. The page names a different day than the one it shows, and the last month of that year reads as still running. **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` (`dashboard-qa` routine) on 2026-11-27 against `https://beancount.io`, production entry chunk `main-BBmi61Hs.js`. QA-owned headless Chromium 158 with isolated contexts, at 1440×1000, in English. Read-only on the public ledger. One budget was added to and deleted from the QA account's disposable synthetic ledger. Local HEAD `460851d03`.

## Repro

1. Signed in or out, open `/ledger/open_ledger/example/budget`. The example's entries end on 2017-09-08, and its only budget (`Expenses:Food:Restaurant`) has targets dated 2026-10-30. Choose **Time span → Last year**. The span reads `2025-01-01 – 2025-12-31`.
   - The caption reads: "Entries end on 2017-09-08, so figures are as of 2017-08-31, the end of the last full month."
   - The row reads: `Monthly · December 2025 | — | 0.00 USD | No target yet · First target Oct 30, 2026`.
2. Control on the same page: **This year** reads "Entries end on 2017-09-08, before this time span begins." and `Monthly · November 2026 | 600.00 USD | 0.00 USD | In progress`. Both are right.
3. On the disposable synthetic ledger (entries from 2026-01-01 to 2026-08-28), add `2025-01-01 custom "budget" Expenses:Food:Groceries "monthly" 300 USD`. Choose **Last year**:
   - The caption reads "Entries end on 2026-08-28, so figures are as of 2026-07-31, the end of the last full month."
   - The row and the card read `Monthly · December 2025 | 300.00 USD | 0.00 USD | Below target -300.00 USD 0%`.
   - All time, This year and Last 12 months each show July 2026 under the same caption, which is consistent. The probe was then deleted through **Delete Budget**.
4. The case that changes the verdict: a ledger whose entries end before last year begins, with a target that covers last year. No disposable ledger has that shape, so it was rendered locally. The real `BudgetSummary` received the inputs the page computes: `time: "year-1"`, `asOf = budgetSummaryDay("2026-11-27", "2024-06-15") = "2024-05-31"`, a 2024-01-01 budget of 200 USD monthly, and empty 2025 buckets. The row reads `Monthly · December 2025 | 200 USD | 0 USD | In progress -200 USD 0%`. December 2025 ended eleven months ago. With `asOf` set to today, as **This year** uses for a span after the entries, it reads `Below target`.

## Cause

- `dashboard/src/features/ledger-data/budget/index.tsx:241–251` derives `spanStart` only for `this-year` and `last-12-months`. For `last-year` it is `undefined`, so `spanAfterEntries` is false. `asOf` then stays the dormant ledger's quiet day (`budgetSummaryDay`), even when that day falls outside 2025. `summaryNote` (`:264–276`) prints `budgetEntriesEnd` with that day.
- `budget-snapshot.ts:182–196` cannot find a 2025 period that holds `asOf`. It falls back to `budgetHeadlinePeriod(…, spanEnded)` (`budget-utils.ts:291–297`), which returns the span's last bucket, 2025-12-31. So the row shows December 2025 while the caption names another day.
- `budget-snapshot.ts:252` sets `inProgress = headline.end > asOf`. When `asOf` falls before 2025 (step 4), December 2025 counts as running, and `:256` returns `"progress"`.
- The existing Last year tests (`__tests__/budget-headline.test.tsx:205–230`) pass `asOf` as today. The page passes the quiet day for a ledger that stopped, and that path is not tested.

## Fix

Owned by `dashboard` (`src/features/ledger-data/budget/index.tsx`). Give **Last year** the treatment the other spans have, measured against the span's own dates:

- Entries end before the span begins: show `budgetEntriesEndBeforeSpan` and summarise as of today, as **This year** does. December is then judged as a completed month.
- The quiet day falls after the span ends (the ledger stopped after that year): drop the "figures are as of" note. December is the period shown and it is complete.
- The quiet day falls inside the span: keep today's behaviour. The note and the period both name that month.

Add no new strings. Leave **All time**, **This year** and **Last 12 months** unchanged.
