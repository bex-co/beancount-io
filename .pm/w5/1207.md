# Overview's month chart changes currency with the month you select: on a USD ledger, picking a month whose only income is in EUR redraws net worth as "5,291.00 EUR" beside a "61,300.00 USD" headline (gap in w4/m21)

**Severity:** minor (the numbers are each correct in their own unit, but the page's main chart contradicts the headline under it and silently drops what it does not draw). **Owner:** dashboard. **Estimate:** 30–45 minutes including tests. **A2:** the Overview is the first page a ledger opens on. Its one chart draws net worth as a line over each month's income and spending. On a ledger whose operating currency is USD, the line and columns are drawn in USD for most months, then in EUR when the month you step to earned only EUR (or, with no income, spent only EUR): a foreign dividend, interest from a foreign account, a month of travel spending. Nothing on the chart names the unit or says what it left out, which is the contract w4/m21 and w4/146 set for every Overview chart. **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` on 2026-11-21 against `https://beancount.io` (dashboard entry chunk `main-CIyNN76T.js`), QA-owned headless Chromium 153 at 1440×1000, English/light, no filters, signed in as the QA owner. Read-only. Local HEAD `918c204c6`.

## Repro

A synthetic USD ledger (`option "operating_currency" "USD"`) holding USD and EUR, with one EUR income posting. The QA account's disposable ledger already has this shape; the relevant entries are:

```beancount
2026-07-01 * "acquire BTC"
  Assets:QaCrypto   1 BTC {30000.00 USD}
  Equity:Opening  -30000.00 USD
2026-08-01 price BTC 60000.00 USD
2026-07-01 * "fund EUR broker"
  Assets:BrokerEur   500.00 EUR
  Equity:Opening    -500.00 EUR
2026-08-15 * "dividend"
  Assets:Cash        12.00 EUR
  Income:Dividends  -12.00 EUR
```

(plus other USD and EUR holdings; net worth at market is 31,300.00 USD and 5,200.00 EUR at the end of July, 61,300.00 USD and 5,291.00 EUR at the end of August).

1. Open the ledger's Overview. It opens on **August 2026**. The Financial position below the chart reads **Net Worth 61,300.00 USD**, "+30,000.00 USD · +95.8% from previous month", Assets "61,300.00 USD · 2 BTC · 5,291.00 EUR".
2. The chart above it is drawn in **EUR**: the y-axis runs 5.2K–5.8K, the August point is labelled "5.3K", and hovering August shows "Net Worth **5,291.00 EUR** · Income 12.00 EUR · Expenses 0.00 EUR". Its Period data table reads 2026-07-31 5,200.00 EUR, 2026-08-31 5,291.00 EUR, 2026-09-30 5,671.00 EUR. The 61,300.00 USD the headline reports is not in the chart at all.
3. Press **Previous** (July 2026). The same chart is redrawn in **USD**: 31.3K → 61.3K → 61.3K, Period data 31,300.00 / 61,300.00 / 61,300.00 USD, and August's income column is now **0** ("0.00 USD"): the 12.00 EUR dividend has vanished.
4. Press **Next** twice (September 2026): USD again. Only the month whose income is all EUR draws in EUR.

Neither drawing names its unit on the axis or labels, and neither says which units it left out. Repeated from a fresh load.

## Cause

`dashboard/src/features/reports/overview/components/month-summary.tsx:80-93` picks the chart's one unit from the **selected month's** income, else its spending: `unitOf(income, true) ?? unitOf(expenses, false) ?? primaryCurrency`. `unitOf` uses `getComparableAmount` (`overview/lib/overview-utils.ts:190-198`), which returns the operating currency when the month holds it and otherwise the month's only currency. So a month with only EUR income makes EUR the unit for the whole chart, the net worth line included, and stepping to another month switches it back. `month-hero-chart.tsx` then reads `point.balance?.[unit]` for every series (`:93-105`) and renders no `ChartUnitScope` note, unlike `income-expenses-chart.tsx:268` and `cash-flow-sankey.tsx:372`. Introduced with the Overview redesign (`aabf2eced`, 2026-11-16); the earlier Overview charts chose their unit through `chooseDisplayUnit` with the operating currency first (w4/146) and disclosed omitted units (w4/m21).

## Fix

Owned by `dashboard`. Choose the hero chart's unit once for the ledger view, not per selected month, and the same way the Financial position headline does: the operating currency when the net worth or flow series hold it at all, else the existing `chooseDisplayUnit` fallback. Keep it fixed while the reader steps months. Render `ChartUnitScope` under the chart naming every unit the net worth, income or expense series hold that the chart does not draw (here "EUR, BTC, …"), as the other Overview charts do.

## Definition of done

- On the ledger above, August, July and September all draw the chart in USD: August's net worth point and tooltip read 61,300.00 USD, matching the headline, and a note under the chart names the units it omits (EUR among them).
- A single-currency ledger (`open_ledger/example`) shows no note and the same chart as today.
- A component test renders `MonthSummary` with USD net worth and a selected month whose income is only EUR, asserts the chart's unit is USD and the omitted-units note names EUR, and fails on the current code. Dashboard gates (`yarn format:check`, `yarn lint`, `yarn test`, `yarn build`) pass.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` (open, `done/`, `blocked/`) for `month-hero-chart`, `month-summary`, `MonthHeroChart`, `ChartUnitScope`, `chooseDisplayUnit`, "chart unit" and Overview with EUR. w4/done/m21 and w4/done/146 set the one-unit-plus-disclosure contract for the charts the redesign replaced. w5/1175 and w5/1177 are axis tick formatting on this chart, not its unit. w5/1173 is report conversion to a second operating currency. Not deployment lag: `month-summary.tsx` last changed in `4be105aa3`, which production includes.

## Unverified
