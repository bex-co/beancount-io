# Every cleared transaction reads "Deleted" in 13 languages: the `*` status is translated as "Gelöscht", "Effacé", "Borrado", "삭제됨", "Gewist"

**Severity:** minor (localization), but on every Journal row: nothing is computed wrongly, yet in German, French, Spanish, Catalan, Dutch, Slovak, Korean, Chinese and the other non-English catalogs the word beside each `*` transaction says it was deleted or erased. **Owner:** `dashboard`. **Estimate:** 20–30 minutes including a test. **A2:** a newcomer reading their own Journal in their language is told that every confirmed entry has been deleted, and the New Entry form offers "* Deleted" as the status of the entry they are about to create. **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` (`dashboard-qa` routine) on 2026-11-26 against `https://beancount.io`, production entry chunk `main-BBmi61Hs.js`. QA-owned headless Chromium 158, fresh isolated contexts, 1440×1000, language chosen with `?lang=`. Reproduced signed out on the public ledger and signed in on a QA-owned ledger. Read-only: no ledger writes. Local HEAD `d5e0d2bdd`.

## Repro

Public ledger, no sign-in needed.

1. Open `/ledger/open_ledger/example/journal?lang=de`.
2. The Status filter reads `Alle`, `* Gelöscht`, `! Ausstehend`, `Andere`. Its tooltip for the same button says `Abgeschlossene Transaktionen` (completed transactions), but the button itself says "Deleted".
3. Every `*` row's flag carries the same word as its tooltip and its screen-reader text: the row's Flag cell is announced `* Gelöscht`. 56 of the first page's 60 rows are `*` transactions.
4. Signed in on a ledger you can write to, Journal → **New Entry** → Transaction: the Status select starts on `* Gelöscht`, and its options are `* Gelöscht` and `! Ausstehend`.

The same, language by language (Status filter button / its tooltip):

English reads `* Cleared` / `Cleared transactions`, which is right: in Beancount `*` marks a completed, confirmed transaction, as opposed to `!` (needs review).

## Cause

- `journal.clearedStatus` (English "Cleared") was machine-translated in the sense of "clear = erase" in every catalog: `dashboard/src/features/journal/locales/de.ts:547`, `fr.ts:566`, `es.ts:566`, `ca.ts:566`, `nl.ts:547`, `sk.ts:586`, `ko.ts:522`, `zh.ts:524`, `ja.ts:523`, `ru.ts:586`, `uk.ts:586`, `bg.ts:546`, `pt.ts:566`, `fa.ts:549`. It came in with `aeec496bf` (2026-08-26).
- The key is read in three places: the Journal row's flag title and screen-reader text (`features/journal/components/journal-lines.tsx:163-168`, rendered at `:204` and `:207`), the Status filter button (`journal-filters.tsx:53-57`, `:374`) and the New Entry status select (`new-directive-dialog/transaction-form.tsx:445`, `:452`). The account page's journal uses the same rows.
- `zh.ts:192` `journal.clearedTransactions` repeats the mistake ("已清除的交易", removed transactions).
- The mobile app translates the same word correctly (`mobile/src/translations/*.ts`, key `cleared`): de `Bestätigt`, fr `Pointée`, es `Conciliada`, ca `Conciliada`, nl `Bevestigd`, pt `Conciliada`, sk `Potvrdené`, ru `Проведено`, uk `Проведені`, bg `Потвърдени`, fa `تأییدشده`, zh `已确认`.

## Fix

- Retranslate `journal.clearedStatus` in all 14 non-English catalogs as "confirmed / completed", agreeing with each catalog's own `journal.clearedTransactions` tooltip and with the mobile app: for example de `Bestätigt`, fr `Validée`, es `Confirmada`, ca `Confirmada`, nl `Bevestigd`, pt `Confirmada`, sk `Potvrdená`, ru `Проведена`, uk `Проведена`, bg `Потвърдена`, fa `تأییدشده`, zh `已确认`, ja `確定済み`, ko `확정됨`. Fix zh `journal.clearedTransactions` the same way (`已确认的交易`); consider aligning ko `결제된 거래` ("paid") to `확정된 거래`.
- Test: render the Journal Status filter and a `*` row in German and Chinese and assert the button reads `Bestätigt` / `已确认`, not `Gelöscht` / `已清除`. Optionally add the English word "Cleared" to `src/test/locale-glossary.test.ts` with forbidden renderings (`Gelöscht`, `Effacé`, `Borrado`, `삭제됨`, `已清除`) so the sense cannot drift back. The German assertion fails on the current code.

## Definition of done

On production, `/ledger/open_ledger/example/journal` shows the `*` status as "confirmed/completed" in every language (German `* Bestätigt`, French `* Validée`, Chinese `* 已确认`), the row tooltips and screen-reader text agree, and New Entry's Status select starts on the same word. English is unchanged. Dashboard gates pass (`yarn i18n:check` included).

## Blast radius

One key, three render sites (Journal rows on the Journal and account pages, the Status filter, New Entry). `journal.pendingStatus`, `journal.otherStatus`, `journal.balanceMatches` and `journal.balanceFailed` were checked in de, fr, es, ko, zh, ja, nl and ru and read correctly.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**`, including `done/` and `blocked/`, for `clearedStatus`, `Gelöscht`, `Effacé`, `Borrado`, `삭제됨`, `Gewist` and "cleared" with translation. Nothing covers it; `main` still has every one of these strings, so this is not deployment lag.
