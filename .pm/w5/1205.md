# Income Statement Markdown and print drop "Total Revenue and Other Income" when the period has no income, and each multi-unit summary line lists only the units it happens to hold (gap in w4/205)

**Severity:** minor (an exported statement leaves out one of its three required summary lines, so the reader cannot see that revenue was zero; no figure is wrong). **Owner:** dashboard. **Estimate:** 30–45 minutes including tests. **A2:** an exported Income Statement is something people share and print, and a statement summary with no revenue line reads as incomplete. `dashboard/src/features/reports/AGENTS.md` asks for "total revenue and other income, total expenses, then net income or net loss" in every Income Statement Markdown and print export. w4/205 fixed the same failure for Cash Flow only.

## Repro

Production `https://beancount.io`, dashboard entry chunk `main-CIyNN76T.js`, QA-owned headless Chromium at 1440×1000, English/light, signed in (the public ledger reads the same way signed out). Read-only.

1. Open `/ledger/open_ledger/example/income-statement?time=2016&account=Expenses:Food`. The page lists `Income -` (zero) and `Expenses 6,859.72`, `Net Profit 6,859.72`. Export is enabled.
2. **Export → Markdown report**. The Statement summary has two rows:
   - `**Total Expenses** | **USD** | **6,859.72**`
   - `**Net Loss** | **USD** | **(6,859.72)**`

   There is no `Total Revenue and Other Income` line.
3. **Export → Print / Save as PDF**, with print media emulated: the summary table rows are `Total Expenses USD 6,859.72` and `Net Loss USD (6,859.72)`. Again no revenue line.
4. **Export → Spreadsheet CSV** keeps the row: `income,Income,Income,0,total,…` with blank unit and amount.
5. Multi-unit form, `/ledger/open_ledger/example/income-statement?time=2016&account=Income:US:Hoogle:Vacation`, Markdown and print alike: Total Revenue has `USD 120,632.20` and `VACHR 130.00` but no IRAUSD line, and Total Expenses has `USD 53,996.60` and `IRAUSD 18,000.00` but no VACHR line. Net Income then lists all three units, including `Net Loss IRAUSD (18,000.00)` and `Net Income VACHR 130.00`, figures whose zero side the summary never states.

Same result on the QA account's synthetic ledger at `?time=2026-02`, where the only entry is a 15.49 USD expense. Each result was repeated from a fresh page load.

Control: the Balance Sheet summary prints every unit on every line, including zeros (`Total Liabilities VACHR 0.00` and `Reconciliation Difference VACHR 0.00` on `/balance-sheet?time=2016`). The Cash Flow summary keeps zero lines since w4/205. A period with no income and no expenses at all (`?time=2026-03` on the synthetic ledger) shows "No Data Available" and disables Export, which is correct and stays as it is.

## Cause

`dashboard/src/features/reports/export/presentation.ts:189-211` `getProfitAndLossSummaryItems` returns the Income, Expenses and Net Income total rows unchanged. A root with no postings in the period has `amounts: []` (`export/model.ts:155-176` `amountsFromBalance` of an empty `balanceChildren`). Both renderers write one line per entry of `item.row.amounts`: `export/markdown.ts:161-175` `summaryLines` and `export/printable-statement.tsx:265-281`. An empty row therefore prints nothing, and a row that holds some of the document's units prints only those. The Balance Sheet avoids both problems because `getBalanceSheetSummaryItems` (`presentation.ts:108-136`) fills each line with `presentationAmounts(balance, orderedUnits(document))`. w4/205 added `getCashFlowSummaryAmounts` (`presentation.ts:265-272`) for Cash Flow but left the Income Statement path alone.

## Fix

Give the Income Statement summary the Balance Sheet's rule. Each of the three lines carries one amount per statement unit (`orderedUnits(document)`, primary currency first), and a unit the row does not hold reads `0.00`. Markdown and print share that one helper. Keep the label choice (`Net Income` or `Net Loss` by sign), the supporting-detail appendix, the CSV rows, exact decimals and the no-data Export gating unchanged.

## Definition of done

- `/ledger/open_ledger/example/income-statement?time=2016&account=Expenses:Food` exports Markdown and print summaries that read `Total Revenue and Other Income USD 0.00`, `Total Expenses USD 6,859.72` and `Net Loss USD (6,859.72)`.
- The `account=Income:US:Hoogle:Vacation` export lists USD, IRAUSD and VACHR on all three lines, with `0.00` for IRAUSD revenue and VACHR expenses. The unfiltered `?time=2016` export is unchanged.
- Rendered Markdown and print tests that use an Income root with `amounts: []` and a partly-filled multi-unit row fail on the current code and pass after the fix. Dashboard gates (`yarn format:check`, `yarn lint`, `yarn test`, `yarn build`) pass.
