# Close Account always closes "today": an account opened later than today offers Close and is always refused, and an account emptied months ago can only be closed with today's date

**Severity:** minor (nothing is written wrongly, but the action is offered where it can never succeed, and the reader cannot record when the account actually closed). **Owner:** `dashboard` (`features/ledger-data/accounts/close-account-dialog.tsx`, `index.tsx`). No API change: GraphQL `bulkEntries`, REST and MCP already take any close date, and `bea add close --date` does too. **Estimate:** 45–60 minutes including the 15 locales and rendered tests. **A2:** someone tidying a chart of accounts closes the card they paid off in August, or the account they opened ahead for next month. Today the dialog writes a close dated the day they click, or refuses with a ledger error they cannot act on from the dialog.

**Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` (`dashboard-qa` routine) on 2026-11-27 against `https://beancount.io`, entry chunk `main-BBmi61Hs.js`, isolated headless Chromium 158 at 1440×1000, English, UTC, signed in as the QA owner on the designated synthetic ledger. Local HEAD `efcd0bb01`. Every write below was removed again and `main.bean` is back to its starting blob `f1a0b83`.

## Repro

1. `/ledger/<owner>/<ledger>/accounts` → **Open Account**. Set Open Date **2026-12-15** (any day after today) and Account Name `Assets:Qa-20261127-Future`, then click **Open Account**. The row appears: opened 2026-12-15, Postings 0, Balance 0.
2. **Actions for Assets:Qa-20261127-Future**. **Close Account** is enabled. Choose it. The dialog reads "This will add a close directive for Assets:Qa-20261127-Future on 2026-11-27." It has no date field.
3. Click **Close Account**. The dialog stays open with "This would add an error to your ledger: Account Assets:Qa-20261127-Future used on 2026-11-27 but not opened until 2026-12-15 (main.bean:98)". Every retry offers the same date and gets the same refusal, until the calendar reaches the open date.

Control: an account opened today with no postings (`Assets:Qa-20261127-Dqa`) closes on 2026-11-27 with the toast "Account Assets:Qa-20261127-Dqa closed".

## Cause

- `close-account-dialog.tsx:55` `const today = () => format(new Date(), "yyyy-MM-dd")`, `:63` `closeDate = offered?.date ?? today()` and `:82` `close: { account, date: closeDate }`. The date is always the browser's today. The dialog (`:130–135`) shows it as text and has no field.
- `index.tsx:298` `canClose = !isClosed && isBalanceEmpty(account.balance)`. The row's `openedAt` (already in `GetLedgerAccountDirectives`) is not consulted, so an account opened after today is offered a close that must precede its open.
- The same fixed date applies to an asset or liability account whose balance reached zero long ago: the only close Beancount.io can write for it is dated today. It also applies to an account with postings dated after today (scheduled entries), whose close today the ledger refuses for the same reason. That last case was reasoned from the code and the refusal above, not reproduced (it needs a future-dated posting on the shared ledger).

## Fix

- Give the Close Account dialog a date field using the shared `DatePicker` (canonical `yyyy-MM-dd`, the same control Open Account uses for Open Date). Default it to today, or to the account's `openedAt` when that is later. Refuse a date before `openedAt` in the form, naming the open date. Send the chosen date unchanged and keep the description's "on {date}" in step with it, including the pending-submission rule from w4/1084.
- Keep the ledger's refusal (posting after the close date) shown beside the submit control, with the draft kept, as it is today.
- New strings (the field label, the before-open message) go in all 15 locales.

## Definition of done

- Repeating the repro, the dialog offers 2026-12-15 for the future-opened account. Closing writes `2026-12-15 close Assets:…` and the row shows Closed.
- An account opened earlier can be closed on a past date the reader picks, and the written directive carries that date.
- A date before the open date is refused in the form without a request.
- Rendered tests cover the default for an account opened before today and after today, a past date sent unchanged, and the before-open refusal. They fail on the current code. Dashboard gates pass.
