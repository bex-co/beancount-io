# Accept native rounding for costed postings with prices in Cash Flow

**Major · cli · 30–45m · A1 · worker1.** Cash Flow refuses a native-valid
costed purchase or sale when its posting also carries a price. The price
does not determine its native balancing weight: the booked cost does.
Nevertheless, `is_priced` excludes the transaction's accepted cost-rounding
residual, and Cash Flow exits **2** calling the trade a priced currency
exchange. A normal break-even sale needs its `@@` price to produce a
realized-gain row; omitting it makes Cash Flow work but removes that row.
No product fix or data-loss claim.

## Reproduce a normal costed sale

Create this fresh `main.bean`; the standalone recipe below creates its sale
with the real CLI and compares it with a separately handwritten native book:

```beancount
option "operating_currency" "USD"
2023-01-01 open Assets:Checking USD
2023-01-01 open Assets:Broker HOOL
2023-01-01 open Equity:Opening USD
2023-01-01 open Income:Gains USD
2023-11-30 * "Capital"
  Assets:Checking 5000.00 USD
  Equity:Opening -5000.00 USD
2024-01-01 price HOOL 330.00 USD
2023-12-31 * "Buy three"
  Assets:Broker 3 HOOL {{1000.00 USD}}
  Assets:Checking -1000.00 USD

2024-01-05 * "Sell three at break-even"
  Assets:Broker              -3 HOOL {} @@ 1000.00 USD
  Assets:Checking       1000.00 USD
  Income:Gains             0.00 USD
```

```sh
bea --json --no-input --offline --file /absolute/main.bean check
bea --json --no-input --offline --file /absolute/main.bean report capital-gains --time 2024-01
bea --json --no-input --offline --file /absolute/main.bean report cash-flow --time 2024-01
```

Check exits 0. Independent native booking retains three shares at cost
`333.3333333333333333333333333 USD`, then closes the lot on January 5.
January cash opens at **4000 USD** and closes at **5000 USD**. Native
cost weight for the sale is **−999.9999999999999999999999999 USD**;
its accepted residual is **+1e−25 USD**, inside **0.005 USD** tolerance.
The `@@ 1000.00 USD` price is also booked, and Capital Gains correctly
reports **three shares / zero gain**. No stock remains.

Cash Flow instead exits 2 with empty stdout and a JSON usage error on stderr:

```text
Cash Flow cannot reconcile this period: priced currency exchanges (... (Sell three at break-even)) move cash in USD by a price, not by cost, so the activity rows would not explain the cash accounts' actual change. The report reads every amount at cost; narrow the period to exclude them, or read the balance sheet.
```

The diagnostic misidentifies a cost-weighted trade as an unsupported currency
exchange. Monthly/yearly/day views, inclusive bounds, a whole account filter,
`--allow-errors`, human output and the full exported/reloaded book all refuse.
Quiet February still reports actual opening=closing=5000 USD. Source files
stay unchanged by all reads; actual/reference/after/export native graphs agree.

Removing `@@ 1000.00 USD` leaves the same booked costs, cash and accepted
residual, and Cash Flow reports correctly with that residual disclosed.
However, Capital Gains then has no sale row, as documented; this is not an
adequate workaround. An exactly divisible 990 USD acquisition and sale
reports correctly with its price intact, because its native residual is zero.

The same branch also affects an opening purchase: three shares with full
`{{1000.00 USD}}` recorded cost and an explicit `@ 330.00 USD` price, paid
by −1000 USD cash. Native balancing uses cost, not 3 × 330; the permitted
−1e−25 residual must still be recognized. Cash is 5000 → 4000 USD.
Its unpriced equivalent works, and the exact 990 USD / 330-per-share control
works with both cost and price. No gain is realized by any opening purchase.

Put checkout bea on PATH, save this recipe and choose an unused directory:

```sh
python3 recipe.py /absolute/unused/costed-sale reduce priced standard root /absolute/managed-engine/bin/python
```

## Cause, bounded repair and caller scope

[`_cash_flow`](../../cli/src/bea_engine/report.py#L1071) sets `is_priced`
whenever a posting has a price whose currency differs from its unit currency.
It does not check whether a booked cost determines the posting's weight.
The transaction is consequently omitted from `own` (:1085) and `residuals`
(:1093–1094). The subsequent guard compares `moved - net_change` against zero
instead of this accepted ±1e−25 residue (:1135–1140), then uses the same
`priced` list to produce the misleading currency-exchange diagnostic.

Native Beancount 3.2.3 `beancount.core.convert.get_weight` gives a known
booked cost precedence over price. Every standalone native snapshot calls
that function and `get_cost` separately and confirms the priced source
posting's weight equals its cost value. On the acquisition it differs from
units × price (990 USD); price is not what balances the trade. The helper's
at-cost inventory conversion itself also reads the booked cost correctly.
This is an incorrect eligibility predicate for residual accounting.

`17d104c18` introduced `is_priced` and its residual-exclusion gate. One engine
dispatch calls `_cash_flow` (:137); the
[frontend](../../cli/src/cli/commands/report.py#L620) carries the same error
to human and JSON output. CLI-local; no backend or dependency changes.

Classify price-dependent native weights, rather than every price annotation,
when deciding which transactions need unsupported-FX handling. A known booked
cost must retain its genuine native rounding allowance even alongside `@` or
`@@`. Keep true cost-less priced-currency refusals, source costs/prices,
native precision, actual cash boundaries, residual disclosure, the realized-
gain sale row, and all date/account/role semantics. No floats, blanket epsilon,
global precision change or deletion of posting prices. Do not bypass native
validation. Add meaningful CLI regressions and run `make check-all` in `cli/`
when implementing.

This differs from open [1077](./1077.md): each failing period here contains
one transaction, and the residual is never collected. 1077's unpriced
multi-purchase periods collect all residuals but compare different aggregation
orders. Correcting this predicate alone leaves 1077's failure; preserve its
independent scope. Open [1076](./1076.md) is the separate Capital Gains
quantity-magnitude issue; these quantities are the small integer three.
