**Trivial · cli · 20–35m · A1 · worker1.** A nonempty position worth exactly
zero has `balance:{}` in `report register`, and the human table prints `—`.

market quote reproduces under `USD` and `at_value`; a stated zero acquisition

## Reproduce and compare with the working tree

```beancount
option "operating_currency" "USD"
2024-01-01 open Assets:Stock HOOL
2024-01-01 open Assets:Cash USD
2024-01-02 * "Holding"
  Assets:Stock 2 HOOL {100 USD}
  Assets:Cash -200 USD
2024-01-31 price HOOL 0 USD
```

`report register Assets:Stock --time 2024-01 --conversion USD` exits0,
`ledger_valid:true`, `valuation:complete`, no missing prices. Its one row
has `amount:{number:"2",currency:"HOOL"}` and `balance:{}`; the human balance
cell is `—`. With identical time/conversion, `balance Assets:Stock` returns
the Stock node's `balance:{"USD":"0"}`. BQL and an uncached native control
retain2HOOL, cost200USD and value0USD. The position still exists and its
converted currency/value are known.

For the zero-cost variant use `{0 USD}`, cash0USD, and a10USD quote.
`--conversion at_cost` then has the same missing known-zero register balance,
while the tree names0USD. Its market conversions correctly show20USD.
The zero-price fixture correctly shows200USD at cost and2HOOL in units.

Save this standalone recipe, put checkout `bea` on PATH, and run
`python3 recipe.py /absolute/new/book zero-price standard /absolute/engine/bin/python`.
Use `zero-cost`, `nonzero`, `empty` or `canceled` in another fresh directory;
`renamed` changes all five roots. The recipe records exact argv/cwd, safe

## Cause, callers and bounded repair

`cli/src/bea_engine/report.py:667` starts Register's running position as
`CounterInventory`. Its conversion at `:704` reduces into
`SimpleCounterInventory`, whose `add` at
`cli/src/fava/core/inventory.py:50–56` removes a0-valued output currency.
`_balance_map` then produces `{}`, and the human renderer's `_amounts`
prints the empty marker. The converted Amount was already0USD; no missing
quote or guessed currency is involved.

The display-tree `_converted` in `cli/src/fava/core/tree.py` already uses
`KeptZeroSource`/`KeptZeroInventory` from the 908 repair to retain that known
currency. Reuse that existing conversion behavior for Register's running
balances. Genuinely empty/fully closed source inventories must remain empty,
and units/nonzero conversions must retain their existing values. Keep the
strict/partial missing-price behavior; do not turn unavailable values into
zero or change quantity cancellation rules.

`report.answer` is the engine caller; `commands/report.py::register` emits
its rows as JSON or the human table. `fava.ledger.account_journal` also uses
a plain inventory, but the CLI Register uses its own loop and no additional
CLI route is claimed here. No new helper dependency or API shape is needed.
