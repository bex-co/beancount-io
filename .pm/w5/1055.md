## Cause, callers and bounded repair

`cli/src/bea_engine/report.py:667` starts Register's running position as
`CounterInventory`. Its conversion at `:704` reduces into
`SimpleCounterInventory`, whose `add` at
`cli/src/fava/core/inventory.py:50–56` removes a0-valued output currency.
`_balance_map` then produces `{}`, and the human renderer's `_amounts`
prints the empty marker. The converted Amount was already0USD; no missing
quote or guessed currency is involved.

The display-tree `_converted` in `cli/src/fava/core/tree.py` already uses
`KeptZeroSource`/`KeptZeroInventory` from the 908 repair to retain that known
currency. Reuse that existing conversion behavior for Register's running
balances. Genuinely empty/fully closed source inventories must remain empty,
and units/nonzero conversions must retain their existing values. Keep the
strict/partial missing-price behavior; do not turn unavailable values into
zero or change quantity cancellation rules.

`report.answer` is the engine caller; `commands/report.py::register` emits
its rows as JSON or the human table. `fava.ledger.account_journal` also uses
a plain inventory, but the CLI Register uses its own loop and no additional
CLI route is claimed here. No new helper dependency or API shape is needed.
