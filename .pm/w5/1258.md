# Cash Flow's Net Cash Flow counts fractions of a cent that no cash account moved: May 2016 reads −2,300.04362 USD where cash fell 2,300.05, the all-time statement opens at −0.00056 USD, and the 2016 view puts the whole year's residue in December (5,043.34337 against 5,043.36) (gap in w0/022)

**Severity:** minor (cent-level, but it is the cash figure itself and the report contradicts itself). **Owner:** `dashboard` (`features/reports/cash-flow/lib/model.ts`). **Estimate:** 45–60 minutes including tests. **A2:** cash accounts only hold whole cents. The Net Cash Flow chart, its Period data and the statement's opening cash should agree with the cash accounts. Today they carry each fund purchase's cost-rounding residue instead. The same month reads differently depending on the Time filter, and the all-time CSV says the household started with −0.00056 USD. **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` (`dashboard-qa` routine) on 2026-11-27 against `https://beancount.io`, production entry chunk `main-BBmi61Hs.js`. QA-owned headless Chromium 158 at 1440×1000, English, signed in as the QA account on the public `open_ledger/example` ledger, At Cost, Monthly. Read-only. Local HEAD `c654550d1`.

## Repro

Public ledger, no sign-in needed. Expand **Period data** under the Net Cash Flow chart in each step.

1. Open `/ledger/open_ledger/example/cash-flow`. Period data lists **6,644.04823 USD** for 2015-01, **−2,300.04362 USD** for 2016-05 and **1,190.75943 USD** for 2016-01. 23 of the 33 months carry digits below a cent. Hovering 2016-05 gives **USD: −2,300.044**. The 33 values sum to **3,609.87056**, while closing cash is **3,609.87**. The statement's Cash & Equivalents at Period Start reads **0.00** where `?time=2015` reads "-". Export → Spreadsheet CSV writes `Cash & equivalents at period start … -0.00056` and `Net change in cash & equivalents … 3609.87056`.
2. Open `?time=2016-05`. The statement reads Period Start **7,583.36**, Period End **5,283.31** and Net Change **−2,300.05**. Period data reads **−2,300.05000 USD**. Financing gains a row **Equity:Conversions Current −0.01**.
3. Open `?time=2016`. Period data for 2016-12 reads **5,043.34337 USD**. In step 1 the same month reads **5,043.36 USD**, and `?time=2016-12` gives Net Change **5,043.36** (1,720.15 → 6,763.51). Financing lists **Equity:Conversions Current −0.02**.

Steps 1–3 were repeated from a fresh load.

The residue comes from fractional fund purchases at cost. Beancount accepts them within tolerance, but the cost leg and the cash leg differ by a fraction of a cent:

- `3.430 VBMPX {139.94 USD}` is 479.9942 against `Assets:US:Vanguard:Cash -479.99 USD`;
- `8.196 RGAGX {87.85 USD}` is 720.0186 against `-720.02 USD`.

Both are on 2015-02-02.

## Cause

`dashboard/src/features/reports/cash-flow/lib/model.ts`:

- `:425-429`: each interval's `net` is `sumBalanceRecords(Object.values(activities))`, the inverse of the non-cash legs. The cash accounts' own changes are in `interval.accountChanges` but are skipped at `:400`.
- `:469-473`: `netChange` is the sum of the activity totals, and `opening = closing − netChange`.

Since w0/022, the conservation check (`:357-370`, `withinBalancingTolerance`) accepts a per-interval residue up to 0.005 per account change. It does not account for that residue anywhere, so the residue flows into every net figure and into opening cash. w0/022 listed "fold an accepted residual into a non-cash conversion/rounding line rather than into opening cash" as part of its fix. Its resolution only tolerated the residue and printed −0.00 as 0.00.

With a Time filter, the clamp (`backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/time-clamp.ts`, beancount's `summarize.clamp_opt`) books the whole period's residue to `Equity:Conversions:Current` on the period's last day. The model then classifies that entry as a Financing row, and it lands in the last interval only. That is why the 2016 view's December is off by 0.01663 while the single-month views are exact.

## Fix

Measure cash by the cash accounts. Each interval's `net` is the sum of the changes of the accounts `isCashAccountByMembership` accepts. `netChange` is the sum of those, and `opening = closing − netChange`.

Book each interval's leftover (`net` minus the sum of activities, which is within w0/022's tolerance) to one explicit non-cash "Rounding" line, so the activities still add up to the net. Treat the ledger's conversions account (`account_current_conversions`, from the clamp) as part of that line, not as a Financing row.

Keep w4/198's rejection of non-conserving valuation (At Market Value, Units) unchanged. Do not round anything to cents for display only.

- `?time=2016` reads 2016-12 **5,043.36 USD**, the same as the no-filter and `?time=2016-12` views. The 12 months sum to the statement's net change of −1,450.36. No Financing row named Equity:Conversions appears in `?time=2016`, `?time=2016-05` or `?time=2015`.

The assertions fail on the current code. w0/022's tolerated, rejected and multi-interval cases still pass. Dashboard gates pass.
