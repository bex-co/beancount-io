# Overview opens a filtered view one month early whenever the filter's last entry is not on its month's last day: `?time=2016-Q3` opens August, `?time=2016-Q1` February, and an account filter an empty month (gap in w5/1149)

**Severity:** minor. Nothing is written wrongly, but the page answers for a month the reader did not choose, and its Financial position date disagrees with the Balance Sheet for the same filter. **Owner:** `dashboard` (`features/reports/overview`). **Estimate:** 40–60 minutes including tests. **A2:** narrowing the Overview to a quarter, or to one account, is how a reader checks a period. They expect the quarter's last month, or the account's latest activity. Today whether they get it depends on whether some entry happens to fall on that month's last day. **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` (`dashboard-qa` routine) on 2026-11-27 against `https://beancount.io`, production entry chunk `main-BBmi61Hs.js`. QA-owned headless Chromium 158 at 1440×1000, English, `en-US`. ## Repro

Public ledger, no sign-in needed. `open_ledger/example` runs to 2017-09-08. The "last entry in range" column comes from BQL `SELECT max(date) WHERE date >= … AND date < …` on the same ledger.

| URL (`/ledger/open_ledger/example…`) | Last entry in range | Opens on | Financial position |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `?time=2016-Q1` | 2016-03-30 | **February 2016** | Feb 29, 2016 |
| `?time=2016-Q2` (control) | 2016-06-30 | June 2016 | Jun 30, 2016 |
| `?time=2016-Q3` | 2016-09-29 | **August 2016** | Aug 31, 2016 |
| `?time=2016-Q4` (control) | 2016-12-31 | December 2016 | Dec 31, 2016 |
| `?time=2015-Q3` | 2015-09-29 | **August 2015** | Aug 31, 2015 |
| `?time=2016-03 - 2016-05` | 2016-05-30 | **April 2016** | Apr 30, 2016 |

1. Open `/ledger/open_ledger/example?time=2016-Q3`. The dateline reads **August 2016**, and Financial position and Money movement answer for August. Recent activity on the same page lists 2016-09-29, 2016-09-28 and so on. September is fully recorded, because the ledger continues to 2016-10-04 and beyond.
2. Press **Next** (`?time=2016-Q3&month=2016-09`). Financial position is dated **Sep 29, 2016**, while `/ledger/open_ledger/example?month=2016-09` (no Time filter) dates the same Net Worth, 73,460.84 USD, **Sep 30, 2016**. `?time=2016-03 - 2016-05&month=2016-05` likewise reads "May 30, 2016". The Balance Sheet for either filter reports at the period's last day.
3. Open `/ledger/open_ledger/example?account=Expenses:Taxes:Y2015`. The account's last posting is the 2016-03-20 "Filing taxes for 2015" (570.15 USD). The Overview opens on **February 2016**, where Money in and Money out both read "No money movement for this period". `&month=2016-03` shows the 570.15 USD.
4. On a QA-owned copy of the bean-example ledger, which runs to 2025-10-11, `?time=2024` opens **November 2024** (December's last entry is 2024-12-30), and `?time=2023` opens December 2023.

Each case was repeated from a fresh load, and the Q1 and Q3 rows were repeated signed out. Controls: the unfiltered page and `?time=2017` both open August 2017, which is right, because the ledger itself stops on 2017-09-08.

## Cause

- `dashboard/src/features/reports/overview/index.tsx:221-231` computes `dataThrough` from the newest transaction **under the dashboard's filters**. `:241-248` passes it to `useOverviewMonth`, whose prop is documented as "The ledger's last day with data" (`hooks/use-overview-month.ts:67`).
- `lib/overview-month.ts:75-84` (`openingMonth`) then calls `settledOpeningMonth` (`lib/overview-utils.ts:659-672`). That function steps back one interval when `isLedgerDormant(dataThrough, today)` (`:635-637`, more than 45 days) and the candidate month ends after `dataThrough` (`isPeriodIncomplete`, `:597-602`). A past Time filter always ends more than 45 days ago, so every filtered view counts as dormant. Its last month counts as unfinished unless an entry falls on that month's last day. The step back goes to the previous interval whether or not it has any activity, which is why step 3 lands on an empty month.
- `index.tsx:422-428` dates Financial position at the earlier of the last net-worth point and the filtered `dataThrough`, hence "Sep 29".
- `loader.ts:107-123` makes the same decision from the same filtered read, so the server render agrees with the page and the wrong month is stable.
- w5/1149 moved the "Last entry" dormancy line to the unfiltered read (`ledgerDataThrough`, `index.tsx:232-239`). It kept the filtered date for the opening month on the assumption that it was still right there. These repros show it is not.

## Fix

Dashboard only. Whether the ledger "stopped part-way through its last month" is a fact about the ledger, as w5/1149 says. Decide it from the ledger's unfiltered newest transaction (`ledgerDataThrough`):

- `openingMonth` and `settledOpeningMonth` take the unfiltered date for `isLedgerDormant`, for `isPeriodIncomplete`, and for the dormant `recorded` cut. A filtered month that the ledger's data runs past counts as finished. A dormant ledger's own last partial month still steps back, as today.
- Financial position's date (`index.tsx:422-428`) uses the unfiltered date too. A filtered month the ledger covers is dated at its close (or the filter's earlier end), and a dormant ledger keeps its last entry's date.
- The route loader reads the same unfiltered newest-transactions query the page already issues (empty `account`/`filter`/`time`) and decides by the same rule. The server-rendered month must not change on hydration (see w5/1217).

Keep `?month=` and Back (w5/1148), the begun-month rule for scheduled entries (w5/1176), and the unfiltered "Last entry" line (w5/1149) as they are.

## Definition of done

- `/ledger/open_ledger/example?time=2016-Q3` opens on September 2016, with Financial position "Sep 30, 2016" and Net Worth 73,460.84 USD. `?time=2016-Q1` opens March 2016, `?time=2016-03 - 2016-05` May 2016, and `?account=Expenses:Taxes:Y2015` March 2016 with Money out 570.15 USD.
- `?time=2016-Q3&month=2016-09` and `?time=2016-03 - 2016-05&month=2016-05` date Financial position Sep 30 and May 31.
- Controls unchanged: the unfiltered page and `?time=2017` open August 2017 with Financial position Aug 31, 2017, and `?time=2016-Q2` and `?time=2016-Q4` still open June and December 2016.
- `lib/__tests__/overview-month.test.ts` gains cases that fail on the current code: a filtered view whose last entry falls before its month's end, on a ledger that runs past it, opens that month, and an account filter opens its latest active month. A case where the ledger's own data stops mid-month still steps back. A loader or page test shows the server and the page agree. Dashboard gates pass.
