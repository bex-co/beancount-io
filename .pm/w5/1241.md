# Statistics squeezes Account Last Entries into a third of the page: on a 1440px desktop "Assets:Crypto:Coinbase:USD" reads "Asset / s:Cryp / to:Coi / nbase: / USD", and at 1024–1280px the Balance column is cut off at "-2,703.29 U"

**Severity:** minor (layout; nothing is computed wrongly). **Owner:** `dashboard`. **Estimate:** 30–45 minutes including tests. **A2:** Statistics is where a newcomer checks which accounts are still active and what each one last held. At ordinary laptop and desktop widths the account names in that table break inside words, four to six lines tall. At 1024–1280px the table is wider than its card, so the currency of every balance is hidden until the reader scrolls the card sideways. **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` (`dashboard-qa` routine) on 2026-11-27 against `https://beancount.io`, entry chunk `main-BBmi61Hs.js`, isolated headless Chromium 158, English, light theme, signed in as the QA account but reading public ledgers only. Read-only. Local HEAD `8757c3311`.

## Repro

Public ledgers, no sign-in needed.

1. At 1440×1000 open `/ledger/open_ledger/crypto-example/statistics`. Statistics lays out three cards side by side: **Postings per Account**, **Account Last Entries** and **Entries Count by Type**. In **Account Last Entries** the Account column is 79px wide. `Assets:Bank:Checking` reads `Asset` / `s:Ban` / `k:Chec` / `king` (4 lines). `Assets:Crypto:Coinbase:USD`, `Assets:Crypto:Binance:USD` and `Assets:Staking:Ethereum:ETH` take 5 lines, and `Assets:Crypto:Wallet:MetaMask:ETH` takes 6 (`Asset` / `s:Cryp` / `to:Wal` / `let:Me` / `taMas` / `k:ETH`). In **Postings per Account**, `Assets:Crypto:Wallet:MetaMask:COMP` breaks as `…:CO` / `MP`.
2. Same width, `/ledger/open_ledger/example/statistics`. The Account column is 104px, and all 15 names in Account Last Entries wrap mid-word, for example `Liabiliti` / `es:US:Cha` / `se:Slate`.
3. At 1280×800 (and at 1024×900) on `open_ledger/example`, the Account Last Entries table is 337px wide inside a 309px card. Its scroll container scrolls horizontally. The header reads `Balan`, the rows read `-2,703.29 U`, `597.748 RGA` and `193.442 VBM`, and the account column drops to 79px (`Asset` / `s:US:V` / `anguar` / `d:Cash`). Postings per Account also splits `Assets:US:Federal:PreTax40` / `1k` and `Expenses:Financial:Commiss` / `ions`.
4. Controls: `open_ledger/stock-example` at 1440 (104px, 3 lines per name) and `open_ledger/fedex` at 1440 (112px, up to 4 lines). At 390×844 the cards stack and names take at most two lines.

All repeated from a fresh load. The document never scrolls sideways; the overflow is inside the card.

## Cause

- `dashboard/src/features/ledger-data/statistics/index.tsx:31` puts the three sections in `grid grid-cols-1 lg:grid-cols-3`. From 1024px up, each section gets a third of the `LedgerPage` content width: 293px at 1024, 309px at 1280 and 363px at 1440. This has been the layout since the dashboard was imported (`af5339de0`).
- In Account Last Entries, the date and balance cells are `whitespace-nowrap` (`account-last-entries.tsx:142,145`), so they keep their full width. The account cell (`account-last-entries.tsx:129`) gets what is left and is `break-all`, so the browser breaks the name at any letter instead of after a colon. Postings per Account uses the same cell class (`postings-per-account.tsx:127`).
- When the nowrap columns alone exceed the card, `statistics-section.tsx:40-41` (`overflow-hidden` around `overflow-x-auto`) turns the excess into a horizontal scroll inside the card. That cuts off the right-hand Balance column.

## Fix

Owned by `dashboard`. No API change.

- Give Account Last Entries enough width for a name, its last date and its balance on desktop. For example, keep three columns only from `xl` and let Account Last Entries span two of them. Or use two columns, with Entries Count by Type below. Choose whatever arrangement meets the outcomes below.
- Break account names only between segments: drop `break-all` from both Statistics account cells and render the name through the shared `AccountPath` (`dashboard/src/common/components/account-name.tsx:6`), which puts a `<wbr>` after each colon. Keep the full name, its link and its `dir` handling.

## Definition of done

- At 1024×900, 1280×800 and 1440×1000, on `/ledger/open_ledger/example/statistics` and `/ledger/open_ledger/crypto-example/statistics`, no account name in either Statistics table breaks inside a segment. Every line break falls after a `:`.
- At the same widths, the Account Last Entries table fits its card: its scroll container's `scrollWidth` equals its `clientWidth`, and every balance shows its full number and currency.
- At 1280 and 1440, no account name in Account Last Entries is taller than two lines on those two ledgers. At 390×844 the stacked layout is no worse than today.
- A rendered test asserts that a Statistics account cell has no `break-all` class and puts a `wbr` after each colon. It fails on the current markup. Dashboard gates pass.
