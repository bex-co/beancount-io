# Prefer the requested currency's quote in Holdings

**Major · cli · 35–50m · A1 · worker1.** Holdings can report a known
**300 USD** position as **250 USD**, `valuation: complete`, exit **0**,
because it ignores the current direct USD quote and instead converts an
older quote in the lot's EUR cost currency. When that EUR quote is absent,
strict Holdings wrongly refuses despite the available USD quote, and
`--allow-errors` substitutes the **250 USD** cost basis for the **300 USD**
market value. Native Beancount, BQL and the balance sheet already return
300 USD. The stored book remains unchanged; no product fix implemented.

The position is **2 HOOL {100 EUR}**, with 200 EUR acquisition basis.
On January 31, EUR is **1.25 USD** and HOOL has a direct **150 USD** quote.
Cost basis is therefore **250 USD**, market value **300 USD**. An earlier
January 10 quote of **100 EUR/HOOL** is a normal older price; its converted
125 USD must not override the current direct quote in the requested USD.

supplies matching current EUR and USD quotes; `cost-only` exercises the
working two-leg fallback without a direct USD quote; `no-price` removes
both security quotes and correctly requires partial mode. Repeat with
`renamed` for all five non-default account roots. The exact recipe captures
