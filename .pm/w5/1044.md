crashes with decimal ConversionSyntax when an existing costed posting has
`@ USD` or `@@ USD` and Beancount retains a MISSING numeric price. That
blocks every full listing, including human table/details and JSON,
instead of exposing known entries for inspection and repair. A search
that excludes the unfinished entry works. Strict refusal of the native
interpolation error is correct; the opt-in partial path needs safe read
