# New Entry's first opening after a page load leaves focus on the "Transaction" tab, not Payee: the Payee field is still a loading skeleton when its one focus attempt runs

**Severity:** minor (keyboard access). **Owner:** dashboard. **Estimate:** 30–45 minutes including a rendered test. **A2:** New Entry is where a newcomer records a first transaction by hand. The form promises to put the cursor in Payee. On the first opening of every page load it does not, so whatever the reader types straight away goes nowhere. **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` on 2026-11-18 against `https://beancount.io` (entry chunk `main-LGv2TZLj.js`), headless Chromium 153 at 1440×1000, English/light, signed in as the QA owner on the synthetic ledger `mcp-agent-eval`. Nothing was saved. Local HEAD `dc3a1d108`.

## Repro

1. Load `/ledger/<owner>/<ledger>/journal?lang=en` and activate **New Entry**.
2. After 1.5 s, `document.activeElement` is the selected **Transaction** tab button. Typing `qa-20261118-back-draft` at that point changes nothing: no field receives it.
3. Close the dialog (still a clean form) and activate **New Entry** again. Focus is now in **Payee**, and so on every later opening.
4. Reload and repeat. Two separate page loads gave the same result: first opening on the tab, second and third in Payee. Loading `journal?action=new-entry&directive=transaction` directly also leaves focus on the tab.

A focus trace of the first opening: the dialog content and then the Transaction tab take focus about 330 ms after the click (Radix's open auto-focus), the Payee `<input>` appears in the DOM about 660 ms after the click, and no `focus()` call ever targets Payee.

## Cause

- `dashboard/src/features/journal/components/new-directive-dialog/transaction-form.tsx:187-208` makes one attempt, 150 ms after the form mounts. It queries `[data-transaction-payee] input` and returns if nothing matches (`if (!payee) return;`). Nothing tries again.
- `common/components/ledger-comboboxes/payees-combobox.tsx:68-70` renders `<Skeleton>` in place of the input while the payees query is in its first load (`initialLoading`, `use-suggestions-fallback.tsx:61-63`). On a page's first New Entry that read is still in flight when the timer fires. Later openings read the payees from the Apollo cache, so the input exists at once.
- `__tests__/early-field-focus.test.tsx` mocks the comboboxes as plain inputs (`vi.mock("@/common/components/ledger-comboboxes", …)`), so no test sees the skeleton window.

## Fix

Give Payee its default focus once its input exists, not only at 150 ms. For example, retry when the payee combobox finishes its first load, or have `PayeesCombobox` render its real input while suggestions load, as the fallback already does after a failed read. Keep w4/1075's rule: a field the reader has already chosen in the form keeps focus, and nothing outside the form is overridden after the reader has moved on. Cancel the pending work on unmount.

## Definition of done

- On the first New Entry of a fresh page load (link or direct `?action=new-entry` URL), focus lands in Payee once it renders, and typed text goes into Payee.
- If the reader tabs to Date (or another form field) before Payee finishes loading, Date keeps focus (w4/1075 still holds).
- A rendered test with a payees read that resolves after the 150 ms mark fails on the current code. Dashboard gates pass.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**`, including `done/`, for autofocus, Payee focus, skeleton and cold first opening. w4/done/1075 fixed early typing being moved into Payee and records that "two cold-first-open probes were not used as default controls". The cold opening was seen but never filed. w4/done/141 changed only a test's wait. Nothing open covers it. Present at HEAD.

## Unverified

Narrow viewports, other ledgers' payee-load times, and screen-reader announcement of the tab focus.
