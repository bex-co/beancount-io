# A public ledger's managed prices go "Unavailable" whenever only signed-out visitors load it: their refresh hits the `/prices` login gate (HTTP 302), and a signed-in viewer's status read does not refresh them

**Severity:** minor (valuation on the public showcase). **Owner:** `backend-cluster/ledger` (managed-price cache), with a product decision on the `/prices/<ALIAS>` gate. **Estimate:** 45–60 minutes once the approach is chosen. **A3:** `open_ledger/crypto-example` is a public showcase built on managed price includes; a visitor who is not signed in, the most common visitor, is the one who sees it with no live prices. **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` on 2026-11-16 against `https://beancount.io` (local HEAD `8adc7ff65`), headless Chromium, signed-in QA account plus fresh signed-out contexts. Read-only.

## What was observed

1. 01:10 PST, signed in: `/ledger/open_ledger/crypto-example/commodities` listed all nine managed sources (BTC-USD, ETH-USD, SOL-USD, ADA-USD, UNI-USD, COMP-USD, USDC-USD, DAI-USD, USDT-USD) as **Unavailable · No price received yet · Last error: fetch failed (redirect): redirects are not followed (HTTP 302)**. Reloading the page as the same signed-in user changed nothing except the next-refresh time.
2. 01:14, signed in: opening `/holdings` (a real ledger load) fetched every feed with this viewer's credential. The commodities page then read **Up to date · Observed 01:13–01:14**.
3. 01:19, signed-out context: `/commodities` read **Up to date**, now with **Last error: … (HTTP 302)** on every source: the anonymous load's refresh had failed again and kept the bytes the signed-in load fetched.
4. Control: `GET https://beancount.io/prices/BTC-USD` answers 302 to `/auth/login?next=%2Fprices%2FBTC-USD` without a cookie and 200 with the `authSess:beancount.io` session cookie, including from a non-browser client.

Holdings show what this costs. At 01:14, before the feed was cached, the signed-in holdings valued BTC at **75637.79 USD**, the implied price of the 2026-09-15 purchase. Once the feed was in, it was **64183.60 USD** (the feed's 2026-11-16 point is 64185.84). A signed-out visitor who arrives after every feed has gone unavailable sees the stale price, with nothing on the page saying so.

## Cause

This is the limitation documented in ADR015's 2026-09-16 amendment and ADR016 §7, and it has real effects on a public ledger:

- `managed-price-cache.ts` `resolveManagedPriceFeed` attaches a cookie only when the caller presented a credential (`managedPriceCookieFor(url, getSessionToken(), …)`). An anonymous load of a public ledger therefore fetches without one, gets the 302, writes `lastError`, and pushes `nextRefreshAt` out by the retry window. A failed fetch keeps the last blob (`servable`), so prices freeze, go stale after `MANAGED_PRICE_STALE_SECONDS`, and become **unavailable** once the head and blob expire (24h TTL).
- The status read behind the Commodities card (`GET /reports/{owner}/{repo}/managed-prices`, GraphQL `getLedgerManagedPrices`) returns records "without parsing the ledger" (ADR015 §8). A signed-in reader who opens Commodities therefore never triggers the refresh their credential could make. Manual refresh needs `ledger.entries.write`, which a reader of `open_ledger/*` does not have.

## Fix (choose one; the first retires the exception as both ADRs already say)

- Mount `/prices/<ALIAS>` as a public route. Per ADR015 the feed is public market data. The route is served outside this monorepo, so the owner of the beancount.io router does this, and the ADR015/016 amendment text then gets retired here.
- Or in the ledger service: when there is no caller credential and the URL's host is the gated host, skip the network fetch, so an anonymous load neither records a 302 nor delays the next refresh, and serve the cached blob. Then also let a credentialed status read refresh feeds that are due. Note that this alone still leaves a ledger with only anonymous visitors unpriced.

## Acceptance

- On `/ledger/open_ledger/crypto-example/commodities` in a fresh signed-out context, after at least `MANAGED_PRICE_STALE_SECONDS` with no signed-in visitor, every source reads **Up to date** with no `HTTP 302` last error, and holdings value BTC at the feed's latest point.
- A signed-in reader who opens only Commodities never sees a source as "Unavailable · No price received yet" while `/prices/<ALIAS>` serves that reader 200.
- A ledger test covers the anonymous gated-host path: no 302 is recorded, and the cached blob is still served.
