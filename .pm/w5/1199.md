# Journal and Overview show a refund as an unsigned "12.00 USD", like a transfer, and returned income as "−50.00 USD" in the money-in green

**Severity:** minor (misleading signal on every journal row of these kinds). **Owner:** dashboard. **Estimate:** 25–40 minutes including a rendered test. **A2:** the journal rows promise "money in is "+" in the money-in colour, money out is "−", and a move between the reader's own accounts is its size, unsigned" (`journal-days.tsx:135-138`). A reader scanning for what came back to them sees a returned purchase written exactly like a transfer, and a salary clawback written as a negative figure in the colour that means money arrived. Mobile already shows a refund as money in (`+$7.00`, w21/745). **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` (`dashboard-qa` routine) on 2026-11-21 against `https://beancount.io`, entry chunk `main-CIyNN76T.js`, isolated headless Chromium at 1440×1000, English/light, signed in as the QA owner on the designated synthetic ledger. ## Cause

`summarizeTransaction` (`dashboard/src/common/lib/beancount/transaction-summary.ts:163-191`) classifies any entry with an expense posting as `kind: "expense"` and any with an income posting as `kind: "income"`, and gives the amount as the negated sum (`sumPostings(…, true)`), so a refund's amount is `+12.00` and a clawback's is `−50.00`. `TransactionAmount` (`dashboard/src/features/journal/components/journal-days.tsx:162-169`) then derives the tone from the kind alone: `expense` → `"out"`, which never prints a `+` (line 216) and never colours, and `income` → `"in"`, which colours the whole figure green regardless of sign (line 197). Only the account-filtered `"change"` tone looks at the sign (lines 212, 216). Both layouts (`journal-lines.tsx:446`, `journal-days.tsx:401,474`) and the Overview's Recent activity (`features/reports/overview/components/recent-activity-card.tsx:143`, through `JournalTable` since `ebbf43fed`) use this one component.

## Fix

Owned by `dashboard`. No API change.

- For `kind: "income"` and `kind: "expense"`, take the direction from the summed amount's sign, as the account-filtered tone does: a positive amount is money in (`+`, money-in colour), a negative one is money out (`−`, default colour). Apply the colour per amount, not to the wrapper, so a multi-currency row cannot colour a negative figure green.
- Leave transfers (unsigned size), the payroll-as-earnings and gross fallbacks, the "Multiple postings" rule, and the account-filtered change as they are (w5/1191 covers sale rows separately).
