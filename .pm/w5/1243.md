# The Journal's "Entry types" and "View" panels are unnamed dialogs: a screen reader announces "dialog" with no name, on the Journal and on every account page (gap in w4/123)

**Severity:** minor. Nothing is lost or written, but the Journal's main filter panel opens as a popup that assistive technology cannot name. **Owner:** `dashboard`. **Estimate:** 15–25 minutes including tests. **A2:** w4/123 established that every popup the dashboard opens carries its own accessible name, and fixed the account search and the shared Combobox. Every other `PopoverContent` in the dashboard now names its popup (see [Blast radius](#blast-radius)). The Journal's **Entry types** panel arrived later with the compact reconciliation Journal (`91daf9b46`, 2026-11-18) and did not follow that rule. A newcomer who uses a screen reader opens **Entry types** to show Open, Price or Document entries and hears only "dialog". **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` (`dashboard-qa` routine) on 2026-11-27 against `https://beancount.io`, entry chunk `main-BBmi61Hs.js`, isolated headless Chromium 158 at 1440×1000, 1024×900 and 390×844, English. Signed in as the QA account, read-only, on the public example ledger. Local HEAD `ad38294e4`.

## Repro

Public ledger, no sign-in needed.

1. Open `/ledger/open_ledger/example/journal?lang=en` at 1440×1000. Activate **Entry types : Transaction, Balance**.
2. The panel opens, and focus moves to its first chip, **Open**. Chromium's accessibility tree (`Accessibility.getFullAXTree`) holds one unignored `dialog` node, and its name is `""`. The panel's visible heading `Entry types` is an `<h3>` that nothing points to.
3. Press Escape. Focus returns to the trigger, as it should.
4. Repeat at 1024×900 and at 390×844. Below the `xl` breakpoint the Journal also has a **View** button, and its panel (headings **Show** and **Layout**) is a second unnamed `dialog`. **Entry types** stays unnamed at both widths.
5. The same two panels are unnamed on an account page, for example `/ledger/open_ledger/example/account/Assets:US:ETrade?lang=en`, because the account journal renders the same `JournalFilters`.
6. Control: a sweep that opened every popover trigger on 18 ledger pages found no other unnamed dialog. Those pages were Overview, Journal, Accounts, Budget, the four statements, Holdings, Statistics, Commodities, Events, Documents, Query, the Files tree and a file, an account page, and Ledger Settings. The time and account pickers, account search and ledger switcher all name their popups.

Repeated from a fresh load at each width.

## Cause

`dashboard/src/features/journal/components/journal-filters.tsx` renders two `PopoverContent`s with no `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`: **Entry types** at line 464, and **View** at line 500 (only in the `compact` branch, narrower than `xl`). Radix gives popover content `role="dialog"` and does not name it. The panel's `section()` helper (lines 438–443) renders each heading as a plain `<h3>` with no `id`, so nothing can reference it. The shared `common/components/ui/popover.tsx` passes ARIA attributes through unchanged, so the fix stays with this caller, as w4/123 required.

## Fix

Owned by `dashboard`. No API change, no new translation key.

- Name the **Entry types** panel with its own localized heading. Either give the `section()` heading an `id` (`useId`) and point `aria-labelledby` at it, or pass `aria-label={t("journal.entryTypes")}`.
- Name the **View** panel with `t("journal.viewLabel")`, the label of the button that opens it. Its two inner headings (**Show** and **Layout**) stay as they are.
- Do not add a default name in the shared `Popover` primitive. Keep the trigger's accessible name, the focus moving to the first chip, focus returning to the trigger on Escape, the chips' `aria-pressed`, and the narrow layout.

## Definition of done

- On `/journal` and `/account/<name>` at 1440×1000, the opened **Entry types** popup is `dialog "Entry types"` in the accessibility tree. At 1024×900 and 390×844, **Entry types** and **View** are `dialog "Entry types"` and `dialog "View"`. In German (`?lang=de`) the names are the German strings for those labels.
- A rendered test of `JournalFilters` opens each panel and queries it with `getByRole("dialog", { name: … })`, in the wide and the compact layout. It fails on the current code. A placeholder or heading-text assertion does not count, because the heading is already on screen today. Dashboard gates pass.

## Blast radius

`JournalFilters` has two callers: `features/journal/pages/journal-page.tsx` (the Journal) and `features/reports/account/index.tsx` (the account journal on every account page). Both are covered by the one change. No other `PopoverContent` in `dashboard/src` lacks a name: `language-selector.tsx`, `date-picker.tsx` (w4/1011), `combobox.tsx` and `go-to-account.tsx` (w4/123) and `ledger-switcher.tsx` (w3/478) use `aria-label`. `ledger-clone-url-menu.tsx` (w4/132), `bulk-target-popover.tsx`, `net-worth-card.tsx` (w4/197) and `ledger-sidebar-group.tsx` (w4/1012) use `aria-labelledby`.

## Dedupe

Searched every board state for `Entry types`, `unnamed dialog`, `dialog has no name`, `PopoverContent`, `View panel` and journal popovers. w4/123 (done, `7829bf2f`) fixed the account search and the shared Combobox, and limited itself to "other popover consumers … not included without their own reproduction". This is that reproduction, for a panel added two months later. w4/1011, w4/1012, w4/132, w4/197 and w3/478 named other popups. w5/1190 and w5/1238 use the Entry types panel to reach their repros but do not touch its name. `git log -S"Entry types"` shows the panel was introduced in `91daf9b46` and has not been changed since.

Actual screen-reader speech, Firefox and Safari, and locales other than English and German (the German check is a source reading: the names will come from the existing `journal.entryTypes` and `journal.viewLabel` catalogs).
