## Objective

Make every Spanish dashboard message address the reader with *tú*, the catalog's majority register and the one `mobile/AGENTS.md` records for Spanish.

## Context

- About 51 of roughly 230 addressing messages use *usted*: the pronoun, *su/sus* addressed to the reader, or formal imperatives (*ingrese, agregue, seleccione, intente, pruebe, busque, revise, asegúrese, verifique*).
- Examples: `importer.upload.fileSizeError` "Por favor, intente con un archivo más pequeño", `collaboration.inviteCollaboratorsDescription` "Busque y seleccione usuarios …", `importer.preview.emptyFileDescription` "… revise su archivo". They cluster in `features/auth` (about 12), `features/bql`, `features/user-settings` and the dashboard home and gallery pages.
- *su* is also the third-person possessive. Only rewrite it where it addresses the reader.

## Steps

1. List the Spanish messages in the *usted* register across `dashboard/src/**/locales/es.ts` and `src/i18n/locales/**/es.ts`.
2. Rewrite each in *tú*, keeping placeholders, plural forms and meaning unchanged.
3. Spot-check Sign in, Import and Ledger Settings → Collaborators with `?lang=es`.

## Files

- `dashboard/src/**/locales/es.ts`, `dashboard/src/i18n/locales/**/es.ts`
