## Objective

Make every Catalan dashboard message address the reader one way, and record the choice so new strings follow it.

## Context

- The Catalan catalog is split evenly: about 152 messages use the plural *vosaltres* form (*vostre/vostra*, imperatives such as *torneu, proveu, afegiu, seleccioneu*) and about 149 use *tu* (*tu/teu/teva*, *pots*, imperatives such as *torna, prova, afegeix, selecciona*). For example, `page.accounts.liabilitiesOwed` "Un saldo negatiu és el que deveu" sits above `page.accounts.closeInLedgerFile` "… afegeix una directiva …". `journal.readFailedLedgerUnchanged` "No ha canviat res del vostre llibre. Torneu-ho a provar …" sits beside `userSettings.manageSubscription` "Gestiona la teva subscripció …".
- `mobile/AGENTS.md` records no Catalan register, and the native Catalan catalog is also mixed (see Out of scope).

## Steps

1. Choose the register: the majority at implementation time, or the established convention for Catalan software, stating the reason in one line.
2. Rewrite the other register's messages across `dashboard/src/**/locales/ca.ts` and `src/i18n/locales/**/ca.ts`, keeping placeholders, plural forms and meaning unchanged.
3. Spot-check Accounts, Sign in, Account Settings and Import with `?lang=ca`.

## Files

- `dashboard/src/**/locales/ca.ts`, `dashboard/src/i18n/locales/**/ca.ts`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every Catalan message that addresses the reader uses the chosen register.
- [ ] The choice and its reason are written where t005 records the per-locale rule.
- [ ] `yarn i18n:check` and `src/test/translations.test.ts` pass.

## Out of scope

- The native app's Catalan catalog (`mobile/src/translations/ca.ts`), which mixes the same two registers. That is a separate mobile note; it should adopt the choice recorded here.
