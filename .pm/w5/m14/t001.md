## Objective

Make every German dashboard message address the reader with *Sie*, the catalog's majority register and the one `mobile/AGENTS.md` records for German.

## Context

- About 34 of roughly 430 addressing messages use *du*: pronouns (*du, dein…, dir, dich*) or informal imperatives (*Füge … hinzu, Wähle, Gib … ein, Prüfe, Versuche, Speichere, Verwende, Öffne, Melde dich …*).
- They cluster in `features/auth/locales/de.ts` (about 15, for example `auth.oidcErrorEmailInUse` "Melde dich mit deinem Passwort an …" and `auth.oidcErrorEmailUnverified`), `features/ai-agent/locales/de.ts` (the chat greeting and placeholder), `features/importer/locales/de.ts` (`importer.access.readOnly`, `importer.access.unresolved`), `features/ledger-list/pages/dashboard-page/locales/de.ts`, `features/user-settings/locales/de.ts` (`userSettings.resumeSubscriptionDescription`), `features/ledger-data/accounts/locales/de.ts` (`page.accounts.closeInLedgerFile`) and the common catalog (`journal.managedPriceEntryExplanation` "… füge für dieses Datum …").
- w21/012's resolution notes that the mobile pass also caught informal imperatives with no pronoun. Look for those as well as the pronouns.

## Steps

1. List the German messages in the *du* register (pronouns and informal imperatives) across `dashboard/src/**/locales/de.ts` and `src/i18n/locales/**/de.ts`.
2. Rewrite each in *Sie*, keeping placeholders, plural forms and meaning unchanged.
3. Spot-check Sign in, Accounts (Equity row menu), Import and the Ask page with `?lang=de`.

## Files

- `dashboard/src/**/locales/de.ts`, `dashboard/src/i18n/locales/**/de.ts`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] No German message uses *du/dein/dir/dich* or a *du* imperative.
- [ ] Placeholders and plural keys are unchanged; `yarn i18n:check` and `src/test/translations.test.ts` pass.

## Out of scope

- Rewording beyond the register change.
