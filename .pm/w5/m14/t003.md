## Objective

Make every Dutch dashboard message address the reader with *je/jouw*, the register `mobile/AGENTS.md` records for Dutch, so the web app and the native app read alike.

## Context

- The Dutch catalog is split nearly evenly: about 147 messages use *u/uw* and about 137 use *je/jouw/jij*. Both appear in every feature, for example `page.accounts.liabilitiesOwed` "Een negatief saldo is wat u verschuldigd bent" beside `page.accounts.closeInLedgerFile` "Hier kun je alleen …", and `journal.readFailedLedgerUnchanged` "… uw grootboek …" beside `journal.discardEntryEditDescription` "Je wijzigingen …".
- The *u* messages cluster in `features/auth` (about 44), `features/user-settings`, `features/importer`, the dashboard home and Ledger Settings → Visibility.
- With *je*, verb forms change too ("Weet u zeker dat u …" becomes "Weet je zeker dat je …", "Probeert u" becomes "Probeer").

## Steps

1. List the Dutch messages in the *u/uw* register across `dashboard/src/**/locales/nl.ts` and `src/i18n/locales/**/nl.ts`.
2. Rewrite each with *je/jouw* and matching verb forms, keeping placeholders, plural forms and meaning unchanged.
3. Spot-check Accounts, Sign in, Account Settings and Import with `?lang=nl`.

## Files

- `dashboard/src/**/locales/nl.ts`, `dashboard/src/i18n/locales/**/nl.ts`
