# The file editor completes a posting's account from the file's loose words: after `Assets:Bank:` it offers "ACME, against, agent, amount…" and never `Checking`, and after `Expenses:Fo` it opens on its own with "Food, for, FOODS"

**Severity:** minor (Fava parity, data entry). The Beancount editor under Files → Edit has Fava's Edit menu: Align Amounts, Toggle Comment, Fold All and Unfold All. Its completion is still Monaco's default word list, though. Monaco splits an account at every `:` and offers any word the file contains, including words from comments, payees and narrations, wherever the cursor is. Suggestions open by themselves as you type, and Enter accepts the highlighted one. Fava's source editor completes the ledger's account names on an indented posting line, plus tags after `#` and links after `^`. **Owner:** `dashboard` (`features/ledger-editor`, `common/lib/editor`). **Estimate:** about an hour, including tests. **A2:** typing postings by hand in the web editor is the fallback when New Entry cannot express an entry. Someone moving from Fava expects account completion there, and here the editor offers wrong words in the account position.

**Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` (`dashboard-qa` routine) on 2026-11-28 against `https://beancount.io`, entry chunk `main-BBmi61Hs.js`. The run used isolated headless Chromium 158 at 1440×1000, English/light, signed in as the QA owner on its designated disposable ledger. That ledger's `main.bean` is the synthetic fixture `backend-cluster/backend-v2/evals/mcp-agent/fixtures/main.bean`. The edit was discarded with **Leave Without Saving**, so nothing was saved and the file's commit stayed `31ecc2c0`. Local HEAD `8a576c696`. ## Repro

On a ledger you can edit whose `main.bean` holds that fixture (it opens `Assets:Bank:Checking`, `Assets:Bank:Savings` and `Expenses:Food:Groceries`, starts with a comment containing "Expected" and "for", and has the payee "WHOLE FOODS"):

1. Open Files → `main.bean` → **Edit**. At the end of the file, add a line `2026-08-30 * "qa" "x"`.
2. On the next line, type `  Expenses:Fo` key by key. The suggestion list opens by itself with **Food, for, FOODS**: a segment, a word from the comment and a word from the payee.
3. Press Enter. The line becomes `  Expenses:Food`, and Enter has accepted a suggestion instead of ending the line.
4. Replace the line with `  Assets:Bank:` and press Ctrl+Space. The list reads **ACME, against, agent, amount, and, are, Assets, balance, Balances, balances, …**. Neither `Checking` nor `Savings`, the two accounts under `Assets:Bank`, is offered.
5. Replace it with `  Exp` and press Ctrl+Space. The list reads **Expected, Expenses**: a comment word and a root, but not one whole account.

Steps 2–5 were repeated from a fresh load with the same lists. Monaco reports `quickSuggestions: {other: "on", comments: "off", strings: "off"}`, `acceptSuggestionOnEnter: "on"`, and word separators that include `:`.

**Expected:** on an indented posting line, and after `open`, `close`, `balance`, `pad`, `note` and `document`, completion offers the ledger's account names whole. Typing `  Assets:Bank:` lists `Assets:Bank:Checking` and `Assets:Bank:Savings`, and choosing one replaces everything from the first segment. Words from comments, payees and narrations are never offered as an account.

## Cause

- `dashboard/src/features/ledger-editor/file-editor/components/ledger-file-view/text-editor.tsx:228-250` (`editorOptions`) leaves Monaco's suggestion defaults in place: quick suggestions on, document word suggestions on, and Enter accepts.
- `dashboard/src/common/lib/editor/monaco-beancount-language-vscode.ts:182` (`registerBeancountLanguage`) registers the language, its tokens and an org-mode folding provider, but no completion provider. Monaco therefore falls back to word-based suggestions, split at `:`.
- The only completion provider in the dashboard is the BQL page's (`features/bql/pages/index.tsx:316`).

## Fix

Owned by `dashboard`.

- Register a Beancount `CompletionItemProvider` once per Monaco instance. In account position (an indented posting line, or after the dated directives above), it offers the ledger's account names. Each suggestion's range covers the whole account token, `:` included. Read the names from the ledger's existing account read (`GetLedgerAccounts`). If tags and links are cheap from the same data, offer them after `#` and `^`.
- Set `wordBasedSuggestions: "off"` for `beancount` models, so loose words stop appearing. Other text files, such as a README, keep Monaco's defaults.
- Leave the Edit menu, read-only mode and the BQL editor as they are.

## Definition of done

- In the repro, `  Assets:Bank:` followed by Ctrl+Space lists `Assets:Bank:Checking` and `Assets:Bank:Savings`, and nothing from comments or payees. Choosing one leaves `  Assets:Bank:Checking` on the line.
- Typing `  Expenses:Fo` offers `Expenses:Food:Groceries` (and the other `Expenses:Food:…` accounts the ledger opens), and never `for` or `FOODS`.
- A test drives the provider through a Monaco model and fails on the current code. For example, it asserts the suggestions at `  Assets:Bank:|` and that a comment word is not among them.

## Adjacent states

- A ledger with no accounts gets an empty list, not word suggestions.
- A failed account read leaves completion empty and editing unaffected.
- Accounts opened in other included files are offered too, since the read is ledger-wide.
