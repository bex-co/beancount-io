# Preserve known Holdings market quotes when basis FX is missing

**Minor · cli · 40–55m · A1 · worker1.** With a direct **150 USD/HOOL** quote,
`report holdings --conversion USD --allow-errors` reports two shares' market
value as **200 EUR**, using acquisition cost or an older EUR quote. Native
Beancount and the real filtered balance sheet both show the known **300 USD**.
The missing EUR→USD rate prevents converting the **200 EUR acquisition basis**;
it should leave that column in EUR while the independently priced market column
shows 300 USD. Partial status and strict exit 1 remain appropriate for the
unconverted basis. This report concerns the partial money fields and quote
selection, not that legitimate warning. No product fix applied.

## Minimal reproduction

Create `main.bean` in a fresh disposable directory:

```beancount
option "operating_currency" "USD"
2024-01-01 open Assets:Cash EUR
2024-01-01 open Assets:Broker HOOL
2024-01-01 open Equity:Opening EUR
2024-01-01 * "Capital"
  Assets:Cash 1000.00 EUR
  Equity:Opening -1000.00 EUR
2024-01-02 * "Purchase"
  Assets:Broker 2 HOOL {100.00 EUR, 2024-01-02, "Original"}
  Assets:Cash -200.00 EUR
2024-01-31 price HOOL 150.00 USD
```

With the isolated environment described above:

```sh
bea --json --no-input --offline --file /absolute/main.bean check
bea --json --no-input --offline --file /absolute/main.bean report holdings --time 2024-01-31 --conversion USD --allow-errors
bea --no-input --offline --file /absolute/main.bean report holdings --time 2024-01-31 --conversion USD --group-by commodity --allow-errors
bea --json --no-input --offline --file /absolute/main.bean report balance-sheet --time 2024-01-31 --conversion USD --account Assets:Broker
```

All four commands exit 0. Check is valid. The filtered balance sheet's known
net worth is **300 USD**. Holdings JSON is partial and gives:

```json
{"cost_basis":{"number":"200.00","currency":"EUR"},
 "market_value":{"number":"200.00","currency":"EUR"},
 "price":null,"price_date":null}
```

Its totals repeat `market_value: {"EUR":"200.00"}`. The human table and footer
print **200.00 EUR** in MARKET VALUE. Diagnostics name missing EUR→USD and
HOOL→EUR. An intermediate HOOL→EUR quote is unnecessary for the available
HOOL→USD market; only the basis lacks an exchange rate.

In another fresh book, add `2024-01-10 price HOOL 100.00 EUR`. Holdings still
shows market 200 EUR, now with that older **100 EUR** quote and January 10 date.
The current direct USD quote remains unused. In either book, adding just
`2024-01-31 price EUR 1.25 USD` restores the working control: basis **250 USD**,
market **300 USD**, price **150 USD**, January 31 date and complete valuation.
The direct security quote did not change. Account and commodity groupings agree.

Independent uncached upstream conversions prove the fields separately:
`convert_position(position, "USD", price_map, 2024-01-31)` yields **300 USD**;
`convert_amount(get_cost(position), "USD", price_map, date)` retains **200 EUR**
without FX and yields **250 USD** with FX. The stock-only balance sheet is a
second real CLI control. The unfiltered sheet contains unconvertible EUR cash,
so its combined headline is not used as the proof.

## Cause and bounded repair

`cli/src/bea_engine/report.py::_holdings:510–523` looks up the basis-currency
rate and direct commodity quote, but enters the target-first branch only when
**both** `direct is not None and rate is not None` (:517). Missing basis FX
therefore sends a known direct market to the cost-currency branch (:525–536),
which uses an older EUR price or falls back to acquisition cost. One `currency`
variable is then attached to both `cost_basis` and `market_value` (:543–544).
The market quote's selection has been coupled to an independent basis conversion.

Separate those two currency/value decisions. Use the available direct market
quote even when basis FX is missing; retain basis/average cost in its own EUR
with the genuine missing EUR→USD basis diagnostic. The market field, price and
quote date must name **300 USD**, **150 USD/HOOL** and **2024-01-31**. Preserve
partial status/strict failure for that unresolved basis. Keep the existing
cost-currency fallback when a direct market quote is genuinely absent, and the
independent complete-basis conversion when FX exists. Reuse the existing native
conversion/price primitives; no new dependency or upstream fork is needed.

The single engine `answer` dispatch at `report.py:130–131` serves both groupings.
`cli/src/cli/commands/report.py:458–503` consumes the same payload: JSON emits
it, the human row prints `group["market_value"]` at :495, and the footer prints
its totals at :503. Decimal serialization preserves those already wrong amounts;
formatting is not the cause. Search found no other `_holdings` caller.
Both output forms and groupings were exercised. No server API change is involved.
