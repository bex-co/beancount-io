# Report actual short-position reductions in Capital Gains

**Major · cli · 40–55m · A1 · worker1.** `bea report capital-gains`
omits positive postings that close negative inventory, so a booked short
cover's **20 USD gain** or **20 USD loss** becomes **no sales / zero total**,
exit **0**, `valuation: complete`. It instead labels the negative posting
that opened the short position a sale of two units at zero gain. Native
inventory, BQL and the income statement retain the actual realized gain/loss.
Source files remain unchanged; no product fix implemented.

The profitable case opens **−2 HOOL {100 USD} @ 100 USD**, then covers
one unit with **+1 HOOL {100 USD, 2026-01-02} @ 80 USD**, Cash **−80 USD**

Root: `cli/src/bea_engine/report.py::_capital_gains:767–775` equates a

a new inventory-based classifier must not skip the opening context needed
to recognize a later reduction. `--time` similarly needs its correct
opening inventory, not only explicitly priced rows inside the window.
