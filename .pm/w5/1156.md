# Ledger Settings: none of its six section titles is a heading, so heading navigation finds only "No Collaborators"

**Severity:** minor (accessibility; WCAG 1.3.1 Info and Relationships, 2.4.6 Headings and Labels). **Owner:** dashboard. **Estimate:** 20–30 minutes including a rendered outline test. **A2:** screen-reader users move through a settings page by its headings. Ledger Settings holds the ledger's rename, visibility switch, collaborators and Delete Ledger, and none of those sections can be reached that way. **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` on 2026-11-18 against `https://beancount.io` (entry chunk `main-LGv2TZLj.js`), QA-owned headless Chromium 153 at 1440×1000 and 390×844, English/light. Signed-in QA owner (admin) on its designated disposable synthetic ledger. Read-only: nothing was saved. Local HEAD `50f9bca2f`.

## Repro

1. Open `/ledger/<owner>/<ledger>/settings` and wait for it to settle.
2. List the headings inside `main` (`h1–h6, [role=heading]`). Result: `h1 "Ledger Settings"`, then `h3 "No Collaborators"` (the collaborators empty state), then `h2 "Related Pages"`.
3. The visible section titles **General Settings**, **Visibility**, **Collaborators**, **Beancount Options**, **Fava Options** and **Danger Zone** are plain `div` text. The page jumps from h1 to an h3 inside a section that itself has no heading.

The same sweep over 15 other ledger routes found untitled card sections only on Connected Accounts (four feature cards) and Cash Flow (one "Cash & cash equivalents in this report" card). Ledger Settings is the only ledger page where every section is untitled. Account Settings, the control, has an h1 and h2 sections since w3/476.

## Cause

Each section titles its card with `CardTitle` (`common/components/ui/card.tsx:31-39`, a `div`): `features/ledger-data/settings/general-settings-section/index.tsx:193`, `visibility-section/index.tsx:176`, `collaborators-section/index.tsx:153`, `options-table-section.tsx:66` (Beancount and Fava Options), and `danger-zone-section/index.tsx:101`. The collaborators empty and error states use `h3` (`collaborators-section/index.tsx:211,234`).

## Fix

Make each section title a real `h2`, in the pattern the account settings pages use (`<CardTitle><h2>…</h2></CardTitle>`, as in `features/user-settings/pages/general/oidc-section.tsx:73-76`). Keep the existing `id`s that the Collaborators and options tables use for `aria-labelledby`, and put them on the element that carries the text. Keep the collaborators empty and error headings as `h3` under their new `h2`. Do not change the shared `CardTitle` primitive, and keep the visual design. The read-only view for viewers without admin rights (w3/466) gets the same `h2` for each section it renders.

## Definition of done

- The admin view's outline is `h1 Ledger Settings`, then `h2` General Settings, Visibility, Collaborators (with its `h3` empty state when there are none), Beancount Options, Fava Options and Danger Zone, then `h2 Related Pages`. The read-only viewer view outlines only the sections it renders.
- The Collaborators, Beancount Options and Fava Options tables keep their accessible names.
- A rendered test asserts both outlines and fails on the current code. Dashboard gates pass.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` for Ledger Settings, `ledger-data/settings`, CardTitle, and settings headings. w3/done/476 gave the account `/settings/*` pages their headings, and explicitly scoped the work to those pages. w4/done/m20 and w4/done/242 named the Settings tables. w0/done/013 named the visibility switch. w3/done/466 made the page read-only for non-admins. None adds section headings to Ledger Settings. Not deployment lag: HEAD has the same markup.
