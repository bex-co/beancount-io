# When the session ends, every open ledger tab jumps to sign-in within 30 seconds and throws away a typed New Entry or file edit, without saying why

**Severity:** major (lost input, no warning). **Owner:** dashboard. **Estimate:** 1–1½ hours including rendered tests. **A2:** a reader who logs out in one tab, or whose session is revoked, loses whatever they were typing in every other tab. The page does not even wait for them to press Save. **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` on 2026-11-18 against `https://beancount.io` (entry chunk `main-LGv2TZLj.js`), headless Chromium 153 at 1440×1000, English/light, signed in as the QA owner on the synthetic ledger `mcp-agent-eval`. Nothing was saved: every refused write was checked with `checkLedger` (16 transactions before and after). Local HEAD `a95dfa7d3`.

## Repro

1. **Logout in another tab.** In tab A, open `/ledger/<owner>/<ledger>/journal?action=new-entry&directive=transaction` and type `qa-20261118-two-tab payee` into Payee. In tab B (same browser), open **Settings → General** and choose **Logout**. Leave tab A alone. About 20 s later tab A navigates by itself to `/auth/login?next=…action%3Dnew-entry…` ("Welcome back"). There is no question and no message. After signing in, `next` reopens New Entry with Payee and Narration empty.
2. **Same with cleared cookies, no click at all.** With a New Entry draft (payee, narration, two postings), clear the site's cookies. The next `GetLatestLedgerCommit` poll (24–25 s later) sends the tab to sign-in, and the draft is gone.
3. **Pressing Create.** Same draft, clear cookies, choose **Create Transaction Entry**. `BulkEntries` answers `UNAUTHENTICATED` and the tab goes straight to sign-in. The draft is gone.
4. **File editor.** Open `main.bean`, choose **Edit**, type a line, and clear cookies. 27 s later, with no action from the reader, the browser raises its own "Leave site? Changes you made may not be saved." `beforeunload` prompt. **Save** raises the same prompt. If the reader chooses **Stay**, the only explanation is a toast, "Please sign in to continue.", with no way to sign in that keeps the draft. **Leave** discards it.

## Cause

- `dashboard/src/common/apollo/links/auth-error-link.ts:103-112`: every `UNAUTHENTICATED` response, from a query or a mutation (outside the auth-flow and identity-probe sets), runs `window.location.assign("/auth/login?next=…")`. It does not look at whether the page holds unsaved input.
- `dashboard/src/common/components/ledger-layout/ledger-out-of-date-indicator.tsx:26-29` polls `GetLatestLedgerCommit` every 30 s on every ledger page, so the redirect fires on its own within 30 s of the session ending.
- The file editor's `useBlocker({ enableBeforeUnload })` (`features/ledger-editor/file-editor/components/ledger-file-view/file-edit-mode.tsx:74-81`) is why the editor gets a bare browser prompt. The `useDiscardDraftGuard` dialogs (New Entry, Add Budget, Open Account, Create Ledger, the key dialogs) install no `beforeunload` (w5/1166), so they leave without any prompt.
- `redirecting` (`auth-error-link.ts:32`, set at `:109`) is never reset. After a cancelled `beforeunload`, no later `UNAUTHENTICATED` redirects again until a full reload.

## Fix

A session that ends while the page holds unsaved input must not navigate away from it.

- Background reads (the out-of-date poll, refetches) must not trigger a full-page redirect while any draft guard reports a dirty draft. Instead show one persistent, focusable notice: "You've been signed out. Sign in again to save what you typed." Give it a **Sign in** action that keeps the draft. For example, open sign-in in a new tab, then retry once the session is back; or ask before leaving and say that the draft will be lost.
- A write refused as `UNAUTHENTICATED` keeps its draft mounted, with the same notice beside the submit control. This follows the AGENTS rule that a failed write keeps its draft.
- With no dirty draft, keep today's redirect. Reset `redirecting` if the navigation is cancelled.
- Registering "this page has a dirty draft" can reuse what `useDiscardDraftGuard` and the editor's `hasUnsavedChanges` already compute.

## Definition of done

- With a typed New Entry draft, logging out in another tab leaves tab A on the journal with the draft intact and a visible signed-out notice. Signing in again (new tab) and pressing **Create** saves the entry once.
- The file editor with a draft shows the same notice instead of a spontaneous `beforeunload` prompt. Save after re-sign-in commits the draft.
- A page with no draft still goes to `/auth/login?next=…` as today.
- Rendered tests cover the poll path and the submit path with and without a dirty draft, and fail on the current code. Dashboard gates pass.
