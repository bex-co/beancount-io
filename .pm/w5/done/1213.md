# Accounts tells every Income, Expenses and Equity row "Only an account with a zero balance can be closed.", even one with no postings or a zero total

**Severity:** minor (no data is at risk, but the stated reason is false and points the reader at the wrong fix). **Owner:** dashboard. **Estimate:** 20–30 minutes including the 15 locales and tests. **A2:** the Accounts row menu now keeps unavailable actions in place, greyed, with the reason under them, so a reader learns what to do next. On an Income, Expenses or Equity row the reason says the account needs a zero balance. That is not why Close is unavailable. Close on these rows is withheld by type (w1/done/025): the API does not compute their balance, so the page never offers it, whatever the account holds. A reader who believes the reason may post an entry to "zero" an expense account, which distorts every income statement the account appears in. An account with no postings (zero by definition) and a clearing account whose postings net to zero get the same contradictory message. **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` on 2026-11-21 against `https://beancount.io` (dashboard entry chunk `main-CIyNN76T.js`), QA-owned headless Chromium at 1440×1000, English/light, no filters, signed in as the QA owner on the designated disposable ledger. Local HEAD `61781b961`.

## Repro

1. On a ledger you can write, open `/ledger/<owner>/<ledger>/accounts` and open **Actions for Income:Salary** (any Income, Expenses or Equity row with postings). The menu reads: **Open register**, greyed **Close Account** with "Only an account with a zero balance can be closed.", greyed **Delete** with "Only an account with no postings can be deleted." `Equity:Opening-Balances` and `Expenses:Food:Coffee` read the same.
2. Add an unused account and a clearing account whose two postings cancel (the probe used here, since removed):

   ```beancount
   2026-08-30 open Expenses:Qa-20261121-Clearing USD
   2026-08-30 open Expenses:Qa-20261121-Unused USD

   2026-08-30 * "qa-20261121 clearing out"
     Expenses:Qa-20261121-Clearing   10.00 USD
     Assets:Cash                    -10.00 USD

   2026-08-30 * "qa-20261121 clearing back"
     Expenses:Qa-20261121-Clearing  -10.00 USD
     Assets:Cash                     10.00 USD
   ```

3. Reload Accounts. `Expenses:Qa-20261121-Unused` (Postings 0) offers **Delete**, but its Close is greyed with "Only an account with a zero balance can be closed." `Expenses:Qa-20261121-Clearing` (Postings 2, lifetime total 0.00 USD) has Close greyed with the same zero-balance reason and Delete greyed too. The page has no way to close it, and the message says it has the wrong balance when it has none.
4. `getLedgerAccountDirectives` returns `{"account":"Expenses:Qa-20261121-Unused","balance":null,"entryCount":0}` and `{"account":"Expenses:Qa-20261121-Clearing","balance":null,"entryCount":2}`. Every Income, Expenses and Equity account gets `balance: null`, and every Assets/Liabilities account gets a units map.
5. Control: an Assets row with a balance (`Assets:Cash`, 49.75 USD) shows the same greyed Close and reason, and that reason is true there.

Repeated from a fresh load. The probe was written and then restored byte for byte (main.bean back to its snapshot blob, no ledger errors).

## Cause

- `backend-cluster/ledger/src/features/ledger/service/ledger-account-service.ts:173-176`: `getAccountDirectives` computes balances only under the asset and liability roots, so every other account's `balance` is `null` (as w1/done/025 documented).
- `dashboard/src/features/ledger-data/accounts/index.tsx:90-93`: `isBalanceEmpty(null)` is `false` (w1/done/025: "unknown, never emptied"), so `canClose` (`:284`) is false for every such row, whatever its postings.
- `index.tsx:331-335` shows `page.accounts.closeNeedsZeroBalance` under every greyed Close, whatever the reason Close is unavailable. Before `97c05f2bb` (the chart-of-accounts rebuild) these rows had no Close item at all. The rebuild made it a greyed item with a reason and reused the balance reason for the type rule. `__tests__/account-row.test.tsx:99-111` pins that text for a `balance: null` row.

## Fix

Owned by `dashboard`; no API change. Keep w1/done/025's rule that Close is offered only for a balance known to be zero, so these rows stay unavailable. Whether a used Income or Expenses account may close at all is the open owner decision in w1/blocked/254 (the CLI side of the same question), and this note does not settle it. Change what the greyed item says:

- A row with a computed balance (Assets, Liabilities) keeps "Only an account with a zero balance can be closed."
- A row whose balance is not computed (`balance == null`) gets a new key saying the true rule and the way to do it. For example: "This page closes only asset and liability accounts. To close this one, add a close line to its ledger file." Do not name the roots by their English defaults, because a ledger may rename them (w5/1189).
- Add the key to all 15 locales (`yarn i18n:prepare`, then translate; no English copies).

If w1/blocked/254 is decided for option (b), offering Close on these rows is a follow-up, not part of this note.

## Definition of done

- On a writable ledger's Accounts page, greyed Close on Income, Expenses and Equity rows (including one with no postings and one whose postings net to zero) shows the new reason, not "zero balance". A funded Assets row still shows "Only an account with a zero balance can be closed.", and an emptied Assets account still offers Close.
- `account-row.test.tsx` asserts the new reason for a `balance: null` row and the zero-balance reason for a computed nonzero balance, and fails on the current code. Translation shape and stray-English tests pass. Dashboard gates (`yarn format:check`, `yarn lint`, `yarn test`, `yarn build`) pass.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` (open, `done/`, `blocked/`) for "zero balance can be closed", `closeNeedsZeroBalance`, `isBalanceEmpty`, `canClose`, "Close Account" and close together with Income/Expenses/flow account. w1/done/025 withheld Close for `balance: null` rows; this note keeps that. w1/blocked/254 is the CLI's open owner decision on closing used flow accounts; this note does not depend on it. w2/blocked/095 is the hosted API accepting closes over a balance. w5/1182 is closed asset accounts reading "Not computed", and w5/1202 is Delete offered for an account a balance assertion names. w4/done/1084 and w4/done/1085 cover the Close dialog itself. None covers the reason text. The rebuild that introduced it (`97c05f2bb`) is in production, so this is not deployment lag.

## Unverified

Other locales were not opened. They translate the same key, so they carry the same claim. The phone layout (menu rows at `pointer-coarse`) was not repeated.
