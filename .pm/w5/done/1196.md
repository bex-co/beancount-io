# On a phone, three ledger pages scroll sideways: the account page's conversion toolbar (gap in w3/147), the Bank Sync review header, and Smart Import's CSV example

**Severity:** minor (layout). Each page still works, but on a phone the whole content area pans sideways. The Bank Sync review is the worst case: its card title reads one letter per line, and **Submit**, the page's main action, sits off-screen. **Owner:** `dashboard`. **Estimate:** 30–45 minutes for all three, including a real-browser width check. **A2:** a reader on a phone browser opens an account, reviews bank rows, or reads the CSV format help, and the page slides under their thumb. **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` on 2026-11-21 against `https://beancount.io` (entry chunk `main-CIyNN76T.js`, unchanged from the last run), headless Chromium, English (German, French and Russian as controls), light theme, signed in as the QA owner. Local HEAD `c9d492c18`. Read-only: no ledger write, no bank action. The report-conversion choice is a browser cookie, so it was set back to At Cost.

All three are measured on the ledger layout's content scroller (`div.relative.flex.min-h-0.flex-1.flex-col.overflow-auto`). Its `scrollWidth` should equal its `clientWidth`.

## 1. Account page: the interval and conversion selects run off the right edge (gap in w3/147)

1. Open `/ledger/open_ledger/example/account/Assets:US:BofA:Checking?lang=en` at 390×844 and choose **At Market Value** in the conversion select.
2. The toolbar keeps **Account Balance**, **Monthly** and **At Market Value** on one line, at x 16–174, 182–284 and 292–444. The scroller is 444px wide in a 390px viewport. Scrolling it moves every card 54px left, cutting off the chart's y-axis and the start of each card.
3. Other widths and choices: **Converted to USD** gives 457 at 390; **At Cost** gives 389 at both 320 and 360, so a 320 or 360px phone pans 69px or 29px even on the default. German (**Umgerechnet in USD**) gives 450 at 390, French 449, Russian 512.
4. Control: at the same widths and choices, Balance Sheet, Income Statement, Cash Flow and Trial Balance wrap their toolbars, and the scroller stays equal to the viewport.

**Cause:** `dashboard/src/features/reports/account/index.tsx:549` lays the toolbar out as `flex items-center justify-between gap-2`, with no wrap, beside a `shrink-0` select group. w3/147's fix (`86ca4531`) changed the same row to `flex flex-wrap items-center justify-between gap-2` in `balance-sheet-content.tsx:228`, `income-statement-content.tsx:247` and `cash-flow-content.tsx:261`, but did not touch the account page.

**Fix:** give the account toolbar the statement reports' wrapping row, so the selects move under the view picker when they do not fit.

## 2. Bank Sync review: the header squeezes the title to one letter per line and pushes Submit off-screen

1. On a ledger with a connected bank and staged rows (a QA-owned ledger with nine), open `/ledger/<owner>/<ledger>/link?lang=en` at 390×900.
2. The card header's **Bank Transactions** title and its "Review and submit 9 transactions to your ledger" description get a 12px-wide column, 240px tall, one or two letters per line. The file picker ("Choose which file to import into"), **Delete** (x 344–437) and **Submit** (x 445–540) stay on one line, so Submit is entirely off-screen. The scroller is 540px wide, 582 in German.
3. Controls: at 768 and wider the title has room (138px at 768, 309px at 1024) and both buttons fit.

**Cause:** `dashboard/src/features/plaid/components/transaction-review-table.tsx:534` lays the header out as `flex items-center justify-between`, with no wrap and no `min-w-0`. Its action group holds a fixed `w-70` (280px) file picker plus Delete and Submit, about 490px that cannot shrink, so the title column takes all of the shrinking.

**Fix:** below `sm`, stack the title above the actions and let the action group wrap, with the file picker at full width rather than a fixed 280px. The title must keep its natural width at every size.

## 3. Smart Import: the CSV format example overflows its card below 379px

1. Open `/ledger/<owner>/<ledger>/import?lang=en` at 360×800 (any ledger you can write).
2. Under **CSV format reference**, the example line `2025-12-01,Starbucks,Coffee Shop Purchase,-4.50` runs past the card. The `<pre>` holds 346px of text in a 294px box (`white-space: pre`, `overflow-x: visible`). The scroller is 379px wide at every viewport narrower than that: it pans 59px at 320, 19px at 360 and 4px at 375.

**Cause:** `dashboard/src/features/importer/components/steps/upload/file-upload.tsx:229` renders `CSV_FORMAT_EXAMPLE` in `<pre className="text-xs bg-background p-2 rounded border">`, which has no overflow handling.

**Fix:** add `overflow-x-auto` (and `max-w-full`) to that `<pre>`, so the example scrolls inside its own box. Do not reflow or shorten the CSV lines: the reader copies them exactly.
