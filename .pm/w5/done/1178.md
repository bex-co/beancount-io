# Overview shows a month with no net worth change as a rise: a green "↗ 0.00 USD · 0%" badge

**Severity:** minor (misleading signal). **Owner:** dashboard. **Estimate:** 15–25 minutes including tests. **A2:** the Net Worth figure in Financial position carries a "from previous month" badge. Every change that does not start with "-" is drawn in emerald with a rising-trend icon, including exactly zero. A month with no balance-sheet movement therefore reads as growth. Mobile shows a flat change in its neutral colour (w21/done/237). **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` on 2026-11-18 against `https://beancount.io` (entry chunk `main-LGv2TZLj.js`), headless Chromium at 1440×1000, English, isolated profile, signed in as the QA owner on a disposable QA ledger. Local HEAD `c12e43044`. Read-only.

## Repro

1. Open the Overview of a ledger whose net worth is the same at the end of the shown month and at the end of the month before. The QA disposable ledger opens on June 2025 with 2,500,570.00 USD in both May and June.
2. **Actual:** Financial position reads "Net Worth 2,500,570.00 USD", followed by an emerald badge "↗ 0.00 USD · 0%" and "from previous month". The same badge appeared on a second ledger state (October and November 2026 both at 2,500,558.00 USD).
3. Controls: +2,013.11 USD on `open_ledger/example` (August 2017) reads "↗ +2,013.11 USD · +1.8%" in emerald ✓. A negative change reads rose with a falling icon (from the code, `:553-560`).

**Expected:** a change of exactly zero is neutral: muted colour, no rising or falling icon (a flat or minus icon is fine), reading "0.00 USD · 0%". Positive stays emerald and rising, negative stays rose and falling.

## Cause

`dashboard/src/features/reports/overview/components/net-worth-card.tsx:419` sets `falling = change?.startsWith("-")`. `:553-562` then picks the emerald classes and `TrendingUp` for everything that is not falling, zero included. The "+" sign already uses `isPositiveDecimalNumber(change)` (`:563`), so zero is already treated as "not positive" there, but not for colour and icon. The badge's share uses `signDisplay: "exceptZero"` (`:425-432`) and prints "0%" for a rounded zero. Last changed in `cbb2c09b5` (table alignment only), so production matches `main`.

## Fix

Owned by `dashboard`. Derive three states from the exact decimal: positive (`isPositiveDecimalNumber`), negative (starts with "-" and is non-zero), and zero (neither, including "-0.00"). Render the zero state with neutral muted classes and a flat icon or none. Keep the rest of the badge as it is.

## Definition of done

- A month equal to the previous one shows the badge "0.00 USD · 0%" in the neutral style, with no `TrendingUp` icon.
- +2,013.11 still reads emerald and rising, and −12.00 still reads rose and falling.
- A `net-worth-card` test renders a zero, a positive and a negative change and asserts the icon and colour class of each. The zero case fails on the current code. Dashboard gates pass.

Searched `.pm/**`, including `done/` and `blocked/`, for `TrendingUp`, "zero change", "no change", "0.00 USD · 0%", rising and falling arrows, and "from previous month". w21/done/237 is the mobile fix for the same signal. w4/done/993 (same-currency comparison), w21/done/995 (future-dated headline) and w5/1144 (badge contrast) do not cover the zero state.
