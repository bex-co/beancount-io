# Statement Markdown and print exports write USD to five decimals (109,529.33944, Reconciliation Difference 0.00000) where the page shows 109,529.34

**Severity:** minor (financial presentation: the page and its own export disagree, and USD is not written to five places). **Owner:** dashboard. **Estimate:** 30–45 minutes. **A3:** an exported statement that someone shares or prints should state US-dollar amounts the way the page does and the way a reader expects. **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` on 2026-11-18 against `https://beancount.io` (entry chunk `main-LGv2TZLj.js`), QA-owned headless Chromium 153 at 1440×1000, English/light, public `open_ledger/example` plus the QA owner's synthetic ledger. Read-only. Local HEAD `50f9bca2f`.

## Repro

1. Open `/ledger/open_ledger/example/balance-sheet` with conversion **At Cost**. The Assets List shows `Assets 109,529.34`, and its children `RGAGX 49,049.67` and `VBMPX 32,700.35`.
2. **Export → Markdown report**. The statement summary reads:
   - `**Total Assets** | **USD** | **109,529.33944**`
   - `**Total Equity** | **USD** | **106,826.04944**`
   - `**Reconciliation Difference** | **USD** | **0.00000**`

   The supporting detail reads `Assets:US:Vanguard:RGAGX | USD | 49,049.66613`.
3. **At Market Value** and **Converted to USD**: Total Assets `120,352.77828`, RGAGX `48,471.38532`.
4. **Units**: Total Equity `906.58000` and Total Liabilities and Equity `3,609.87000`, beside Total Assets `3,609.87`. Equity:Unrealized reads `(105,919.46944)`, and Equity:Conversions reads `(0.00056)` where the page shows `0.00`.
5. **Cash Flow → Export → Markdown**: Net Cash from Investing `(105,919.46944)`, Period Start `(0.00056)`, Net Change `3,609.87056`. The page shows `-105,919.47`, `0.00` and `3,609.87`.
6. **Print / Save as PDF** (the print-media DOM) prints the same digits, for example `Total Equity USD 906.58000`.

Control: on the synthetic ledger, whose USD amounts all have two decimals, the exports print `17,251.76` and `0.00`, matching the page. Each export was repeated from a fresh load.

## Cause

`dashboard/src/features/reports/export/amount.ts:2-27` `formatStatementAmount` keeps every fraction digit of the decimal it gets, padded to at least two. It never applies the currency's minor units. Amounts priced from lots (units × cost or price) arrive with five digits, and exact sums keep the widest scale (`export/model.ts` `addDecimals`), so `906.58` becomes `906.58000`. The Markdown renderer (`export/markdown.ts:96,142,170,190`) and the printable statement (`export/printable-statement.tsx:274,319,360`) both format through it. The page formats the same values with `formatNumber(value, currency)`, which writes an ISO currency at its minor units (w0/010; `common/lib/format/format-number.ts` `currencyMinorDigits`). That is the rule the dashboard `AGENTS.md` states: "ISO currencies keep their minor units."

## Fix

In Markdown and print, write an ISO 4217 currency amount at its minor-unit digits (`currencyMinorDigits`), with the same rounding the page uses, so page and export agree digit for digit. Non-ISO commodities (GLD, RGAGX, VACHR, …) keep their source digits exactly as today. Keep computing totals and the Reconciliation Difference on the exact decimals, and round only for display, once per printed figure: a statement that balances must still print `0.00`, never a rounded-away difference. Leave CSV `raw_amount` exact. Review `cash-flow-content.tsx:101`, the one page caller of `formatStatementAmount`, so the page stays consistent.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` for `formatStatementAmount`, `33944`, `0.00000`, five decimals, and export precision. w0/done/010 fixed the page side of this same disagreement (it noted "the page and its own Markdown export disagree on the digits") and brought the page to minor units. The exports were left on the exact formatter. w4/done/289 fixed print clipping of long amounts, not their precision. w4/done/m45 preserved commodity digits, which this fix keeps. Not deployment lag: HEAD has the same formatter.
