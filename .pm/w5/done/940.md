# Keep known net profit when unpriced income and expense cancel

**Minor · cli · 15–25m · A1.** Equal income and expense in the same unpriced
commodity cancel exactly, leaving a known 20 USD profit. The income statement
headline and balance sheet report that 20, but its sole monthly period says
`net_profit: {"USD": null}` / `Unavailable USD`. No product fix implemented.

Real CLI QA, 2026-10-21, HEAD `a0e7a44ef`, checkout `bea 0.3.1`, macOS
26.5.1 arm64, frontend Python 3.13.3, existing managed Python 3.12.10.
Separate subprocesses, closed stdin/no TTY, isolated absolute
`BEA_CONFIG_DIR`/`XDG_CACHE_HOME`, `BEA_NO_UPDATE_NOTIFIER=1`, no inherited
`BEA_FILE`/`BEA_TOKEN`. All fixtures are synthetic, local and plugin-free.

Create disposable `/abs/books/main.bean`:

```beancount
option "operating_currency" "USD"
2024-01-01 open Income:Gift GOLD
2024-01-01 open Expenses:Gift GOLD
2024-01-01 open Income:Salary USD
2024-01-01 open Expenses:Fee USD
2024-01-01 open Assets:Cash USD
2024-02-01 * "Gift received and consumed"
  Income:Gift -10.00 GOLD
  Expenses:Gift 10.00 GOLD
2024-02-01 * "Work less fee"
  Income:Salary -25.00 USD
  Expenses:Fee 5.00 USD
  Assets:Cash 20.00 USD
```

From `/abs/books`, in the isolated child environment above:

```sh
bea --json -f /abs/books/main.bean check
bea --json -f /abs/books/main.bean query "SELECT currency,sum(number) WHERE account ~ '^(Income|Expenses):' GROUP BY currency ORDER BY currency"
bea --json -f /abs/books/main.bean report income-statement --time 2024-02 --conversion USD --allow-errors
bea -f /abs/books/main.bean report income-statement --time 2024-02 --conversion USD --allow-errors
bea --json -f /abs/books/main.bean report balance-sheet --time 2024-02 --conversion USD --allow-errors
```

Actual: all five exit 0; sources remain byte-identical. BQL gives net income
plus expense of 0 GOLD and -20 USD. The income statement's headline
`net_profit` is `{"USD":"20.00"}`, as is the balance sheet's. Its only
period, dated 2024-02-29, instead returns `{"USD":null}` for net profit.
Text likewise prints `Net Profit: 20.00 USD` above a period whose net profit
is `Unavailable USD`. The income and expense cells appropriately retain their
unpriced GOLD components and `valuation` is `partial`.

Expected: the period's net profit is 20 USD too. The same-day -10/+10 GOLD
movements net to zero at any exchange rate; a missing quote cannot change
the remaining `-(-25 + 5) = 20 USD`. Keep component-level partial valuation,
missing-price details and the existing strict-mode exit-1 policy. This is a
known combined total, not permission to treat an unknown residual as zero.
USAGE defines partial combined profit from whether that total itself still
holds an unvalued commodity, with the same rule for each interval row.

Working public control: in a pristine copy add
`2024-01-01 price GOLD 2.00 USD`. The monthly period then correctly reports
20 USD alongside the same headline, even though the profit calculation did
not need that quote. Removing the USD transaction gives the smaller failure:
headline zero USD and sole period null. Both variants reproduced twice.

Runtime coverage: eight initial books, two repetitions × cancelling
unpriced/residual unpriced/cancelling priced/cancelling USD. All checks pass;
only cancelling-unpriced has a known zero headline with a null sole period.
Twelve further fresh books, two repetitions × same-day/across-days/
across-months/residual GOLD/different commodities/priced control, retain

income statements, balance sheet, strict mode, Income-only filtering and

Same-day cancellation is hidden in the bucket containing that day at every
grain. Across-day cancellation within one month/week is also hidden when the
bucket contains both legs. Across-month controls correctly retain null for
the separate January and February residuals; only their combined quarter has
a known 20 USD profit. A remaining 1 GOLD or noncancelling GOLD/SILV pair
correctly keeps headline and relevant periods null. Income-only filtering
correctly remains unknown because the expense is excluded. Priced controls
return known period profits throughout. Strict unpriced calls still exit 1
with the expected missing-price details; no strict-policy change is needed.

Cause: `cli/src/bea_engine/report.py:_income_statement` (:272–283) formats
the net-profit period with `_summary(-profit.balance, ...)`, but sets
`incomplete` from `_unvalued(income.balance)` **or**
`_unvalued(expenses.balance)`. It never checks whether their unpriced
components cancel in that bucket's already-combined `profit.balance`.
The headline instead passes the combined total through `_headline`
(:979–988), whose `_unvalued` predicate (:892–901) correctly ignores a
zero foreign-commodity remainder. The balance sheet and net-worth summary
series use that total-based rule as well.

Fix the period net-profit scalar to judge its own combined balance, as the
headline does. Preserve each component's unpriced amounts and missing-price
warnings, real residual uncertainty, account filtering, bucket boundaries
and per-unit conversions. Do not copy the full-period headline into rows:
separate buckets may genuinely remain unvalued even when the whole period
nets to known USD. Add regressions for both exact zero and known nonzero USD
profit, plus the noncancelling and cross-bucket controls. No dependency or
backend/API change needed.

**Resolved 2026-10-22** — fixed: the income statement's per-period `net_profit` (`cli/src/bea_engine/report.py::_income_statement`) now judges its own combined balance, as the headline and balance sheet do, instead of marking itself unavailable whenever income or expenses alone held an unpriced commodity. Unpriced income and expense that cancel within a bucket leave the known profit (20 USD in the public recipe, zero when only the cancelling pair exists); a genuine residual, an income-only `--account` filter and separate unpriced months stay unavailable, and component-level partial valuation is unchanged. Regression tests in `cli/tests/test_report_income_statement_cancelling_unpriced.py` fail without the fix. Shipped in this commit (`cli`).
