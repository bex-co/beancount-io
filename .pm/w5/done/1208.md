# Journal rows name one account twice and hide another: a margin buy reads "Assets:QaMargin → Assets:QaMargin" with no "+1", though Equity:Opening is the third posting

**Severity:** minor (no amount is wrong, but the row's account summary hides an account the entry touches and does not count it). **Owner:** dashboard. **Estimate:** 20–30 minutes including tests. **A2:** each Journal row says where the money went as "from → to", then "+N" for the other accounts the entry touches. When one account sits on both sides of an entry, the row names that account twice and the "+N" leaves out an account. A buy inside one brokerage account that holds both cash and the security (`Assets:Broker -1,005.00 USD`, `Assets:Broker 10 VTI {100.00 USD}`, `Expenses:Commissions 5.00 USD`), a currency exchange inside one account, or a margin buy are ordinary entries of this shape. Since `ebbf43fed` the same rows also make up the account register and the Overview's Recent activity. **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` on 2026-11-21 against `https://beancount.io` (dashboard entry chunk `main-CIyNN76T.js`), QA-owned headless Chromium 153 at 1440×1000, English/light, no filters, signed in as the QA owner. Read-only. Local HEAD `918c204c6`.

## Repro

The QA account's disposable synthetic ledger holds this entry:

```beancount
2026-07-01 * "qa-20261102 margin buy VEUR"
  Assets:QaMargin   10 VEUR {50.00 EUR}
  Assets:QaMargin  -400.00 EUR
  Equity:Opening   -100.00 EUR
```

1. Open the ledger's Journal (default Compact layout). The row reads "qa-20261102 margin buy VEUR · **Assets:QaMargin → Assets:QaMargin** · 10 VEUR · 3". There is no "+1".
2. Expand its postings: Assets:QaMargin 10 VEUR (cost 50.00 EUR), Assets:QaMargin -400.00 EUR, **Equity:Opening -100.00 EUR**. The row summary never mentions Equity:Opening.
3. Switch to the Comfortable layout: the row's account cell reads the same, "Assets:QaMargin Assets:QaMargin".
4. Control on the same build: an entry with four distinct accounts reads "Equity:Opening-Balances → Assets:Bank:Checking **+2**", so the "+N" convention is to count every account the row does not name.

Repeated from a fresh load.

## Cause

`dashboard/src/features/journal/components/journal-money-flow.ts:8-21` (`moneyFlow`, from `91daf9b46`) collects the distinct accounts of the negative postings (`from`) and of the positive ones (`to`) separately, returns `from[0]` and `to[0]`, and sets `more` to the number of distinct accounts touched minus 2. It assumes `from[0]` and `to[0]` differ. Here `from` is `[Assets:QaMargin, Equity:Opening]` and `to` is `[Assets:QaMargin]`: the pair is one account shown twice, and `more` is 2 − 2 = 0, so Equity:Opening is neither named nor counted. `shortAccountPair` (`:29-40`) leaves an identical pair as it is. Both layouts render the result: `journal-lines.tsx:94` (shown at `:300-313`, `:354`, `:406`, `:427`) and `journal-days.tsx:307` (`:452`, `:564`). With the commission buy above, the same lines name `Assets:Broker → Assets:Broker` and hide Expenses:Commissions.

## Fix

Owned by `dashboard`. In `moneyFlow`, name two different accounts whenever the entry touches two or more: keep the first account taken from, and pick as `to` the first account added to that is not it (if every account added to is that same account, keep `to` and pick the next account taken from instead). Compute `more` as the accounts touched minus the accounts actually named. An entry that touches a single account (an exchange inside one account) names it once and shows no arrow. The margin buy then reads "Equity:Opening → Assets:QaMargin", and the commission buy "Assets:Broker → Expenses:Commissions", never one account on both ends with no "+N".

## Definition of done

- The margin buy row names Equity:Opening or shows "+1"; the commission buy names Expenses:Commissions or shows "+1"; no row shows the same account on both sides of the arrow while another touched account goes unnamed and uncounted.
- Rows whose first from- and to-accounts already differ (the four-account opening entry, "+2") are unchanged.
- A unit test of `moneyFlow` covers the margin buy, the commission buy, a single-account exchange and the existing distinct-account cases, and fails on the current code. Dashboard gates (`yarn format:check`, `yarn lint`, `yarn test`, `yarn build`) pass.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` (open, `done/`, `blocked/`) for `moneyFlow`, `journal-money-flow`, `shortAccountPair`, money flow, "+N", and same account on both sides. w5/1179 is about leaf names that collide between two different accounts ("Checking · Checking"), not one account taken for both ends. w3/done/218 is truncation at large text sizes. Not deployment lag: `journal-money-flow.ts` is unchanged since `91daf9b46`, which production includes.

When the Journal is filtered to one account (`?account=…`) or the row is on that account's page (the register, since `ebbf43fed`), the amount is that account's change, but the "from → to" still names the entry's first account taken from and first account added to. When the filtered account is neither, it is hidden inside "+N", and the row reads as if the money went somewhere else. A payroll that also funds a retirement account is the everyday case.

**Cause:** `moneyFlow(transaction)` (`dashboard/src/features/journal/components/journal-money-flow.ts:8-21`) takes no account. Both callers pass only the transaction (`journal-lines.tsx:94`, `journal-days.tsx:307`), even though the row already has the account it reports in `money.accountFilter` (`hooks/use-journal-money.ts`), which `summarizeTransaction` uses for the amount (`journal-lines.tsx:85`).

**Fix (added to the one above):** pass the row's account (the filter or the register account) into `moneyFlow`. When it is set and the entry touches accounts outside it, name it on the side of its change. If the change is money in, `to` is the first posting account at or under it, and `from` is the first account outside it that the entry takes from. If the change is money out, `from` is that account, and `to` is the first account outside it that the entry adds to. "+N" counts every other touched account. An entry that moves only inside the filtered account's subtree (Vanguard:Cash → Vanguard:RGAGX on the Vanguard register) keeps today's pair. Without a filter, rows are unchanged apart from the fix above.
