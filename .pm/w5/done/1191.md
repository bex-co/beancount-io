# Journal rows show a profitable sale as its gain in money-in green ("+286.00 USD" for a 3,670.00 USD sale) and a loss sale as an unexplained 1,049.95 USD

**Severity:** minor. **Owner:** `dashboard`. **Estimate:** 30–45 minutes including tests. **A2:** a reader scanning the Journal for a sale sees a figure that is neither the proceeds nor the result. The label alone does not say that `+286.00` is the gain, and the loss sale's `1,049.95` appears nowhere in the entry. **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` (`dashboard-qa` routine) on 2026-11-20 against `https://beancount.io`, production entry chunk `main-CcF8TRB_.js`, isolated headless Chromium at 1440×1000. Public ledgers, read only, reproduced from fresh loads.

## Repro

`/ledger/open_ledger/stock-example/journal?filter=Sell`, no account filter, compact layout:

| Row | Postings (from `getLedgerJournal`) | Amount shown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-02-20 Sell 60 ACME — long-term gain | Cash 5,778.00 · Commissions 4.95 · Cash −4.95 · CapitalGains −1,686.00 | **+1,686.00 USD** (green) |
| 2026-04-17 Sell 40 ACME — short-term gain | Cash 3,670.00 · Commissions 4.95 · Cash −4.95 · CapitalGains −286.00 | **+286.00 USD** (green) |
| 2026-05-12 Sell 100 NWRB — long-term loss | Cash 910.00 · Commissions 4.95 · Cash −4.95 · CapitalLoss 135.00 | **1,049.95 USD** (no sign) |

`/ledger/open_ledger/example/journal?filter="Sell shares"` shows the same split: `Sell shares of GLD` (gain 642.80) reads `+642.80 USD`, and `Sell shares of VEA` (loss 11.48) reads `594.36 USD`, the sum of the positive USD postings.

Control: filtered to the cash account (`?account=Assets:US:ETrade:Cash`), the same VEA sale reads `+573.93 USD`, the account's exact change.

## Cause

`dashboard/src/features/journal/components/journal-days.tsx:170-181` (`TransactionAmount`, which both layouts use). When `summarizeTransaction` returns no amount, it substitutes `incomeAmounts` (`:116`), which gives the income postings signed as money in, and otherwise `grossAmounts` (`:85`), which gives the larger side of the operating currency. `summarizeTransaction` (`common/lib/beancount/transaction-summary.ts:157-161`) leaves a sale without an amount on purpose: "a sale carries a gain and its commission … Say there are several postings and let the entry itself explain them". w4/done/196 accepted exactly that for loss sales, as "do not … replace one unexplained subtotal with another", and w4/done/074 warned against simply giving income precedence. The payroll-as-earnings rule from `91daf9b46` / `ebbf43fed` catches sales too: a gain sale gets its gain as money in, and a loss sale gets a gross figure with no meaning of its own.

## Fix

Owned by `dashboard`. No API change.

- Without an account filter, a transaction that reduces a position held at cost (a posting with negative units and a `cost`) gets the "Multiple postings" fallback (`journal.multiplePostings`) whether it made a gain or a loss. It is never given `incomeAmounts` or `grossAmounts`.
- Keep the payroll-as-earnings rule for entries with no costed reduction, the account-filtered change, and the purchase and transfer rows as they are.
- Tests in `journal-amount-direction.test.tsx`: a gain sale and a loss sale (with commission and gain/loss postings) both render "Multiple postings", and a payroll still renders its earnings. They fail on the current code.

## Blast radius

Production today: the Journal, both layouts. At `main` (`ebbf43fed`, not yet deployed), the Overview's Recent activity renders through the same `JournalTable` and `TransactionAmount`. After the next deploy, the Overview will show these sale figures too, where it shows "Multiple postings" today (w4/196). The account page shows the account's own change and is unaffected.
