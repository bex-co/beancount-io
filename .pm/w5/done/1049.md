`--to-date 9999-12-31` to `report cash-flow` empties closing cash and
makes opening negative. A finite February window has opening1000 USD,
change250 and closing1250. With that end bound, change stays250 but

**Resolved 2026-10-27** — a `--to-date` of the calendar's last day is treated as no extra limit (`_cash_flow` in `cli/src/bea_engine/report.py`): the period's own finite end stays the cutoff, and a missing cutoff now means "no end" rather than "skip the closing balances". `--to-date 9999-12-31` therefore returns the same opening, movement and closing cash as the adjacent and unbounded controls, in JSON and human output, with per-account balances populated. The same maximum-date rule also fixes an OverflowError in the bounded-window helper added for [1036](./done/1036.md), which crashed `capital-gains` and `cash-flow` on that date. ADR024 is updated. Tests: max-date cases in `cli/tests/test_report_cash_flow_boundaries.py` (February and whole history against no bound, `9999-12-30` and BQL; an undated ledger) and a `maximum-to` case in `cli/tests/test_report_bounds_heading.py` for both reports; they fail with the fix reverted. `make check-all` passed on a combined tree of this and the other fixes queued with it (its `test_release` clean-checkout test timed out under concurrent load and passed alone).
