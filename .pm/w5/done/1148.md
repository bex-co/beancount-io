# Overview's stepped month is lost on Back and reload: returning from a drill-down shows a different month

**Severity:** minor (navigation loses context). **Owner:** dashboard. **Estimate:** 30–45 minutes. **A2:** a reader who steps the Overview to May, opens the Income Statement from Money movement, and presses Back should land on May again, not on the default month. **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` on 2026-11-16 against `https://beancount.io`, using headless Chromium at 1440×1000 in English/light. The account was the QA owner, on its designated disposable synthetic ledger. Local HEAD `c040852e8`; production serves the redesign from `aabf2eced`. Read-only.

## Repro

1. Open the Overview with no filters. It opens on **JULY 2026** (the QA ledger's last entry is Aug 28, 2026).
2. Press **Previous** twice: the header reads **MAY 2026**, and Financial position and Money movement show May. The URL does not change.
3. Follow any drill-down (Money movement **View all** → `/income-statement?time=2026-05`, or the Financial position footer link), then press the browser's Back button.

Expected: the Overview shows May 2026 again. Actual: it shows JULY 2026. A reload in step 2, or sharing the URL, also shows July. Reproduced twice from fresh loads.

## Cause

`dashboard/src/features/reports/overview/hooks/use-overview-month.ts:65` keeps the chosen month in `useState` (`requestedDate`). Only the dashboard's global Time filter lives in the URL. Remounting the route resets the state to `openingMonth(...)`.

## Fix

Keep the stepped month in the URL as its own search parameter (for example `month=2026-05`), written with `replace` so stepping does not flood history, and read it back in `useOverviewMonth`, falling back to the current opening rule when it is absent or not among the ledger's months. Do not reuse `time`: stepping must not narrow the Time filter that the rest of the app follows. The route loader's opening-month balance read (see the redesign commit) should honor the parameter so a server render matches.

## Definition of done

- In the repro, Back from the Income Statement shows MAY 2026 with May figures; a reload of `/ledger/<owner>/<ledger>?month=2026-05` opens on May.
- An unknown or out-of-range `month` falls back to the default month without an error.
- Stepping months does not add one history entry per press.
- A test of `useOverviewMonth` (or the Overview route) covers reading the month from the URL and the fallback. It fails on current `main`.
