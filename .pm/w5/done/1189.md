# Upload Receipt: the Payment Account list never offers a credit card, and on a ledger with renamed roots both account lists are empty

**Severity:** minor. A receipt paid by card cannot be booked from the list: the user has to type `Liabilities:CreditCard:Visa` in full and confirm it as a custom value, and if they start typing they are told "No accounts found". When the receipt reader itself suggests the card, the field shows it while the open list says the account does not exist. On a ledger with `option "name_expenses"` or `option "name_assets"`, neither Expense Account nor Payment Account lists anything. **Owner:** `dashboard`. **Estimate:** 30–45 minutes. **A2:** a newcomer's first receipt is often a card purchase. **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` on 2026-11-18 against `https://beancount.io` (entry chunk `main-LGv2TZLj.js`), headless Chromium, isolated profile, signed in as the QA owner on the designated synthetic ledger. Local HEAD `9fba47fd9`. The upload URL, the upload PUT and `ParseReceipt` were answered by the test harness before they left the browser, so no file was stored, no AI call was made and no quota was spent. Every mutation was aborted. Nothing was written.

## Repro

The ledger has `Assets:Bank:Checking`, `Assets:Bank:Savings`, `Assets:Cash` and `Liabilities:CreditCard:Visa` open. `getLedgerAccounts(status: "open")` returns all four.

1. Open `/ledger/<owner>/<ledger>/receipt` at 1440×1000 and choose a small PNG. The parsed receipt used here: 2026-11-01, payee `qa-20261118 Cafe`, 4.50, `targetAccount: Expenses:Food:Coffee`, `sourceAccount: null`.
2. Open **Payment Account**. It lists `Assets:Bank:Checking`, `Assets:Bank:Savings` and `Assets:Cash`, with no Liabilities account.
3. Type `Liab`. The list says **No accounts found** and "Press Enter to use "Liab"". Typing the full `Liabilities:CreditCard:Visa` gives the same answer. Enter accepts it as a custom value, and the preview then reads `Liabilities:CreditCard:Visa  -4.50 USD`.
4. Reload and parse again with `sourceAccount: "Liabilities:CreditCard:Visa"`, which the reader's prompt allows ("the payment method (an Assets or Liabilities account)"). The field is prefilled with the card, but opening it shows **No accounts found** under it, as if that open account were unknown.
5. Renamed roots: answering `GetLedgerAccounts` in the browser with `Activos:Banco`, `Pasivos:Visa`, `Gastos:Comida`, `Ingresos:Sueldo` and `Capital:Apertura` (a local model of a ledger with `name_assets`, `name_liabilities` and `name_expenses` set) leaves **Expense Account** and **Payment Account** with nothing to pick. **Document Account**, which has no filter, lists all five.

Control: switching ledgers from the review re-mounts the page on the other ledger with an empty upload. No state leaks across ledgers.

## Expected / Actual

Expected: Payment Account lists every open account under the ledger's assets and liabilities roots, under whatever names the ledger gives them. Expense Account lists every open account under the ledger's expenses root. A prefilled suggestion that is one of those accounts appears as a matching option. Actual: both filters are fixed English prefixes, and Payment Account leaves out liabilities entirely.

## Root cause

`dashboard/src/features/receipt/components/receipt-review-form.tsx:288` passes `filterPrefix="Expenses:"` and `:310` passes `filterPrefix="Assets:"` to `AccountCombobox`. `dashboard/src/common/components/ledger-comboboxes/account-combobox.tsx:58–59` keeps only `account.startsWith(filterPrefix)`. Both have been there since the dashboard was imported (`af5339de0`). The receipt reader's own recommendation contract, `backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/features/llm/utils/recommend-accounts/prompts.ts:12`, expects the payment method to be "an Assets or Liabilities account". The preview's placeholders at `receipt-review-form.tsx:146–147` (`Expenses:Account`, `Assets:Account`) are also English. The rest of the dashboard already reads the configured roots: `useLedger().ledgerData.options.name*` in `add-budget-dialog.tsx:114` and `ledgerAccountRoots` in `features/reports/cash-flow/lib/account-roots.ts`.

## Fix

- Let `AccountCombobox` take a set of roots in place of one string prefix, and match whole root components: an account is in the root, or begins with the root followed by `:`. `IncomeTax` is not under `Income`, the same rule as `budget-utils.ts:74`.
- Receipt review: Payment Account gets the ledger's assets and liabilities roots, and Expense Account gets its expenses root. Both come from `ledgerData.options.nameAssets`, `nameLiabilities` and `nameExpenses`, falling back to the English names.
- Use the same root names for the preview placeholders.
- Keep `allowCustom`, the Document Account auto-sync from Expense Account, the exact-decimal amount (w4/982), the escaped preview (w4/981) and the pending lock (w4/m48).
- Tests: a receipt-review test where a `Liabilities:` account and a renamed-root ledger's accounts appear in the right lists, a `Liabilities:` suggestion matches its option, and `IncomeTax`-style siblings stay out.

## Blast radius

`filterPrefix` has no other caller (`grep -rn filterPrefix dashboard/src`). The New Entry, Open Account, Budget, Smart Import and Bank Sync pickers do not filter by root. Mobile's receipt flow uses the multi-posting form, which has no root filter. The REST, GraphQL and MCP `insertReceiptTransaction` surfaces take any account, so this is a dashboard-only client fix with no API change.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` (open, `done/` and `blocked/`) for `Payment Account`, `paymentAccount`, `filterPrefix`, and receipt together with `Liabilities`, `credit card`, `renamed root` and `name_expenses`. Related but different: w4/981 (preview escaping) and w4/982 (amount precision), both done; w21/206 and w21/871 (mobile receipt refund sign and document attachment), both blocked; w4/1020 and w4/1022 (renamed roots in Overview and Cash Flow, done, which added the shared root helper). `git log -S 'filterPrefix="Assets:"'` finds only the import commit. Not deployment lag: current `main` has the same lines.
