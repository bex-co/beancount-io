# The sidebar's Collapse/Expand control drops keyboard focus to the page body and no longer says whether the sidebar is open (regression of w4/125)

**Severity:** minor (keyboard and screen-reader access). **Owner:** dashboard. **Estimate:** 30–45 minutes. **A2:** from 768px up, the sidebar's own **Collapse sidebar** / **Expand sidebar** button is now the only on-screen way to show or hide the navigation. A keyboard user who presses it loses their place on the page, and a screen reader is no longer told whether the sidebar is open. **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` on 2026-11-20 against `https://beancount.io`, production entry chunk `main-CIyNN76T.js` (built from `main` at or after `ebbf43fed`). Headless Chromium at 1440×1000, 1024×768 and 390×844, English/light, signed in as the QA owner on its disposable synthetic ledger. Read-only. Local HEAD `6bec0b1ee`.

## Repro

1. **Desktop (1440×1000).** Open `/ledger/<owner>/<ledger>/accounts`. Tab to **Collapse sidebar** at the bottom of the sidebar and press Enter. The sidebar collapses to the 48px rail, and `document.activeElement` is `<body>`. Pressing Enter again does nothing: no control has focus.
2. **Tablet (1024×768).** Same page, which opens with the 48px rail. Focus **Expand sidebar** and press Enter. The sidebar opens over the page (256px), and focus is again `<body>`. The next Tab lands on **Collapse sidebar**, and the Tab after that leaves the sidebar for the header's **Help and support**, so the sidebar's links come before the reader's position and are skipped.
3. **Ctrl+B.** At 1440px, focus the sidebar's **Journal** link and press Ctrl+B. The sidebar collapses and focus falls to `<body>`.
4. In both states the button is `button "Collapse sidebar"` / `button "Expand sidebar"`, with no `aria-expanded` and no `aria-controls`.

Control: at 390×844 the header's **Toggle Sidebar** opens the drawer with focus on the ledger switcher, and Escape returns focus to **Toggle Sidebar** (w3/161 still works there). Each case was repeated from a fresh load.

## Cause

- `97c05f2bb` (three-tier sidebar) changed `dashboard/src/common/components/ledger-layout/layout-header.tsx` from `showTrigger = isMobile ? !openMobile : true` to `isMobile && !openMobile`. The header's `SidebarTrigger` (`dashboard/src/common/components/ui/sidebar.tsx:460`, which carries `aria-expanded` since w4/125 at `:476`) now renders on phones only.
- Its replacement from 768px up is `SidebarCollapseToggle` in `dashboard/src/common/components/ledger-layout/ledger-sidebar.tsx:101-121` (rendered at `:534` when `!isMobile`). It is a `SidebarMenuButton` with `tooltip={label}` and `onClick={toggleSidebar}`, and it has no `aria-expanded`.
- `SidebarMenuButton` (`sidebar.tsx:849-863`) returns the bare `<button>` unless the sidebar is collapsed, and wraps it in `<Tooltip><TooltipTrigger asChild>` when it is. Toggling the sidebar therefore changes the element type at that position. React unmounts the focused `<button>` and mounts a new one, and the browser moves focus to `<body>`. The same remount hits any focused sidebar item when Ctrl+B toggles the sidebar. That branch dates from w3/480 (`49ff753d6`), which kept hidden tooltip content from swallowing Escape in the mobile sheet. It only became a keyboard trap once the toggle itself moved into the sidebar.

## Fix

Owned by `dashboard`.

- Keep the toggle's DOM node across the state change. For example, keep the tooltip wrapper mounted in both states with its content suppressed (controlled `open={false}`) while expanded or on mobile, so w3/480's Escape behaviour stays intact. Or render `SidebarCollapseToggle` without the conditional wrapper. Apply the same stable-tree rule to every `SidebarMenuButton`, so Ctrl+B keeps focus on the item that had it.
- Give the toggle `aria-expanded`: `state === "expanded"` on desktop, and the overlay's open state on the tablet rail. This matches what w4/125 required of the toggle.
- On the tablet rail, Expand keeps focus on the toggle, now **Collapse sidebar**, or moves it to the first sidebar link. Escape, an outside click and Collapse close the overlay and put focus back on **Expand sidebar**.

## Definition of done

- At 1440px, Enter on **Collapse sidebar** leaves focus on the same button, now **Expand sidebar** with `aria-expanded="false"`, and a second Enter expands it again with `aria-expanded="true"`.
- At 1024px, Enter on **Expand sidebar** opens the overlay with focus inside it (on the toggle or the first link), Tab reaches the sidebar's links, and Escape closes it with focus back on **Expand sidebar**.
- At 1440px, Ctrl+B with focus on a sidebar link keeps focus on that link.
- At 390px, the drawer still opens and closes as w3/161 and w3/480 require.
- A rendered test of the real `SidebarProvider` with `LedgerSidebar` asserts focus retention and `aria-expanded` through a collapse and expand. It fails on current `main`. Dashboard gates pass.

## Blast radius

Every ledger page renders `LedgerSidebar`. The toggle shows at 768px and up, which covers both tiers that `97c05f2bb` introduced. Ctrl+B affects every focused `SidebarMenuButton` that has a `tooltip`: all nav items, group triggers, Ask Beancount.io and Ledger Settings. The `/ledger` dashboard sidebar (`dashboard-sidebar.tsx`) was not checked.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**`, including `done/` and `blocked/`, for collapse/expand sidebar, Toggle Sidebar, `SidebarMenuButton`, Ctrl+B and sidebar focus. w4/done/125 added `aria-expanded` to `SidebarTrigger`, the control that then toggled the sidebar at every width. This note is that item's regression. w3/done/161 covers the mobile drawer's focus return, which still works. w3/done/480 covers Escape in the mobile sheet and introduced the conditional tooltip wrapper. w4/done/1012 and w4/done/097 cover rail flyouts. No open item covers the new toggle. Not deployment lag: production carries every dashboard commit on `main`.

## Unverified

Screen-reader speech, Firefox and Safari, a dragged (non-default) sidebar width, and the `/ledger` dashboard's own sidebar.

---

**Resolved 2026-11-23** — The toggle keeps its DOM node, reports the rail or overlay state through `aria-expanded`, and references the Ledger navigation with `aria-controls`. The shared tooltips and grouped navigation keep their controls mounted during collapse and expansion. The tablet overlay opens at the Overview link and restores focus to Expand after Escape, an outside click, or Collapse. Five rendered regressions using the real provider and LedgerSidebar failed before the fix. Chromium verified 1440px desktop focus retention, 1024px tablet dismissal, and the 390px mobile drawer's switcher and Escape focus behavior. All 8,652 dashboard tests, format, lint, and build passed. Shipped in `df1095031` (`dashboard`).
