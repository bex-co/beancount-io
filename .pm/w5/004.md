# Evaluate opening /ledger-gallery to signed-out visitors

`/ledger-gallery` is `requireAuth`-gated yet carries `seo.ledgerGallery.*` meta that crawlers can never reach behind the login redirect. Public ledgers and `/ledger/$username` profile pages are already signed-out-visible, so precedent exists. Investigation + written recommendation only (privacy review of what the gallery exposes); if the answer is "open it", that becomes its own milestone.
