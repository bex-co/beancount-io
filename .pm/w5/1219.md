# Holdings and commit pages build their counts by hand, so Russian reads "115 Строки" and "31 файлов", Persian "۱۱۵ ردیف‌ها" (gap in w5/1137)

**Severity:** minor (localization/grammar). **Owner:** `dashboard`. **Estimate:** 30–45 minutes including tests. **A2:** a newcomer reading the dashboard in their own language sees ungrammatical counts on two pages that otherwise speak it correctly. `dashboard/AGENTS.md` requires "Counts are plural messages, not `Label: {count}` workarounds". **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` (`dashboard-qa` routine) on 2026-11-26 against `https://beancount.io`, production entry chunk `main-BBmi61Hs.js`. QA-owned headless Chromium 158, isolated contexts, 1440×1000, language set through the app's preference and again through `?lang=`. Reproduced signed out on public ledgers. Read-only: no ledger writes. Local HEAD `ebed3ee95`.

## Repro

Public ledgers, no sign-in needed. Pick the language with `?lang=ru` (or `uk`, `sk`, `fa`).

1. Open `/ledger/open_ledger/example/holdings?lang=ru` and select each of the four tabs. The count badge beside "Results" reads:

   | Language | Holdings (115) | By account (11) | By currency (8) | By cost currency (2) | Correct |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | Russian | 115 Строки | 11 Строки | 8 Строки | 2 Строки | 115 строк, 11 строк, 8 строк, 2 строки |
   | Ukrainian | 115 Рядки | 11 Рядки | 8 Рядки | 2 Рядки | 115 рядків, 11 рядків, 8 рядків, 2 рядки |
   | Slovak | 115 Riadky | 11 Riadky | 8 Riadky | 2 Riadky | 115 riadkov, 11 riadkov, 8 riadkov, 2 riadky |
   | Persian | ۱۱۵ ردیف‌ها | ۱۱ ردیف‌ها | ۸ ردیف‌ها | ۲ ردیف‌ها | ۱۱۵ ردیف (a counted noun stays singular) |

   Every language also capitalizes the noun mid-phrase ("115 Rows", "2 Lignes", "2 Filas"), because the plural word is the shared column-header string `common.rows`.

2. Open `/ledger/open_ledger/crypto-example/commit/37814c4?lang=ru` (a commit touching 31 files). The metadata line reads `31 файлов` in Russian (correct: `31 файл`), `31 файлів` in Ukrainian (`31 файл`), `31 súbory` in Slovak (`31 súborov`), and `31 فایل‌ها` in Persian, with a Latin `31` beside the Persian `+۶٬۰۶۸ -۸۰۶` on the same line (correct: `۳۱ فایل`). English and French read correctly for 31.

## Cause

- `dashboard/src/features/ledger-data/holdings/holdings-table.tsx:221-222` writes `{formatCount(rows.length)} {rows.length === 1 ? t("page.holdings.row") : t("common.rows")}`: an English one/other choice between two unrelated messages.
- `dashboard/src/features/git/commits/components/commit-metadata.tsx:121` writes `{fileCount} {fileCount === 1 ? t("commits.file") : t("commits.files")}`, with the same choice and the raw number, so Persian gets Latin digits (missed by w2/125's digit sweep).
- w5/1137 converted every `t(key, { count })` message that was not a plural family and added a guard in `src/i18n/__tests__/plural.test.ts` for English messages holding a `{count}` phrase. These two sites interpolate no `{count}` at all, so neither the conversion nor the guard could see them. A source search for `{" "}` after a count and for `=== 1 ? t(` finds only these two.

## Fix

- Replace both with plural messages called as `t(key, { count })`: for example `page.holdings.rowCount` ("{count} rows") and `commits.fileCount` ("{count} files"), with one form per category each language uses (`yarn i18n:prepare`), real translations in all 15 catalogs, and the base keys in `src/i18n/plural-keys.ts`. Format the count in the language's digits as the rest of the page does (`useFormatCount`).
- Remove `page.holdings.row`, `commits.file` and `commits.files` once unused (and their entry in `src/test/locale-glossary.test.ts`). `common.rows` stays: it is also a column header.
- Tests: the Holdings badge and the commit metadata render Russian `2 строки`/`115 строк` and `31 файл`, and Persian `۳۱ فایل`; English keeps `1 row`/`2 rows` and `1 file`/`31 files`. Consider extending the w5/1137 guard to fail on a source line that picks between two `t(...)` calls on `=== 1`. The Russian assertions fail on the current code.

## Definition of done

- On production, the four Holdings tabs of `open_ledger/example` read `115 строк`, `11 строк`, `8 строк`, `2 строки` in Russian and `۱۱۵ ردیف` … in Persian, and the crypto-example commit `37814c4` reads `31 файл` in Russian and `۳۱ فایل` in Persian. English is unchanged apart from lower-case `rows`. Dashboard gates pass.

## Blast radius

Two call sites, found by searching `features/` and `common/` for a count followed by a separately translated noun. The BQL page's `Rows: {count}` and the `+{count}`/`-{count}` line counts are labels that w5/1137 deliberately left as they are.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**`, including `done/` and `blocked/`, for `plural`, `commits.file`, `page.holdings.row`, `common.rows`, `файлов`, `Строки` and Holdings/commit counts. w4/m28 introduced plural messages, w5/1137 converted six remaining `{count}` messages, w4/1071 CSV preview counts, and w2/125 Persian digits in counts (it listed `115 ردیف‌ها` for its digits only). None covers these two concatenations, and `main` still has both, so this is not deployment lag.

**Unverified:** Bulgarian, Catalan, Dutch, Portuguese and Spanish wording beyond capitalization (their two-form plurals read correctly for these counts); the 390px layout.
