# The filter field suggests only for its first term: with `#trip-boston-2017` applied, typing ` #trip` or ` Star` offers nothing, though Fava completes the term at the caret

**Severity:** minor. The filter language composes (`#trip-boston-2017 payee:"Starbucks"` reads "Showing 1–4 of 4", the two terms ANDed), but the field's suggestions only ever match and replace the whole field. Once one term is applied, the field offers nothing for a second one. A reader who wants a tag or an escaped payee as the second term has to know its exact spelling and Fava's quoting, or delete the first term to get suggestions, and then picking one replaces the term they meant to keep. Fava's filter field completes the word at the caret for exactly this reason. **Owner:** `dashboard` (`common/components/ui/combobox.tsx`, `common/components/ledger-search-controls`). **Estimate:** 45 minutes including tests. **A2:** a newcomer who narrows the Journal with one suggestion cannot narrow it a second time the same way.

**Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` (`dashboard-qa` routine) on 2026-11-27 against `https://beancount.io`, entry chunk `main-BBmi61Hs.js`, isolated headless Chromium 158 at 1440×1000 and 390×844, English, browser zone America/Mexico_City. Read-only, as the QA owner and on the public `open_ledger/example` ledger. Local HEAD `20e7c819b`. No dashboard commit has ever completed a filter term by word (`git log -S value_extractor -- dashboard` finds only the initial import), so this is not deployment lag.

## Repro

1. Open `/ledger/open_ledger/example/journal`. Focus **Filter by tag, payee, …** and type `boston`. The list offers `#trip-boston-2017`; pick it. The URL reads `?filter=%23trip-boston-2017` and the Journal "Showing 1–19 of 19".
2. Focus the field again, press End and type ` Star`. The list is empty (no `option`), though `payee:"Starbucks"` and `payee:"Star of Siam"` are suggested for `Star` in an empty field.
3. Same with ` #trip` (no tag suggestions) and ` Giacomo` (no payee suggestion).
4. The only way to get the payee suggestion is to delete `#trip-boston-2017`. Picking `payee:"Starbucks"` then applies `?filter=payee:"Starbucks"` ("Showing 1–10 of 10") instead of `#trip-boston-2017 payee:"Starbucks"` ("Showing 1–4 of 4", which the field accepts when typed in full).
5. At 390×844 the Filters sheet's field behaves the same way.

Repeated from a fresh load.

## Cause

- `dashboard/src/common/components/ui/combobox.tsx:108-118` filters the options against the whole `inputValue` (`option.value/label/searchText.includes(search)`), and `handleSelect` (`:120-129`) sets the whole input to the picked value and sends it.
- `dashboard/src/common/components/ledger-search-controls/index.tsx:192-201` builds `fql_filter_suggestions` the way Fava does, and `:254-264` passes them to that combobox with no way to complete a single term.
- Fava's `frontend/src/sidebar/FilterForm.svelte` passes the same suggestions to its filter field with a `value_extractor` (match the suggestions against `/\S*$/` of the text before the caret) and a `value_selector` (replace only that word, keep the text before and after it). Its Time and Account fields complete the whole field, as the dashboard's do.

## Fix

Owned by `dashboard`. Give `Combobox` (through `SearchControlCombobox`) an optional term mode, used only by the filter field: match the options against the word that ends at the caret (`/\S*$/` of the text before `selectionStart`), and on selection replace only that word, keeping the text before and after it and leaving the caret after the inserted value. Enter on a highlighted option and a click behave the same. An empty word (caret after a space) lists every suggestion. The Time and Account fields, and every other Combobox, keep whole-field matching. Blur and Enter without a highlighted option still apply the field's whole text.
