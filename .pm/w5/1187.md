# Query History: at 640px and wider, "Download CSV" and Delete cover the end of the query's first line ("2016 ORDER BY" under "CSV herunterladen") (regression of w4/119)

**Severity:** minor. On any desktop or tablet width, a history card's first line runs under its own action buttons. The hidden part is about 69px of SQL in English and 96px in German, often the clause that tells two similar queries apart (`ORDER BY`, `DESC`, the end of an account name). **Owner:** `dashboard`. **Estimate:** 20–30 minutes including a real-layout check. **A2:** a newcomer comparing queries in their history cannot read what each one asked. **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` on 2026-11-18 against `https://beancount.io` (entry chunk `main-LGv2TZLj.js`), headless Chromium, isolated profile. Reproduced signed in and from a fresh signed-out context on the public ledger. Local HEAD `b6efcfbdf`. Read-only: no ledger writes.

## Repro

1. Open `/ledger/open_ledger/example/query` at 1280×900, signed out, English.
2. Run `SELECT date, payee, narration, position WHERE account ~ 'Expenses:Food' AND year = 2016 ORDER BY date DESC LIMIT 5`.
3. In the Query History card, the characters `ORDER BY ` on the first line sit under "Download CSV". A per-character `Range` rectangle check finds them inside the action group's box.
4. Set the language to German and run the query again. `16 ORDER BY ` is now hidden under "CSV herunterladen".

Measured on the same card, signed in, with `SELECT date, balance WHERE account = 'Assets:US:BofA:Checking' AND year = 2016 AND month <= 2 ORDER BY date DESC`:

| Viewport | Summary `padding-right` | Action group width | Text hidden under it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1440 | 112px | 181px | ` DESC` |
| 1024 | 112px | 181px | `king' AND ` |
| 700 | 112px | 181px | `nth <= 2 ` |
| 390 | 112px | 76px (icons only) | none |
| 1280, German | 112px | 208px | `16 ORDER BY ` |
| 1280, Persian | 112px | 150px | `Y`, and the disclosure chevron: in RTL the chevron sits at the right edge, so it is drawn between Delete and Download |

## Expected / Actual

Expected: every character of the query is visible, wrapping beside the actions and never underneath them, at every width and in every locale. Actual: from 640px up, the action group is wider than the space the summary keeps free, so the end of the first line is drawn underneath it.

## Root cause

`dashboard/src/features/bql/components/query-result-card.tsx:356` gives the `<summary>` a fixed `pr-28` (112px). `:425` places the actions in an absolutely positioned `div.absolute right-3 top-2`. That group holds Download CSV (36px icon-only below `sm`, 141px in English and 168px in German from `sm` up, because `query-result-export.tsx:39` shows its label with `hidden sm:inline`) plus the 36px Delete button. 112px holds only the icon-only layout. `5901116ef` (w3/631) moved the actions out of `<summary>` so they would not nest inside it, and introduced this fixed padding. w4/119 (resolved 2026-09-19) had fixed actions clipped off phone-width cards with `min-w-0`, and its acceptance required the SQL to wrap "within the available width while both actions remain visible". The w3/631 layout keeps the actions visible but puts text under them.

## Fix

- Reserve exactly the space the actions take, in every locale and at every width. For example, lay the summary and the action group out side by side in one flex row on the card, so the action group's real width decides the gap, while the actions stay outside `<summary>` (w3/631's nested-interactive fix). Or float the actions so the SQL's first line wraps around them. A larger fixed padding would break again in the next longer locale.
- Keep w4/119's narrow-card guarantees: at 320 and 390px both actions stay inside the card, and the card does not scroll sideways on Tab.
- In RTL, keep the disclosure chevron clear of the actions.
- Keep the disclosure named by the query alone, the Delete button's accessible name, click propagation, and loading and error states.
- Add a real-browser geometry check (not JSDOM: `query-result-geometry.test.tsx` mocks the export button and loads no stylesheet). At 1440, 1024, 390 and 320px, in English, German and Persian, neither a query glyph's rectangle nor the chevron may intersect the action group.

## Blast radius

`QueryResultCard` is used only by the Query page (`features/bql/pages/index.tsx`), and every history card shares this header. Other locales with longer labels (fr `Télécharger CSV`, uk `Завантажити CSV`) hide more text. Short queries that end before the action area are not affected, which is why a quick look at `select * from balances` passes.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**`, including `done/` and `blocked/`, for query history header, summary overlap, `pr-28`, Download CSV overlap, and clipped actions. w4/done/119 is the same header on phones (the actions were pushed outside the card), fixed with `min-w-0`. w3/done/631 introduced the current absolute layout. No open item covers text under the actions. Not deployment lag: `main` still has `pr-28` and the absolute group.
