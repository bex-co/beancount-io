# Overview's Financial position rows open the account's all-time page, not the month the row's balance is for: "Liabilities:US:Chase:Slate −2,511.58 USD" (Aug 31, 2017) opens a register ending at −2,703.29 USD (gap in w5/1147)

**Severity:** minor (a drill-down that shows a different number from the one clicked). **Owner:** `dashboard` (`features/reports/overview`). **Estimate:** 15–25 minutes including tests. **A2:** a reader who opens an account from the Overview's balances wants to see how that balance came about. The card names one day, but the account page shows the account to today, or to the whole Time filter, so the balance the reader clicked appears nowhere on it. **Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` (`dashboard-qa` routine) on 2026-11-27 against `https://beancount.io`, production entry chunk `main-BBmi61Hs.js`. QA-owned headless Chromium 158 at 1440×1000, English, `en-US`. Reproduced signed out in a fresh context and signed in as the QA account on the public `open_ledger/example` ledger. Read-only. Local HEAD `8268c3271`.

## Repro

Public ledger, no sign-in needed.

1. Open `/ledger/open_ledger/example`. It opens on August 2017, and Financial position is dated **Aug 31, 2017**. The Liabilities row reads **Liabilities:US:Chase:Slate −2,511.58 USD**. Its `href` is `/ledger/open_ledger/example/account/Liabilities%3AUS%3AChase%3ASlate`, with no `time`.
2. Click the row. The account page shows the account to the ledger's end. Its newest row is 2017-09-08 with Balance **−2,703.29 USD**. `/account/Liabilities%3AUS%3AChase%3ASlate?time=2017-08` shows −2,511.58 USD, the figure clicked.
3. Open `/ledger/open_ledger/example?month=2016-08`. Financial position is dated Aug 31, 2016 and lists **Assets:US:BofA:Checking 3,014.75 USD**. The row links to `/account/Assets%3AUS%3ABofA%3AChecking?month=2016-08`. The account page ignores `month`, so it opens on 2017-09-07 with Balance 2,754.06 USD. `?time=2016-08` shows 3,014.75 USD.
4. Under a Time filter, open `/ledger/open_ledger/example?time=2016-Q3&month=2016-08`. The card answers for Aug 31, 2016, but its rows link to `?time=2016-Q3&month=2016-08`, which shows the account through Sep 30.

Controls on the same pages: the card's footer, **Open the balance sheet for this period**, links to `/balance-sheet?time=2017-08` (step 1) and `?time=2016-08` (steps 3–4), as w5/1147 made it. Money movement's account rows link with the month's `time` (`/account/Income%3AUS%3AHoogle%3ASalary?month=2016-08&time=2016-08`). Steps 1–3 were repeated from a fresh load, and step 1 signed out.

## Cause

`dashboard/src/features/reports/overview/components/account-balances-card.tsx:188-201`: each row is a `Link` to the account page with `search={(prev) => prev}`. That passes on the Overview's own URL search (`month`, and any Time filter), not the period the card answers for. The card already receives that period as `time` (`index.tsx:468`, `positionPeriod?.time ?? ledgerFilters.searchParams.time`), and its footer uses it (`account-balances-card.tsx:380-392`). Money movement sets `time` the same way (`components/money-movement-section.tsx:184-187`). The rows were buttons calling `navigateToAccount` before w5/1211 (`e4aea5864`), which made them links and kept their search as it was. w5/1147 (`4be105aa3`) fixed only the footer.

## Fix

Give each row the footer's search: `month` cleared, and `time` set to `toLedgerFilterSearchParam(time)` whenever the card has a `time` (`filtered` in the component). Keep `account` and `filter` as the footer does. Without a `time`, the row stays as it is today.

## Definition of done

- On `/ledger/open_ledger/example`, the Liabilities:US:Chase:Slate row links to `/account/Liabilities%3AUS%3AChase%3ASlate?time=2017-08`, and that page's latest balance reads −2,511.58 USD.
- On `?month=2016-08`, the Assets:US:BofA:Checking row links with `time=2016-08` and no `month`, and opens on 3,014.75 USD. On `?time=2016-Q3&month=2016-08` the row also carries `time=2016-08`.
- `__tests__/account-balances-card-period.test.tsx` gains row-link cases for a stepped month with no URL filter, for a Time filter, and for a card with no `time`. The first two fail on the current code. Dashboard gates pass.
