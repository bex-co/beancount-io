# Cash Flow's "Cash & cash equivalents in this report" leaves out every cash account that ends the period empty, though the statement counts it as cash, so "Show closed accounts" never appears (gap in w4/m2)

**Severity:** minor. The panel is the statement's trust surface: "Accounts treated as cash & equivalents when building this statement." An account that held cash during the period but is empty at its end is still counted as cash: its opening balance is in **Cash & Equivalents at Period Start**, and what it paid out is reported as activity. The panel and the **Net change in cash & equivalents** rows still leave it out. A reader who switched banks, or whose escrow account was refunded, cannot see where part of the opening cash came from. w4/m2/t004 promised "Panel lists exactly the accounts the CCE heuristic included" and "Closed zero-balance accounts are hidden by default and revealable". On production, a closed account never reaches the panel, so the **Show closed accounts** toggle is never offered. **Owner:** `dashboard` (`features/reports/cash-flow`). **Estimate:** 30–45 minutes including tests. **A2:** closing an old checking account is one of the first things a newcomer's ledger records, and the cash statement should still account for that money.

**Found by:** `/qa-find-bugs-dashboard` (`dashboard-qa` routine) on 2026-11-28 against `https://beancount.io`, entry chunk `main-BBmi61Hs.js`, isolated headless Chromium 158 at 1440×1000, English, browser zone America/Mexico_City. Signed in as the QA owner. The emptied-account case is read-only, on the public `open_ledger/real-estate-example`. The closed-account case used a fixture on the QA account's designated disposable ledger, which was restored byte-for-byte afterwards (blob and SHA-256 match, zero errors). Local HEAD `5251e89bc`. ## Cause

- `dashboard/src/features/reports/cash-flow/lib/model.ts:308-309` `collectCashAccounts` keeps a cash leaf only when `Object.keys(balance).length > 0`. For an emptied account, the closing hierarchy returns `balance: {}` and `balanceChildren: {}`. Both appear live in `getLedgerBalanceSheet.assetsHierarchyData` (`Assets:Bank:EscrowImpound bal={}` for 2025, `Assets:Bank:Qa-20261128-OldChecking bal={}` for 2026-11). So every emptied cash account is dropped, whether it is open or closed.
- The statement does not use that set to decide what is cash. Since w4/m56, `index.tsx:111` resolves membership from every account directive (`cashAccountMembership`), and activity is gated on it. `model.ts:473` derives opening cash as closing minus net change. The dropped account's opening balance and payments are therefore counted, but the account itself is never shown.
- The panel (`index.tsx:205` `joinCashAccountStatus(closingCashAccounts, …)`) and the Net change card (`cash-flow-content.tsx:194` `buildCashAccountForest(closingCashAccounts, …)`) both list only `closingCashAccounts`. `lib/cash-account-status.ts:37` `isClosedZeroBalance` and the panel's toggle (`cash-account-status-panel.tsx:50-63`) are written for closed zero-balance rows, but such a row never arrives. `__tests__/cash-account-status-panel.test.tsx:25` feeds `{ USD: "0.00" }`, a shape the API does not return for an emptied account.

## Fix

Owned by `dashboard`. Keep every cash-member leaf that the closing hierarchy carries, including those whose balance is empty, as a snapshot with a zero (empty) balance. Totals do not change. The statement, the panel and the Net change card then agree on one cash set:

- The panel lists an emptied open account with status Open and a zero balance. A closed, zero-balance account is hidden by default, **Show closed accounts** appears, and the toggle reveals it as Closed. `fava-option "show-closed-accounts"` sets the initial state, as it does now.
- The Net change card lists an emptied open account with a zero closing amount, and hides a closed zero-balance account unless the panel is showing closed accounts.
- `hasHeuristicCashAccounts` counts the same set, so the heuristic disclosure stays when the only heuristic cash account ended the period empty.

Do not change the opening, closing and net change figures, the activity rows, membership, the exports, or the reconciliation check.
