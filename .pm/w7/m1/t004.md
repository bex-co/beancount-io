## Objective

While a store subscription will renew, the Stripe verbs that create or change a subscription refuse with a typed `BILLED_BY_STORE` error: `createSubscriptionSession`, `upgradeSubscription`, `previewPlanChange`, `changePlan`, and `resumeSubscription` (D5). The Customer Portal opens with a configuration that offers payment methods, invoices, and cancellation only. An account billed by Stripe and a store at once is flagged as duplicate billing.

## Context

- Milestone: [w7/m1](./README.md). Design: [ADR 001](../../../docs/adrs/ADR001-mobile-billing.md). Package: `backend-cluster/backend-v2/` (see `backend-cluster/backend-v2/AGENTS.md`; follow its Required API parity workflow).
- The double-Checkout guard in `backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/features/stripe/service/subscription-service.ts` checks Stripe only; `plan-change-preview.ts` takes `fromTier` from Stripe only.
- `createCustomerPortalSession` in `backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/features/stripe/service/stripe-service.ts` passes the client's `portalConfigurationId`.
- The restricted portal configuration is created in Stripe by m2/t004; until it exists, use a test double.

## Steps

1. Add `BILLED_BY_STORE` to the domain errors.
2. Check for a renewing store row in each of the five verbs.
3. Choose the restricted portal configuration while a store renews.
4. Compute the duplicate-billing flag.
