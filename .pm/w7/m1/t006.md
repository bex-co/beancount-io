## Objective

`ResourceLimitReachedError` carries whether the limit is the requester's own plan or the ledger owner's, and the plan's tier, identically on GraphQL, REST, and MCP (D13). Its default message stops saying "Upgrade to Premium or a higher plan to continue".

## Context

- Milestone: [w7/m1](./README.md). Design: [ADR 001](../../../docs/adrs/ADR001-mobile-billing.md). Package: `backend-cluster/backend-v2/` (see `backend-cluster/backend-v2/AGENTS.md`; follow its Required API parity workflow).
- The entry limit is the owner's: `backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/features/ledger/operations/directive-limit-lookup.ts` resolves the ledger owner's tier. Ledger creation and AI allowances are the requester's own.
- `backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/shared/errors/domain-errors.ts` holds the error; the directive limit already passes its own message about deleting entries.

## Steps

1. Add the plan-owner and tier fields.
2. Set them at each refusal site.
3. Expose them through the GraphQL, REST, and MCP error mappings.
4. Regenerate OpenAPI.
