## Objective

Add the D4 tables `billing_identities`, `store_subscriptions`, and `store_billing_events`, with their models, constraints, and a migration, plus the rule that decides whether a row grants its tier.

## Context

- Milestone: [w7/m1](./README.md). Design: [ADR 001](../../../docs/adrs/ADR001-mobile-billing.md). Package: `backend-cluster/backend-v2/` (see `backend-cluster/backend-v2/AGENTS.md`; follow its Required API parity workflow).
- D4 lists every field: `store_subscriptions.user_id` is nullable for unclaimed purchases (D9); uniqueness is (`provider`, `environment`, `external_id`) and, for events, (`provider`, `environment`, `external_event_id`).
- Model pattern to follow: `backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/features/stripe/data/paid-customer-model/` (`schema.ts`, `postgres-impl.ts`, `types.ts`). Migrations live in `backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/drizzle/migrations/`.

## Steps

1. Add the three schemas and models.
2. Generate the migration with the package's Drizzle workflow.
3. Add the grant rule: `status` is `ACTIVE` or `GRACE`, `now < access_ends_at`, and `user_id` is set.

## Files

- `backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/features/store-billing/data/` (new models)
- `backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/drizzle/migrations/`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The migration applies to an empty database and to the current schema.
- [ ] Tests prove one store purchase cannot be stored for two accounts and one notification cannot be recorded twice.
- [ ] The grant rule is unit-tested for every normalized status, an expired row, and an unowned row.
