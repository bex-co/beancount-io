## Objective

Buy and change plans on iOS (D7, D6): fetch `storeBillingIdentity`, purchase with the `appAccountToken`, send the signed transaction to `confirmStorePurchase`, and finish the transaction only after the backend accepts it. Re-send unfinished transactions at launch. A purchase awaiting Ask to Buy shows as waiting and grants nothing.
