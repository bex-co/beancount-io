# `bea` prints a limit refusal without saying whose plan set it

`cli/src/cli/errors.py` keeps the server's sentence for `RESOURCE_LIMIT_REACHED`, and that sentence no longer says what to do (w7/m1/t006). REST now carries `error.metadata.limitOwner`, which the CLI ignores. A collaborator on another person's ledger cannot tell that only the owner can lift the limit.

Print a next step from `limitOwner`: `REQUESTER` points at plans, `LEDGER_OWNER` points at the ledger's owner, no owner adds nothing. Found by the w7/m1/t007 surface walk.
