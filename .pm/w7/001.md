# Ledger limit refusals still say "Upgrade to Premium" on limits no plan sets

`backend-cluster/ledger/src/shared/errors/domain-errors.ts:315` ends every `ResourceLimitReachedError` with "Upgrade to Premium or a higher plan to continue." Its callers are the snapshot-size, repository-size and file-map loader bounds, which no plan lifts. backend-v2 passes that sentence through, so a limit carrying no `limitOwner` still tells people to upgrade, and the dashboard importer's keyword match shows its upgrade panel for it.

Drop the sentence to match backend-v2's default (w7/m1/t006, ADR 001 D13). Found by the w7/m1/t007 surface walk.
