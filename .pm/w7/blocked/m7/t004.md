## Objective

Every beancount.io page the app opens carries the m6 app marker, and the sign-in session never sends `next=/pricing`. Audit every place the app opens a beancount.io URL, in an in-app browser or outside the app.

2. Use it at every site.
3. Check the sign-in URL.
