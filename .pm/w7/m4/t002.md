## Objective

Add the Pub/Sub push endpoint. It verifies the push's OIDC token (audience and service account), records the event idempotently, and re-reads the subscription; it never acts on the notification type alone. A voided purchase revokes the plan at its time (D11).

- Milestone: [w7/m4](./README.md). Design: [ADR 001](../../../docs/adrs/ADR001-mobile-billing.md). Package: `backend-cluster/backend-v2/` (see `backend-cluster/backend-v2/AGENTS.md`; follow its Required API parity workflow).

## Steps
