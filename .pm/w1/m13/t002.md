## Objective

Make the default conversion behave like Fava: positions with a quote on or before the valuation date are converted, positions without one stay in their own commodity, and the report says so in one line per commodity.

## Context

- `cli/src/cli/commands/report.py` computes `valuation` (around the `_missing_price_message` helper): it collects `(from, to, date)` triples and raises unless `--allow-errors`, then prints one line per triple. The stock `bean-example` ledger yields over 40 lines for `VACHR` and `IRAUSD`.
- The partial path already exists: `valuation: "partial"`, `missing_prices`, `missing_price_dates`, and `incomplete` totals rendered as `Unavailable`.
- Two cases differ for the reader: a commodity with no quote at any date (vacation hours, a 401k unit) and a commodity with a stale quote (EUR priced in August, not June).

## Steps

1. Under the lenient policy from t001, treat missing prices as partial by default: keep unconverted positions in their own commodity in trees, totals, and interval rows.
2. Summarize on stderr with one line per commodity: "VACHR has no USD price at any date; shown in units" or "EUR → USD has no price before 2026-08-31; earlier rows shown in EUR".
3. Under strict mode keep the exit-1 refusal, but use the same per-commodity summary in `details` and keep the dated triples in `error.result.missing_price_dates` for automation.
4. Keep `--conversion units`, `at_cost`, and `at_value` unchanged.

## Files

- `cli/src/cli/commands/report.py`
- `cli/src/fava/core/conversion.py` (read-only; reuse, do not fork)

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `bea report overview`, `balance-sheet`, and `income-statement` on a `bean-example` ledger exit 0 in a terminal and show `VACHR` positions in units.
- [ ] stderr carries at most one line per missing commodity.
- [ ] `bea --json report balance-sheet` on the same ledger exits 1 without `--allow-errors` and lists the dated triples in `error.result`.
- [ ] A ledger with a single later EUR quote reports earlier intervals in EUR and later intervals in USD.

## Out of scope

- Fetching prices from the network.
- Display precision (t003).
