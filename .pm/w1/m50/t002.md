## Objective

A row's preview diff stops copying and diffing the whole file: it is built from the lines around the row's edits, with the hunk header offset, and equals the diff the whole file gives.

## Context

- `cli/src/bea_engine/ledger/updating.py`: `_row_payload`, `_file_diff`; `cli/src/bea_engine/ledger/text.py`: `unified_file_diff`, `physical_lines`.
- The whole-file diff keeps a byte-order mark on line 1 and marks a last line without a terminator with `\ No newline at end of file`.

## Steps

1. Diff a window of the edited lines plus enough context on each side, then shift the hunk headers to the file's line numbers.
2. Cover the byte-order-mark, no-final-newline, first-line, last-line and removed-continuation-line cases.

## Files

- `cli/src/bea_engine/ledger/updating.py`, possibly `cli/src/bea_engine/ledger/text.py`
