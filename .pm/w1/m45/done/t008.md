## Objective

Pin the move's behavior and failure modes with tests that fail without it.

## Steps

1. Workflow: a move across a balance assertion, entries from two source files, an existing target, a new target with `includeFrom`, byte-identical entry text and order.
2. Refusals: unreachable target, unloaded `includeFrom`, stale `sha256sum`, same-file target, new errors without `allowInvalid`.
3. Surfaces: parity rows for every case above and `FORBIDDEN`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Each test fails when the behavior it covers is reverted, and the full backend-v2 suite passes.

## Outcome — 2026-11-13

**Workflow on the live engine (`backend-cluster/ledger/scripts/entry-move.ts`, run by `yarn verify:rustledger`).** It drives the real journal service and real parse over an in-memory repository that commits. The fixture has a `2026-09-01 balance Assets:Cash 77.25 USD` that depends on three August transactions: two in `main.bean` and one in an included `imports.bean`. Moving all three to a new `2026/08.bean` with `includeFrom: main.bean`:

- the preview reports no new error and names the three entries;
- the commit is exactly one, writes the three files, and returns its sha;
- the target holds the entries' text byte for byte, in ledger order (path, then line);
- `main.bean` includes the target once, and neither source file still holds a moved entry;
- the ledger's errors and the balances of every root are identical before and after;
- a stale checksum for one of two listed entries refuses the whole move.

Mutation check: with the include line dropped, the engine reports "Balance failed for Assets:Cash: expected 77.25 USD, got 100.00 USD", and the gate refuses the move.

**Refusals and workflow details (`move-source-slices.test.ts`, 12 tests, two of them added in t007):**
- an existing and a new target, and an `includeFrom` in a subfolder;
- the CRLF target and the include placement;
- an unreachable target and an unloaded `includeFrom`;
- a stale checksum, a same-file target, and a non-portable new name;
- new errors without `allowInvalid`.

**Surfaces (`move-entry-sources-parity.test.ts`, 30 tests).** Each row runs on REST, GraphQL and MCP. Added in this task:
- an unloaded `includeFrom` (`BAD_USER_INPUT`);
- a target an entry already lives in (`BAD_USER_INPUT`);
- an entry in a managed price include (`OPERATION_NOT_ALLOWED`);
- more than 1000 entries, refused before the ledger is called. This is `VALIDATION_FAILED` on REST, whose schema refuses it as the batch delete's cap does, and `BAD_USER_INPUT` elsewhere.
- the two spellings of a flag disagreeing (`BAD_USER_INPUT`, ledger never called).

These join t003's rows: commit, preview, breaking preview and refused commit, unloaded target, stale checksum, and `FORBIDDEN`.

**Bug the new rows found.** The ledger's `OperationNotAllowedError` travels as a bare 403 with no code, and backend-v2 read it as `FORBIDDEN`. A move, edit or delete of a managed price line therefore told the agent it lacked access. `favaApiErrorToDomainError` now maps the ledger's "Operation '…' not allowed: …" 403 to `OPERATION_NOT_ALLOWED`, and every other 403 stays `FORBIDDEN`, pinned in `unwrap-response.test.ts`. Without the fix, the managed-price row fails on all three surfaces (3 of 30).

- backend-v2: typecheck, lint:deadcode, format:check, and `yarn test` (479 suites, 10,677 tests);
- ledger: typecheck, ESLint on the new script, and `yarn verify:rustledger`.
