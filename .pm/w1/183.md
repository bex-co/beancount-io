# Billing's plan card shows the list price when a discount applies

**Bug · dashboard · ~45m · A2.** Found by the w1/m39/t014 hosted check on 2026-11-02.

A subscription with an ongoing 100%-off discount reads "$959.88/year" on the Billing plan card and in the billing list. Its change sheet correctly says "Due today $0.00, then $0.00/year" (fixed in [182](./done/182.md)). The card and the list show the price's list amount and ignore the subscription's discounts, so the two surfaces disagree about what the customer pays.

- **Where:** `dashboard/src/features/user-settings/`, the plan summary card and the billing subscription list. Neither has any discount handling.
- **Fix:** show what the subscriber pays, using the same discount-aware amount the server already computes for the change sheet's "Then $X", with the list price as secondary text when they differ. Cover percent-off, amount-off, and expired-repeating discounts.
- **Check:** a synthetic subscription with a forever 100%-off coupon shows $0.00/year (list $959.88) on the card, the list, and the sheet alike.
