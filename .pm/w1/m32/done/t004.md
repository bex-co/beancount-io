## Objective

Prove the connection lifecycle with a controlled clock rather than reading config values back.

## Context

- backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/features/oauth/api/__tests__/

## Steps

1. Refresh on days 13, 44, and 300 of continuous use succeeds.
2. An idle gap of 46 days fails with `invalid_grant`.
3. A chain past one year from authorization fails even if used daily.
4. Static clients keep their lifetimes.

## Files

- backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/features/oauth/api/__tests__/oidc-route.test.ts

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Each listed scenario has a test that fails if the behavior is removed.

## Out of scope

- Load testing.

## Result

2026-10-06. `oidc-route.test.ts` › "third-party lifecycle on a moving clock" moves `Date.now()` (which oidc-provider and the memory adapter both read) across a real DCR connection: refreshing on days 13, 44, then every 30 days through 300 succeeds with each rotated token; a first refresh on day 47 fails; monthly use through day 360 plus day 364 succeeds and day 366 fails (the yearly ceiling). Static clients' lifetimes are pinned by the unit tests from t001. A 10-year ceiling or a 30-day idle window each turn the matching tests red.
