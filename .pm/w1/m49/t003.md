## Objective

Give the hosted statements `bea`'s rule: under a currency target or `at_value`, an Income or Expenses posting is valued at the price on its own date, in the trees and in the interval totals, so REST, GraphQL and MCP (which all read this service) agree with `bea`.

## Context

- `backend-cluster/ledger/src/features/ledger/service/ledger-finance-service.ts`: `getIncomeStatement`, `getOverview` and `getTrialBalance` build `accountHierarchy(…, valuationDate)` and `intervalTotals(…)`.
- `backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/account-tree.ts` (`accountHierarchy`, `trialBalanceLines`) and `interval-series.ts` (`intervalTotals`).
- The trial balance's `valuation_adjustment` is the trees' residual, so it takes the difference once the flow trees change.
- The hosted balance sheet is out of scope (milestone README).

## Steps

1. Value flow postings at their own date in the flow trees and in `intervalTotals`, under a valuing target only.
2. Unit tests over the service on the repro ledger: income statement trees, net profit rows and overview at 300.00; the trial balance's valuation line at 100.00; `units` and `at_cost` unchanged.
