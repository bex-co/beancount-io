## Simplify — 2026-11-20

`/simplify` ran four reviewers (reuse, simplification, efficiency, altitude) over the m49 diff (`6f37b1c36`, `b6b184099`). Applied:

- **Altitude, hosted.** The rule moved from a `{ flows: true }` flag each caller had to remember to the account itself: `accountHierarchy` and `intervalTotals` take the ledger's `flowRoots` and value an income or expenses tree or total by day (`isFlowAccount`). That reached four reads the flag had missed: the account report's `interval_totals_data`, the generic interval-totals read (the dashboard's cash flow and budget pages read income and expenses through it), and the engine-direct `/hierarchy` route, which now agree with the statements for an income or expenses account. Asset and liability totals keep the interval end.
- **Altitude, CLI.** `ChartModule.hierarchy` values any income or expenses subtree by day, not only a tree whose name is exactly a root. Missing-price dates come from the accounts each report shows (`_dated_balances`, shared by `bea balance`, the trial balance and the overview; the income statement passes its own trees).
- **Simplification and reuse.** One `_valued_days` generator values account-days for both the trees and `flow_days`; `_in_scope_flows` is shared with `_flow_ranges`; `flow_days` no longer sorts. `FlowLots` takes its target at construction and `settle()` returns the inventory with the target that reads it, so the pair cannot drift; the finance service's three copies of the tree and totals closures are one `statementBuilders`.
- **Efficiency.** Settling adds the valued amount directly (`LotInventory.addAmount`) instead of a string round-trip, interval totals merge the settled per-account inventories instead of settling a second total, and reads that are not flows keep one inventory instead of one per day.

Skipped: valuing the income statement's account-days once for all three totals (about 60 ms on 50k postings, a larger refactor), and caching the CLI's per-day valuation across a report's calls (about 0.1 s against a multi-second load). The hosted overview's cumulative `income_data`/`expenses_data` series, which no CLI report shows, still value each point at its date.

Checks: `make check-all` (cli); `yarn lint`, `yarn typecheck`, `yarn test` (3,521) and `yarn verify:rustledger` (ledger).
