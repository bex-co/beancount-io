## Objective

Give the hosted statements `bea`'s rule: under a currency target or `at_value`, an Income or Expenses posting is valued at the price on its own date, in the trees and in the interval totals, so REST, GraphQL and MCP (which all read this service) agree with `bea`.

## Context

- `backend-cluster/ledger/src/features/ledger/service/ledger-finance-service.ts`: `getIncomeStatement`, `getOverview` and `getTrialBalance` build `accountHierarchy(…, valuationDate)` and `intervalTotals(…)`.
- `backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/account-tree.ts` (`accountHierarchy`, `trialBalanceLines`) and `interval-series.ts` (`intervalTotals`).
- The trial balance's `valuation_adjustment` is the trees' residual, so it takes the difference once the flow trees change.
- The hosted balance sheet is out of scope (milestone README).

## Steps

1. Value flow postings at their own date in the flow trees and in `intervalTotals`, under a valuing target only.
2. Unit tests over the service on the repro ledger: income statement trees, net profit rows and overview at 300.00; the trial balance's valuation line at 100.00; `units` and `at_cost` unchanged.

## Progress — 2026-11-20

`FlowLots` (`lot-inventory.ts`) keeps income and expenses one inventory per day and settles each at that day's price under a currency or `at_value` (`units`, `units_by_cost` and `at_cost` keep their lots). `accountHierarchy(…, { flows: true })` and `intervalTotals(…, { flows: true })` use it, and `getIncomeStatement`, `getOverview` and `getTrialBalance` pass it for the income and expenses roots, so the trial balance's `valuation_adjustment` takes the translation. The generic interval-totals read keeps its interval-end valuation. On the repro the hosted income statement reads 300 USD in the trees and the monthly, weekly and yearly rows, the overview the same, and the trial balance Expenses 300 with a 100 valuation line.

Tests: `flow-valuation-date.test.ts` (12 cases, a `time` period among them; 8 fail with the source reverted, the 4 controls pass either way). `tree-valuation-date.test.ts` and one `trial-balance-adjustment.test.ts` fixture moved to the rule (the gift now has a quote on its day; the foreign split is dated on its quote). The report oracle's two `usd` cases on `reports-priced.bean` now differ from stock `convert(sum(position), 'USD')` by design (the hotel is 88.00 USD at its day's 1.10, not 89.60 at the latest 1.12): allowlisted in `report-allowlist.json` and noted in `scripts/bql-oracle/README.md`. `yarn lint`, `yarn typecheck`, `yarn test` (3,519) and `yarn verify:rustledger` pass.
