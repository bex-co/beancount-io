# A `bea query` sum that cancels a quotient leaves a 1e-26 residue such as `-0.00000000000000000000000001 USD`

**Source:** follow-up from [325](./done/325.md) (shipped in `d9667954c`, 2026-11-19). The 325 worker found it after its fix and wrote it into the resolution; nobody has filed it until now. **Owner:** cli. **A1:** an agent that runs `SELECT sum(weight)` over a ledger holding a `{{…}}` total cost or an `@@` total price expects zero when the postings balance. It gets `-0.00000000000000000000000001 USD`, which reads as a real non-zero amount.

**Cause, to confirm in triage:** each 28-digit quotient from a total cost or total price is summed at full precision, so the rounding residue survives a sum that should cancel exactly. The 325 fix changed only how many digits are displayed, not the value that is summed.

**Done when:** a text-output sum that cancels to within the currency's display tolerance prints as zero at the currency's precision. JSON and CSV still give the exact value unless triage shows they should not. Add a regression test that fails without the fix.
