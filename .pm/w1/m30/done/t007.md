- **Conformance:** `yarn mcp:conformance https://beancount.io` gave 10 passed, 0 failed, 6 skipped. The skipped checks need a bearer token. Checks 11–16 all pass, including 16 (CIMD).
- **Cursor-shaped DCR:** registered with its three redirect URIs, no `application_type`, and a synthetic `client_name` of "Cursor (m30 verification)". Answered `201` and was stored as `native`. `/auth` with `cursor://anysphere.cursor-mcp/oauth/callback` answers `303 → /oauth/consent`. After sign-in with the QA account, the consent page shows "After you approve, you return to cursor://anysphere.cursor-mcp." and "It calls itself “Cursor (m30 verification)”. Apps choose their own names, so check the address above." Approve stays disabled until a ledger is chosen. Cancel redirects to the Cursor URI with `error=access_denied` and `iss`.
- **VS Code-shaped DCR:** registered with its four redirect URIs and no `application_type`. Answered `201` and was stored as `native`. `/auth` from `http://127.0.0.1:50123/`, a port other than 33418, answers `303 → /oauth/consent`. The consent page shows "you return to 127.0.0.1:50123." and the labelled self-asserted name. There is no loopback-only warning, which is correct: VS Code also registers `https://vscode.dev/redirect`. Cancel redirects to the loopback port.
- **Cleanup:** both flows were cancelled, so no grant or token exists. The two synthetic client registrations remain. Dynamically registered clients are stored with no TTL (`oauth-adapter-model/schema.ts`). They hold no grant, so they are inert, like any abandoned host registration.
