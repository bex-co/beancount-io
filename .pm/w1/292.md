Follow-up from [290](./done/290.md) (shipped in `5a9a9129d`, 2026-11-18). The 290 worker found these while porting the direct-quote rule; nobody has reproduced them against `bea` side by side yet. backend-v2's holdings composer (`holdings.ts`, the code under REST, GraphQL and MCP) still differs from the CLI in three ways:
- it drops a quote whose price is zero;
- it drops a group whose units net to zero;
- a merged group keeps the first part's quote rather than the latest one.
