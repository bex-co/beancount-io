## Objective

`bea` treats the NFC and NFD spellings of the same account name as two different accounts, splitting balances and reporting a false unknown account, and `--search` and BQL `~` miss Unicode-equivalent narrations. Editors on different platforms produce different normalizations of identical text.

## Context

- `w3/278` (major): NFC and NFD account names are different accounts — split balances plus a false unknown-account error.
- `w3/275`: `list transaction --search` and BQL `~` miss Unicode-equivalent narrations (NFC versus NFD).
- Account handling spans `cli/src/bea_engine/ledger/models.py`, `reader.py`, and `writer.py`.

## Steps

1. Normalize account names to NFC at every comparison point: reading, matching, filtering, and writing.
2. Normalize both sides of a text search and of BQL regex matching so equivalent strings match.
3. Emit NFC from the writer, so newly written names are canonical without rewriting existing files.
4. Confirm the normalization does not alter amounts, dates, or metadata keys.

## Files

- cli/src/bea_engine/ledger/models.py
- cli/src/bea_engine/ledger/reader.py
- cli/src/bea_engine/ledger/writer.py
- cli/src/cli/commands/list.py
- cli/tests/test_customer_workflows.py

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An account written NFD in one file and NFC in another resolves to one account with one balance, and no false unknown-account error is reported.
- [ ] `--search` and BQL `~` match a narration regardless of its normalization.
- [ ] The writer emits NFC for new directives.
- [ ] ASCII-only ledgers are byte-identical and behave exactly as before.

## Out of scope

- Normalizing existing files on disk — that would be a rewrite, not a read fix.
- Case-insensitive account matching.
