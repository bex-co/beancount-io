## Objective

Prove the rename works on the deployed backend: preview, commit, and undo, on a disposable synthetic ledger with the QA account.

## Context

- Hosted MCP QA runs against `https://beancount.io/api-gateway/mcp` with the QA account and a disposable ledger, and records the deployed revision (`.pm/DO_NOT_DO.md`, no duplicate stacks).
- `revertCommit` fails with `INTERNAL_SERVER_ERROR` in production until [w23/242](../../../w23/242.md) is fixed and deployed.

## Steps

1. Confirm the deployed revision includes this milestone.
2. On the disposable ledger, preview a rename of an account used in two files, commit it, read the ledger back, and undo it with `revertCommit`.
3. Record the requests, results and deployed revision in this task's Outcome.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The preview, the single commit, the read-back and the undo are recorded with the deployed revision.
