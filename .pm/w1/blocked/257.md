# Decision needed: cost currencies count as cash in `report holdings`

**Source:** follow-up surfaced by the w1/255 worker, 2026-11-15.

After w1/255 (`849766134`), quote and posting-price currencies no longer make a currency cash, but **cost** currencies still do. Dropping them too listed EUR settlement cash as holdings in USD ledgers with EUR-costed lots and broke 18 existing holdings tests. The cost of keeping them: a BTC balance held beside another lot costed in BTC (`10 ALT {0.01 BTC}`) is treated as cash and hidden from holdings.

**Owner chooses:**
- (a) keep cost currencies as cash and document that a currency used as a lot's cost is treated as money; or
- (b) define cash explicitly (for example operating currencies plus an opt-in `option`/metadata list) and update the tests and docs accordingly.

Applies to both cli `_money_currencies` and backend-v2 `moneyCurrencies` (parity). Done when the chosen rule is implemented on every surface and `cli/docs/USAGE.md` states it.

**Blocked 2026-11-15** — This is a product decision that only the owner can make. No ADR, board item or commit records which rule holds. A search of `.pm/`, `docs/adrs/` and `cli/docs/USAGE.md` found nothing. Current baseline on `main` (after `849766134` and `5998a194f`): quote and posting-price currencies are not cash, but cost currencies are, in both cli `_money_currencies` and backend-v2 `moneyCurrencies`. No code has changed for this note. **Unblock:** the owner picks (a) keep cost currencies as cash and document it, or (b) define cash explicitly (operating currencies plus an opt-in option/metadata list) and update the tests and docs. They record the choice here, for example through `/unblock w1`. A worker then implements it on cli and backend-v2 (parity) and states the rule in `cli/docs/USAGE.md`.
