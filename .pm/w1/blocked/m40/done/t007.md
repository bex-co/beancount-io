## Objective

Downgrade and switch-to-monthly buttons open a sheet matching PRFAQ005 question 15. It shows:

- the effective date, "you keep <plan> until then" and nothing to pay today;
- what changes: each limit from → to beside current usage, with a warning when over, and the plan features exactly as the pricing table lists them;
- the over-limit explanation: everything stays yours and keeps working, but you can't add more;
- **Schedule downgrade** and **Keep <plan>**.

A higher tier combined with a switch to monthly shows both split options, with server amounts. A subscriber on a legacy price sees that the price ends with the change.

## Implementation notes

- **Picker**: every tier card except the current one opens the change sheet. A change that waits for the period end says "Takes effect at your renewal" under its button. Free's card waits for t008's cancel sheet.
- **Scheduled sheet** (`pages/general/scheduled-change-body.tsx`), rendered when the preview's effect is `SCHEDULED`:
  - the effective date, "you keep <plan> until then", nothing to pay today, and the next charge with its cycle;
  - "What changes on <date>": each limit that differs in the server's `limitChanges` (ledgers, collaborators per ledger, AI tokens per month, directives), from → to. Ledgers show current usage, with a warning only when over, plus the over-limit reassurance (PRFAQ005 question 5). Collaborator usage is per ledger, so it gets no usage figure;
  - "No longer included": the plan features the target lacks, mirroring the pricing table (`featuresLost`);
  - **Schedule downgrade** or **Schedule switch**, and **Keep <plan>**.
- **Split** (a higher tier while leaving yearly): the sheet runs a second preview for `alternativePriceId` and offers both options with server amounts:
  - the higher tier today on yearly, at the alternative's amount due and proration time;
  - the higher tier monthly from the renewal date, with nothing due today.
  
  Confirming applies the matching change. It defaults to the choice the reader clicked (monthly later).
  - With "now" chosen, the renewal-date copy is hidden.
  - An alternative that fails or is refused shows a message and a retry. "Try again" re-prices both options.
  - The choice lives in the sheet, so retrying after a declined payment keeps it.
  - Analytics report the confirm as the `SPLIT` it was previewed as, with `to` on yearly.
- **Notes on every sheet** (`PendingChangeNotes`): the scheduled change a choice replaces, a cancellation it withdraws (t002's preview flags), and a legacy price that ends.
- A `SCHEDULED` result shows "Scheduled for <date>", then refreshes plan and status. The Scheduled panel that shows it afterwards is t008.
- 20 new strings in all 15 locales; the stale "coming soon" string is retired. A `SCHEDULED_CHANGE` refusal (legacy mutation only) now reads "Takes effect at your renewal".
- **Left as found**: a confirmed change refetches subscription status twice, once in `useChangePlan` and once in the section's `onChanged`. This predates the task and only costs one extra request.
