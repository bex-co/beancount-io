## Objective

A paid subscriber whose renewal charge fails keeps their plan while Stripe keeps retrying, for long enough that a temporary failure (a debit card short on funds, a bank's fraud hold) recovers without interruption, and hears from us as little as possible. User request 2026-10-19 ("统一搞宽松一点，不要给用户太多发送邮箱 noise"), prompted by a monthly Organization subscriber whose October renewal failed twice for insufficient funds and was paid twelve days late.

## Verified first in Stripe test mode (2026-10-19)

A test clock, a declining test card and a test automation (Smart Retries up to 4 within 1 month, then cancel) showed:

- The invoice keeps `next_payment_attempt` on every retry (about weekly), so `holdsPaidTier` keeps the paid tier for the whole window.
- The `invoice.payment_failed` payload carries `next_payment_attempt: null` under an automation, as Stripe documents; `invoice.updated` carries it. t015's email already handles a missing date.
- The automation's cancellation sets `cancellation_details.reason = "payment_failed"`, so t015's "plan has ended" email fires.

The test clock, customer and subscription were deleted and the test product archived; the test automation remains in test mode against that archived product.

## Applied in live Stripe

- **Automation "Beancount monthly - retry for a month":** subscription payment fails, invoice contains Beancount.io, Beancount Growth or Beancount Organization, monthly interval → Smart Retries up to 8 times within 28 days → cancel on day 28.
- **Automation "Beancount yearly - retry for two months":** the same products, yearly interval → Smart Retries up to 8 times within 63 days → cancel on day 63.
- An automation that runs replaces the account's global failed-payment settings for that invoice; every other product on the account keeps the global 4-retries-in-3-weeks rule. The account allows one more live automation.
- **Account-wide:** Stripe's reminders for a payment awaiting bank confirmation cut from three (days 3, 5, 7) to one (day 5).

## Code

t015's first failed-renewal email now waits until the renewal has been failing for six days (7af75f3f1), so a renewal that recovers in the first week sends nothing.

## Done — 2026-10-19

At most two Beancount emails per failed renewal (about day 7, and "plan has ended" on cancellation), none when it recovers within a week; the paid tier holds for the full retry window. Takes full effect with the backend deploy carrying t015's commits; the automations are already live and work with the deployed backend's tier rule.
