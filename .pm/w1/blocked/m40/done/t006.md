## Read — 2026-10-19

Read in the Stripe Dashboard with the user signed in; nothing was changed. These are account-wide settings, and the account also bills two other products.

- **Retries (step 1):** Smart Retries on cards, up to 4 retries within 3 weeks. The first failed renewal leaves the subscription `past_due`. When every retry fails, the subscription is **canceled** (not `unpaid`) and the invoice stays past due.
- **Payments needing confirmation (3D Secure):** a renewal left incomplete for 15 days cancels the subscription; the invoice is left as is.
- **Upcoming renewal events:** already 7 days, so step 2's lead time needs no change.
- **Customer emails:** all off — upcoming renewals, failed card payments, failed bank debits, expiring cards, the hosted link to confirm a payment, trial ending. On: finalized invoices and credit notes (both `send_invoice` only).
- **Step 2's yearly-only reminder is not a Stripe option.** Stripe's docs say the renewal email goes to every `charge_automatically` subscription on the configured lead time, monthly included, and here that means the other products' customers too.
- **Found gap:** the backend handles no `invoice.payment_failed` and sends no failed-payment email, so with Stripe's email off a customer whose renewal card fails is told only by the dashboard banner, then canceled after the last retry.

**Waiting on the user:** whether to turn on Stripe's account-wide emails or have the backend send Beancount-only ones.

## Decided and applied — 2026-10-19

The user chose, with the worker driving the Dashboard:

- **On:** Stripe's hosted link for customers to confirm a payment that needs it (3D Secure), with Stripe's default reminders on days 3, 5 and 7. It always uses Stripe's own page, so it is right for every product on the account.
- **Off, by decision:** Stripe's failed-card-payment email. The account's payment-update links are the legacy mixed setup pointing at the company homepage, where nobody can change a card, and leaving that setup is irreversible and account-wide. Beancount sends its own instead (m40/t015).
- **Not enabled:** Stripe's renewal email, which cannot be limited to yearly plans. Beancount's own yearly reminder replaces it (m40/t015).
- **Webhook:** `invoice.payment_failed` added to the beancount.io endpoint for t015.
- **Unchanged:** the retry schedule, the post-retry cancellation and the 7-day upcoming-renewal lead time.
