- **Dead plumbing.** `McpRequestContext` loses `aiCfoUsage`, `subscriptionService`, and `publicKeyService`, and `accountService` narrows to `getUserProfile`. `ToolContext` and `AgentHandlerContext` lose `apiKeyService`. The MCP route, agent route, and self-hosted agent handler stop wiring them.
