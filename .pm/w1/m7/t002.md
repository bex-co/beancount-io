## Objective

Collapse the signed-in path to a single tap that names the account, with no permission list, and remove the scope-copy machinery that only existed for the approve step.

## Context

- `funcs/mobile-loader.ts` returns `approve` for a signed-in browser; m6 added a `choose_account` step for the sign-up hint.
- `funcs/mobile-scope-copy.ts` (and its test) renders the `ledger.*` scope descriptions; nothing else uses it.
- The tap is deliberate: `io.beancount.*:/oauth/callback` is a custom scheme any app on the device can register, so a signed-in browser must not hand out a code with zero interaction (RFC 8252 §8.6). Fully silent continuation belongs with verified https redirects — inbox note `w1/001`.

## Steps

1. Replace `ApproveStep` with a `continue` step: "Continue as <email>" submits the grant (same POST as t001); "Use another account" keeps its logout-and-switch behavior and lands on login.
2. Fold m6's `choose_account` into this step (its "Create a different account" action lands on register).
3. Delete `mobile-scope-copy.ts`, its test, and the now-unused scope copy keys.
