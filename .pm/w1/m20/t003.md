## Objective

Expose optional upstream quote retrieval through an engine child process.

## Context

- bea add price records supplied quotes and remains a separate operation.
- Provider availability must not determine CI results.

## Steps

1. Launch the selected engine's bean-price executable with supported source/date/history/output/cache and failure options.
2. Compare quote directives, source selection, inversions where supported, and provider failures using deterministic local fixtures.

## Files

- cli/src/cli/main.py; CLI price adapter; optional engine profile; cli/tests/

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] bea price matches the chosen upstream version and bea add price retains its original behavior.
- [ ] Beanprice/provider imports stay outside the frontend and base engine; tests use no live market accounts.

## Out of scope

- New providers, trading features, or treating fetched quotes as an automatic ledger write.
