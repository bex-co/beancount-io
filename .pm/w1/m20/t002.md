## Objective

Expose Beangulp's ingest lifecycle through engine subprocesses while preserving bea import's own review/apply workflow.

## Context

- The user's ingest script owns importer configuration and hooks; engine-side execution must preserve that lifecycle.

## Steps

1. Run the user's ingest script with the selected engine interpreter and forward identify, extract, and archive through the shared process launcher.
2. Preserve batch/path inputs, hooks, raw extraction, deduplication, archive naming, dry runs, and collisions/errors.
3. Use real synthetic importers to verify results and filesystem effects; keep ordinary CSV preview/apply working without optional packages.

## Files

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All three operations match upstream fixture behavior, including hooks and archive failure cases.
