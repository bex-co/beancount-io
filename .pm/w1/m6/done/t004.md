## Objective

Make the interaction pages read as Beancount signing into its own app rather than a third party asking for access, and cut the registration form to what a phone user has to type.

## Context

- The login step is headed "Sign in to continue" with the line "Beancount Mobile wants access to your account." (`mobile-consent.tsx`, key `auth.oauthSignInToContinue` and its sibling).
- The register step renders six fields; the username is prefilled by `generateDefaultUsername()` in `dashboard/src/features/auth/hooks/use-register-form.ts` and is editable later in settings.
- Terms and Privacy links already open in a new tab (`register-form/index.tsx`), so they do not strand the interaction.

## Steps

1. Login step: heading "Sign in to Beancount", drop the "wants access to your account" line. Register step: heading "Create your Beancount account".
2. Hide the username field in the in-interaction register step only (keep the generated value in the submitted form); leave the standalone `/auth/sign-up` page unchanged.
3. Add the new copy keys to every dashboard locale (English base; others may fall back per the i18n config).

## Files

- `dashboard/src/features/oauth/pages/mobile-consent.tsx`
- `dashboard/src/features/auth/components/register-form/index.tsx` (a prop to hide the username field)
- `dashboard/src/i18n/locales/*`

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] No mobile interaction page contains the phrase "wants access to your account".
- [x] The in-interaction register step shows first name, last name, email, password, confirm password — and no username field — and still registers with a valid generated username.
- [x] `/auth/sign-up` is visually and functionally unchanged.
- [x] `yarn lint && yarn test` pass from `dashboard/`.

## Out of scope

- Making first/last name optional (a product decision for the web sign-up too).
