## Objective

Remove MCP's blanket ledger-pin requirement while preserving every existing pinned credential's authority and behavior.

## Context

- Follow this milestone's README.md parity contract and COVERAGE.md baseline map, including after the milestone is archived.
- Read the owning package's CLAUDE.md before implementation. Keep current credential authority and named structural exceptions unless the milestone contract explicitly resolves a contradiction.

## Steps

1. Implement the ADR 0007 D11 selection rules across tools, resource URIs, and bank calls using a shared request-target resolver, with no implicit default ledger for unpinned credentials.
2. Keep pinned plus omitted/same-ledger calls working; reject a different ledger. Require an explicit target for unpinned ledger operations, and authorize it afresh for every call.
3. Permit discovery and account-scoped operations without manufacturing a ledger ID. Keep the transport stateless and record the actual target in request-local audit context.
