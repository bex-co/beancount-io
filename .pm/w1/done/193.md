# Balance-sheet summary labels as-of valuation `latest-price`

**Docs/copy · backend-v2 · minor · ~20m · A1.** From `/qa-find-bugs-mcp`, 2026-11-08, production, read-only.

The numbers are correct; the label is stale.

- **Repro:** `beancount://<owner>/<name>/statements/balance-sheet?time=2016&conversion=at_value` (REST returns the same) reports `valuation: "latest-price"`. Its `netWorth` matches `value(sum(position), 2016-12-31)` from a local beanquery, not the latest price.
- **Cause:** de08900db (w3/500) made the trees value at the last day of the time filter. That change came after 4155e3d70 (w6/009) wrote the label and its docs. The stale text is in `report-summaries.ts:66-73` and at `docs/mcp.md:990` ("priced at the ledger's most recent price") and `:997`.
- **Fix:** describe the valuation as "priced as of `asOf`". Either keep the enum value and correct its docs, or add a new value and keep `latest-price` as an alias. Update the `balance-summary-parity` expectation.

---

**Resolved 2026-11-10.** The label's docs now say what de08900db (w3/500) made true. `latest-price` means priced as of `asOf`, at the latest price on or before it. A past period is therefore valued as of its own last day. The enum value is unchanged, so no client or spec changes.
- **What changed:** the corrected text is in the `BalanceSheetSummary.valuation` comment (`report-summaries.ts`) and in `docs/mcp.md`. The docs used to say "priced at the ledger's most recent price", and also "`asOf` is the position cut-off, not the price date". Both were wrong.
- **Tests:** the dated numbers were already pinned by w3/500's tests, and the `balance-summary-parity` expectation (`latest-price`) still holds, so no test changed. (`backend-v2`)
