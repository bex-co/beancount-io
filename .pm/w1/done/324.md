# Report tables print a lot's per-unit cost by the wrong precision: holdings AVG COST shows 28 digits for a total-cost lot, and the register's COST column rounds a `{0.0042 USD}` lot to `0.00 USD`

**Bug · cli · minor · 45–60m · A1.** From routine cli-qa, 2026-11-19. Two text tables show a lot's per-unit cost, and each uses a different precision rule. Each rule fails where the other works.

- `bea report holdings` AVG COST counts every fractional digit of the booked per-unit cost (w1/267). A per-unit cost that Beancount divided out of what was written has 25 or more digits. That happens with a total cost `{{100 USD}}`, a compound cost `{100.00 # 9.95 USD}`, or an interpolated `{USD}`. AVG COST then prints `33.33333333333333333333333333 USD`. `bea add` itself steers users here: its refusal of `{100/3 USD}` (w1/317) says to "give the lot's total cost instead, which stays exact", and that is the form that prints 28 digits.
- `bea report register` COST rounds the per-unit cost to the currency's posting precision. A `{0.0042 USD}` lot in a cents ledger reads `0.00 USD (2026-01-02)`. A `{0.30 USD}` lot in a whole-dollar ledger reads `0 USD`, while holdings shows the same lot as `0.30 USD`. w1/267 fixed this for holdings only. Its blast-radius note said `register` prints "amounts, not per-unit figures", but its COST column is per-unit.

Both are text-only. `--json` and `--format csv` stay exact (`"cost": {"number": "0.0042", …}`, `average_cost` `333.3333333333333333333333333`), and the totals are right. A user, or an agent pasting the table into a summary, reads a 28-digit price or a lot that cost nothing.

Environment: main `7523eafdc`, macOS 26.5.1, `bea 0.4.1` from the checkout (`uv run bea`), isolated `BEA_CONFIG_DIR`/`XDG_CACHE_HOME`, `BEA_NO_UPDATE_NOTIFIER=1`, `BEA_FILE`/`BEA_TOKEN` unset, stdin closed, no TTY. Every command exits 0.

## Repro 2: register COST

```sh
cat > main.bean <<'EOF'
option "operating_currency" "USD"
2026-01-01 open Assets:Bank:Checking USD
2026-01-01 open Assets:Broker
2026-01-01 open Equity:Opening
2026-01-01 * "open"
  Assets:Bank:Checking 5000.00 USD
  Equity:Opening
2026-01-02 * "buy"
  Assets:Broker 100000 SHIB {0.0042 USD}
  Assets:Bank:Checking -420.00 USD
2026-01-03 * "buy penny"
  Assets:Broker 1000 PENNY {0.305 USD}
  Assets:Bank:Checking -305.00 USD
EOF
bea --file main.bean report register Assets:Broker -x units
```

Actual: the COST cells read `0.00 USD (2026-01-02)` and `0.31 USD (2026-01-03)`. With the default conversion and `--allow-errors` they read the same. The CSV row carries `0.0042` and `0.305`. In a whole-dollar ledger (`5000 USD` postings, `1000 PENNY {0.30 USD}`), the register shows `0 USD (2026-01-02)`, and `report holdings` shows `0.30 USD` for that lot.

## Expected

Both columns follow one rule, the one w1/267 set out. A per-unit cost renders at the larger of two precisions: the currency's display precision, and the digits the lot's per-unit cost was written with. A per-unit cost the ledger never wrote counts for nothing beyond the display precision. That covers the per-unit part of a total-only cost, a compound cost, or a cost Beancount interpolated, since those digits are division noise. A weighted average that does not divide evenly still rounds to that precision.

- Holdings: `{{100 USD}}` over 3 shows `33.33 USD`, and `{{1000.00 USD}}` over 3 shows `333.33 USD`. `{100.00 # 9.95 USD}` over 3 shows `103.32 USD`, and `{USD}` against `-1000.00 USD` shows `333.33 USD`. `{{1000 JPY}}` over 3 in a whole-yen ledger shows `333 JPY`. The w1/267 controls do not change: `{0.0042 USD}` shows `0.0042 USD`, `{0.30 USD}` in a whole-dollar ledger shows `0.30 USD`, and 1600 USD over 15 VTI on whole-dollar lots shows `107 USD`.
- Register: `{0.0042 USD}` shows `0.0042 USD (2026-01-02)`, `{0.305 USD}` shows `0.305 USD`, and `{0.30 USD}` in a whole-dollar ledger shows `0.30 USD`. The total-cost and compound rows keep `33.33 USD`, `103.32 USD` and `333.33 USD`.
- JSON and CSV stay exact and unchanged.

## Root cause

- `cli/src/bea_engine/report.py` `_holdings` (about lines 823 and 828) calls `_fraction_digits(cost.number)` (about line 975) on the booked `Cost.number`. Booking has already divided a `{{T}}` or `{per # T}` cost into a 28-significant-digit per-unit number, and the same happens when the cost is interpolated. The `average_cost_precision` hint therefore reaches 25 or more digits, and `_average_cost` in `cli/src/cli/commands/report.py` (about line 684) renders to that many.
- `cli/src/cli/commands/report.py` `_row_cells` (about line 755) renders the register's `row["cost"]` through `_money(cost, conversion, precision)`. That is the posting display precision, so a sub-cent per-unit cost rounds away.

## Fix

Compute one bounded per-unit-cost precision and use it in both places. A lot's cost contributes its written per-unit digits, read from the source posting's `CostSpec.number_per`. `bea_engine.ledger.reader._spelled_entries` already serves `_typed_precision` (about line 2719) in this module for the same purpose. A total-only, compound or interpolated per-unit cost contributes nothing. A cheaper bound is also acceptable: count a cost's digits only when the number has fewer significant digits than the decimal context's precision, because a context-length quotient is division noise. Send the register a per-row hint (a text-only key stripped from `--json`, like `display_precision` and `average_cost_precision`), or derive it in the frontend from the exact string with the same bound. Update the w1/267 sentence in `cli/docs/USAGE.md` "Reports" ("The one per-unit figure, `report holdings`' AVG COST, …") to cover the register's COST column too and to say division-derived digits do not count. Add regression cases to `cli/tests/test_report_holdings_register_frontend.py`: the three holdings forms and the whole-yen case, the register sub-cent and whole-dollar cases, and the w1/267 controls. They must fail with the fix reverted. Run `make check-all` in `cli/`. backend-v2 has no text renderer and its JSON is exact, so there is no REST/GraphQL/MCP parity change. The dashboard's equivalent of this bug was w21/160 (Money flow), fixed there.

## Blast radius

Searched `cli/src/cli/commands/report.py` for per-unit cells: holdings AVG COST and the register COST column are the only two. `capital-gains` and the statements print quantities times costs, which display precision governs correctly. `bea list transaction` spells the booked cost on purpose ("what the ledger holds", USAGE.md "Listing directives"), so it is out of scope.

**Resolved 2026-11-19** — Holdings AVG COST and the register's COST column now follow one rule: a per-unit cost renders at the larger of the currency's display precision and the digits its lot cost is written with, and a quotient that ran to the decimal context's full precision (a total, compound or interpolated cost that does not divide evenly) contributes no digits. This is the cheaper bound the note allows, so an exact unwritten quotient such as `{{1 USD}}` over 1000 units still shows `0.001 USD`. On the repro ledgers holdings prints `33.33 USD`, `103.32 USD` and `333.33 USD` (also under `--group-by commodity` and `-x at_cost`), `{{1000 JPY}}` over 3 prints `333 JPY`, and the register prints `0.0042 USD (2026-01-02)`, `0.305 USD (2026-01-03)` and `0.30 USD` for a whole-dollar ledger's `{0.30 USD}` lot, with the total-cost and compound rows unchanged. The w1/267 controls still hold. The engine helper is `_cost_digits` in `cli/src/bea_engine/report.py`. The register gets a per-row text-only `cost_precision` hint, stripped from `--json`, and `_per_unit_cost` in `cli/src/cli/commands/report.py` renders both columns. JSON and CSV stay exact. `cli/docs/USAGE.md` (Reports) covers both columns and the division rule. Tests: 4 new cases in `cli/tests/test_report_holdings_register_frontend.py`. 3 fail with the fix reverted, and the register total-cost case is a control that fails when the bound is removed. `make check-all` passes. No REST/GraphQL/MCP change, since backend-v2 has no text renderer and its JSON is exact. Shipped in `ab076ddfa` (`cli`).
