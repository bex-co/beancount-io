## Objective

A subscriber whose renewal charge fails hears about it by email, and a yearly subscriber is reminded before the renewal charge. Stripe's own emails cannot do this for Beancount alone: the account bills other products too, its failed-payment email's card-update link points at the company homepage, and the only fix there is an irreversible, account-wide switch. The user decided on 2026-10-19 that the backend sends Beancount-only emails instead (t006 records the settings).

## Shipped

- **Failed renewal** — `invoice.payment_failed` (added to the beancount.io webhook endpoint on 2026-10-19) for a renewal (`billing_reason: subscription_cycle`) whose invoice bills one of our prices:
  - first failed attempt: "Your {plan} renewal didn't go through", with the amount, the next retry date and an "Update your card" button to Billing settings, where the failed-renewal banner opens the billing portal;
  - no retry left (`next_payment_attempt` null): "Your {plan} plan has ended", saying the account is on Free, nothing is deleted, and how to choose the plan again;
  - the retries in between send nothing.
- **Yearly renewal reminder** — on `invoice.upcoming` (7 days before), for a yearly plan that is not set to end and has no scheduled change landing at that renewal (that change has its own reminder): "Your {plan} plan renews on {date}", with the upcoming invoice's amount, discounts included. A renewal that charges nothing sends none.
- Each email is sent once per invoice (failures) or per renewal (reminder) through `sent_billing_emails`; a failed send gives the claim back and the webhook answers with an error so Stripe redelivers, as the plan-change reminder already did.
- The claim-and-send, plan-name, date and money formatting now live once in `operations/billing-email.ts`, shared with the plan-change emails.
- The template is `renewal.template.ts` (English only, like every other template), with preview entries and a README row.

## Review fixes — 2026-10-19

A review of the first version found that `next_payment_attempt` being null does not mean the plan ended. Stripe leaves it null under Billing automations, when no payment method is on file, for India-issued cards, for `send_invoice`, and while a payment waits for 3D Secure. In all of those the subscription is still `past_due`, so "Your plan has ended" would have been wrong.

| Email | Event | Sent when |
|---|---|---|
| "Your {plan} renewal didn't go through" | `invoice.payment_failed` | A renewal (`subscription_cycle`) billing one of our prices, `attempt_count` 1, bound customer, and the payment is **not** waiting on the bank (no payment intent in `requires_action` or failed with `authentication_required`; a failed lookup still sends). It names the next retry date when one is scheduled, and promises no retry when none is. |
| "Your {plan} plan has ended" | `customer.subscription.deleted` | Status `canceled`, `cancellation_details.reason` `payment_failed`, one of our prices, bound customer. Sent after the cached plan is refreshed, once per subscription. It quotes no amount. |
| "Your {plan} plan renews on {date}" | `invoice.upcoming` | A yearly plan with no `cancel_at_period_end` and no `cancel_at` at or before the renewal, a non-zero amount due, and no scheduled change at that renewal. |

A payment waiting on the bank is left to Stripe's hosted confirm-payment email, which t006 turned on and which links to the page that completes it.

## Quieter first notice — 2026-10-19

By user decision, renewals are recovered leniently (a Stripe Billing automation scoped to Beancount's products retries for about a month, then cancels) and subscribers get less email.

- **Verified in Stripe test mode** with a test clock and that kind of automation: the invoice keeps `next_payment_attempt` set on every retry, so the paid tier holds while past due; the `invoice.payment_failed` payload carries no retry time under automations; retries landed about weekly; the cancellation carries `cancellation_details.reason = "payment_failed"`.
- **The failed-renewal email now waits a week.** It goes out once per invoice, on the first failed charge at least six days after the renewal invoice was finalized, timed by the event rather than the clock. A renewal that recovers in its first week emails nobody.
- **No-date copy:** "We'll keep retrying automatically over the coming weeks, and you keep {plan} meanwhile." The preview shows that variant, since automations leave the retry time out.
