## Objective

Billing settings shows a Scheduled panel when a change or cancellation is pending, for example "Changes to Premium · Monthly on <date>" with **Keep current**, or "<plan> ends <date>" with **Resume**.

**Cancel subscription** opens a sheet matching PRFAQ005 question 15:

- what you keep, and until when;
- the Free limits afterwards;
- a reminder that your books are plain text and nothing is deleted;
- one alternative: **Switch to yearly** for a monthly plan, or **Downgrade to <next lower tier>** from Growth up;
- an optional reason;
- equally weighted **Cancel subscription** and **Keep <plan>**.

## Context

- t002's status and undo operations. Cancel and resume exist today (`subscription-dialogs.tsx`, `use-subscription-cancel.ts`, `use-subscription-resume.ts`).
- Events from PRFAQ005 question 19: `cancel_started`, `cancel_confirmed {reason}`, `subscription_resumed`, `plan_change_scheduled`, `plan_change_reverted`.

## Steps

1. Build the Scheduled panel with its undo actions.
2. Replace the cancel dialog with the cancel sheet and its alternatives.
3. Emit the events and document them in `dashboard/docs/event-taxonomy.md`.

## Files

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The panel appears only with a pending change, and its action undoes that change.
- [ ] The cancel sheet offers the right alternative per plan and cycle, and records an optional reason.
- [ ] Both buttons carry equal weight, and the events fire once each.

## Out of scope

- Retention offers or discounts beyond the alternatives above.

## Implementation notes

- **Scheduled panel** (`pages/general/scheduled-panel.tsx`), under the plan banner:
  - with a scheduled change: "Changes to <plan> on <date>", with **Keep current** (`cancelScheduledPlanChange`, then a toast and a status refetch);
  - with a pending cancellation: "<plan> ends <date>", with **Resume** (the existing resume confirmation);
  - nothing pending renders nothing.

  The billing section no longer repeats the cancelled notice or its Resume button; it keeps **Manage billing** and **Cancel subscription**.
- **Cancel sheet** (`pages/general/cancel-sheet.tsx`) replaces the cancel dialog. It mounts only while open, so the chosen reason resets each time. It shows:
  - what you keep until the period end, and the Free limits afterwards (`describeTierQuota`);
  - that books are plain text and nothing is deleted;
  - one alternative: **Switch to yearly — <price>/mo** for a monthly plan, else **Downgrade to <next tier down>** from Growth up. Either opens the change sheet with surface `cancel_sheet`. Premium yearly has none;
  - an optional reason;
  - **Cancel subscription** and **Keep <plan>** with identical styling.
- **Reason recorded server-side**: `cancelSubscription` takes an optional `reason: CancellationReason`. It is stored as Stripe's `cancellation_details.feedback` (`too_expensive`, `missing_features`, `unused`, `switched_service`, `other`), where Stripe already reports churn. The argument is additive and GraphQL-only, like the mutation.
- **Events** (taxonomy §6):
  - `cancel_started` when the sheet opens;
  - `cancel_confirmed { from, reason? }` and `subscription_resumed` on the server's success only;
  - `plan_change_scheduled` on a `SCHEDULED` result;
  - `plan_change_reverted { from, to }` on **Keep current**.
- **Hardening from review**:
  - Cancel and resume refetch status through the mutation (`awaitRefetchQueries`), so their buttons stay disabled until the Scheduled panel reflects the result. A double click cannot cancel or count twice.
  - While a cancellation is being sent, the sheet can't be left with Keep, Escape or its alternative.
  - The sheet describes the subscription that was clicked.
  - One check (`isPendingCancellation`: the period-end flag or a `cancel_at` date set elsewhere) drives both the panel and the billing section.
  - Resuming, or cancelling without a reason, clears Stripe's stored feedback, so an old reason never lingers.
  - Undo failures get their own message.
- 18 new strings in 15 locales. The Free plan is named with each locale's own term, because a literal "Free" fails the stray-English gate. The old dialog's five keys are removed.
