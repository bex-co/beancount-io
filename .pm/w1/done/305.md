# The hosted trial balance still reports Beancount's accepted rounding as a "Valuation/translation adjustment"

Split from w1/304 (shipped in `4abbffea3`), which fixed this for `bea`. **Owner:** backend (ledger service, backend-v2) and dashboard. **A1.**

`bea report trial-balance` now separates two things on the line that nets the valued trees. `valuation_adjustment` is what valuing moves: a holding's unrealized gain and a currency's translation. `rounding_residual` is what Beancount's tolerance accepted. On a USD-only ledger with no prices, `-6.67 USD` split as two `3.333 USD` legs leaves 0.004 USD.

The hosted trial balance still adds that 0.004 USD into `valuation_adjustment`. The REST, GraphQL and MCP `getLedgerTrialBalance` results all do this, and the dashboard's trial balance page prints it as "Valuation/translation adjustment". The cause is in `backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/account-tree.ts`: `trialBalanceAdjustment` negates the sum of all five roots, so nothing separates the rounding. `ledger-finance-service.ts` passes that sum on unchanged. `cli/docs/USAGE.md` (Reports) records this as a known difference.

**Repro:** `bea`'s w1/304 ledger (`Assets:Cash 100.00 USD` opened against `Equity:Opening`, then `Expenses:Food 3.333 USD`, `Expenses:Rent 3.333 USD`, `Assets:Cash -6.67 USD`) pushed to a hosted ledger. Expect `valuation_adjustment` `{"USD":"0.004"}` on every surface.

**Done when:** on that ledger, the hosted trial balance on REST, GraphQL and MCP returns the 0.004 USD as a rounding residual field beside a zero valuation adjustment, derived as `bea` does it (the trees read at cost, less what priced exchanges leave there). Holdings and FX fixtures keep their adjustment. Following the parity workflow in `backend-cluster/backend-v2/AGENTS.md`, the field reaches every surface in one change. The dashboard trial balance page shows it under its own label in every locale. A parity test pins it, and the USAGE note about the hosted difference is removed.

**Resolved 2026-11-19** — The hosted trial balance now returns `rounding_residual` beside `valuation_adjustment` (`trialBalanceLines`, `backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/account-tree.ts`). It is the at-cost remainder of every transaction no price weighs, derived as `bea` does it (the trees read at cost, less what priced exchanges leave there), valued at the as-of date; valuation is the rest. On the repro, REST, GraphQL (`roundingResidual`) and the MCP `ledgerTrialBalance` resource return `valuation_adjustment: {}` and `rounding_residual: {"USD": "0.004"}` at market, at cost and at value, with and without `time`; the live `yarn verify:rustledger` check runs it through the real engine. Holdings and the priced exchange keep `-109.0000 USD` (at cost `99.00 USD -90.00 EUR`) beside the rounding. Both lines are null under `units`/`units_by_cost`, with ledger errors, and while a root holds a commodity the conversion could not value. Under `time`, the hosted opening entries net earlier transactions, so the line starts at the period's first day (`bea`'s rule when the clamp keeps its plugs). The IDL, backend-v2's ledger client, the v1 OpenAPI snapshot and the CLI pin carry the field; `docs/mcp.md` says which part each line carries. The dashboard trial balance shows a "Rounding residual" row in all 15 locales, and its reconciliation difference includes it. The USAGE note about the hosted difference is removed. Covered by `backend-cluster/ledger/src/features/ledger/service/__tests__/trial-balance-adjustment.test.ts` (12 new cases fail with the split neutralized), `backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/features/ai-agent/api/__tests__/trial-balance-adjustment-parity.test.ts` (REST, GraphQL, MCP), the resolver test, and the dashboard summary and content tests. Shipped in `ca86b24c2` (`ledger`, `backend-v2`, `idl`, `dashboard`, `cli` docs).
