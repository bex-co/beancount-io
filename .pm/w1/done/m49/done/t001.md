## Objective

Under a valuing conversion (a currency list or `at_value`), value each Income and Expenses posting at the price on its own date before it is aggregated, in the account trees and in the interval rows, so the rows add up to the headline at every interval.

## Context

- `cli/src/fava/modules/chart.py`: `ChartModule.hierarchy` serialises the whole period's inventory at `filtered.end_date`; `interval_totals` converts each bucket at `date_range.end_inclusive`.
- `cli/src/fava/modules/financial_statements.py`: `income_statement`, `overview`, `balance_sheet` (`current_earnings`, the residual split by `_split_residual`) and `trial_balance` read those two calls. `bea balance` reads `trial_balance` (`cli/src/bea_engine/report.py` `_balances`), and `_scoped_earnings` reads `chart.hierarchy` for a filtered balance sheet.
- A posting the conversion cannot value on its own date stays in its own commodity, in the row and in the tree alike; it must not be revalued at the as-of date afterwards.
- `units` and `at_cost` value nothing by date and must not change.

## Steps

1. Value flow postings by (account, date) under a valuing conversion, in the trees rooted at the income and expenses roots and in `interval_totals`.
2. Leave balance trees and balance series at the as-of date.
3. Regression tests: the repro at `-i monthly`, `weekly` and `yearly` all reading 300.00 with rows summing to the headline; the balance sheet reconciling with a 100.00 adjustment; the trial balance netting; a ledger whose rates never move unchanged; `units` and `at_cost` unchanged; `Expenses:Charity 5 VOO {400 USD}` valued at the VOO price on its date.
