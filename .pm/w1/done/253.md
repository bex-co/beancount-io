# `bea report holdings` drops every position held without a cost basis, so `@`-bought shares vanish

Found by `/qa-find-bugs-cli w1` on 2026-11-15 (HEAD `14d28d9d3`, `bea 0.3.1`, macOS 26.5, checkout `uv run bea`, stdin closed, isolated `BEA_CONFIG_DIR`/`XDG_CACHE_HOME`, offline synthetic ledger). Severity: minor. A1: an agent asked "what do I hold and what is it worth" gets a portfolio that silently leaves out every lot recorded with a price but no cost (`3 AAPL @ 200 USD`, the usual spelling for users who do not track lots, and for most crypto ledgers). The answer exits 0 with `valuation: "complete"`.

**Contract.** `cli/docs/USAGE.md:1953` says `holdings` "lists every non-cash position with units, average cost, cost basis, and market value". `USAGE.md:2179` says "Holdings without a cost basis (cash, `@`-priced lots) keep amounts already in an operating currency, are valued into the first operating currency that has a price, and otherwise stay in their units". Both the trial balance (`bea balance -x USD`) and `report balance-sheet` count and value these lots.

**Repro** (synthetic):

```sh
cat > main.bean <<'EOF'
option "operating_currency" "USD"
2024-01-01 open Assets:Bank USD
2024-01-01 open Assets:Mixed
2024-01-01 open Assets:Plain
2024-01-01 open Equity:Opening
2024-01-02 * "fund"
  Assets:Bank 10000 USD
  Equity:Opening
2024-02-01 * "buy costed"
  Assets:Mixed 5 AAPL {150 USD}
  Assets:Bank -750 USD
2024-02-02 * "buy priced into mixed"
  Assets:Mixed 2 AAPL @ 200 USD
  Assets:Bank -400 USD
2024-02-03 * "buy priced into plain"
  Assets:Plain 3 AAPL @ 200 USD
  Assets:Bank -600 USD
2024-03-01 price AAPL 170 USD
EOF
bea --file main.bean check                                   # clean, exit 0
bea --file main.bean report holdings                         # exit 0
bea --file main.bean report holdings -a Assets:Plain         # exit 0
bea --file main.bean balance -x USD -t 2024-03 Mixed Plain
```

Actual: holdings lists one row, `Assets:Mixed 5 AAPL`, market value `850 USD`, and no `Assets:Plain` row at all. `-a Assets:Plain` prints "No holdings." (`groups: []`, `account_filter_empty: false`). `--group-by commodity` and `-x units` show the same 5 AAPL. The trial balance values `Assets:Mixed` at `1,190 USD` (7 AAPL) and `Assets:Plain` at `510 USD` (3 AAPL); `report balance-sheet -t 2024-03` agrees; BQL `sum(position)` shows `5 AAPL {150 USD}, 2 AAPL` for Mixed.

Expected: `Assets:Mixed` holds 7 AAPL and `Assets:Plain` holds 3 AAPL, each valued at the 170 USD quote (`1,190 USD` and `510 USD`; holdings total `1,700 USD`, the same as the balance sheet's two accounts). A lot with no cost has an unknown basis: `average_cost` and `cost_basis` are `null` (text `—`), or the row carries the costed part's basis only and says so. Cash stays out: an uncosted position in an operating currency (or a currency the ledger uses only as money) is not a holding.

**Root cause.** `cli/src/bea_engine/report.py:626` (`_holdings`) skips every inventory key with `cost is None`, on the rule in its docstring at :605: "Lots without cost are cash, not holdings (w22/m3/t001)". That rule treats a commodity's identity as a cash test by proxy, and nothing in w22/m3/t001's acceptance asked for it. The hosted holdings composer copies it on purpose: `backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/features/ledger/utils/holdings.ts:267` ("Costless lots are cash, not holdings — same exclusion as the CLI").

**Fix** (`cli` plus `backend-v2` for parity, about 1h). Classify cash by commodity, not by the presence of a cost: an uncosted position is cash when its commodity is an operating currency (fall back to "used as a cost or price currency in the ledger" when no operating currency is set); every other uncosted position is a holding with units, a market value from the price map at the period end (missing-price policy unchanged), and a null basis and average cost. Totals must say when some basis is unknown rather than summing it as zero. Update USAGE.md:1953-1957 to state the rule. Apply the same rule in the hosted holdings composer so REST, GraphQL and MCP keep answering what the CLI answers (root `AGENTS.md` parity rule), with the parity suite covering it. Regression tests: the fixture above in `--json` (Mixed 7 AAPL, Plain 3 AAPL, market values 1190 and 510), the `--group-by commodity` row (10 AAPL), CSV output, and a cash control (an uncosted USD and an uncosted EUR balance with EUR listed as an operating currency stay out). Revert the fix and check the tests fail.

**Blast radius.** Only `_holdings` (CLI) and `holdings.ts` (hosted) apply the exclusion; `report_csv.py` renders the same groups. Balance, balance-sheet, overview and the interval series already include uncosted lots.

**Dedupe.** Searched `.pm/**` for `holdings` with `cash`, `without cost`, `uncost`, `@-priced` and `cost basis`; ran `git log -S "Lots without cost are cash"` (introduced in `acb7868e4`, w22/m3/t001, no decision recorded). w1/050 (done) fixed `at_value` revaluation of cost-less positions in `balance`, not holdings; w1/229 (done) is about an implied price from an `@` sale. No open item covers this.

---

**Resolved 2026-11-15.** Holdings now tell cash apart by commodity, not by a missing cost: an uncosted balance is cash when its commodity is money in the ledger (an operating currency, or any currency a cost, a posting price, or a `price` directive is quoted in, across the whole ledger), and every other uncosted position is a holding. It joins the costed group of the same commodity when there is exactly one, is valued at the period-end quote (first operating currency, then any other quote currency, when it has no cost currency), and its group reports `cost_basis` and `average_cost` as `null` (text `—`, CSV blank); `totals.cost_basis` leaves it out and a new `cost_basis_complete: false` says so (text: "excludes positions without a cost basis"). The repro now reads `Assets:Mixed` 7 AAPL at 1,190 USD and `Assets:Plain` 3 AAPL at 510 USD (total 1,700 USD); `--group-by commodity` gives one 10 AAPL row. `cli/src/bea_engine/report.py`, `cli/src/cli/commands/report.py`, `cli/docs/USAGE.md`. The hosted composer (`backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/features/ledger/utils/holdings.ts`) applies the same rule, reading posting price currencies through one more BQL query, so REST, MCP (`ledgerHoldings`) and GraphQL (`averageCost`/`costBasis` now nullable, new `costBasisComplete`) answer the same; OpenAPI snapshot and the CLI's pinned spec refreshed. Regression tests: `cli/tests/test_report_holdings_uncosted.py` (JSON, `-a`, commodity grouping, CSV, text, cash controls, no-operating-currency and unquoted cases; all fail with the fix reverted) and the `uncosted positions` block in `backend-v2/.../utils/__tests__/holdings.test.ts` plus `ledger-finance-service.test.ts`. (`cli`, `backend-v2`)
