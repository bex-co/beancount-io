# `archive-parity` times out under the full backend-v2 suite

**Flaky test · backend-v2 · minor · ~30m.** Seen 2026-11-09 on `main` (`8a2e14649`).

- **Symptom:** a full `yarn test` failed 1 of 9579 tests: "publishes an example that reaches the archive on REST and MCP" in `src/features/ai-agent/api/__tests__/archive-parity.test.ts:346`. An earlier full run the same day hit the same timeout. Run alone, the suite passes, twice in a row.
- **Likely cause:** the fixture is slow, and the test's timeout is too short when the whole suite runs in parallel. Root-cause it with `/routine-flaky-test-fixer` (repeat runs at full parallelism), not by raising the timeout blindly.
- **Check:** 20 consecutive green full-suite runs.

---

**Resolved 2026-11-10.** The root cause was CPU in the test body. The two contract tests in `archive-parity.test.ts` called `generateV1OpenAPIDocument()` inside their bodies, and each call regenerates the whole v1 document. That is about 2 s of CPU even on an idle machine. Under the full suite's parallel load it stretched past the 10 s test timeout.
- **Fix:** the suite now generates the document once in a `beforeAll`, with a budget of its own, and the tests read the cached paths. Both tests dropped from about 2,000 ms to about 10 ms. Assertions and the global timeout are unchanged.
- **Check, met:** 20 consecutive green full-suite runs (9,729 tests each), in an isolated worktree at HEAD plus the fix.
- **Same pattern elsewhere:** `configuration-parity.test.ts` and `openapi-completeness.test.ts` also generate the document inside test bodies. Neither has been seen failing. (`backend-v2`)
