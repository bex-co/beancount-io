# `bea report cash-flow` refuses a whole period over one foreign-currency purchase paid from a cash account (`Expenses:Travel 20.00 EUR @ 1.10 USD` / `Assets:Checking -22.00 USD`), calling it a currency exchange that "moves cash in EUR, USD", while the same purchase on a credit card reports fine (gap in w5/1068)

**Bug · cli · major · ~45m · A1.** From routine cli-qa, 2026-11-22. w5/1068 made `report cash-flow` refuse a period its at-cost rows cannot explain, so a real currency exchange between cash accounts (`Assets:Euro 100 EUR @ 1.10 USD` / `Assets:Checking -110 USD`) no longer invents an opening balance. The guard reaches further than that. Any transaction that touches a cash account and has a price-weighted leg is treated as an exchange, including the most ordinary traveller's entry: a home-currency bank or debit card paying a bill stated in a foreign currency. The cash leg is plain USD, and nothing in EUR is cash. One 22.00 USD taxi fare makes `--time 2026` exit 2, so the year has no cash-flow statement at all. `--allow-errors` and `--json` give the same refusal (`category: usage`). The same EUR hotel bill charged to a credit card and paid off from checking reports normally. A EUR client invoice paid into a USD account (`Income:Consulting -500.00 EUR @ 1.10 USD`) is refused the same way. Downstream, `skills/beancount-business-metrics/scripts/business_metrics.py` reads `report cash-flow --time <month>` for each month, so it stops with exit 2 on any month that has such an entry. The report-pack and `beancount-ask` cash-movement recipes call the same report.

Environment: main `7c8cb4861`, `bea 0.5.0` from the checkout (`uv run bea`), Python 3.12.14, macOS 26.5.1. Isolated `BEA_CONFIG_DIR`/`XDG_CACHE_HOME`, `BEA_NO_UPDATE_NOTIFIER=1`, `BEA_FILE`/`BEA_TOKEN` unset, stdin closed, no TTY, scratch cwd, fresh processes. The ledger file's hash is unchanged after every command.

## Repro

```sh

cat > books/main.bean <<'EOF'
option "operating_currency" "USD"
2026-01-01 open Assets:Checking USD
2026-01-01 open Liabilities:Card USD
2026-01-01 open Equity:Opening-Balances
2026-01-01 open Expenses:Travel

2026-01-01 * "Opening"
  Assets:Checking  5000.00 USD
  Equity:Opening-Balances

2026-01-05 * "Paris hotel, credit card"
  Expenses:Travel   100.00 EUR @ 1.10 USD
  Liabilities:Card -110.00 USD

2026-01-20 * "Pay card"
  Liabilities:Card  110.00 USD
  Assets:Checking  -110.00 USD

2026-02-05 * "Paris taxi, debit card"
  Expenses:Travel    20.00 EUR @ 1.10 USD
  Assets:Checking   -22.00 USD
EOF
bea --file books/main.bean check                               # exit 0
bea --file books/main.bean report cash-flow --time 2026-01     # exit 0 (control: card purchase)
bea --file books/main.bean report cash-flow --time 2026-02     # exit 2
bea --file books/main.bean report cash-flow --time 2026        # exit 2
bea --file books/main.bean query "SELECT account, sum(weight) WHERE date >= 2026-02-01 AND date < 2026-03-01 GROUP BY account"
```

Actual: January reports financing `Equity:Opening-Balances 5,000.00 USD` and `Liabilities:Card -110.00 USD`, closing cash 4,890.00 USD, exit 0. February and the whole year exit **2** with `Cash Flow cannot reconcile this period: priced currency exchanges (2026-02-05 at main.bean:20 (Paris taxi, debit card)) move cash in EUR, USD by a price, not by cost, so the activity rows would not explain the cash accounts' actual change. … narrow the period to exclude them, or read the balance sheet.` No EUR cash account exists. The query, as the independent control, shows `Expenses:Travel 22.00 USD` and `Assets:Checking -22.00 USD`: the transaction's own weights explain the cash movement exactly.

## Expected

February reports `Operating: Expenses:Travel -22.00 USD`, net change -22.00 USD, opening 4,890.00 USD and closing 4,868.00 USD, with exit 0. The year reports opening 0.00 USD and closing 4,868.00 USD. Basis: Beancount balances each transaction by posting weight, so a price-weighted leg's weight (`20.00 EUR @ 1.10 USD` weighs 22.00 USD) is the cash it moved whenever the transaction's cash legs carry no price. The direct method reports a payment at the cash paid. The refusal stays, with today's text, for the case w5/1068 actually targeted: a transaction whose *cash* leg is price-weighted, such as an exchange into a foreign-currency cash account. There the cash account's change is in units the weights do not state.

## Cause

`_cash_flow` (`cli/src/bea_engine/report.py:1708`) accumulates every leg into `flow_books` (`:1815`) and converts it with `cost_or_value(…, "at_cost")`. A posting without a cost stays in its units, so the priced expense leg becomes a -20.00 EUR row while the cash moved -22.00 USD. The conservation check (`:1877`) then finds USD and EUR unexplained and raises the refusal (`:1887`). `priced_exchange` (`cli/src/fava/modules/financial_statements.py:100`) is true for any price-weighted leg, so the message names the purchase as an exchange whatever account carried the price.

## Fix

In `_cash_flow`, read a non-cash leg whose weight comes from a price (price set, no cost, price currency different from the units currency) at its weight (`beancount.core.convert.get_weight`) when it goes into `flow_books`. Keep cost or units for every other leg, and keep units for the cash members' own movement. Then a transaction whose cash legs are unpriced conserves exactly, and the existing check still refuses only a period whose cash legs are priced. Narrow the refusal's example list to those transactions, so the message never calls a purchase an exchange. Update the w5/1068 sentence in `cli/docs/USAGE.md` (`:2281`) and ADR024's "No reconciliation refusal" bullet to say which transactions are refused.

Regression tests through real `bea report cash-flow` subprocesses:

- The repro's February and whole-year windows report the expected figures in text and `--json`.
- A priced income leg into a USD account reports an operating inflow at its weight.
- The w5/1068 fixtures (an exchange into a EUR cash account, with and without a fee, and the round trip) are still refused.
- The costed purchase, the card purchase and the plain transfer controls are unchanged.

Revert the fix and watch the new cases fail. Run `make check-all` in `cli/`. Owned by `cli`. No REST, GraphQL or MCP surface computes this report, so parity is not affected. The dashboard's Cash Flow page was not exercised.

## Blast radius

`_cash_flow` is the only caller of `priced_exchange` that refuses. The other caller, `_split_residual` (`cli/src/fava/modules/financial_statements.py:153`), only splits the balance sheet's and trial balance's residual into rounding and valuation, and must keep its current definition. The callers of `report cash-flow` in this repo are `skills/beancount-business-metrics/scripts/business_metrics.py:102` (one call per month), the report pack (`skills/beancount-report-pack/scripts`, `cash-flow.csv` and the trial-balance tie-out), and the `beancount-ask` cash-movement recipe. A period with only card-paid foreign purchases, costed investments, same-currency transfers or no priced legs reports as before (controls run).

Cash legs stay in units, so a transaction whose cash legs are unpriced conserves exactly, and the w5/1068 refusal, with its text unchanged, lists only transactions with a priced cash leg, so it never calls a purchase an exchange.
