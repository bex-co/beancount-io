# `bea report budget --account` drops a subaccount's spending when the filter leaves out the parent whose budget covers it, then says "No accounts match" and "Nothing budgeted or spent"

**Source:** routine cli-qa, 2026-11-19. **Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 30–45 minutes. **Owner:** cli. **A1:** an agent asked "how is dining doing against budget?" runs `bea report budget -a Expenses:Food:Dining`. It gets exit 0, "No accounts match Expenses:Food:Dining." and "Nothing budgeted or spent." for an account that spent 50 USD in the period. With a regex such as `-a 'Dining|Rent'`, Dining's money is dropped with no warning at all, so the budget report shows 1,000 USD of actuals where the income statement under the same filter shows 1,050 USD.

Found at `4a6a8c280` with the checkout's `uv run bea` 0.4.1, Python 3.12.14, macOS 26.5.1. The run used an isolated `BEA_CONFIG_DIR`/`XDG_CACHE_HOME` with `BEA_FILE`/`BEA_TOKEN` unset, stdin closed, stdout piped, and a synthetic ledger. Reproduced in fresh processes from a pristine copy. The ledger's hash was unchanged.

## Actual

- `-a Expenses:Food:Dining`: stderr says `No accounts match Expenses:Food:Dining.` and stdout shows the period with `Nothing budgeted or spent.` JSON has `account_filter_empty: true` and `periods[0].rows: []`.
- `-a Income:Job:Bonus`: the same, although the account earned 500.00 USD.
- `-a 'Dining|Rent'`: only `Expenses:Rent  1,000.00 USD  1,000.00 USD  0.00 USD`. Dining's 50.00 USD is missing from text and CSV, and nothing on stderr says so.
- Controls: the income statement under `-a 'Dining|Rent'` shows Dining 50.00 and Rent 1,000.00 (1,050.00 USD). BQL gives Dining 50.00, Rent 1000.00 and Bonus -500.00. `-a Food` and `-a Expenses:Food` keep the parent in scope and correctly show `Expenses:Food 300.00 / 150.00`, with Dining rolled in.

## Expected

When the filter selects an account whose only covering budget is on an ancestor outside the filter, the account's actual is still reported, as the income statement reports it. Its row has `budget: null` and `variance: null`, and it names the budget that covers it, so the reader knows the money counts against `Expenses:Food` and can widen the filter. For example, the JSON row is `status: "covered"` with `covered_by: "Expenses:Food"`, the CSV status is `covered`, and the text BUDGET cell reads `in Expenses:Food` instead of `no budget`. `account_filter_empty` is `false`, and no "No accounts match" note is printed. The Income case reads as earned (`Income:Job:Bonus` actual `500.00`). Results are unchanged when the covering budget is in scope: unfiltered, `-a Food` and `-a Expenses:Food` still roll Dining into the Food row with no separate Dining row.

## Root cause

`cli/src/bea_engine/report.py`, `_budget`:

- Line 409 skips a budgeted account the filter does not select, so `Expenses:Food`'s row is dropped (correct).
- Line 435 then skips any account under *any* budgeted account (`any(account == name or account.startswith(name + ":") for name in budgeted)`), even when that budget was skipped at line 409. `Expenses:Food:Dining`'s postings are collected into `per_account` (line 405) but reach no row.
- Line 454 then sets `account_filter_empty` from the empty rows, and the frontend prints `No accounts match …` (`cli/src/cli/commands/report.py:299`) and `Nothing budgeted or spent.` (`:971`).

This is a residual of w5/1121 (`0be12893a`), which made coverage directional (a budget covers its descendants) but did not limit it to budgets inside the filter.

## Fix

At line 435, count only budgets the filter selects (`in_scope(name)`) as covering. Emit an account that only an out-of-scope budget covers with the `covered` status and its `covered_by` ancestor (the nearest budgeted one). Render that status in `commands/report.py` (text) and `report_csv.py` (CSV). Document it in `cli/docs/USAGE.md` § Budgets, beside "Income and expense accounts that no budget covers are listed `unbudgeted`". Add regressions to `cli/tests/test_report_budget.py` for `-a Expenses:Food:Dining`, `-a 'Dining|Rent'` and the Income child, across JSON, text and CSV. Keep the unfiltered, `-a Food` and `-a Expenses:Food` controls, and the w5/1121 and w5/1127 tests. Run `make check-all` in `cli/`.

## Blast radius

The budget report is computed only in `bea_engine/report.py::_budget`. The backend, dashboard and mobile have no `unbudgeted` budget report (searched `backend-cluster/backend-v2/src`, `backend-cluster/ledger/src`, `dashboard/src` and `mobile/src`), so there is no hosted surface to keep in parity. Other reports scope through `account_predicate` per posting and already show Dining under the same filter.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` (including `done/` and `blocked/`) for `report budget`, `unbudgeted`, `Dining`, budget plus subtree/descendant/ancestor/filter, and `account_filter_empty`, and checked `git log -L` on the lines involved. w5/1121 (done) fixed the opposite direction: a child's budget hid its parent's direct postings. w5/1127 (done) fixed a budget-only match being reported as empty. w21/m7 (done) introduced the report. No open item covers a covered child whose budget the filter excludes. This is not deployment lag: HEAD has the behavior.

---

**Resolved 2026-11-19** — `bea report budget` now counts only budgets the account filter keeps as covering. An account that only an out-of-scope ancestor's budget covers is reported, not dropped: `-a Expenses:Food:Dining` lists `Expenses:Food:Dining  in Expenses:Food  50.00 USD  —`, with no "No accounts match" note, and `-a Income:Job:Bonus` lists 500.00 USD earned in `Income:Job`. `-a 'Dining|Rent'` keeps both rows, so actuals total 1,050.00 USD as the income statement does. JSON rows carry `status: "covered"`, null `budget` and `variance`, and `covered_by` naming the nearest budgeted ancestor (`null` on every other row), and `account_filter_empty` is `false`. CSV gains a `covered_by` column, which `--spreadsheet-safe` neutralizes. Unfiltered, `-a Food` and `-a Expenses:Food` still roll Dining into the Food row, and a kept ancestor budget still absorbs a child whose nearer budget is filtered out. `cli/docs/USAGE.md` § Budgets documents the status, and the beancount-ask budget rule and a bea-cli version note tell agents how to read it (skill suite 0.4.40). The new cases in `cli/tests/test_report_budget.py` (JSON, text and CSV for the Expenses and Income children, the regex filter, the in-scope controls and the nearest-ancestor case) fail with the fix reverted, beside the w5/1121 and w5/1127 tests. `make check-all` passes. Shipped in `7988e1401` (`cli`, `skills`).
