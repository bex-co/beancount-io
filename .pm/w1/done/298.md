# With `-t`, one earlier expense in a currency nobody holds anymore (a spent trip budget) brings back w1/296: foreign cash and `@`-bought shares again move their translation into `Equity:Opening-Balances`, and the adjustment reads 0.00

Found by `/qa-find-bugs-cli w1` on 2026-11-19 (HEAD `d1d681e2f`, `bea 0.4.1`, macOS 26.5.1, checkout `uv run bea`, stdin closed, isolated `BEA_CONFIG_DIR`/`XDG_CACHE_HOME`, synthetic ledgers). Severity: minor (totals agree; the equity split does not). A1: an agent or the `beancount-report-pack` skill that reads a period's balance sheet or trial balance gets an opening equity the ledger never had, and an adjustment that leaves out the period's exchange or market gain, with exit 0. The trigger is common: any earlier trip whose foreign cash was spent in full.

**Gap in [w1/296](./296.md)** (`6bf1f3a21`). That fix drops the period clamp's `<summarize>` plugs from the equity tree, but keeps *every* plug, for every commodity, as soon as one commodity of the earlier earnings is held by no account at the period start (the w5/790 exception). One spent trip is enough to switch the whole period back to the old behaviour, even when that commodity has a price and needs no plug.

**Repro** (synthetic `main.bean`; `bea check` exits 0). It is w1/296's EUR repro plus a January trip to Tokyo:

```beancount
option "operating_currency" "USD"
2026-01-01 open Assets:Wallet USD
2026-01-01 open Assets:Euro EUR
2026-01-01 open Assets:Yen JPY
2026-01-01 open Expenses:Travel
2026-01-01 open Equity:Opening-Balances USD
2026-01-02 * "Opening"
  Assets:Wallet  1000.00 USD
  Equity:Opening-Balances
2026-01-10 * "Exchange"
  Assets:Euro  100.00 EUR @ 1.10 USD
  Assets:Wallet  -110.00 USD
2026-01-10 price EUR 1.10 USD
2026-01-12 * "Buy yen"
  Assets:Yen  10000 JPY @ 0.0070 USD
  Assets:Wallet  -70.00 USD
2026-01-12 price JPY 0.0070 USD
2026-01-14 * "Tokyo hotel"
  Expenses:Travel  10000 JPY
  Assets:Yen
2026-02-15 price EUR 1.20 USD
2026-02-28 * "noop"
  Assets:Wallet  0.00 USD
  Assets:Wallet  0.00 USD
```

```sh

bea -f main.bean --json report balance-sheet -t 2026-02
bea -f main.bean report balance-sheet -t 2026-01
bea -f main.bean report trial-balance -t 2026-02
bea -f main.bean report register Equity -t 2026-02
```

**Actual** (all exit 0, as of 2026-02-28, net worth 940.00 USD in both):

| | `Opening-Balances` | `Earnings:Previous` | `valuation_adjustment` | `equity_total` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| no `-t` | `-1000.00` | — | `-10.0000` | `-940.0000` |
| `-t 2026-02` | `-1010.0000` | `70.0000` | `0` | `-940.0000` |
| control: no yen trip, `-t 2026-02` | `-1000.00` | — | `-10.0000` | `-1010.0000` |

- `-t 2026-01` closes January at `Opening-Balances -1,000.00 USD` with a 0.00 adjustment. February then opens at `-1,010.00`, a figure that is nowhere in the ledger, and the adjustment reads `0.00` for a month in which EUR rose from 1.10 to 1.20 USD.
- `report trial-balance -t 2026-02` (and `bea balance -t 2026-02`) shows the same `Opening-Balances -1,010.00 USD` and no adjustment line.
- `report register Equity -t 2026-02` says `Opening-Balances -1,000.00 USD` and `Earnings:Previous 10,000 JPY` (70.00 USD). So two reports for the same period disagree.
- **`@`-share variant:** replace the exchange with `Assets:Broker 2 AAPL @ 50.00 USD` (open `Assets:Broker AAPL`) and the EUR prices with `AAPL 50.00` / `60.00 USD`. `-t 2026-02` then gives `Opening-Balances -1020.0000` and adjustment `0`, where no `-t` gives `-1000.00` / `-20.0000`.

**Expected.** The w1/296 promise holds whatever else the ledger did before the period. The JPY earnings have a price at the as-of date, so they value without a plug. With `-t 2026-02`, the sheet and the trial balance should read `Opening-Balances -1,000.00 USD`, `Earnings:Previous 70.00 USD` and a `-10.00 USD` adjustment (`-20.00` for the shares), as the register does. Then February opens where January closed.

**Root cause.** `_period_equity_view` (`cli/src/fava/modules/financial_statements.py:35`) computes one `keep_plugs` flag for the whole ledger (`:68`). It is true when any commodity summed on the previous-earnings and previous-balances legs has a non-zero balance and is held by no other account (here JPY: `Earnings:Previous` holds 10,000 JPY and `Assets:Yen` is empty). When it is true, `:74` keeps every `<summarize>` plug, including `Assets:Euro`'s `-100 EUR` plug on `Opening-Balances`. That plug is then valued at the closing rate, which is the w1/296 defect. Prices are not consulted, although w5/790's reason for keeping the plugs only applies to a commodity the report cannot value.

**Fix** (cli only). Keep the plugs only when an unheld earnings commodity cannot be valued in the report's conversion at the as-of date: no price, as in w5/790. A commodity with a price needs no netting, so its plugs go, as they do in the control. Callers to recheck: `trial_balance` (`:183`) and `balance_sheet` (`:215`); both take `_period_equity_view`. Out of scope: the unpriced mixed case (an unpriced spent currency beside foreign cash) cannot recover the trip's USD cost from prices. Leave it as today unless a cleaner per-commodity split proves right on both repros.

**Regression.** Extend `cli/tests/test_report_period_opening_plugs.py` with the repro and the `@`-share variant: `report balance-sheet` and `report trial-balance` give `Opening-Balances -1000.00`, `Earnings:Previous 70.00` and adjustment `-10` / `-20` with `-t 2026-02`, in text, JSON and CSV. Each assertion must fail with the fix reverted. The existing w5/790 and GIFT cases and the `units` trees stay byte-identical.

**Blast radius.** `bea report balance-sheet`, `bea report trial-balance` and `bea balance` (no terms, or terms matching equity), under any `-t` that starts after foreign cash or `@`-priced shares were acquired *and* after earlier earnings in a commodity that is now fully spent. `report register` already drops the plugs unconditionally (w1/279) and is correct. `overview` shows no equity.

**Dedupe.** I searched `.pm/**` for `Opening-Balances` with `plug`, `summar` and `translation`. [w1/296](./296.md) (done) records the exception as a deliberate deviation, but only for w5/790's case: nothing else foreign was held, and there was no price. [w5/790](../../w5/done/790.md) (done) is the no-price case the exception exists for. [w1/279](./279.md) covers the register. None of them covers a priced spent currency beside foreign cash or `@` shares. Estimate: 30–45 minutes.

---

**Resolved 2026-11-19** — the period clamp's `<summarize>` plugs now stay only when earlier earnings hold a commodity no account held at the period start *and* the report cannot value it at the as-of date. A spent currency with a price (the Tokyo trip's yen) values as previous earnings, so `-t 2026-02` reads `Opening-Balances -1,000.00 USD`, `Earnings:Previous 70.00 USD` and a `-10.00 USD` adjustment (`-20.00` for the `@`-share variant) on the balance sheet and trial balance, in text, JSON and CSV, as the register does. The report currency itself counts as valued. The unpriced [w5/790](../../w5/done/790.md) ledger, the GIFT ledger, an unpriced trip beside foreign cash, and every `units` and `at_cost` tree are byte-identical. One deliberate change: w5/790's made-up-price test now pins that a price changes no total while the equity split follows the unfiltered report (`Opening-Balances -1,000.00 EUR`, adjustment `42.00`), where it used to pin the w1/296 split. Covered by `cli/tests/test_report_period_opening_plugs.py` and `cli/tests/test_report_cut_equity_netting.py`; shipped in `d88898b24` (`cli`).
