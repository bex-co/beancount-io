# Holdings and capital-gains text totals may miss their printed rows by a cent

Follow-up from w1/358 (a612e4a25, `cli`). That fix made the text trial balance, income statement, balance sheet and cash-flow report foot each derived line from the rounded figures printed above it, so a half-cent tie no longer leaves a total a cent away from the sum of its own lines (JSON and CSV keep exact figures).

The worker noted that `bea report holdings` and `bea report capital-gains` also print totals that add up rows, and can miss by a cent the same way. Not reproduced yet.
