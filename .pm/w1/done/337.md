# `bea report register` on an income or expenses account no longer ends at the trial-balance total: two 100 EUR trips at 1.00 and 2.00 USD run to 400.00 USD, or to a closing row "Valued at the as-of date" of 600.00 USD, where the trial balance, `bea balance` and the income statement say 300.00 USD (regression from w1/m49)

**Bug · cli + hosted · minor · ~1h · A1.** From routine cli-qa, 2026-11-20. [w1/m49](./done/m49/README.md) made every income and expenses posting worth its value on its own date, in the income statement, the overview, the balance sheet's earnings, the trial balance and `bea balance`. The register was not part of it, and it still values the whole running inventory at each row's date, then appends a closing row at the as-of rate. `cli/docs/USAGE.md` promises that the register's "last row is the trial-balance total" ([w22/m3/t002](../w22/done/m3/done/t002.md), kept by [w1/252](./done/252.md)). Before m49, both figures used the as-of rate and matched. Now, for any income or expenses account under a currency conversion (the default on a one-operating-currency ledger) or `at_value`, the register ends at a different figure than every statement. An agent or a person auditing one expense account sees its total change between reports.

Environment: main `947a3b446`, `bea 0.5.0` from the checkout (`uv run bea`), Python 3.12.14, macOS 26.5.1. Isolated `BEA_CONFIG_DIR`/`XDG_CACHE_HOME`, `BEA_NO_UPDATE_NOTIFIER=1`, `BEA_FILE`/`BEA_TOKEN` unset, stdin closed, no TTY, cwd a scratch directory. Each case was rerun in fresh processes; the ledger file's hash was unchanged by every read.

## Repro

m49's own repro ledger:

```sh
cat > main.bean <<'EOF'
option "operating_currency" "USD"
2026-01-01 open Assets:Euro EUR
2026-01-01 open Expenses:Travel EUR
2026-01-01 open Equity:Opening EUR
2026-01-01 price EUR 1.00 USD
2026-01-02 * "Opening"
  Assets:Euro  1000.00 EUR
  Equity:Opening
2026-01-15 * "Trip one"
  Expenses:Travel  100.00 EUR
  Assets:Euro
2026-02-01 price EUR 2.00 USD
2026-02-15 * "Trip two"
  Expenses:Travel  100.00 EUR
  Assets:Euro
EOF
bea --file main.bean report register Expenses:Travel
# 2026-01-15  Trip one  100.00 EUR  100.00 USD
# 2026-02-15  Trip two  100.00 EUR  400.00 USD        <- last row
bea --file main.bean report trial-balance        # Expenses / Travel  300.00 USD, valuation line 100.00 USD
bea --file main.bean balance Travel              # Travel 300.00 USD
bea --file main.bean report income-statement     # Travel 300.00 USD, Net Profit: -300.00 USD
bea --file main.bean query "SELECT sum(convert(position,'USD',date)) AS by_day, sum(convert(position,'USD')) AS at_latest WHERE account = 'Expenses:Travel'"
# by_day 300 USD, at_latest 400 USD
```

All exit 0. `--json` and `--format csv` carry the same `400.0000` balance on the last row.

With a later quote, the closing row adds a third figure. Append these lines:

```beancount
2026-03-01 price EUR 3.00 USD
2026-03-10 * "Refund"
  Assets:Euro  50.00 EUR
  Equity:Opening
```

Now the register reads 100.00, then 400.00, then a closing row `2026-03-10  Valued at the as-of date  600.00 USD`, while the trial balance still says Travel 300.00 USD. With `-t "2026-02 - 2026-03"`, the window holds one posting (Trip two, 200.00 USD at 2.00). The register reads `200.00 USD` and then a closing row of `300.00 USD`, while `report trial-balance -t "2026-02 - 2026-03"` reads Travel 200.00 USD.

If the price starts only on 2026-02-01 (so Trip one has no quote), both reports need `--allow-errors`. The trial balance then reads `100.00 EUR  200.00 USD`, but the register's last row reads `400.00 USD`. The January euros are revalued at February's rate.

## Expected

The register of an account under the ledger's income or expenses root (`name_income`, `name_expenses`) follows m49's rule. Each posting is valued at the price on its own date. The running balance is the sum of those values, and a posting with no quote on its day stays in its own commodity, with its date in `missing_price_dates`. A flow is never revalued, so such a register needs no closing row. On the repro, the rows read 100.00 then 300.00 USD. With the March lines appended, there is no closing row and the last row reads 300.00. Over the February–March window, the register reads 200.00 with no closing row. Each of these equals the trial balance, as USAGE.md promises. Asset, liability and equity registers keep today's behaviour: each row valued at its own date, plus the closing row. `units` and `at_cost` do not change. The basis is m49's definition of done ("a period's income and expenses have one value"), and the w22/m3/t002 acceptance that "the last running balance equals the account's trial-balance total".

## Cause

`cli/src/bea_engine/report.py` `_register` (around lines 1215–1299): `running.add_position(posting)` collects units, and `_converted(running, conversion, prices, when)` values the whole inventory at each row's date. Then `_converted(running, …, filtered.end_date)` adds the closing row whenever that value differs. m49 changed only `cli/src/fava/modules/chart.py`, where `ChartModule.hierarchy` uses `_flow_inventories`/`_valued_days` when the account sits under `_flow_roots(filtered)`, plus the report helpers that read those trees. `git log -L` on `_register` shows that m49 never touched it.

## Fix

- **`bea`:** in `_register`, when `root` is under the ledger's income or expenses root and the conversion reads prices, accumulate each posting's own-date value instead. Use `cost_or_value(<posting inventory>, conversion, prices, row date)`, added into a `units` inventory, as `_valued_days` does. Read that inventory back as units, and skip the closing row. Reuse `_flow_roots`, `_under_roots` and `_reads_prices` from `fava/modules/chart.py`; do not write a third copy. Add a sentence to USAGE.md's register paragraph (around line 2132) saying that an income or expenses register follows the statements' flow rule. Add regressions in `cli/tests/test_report_register.py` that fail with the fix reverted: the repro's 100/300 rows equal the trial balance, no closing row after a later quote, the `-t` window, a row with no quote on its day, and an `Assets` register that keeps its closing row.
- **Hosted (same change, for REST/GraphQL/MCP parity):** `getLedgerRegister` (REST, GraphQL and the MCP `ledgerRegister` resource; `backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/features/ledger/utils/register.ts`) builds its rows from the ledger service's account journal. `accountJournalItems` in `backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/journal-running-balance.ts` (around lines 494–537) values the whole running `LotInventory` at each entry's date, so it has the same split against the hosted trial balance that m49 (`b0c02b26c`) moved to by-day values. Apply the same rule there for flow accounts, using the `flowRoots`/`isFlowAccount` that m49 introduced. A row's `balance` is then the sum of its changes, each valued on its own date. That also aligns the dashboard's account journal with the account report's interval totals, which m49 already values by day.

## Blast radius and controls

- `_register` is the only `bea` report that builds a running balance. Working controls on the same ledgers: `report trial-balance`, `bea balance`, `report income-statement` (trees and `-i monthly|weekly|yearly` rows), the overview and the balance sheet's current earnings all read 300.00. `-x units` and `-x EUR` registers read 100.00 then 200.00 EUR and match the trial balance in EUR. An `Assets:Euro` register still ends at the trial-balance total (2,550.00 USD in the extended ledger).
- Hosted: the cause was traced in code only. No hosted ledger was written in this run, and m49's hosted commits landed today, so production may not run them yet.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` (including `done/` and `blocked/`) for `register`, `closing row`, `Valued at the as-of`, `trial-balance total` and `own date`, crossed with income or expenses. The only hits are completed history: w1/252 (rows at their own date), w1/260, w1/279, w1/289, w5/1056, w22/m3/t002 and w1/m49, whose scope list leaves out the register. There is no open item. This is a new regression, introduced when `6f37b1c36` moved the trial balance to by-day values.

**Resolved 2026-11-20** — Reproduced at `a2d7d6533`: the register read 100.00 then 400.00 USD, a closing row of 600.00 after the 3.00 quote, and 200.00 then a 300.00 closing row over `-t "2026-02 - 2026-03"`, against a trial balance of 300.00 and 200.00. `_register` in `cli/src/bea_engine/report.py` now treats a register rooted under the ledger's income or expenses root as a flow when the conversion reads prices: each posting's own-date `cost_or_value` is added into a units inventory, there is no closing row, and only the posting's own date is reported missing when it has no quote. It reuses `_flow_roots`, `_under_roots` and `_reads_prices` from `fava/modules/chart.py`. On the repro the rows read 100.00 then 300.00 with or without the March lines, the window reads 200.00, and the no-January-quote case reads `100.00 EUR` then `100.00 EUR 200.00 USD` with `missing_price_dates` naming 2026-01-15 only, each equal to the trial balance; the `Assets:Euro` register still ends at its closing row, and `units`/`at_cost` read 100.00 then 200.00 EUR. `cli/docs/USAGE.md`'s register paragraph states the flow rule. `cli/tests/test_report_register.py` covers the repro (account and parent), the window, the no-quote day, units and at_cost, and an asset register's closing row; the first three fail with the fix reverted.
