# With `-t`, foreign cash or `@`-bought shares held before the period move their translation gain into `Equity:Opening-Balances`, so the balance sheet and trial balance report a 0.00 adjustment and an opening equity the ledger never had

Found by `/qa-find-bugs-cli w1` on 2026-11-19 (HEAD `f171f1afd`, `bea 0.4.1`, macOS 26.5.1, Python 3.12.14, checkout `uv run bea`, stdin closed, isolated `BEA_CONFIG_DIR`/`XDG_CACHE_HOME`, synthetic ledgers). Severity: minor (totals agree; the equity split does not). A1: an agent or the `beancount-report-pack` skill that reads a period's balance sheet or trial balance gets an opening equity that does not match the ledger or the previous period's close, and a "Valuation/translation adjustment" that leaves out the period's exchange gain, with exit 0.

**Repro** (synthetic `main.bean`; `bea check` exits 0):

```beancount
option "operating_currency" "USD"
2026-01-01 open Assets:Wallet USD
2026-01-01 open Assets:Euro EUR
2026-01-01 open Equity:Opening-Balances USD
2026-01-02 * "Opening"
  Assets:Wallet  1000.00 USD
  Equity:Opening-Balances
2026-01-10 * "Exchange"
  Assets:Euro  100.00 EUR @ 1.10 USD
  Assets:Wallet  -110.00 USD
2026-01-10 price EUR 1.10 USD
2026-02-15 price EUR 1.20 USD
2026-02-28 * "noop"
  Assets:Wallet  0.00 USD
  Assets:Wallet  0.00 USD
```

```sh
bea -f main.bean --json report balance-sheet              # as of 2026-02-28
bea -f main.bean --json report balance-sheet -t 2026-02   # as of 2026-02-28
bea -f main.bean report balance-sheet -t 2026-01
bea -f main.bean report trial-balance -t 2026-02
bea -f main.bean balance Opening -t 2026-02 -x units
bea -f main.bean query "SELECT account, sum(position) WHERE account ~ 'Opening' GROUP BY account"
```

**Actual** (all exit 0, same as-of date 2026-02-28, net worth 1,010.00 USD in both):

| | Equity `Opening-Balances` | `valuation_adjustment` | `equity_total` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| no `-t` | `-1000.00` | `-10.0000` | `-1010.0000` |
| `-t 2026-02` | `-1010.0000` | `0` | `-1010.0000` |

- `-t 2026-01` closes January with Equity `-1,000.00 USD` and net worth `1,000.00 USD`, but February's sheet opens `Opening-Balances` at `-1,010.00 USD`. That figure appears nowhere in the ledger: BQL says `Equity:Opening-Balances -1000.00 USD`. The translation adjustment reads `0.00` for a month in which EUR went from 1.10 to 1.20 USD.
- `report trial-balance -t 2026-02` and `bea balance Opening -t 2026-02` show the same `-1,010.00 USD`. Under `-x units`, the USD-only account holds `-100.00 EUR -890.00 USD`.
- The same thing happens with shares bought at a price instead of a cost: replace the exchange with `Assets:Broker 2 AAPL @ 50.00 USD` and the prices with `AAPL 50.00` / `60.00 USD`. Then `-t 2026-02` gives Equity `-1020.00` and adjustment `0`, against `-1000.00` / `-20.00` without `-t`.
- **Control:** the same purchase at cost (`2 AAPL {50.00 USD}`) gives Equity `-1000.00` and adjustment `-20.00` both with and without `-t`.

**Expected.** One equity split per as-of date, whatever the period start, as USAGE (Reports) promises: "A period therefore splits equity the same way with and without `--time`". With `-t 2026-02`, `Opening-Balances` should read `-1,000.00 USD` and the adjustment `-10.00 USD`, matching the unfiltered report. February's opening equity should then equal January's closing equity. Only current-period versus previous earnings may differ with the period.

**Root cause.** `TimeFilter.apply` (`cli/src/fava/core/filters.py:412`) runs Beancount's `clamp_opt`. Its `summarize` step writes one `<summarize>` transaction (flag `S`) per pre-period position: the account's own leg, then a plug on `Equity:Opening-Balances` (`account_previous_balances`) at `convert.get_cost(position)`. A lot held at cost plugs in USD. Uncosted foreign cash, or shares bought with `@`, plug in their own units (`-100 EUR`). `FinancialStatementsModule.balance_sheet` (`cli/src/fava/modules/financial_statements.py:185`) and `trial_balance` (`:153`) value the equity tree at the as-of price. So that plug is translated at the closing rate inside `Opening-Balances`, and the residual `valuation_adjustment` never sees it. `_without_clamp_conversions` (`:33`) already removes the clamp's `C` booking for the same reason (w5/789, w1/287), but not these `S` plugs.

**Fix** (cli only). In the balance sheet's equity tree, and in the trial balance's at every valuation but `units`, leave out the clamp's `<summarize>` plugs, the odd legs. Keep each account's own opening leg, as `bea_engine/report.py:1124-1135` already does for the register (w1/279). The residual `valuation_adjustment` then carries the translation, and `Opening-Balances` shows its real balance. A pure single-currency ledger is unchanged: its plugs and the `Opening-Balances` legs net to the same figure. The phantom `Opening-Balances 0.00` row of w3/288 can no longer arise. `units` keeps today's trees (w5/501).

**Regression.** For the repro and the `@`-share variant, `report balance-sheet` and `report trial-balance` give the same equity tree, `valuation_adjustment` and `equity_total` (text, JSON and CSV) with and without `-t 2026-02`. The `{cost}` control and a USD-only ledger are byte-identical to today. Each assertion fails with the fix reverted. The report-pack tie-out still passes on the repro with `--time 2026-02`.

**Blast radius.** `bea report balance-sheet`, `bea report trial-balance`, and `bea balance` (with no terms, or with terms matching equity), under any `-t` that starts after a foreign-currency or `@`-priced balance was acquired. Text, `--json` and `--format csv` are all affected. `report overview` shows no equity and is unaffected. Searched callers of `_without_clamp_conversions` and `chart.hierarchy(..., name_equity, ...)`: only these two methods. Not checked: the hosted balance sheet and trial balance (backend-v2/ledger service), which do their own period cut.

**Dedupe.** Searched `.pm/**` for `summar` with `Opening-Balances`, `translation`, `valuation_adjustment` and `clamp`. [w5/789](../../w5/done/789.md) folded the clamp's `Conversions` booking into the adjustment. [w1/287](./287.md) gave the trial balance the same line. [w3/288](../../w3/done/288.md) removed a phantom zero `Opening-Balances`. [w1/279](./279.md) dropped the `<summarize>` plugs from the register only. None of them looked at a pre-period uncosted balance. Estimate: 45–60 minutes.

---

**Resolved 2026-11-19** — the balance sheet's equity tree, and the trial balance's at every valuation but `units`, leave out the period clamp's `<summarize>` plugs. Each account keeps its own opening leg, as the register does, so `-t 2026-02` reads `Opening-Balances -1,000.00 USD` with a `-10.00 USD` adjustment (`-20.00` for the `@`-share variant), the same as without `-t` in text, JSON and CSV, and `bea balance Opening -t 2026-02` agrees. The `{cost}` control, a USD-only ledger, and the [w5/790](../../w5/done/790.md) ledger are byte-identical to before. The report-pack tie-out reconciles on the repro. One deviation from the proposed fix: when earlier earnings hold a commodity that no account held at the period start (w5/790's foreign cash spent on a trip), the plugs stay, because only they net that commodity without a price. Covered by `cli/tests/test_report_period_opening_plugs.py`; shipped in `6bf1f3a21` (`cli`).
