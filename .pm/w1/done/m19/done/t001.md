## Objective

Turn ADR014's chosen independent-program design into an evidence-based release checklist.

## Context

- ADR014 requires one customer installation, separate frontend and engine environments, and no direct or indirect engine imports in the frontend.
- The existing PyPI/Homebrew artifacts and the vendored Fava notices are the audit baseline; MIT source licensing alone is not a compliance verdict.

## Steps

1. Map actual frontend, engine, helper, AI, and optional-package licenses and distribution paths. Distinguish upstream downloads from artifacts we redistribute.
2. Record required license texts, copyright notices, metadata, corresponding-source delivery, and the evidence needed for any combined-work assessment.
3. Identify unresolved conditions explicitly. Keep the engine dependency compatibility resolution in t013; use qualified license review where public license evidence is insufficient.
