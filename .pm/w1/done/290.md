# Hosted holdings value a converted lot without the direct-quote rule `bea` uses, so the same ledger reports different totals

Follow-up from [288](./done/288.md) (shipped in `dc5a1e5a6`, 2026-11-18). A1: REST, GraphQL and MCP holdings should agree with `bea report holdings` on the same ledger.

On 288's repro ledger, `bea report holdings` reports the position at 2,200 USD and complete, while hosted holdings (backend-v2 `holdings.ts`, the same answer on all three surfaces) report 2,180 USD and partial. The gap is the w5/1033 direct-quote valuation (`8fe77e17c`): the CLI values a lot from a direct price quote in the target currency before falling back to conversion through its cost currency, and the hosted composer was never given that rule.

**Do:** port the direct-quote valuation to the hosted holdings composer, so that 288's repro gives 2,200 USD and complete on REST, GraphQL and MCP. Add a backend-v2 regression test that fails without the port, and keep the parity gate and `yarn test` green in `backend-cluster/backend-v2`.

---

**Resolved 2026-11-18** — the hosted holdings composer now values a lot at its commodity's own quote in the conversion currency when the ledger has one, the rule `bea report holdings` follows (w5/1033, w5/1117): `valueHoldingRows` also reads that quote (`direct_price`), and `valueLot` in `backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/features/ledger/utils/holdings.ts` takes the market value and price from it while the basis converts through the cost currency. Without that rate the basis stays in its cost currency beside the market value, with the rate named missing while the group has a known basis. On 288's repro, REST, GraphQL and MCP (all served by `LedgerFinanceService.getHoldings`) now answer one `20 VT` holding at `104 USD` average, `2,080 USD` basis and `2,200 USD` market value, `valuation: complete`, as `bea` does. Regression: the 288 repro in `holdings.test.ts` (both groupings) and `ledger-finance-service.test.ts`, failing with the fix reverted (2,180 USD), plus the CLI's direct-quote cases ported to the composer. `backend-v2` `yarn typecheck`, `yarn lint:deadcode`, `yarn format:check`, `yarn test` and the v1 OpenAPI drift check pass.
