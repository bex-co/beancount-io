# A `bea query` sum that cancels a quotient leaves a 1e-26 residue such as `-0.00000000000000000000000001 USD`

**Source:** follow-up from [325](./done/325.md) (shipped in `d9667954c`, 2026-11-19). The 325 worker found it after its fix and wrote it into the resolution; nobody has filed it until now. **Owner:** cli. **A1:** an agent that runs `SELECT sum(weight)` over a ledger holding a `{{…}}` total cost or an `@@` total price expects zero when the postings balance. It gets `-0.00000000000000000000000001 USD`, which reads as a real non-zero amount.

**Cause, to confirm in triage:** each 28-digit quotient from a total cost or total price is summed at full precision, so the rounding residue survives a sum that should cancel exactly. The 325 fix changed only how many digits are displayed, not the value that is summed.

**Done when:** a text-output sum that cancels to within the currency's display tolerance prints as zero at the currency's precision. JSON and CSV still give the exact value unless triage shows they should not. Add a regression test that fails without the fix.

---

**Resolved 2026-11-19** — Confirmed in triage: the 28-digit quotient is summed at full precision. Once a sum cancels its leading digits, the coefficient is shorter than the context's 28 digits, so the w1/325 test (`report._cost_digits`) no longer saw it as noise. Its 26 digits also spread to every other USD value in the table (`SELECT sum(weight), sum(position)` printed `-100.00000000000000000000000000 USD`). The same residue rides on real amounts too: with a `4.50 USD` coffee on the purchase day, `sum(weight) WHERE account ~ '^Assets' GROUP BY date` read `-4.50000000000000000000000001 USD`. In a text table, a summed inventory position's digits after a run of ten zeros or nines now count for nothing (`bea_engine.query._before_residue`). A 28-digit quotient on any amount below a trillion ends at least fourteen places past the cent. A text-table amount that rounds to zero prints without a sign. On the note's ledger, `SELECT sum(weight)` prints `0.00 USD`, also on stdin. The column beside it prints `3 HOOL { 33.33 USD}  -100.00 USD`, and the coffee day prints `-4.50 USD`. Under `-m` the zero sum is an empty cell, as an exact cancellation already is. Lone amounts are never sums and keep their digits: a `0.000000000012 USD` price shows twelve decimals and a `0.0042 USD` price four. A summed satoshi's `0.0006123456789012 USD` and an eight-decimal `convert()` keep theirs too. `--json` and `--format csv` keep the exact value (`-0.00000000000000000000000001`), and are unchanged because triage found nothing that says they should round. The rule is documented in `cli/src/bea_engine/query.py`'s module docstring and in `cli/docs/USAGE.md` (query precision paragraph). Tests: 11 new cases in `cli/tests/test_query_result_precision.py`. With the fix reverted, 6 fail. The sign test also fails without the renderer swap, and the price control fails if the run rule applies to lone amounts. `make check-all` passes. No REST/GraphQL/MCP change: the hosted query surfaces return structured rows, not this text table. Shipped in `d89215fe8` (`cli`).
