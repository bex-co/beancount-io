## Objective

When a file's combined rewrite fails, the rows whose spans hold the error's located lines are rejected from that one load, the rest are confirmed with one more load, and only what cannot be attributed is searched by bisection, as `add transactions --partial` does since w5/720.

## Context

- `cli/src/bea_engine/ledger/updating.py`: `_validate_plans`, `_validate_text`.
- `cli/src/bea_engine/ledger/write.py`: `CandidateRejected.locations`, `details_at`, `preexisting_only`.
- `cli/src/bea_engine/ledger/adding.py`: `_named_rows`, `_refused_in_order`, `_partitioned_rows`.

## Steps

1. Map each located error of the combined failure to the row whose span holds it in the rewritten file.
2. Reject those rows with their own details; validate the rest once.
3. When errors cannot be placed, or the rest still fail, find the guilty rows by bisection in input order; keep the "valid alone, but … fail together" outcome for rows that only fail in combination.

## Files

- `cli/src/bea_engine/ledger/updating.py`

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] With one bad row among N, the batch spends a bounded number of `validate_candidate` calls (around log N), not N.
- [x] Each rejected row's `reason` and `details` equal the pre-change answer.

## Out of scope

- `add transactions`' own attribution.

## Done — 2026-11-23

`_judge_file` replaces the trial of every row alone. The rows whose spans hold a line the combined refusal locates (`CandidateRejected.locations`, renumbered past dropped continuation lines) are tried alone, so their `reason` and `details` are the ones a trial alone gives, and the rest are retried together. An error no row's lines hold is searched by halving the rows in order, with `add --partial`'s spending cap (w5/720). When a row passes alone but breaks the rows before it, every row is tried alone, as before, so "valid alone, but … fail together" stays true. Trials are memoized and kept without their tracebacks. One bad row among 64 costs 3 validations (the batch, the row, the rest), 4 with `--apply`; an assertion one row breaks costs at most 2·log2 N + 3.

Deviation: rows that load together are written together, as they are in a batch without the refused rows, even one that fails alone. Two rows moving an amount between two asserted accounts each fail alone and pass together; beside a third, mistyped row they were rejected before and are written now (`test_rows_that_load_together_are_written_together_beside_a_rejected_row`).
