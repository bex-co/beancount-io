## Objective

A row's preview diff stops copying and diffing the whole file: it is built from the lines around the row's edits, with the hunk header offset, and equals the diff the whole file gives.

## Context

- `cli/src/bea_engine/ledger/updating.py`: `_row_payload`, `_file_diff`; `cli/src/bea_engine/ledger/text.py`: `unified_file_diff`, `physical_lines`.
- The whole-file diff keeps a byte-order mark on line 1 and marks a last line without a terminator with `\ No newline at end of file`.

## Steps

1. Diff a window of the edited lines plus enough context on each side, then shift the hunk headers to the file's line numbers.
2. Cover the byte-order-mark, no-final-newline, first-line, last-line and removed-continuation-line cases.

## Files

- `cli/src/bea_engine/ledger/updating.py`, possibly `cli/src/bea_engine/ledger/text.py`

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] For every case above, the row's diff equals `unified_file_diff` over the whole file.

## Out of scope

- The combined per-file diff, which is computed once per file.

## Done — 2026-11-23

`FileDiffer` (`cli/src/bea_engine/ledger/text.py`) diffs one row's edits from a window around them. Two rules keep it equal to `difflib` over the whole file: a line that fills more than 1% of a 200-line-or-longer file can start no match (judged on the whole file's counts, not the window's), and each side of the window reaches a line found once in the file before and after the edit, or the file's edge. A plain three-line window differed on a line every entry repeats between two edited lines, and after the last entry's header. The answers' per-row diffs match the whole-file diff on the byte-order-mark, no-final-newline, first-line, last-line and removed-continuation-line cases, and on 24,000 generated row edits.

Deviation: where entries repeat each other line for line (no metadata, the same payees and amounts), `difflib` over the whole file can pair a line with its copy in another entry and report dozens of unchanged lines around a one-line edit; about 0.4% of the generated edits on such files did. The window reports the edit's own lines there, and those diffs apply (tested). Edits to many entries make that pairing likely, so the batch's combined diff stays one whole-file `difflib` per file.
