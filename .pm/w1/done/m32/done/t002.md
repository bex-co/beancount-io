## Objective

Extend the mobile-only sliding grant to every DCR and CIMD client, so a connection used at least every 45 days keeps working, but never past one year from the original authorization.

## Context

- backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/features/oauth/api/oidc-route.ts — the re-save is mobile-only today (the mobile check inside the refresh handler).

## Steps

1. Generalize the re-save to non-static clients.
2. Clamp the extended expiry to authorization time + one year.

## Files

- backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/features/oauth/api/oidc-route.ts

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A refresh by a non-static client extends its grant, never beyond the ceiling.
- [x] Mobile behaves exactly as before.

## Out of scope

- Revocation UI.

## Result

2026-10-06. The token-endpoint middleware that re-saves the grant after a successful refresh now covers every third-party client as well as mobile; it compares against each client's own refresh-token lifetime and skips once nothing is left of the yearly ceiling. `oauthLifetimes().grant(clientId, issuedAt, now)` caps a third-party grant at `issuedAt + 365 days` (oidc-provider keeps a grant's `iat` across re-saves), and `ttl.Grant` passes `grant.iat`. Tests: unit tests for the cap arithmetic; integration tests that a refresh slides the grant past 45 days, that a slide 360 days in stops at the ceiling, and that a grant past the ceiling is left as it was. Mobile tests unchanged and passing. Mutating either the client condition or the cap turns the matching tests red. backend-v2 301/301 suites, 5051 tests.
