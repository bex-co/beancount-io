# Make Awesome Plain Text Accounting a trustworthy decision tool

The live [`/awesome-plain-text-accounting`](https://beancount.io/awesome-plain-text-accounting)
page is a strong visual catalog but does not yet help newcomers choose a plain-text
accounting stack. A 2026-08-29 review found nine broken destinations, no visible
review date or inclusion methodology, ambiguous editorial badges, a roughly
10,800 px mobile journey without directory navigation or filtering, and a
hosted-product CTA before the visitor has enough information to decide.

The intended outcome is an A3 — Community & distribution milestone that:

- repairs or removes dead destinations and adds automated link validation;
- publishes a last-reviewed date, selection criteria, and curator disclosure;
- leads with a Beancount / hledger / Ledger chooser and comparison table;
- reorganizes the catalog as engine → editor → importer → reporting → mobile;
- adds mobile-friendly section navigation plus catalog search/filtering;
- moves the hosted-product CTA after the neutral decision layer;
- exposes a direct public contribution path and measurable qualified actions.

Promoted to `w1/m8` on 2026-08-29 after establishing a public,
repository-owned implementation in `dashboard`.
