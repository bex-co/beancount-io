## Objective

Every upgrade or switch-to-yearly button opens a sheet. It shows from → to, a "starts today" line, the amount due today with its line items, the next charge and the card. It also shows a "What you get" block listing the limits and plan features as the pricing table does. The confirm button carries the amount. Confirming calls `changePlan`. `REQUIRES_ACTION` is completed without leaving the page, and failures say that nothing changed.

## Context

- Contracts from t001 and t002; wireframe and copy in PRFAQ005 question 15.
- Today `subscription-section.tsx:137` and `:158` pass `stripeConfig[tierKey].monthly` for every checkout and upgrade. The `clientSecret` branch only shows a toast.
- Bank authentication options:
  - Stripe.js — adding `@stripe/stripe-js` needs the user's approval (root `AGENTS.md`); loading Stripe's script directly needs a dashboard CSP check.
  - Without Stripe.js — open the invoice's hosted payment page.
- Plan features (automatic backups, automation & integrations, OIDC SSO, multi-entity consolidation) are provided on request to the plans the pricing table lists them under. Mirror that table here; no backend check exists or is added (PRFAQ005 question 21 item 6).

## Steps

1. Build the sheet from `previewPlanChange`, and re-preview when the cycle toggle changes.
2. Send a customer without a subscription to Checkout on the chosen cycle's price, and remove the hard-coded monthly price.
3. Confirm with `changePlan` using the preview's proration time. On `REQUIRES_ACTION`, run the in-place confirmation or fall back to the hosted invoice page. On success, refetch status and limits.
4. Render the failure states: "Your bank didn't approve the payment. Nothing changed.", with **Try again** and **Use another card**. The second opens the pinned portal.

## Implementation notes

- Every Upgrade, Switch to yearly and "Pay yearly" button opens `pages/general/plan-change-sheet.tsx`, and so does a free user's choice. The sheet runs `previewPlanChange` for the chosen price and shows:
  - from → to and "starts today";
  - due today, with the credit for unused time;
  - the next charge with its date;
  - the card;
  - "What you get", from the target tier's larger quotas plus the pricing table's plan features (`common/lib/subscription/plan-features.ts`).
- The confirm button carries the amount. It calls `changePlan` with the preview's proration time, and a free user's choice goes to Checkout.
- `REQUIRES_ACTION` opens **Stripe's hosted invoice page** (`hostedInvoiceUrl`) to approve the payment, with a refresh afterwards. `FAILED` says nothing changed and offers Try again and Use another card (the portal). Refusals explain themselves. No outcome is a toast alone.
- **Deviation:** bank approval does not complete *inside* the sheet. That needs Stripe.js; adding `@stripe/stripe-js`, or loading Stripe's script under the dashboard CSP, needs the user's approval (root `AGENTS.md`). The hosted invoice page is the fallback this task's Context allows. Follow-up: [175](../../175.md).
- The dashboard no longer calls `upgradeSubscription`, so its document and hook were removed; the backend keeps the mutation for other callers. Twenty-four new strings ship in all 15 languages.
