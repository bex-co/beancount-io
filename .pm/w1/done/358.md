# Text reports print totals that miss their own lines by a cent on a half-cent tie: the trial balance nets to 0.01, `Net Worth` reads -10.00 under `10.01` and `-20.00`, and `Net Profit` reads -10.00 under `-10.01` and `20.00`

**Bug · cli · minor · 45–60m · A1.** From routine cli-qa, 2026-11-22. Text reports round each printed figure on its own, half up with ties away from zero. When an exact figure ends in a half cent (half a share or half a coin at a two-decimal price, a EUR amount at a one-decimal rate) and a total built from it has the opposite sign, the total and its parts round in opposite directions. The printed statement then does not foot. The trial balance's `Valuation/translation adjustment` exists so that the five trees net to zero (w1/287), but its printed lines net to 0.01. `--json` and `--format csv` stay exact and do foot, so `beancount-report-pack`'s tie-outs pass. A person, or an agent reading the text a user pasted, sees books that are a cent out.

Environment: main `c9a096e89`, `bea 0.5.0` from the checkout (`uv run bea`), Python 3.12.14, macOS 26.5.1. Isolated `BEA_CONFIG_DIR`/`XDG_CACHE_HOME`, `BEA_NO_UPDATE_NOTIFIER=1`, `BEA_FILE`/`BEA_TOKEN` unset, stdin closed, no TTY, scratch cwd, fresh processes. Every command exits 0 and `bea check` passes.

| Command | Printed lines | Printed total | The lines add to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `report trial-balance`, `bea balance` | Assets `9,988.63`, Equity `-10,000.00`, adjustment `11.38` | (nets to zero by definition) | `0.01` |
| `report balance-sheet` | Equity `-10,000.00`, earnings `0.00`, adjustment `11.38` | Total equity `-9,988.63` | `-9,988.62` |
| `report income-statement` | Income `-10.01`, Expenses `20.00` | Net Profit `-10.00` (headline and the 2026-01-06 row) | `-9.99` |
| `report overview` | Assets `10.01`, Liabilities `-20.00` | Net Worth `-10.00` | `-9.99` |

`--json` shows the exact figures behind them: assets `9988.625`, equity `-10000.00` and `valuation_adjustment` `11.375`, which do net to zero, and `net_profit` `-9.995` beside income `-10.005`. Control: at `price VTI 95.44 USD` (no tie) the trial balance prints Assets `9,988.60`, adjustment `11.40`, and nets to zero.

## Expected

In text output, a line that is defined as a sum of lines printed above it equals the sum of those printed lines. The trial balance's printed lines net to zero (adjustment `11.37`). The balance sheet's components add to its `Total equity`, which stays `-(Net Worth)`. `Net Profit` reads `-9.99` and `Net Worth` reads `-9.99` here. Basis: `cli/docs/USAGE.md` defines `net_profit` as `-(income + expenses)`, and the adjustment as the residual that nets the trees (w1/287). A statement whose printed figures do not foot reads as an error in the books. JSON and CSV keep their exact values.

## Cause

`_quantize` (`cli/src/cli/commands/report.py:119`) rounds every text figure with `ROUND_HALF_UP` (`:137`), which sends ties away from zero, and `_amounts` (`:148`) applies it to each line on its own. A tie at `+x.xx5` rounds up, while a total of opposite sign that carries the same half cent (`-y.yy5`) rounds down. No single rounding mode fixes this: ties toward +∞ commute with adding whole cents but not with the negation in `net_profit`, and half-even fails when an odd number of cents is added. The derived lines are rounded from their exact values at `:226` (`_print_trial_balance`), `:584`–`:591` (balance sheet adjustment and total equity), `:596` (Net Worth), `:468` (overview totals) and `:527`/`:536` (Net Profit headline and rows).

## Fix

Owned by `cli`, text rendering only. Print each derived line from the rounded figures printed above it, per currency. Net Profit is `-(income + expenses)` as printed, in the headline and in every interval row. Net Worth is assets plus liabilities as printed. The trial balance's and balance sheet's `Valuation/translation adjustment` is the plug that nets the printed lines, beside any `Rounding residual` line. `Total equity` is the sum of the printed equity lines. Rounding of account rows themselves stays as it is, and `--json` and `--format csv` are unchanged. Say in `cli/docs/USAGE.md` (the "Text amounts are rounded…" paragraph) that text totals are footed from the printed figures. No REST, GraphQL or MCP surface renders bea's text.

Regression tests through real `bea` subprocesses on both repro ledgers: the four commands above foot in text, the JSON figures are unchanged, and the `95.44` control is byte-identical. Revert the fix and watch them fail. Run `make check-all` in `cli/`.

## Blast radius

`_amounts` renders every text report. Footing derived lines matters only where a line is defined from others: the trial balance and `bea balance` with no terms (`_print_trial_balance`), the balance sheet's equity block, Net Worth and its "Net Profit is …" sentence, the income statement's headline and rows, and the overview's totals. `report cash-flow`'s section totals and `Net change in cash` follow the same pattern but were not reproduced. A ledger whose valued figures have no more digits than the display precision is unaffected. The hosted dashboard renders its own statements and was not exercised.

**Resolved 2026-11-22** — Text reports now foot every derived line from the rounded figures printed for it, per currency (`_footed` in `cli/src/cli/commands/report.py`); account rows keep their own rounding, and `--json` and `--format csv` are byte-identical to before. `Net Profit` is `-(income + expenses)` as printed, in the headline and every interval row; `Net Worth` is assets plus liabilities as printed, on the overview and the balance sheet, whose as-of series row prints the same figure; the trial balance's and `bea balance`'s `Valuation/translation adjustment` is the plug that nets the printed lines beside any `Rounding residual`, and so is the balance sheet's, whose `Total equity` sums its printed equity lines. Two additions beyond the note: the balance sheet's earnings line is income plus expenses as the income statement prints them, so its "the same period's Net Profit is …" sentence agrees with `report income-statement` (the engine passes the two roots as a text-only `earnings_parts` hint that `--json` strips; `BalanceSheetData.earnings_parts` in the vendored `cli/src/fava/modules/financial_statements.py`); and `report cash-flow`, which reproduced too (rows `-25.03` and `-5.01` under `Total investing -30.03`), now sums its section totals from their rows, `Net change in cash` from the totals, and shows a `Rounding residual` that takes the printed opening and net change to the printed closing cash. The repro now prints adjustment `11.37 USD` (trial balance nets to zero), `Total equity -9,988.63 USD` from `-10,000.00 + 0.00 + 11.37`, and `Net Profit`/`Net Worth` `-9.99 USD`; the `95.44` control, partial valuation (`Unavailable USD`), and `units`/`at_cost`/`at_value` text are unchanged. `cli/docs/USAGE.md` says text totals are footed from the printed figures. Tests: `cli/tests/test_report_text_totals_foot.py` runs real `bea` subprocesses on the three ledgers; its six footing tests fail with the fix reverted, and its JSON, CSV, and byte-identical control tests guard what must not change. `make check-all` passes in `cli/`. Not covered: `report holdings` and `report capital-gains` totals, which sum rows the same way, and the dashboard. Shipped in the commit that closes this note (`cli`).
