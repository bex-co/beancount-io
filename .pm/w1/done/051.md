Expected: USAGE — balance series reach the as-of date and interval breakdowns cover the requested period ("2024-01-05 through 2024-03-31"): series Jan 500, Feb 500, Mar 700; income statement Jan −10, Feb 0, Mar 0.

Cause: `cli/src/bea_engine/report.py:427` (from `w3/292`) takes the period from all entries under `--account`, but `cli/src/fava/ledger.py:195–203` (from `w3/349`) still sets `_date_first/_date_last` from the account-filtered entries, and `interval_ranges` (`ledger.py:214–219`) plus `chart.py` series use those.

Fix/acceptance: with `account` set and no time filter, derive `FilteredLedger` bounds from the same set `_metadata` uses (one shared helper). On the fixture: Broker series dates 01-31, 02-29, 03-31, last value 700 USD; Food periods cover three months. Unfiltered output and `w3/292`/`w3/349` tests unchanged.

Dedupe: gap in `w3/done/349` (its "Match report `_metadata` period bounds" missed the account branch); read `w3/292`, `349`, `375`, `384`, `395`. No open item.
