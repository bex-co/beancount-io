# `bea report income-statement` and `overview` value a period's foreign-currency spending at the as-of rate in the headline but at each interval's own rate in the rows, so `Travel 400.00 USD` sits above monthly rows that add up to 300.00 USD, and `-i yearly` reads 400 where `-i monthly` reads 300 for the same days

**Bug · cli, ledger · major · 90–150m · A1.** From routine cli-qa, 2026-11-20. Under a currency conversion (the default on a single-operating-currency ledger), the income statement's account trees and its `Net Profit` value every income and expense posting of the period at the price on the report's as-of date. Its interval rows value each bucket at that bucket's end date. Once the rate moves inside the period, the rows no longer add up to the headline above them. The same period's total also changes with `--interval`. The overview's `Expenses:` line and its breakdown, and the balance sheet's `Current-period earnings` and "the same period's Net Profit", inherit the headline figure. A1: an agent that totals `periods[].net_profit` from `--json`, or reconciles the monthly table against the headline, gets two different answers from one report. Neither answer is what the spending cost on the days it happened.

Environment: main `9ec0b295c`, `bea 0.5.0` from the checkout (`uv run bea`), macOS 26.5.1. Isolated `BEA_CONFIG_DIR`/`XDG_CACHE_HOME`, `BEA_NO_UPDATE_NOTIFIER=1`, `BEA_FILE`/`BEA_TOKEN` unset, stdin closed, no TTY, cwd a scratch directory. Every case was rerun in fresh processes, and the ledger's hash was unchanged after each run.

## Repro

```sh
cat > main.bean <<'EOF'
option "operating_currency" "USD"
2026-01-01 open Assets:Euro EUR
2026-01-01 open Expenses:Travel EUR
2026-01-01 open Equity:Opening EUR
2026-01-01 price EUR 1.00 USD
2026-01-02 * "Opening"
  Assets:Euro  1000.00 EUR
  Equity:Opening
2026-01-15 * "Trip one"
  Expenses:Travel  100.00 EUR
  Assets:Euro
2026-02-01 price EUR 2.00 USD
2026-02-15 * "Trip two"
  Expenses:Travel  100.00 EUR
  Assets:Euro
EOF
bea --file main.bean check                               # exit 0
bea --file main.bean report income-statement             # monthly (default)
bea --file main.bean report income-statement -i yearly
bea --file main.bean report overview
bea --file main.bean --json report income-statement
bea --file main.bean query "SELECT sum(convert(position, 'USD', date)) AS at_posting_date, sum(convert(position, 'USD')) AS at_latest WHERE account ~ '^Expenses'"
```

## Actual

All commands exit 0.

- `report income-statement` shows `Expenses 400.00 USD`, `Travel 400.00 USD` and `Net Profit: -400.00 USD`. The monthly breakdown below it reads `2026-01-31 … 100.00 USD` and `2026-02-15 … 200.00 USD`, which add up to 300.00 USD.
- `-i yearly` shows one row, `2026-02-15 … 400.00 USD`. The same two trips total 400 by year and 300 by month. `-i weekly` gives 100 + 200 = 300.
- `report overview` shows `Expenses: 400.00 USD` above monthly `EXPENSES` of `100.00 USD` and `200.00 USD`.
- `--json`: `net_profit` is `{"USD": "-400.0000"}`, while the `periods[].net_profit` are `-100.0000` and `-200.0000`.
- `report balance-sheet` repeats the headline: `Current-period earnings (credit): 400.00 USD`, "the same period's Net Profit is -400.00 USD".
- Independent control: the BQL query reads `300 USD` at each posting's date and `400 USD` at the latest price. The January trip cost 100 EUR when 1 EUR was 1.00 USD. The headline revalues it at February's 2.00 USD.

## Expected

One period's flows have one value, whatever the interval. The headline trees, `Net Profit`, and every interval row value each income and expense posting at the price in effect on that posting's date: the rule `convert(position, 'USD', date)` applies. The rows then add up to the headline at every interval. On the repro, every view reads 300.00 USD: monthly 100 + 200, yearly 300, the headline 300, and `Net Profit: -300.00 USD`.

The basis is in `cli/docs/USAGE.md`. It promises "actual period rows with the same signed income and expenses as the account trees", and "a later quote cannot value an earlier interval". The headline breaks the second promise for January's trip. [w5/617](../w5/done/617.md) already treated weekly rows that summed to 960 under a 965 headline as a bug. Translating income and expenses at the rate on the day they happened, and balances at the closing rate, is also the usual accounting convention for foreign-currency books. The balance sheet keeps valuing balances at the as-of date. Its existing `Valuation/translation adjustment` line takes the difference: on the repro, assets 1,600.00, equity -2,000.00, current-period earnings 300.00, and an adjustment of 100.00, so total equity is still -1,600.00. The income statement and the balance sheet keep giving the same `net_profit`. `units` and `at_cost` reports do not change: they convert nothing.

Rejected alternative: valuing each row at the as-of date too. That would make the rows add up, but a later quote would then value every earlier month, against the rule above. It would also break the net-worth series, which must stay at each row's own date.

## Cause

- `cli/src/fava/modules/financial_statements.py:240-260`, `income_statement`. The trees come from `chart.hierarchy(filtered, root, conversion)`, valued once at `filtered.end_date`. The rows come from `chart.interval_totals`, valued at each interval's end. The overview and the balance sheet's earnings use the same two calls.
- `cli/src/bea_engine/report.py:309-354`, `_income_statement`. It takes `net_profit` from the trees and `periods` from the series, and never checks one against the other.
- Hosted, by code reading only: `backend-cluster/ledger/src/features/ledger/service/ledger-finance-service.ts:144-211`, `getIncomeStatement`. It builds `accountHierarchy(…, valuationDate)` beside per-interval `intervalTotals(…)`, so REST, GraphQL and MCP income statements likely show the same split. The dashboard's Reports page reads flows `at_cost` (w4/m26), so EUR spending stays in EUR there and is not affected.

## Fix

Under a currency conversion, and under `at_value`, value Income and Expenses postings at their own date before they are aggregated:
- in the income statement's trees, `net_profit` and interval rows;
- in the overview's income and expense totals and series;
- in the balance sheet's `current_earnings`, so its residual moves into `valuation_adjustment`;
- in the hosted ledger service's `getIncomeStatement` and overview, for REST, GraphQL and MCP parity (root `AGENTS.md`).

Balance series and balance-sheet roots keep as-of valuation. Update `USAGE.md`'s income statement and conversion paragraphs. Add regression tests:
- the repro at `-i monthly`, `weekly` and `yearly`, all reading 300.00, with rows summing to the headline;
- the balance sheet still reconciling, with a 100.00 adjustment;
- a ledger whose rates never move, unchanged;
- a flow posting at cost (`Expenses:Charity 5 VOO {400 USD}`), valued at the VOO price on its date.

## Blast radius and controls

- Affected: `report income-statement`, `report overview` (`Expenses:`/`Income:` lines and series), and `report balance-sheet` (`Current-period earnings`, Net Profit sentence). Also the Income and Expenses roots of `bea balance` and `report trial-balance`. They read `Travel 400.00 USD` and net to zero with no adjustment today. Under the fix they read 300.00 and carry the 100.00 in the `Valuation/translation adjustment:` line that w1/287 added.
- Working controls: a ledger whose prices do not change between the postings and the as-of date. `-x units` and `-x at_cost`, which read 200.00 EUR in the headline and 100.00 + 100.00 EUR in the rows. `report register Expenses -x USD` is a running balance, so it is not a flow control: it reads 100.00 then 400.00 USD, the cumulative 200 EUR at each row's own date (w1/252).
- Not affected: `report budget`, which never converts. `report capital-gains`, which stays in cost currency.
