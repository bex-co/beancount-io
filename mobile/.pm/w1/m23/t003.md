## Objective

Two files still drive animation with React Native's core `Animated` and `useNativeDriver: false` — the exact pattern `d0c1f13` identified as the cause of the ledger drawer's drag flicker before it moved to the UI thread.

## Context

- **`src/common/progress-bar.tsx:28-61`** — `Animated.timing` at 200ms interpolating width `"0%"→"100%"`. This **duplicates** `src/components/progress/index.tsx`, which already does the same job on Reanimated with a `withTiming` width. Check callers, then delete in favour of the Reanimated one rather than porting it.
- **`src/screens/setting/logout-screen.tsx:70-139`** — two `Animated.Value`s: a 300ms fade-in plus `translateY` 20→0 (`useNativeDriver: true`) and a progress bar (`useNativeDriver: false`). Worth knowing this is the most deliberately "designed" motion moment in the app, and it is on the logout screen.
- Motion tokens and `useReduceMotion()` shipped in `w1/m20/t001`; no literal durations should survive this task.

## Steps

1. Find `progress-bar.tsx`'s callers and migrate them to `src/components/progress/`; delete the duplicate.
2. Port the logout screen's fade/translate and progress to Reanimated, reading durations and easings from the motion tokens.
3. Verify the logout sequence still reads as designed, and that reduce-motion collapses it to its end state.

## Files
