## Objective

Twelve pull-to-refresh sites use three different conventions. Pick one, give it a home, and adopt it everywhere.

## Context

Today's three groups:

- `tintColor={theme.primary}` — `transactions-screen.tsx:274-277`, `notifications-screen.tsx:218-222`, `ledger-file-browser-screen/index.tsx:563-567`, `account-table.tsx:372-376`
- `tintColor={isDark ? "white" : "black"}` — `budget-screen.tsx:210-214`, `account-detail-screen.tsx:293-297`, `dashboard-scroll-view/index.tsx:53-59`
- no `tintColor` at all — `quick-add-accounts-selector.tsx:174-181`, `ledger-drawer.tsx:389-390`

`src/components/dashboard-scroll-view/index.tsx` is the closest thing to a shared wrapper (Home + Reports) and already sets `indicatorStyle` per theme (:52) and `flexGrow: 1` so short content stays refreshable (:19-21). No Android props (`colors`, `progressBackgroundColor`) are set anywhere. The `journal-screen` sites from the original note are gone with `w1/m22/t001`.

## Steps

1. Decide the convention — a theme token, not a literal — and export a single `refreshControlProps(theme)` (or extend `dashboard-scroll-view` to own it).
2. Adopt at every site above, including the Android props.
3. Verify on iOS in light and dark; confirm short lists still pull (the `flexGrow: 1` trick).
