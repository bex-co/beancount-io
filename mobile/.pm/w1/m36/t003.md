## Objective

- Reuse verbatim: `EntryRow`, `groupToSections`, `DateSectionHeader` (`src/screens/transactions-screen/`), `openTransactionDetail(router, entry, "merchants")`.

## Steps

1. Query wiring with `directiveTypes: [TRANSACTION]`, `PAGE_SIZE` paging, exact-match re-filter.
2. `SectionList` with `stickySectionHeadersEnabled={false}`, `contentContainerStyle={{flexGrow:1}}`.
3. Row tap → transaction detail with source `"merchants"`.
