## Objective

Create a deterministic, locale-aware screenshot build surface for the three product messages most likely to affect App Store conversion.

## Context

- `mobile/scripts/build-screenshots.sh` currently resizes ten English phone captures with no localized marketing caption layer.
- The first three assets are deliberately ordered as the strongest search/product-page story.
- The app supports iPad, whose App Store set still needs a current capture source; generated PNG output is gitignored and should remain reproducible.

## Steps

1. Define a manifest for locale, display type, source capture, localized caption, output filename, and display order.
2. Extend the existing ImageMagick workflow with a consistent, accessible caption/frame treatment that preserves legibility in every script.
3. Use deterministic demo-ledger captures for financial overview, plain-text/Git ownership, and reporting depth on phone and tablet.
4. Validate safe areas, text wrapping, contrast, dimensions, color profile, and alpha removal without introducing a new package dependency.

## Files

- `mobile/scripts/build-screenshots.sh`
- Screenshot manifest/caption inputs under `mobile/metadata/` or `mobile/docs/marketing-showcase/`.
- `mobile/docs/marketing-showcase/`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] One manifest deterministically defines all target locale/device/order outputs.
- [ ] Captions render correctly for Latin, Cyrillic, and Simplified Chinese scripts without clipping.
- [ ] Source captures contain only stable public demo data and no account, credential, or real financial information.
- [ ] Generated files meet Apple dimensions and have no alpha channel.

## Out of scope

- App preview video, custom product pages, or a new general-purpose design system.
