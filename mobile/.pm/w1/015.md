# Loading and refresh inconsistency across lists

Two small divergences, one unit of work.

**Three `RefreshControl` tint conventions across 12 sites.**

- `tintColor={theme.primary}` — `transactions-screen.tsx:274-277`, `journal-screen.tsx:297-300`, `notifications-screen.tsx:218-222`, `ledger-file-browser-screen/index.tsx:563-567`, `account-table.tsx:372-376`
- `tintColor={isDark ? "white" : "black"}` — `budget-screen.tsx:210-214`, `account-detail-screen.tsx:293-297`, `dashboard-scroll-view/index.tsx:53-59`
- no `tintColor` at all — `quick-add-accounts-selector.tsx:174-181`, `ledger-drawer.tsx:389-390`

`src/components/dashboard-scroll-view/index.tsx` is the closest thing to a shared wrapper (Home + Reports) and also sets `indicatorStyle` per theme (:52) and `flexGrow: 1` so short content stays refreshable (:19-21). No `progressBackgroundColor`/`colors` (the Android props) are set anywhere. Pick one convention and give it a home.

**Journal shows a spinner where transactions shows a skeleton.** `journal-screen.tsx:313` renders a raw `ActivityIndicator` while the near-identical `transactions-screen.tsx:288` renders `TransactionsListSkeleton`. Both are `SectionList`s over ledger entries with the same shape. `mobile/CLAUDE.md` calls for skeletons, not spinners, in content areas waiting on a query.
