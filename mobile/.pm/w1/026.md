# An unmatched `(tabs)` route renders black, not `+not-found`

Deep-linking `beancount:///(app)/(tabs)/index` — a path that looks right but is not a route, since the Home screen is `/(app)/(tabs)/` — leaves the app on a fully black screen with no tab bar and no way back except a relaunch. `app/+not-found.tsx` exists and is not what renders.

**Pre-existing, not caused by `w1/m22`.** Confirmed by stashing only `app/(app)/(tabs)/_layout.tsx` (back to `lazy: false`), firing the same URL, and getting the same black screen.

Not user-reachable today: it takes a hand-typed deep link, and every real route (`/(app)/(tabs)/accounts`, `/(app)/(tabs)/`, …) resolves fine. It matters for two smaller reasons — an agent or a QA script driving the app by deep link hits it and reads it as an app crash, and a future universal link with a typo would land a real user there.

Worth checking whether `+not-found` is reachable at all from inside the `(app)` group, or only at the root.
