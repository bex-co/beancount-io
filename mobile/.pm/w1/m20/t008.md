## Objective

Verify everything this milestone shipped against the app's cross-cutting quality bar, on a real device, in both themes and with the accessibility setting both on and off.

## Context

- Theme tokens come from `src/common/theme/`; never hard-code colors. `LoadingTile` resolves the effective theme via `useTheme()` — never compare `themeVar` to `"dark"` directly, it can hold `"system"`.
- Strings go in the English base under `src/translations/` and render via `useTranslations()`, not `i18n.t()`.
- Analytics: `analytics.track(name, props)` from `src/common/analytics.ts`, snake_case event names (see `tap_account_picker_confirm` from `w1/m18`).
- Reduce-motion is the headline accessibility claim of this milestone and cannot be verified in a simulator screenshot — it needs a device toggle with the app running.
- Motion budget from t001: entrance/transition under 500ms, direct feedback under 300ms.

## Steps

1. Walk Home, account detail, Reports and Budget in light **and** dark: chart draw-in, bar grow-in, headline count-up, range morph, pill indicator, refresh, first-load crossfade.
2. Toggle the OS reduce-motion setting with the app open and re-walk all of it. Every animation must land at its end state with no travel, and `LoadingTile` must stop looping. Confirm scrubbing is **not** gated — it is direct manipulation, not decorative motion.
3. Confirm no literal durations or easings remain in the milestone's diff; everything reads from the t001 tokens.
4. Check for hard-coded colors in the new code, including gradient stops and indicator fills.
5. Verify the time-range pills in a non-English locale (label widths differ; `YTD` and `ALL` are localized) and confirm the indicator still lands correctly.
6. Confirm loading states still set background colors, and `SafeAreaView` spacing is unchanged.
7. Time the animations against the motion budget on a real device, not a simulator.
8. Add analytics only if this milestone introduced a new user-visible surface worth measuring; do not instrument animation frames.

## Files

- Any file touched by t001–t007.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every animated surface verified in light **and** dark on a real device.
- [ ] With reduce-motion on, all entrance/transition motion is absent and `LoadingTile` is static; scrubbing still works normally.
- [ ] No hard-coded colors, no literal durations in the milestone diff.
- [ ] Time-range pills verified in at least one non-English locale.
- [ ] Loading states keep their background colors; no layout shift on any skeleton→content handoff.
- [ ] Animations measured within the motion budget (< 500ms entrance/transition, < 300ms feedback).

## Out of scope

- Behavior changes — this is a verification pass; file real defects as follow-up work rather than expanding scope here.
