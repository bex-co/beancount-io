## Objective

Give the time-range pills a selection indicator that slides between pills, so the control that drives every chart transition stops hard-restyling on each tap.

## Context

- `src/components/time-range-pills/index.tsx:51-83` — generic over key, one `TouchableOpacity` per pill (:62). The active pill is a solid `theme.primary` background applied by re-styling on re-render (:34-36). `Haptics.selectionAsync()` fires on change (:66-68). There is no indicator view and no animation.
- Consumers: `src/screens/home-screen/components/account-charts-card.tsx:122-134` (pills sit outside the pager so the range survives tab switches), `src/components/balance-chart-card/index.tsx:75-82`, `src/screens/reports-screen/reports-screen.tsx`, and `src/screens/budget-screen/budget-screen.tsx:181-188` over `BUDGET_TIME_SPANS`.
- Monarch reference: `1M 3M 6M YTD 1Y ALL` as a segmented row directly under the plot.
- Note `w1/010` proposes generalizing this component to absorb the account picker's chips and the journal header's filter pills (adding `scrollable`/`haptics` props). Do not do that here, but do not make it harder either.
- The one existing piece of chart-adjacent motion is `src/components/segmented-pages/index.tsx:96-103`, which moves its highlight optimistically one frame ahead of `onPageSelected`. Match that responsiveness.

## Steps

1. Render a single absolutely-positioned indicator behind the labels and animate its `translateX` (and width, if pills differ in width) with `useAnimatedStyle`.
2. Measure pill positions with `onLayout` and store them; do not assume equal widths — the labels are localized and `YTD`/`ALL` differ in length across the 13 locales.
3. Move the indicator on press immediately, not after state round-trips, mirroring `segmented-pages`' optimistic highlight.
4. Use the `fast` duration token and the spring/ease-out from t001 — this is direct feedback, so keep it under 300ms.
5. Keep the existing `selectionAsync()` haptic on change and the current active/inactive label colors.
6. Verify all four consumers, including the budget screen's different label set.
7. Confirm the indicator lands correctly on first mount when a non-default range is already selected (Home defaults to `6M`).

## Files

- `src/components/time-range-pills/index.tsx`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The indicator slides between pills on tap in under 300ms, and starts moving on press rather than after a state round-trip.
- [ ] Correct with unequal pill widths and verified in a non-English locale.
- [ ] The indicator is positioned correctly on first mount with a pre-selected range.
- [ ] With reduce-motion on, it jumps to the selected pill with no travel.
- [ ] All four consumers render correctly in light **and** dark; the existing haptic still fires only when the selection actually changes.
- [ ] No literal durations in the diff.

## Out of scope

- Changing the range set or the labels.
