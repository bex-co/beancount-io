# Extend the skeleton→content crossfade app-wide

Every skeleton in the app is a bare ternary — `{isLoading ? <Skeleton/> : <Real/>}` — so the pulsing tile hard-cuts to real content at whatever opacity the pulse happened to reach. `src/components/loading-tile/index.tsx:44-61` pulses opacity 1 → 0.5 → 1 on a 600ms cycle with no easing, which makes the cut land at a random brightness each time.

Roughly 18 sites: `transactions-list-skeleton.tsx:50-67`, `category-breakdown.tsx:334-350`, `notifications-screen.tsx:117-124`, `commit-detail-screen.tsx:124-131`, `ledger-file-browser-screen/index.tsx:181-188`, `multi-postings-transaction/list-item.tsx:98-109`, `accounts-screen.tsx:129-135`, `account-picker-screen.tsx:344-352`, `suggestion-chips.tsx:83-85`, `quick-add-accounts-selector.tsx:188-192`, `text-input-screen/index.tsx:212-223`, `multi-postings-transaction-screen.tsx:521-526`, `ledger-file-editor-screen/index.tsx:552-554`, `home-screen/components/*`, `budget-screen.tsx:195-198`, `reports-screen/components/account-transactions-card.tsx:101`, `setting/account-header.tsx:60`.

Low risk: layout is already pixel-matched at most of these (see the comments cited in `w1/014`), so a crossfade has nothing to shift. `entering={FadeIn}` on the loaded branch is roughly a one-line change per site.
