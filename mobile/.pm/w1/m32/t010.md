## Objective

## Steps

1. Transport construction: headers carry Bearer + `Accept-Language`, body carries `ledgerId`/`sessionId`, `expo/fetch` is the fetch.

3. Deep-link prefill: `q` lands in input state, send is not called.
4. Session reset: New chat yields a fresh `sessionId` and the reseeded welcome thread.
5. Quota-vs-retryable error classification (t005's split).
