# Extract the scrollable-axis chart shell (`budget-bar-chart-d3` / `income-expense-bar-chart`)

Found during `w1/m20/t009`'s simplify pass, and deliberately left undone there.

`chart-chrome.tsx` took the low-risk half of the duplication between these two
charts — the styles factory, `LEGEND_HEIGHT`, the layout constants, the
placeholder and the legend. What remains duplicated is the **shell**: a fixed
y-axis `Svg` column, a horizontal `ScrollView` that auto-scrolls to the latest
period (`onContentSizeChange` → `scrollToEnd`), the grid lines and zero
baseline, and the `GestureDetector` swipe-ownership wrap. Two independent
reviewers measured it at roughly 60–90 near-verbatim lines per file.

The natural shape is a `ScrollableAxisChart` taking
`{ chartHeight, leftPadding, yTicks, yScale, currencySymbol, plotWidth, isEmpty, legend }`
plus a children slot for the plot-specific SVG.

**Why it was not done in t009:** unlike the chrome, this restructures the render
tree of two shipped charts, and t009 was scoped behavior-preserving. The plot
contents are genuinely different — budget draws favorable-tinted bars plus a
dashed step line, income/expense draws grouped bars plus a net line and dots —
so the merge has to stop precisely at the shell or it becomes the wrong kind of
sharing. Worth its own pass with its own verification, not a cleanup rider.

Sub-hour is optimistic; closer to a small milestone once both charts are
re-verified in light and dark.
