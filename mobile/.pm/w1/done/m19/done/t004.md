## Objective

Close the app's biggest invalidation inconsistency: **editing** a transaction refetches seven queries, while **creating** one refetches nothing at all — recovery is delegated to whichever screen happened to launch the add flow.

## Context

- `src/screens/multi-postings-transaction/hooks/use-add-entries-to-remote.ts` wraps `useAddEntriesMutation()` with no options whatsoever.
- The only thing keeping the UI honest today is the `AddTransactionCallback` global-fn hack (`src/common/globalFnFactory.ts:49`): the launching screen registers its own refresh — Home `home-screen.tsx:110,:125` (ledgerMeta + balanceSheet + a `refreshSignal` to the cards), Transactions `transactions-screen.tsx:218`, Journal `journal-screen.tsx:249` (each just its own `refetch()`).
- Consequence: add a transaction from Home and Accounts (`trialBalance`), Reports (`incomeStatement`), the Transactions list, budget actuals, `getLedgerErrors` and `listCommits` all keep pre-write values — and with `lazy: false` every one of those is mounted and on screen one tab-tap away.
- Two call sites go through the hook: `multi-postings-transaction-screen.tsx:420` and `add-transaction-next-screen.tsx:257`.
- **Keep `AddTransactionCallback`.** It does a different job — navigate back to the originating screen and let it settle — and t006 still needs it for the `refreshSignal` cards. It just stops being the only thing guarding data freshness.
- Invalidate only on success. `add-transaction-next-screen.tsx:262` checks `result.data?.addEntries?.success`; `multi-postings-transaction-screen.tsx:423` checks the hook's `error` field instead. Use the payload's `success` in both — the hook's `error` is stale-by-a-render at that point.

## Steps

1. Fire `invalidateLedgerData(client, "entries")` from the hook on a successful `addEntries`, so both call sites inherit it.
2. Make the multi-postings screen branch on the mutation payload rather than the hook's `error` field.
3. Verify from each entry point (Home quick-add, Home multi-leg, Transactions, Journal) that Accounts, Reports and the budget cards all reflect the new transaction.
