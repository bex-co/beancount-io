## Objective

The liabilities page of the account-charts carousel becomes a door to the Accounts tab — without taps firing during a horizontal swipe of the pager.

## Context

- `src/screens/home-screen/components/account-charts-card.tsx` — the `PagedCarousel` pages net worth / assets / liabilities (`liabilitiesSeries` at :44, page keys at :86).
- **The pager is the hazard named in the source note:** a tap must not fire while the finger is swiping pages horizontally. Prefer the platform's own press-vs-scroll disambiguation (a header `Pressable` inside the scrollable pager already cancels on drag); add an explicit guard only if verification shows ghost taps.
- Whether the whole page or only the card header is tappable: header-only via the shared t001 affordance, consistent with every other door.

## Steps

1. Wire the shared affordance on the card; navigate to the Accounts tab.
2. Decide/verify behavior on the other pages (net worth, assets): same destination is acceptable v1 — the chevron then lives on the card header regardless of page.
3. Verify no navigation fires during page swipes and during scrub gestures on the embedded chart.

## Files

- `src/screens/home-screen/components/account-charts-card.tsx`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Tap on the header opens Accounts; a horizontal page swipe never navigates.
- [ ] Chart scrubbing on the card still owns its gesture (no regression against `useHorizontalSwipeOwnerGesture`).

## Outcome 2026-08-18

The hazard this task was sized around no longer exists. `PagedCarousel` is gone;
the card now uses `SegmentedPages`, whose `PagerView` sets `scrollEnabled={false}`
— pages change by tab press only, so there is no horizontal page swipe for a tap
to fire during. Verified anyway with real XCUITest drags: a horizontal drag
across the plot and one across the tab strip both left the app on Home, and the
chart kept its scrub gesture. The affordance sits on the card header for all
three pages, and the skeleton carries the same header so the card does not grow
when data lands.

## Out of scope

- Per-page deep destinations (e.g. filtered-to-liabilities Accounts view) — no such filter route exists.
