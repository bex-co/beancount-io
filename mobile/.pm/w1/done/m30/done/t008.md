## Objective

Walk the newly translated screens in the six locales this milestone added, and confirm nothing truncates in light or dark — and that `fa`, the app's only right-to-left locale, lays out correctly on screens that have never rendered RTL text before.

## Context

- Highest-risk controls, same as m29/t009: reports range chips (`range1M`…`rangeAll`), the receipt-capture action row, the multi-posting toolbar (`addPosting`, `remainderLabel`, `autoLabel`), the ledger editor's save/conflict buttons, and the open-account type/currency pickers.
- `bg` and `nl` are the length risks; `fa` is the layout risk — icon/label order, numeric alignment in amounts, and the chart headline that swaps during scrub.
- Simulator driving is documented in `mobile/CLAUDE.md` (`xcrun simctl` launch, deep links, screenshots, `xcrun simctl ui $UDID appearance dark|light`). Locale switches in-app from the ledger drawer.

## Steps

1. In `fa`, walk the reports tab, Home cards, multi-posting entry, open-account, the ledger file list and editor, notifications, and receipt capture; screenshot each in light and dark.
2. Check specifically that amounts, dates, and `{{token}}` insertions read correctly under RTL rather than merely being mirrored.
3. In `bg` and `nl`, screenshot the same high-risk controls in both themes.
4. Spot-check `ca`, `sk`, and `uk` on the reports chips and the receipt action row.
5. Shorten any truncating string rather than changing layout; file a note if a layout is too tight for every valid translation.

## Files

- `src/translations/{bg,ca,fa,nl,sk,uk}.ts` (wording fixes only)

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Light and dark screenshots for `fa` across all seven screen groups above.
- [ ] No truncated, clipped, or overlapping string in any locale checked.
- [ ] RTL layout verified on amounts, dates, and interpolated sentences — not assumed from the mirroring.
- [ ] Integrity suite still green after any wording fix.

## Out of scope
