## Objective

Run `/simplify` over this milestone's diff. The specific known duplication: whole-file integrity strictly subsumes `expectLocaleParity(prefix)`, so the two prefix suites and their exception lists now say the same thing twice, in two places, with two allowlists that can disagree.

## Context

- `src/translations/__tests__/account-picker-parity.test.ts` — one line, `expectLocaleParity("accountPicker")`.
- `src/translations/__tests__/budget-parity.test.ts` — three exceptions and a `minKeys: 21` guard.
- `src/translations/__tests__/locale-parity.ts` — hosts both `expectLocaleParity` and the new whole-file extractor; the two share the key-extraction and file-reading code.
- `budget-parity`'s exceptions are the same idea as `INTENTIONALLY_UNTRANSLATED` — but `budgetAccountPlaceholder` is a real permanent exception while `budget` / `budgetEntries` were only ever "owned by another feature", which whole-file parity makes moot.
- `src/translations/__tests__/index.test.ts` derives `SUPPORTED_LOCALES` from `translationLocales()`; keep that dependency working.

## Steps

1. Fold the two prefix suites' real content into the integrity suite: keep `budgetAccountPlaceholder` as an `INTENTIONALLY_UNTRANSLATED` entry with its reason, drop the two "predate the feature" exceptions.
2. Delete `account-picker-parity.test.ts` and `budget-parity.test.ts`; delete `expectLocaleParity` and its `LocaleParityOptions` if nothing else calls it, keeping `translationLocales()` and the shared readers.
3. Update the `locale-parity.ts` header comment — it currently explains itself as "the shared body of the per-feature parity suites", which stops being true.
4. Re-read the six locale diffs for accidental duplication: a key translated twice, or a group comment copied out of order.
5. `yarn lint` / `yarn typecheck` / `yarn test:unit`.
