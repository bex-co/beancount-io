## Objective

Keep the charts reading left-to-right under RTL — earliest date on the left, latest on the right — and make that an explicit decision in the code rather than an accident of whichever container they land in.

## Context

- Three files: `src/common/d3/budget-bar-chart-d3.tsx`, `src/common/d3/income-expense-bar-chart.tsx`, `src/common/d3/interactive-line-chart.tsx`. All three use physical margin/padding, which `t003` deliberately left alone, and `interactive-line-chart.tsx` also uses absolute `left:` / `right:`, which `t004` left alone.
- **Correction, found during implementation:** this task assumed those physical margins were chart geometry and should stay. They are not. All five are legend and label chrome — two swatch margins in each bar chart, and the scrub label's margin in the line chart — which live outside the `<Svg>` and mirror like any other row. They were **converted** to logical props, not kept, and the step and criterion below are amended to match.
- Chart geometry is computed in a d3 scale's own coordinate space and drawn with `react-native-svg`. SVG coordinates do not mirror under RTL, but the **React Native container around the SVG does** — so a chart whose legend, labels, or overlay live in RN views outside the `<Svg>` will half-mirror unless pinned.
- Time on a financial axis reads left-to-right in Persian financial interfaces as it does elsewhere. Mirroring the axis would invert the meaning of every trend line in the app, so the decision here is: **charts do not mirror; their surrounding chrome does.**
- `interactive-line-chart.tsx` is also the subject of open milestone **m26** (UI-thread scrubbing) and `m20/t009`. Keep this change small and additive so it does not collide — touch positioning only, not the `PanResponder`/state work those own.
- The scrub overlay is the one place where direction genuinely matters for interaction: a touch x-coordinate maps to a data point through the scale, and if the container mirrors while the SVG does not, the readout tracks the wrong point.

## Steps

1. Pin the chart containers to LTR explicitly — `direction: "ltr"` on the wrapping view, or an equivalent that survives the parent being RTL — rather than relying on the SVG not mirroring.
2. Verify the axis label rows, legends, and value readouts that live outside the `<Svg>` are pinned with it, so a label does not jump to the other end.
3. Confirm the `interactive-line-chart` scrub readout maps a touch to the same data point in Persian as in English.
4. ~~Leave the three files' internal physical margins physical — they are chart coordinates — and comment them so the next spacing sweep does not convert them.~~ Amended: none of them were chart coordinates, so all five were converted like the rest of the app's spacing.
