## Objective

Drive the app in Persian, in right-to-left layout, and look at it. Mirroring defects are visual by nature — a chevron pointing the wrong way, a gap on the wrong side, a label colliding with an amount — and no unit test finds any of them.

## Context

- This is the same walk `m30/t008` did for text length, now for layout. That pass found a real defect (Persian digits in the range chips reading as a word), which is the argument for doing it rather than trusting the diff.
- The simulator has no camera permission, so the receipt-capture flow stops at the permission screen. `t004` converts those three views without being able to walk them — record that gap here honestly rather than implying coverage, exactly as m29 and m30 did.
- `xcrun simctl screenshot` is the capture method; the expo-mcp OAuth path is broken.
- Walk both themes. m24 fixed the light neutral ramp, and a mirrored layout can expose contrast at edges that were never on the light side before.

## Steps

1. Set the app to Persian from Settings and confirm the restart prompt from `t002` fires and comes back right-to-left in Persian.
2. Walk, in light and dark: home dashboard (cards, charts), reports, accounts list, account detail, transactions list and a transaction detail, multi-posting entry, open-account, the account picker, notifications, the ledger drawer (swipe open and closed), and settings.
3. At each screen check four things: which edge content is anchored to, which way chevrons and back arrows point, whether amounts and labels collide, and whether anything truncates that did not before.
4. Open and close the drawer with both a flick and a slow drag.
5. Scrub the interactive line chart and confirm the readout tracks the touch.
6. Switch back to English and confirm every screen is byte-identical to before the milestone.
7. Record what was found and what could not be reached.
