# Time-range pill indicator lands beside the pills, not under them

`src/components/time-range-pills/index.tsx` — the sliding indicator sits to the **right of "ALL"**, half off the row, instead of under the selected pill. No label renders in `labelActive` white, so the row reads as having no selection at all. Reproduced on the Home net-worth chart in the iOS simulator (iPhone 17 Pro), light theme, both in Expo Go and the dev build.

**Pre-existing, not caused by `w1/m21`.** Confirmed by screenshotting the same screen with the m21 working tree stashed — identical. m21 only replaced the raw `Haptics.selectionAsync()` call in `onPress` with `haptics.selection()`; it touches no layout.

Where to look: the indicator is `position: absolute` as the row's **first child** with no `left`, so its static position is whatever Yoga gives an absolute child inside a `justifyContent: "center"` row — then `translateX: layout.x` is added on top of that offset rather than from the row's content origin. `moveTo` (:97-119) sets `x` from `event.nativeEvent.layout.x`, which is relative to the row. Likely fix: pin `left: 0` on `styles.indicator` so the transform starts from a known origin.

Sub-hour. Worth a `LayoutChangeEvent`-driven check in both themes and at least one RTL/long-label locale, since the widths are measured rather than assumed.
